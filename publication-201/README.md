The connected 43/16 crossing region contains 183 skin faces, the incident stars of all 43 sampled inside nodes, and a simple 91-node boundary. The complete boundary is outside the 185 queried bone banks and has no exact boundary-edge intersections among the tested candidates. A 167-parent fan fit nevertheless introduced 96 exact skin self pairs and increased target crossing identities, so it is rejected. This demonstrates why a clear patch boundary alone is insufficient.

For 109/114, a common-direction LP with 45 bone separation rows was feasible to numerical precision but allowed only 0.012912 mm common progress. A scaled QP converged but was rejected at shape. A second 15-row closest-feature fit allowed 0.019386 mm common progress but likewise failed strict shape. The failed attempts are retained; neither result proves that no other valid repair exists.

A subsequent 43/16 connected-position fit at alpha 0.5 passed its initial area check but had 49 exact skin self pairs and failed the layer-volume guard, including 3 previously positive tetrahedra below their margin; it was rejected. A 65/99 exact-witness fit at alpha 0.25 preserved affected failure identities but introduced 9 exact self pairs and did not change the 148 raw target crossings. Pre-existing invalid cells also experienced signed-volume decreases; its surface result is not a physical repair. A 109/114 shape-passing, zero-self construction candidate was rebased onto a69, but introduced six layer-failure identities, so its full all-bank and physical checks were stopped. These are failure and method-development receipts, not passing replacements.

The layer checks are only nonregression diagnostics for a transferred offset-prism construction. It still contains thousands of authored prism orientation failures and two nonpositive Float32 tetrahedra in each layer, lacks conservative donor cell-volume and mass ancestry, and has not been admitted to the native physics. No motion, physical mass, moving-pose, full solid containment, native physical step or viewer check is demonstrated. The skin shell retains 45 boundary edges. No clean product source was committed.

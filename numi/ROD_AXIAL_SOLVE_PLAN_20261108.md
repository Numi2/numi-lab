# Scoped constrained axial solve

The retained failure shows that a stretch consistency guard alone rejects valid motion. Test a direct implicit constrained axial chain solve as a separately isolated candidate: arbitrary node count and per-edge axial stiffness, two hard endpoint targets, positive oriented edges, and freely moving internal nodes. For this first implementation admit only exactly world-X-aligned, non-colliding, non-rigid, undamped, zero-rest-curvature and zero-twist configurations with no transverse motion or gravity. All other cases use the separately retained stretch-certificate solver. This is a deliberately limited research path, not a general rod repair or an enabled production option.

Solve the complete linear tridiagonal inertial plus elastic equations at the end of the step, accounting for predicted positions, target motion and gravity in X. Calculate support impulses from elastic forces and endpoint momentum including prescribed target velocity. Reject nonfinite, singular or crossed geometry. Preserve the accepted/rollback behavior of the owner and leave the normal output path in charge. Use FP32 on Metal; compare against independent FP64 equations at the same model and timestep.

Before promoting anything require: the predeclared two-node axial force/work, free-middle-node symmetric/asymmetric/moving states and support reactions, multiple free interior nodes, unequal edge stiffness/mass, endpoint target motion and gravity, continuation/replay, degenerate and reversed-edge failure or safe fallback, an unrelated bent/contact nine-node probe, and explicit evidence of whether general transactional state was restored. Gates are independent state and reaction agreement (1 micrometre, 0.001 m/s, 1% or 1e-6 N absolute floor), with 1% work/energy on the separately registered two-node series. Any unsupported physical domain remains unqualified. Keep the earlier baseline failures and failed initial candidate.

# Metal single-edge pinned rod: research result

The unchanged Metal owner published a 99.8% low support reaction on a prestrained, two-endpoint axial rod; the original failing witness is retained on `codex/suture-earned-continuation` at `29817dfaa`. This isolated candidate solves the single edge analytically only when both distinct ends are hard-prescribed, self/tool/CCD flags are clear, no rigid body is coupled, linear damping is zero, and the step is not an event segment. It constructs target impulses from end-of-step elastic force and endpoint momentum balance and uses the existing publication/failure path. Other cases retain the original shader and its unresolved convergence limitation.

The source at `b64625160` and the linked Metal library are identified in [the retained evidence](metal-pinned-rod-20261108). A one-step pinned witness reduced force relative error to `3.27e-8`. A 4-second prescribed 8-0 Nylon effective axial ramp used 41 accepted samples at a **0.1 s** step; maximum relative force error against the fixed affine relation was `1.02e-6`. Integrated target work was `2.1927047055214681e-4 J`, versus `2.1927014910849644e-4 J` change in elastic-plus-kinetic energy, a relative difference `1.47e-6`. This is an algebraically constrained single-edge fixture, so it does not establish that 0.1 s is a suitable timestep for unconstrained rods or tissue.

Interpolating that Metal trajectory at the four held-out ophthalmic measurements yields 3.0550%, 3.0172%, 1.7589%, and 2.9594% relative absolute force error, all within the preregistered 10% gate. The effective relation was fit on one specimen and restricted to 2–4 mm; it is not a measured stress-free length, independently identified modulus, or a prediction across materials or rates. The physical-data prediction, comparator and pinned Metal programs are retained on `codex/suture-earned-continuation` at `9f9934775`.

An additional standalone Metal witness passed swapped anchors, nonzero gravity and endpoint velocities, independent endpoint momentum/reaction balance, repeated continuation from the same copied state, and preservation of output on a degenerate-target rejection. This tests the standalone rod state and result object; it does not prove an encompassing MetalWorld checkpoint. The existing nine-node Metal rod probe passed on both baseline and candidate libraries, including its documented self/tool contact and deterministic checks. Its reported diagnostics matched except elapsed timing; it is not an exhaustive proof of unchanged behavior.

**Research only.** The candidate has not been integrated into the release or tested on a coupled surgical state. The original multi-edge premature-convergence issue remains, as do rod–tissue force transfer, full transaction/rollback and force accuracy in suturing. No completion or performance claim follows from this fixture.

## Three-node counterexample

A separately committed witness `998e12e15` used the same prestrained axial interval and three equally spaced nodes, all three hard-prescribed, with two equal rest edges. Both the original and candidate shaders reported success and published an endpoint reaction of `0.00007962263043737039 N`, compared with the independently computed `0.089379112368462382 N` axial force: 99.9109% relative error. The source/binary identities and both outputs are retained alongside the preceding evidence. This confirms that the single-edge branch does not address even this simple multi-edge configuration; it does not quantify the surgical rod's force error.

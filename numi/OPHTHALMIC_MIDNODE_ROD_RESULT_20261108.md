# Free-middle-node axial rod: rejected continuation

The [preregistered](OPHTHALMIC_MIDNODE_ROD_PLAN_20261108.md) three-node free-middle reference test ran on the Apple M6 with the [pinned instrument](measurements/metal_rod_midnode_probe.mm). Both the existing Metal shader and the isolated two-node candidate returned GPU status 4 (MR_ROD_GPU_DID_NOT_CONVERGE) and published no state in all three cases, with the configured 128-iteration limit. Every reference had positive edge lengths. No force or state output from a rejected run qualifies as an accepted physical result.

The FP64 discrete reference predicts endpoint reactions of approximately ±0.08938 N. In the symmetric case the middle node remains at 0.061 m. In the asymmetric case it moves from 0.060 m to 0.060998998 m during the 1 ms implicit step; the velocity case has a separate prediction. All three completion gates fail through explicit rejection. Raw output, failed status, binary/source/library/Metal hashes and both shader identities are in [the evidence directory](measurements/evidence/metal-midnode-20261108). The two-node specialization intentionally does not apply here, and identical outcomes are consistent with that restriction.

This complements the earlier fully pinned three-node case, which *was* published but failed force accuracy. Together they show that both false accepted reaction and rejected ordinary multi-edge axial motion need attention. They do not establish failure of all rods or the clinical response of a suture. A general constrained solve and physically scaled reaction certificate must pass both kinds of test, as well as ordinary moving, bent, contact and transactional paths, before promotion.

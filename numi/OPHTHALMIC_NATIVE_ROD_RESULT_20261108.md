# Effective axial rod: failed native reaction and work gates

The [preregistered CPU rod test](OPHTHALMIC_NATIVE_ROD_PLAN_20261108.md) failed its physical gates. The unchanged native owner at source commit `57aa9a6170e5c49d984cafb2d31fe61e13e0a450` reported 4,001 accepted states, but maximum relative deviation from the fitted axial force was 99.7998%. Integrated moving-target work was `4.390368850509814e-7 J`, while the independently evaluated elastic-plus-kinetic energy rose `2.1927053487140095e-4 J`: a 99.7998% relative mismatch. These results do not qualify force transfer, work, the four held-out specimens, or the surgical workflow.

The two-node fixture has both endpoints hard-attached and begins from an explicitly constructed prestrained state. Its fitted affine offset is an effective local approximation, not an identified stress-free length. Both initial and final energy were evaluated using the rod's public energy function; target work was integrated from its public reaction output. The owner exposed its states as successful, so accepted-step count alone would miss this failure. Complete original trajectory, source/header/program hashes, compiler and result are [retained](measurements/evidence/native-rod-20261108/unchanged).

## Discriminating test-only diagnosis

The unchanged CPU solver stops when the **net** end-of-sweep positional and rotational changes are small. In this fully attached case, intermediate stretch and attachment projections can cancel in positions while their accumulated multipliers and reaction are still progressing. To test that hypothesis, an isolated copy required 8,000 sweeps before the original final position criterion could terminate and ran two diagnostic samples only. The maximum reaction-force error fell to `1.08935e-7` and work discrepancy to `1.08935e-7`. The precise source/probe patches, two-sample output, and hashes are [retained](measurements/evidence/native-rod-20261108/forced-sweeps). This supports premature termination as the cause in this fixture. It is not a production repair, an efficient solver, a four-second qualification, or Metal evidence.

The next repair must certify the relevant constraint and multiplier equations, not just end-of-sweep net displacement. It must reproduce correct force and work under the original full trajectory, handle multiple supported rod and attachment cases without prohibitive iteration counts, fail explicitly when uncertified, and independently qualify Metal and restoration. Raising the iteration budget alone is not the completion criterion.

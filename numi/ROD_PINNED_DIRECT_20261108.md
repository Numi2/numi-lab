# Two-endpoint axial rod: bounded direct-solve experiment

A source-level CPU research candidate handles a single-edge rod whose two distinct endpoints are both hard-prescribed, linear damping is zero, and self-collision is disabled. It evaluates the endpoint stretch and solves the two support impulses from elastic force and node momentum balance, publishing only after geometry, finite values and energy checks. All other cases continue through the separately retained conservative convergence-guard experiment. This is not a general rod solver.

Against the earlier four-second, 4,001-sample effective 8-0 Nylon fixture, source commit `1a3120905` returned no failed steps. Maximum relative deviation from the frozen affine force was `3.09e-15`; integrated support work was `2.1927053487140092e-4 J` and stored-plus-kinetic energy change was `2.1927053487140095e-4 J`. These near-machine-precision agreement figures test the implemented discrete relation, not independent validation of a general material law.

The recorded native trajectory, interpolated at the same held-out raw extensions under the preregistered procedure, has relative absolute force errors 3.0550%, 3.0172%, 1.7589% and 2.9593% on the four specimens, all below 10%. The four measurements are from the same 8-0 Nylon study and loading rate. The effective rest length and stiffness were fit to one specimen in the 2–4 mm interval, and the nonzero fitted offset is represented by an explicitly constructed prestrained state. They do not establish true rest length, Young's modulus, geometry, loading-rate behavior, or applicability outside that range.

A separate standalone CPU test passes swapped anchor order, nonzero endpoint velocities, gravity and force/momentum balance, twist evolution, deterministic repeated execution, and rejection with state and reaction restoration for degenerate targets. The exact trajectory, input/source/binary hashes and test output are [retained](rod-pinned-direct-20261108). The held-out comparison program and preregistration live on `codex/suture-earned-continuation` at `e6e62b31f` and `53908aec5`.

**Not promoted.** The inherited correction-based guard still fails the existing ordinary nine-node rod probe, and the direct path applies only to the constrained single-edge CPU case. Metal, full state save/restore, deformation beyond the calibrated interval, and surgical interactions remain unqualified. Correction magnitude alone is not a general force-error certificate. General dimensional residuals and a practical coupled solve must be established before integration.

# Source-pinned no-step owner seed: implementation and test plan

Status: design only. The accepted capture at source `0cba39b21e8adf7d8e95f705d386dac63b6a3d9b`, evidence commit `701c4b92b`, and its `numi.surgical-partial-owner-capsule.v1` files remain permanently `complete=0`, `restorable=0`. They cannot be promoted by adding metadata after the fact. They arise from a fresh synthetic trajectory initialized from a partial step-1389 predecessor, not the separate historical v3 step-1870 tissue-bridge checkpoint. Its `0x689a5b7f961d187f` compiled fingerprint identifies only the actual world compiled for that capture; it does not identify a missing historical runtime.

## Source observations and minimum supported scope

1. `MetalWorldContext::initializeResidentState` calls `submitImpl`; `validateAndBuildLayout` rejects zero control steps. `uploadBatch` and `encodeResidentStateInitialization` then clear private state and a physics submission follows. Submitting one step is not a no-step seed, and weakening the general zero-step validation would change unrelated behavior.
2. The accepted resident consists of active selectors for q/v, scene bodies, manifold headers/points/counts, rod nodes/edges and rod witnesses, plus world/task/device-physics/Millard fingerprints, task seed, environment count, `supportsReset`, arena generation and owner pool/context. `finishResident` makes selectors current only after the command completes. Preserve these ownership and generation checks. The captured v1 arrays cover the active vector data and Matter sidecar but not every required identity or owner state.
3. `kConvexCaches` is persistent, read by the next convex narrowphase as the previous separating direction and by CCD/contact diagnostics, and transactionally published from candidate caches after a successful solve. It is not part of v1. `MRConvexQueryCacheGPU` is 80 bytes and pair mapping depends on the compiled eligible-pair table. Clearing it is not an exact restore. Capture the entire logical cache capacity with the same completion-boundary owner selector and pair/topology identities, not only active or nonzero entries. Determine by source audit whether any other persistent buffer is live for the precise selected fixture; unsupported live fields mean rejection, not silent zero-initialization.
4. The present fixture has one environment, two articulations, two scene bodies and one rod, uses the temporal-cone contact solver, speculative CCD, warm starting, a coupled Matter device program, and no reset masks in its handoff batch. It uses no native task program in this path. A diagnostic may initially reject tasks, multicopters, reset-enabled owners, identification or topology mutation unless their complete continuation authority is captured and verified. It must explicitly record and compare this restricted configuration, not assume it based on an empty array. Reset support includes `kResetQ`, `kResetV`, `kResetSceneBodies`, `kResetRodNodes` and `kResetRodEdges`; if reset is admitted, each applicable buffer must be captured, seeded and read back. Include all enabled feature identities and reject unsupported cases.
5. `Runtime::restore` locks Matter `commandOwnership` and rejects active/prepared transactions, restore-required and pending topology growth before validating source/device-program identity. In contrast, `Runtime::snapshot` checks ownership and copies host metadata inside a short mutex scope, then unlocks before it creates and waits for the GPU readback. Thus its own check does not reserve Matter throughout capture. A single-threaded fixture with demonstrably private owner references and no outstanding command can supply a *local diagnostic exclusion*, but that is not a reusable atomic cross-owner snapshot protocol. For a general joint snapshot, implement an owner reservation checked by all submit, restore, growth and identification mutation paths and held logically through both captures; do not simply hold a mutex across a GPU wait because completion handlers may require it. Audit lock order and timeout/lifetime release before exposing it.

## Minimal import boundary

Introduce a new versioned, opt-in diagnostic API distinct from `submitImpl` that takes a fully specified capture and creates a fresh unpublished candidate in a fresh reserved context arena. Factor the existing layout calculation into shared structural model/feature/capacity validation and submission-only validation; keep nonzero control-step and trajectory checks unchanged for normal submissions. The no-step variant may derive a requirements layout but may not enqueue dynamics, task/reset masks, device-physics callbacks, or mutate the source owner. It must require an empty output token and reject a mismatched pool, compiled-world fingerprint, topology, eligible-pair mapping, environment/capacities, device physics/program identity, solver/integrator configuration, ABI, task and reset contract, timestep/cadence, source revision and payload hashes. The full Matter archive must match its independently validated source/device identities and accepted epoch; a successful match does not itself reserve either owner.

Reserve the new slot with the existing pool/context `inFlight` and resident-owner discipline, assign a new arena generation, and construct a private, unpublished resident candidate. Upload exact active arrays into fixed designated buffers and the convex cache into its persistent buffer; explicitly initialize only proven scratch/inactive buffers. Keep buffers, command buffer, reservations and payload alive through completion. In the same command buffer issue private-to-shared copies for every imported logical byte; check command status, mapped capacities and byte equality before setting `initialized` or exposing the token. A command failure, truncated mapping, wrong byte, exception or timeout must expose no token and no accepted state. Timeout is not GPU completion: transfer resource ownership to an object that waits or receives completion before recycling the arena. Distinguish a completed GPU error from a host timeout. Do not add seed logic by modifying `finishResident` in a way that publishes before readback validation.

Matter restoration and MetalWorld candidate seeding must occur in fresh owners, never by overwriting the sole accepted resident. Until a joint reservation and publication barrier are proven, treat a candidate that restores each owner sequentially as a private diagnostic pair: it must not replace an active public pair or be described as an atomic restore. If one side fails, destroy or quarantine both candidates after outstanding work completes and show the original owner remains unchanged. A joint public operation additionally requires an exact shared epoch, comprehensive ownership exclusion across both captures and both candidate validations, and a failure-safe atomic handle swap; prove that separately.

## Predeclared reversible experiment

Before any native run, commit source and this plan, capture binary/dylib/Metal libraries/model/material/input hashes, seed/configuration and full command environment, and schedule exclusive GPU ownership. First add a fresh **source-bound** accepted-boundary capture for the existing two-stage same-resident fixture. It must capture the v1 fields plus exact full convex cache, owner selectors and required identity/configuration fields while proving local no-concurrency and completed Matter state. Preserve v1 separately and record lineage to it; do not retrofit it. Validate all hashes, lengths, layout and source-device identities, and retain the untouched uninterrupted resident and matching Matter owner as the reference.

1. CPU contract tests: reject absent/extra/truncated/oversized components, altered bytes, wrong world/device/ABI/topology/generation/pair mapping/epoch/feature configuration, unsupported reset/task, and v1. Verify the original token and all original bytes are unchanged after each rejection.
2. GPU no-step trial: seed into the fresh private owner with no dynamics. Read back every imported buffer, including convex cache and both active witnesses and manifold arrays, and require exact byte equality, identical capacities and identity. Snapshot the private Matter owner after its verified restore, compare every snapshot field against the source, and separately compare original owners before and after. Require zero physical advancement and no publication on failure. Injection controls must include command failure or aborted submission, truncated mapping, one-byte mismatch and host timeout; collect explicit outcome and resource-lifetime status for each. A fault-injection path cannot substitute a fake device witness.
3. Only if no-step gates pass, advance the uninterrupted and candidate-private pairs with identical control inputs, timestep, solver settings and unchanged physical/puncture gates. Compare full authoritative captured state at each step, including convex/rod/manifold histories and full Matter snapshot; separately compare normal/tangential impulses, work, failure status, and timing without claiming a performance study. Check deterministic replay across at least one further step and compare a forced rejected-step rollback against a no-rejection control, preserving all results. A mismatch is a failed exact-continuation gate, even if both animations or aggregate norms look plausible.
4. The threshold for claiming complete owner restore is all required domains and identities explicitly covered, device readback exact before the first step, equivalent bounded continuation and rejection/replay, no surviving leaked command, and demonstrated atomic publication across owners. Physical force and anatomical validation remain separate; the retained rod-force defect blocks those claims regardless of checkpoint outcome.

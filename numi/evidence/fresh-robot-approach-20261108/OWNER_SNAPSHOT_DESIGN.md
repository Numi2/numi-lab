# Surgical owner snapshot: scope and proposed diagnostic

This is a design based on source `56cdae698953a2846e4b2d577ed7e4f14089a88a`, not an implemented checkpoint format or evidence of exact restoration. The saved entry and contact-advance artifacts preserve Matter authority and a separately bound rigid manifold cache, but do not capture the complete MetalWorld resident owner. They must not be relabeled as full-state restores.

## Evidence and missing state

`MetalWorldResidentStateData` records the active buffer of each ping-pong pair for q, v, scene bodies, manifold headers/points/counts, rod nodes/edges and rod/tool witnesses. `MRRodToolWitnessGPU` carries pair and feature identities, rod topology generation, anchors and normal, two tangential and torsional impulses. A new resident initialization clears both rod witness buffers; neither the TSV nor `MetalWorldBatch` initial spans restore them. The TSV writes selected rod position, velocity, twist and twist rate, and cannot on its own demonstrate byte equality of the owner. The private-persistent inventory also includes convex caches and reset buffers; their use and semantic necessity must be audited before any omission. Other task, actor, sensor and controller buffers are enabled only if the selected configuration uses them. This fixture must record and enforce which features are disabled, rather than silently dropping such buffers from a general checkpoint.

A cache-enabled run validates archive ABI, content hash, saved TSV hash and state step, plus collider indices and slot generations, then submits the archive as initial manifolds. That does not read the uploaded device buffers back before the first physical step. It also does not restore rod/tool witnesses. `initializeResidentState` submits a graph that advances physics; there is no current public seed-only resident upload/readback operation. Exact full-owner/device restoration remains unverified.

## Minimal testable path

1. First establish a **same-owner uninterrupted control**: retain one live `MetalWorldContext`, `MetalWorldResidentState` and attached Matter runtime across both existing entry and contact-advance commands; wait for the entry submission to finish before `submitResident`, with unchanged physical gates and commanded motion. Do not use the TSV to reconstruct the owner between these stages. Record the accepted q/v, rigid bodies, rod nodes/edges, manifold and rod-witness state, Matter authority, physical time, controls and per-gate diagnostics. This is a live continuation, not proof of restart.
2. Build a diagnostic-only, versioned resident export/import for the actual single-environment surgical configuration. Capture each semantically live active arena and its full logical contents, including rod witnesses, manifolds and any convex cache shown to affect the next solve, plus required reset state and semantic counters. Retain active buffer selection or canonicalize it explicitly. Bind source/world/topology, ABI, device-program and material fingerprints, capacity/layout, config, control/time epoch and Matter snapshot. Any unresolved authoritative field blocks the complete label.
3. At a completed accepted transaction reserve the owner before snapshot: exclude new submissions under the resident mutex (`pending`), reserve the matching context arena (`inFlight`) with a documented lock order and lifetime, and synchronize a matching Matter snapshot in the same quiescent transaction. Do not hold a thread-owned mutex across an asynchronous completion callback. The reservation must survive command-buffer completion, errors, cancellation and host timeout; failure publishes no checkpoint and releases only after GPU use is complete. Prove cross-owner ordering and quiescence before exposing the API.
4. Export via private-to-shared Metal blit after the accepted owner boundary and hash every buffer. Import into a fresh arena without any physical step, revalidate identities, geometry/slot generations, sizes and finiteness, and blit/upload all required components. On the device, independently read back every canonicalized imported buffer before stepping and compare bytes and digests against export, including Matter authority. Do not claim exactness merely because the host input hashes or submission succeeded.
5. Replay the next same-command step from the imported state and compare to the uninterrupted control, including force/contact and all owner state, not just animation or step status. Test repeated independent restores, altered cache bytes, wrong shape generation and source fingerprint, truncated payload, wrong epoch, missing rod witness, concurrent begin and snapshot, GPU failure, rejection, and timeout. Faults must fail closed, avoid partial publication and leave the accepted state unchanged. Where a solver is not bitwise deterministic, predeclare numeric tolerances and report the limitation instead of saying byte-exact replay.

The design must preserve the puncture impulse threshold, topology and channel validation, contact residual requirements, and all existing failure gates. The existing rod reaction defect and authored tissue prevent force or biological validation even if the transaction mechanics pass.

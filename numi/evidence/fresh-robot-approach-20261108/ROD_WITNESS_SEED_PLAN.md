# Fresh rod/tool witness seed: scoped plan

This is a separate synthetic one-environment no-Matter fixture based on the existing `metal_discrete_elastic_rod_probe.mm` five-node straight rod and 2 mm tool sphere. The host-only fixture composes one free sphere and one rod, with its static floor away from contact. Its source and compiled world fingerprint must be recorded before any GPU run. Contact and force accuracy, anatomical fidelity and prior surgical history are not assumed.

A proposed opt-in rod seed extension must separately version or explicitly extend the existing rigid-only diagnostic. Validate the exact compiled world, solver/config identity, environment count, complete rod node/edge lengths, full canonical pair-major witness capacity, and every valid witness slot against its immutable compiled pair, rod collider, rigid collider and topology generation. Validate finite position, velocities, twist, all geometric witness vectors and impulses; reject invalid flags, malformed values, wrong/truncated capacity, mismatched source identity and payload checksum before acquiring an unpublished destination. Retain all inactive witness slots as bytes, not just valid ones. The identity audit already used for surgical evidence checks only impulse finiteness and is insufficient on its own.

A fresh reserved arena must initialize rod pipelines, upload accepted rod nodes/edges through the existing initial batch path, explicitly clear only inactive or recomputable scratch, then seed the active witness buffer after the standard initializer clears it. In the same no-physics command, read back and compare q/v, scene, manifold, convex caches, rod nodes, rod edges and the entire witness arena before exposing a resident. Map owner selectors to the exact seeded buffers, retain the no-reset and no-task contract, and require the owner/configuration guard on later submissions. Audit whether any other persistent rod state or witness counts are read before first recomputation; any unresolved authority prevents a completeness claim.

Prerecorded GPU gates: use two independently initialized owners and one bounded uninterrupted continuation with identical inputs; require at least one valid populated real rod/tool witness, explicit stable pair/collider/topology identity, a nonzero solved impulse, accepted step statuses and valid owners. Save full source arrays and witnesses. Exercise wrong world/config, topology generation, pair/collider identity, truncated data, nonfinite geometry/impulse, payload corruption, occupied destination and post-completion mismatch injection; require no new resident and source/occupied state unchanged. Retry exact state on a fresh context, require byte-exact no-step device readback, then compare one further seeded step with uninterrupted continuation for q/v, scene, all manifold/convex state, rod nodes/edges, full witnesses and status. Preserve all raw data and failures; no geometry tuning after a failed trial without a separately preregistered fixture.

The post-completion injected mismatch is not a genuine Metal command-buffer failure. No real device fault, timeout, multi-owner concurrency, cross-owner atomicity, physical force accuracy or longer replay is claimed by this plan. In particular, a rod/tool-only synthetic success cannot restore Matter or establish a complete surgical checkpoint. The surgical capsule and the distinct historical v3 step-1870 checkpoint remain non-restorable.

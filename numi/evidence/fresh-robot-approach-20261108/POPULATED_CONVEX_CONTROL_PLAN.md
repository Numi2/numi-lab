# Independent convex population control: pre-run plan

The separate source-pinned synthetic diagnostic uses the existing contact probe cooked-convex/cylinder geometry, zero gravity, one environment, speculative CCD and one small initial closing velocity. It initializes two independent owners from identical authored initial inputs; each performs one resident step and one same-resident continuation with identical controls. It is not a new surgical trajectory, a seeded capsule, or a historical checkpoint.

Before execution, commit source, pin binary, library and shader hashes, and request an exclusive GPU slot. Require a real compiled convex pair, successful submissions, accepted steps and valid residents, nonzero accepted contact count, nonzero bytes in the full captured convex cache, and bitwise equality of cache, q and v across the two independently constructed owners at both steps. Save all raw cache/q/v data, submitted/wait status, inputs and timings even if a gate fails. Independently rehash and compare the saved data. A mismatch or zero cache fails; do not tune scene conditions in place after observing results. Any later correction is a separate preregistered trial. Do not claim restart, import, rollback, atomic coupled state or full physical validation from two independent owners.

# Native local-frame rod held-out data comparison

Use the same 128-node, two-offset, 41-step effective 8-0 nylon axial fixture and unchanged force, state and work gates as the corrected v3-002 run. Record each actual native support reaction at its prescribed extension, without smoothing or time alignment. Compare linearly interpolated native forces at all unchanged 2–4 mm extension samples from held-out specimens 8-0NS2–5 of the publisher's SHA-pinned S4 workbook. Require the same predeclared relative absolute-error limit of 10% per specimen used for the earlier physical-data screen. Require successful native publication on all steps and all existing FP64 and energy/work gates, and evaluate both 0 m and 16 m offsets separately. Refuse extrapolation or changed workbook bytes. Retain both results and failures.

The effective axial relation is fitted to 8-0NS1 alone. Its rest length encodes the local fitted intercept and is not a measured unstressed material length; radius and density are fixture assumptions. This is a narrow measured axial comparison at one experimental rate and material setting. It does not qualify bending, twist, tissue, collision, rate dependence, resident MetalWorld, restored owner state, or surgery.

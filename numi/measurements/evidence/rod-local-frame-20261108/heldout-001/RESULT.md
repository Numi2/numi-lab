# Native 8-0 nylon axial held-out comparison

The predeclared fixture ran 41 accepted 0.1-second steps, covering 2–4 mm extension at 0.5 mm/s (the study's 30 mm/min crosshead speed). The candidate's actual moving-support reactions were linearly interpolated at each unchanged held-out measurement in the SHA-pinned publisher workbook. All four specimens passed the previously fixed 10% relative absolute force-error gate in both the 0 m and 16 m offset environments:

| Specimen | Measured samples | Error at both offsets |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 8-0NS2 | 400 | 3.0549% |
| 8-0NS3 | 400 | 3.0184% |
| 8-0NS4 | 401 | 1.7600% |
| 8-0NS5 | 400 | 2.9605% |

The independent FP64 discrete-state/force checks and the support-work versus elastic-plus-kinetic energy check also passed. The starting effective state is constructed from a fit to 8-0NS1, including its nonzero intercept via a shifted effective rest length. These data are from one reported material, batch, gauge and rate; they include the apparatus response. This establishes a narrow held-out axial agreement for the standalone experimental path, not a measured unstressed length, a transferable material law, owner save/restore, a MetalWorld resident path, twist, bending, friction, tissue or surgery.

The 41-point native trace, workbook identity, binary and source hashes, full raw per-specimen errors and prior instrument failure are retained. Binaries and the original workbook remain on the host; independently rebuilding the linked owner and shader from a clean source checkout remains open.

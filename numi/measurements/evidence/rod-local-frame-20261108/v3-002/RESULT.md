# Local-frame rod, corrected work diagnostic

The corrected accumulator uses the trapezoidal average of the forces applied by the moving support. This reruns the unchanged 41-step, two-environment fixture and preregistered gates after retaining the incorrect diagnostic and its failure in v3-001. The native standalone owner published all 41 steps at both 0 m and 16 m offsets. An independent dense FP64 solve started from each actual input state. Maximum position error was 2.366e-7 m, velocity error 2.385e-6 m/s, and support-force error 1.368e-5 N. Work agreed with elastic-plus-kinetic energy change to 1.077e-5 relative for this fixture.

The nine-case stress and six-step contract tests, run against the current helper before this single instrumentation correction, passed in v3-001. Its work result failed because of the measurement bug; its logs remain unchanged. The no-publication CPU stub also passed. These results cover a standalone, straight, frictionless, contact-free rod and a host-side local-frame adapter only. They do not qualify a resident MetalWorld path, collisions, arbitrary rod geometry, surgery, long-term stability, or material realism. The native binaries remain on the host; the hashes and source are retained here, and an independent clean rebuild has not been demonstrated.

# Publication 061: exact per-bone export, route witnesses, and material-J rejection

This compact, hash-pinned evidence package covers the 039 exact emitted-bank export, the 041 route-entry construction constraints, and the independent 062 material-specific J audit. It is separate from the already published joint-progress-publication-061.

The 039 exporter ran an intentional source-only preflight and stopped before physical simulation. It emitted 469,136 Float32 triangles across 185 typed bone banks, 7,233 candidate nodes and 157 body transforms. The exact-frame review verified all 4,222,224 triangle-coordinate components, the 185-bank inventory, the 1,338,105 node-bank domain (2,109 queried rows and 1,335,996 AABB-proven outside), and one exact rational-sign row-946 segment hit on triangle 1381 at t=0.30025836149950424. This is an export/frame result and one segment witness; it is not a complete skin-clearance, anatomy, or physical acceptance gate.

The 139 MB triangle CSV is stored as deterministic gzip. `verify_publication_061.py` decompresses it in memory and checks the original CSV SHA-256. The 24,135,771-byte registered-skin source CSV is not duplicated; its path and hash are listed as an external, non-archived input. Inputs already present in publication 060 are referenced by Git path, commit and blob hash.

The first 039 launch is retained as failed: the checked runner rejected the requested initial skin-bone witness CSV because it was missing. Run 002 is the valid source-only export. Its as-run report is preserved unchanged. The as-run field named `source_app_sha256` contains the compiled binary digest (`dc463f…`); a companion correction record and corrected report identify the app source digest (`72b072…`) and binary digest separately. This provenance correction does not change the execution or measurements.

The 041 source route analysis records 241 exact proper-interior segment/triangle hits across 121 segment-bank pairs and 121 columns with hits. Its adapter independently confirms 121 local construction planes expanded into 125 endpoint constraints, with no uncertain rows and a 53.709 µm minimum exterior start distance. The constraints remain violated in the unchanged candidate (125 positive margin violations, maximum 9.763 mm); they are construction rows, not a successful fit. They do not certify layer surface or tetrahedron clearance, and do not admit a native or physical state.

A separate bounded baseline-O diagnostic adds 12 nearest-exit construction planes for O-layer points inside the per-bone bank mesh. The plane-frame residual is 3.48e-18 m. These are local nearest-boundary directions, not first-entry route witnesses; they do not establish network or surface clearance. Its two larger source inputs are path/hash references only.

The 062 audit uses candidate 037-002 and the owner descriptor per-material bounds 0.35 <= J <= 2.5. Its independent reference-relative recomputation confirms 162 violations: skin has 26 below and 3 above; fat has 92 below and 41 above. Baseline has zero violations. Candidate 037-002 passed the existing positive geometry floor (zero nonpositive cells and zero below q=1e-5, minimum q=1.2160859598e-5) but 24 cells are below the stricter q=1.25e-5 construction target. That geometry result does not override the material-J failure. Candidate 037-002 remains rejected and unadmitted.

These are offline/source diagnostics. They do not establish native material response, contact, anatomy, physiological thickness, clinical validity, complete tissue clearance, or physical qualification. No candidate is activated by this publication.

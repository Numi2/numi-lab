# D37 bone-clearance feasibility and fit evidence

This package collects bounded 033–038 source-preparation and fit work. It preserves negative results and separates source geometry checks from native physical qualification. No candidate is activated or admitted by this package.

The 033 projection diagnostics reject both naive transfers: independent node projection produces 24 nonpositive cells; whole-column translation produces 33 nonpositive cells and misses two witness-plane margins. The 034 joint fit satisfies its fixed witness planes and layer order, but fails the emitted Float32 cell-quality floor in 17 cells (minimum normalized quality -0.03493494). It is not an admissible transfer.

The 035 owner capture checked 7,233 O/I/D nodes against all 185 closed bone banks (1,338,105 node-bank pairs). It recorded 139 inside pairs/unique nodes (O=12, I=7, D=120), with zero ambiguous or invalid classifications. The captured run intentionally stopped after source diagnostics; this is not a native physics or all-geometry acceptance result. The 029 nearest-body union count of 138 was not a complete per-bone result; 035 adds one inside pair at node 8704, body 120, bone 121.

The 036 affine replay is limited to the exact raw-Float32 affine and closest-point roundtrip. The independent review reports a maximum closest-world error of 1.153e-16 m and maximum effective-offset spread of 5.019e-17 m. The companion covector receipt is distinct from the escape-vector transform; its maximum vector-versus-covector direction difference is 1.783e-7 in the standalone covector report (the independent affine review reports 2.462e-7 under its separate comparison). These directions must not be conflated. Neither receipt establishes physical clearance.

The 037 fit uses all 139 per-bone inside witnesses and expands quality constraints over all 7,060 affected cells. The largest component hit its solver time slice, so the fit remains partial and is not an admitted final candidate.

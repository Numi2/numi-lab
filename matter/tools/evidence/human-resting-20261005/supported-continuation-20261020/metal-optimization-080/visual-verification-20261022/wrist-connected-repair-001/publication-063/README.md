# Publication 063 — measured shared-column and local-depth trials

This package records offline construction trials and failure evidence. No candidate is admitted, no scene was activated, and no native physical qualification is claimed.

**Route provenance and scope.** The route/O12 rows in the 040 fits and 008/009 depth trials are residuals to construction planes derived from proposal 037002. The 041 producer asserts that its query nodes equal that candidate before generating those planes. Those residuals are not baseline exact-clearance measurements. A separate 010 audit used registered baseline 035 O/I/D nodes and exact segment-versus-bank triangle tests: 192 hit segment/bank pairs, 189 columns, 191 column/bank groups, 256 proper hits and zero ambiguous candidates. The 008/009 route residuals were not recomputed as exact post-candidate route gates. See the provenance sidecar and included reports.

The later 013/014 experiment uses the true baseline 010 route planes. Candidate 013 fixed the outer layer and shortened local layer-depth directions, but left two nonpositive tets (minimum q -0.01086) and 12 O12 residuals. The bounded 014 transition changed five columns over 167 cells in four iterations (0.0312 s), kept all outer nodes fixed, and reached zero nonpositive tets and zero q below 1e-5 (minimum q 1.23256e-5), with zero residual to the baseline route planes. Its exact 015 O/I/D route requery reduced segment/bank hits from the baseline 192 to 8 (8 proper hits, 8 columns, zero ambiguity). This is an actual improvement for that narrow route-segment gate; 8 intersections remain, and 12 O12 residuals remain. Satisfying endpoint planes did not eliminate all exact segment hits. Reference strain, mass conservation/remapping, detailed transfer, full skin/FEM clearance, tissue response, and native behavior remain unqualified.

Its saved Float32 report has minimum normalized tet quality -0.02033, two nonpositive tets, minimum deformation Jacobian -0.6629, with 13 J values below 0.35 and 18 above 2.5. Its residual to the 037-derived route/O12 construction planes had seven violations and a minimum signed gap -0.219 mm. The as-run regional J summary mixed millimetres and metres; corrected-regional-audit.json is a derived post-solve SI recomputation; the as-run report remains unchanged. Skin/fat floor minima were 0.5002/0.5051 mm. The separate regions-2–5 partial had no inverted tets and minimum q 2.60e-5, but retained 121 route-plane and 12 O12 residual violations; regions 0–1 were restored to the pass-only start. It is incomplete and unadmitted.

The corrected 002 run (9.40 s) was rejected: four nonpositive tets, minimum q -0.00437 (six below 1e-5), minimum J -3.884 and maximum J 9.845 (10 below 0.35, 16 above 2.5), three Piola values above 5 MPa, one energy density above 5 MJ/m3, maximum |P| 39.05 MPa, and 103 residual violations to the 037-derived route/O12 planes with minimum signed gap -3.710 mm. The 008/009 local depth construction was diagnostic. The 008 plane-only solve was infeasible on six columns; its emitted geometry had one nonpositive tet, one q below 1e-5 (minimum q -0.01555), 10 route-plane residual violations and 12 O12 residual violations. The 009 five-variable, five-iteration transition repaired its local tet-quality checks (zero nonpositive; minimum q 1.247e-5, with all O positions fixed), but retained 10 route-plane and 12 O12 residual violations. These counts refer to the 037-derived plane set, not baseline clearance. The 011 route requery is preserved as a failed attempt because it emitted endpoint planes for endpoints it had not queried; the corrected 012 report has zero inferred planes and zero uncertainty. A separate escape-side diagnostic remains unresolved.

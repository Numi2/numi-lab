# Native integrated resting Human increment

`numi human-resting` routes to the Human asset/launch owner and the existing
`numi-human-native` Metal executable. The scene joins the existing articulated
body, MyoSim/tendon, Matter CVSim21, respiratory and NumiBrain accepted states.
The native viewer has seven anatomical layers, five cameras, same-state
physiological measurements and an unretimed AVFoundation recording. Skin
contact witnesses are computed on the GPU from all source skin weights; the
existing stand solver remains the sole contact-force owner.

The retained `native-dt2-30s-001` Mac mini diagnostic accepted 15,000 steps at
2 ms, completing 30.0000014 simulated seconds in 300.51213125 native wall
seconds (0.09983 real-time factor). The transaction probe rejected a physical
step without advancing body, circulation, respiratory or Brain history, then
matched uninterrupted replay. The 51 terminal source equality residuals were
at most 4.35e-10. Three independent joint coordinates were slightly outside
the existing numerical limit band; the separate constraint repair is being
verified. See `support-and-timestep-20261005/README.md` for exact receipts,
profiling, the failed 4 ms admission and sensitivity limitations.

All builds, numerical calculations, asset preparation and runtime checks in
these increments used SSH Mac mini, Apple M4 Pro, macOS 26.6. The Air was used
for source editing, version control and reviewing transferred images. The
Mini was at its login window: the real native renderer and AppKit measurement
panel were recorded, but interactive desktop presentation is not verified.

This increment is working integrated software, **not the requested final
acceptance**. Exact anatomy audits still find respiratory and cardiac phase
intersections; their repairs are underway. The full native body has not yet
completed five simulated minutes. The older 300-second physiology-only run
does not satisfy that requirement. Real-time performance is not achieved.
The final paired respiratory intervention, long native recording, complete
cycle anatomy audit and recovery comparison remain required. Passive atlas
structures do not acquire constitutive tissue or complete organ physiology
by being displayed. No clinical or measured-individual claim is made.

# Contact iteration convergence evidence

The completed 20 s comparison supports an iteration-sensitive contribution to supported-body motion: reducing 64 to 32 iterations increased post-initialization COM displacement from 0.122 to 0.361 mm and pre-step contact-J tangential-speed p95 from 25.4 to 247.3 micrometres/s. The examined physiological fields were unchanged. Production remains at 64 iterations.

This is a predeclared numerical diagnostic, not a registered Numi Science study or a new performance optimization. The 128-iteration attempt was rejected by the owner before native launch; its declaration and failure log are retained in 882/. The complete original analysis, exact inputs and reproducer are preserved under analysis/; publication-inputs.json binds the copies to their retained Mac mini evidence.

These runs use the historical skin-004 candidate. Its individual owner binding replacements were subsequently rejected as incompatible with shared-atlas skinning, so this evidence does not qualify anatomy or transfer clearance to the canonical-binding replacement. The observed contact sensitivity also does not uniquely explain the 2.8 mm COM drift in the five-minute pair: the measured slip uses the pre-step contact Jacobian, and force/energy closure is incomplete.

The source observer was published as 5406e029241ff2a4528b8f040493c658ae075196; its executable and GPU kernels are identified in the original run metadata. This commit adds only retained numerical evidence. It changes no simulation state, contact configuration, assets, or native binary.

# Numi Human P183: hand topology and failed connected-fit evidence

This is static construction evidence, not an admitted human scene, a functional skin/fat model, or a physical simulation. The baseline is P182, commit 005aa7f0c7023dea6bd055d4de3d1c6284a3a2eb, 653 comparable parent-face/bone-triangle pairs and 985 refined child-triangle pairs. Its candidate SHA-256 is 7f2cb323550b273ae772ffc92d1dd714ab047d7f973af7f60793367fe43222af.

Ten same-immediate-parent 2-to-2 flips retain all coordinates, 237 typed anchors, singleton immediate-parent ancestry and the earlier 169 flip-history entries. Exact independent checks across all 185 banks and the whole skin report 975 child-triangle pairs, 653 parent identities (same set), zero self intersections and 125 sampled inside node/bank pairs (unchanged). The 975/985 child counts reflect a changed triangulation and do not imply ten anatomical intersections were cleared. The skin still has 45 boundary edges; sampled node containment is not a closed-solid certificate. Candidate SHA-256 308007c045ca2bda7d42a6267aa6558cac0f74affbd42e015e7f13e537db0083.

The topology selector was adapted only after independently verifying the new face-parent CSR consists of singleton records exactly equal to each immediate parent. Both before and after every same-parent proposal it checks this invariant; the earlier flip-history arrays were explicitly carried into the derived artifact. Complete Float32 area, anatomical ancestry and full surface/bank receipts are included. These tests do not qualify volume cells, mass, tissue mechanics, moving poses, runtime behavior or physical admission.

Fifty-five interior vertices in the three largest banks had outward proposal endpoints independently checked outside all 185 banks. The batch fits did not meet full geometric gates. A 109/114 fit initially introduced 91 skin self intersections; successive fits with accumulated exact self witnesses produced 19, 9, 7 and 12. None was promoted. The 59/95 triangle-normal remesh increased refined crossings and its bone/source normal directions conflicted. The 115/118 remesh preserved its total pair count but introduced 17 self intersections; its earlier self report is invalid because of incorrect vertex identity handling and the corrected report supersedes it. The 74/104 multi-Steiner patch failed against face-interior/bone witnesses despite exterior sample vertices. Its exact 25-segment intersection contour forms a closed loop, but the corrected planar patch covers 0.5234636035 versus the fixed boundary area 0.5; no valid 3D candidate was emitted. Preserve these failures and do not use their candidate files as accepted skin.

The entrypoints are diagnostic snapshots pinned to the retained SSH Numi owner tools and source paths. Reproduction requires the prior evidence and native owners; this archive does not present these scripts as a general artifact format or separate simulator. No visual check, integrated native step, or clinical claim is implied.

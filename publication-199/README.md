# Human hand surface and skin/fat layer diagnostic

This is construction evidence from SSH Numi, not an admitted physical skin or native scene. It follows P198, archive commit 86d263a3f14dc005fae0340f39f23066198a8832, which remains available with failed attempts. The P198 reference-pose candidate 2e6b219fb6092ac9edf7b9efa8ee18a388c9ef2ed9a5ebd4b1bbedfd24080b3e was later found to introduce two fat authored-prism orientation failure identities, and one new skin and one new fat Float32 conditioning failure identity, despite unchanged aggregate totals. It is not selected for physical use.

Retained construction candidate 8d3faeb6f5218cad8a5c1dae033f59c651cabedf5bffdba982a3f9d09e813a11 is based on P182 candidate 7f2cb323550b273ae772ffc92d1dd714ab047d7f973af7f60793367fe43222af. Exact reference-pose checks cover 185 typed banks and the complete skin surface. Mapped parent/bone triangle identities are 653 to 651 (three added, five removed); raw refined triangle pairs are 985 to 983 (four added, six removed). The skin self count is zero and 125 sampled inside node-bank pairs remain 125; those are samples, not a whole-solid certificate. All eleven banks still have crossings, including all three highest-count banks.

The construction fit reuses existing point-depth, self-pair and area owners and adds linearized skin/fat offset-prism non-regression rows to its private CPU construction QP. Six finite-difference checks test the layer-volume gradients and bounds; six parity cases were also checked. It is not a new runtime or GPU simulator. Candidate 008 has one additional skin Float32 conditioning failure; a half-step avoids that failure but maps 653 rather than 651 parent pairs, so neither replaces candidate 007.

The 541 transferred-layer diagnostic compares exact cell failure identities, not only counts. Retained 007 adds no authored-prism, nonpositive-Float32 or conditioning failure identities in either layer; it removes fat authored-prism failures 26958 and 42129 and fat conditioning failure 80876. Nevertheless, its inherited offset-prism construction still has 7,613 skin and 8,530 fat authored-orientation failures, and two nonpositive Float32 tetrahedra per layer. This diagnostic geometry is not the native scene. Its source lacks conservative donor cell-volume and mass ancestry. No global layer collision, mass validation, moving-pose assessment, physical steps or native admission were run. The shell has 45 unresolved boundary edges. The evidence is not clinical validation.

Work used SSH Numi, Apple M6, macOS 27.0 build 26A425 arm64; source owner checkout was detached at 868486402da2ff0a208bf60c267df0928e2f91e0 with unrelated dirty changes. Exact input bank hashes are in P198 input-bank-pins.json. Entry points use pinned owner paths and do not form a standalone simulator.

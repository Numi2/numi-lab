# Evidence entry point

Start with `HANDOFF_SCENE.md` and the delivered `evidence/assessment.json` and `evidence/video_record.json`.

The delivered scene has eight simulated seconds, zero coins, a receiver approaching from above, a gentle 0.4 N per-jaw grip, donor release and retreat, and receiver-only support. The donor's approved physical prefix through step 1200 is inherited exactly; `capture` and `finish` are the new native two-arm integration segments. Current source and input identities are in `capture_launch_identity_sha256.txt`, `direct_launch_identity_sha256.txt` and `direct_runtime_binding.json`.

The archive retains earlier alternatives for diagnosis. `translation_only_capture`, `previous_side_capture`, `original_short_pad_geometry`, `rejected_*`, older direct/spread planning files, and their launch manifests are historical evidence. Some have energy failures or housing crossings. They are not components of the delivered physical trajectory. Runtime helper `plan_upright_handoff.py` defines the delivered kinematic path; older planners are preserved only in the complete archive.

Mesh clearance audits cover all saved states. Finite fingertip contacts provide the forces; arm housings have no coupled dynamic collider, and there is no continuous whole-robot collision qualification. Read all material and finite-contact limitations in the scene document. The video is a presentation of native solver states, not a substitute for physical material measurements.

The original backtrack checker had coordinate-frame and identity bugs; its raw report is preserved as pre-fix evidence, not a valid conclusion. Backtracking with per-face 95% area/angle and 105% aspect limits did not yield a strict net identity reduction with no new contacts/self intersections; rejected. These construction limits are not physical acceptance.

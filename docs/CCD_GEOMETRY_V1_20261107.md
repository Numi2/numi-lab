This is a research-only solver and the checkpoint-order repair, not an enabled CCD experiment or a production qualification. The reference model is a 1 kg free body, diagonal inertia 0.1 kg m², gravity and restitution zero, initially identity orientation, plane penetration 0.1 mm or 50 mm clearance, 10 ms step and two velocity sweeps. Normal initial speed is -1, 0 or 1 m/s; friction cases add 1 m/s tangent speed. Shapes are sphere, vertical capsule and axis-aligned box; each uses coefficient 0 or 0.5. There are 24 requested cases, including force-free clearance controls; both experimental contact and CCD options remain off. This is discrete contact, not swept-contact qualification.

Twenty-two cases accepted and two rejected with status 11 and restored the observed q, v and scene input: the frictionless box impact at -1 m/s had normal residual 0.01029847655 m/s against a 0.0101 m/s gate; the frictional box sliding at zero normal speed had 0.001476235921 m/s against 0.0001 m/s. Deterministic replay held for the tested outputs. This is not an acceptable completion rate for ordinary impacts; no certificate relaxation or promotion is justified. Sphere and capsule frictionless impacts gave 1 N s impulse and -0.5 J kinetic work, consistent with the isolated 1 kg, inelastic normal-impact reference.

The v1 angular impulse check uses the reported midpoint contact location, and its approximate 2.5e-5 N m s mismatch in some accepted friction cases is NOT a verified physics failure: the solver Jacobian has separate body-local witnesses. A corrected check must use the actual dynamic-body witness at the same pre-solve orientation. The kinetic energy uses the explicit model mass and inertia, and the work is net kinetic-energy change in this gravity-free case; it is not a complete energy assessment for other models.

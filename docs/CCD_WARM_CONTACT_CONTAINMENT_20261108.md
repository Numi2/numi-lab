# Warm-start normal response containment

Research candidate, based on clean main `bb58f524`. This is a fail-closed publication check for one wave-solver path, not a convergent response correction or a general contact certificate. It remains unpromoted pending independent review and ordinary-motion qualification. Neither experimental CCD nor augmented-Lagrangian contact is enabled.

Each non-distributed wave row records whether its projected cached normal impulse exceeds the existing cone epsilon. After the configured solve, such rows are checked for finite impulse, cap and equation values, impulse bounds, and normal complementarity using `1e-4 m/s + 0.01 * max(abs(preSolveVelocity), abs(targetVelocity))`. Upper-capped rows use the appropriate reversed complementarity sign. A failure reports `MR_STEP_DID_NOT_CONVERGE` (11) and routes through the existing transaction rollback. The historical threshold is an interim dimensional publication gate, not a force-accuracy guarantee. Cold rows, distributed contact, tangential maximum dissipation, geometric clearance, and rows newly activated by coupled contacts are not covered by this narrow check. No claim is made that these omitted cases are safe.

The clean-main control accepts a repeated zero-gap box impact with step-2 normal impulse 0.560104 N s and residual 0.56243 m/s. The candidate accepts the first step and rejects every attempted second step at both zero initial gap and 0.1 mm initial overlap. Duplicate resident runs replay identically, and each rejected attempt restores q, v, scene and full populated manifold counts, headers and points byte-for-byte. Rejection means the box cannot progress through the fixture; the repair of useful impact response remains open.

The 10-step 1.5 mm-gap discrete sliding near miss is accepted without impulse. Two further 10-step controls with gravity and populated cache are accepted: a stationary supported box and a box initially sliding at 0.2 m/s. A 1 kg body receives approximately 0.0981 N s per step, as expected for gravity of 9.81 m/s² and h=0.01 s, with linear momentum errors below 2e-6 N s. The sliding residual peaks at 0.000632 m/s and passes the stated scale-dependent gate. This is not a sliding-force accuracy certification. Both candidate and clean main accept 25/25 one-step geometry cases, 36/36 friction-case solver statuses and 48/48 moving-contact solver statuses; prior independent local friction and zero-normal-load physical failures in clean main remain. Single-step counts do not establish reliable long-run simulation.

The independent standalone test `metalrobo.ccd.warm_contact_containment` verifies the rejection, repeated rollback, replay, near miss and both sustained controls; `metalrobo.ccd.kinematic_rollback` also passed. The latter still has the documented reset-specific inactive-cache byte differences despite live-state and continuation agreement; do not interpret it as reset full-buffer equality.

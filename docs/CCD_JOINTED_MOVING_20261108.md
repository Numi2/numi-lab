# Bounded jointed and moving-contact checks

The shader from CCD_BOX_PAIR was tested with a two-link free-floating articulation whose child translates vertically on a free prismatic joint, and a plane contact applied to the child. The parent has mass 1 kg, child 0.5 kg, gravity, restitution and friction are zero, initial rotation is identity, clearance is -0.1 mm, step 10 ms and two velocity sweeps. The CCD mode is disabled. For an initial child speed -1 m/s, the child velocity is stopped by 0.5 N s while the independent parent speed is unchanged; the kinetic work reference is -0.25 J. The reference follows directly from the independent child mass and the unconstrained joint. Four runs cover parent/joint speeds (-1,0), (-0.25,-0.75), (0,0) and (1,0) m/s.

All four accepted and matched impulse, parent and child velocity, and kinetic-work references to the printed precision; rest and separating cases had zero impulse and work. Outputs replayed identically. The fixture is centered, frictionless, one-contact and one-joint; it is not general articulated, off-axis or multiple-contact validation. The work is net kinetic change for this isolated gravity-free fixture, not a general energy audit.

The 48-case moving-kinematic-body matrix completed 47/48. Its established 33-contact frictional impact at one sweep rejected status 11 with normal residual 0.0208123 m/s and retained input state, empty rejected cache, and replay. At two sweeps, frictionless 33/257-contact impacts gave about 1 N s impulse and -0.25 J kinetic work; the 33-contact frictional impact gave 0.999567 N s normal, 0.499784 N s tangent impulse and -0.6249998741 J, while 257 contacts gave 0.9999999944 N s normal, 0.4999999976 N s tangent and -0.6249999879 J. These are moving-surface fixture references, not material or broad friction qualification. All output replay comparisons passed; exact populated-cache rejection restoration was not tested here.

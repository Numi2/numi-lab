# Mixed rod–articulation wave containment

Research candidate only, based on clean main `bb58f524`. It adds a final-state normal-complementarity check only for wave-solver islands marked as containing both rod and articulated nodes. A row's tolerance is `1e-4 m/s + 0.01 * max(abs(pre-solve normal speed), abs(target normal speed))`. The check includes finite values, impulse bounds and lower/upper-bound complementarity. A failed row reports `MR_STEP_DID_NOT_CONVERGE`; a nonfinite row reports `MR_STEP_NONFINITE_RESULT`. This is interim containment, not a convergent response repair or a general contact certificate. Friction accuracy, swept trajectory validity and other solver paths are not certified.

In the three retained overlapping mixed fixtures, clean main accepted normal residuals 0.45334882, 0.11573270 and 0.01764059 m/s. The candidate rejected all three with status 11 and respective tolerances 0.0101, 0.0002 and 0.0101 m/s; all restored and replayed the captured one-shot q/v, scene, rod nodes and rod edges. On a zero-gap fixture, stationary and separating mixed contact were accepted and replayed; an approaching case was rejected. Thus the check does not reject every mixed island, but it still prevents ordinary useful approaching contact and must not be promoted as a completed solution. The existing one-step geometry matrix was accepted/replayed/restored 25/25; the independent kinematic rollback probe passed its populated-cache and continuation checks. Its previously recorded reset-specific inactive-byte mismatch remains.

The corrected resident experiment first accepted a stationary zero-gap mixed scene and populated one contact manifold. A kinematic ground target then drove the actual ground row at -1 m/s. Clean main accepted the resulting step at normal residual 0.449047 m/s. The candidate rejected two repeated attempts with status 11; q, v, scene state, rod nodes, rod edges, and the captured manifold counts, headers and points matched the pre-attempt bytes, and the neutral continuation matched an independently initialized resident. This qualifies the captured fields and subsequent continuation for this fixture; it does not establish every hidden GPU cache or general reset semantics. Rejected trial impulses are diagnostics, not accepted dynamics.

For failures from this new guard, the existing wave status payload uses residual x for the maximum measured normal violation and residual y for the maximum failed-row tolerance; these maxima need not belong to the same row; residual y must not be interpreted as the measured residual.

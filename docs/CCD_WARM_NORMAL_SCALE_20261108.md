# Bounded warm-start normal-ray prototype

Research only, based on the standalone warm-row containment `40e8eda6`. The prototype is not promoted. For non-rod wave islands of at most four constraints with positive isotropic friction and no impulse cap, it computes the pre-warm normal-only quadratic along the cached normal-impulse vector, and scales every cached impulse component by the same factor in [0,1]. This retains the cached ray and friction cone while selecting an initial iterate; the existing final warm-row complementarity certificate remains unchanged. The normal-only model omits tangential coupling and is not itself a physical contact solution.

The pinned matched resident comparison shows a failure to meet the response gate: after the common two-sweep first impact at zero gap, the prototype computes 0.001541 N s on step 2, compared with 0.560104 N s for containment and clean main, but its 0.000464 m/s final normal residual exceeds the certificate and the step is rejected. At 0.1 mm initial overlap the step is also rejected, with 0.002528 m/s residual. Both failed states restore populated manifold bytes, q, v and scene, and repeat deterministically. Rejected trial impulse or work is diagnostic only. The cold zero-gap control has comparable candidate impulse and residual but has no warm-row gate; it does not establish force accuracy.

The 10-step discrete near miss remains accepted and force-free; the two 10-step gravity-supported resting and initially sliding cases are accepted, matching the containment outputs. These narrow controls do not establish general coupled friction, articulated, anisotropic, capped, rod or distributed behavior, or demonstrate an end-to-end speedup. No thresholds were relaxed and production iteration counts remain unchanged.

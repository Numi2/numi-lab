It validates every proposed local normal and friction residual before use, and retains the post-application certificate and fallback. This is neither general contact qualification nor a performance claim. The previous cold candidate's four local friction failures are retained in `CCD_COLD_CERTIFIED_20261108.md` and its associated evidence.

# Distributed contact response research (held)

The candidates in this branch are retained research, not a production repair. The initial uniform 1/N relaxation solved aligned contacts but rejected weakly coupled 257-contact impacts at the production sweep budget. The following coupling-aware normal relaxation improved those fixtures but generated premature normal impulse during tangential motion. A normal-first, conditional tangent variant then passed its 72-case diagnostic matrix, including aligned and mixed near-orthogonal contacts, frictionless and frictional cases, at 1, 2, 3, and 16 sweeps. That matrix did not establish rollback, because its runs succeeded, nor does it establish arbitrary cone or coupled-island correctness.

The revised variant still rejects the original noncoincident 257-contact owner-shaped impact: its normal residual is about 0.358 m/s at two sweeps. FP64 reconstruction of the captured row operator matches the GPU residual, and identifies an exact solution concentrated on the x=0 contact. A clean 304fd4be baseline separately accepts an incorrect 33-contact response and oscillatory 257-contact response. Full raw evidence, failed candidates, fixture sources, patches and binary hashes are in [ccd-research-evidence-20261107.tar.gz](evidence/ccd-research-evidence-20261107.tar.gz), SHA-256 66071749f28860da9b8b2e6005eb17ea6c495420068b3f899120f4361affc594. Metal libraries and executable binaries remain host-only. The alternative experimental CCD and AL options remain disabled. No performance claim or promotion is warranted.

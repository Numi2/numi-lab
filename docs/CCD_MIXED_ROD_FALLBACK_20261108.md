This separate, unpromoted candidate restores the prior per-endpoint branch in rod-containing islands; the coupled predictor remains limited to islands without rods. The earlier shader and its outcomes remain unchanged in CCD_FRICTION_PREDICTIVE and CCD_PREDICTIVE_MOVING_RESTORE.

The corrected shader retained the fixed 36/36 friction-gate passes, 71/72 coupled, 47/48 moving and 4/4 prismatic-link outcomes; the same one-sweep frictional impact rejects in the latter two matrices. Those fixtures have no rod contacts and cannot qualify this corrected branch. The corrected shader's separate mixed fixture combines a 1 kg free sphere touching a static plane and a three-node rod touching the sphere, coefficient 0.5, no gravity, 1 ms step and two sweeps. It reports an island containing both articulation and rod nodes, with one rigid and two rod contacts. This is a genuine failure to complete the mixed fixture under the tested budget, not successful rod qualification. It does not identify whether the residual originates in pre-existing behavior or the new candidate.

# Composed warm-block and two-phase friction research

Unpromoted research only. This branch composes the earlier small-island two-phase friction update with the bounded warm-start sticking/sliding initializer. The preconditioner padding stores the positive regularized normal denominator magnitude and the cached-normal marker in its sign; all predictor uses take the magnitude. The supported island domain and existing local checks remain as documented in the parent experiments. No general solver qualification is implied.

All 50 warm fixture steps across two impacts, the discrete near miss, stationary support and initially sliding support are accepted and replay exactly. The previously declared independent per-row sliding maximum-dissipation gate has zero warm failures. The state-matched FP64 zero-gap impact step-2 reference gives normal impulse 0.00109048206 N s and work −0.00027282381 J; the candidate gives 0.00109048496 N s and −0.00027282435 J. The later state-matched sticking reference work differs by approximately 4e−10 J.

The cold matrix also completes but fails the same independent local friction gate on all four rows of sliding step 4. The largest discrepancy is 3.19197e−5 N s against 2.23844e−5 N s allowed, at final slip speed 0.00371 m/s. Thus completion, warm results and global momentum do not establish cold physical accuracy. Anisotropic and capped fallback, genuinely coupled/articulated cases, and broad rollback remain unqualified; no promotion is warranted.

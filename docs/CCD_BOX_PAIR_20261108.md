# Bounded two-row frictionless research candidate

This unpromoted experiment extends the existing non-distributed frictionless priority solve with a constrained two-row quadratic update. It retains eight pivots per configured sweep, the original certificate, the single-row fallback for capped normal impulses, boundary candidates for near-singular pairs, and skips pairs whose symmetric quadratic is materially indefinite. It does not enable either experimental CCD or contact law. The separate normal-first frictional diagnostic remains part of the candidate and its anisotropic limitations remain open. No convergence guarantee is established for all singular, nearly singular or nonsymmetric operators.

The unchanged discrete geometry matrix passed 24/24, with deterministic replay and observed q/v/scene restoration in all cases. The formerly rejected frictionless four-corner impact accepted at two sweeps with normal impulse 1.000000032 N s, corner impulses [0.4999996424, 0.0000003825, 0.0000003949, 0.4999996126] N s, residual 6.51e-14 m/s, work -0.5 J and witness-based angular impulse error 8.45e-10 N m s. The independent FP64 solution in CCD_BOX_REFERENCE predicts opposing impulses of 0.5 N s each, zero final normal velocity and work -0.5 J. The force-free sliding box also remains accepted with zero normal impulse and zero work.

The 72-case matrix remains 71/72: the previously retained 33-contact frictional impact rejects at one sweep with status 11 and residual 0.0208123 m/s; rollback and deterministic replay hold. The owner-shaped 33/257-contact fixture passed all 14 sweep configurations, including the production two-sweep cases with 0.08174999803 N s impulse, zero measured linear momentum error and work -0.003341531089 J. Higher sweeps are diagnostics, not production configuration. Maximum accepted linear impulse error in the geometry matrix was 3.24e-8 N s, and in the 72-case matrix 5.59e-8 N s. These synthetic outcomes do not establish broadly accurate friction, generalized articulated motion or complete restoration of every possible cache.

This candidate must remain unpromoted until singular and anisotropic behavior, frictional impacts, moving bodies, articulated response, full restoration and total cost are independently qualified. The known helper anisotropic and one-axis counterexamples remain blockers. No timing claim is made.

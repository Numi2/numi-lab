# Metric projection: bounded world checks and injected failure

The normal research shader from CCD_ANISOTROPIC_METAL was run on the unchanged geometry, 72-case coupled, 48-case moving-body, and four-case prismatic-link fixtures. Acceptance was respectively 24/24, 71/72, 47/48 and 4/4. The same 33-contact frictional one-sweep impact rejects in both coupled and moving fixtures with status 11 and observed state/cache restoration. The jointed analytic impulse and work references still match; all observed replay comparisons pass. Maximum accepted linear momentum error was 5.97e-8 N s in geometry, 5.59e-8 N s in coupled and 8.39e-8 N s in moving; maximum witness-based angular momentum error was 1.53e-9 N m s in geometry. Full fixture assumptions and non-generalizability appear in CCD_BOX_PAIR and CCD_JOINTED_MOVING. These checks do not establish general friction accuracy or global convergence.

Separately, a **test-only** shader changes the evaluated cone to (0.5,0) for a sentinel material coefficient 12345 after normal contact compilation. This unsupported cone cannot be requested through current collision compilation and the instrumented shader must not be deployed. Nine contact-bearing cases across sphere, capsule and box, with incoming, stationary and separating normal speed, rejected with status 3 (`MR_STEP_NONFINITE_RESULT`); q, v, scene and empty manifold count were restored and replayed. Three far, no-contact cases accepted. The same 12 sentinel fixtures all accepted with the non-instrumented shader. This confirms the tested non-distributed empty-cache failure path; it does not show that unsupported inputs are reachable in production or prove restoration of a populated cache or distributed failure.

All experimental CCD and contact law options remain disabled. The 33-contact frictional one-sweep rejection, exact populated-cache continuation, broader anisotropic, singular and articulated qualification, and total performance are still unresolved; this candidate remains unpromoted. No speed claim.

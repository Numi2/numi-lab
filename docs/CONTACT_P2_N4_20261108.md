# Independent P2 n=4 spatial comparison

The n=2 order-18 P2 trajectory passed its temporal, sampled quadrature,
momentum, energy and work checks, while continuous clearance and spatial
qualification remain open. The n=4 study uses the same geometry, material,
density, gravity, initial gap, regularized continuum energy, consistent mass
and order-18 surface quadrature. It differs from the production P1 nodal
contact discretization and is an independent reference experiment only.

## Prediction and gates registered before execution

Run the same DOP853 coarse/fine 10 ms configurations as n=2 (max steps 8/4
µs, rtol 1e-8/1e-10, displacement atol 1e-12/1e-13 m; velocity atol 1000
times greater). The 0.25% force-bin, total-impulse and contact-work temporal
limits, sampled gap and positive volume Jacobian, momentum, energy and work
balances remain exactly as declared in the n=2 order-18 plan. At every
snapshot compare order-18 force to independent Duffy-64 with a 0.25% maximum
difference, and check Duffy-48 vs Duffy-64 within 0.05%, both normalized by
fine n=4 peak 1 ms force bin. Reject nonpositive order-18 stage gaps and
volume Jacobians; additionally require positive sampled Duffy gaps.

Only if all n=4 prerequisites pass compare n=2 versus n=4 fine force bins
and integrated impulse with the unchanged 0.25% limits, normalized by fine
n=4 peak bin and impulse. No spatial outcome is assumed. Even a passing n=2
to n=4 comparison would not by itself establish a converged spatial reference,
continuous clearance or a production/physical-material qualification.

The n=4 mesh has 384 tets (8× n=2) and 729 P2 DOFs. Based on n=2's
18.3/36.4 s and element scaling, expected coarse/fine cost is roughly
2.5/5 minutes, with substantial uncertainty. Use sequential 480/900-second
process-group timeouts, one BLAS thread, and no GPU; preserve failures and
stop on execution failure.

## Result

Both runs completed, in 104.1 s and 237.2 s. Force-bin and impulse
coarse/fine differences were 7.27e-8 and 3.05e-8; work difference was
4.53e-6 of the declared work scale. The maximum order-18 versus Duffy-64
force difference was 0.01091% of fine peak bin, and Duffy-48 versus
Duffy-64 differed by 0.0000624%; all these checks pass. Minimum sampled
gap was 89.79 µm, and sampled Jacobians, momentum, energy and work checks
passed. These checks do not certify clearance between quadrature points or
solver evaluations.

After those prerequisites passed, n=2→4 force-bin difference was 22.05%
and total-impulse difference 0.2570%, each normalized by its fine n=4
reference. **Both exceed the unchanged 0.25% spatial gates.** This P2
comparison is therefore not a spatially converged force reference; no
production, hard-contact, replay or physical-material conclusion follows.

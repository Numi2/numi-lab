# Mixed rod–rigid friction direction and work screening

**No runtime change was made and no candidate was promoted.** This is a native Apple M6 screen of the previously retained isolated split-step Wave32 research shader, not the production shader. At two sweeps, 23 of 30 fixtures were accepted with normal-complementarity residual above the preregistered 0.002 m/s threshold (maximum 0.453349 m/s). At 64 sweeps none of these 30 fixtures exceeded it (maximum 5.69e-8 m/s). This is not justification to replace a convergence certificate with a larger fixed budget: useful runtime completion still needs bounded convergence, an explicit publication criterion and complete rollback when that criterion cannot be met.

The prerecorded [plan](PLAN.md) varies normal speed, friction, tangent direction and the two explicit sweep budgets in an overlapped, zero-gravity sphere/rod/plane fixture. All 60 cases executed with one mixed island, one rigid contact and two rod contacts. The trace and control exposed outputs match after excluding the instrument-only fields; their state replay flags, momentum checks, finite values and friction-cone checks pass. Across 64-sweep frictional cases, 18 rows met the predeclared threshold for a sliding-direction test; the largest angular difference from opposing slip was 2.37e-7 rad, with largest relative cone-bound difference 2.03e-7. No observed row's tangent impulse had a positive final-slip product above 1e-6 J. Frictionless rows had zero tangent impulse. The maximum difference between independently reconstructed final sphere-plane point velocity and the GPU trace was 2.82e-8 m/s. The [negative control](NEGATIVE-CONTROL.md) intentionally corrupts a traced slip vector and is detected by both the direction and independent-velocity checks. None of this independently validates rod surface velocities.

The subsequent [work plan](WORK-PLAN.md) captures each row's stored pre-solve and final relative velocities and computes `J dot (v_pre + v_post)/2`, split into normal and tangential terms. All 60 control and traced outputs matched the earlier runs after removing the new field, and independently reconstructed initial sphere point velocity matched exactly. Per-case sums of the tangential quantity range from -0.377366 J to zero; total contact sums range from -0.877316 J to +5.012e-5 J. The positive result occurs in a fixture with authored initial overlap and stabilization. It is not by itself proof of energy creation: a full assessment must include rod internal and stored energy, regularization and the discrete integration rule. These work quantities use the solver's reported rod-relative velocities and do not establish a complete system energy balance or accurate surgical forces.

## Evidence and boundaries

The analyzer checks both frictionless and frictional constraints and does not discard low-iteration failures. The `negative-control/` results are intentionally modified data, not a real physical run.

The library and shader identities—not the current repository head alone—bind the native results. The probe links the recorded research build at `/Users/numi/numi-reconciliation-build-20261108/lib`; other shader AIR inputs are recorded with hashes. `prepare.py` reflects the actual original remote source paths; for independent reconstruction, apply `instrumentation.patch` to `split-research.metal`. This is a one-step fixture with imposed overlap, friction coefficients 0 and 0.5, and a static plane. It does not qualify arbitrary materials, anisotropic friction, force accuracy, sustained coupling, rollback, physical replay, surgery, timing or the general contact law. Instrument equivalence covers the fields exposed by the probe, not every hidden cache. Both the experimental directional/interval CCD and augmented-Lagrangian options remain disabled.

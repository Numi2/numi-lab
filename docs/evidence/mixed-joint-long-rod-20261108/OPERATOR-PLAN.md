# Independently reconstruct the longer-rod response

For the accepted step 5 of the nine-node fixture, capture the current 9-by-9 regularized contact response, its rigid-only contribution, the complete nine-node translation and eight-edge twist factors, and witness geometry. Use separate post-solve shader variants and require each to reproduce the uninstrumented exposed physical trajectory, statuses and repeat/capture checks. Reconstruct the rod contact Jacobian independently in FP64 from captured witness endpoints, interpolation weights, edge geometry, radial direction and constraint axes. Reconstruct the dense inverse of the captured banded factors and compare all 81 entries of `J A^-1 J^T` to the GPU full-minus-rigid response. Require maximum absolute difference <=0.01 velocity-per-impulse and relative maximum <=1e-5, with a deliberately perturbed-entry negative control. Report subtraction cancellation and failures instead of replacing the gate. This independently checks the action of the captured factor and witness at one accepted configuration, not the underlying rod model, physical force, or out-of-band cases.

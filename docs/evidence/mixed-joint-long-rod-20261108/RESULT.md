# Longer-rod mixed-contact research: retained results

The band-limited joint block is a research shader only. It is not enabled in the engine and this evidence does not qualify it for production. The strict fallback equivalence gate failed. Neither the directional CCD option nor the augmented-Lagrangian contact option is promoted by this work.

On the Apple Metal host, the original three-node exposed trajectory was unchanged from the earlier joint candidate. The GPU band predicate passed 12 boundary and out-of-band cases. The new candidate completed all 20 steps for each 5-, 9- and 17-node synthetic rod–sphere–plane fixture; the prior three-node-only candidate completed 10, 13 and 20 steps respectively. The fixed-128 split control completed 20 in each case. Relative to that discrete control, the largest force discrepancy normalized by peak force was 0.00192%, 0.00295% and 0.00606%, respectively; integrated impulse discrepancies were 0.000185%, 0.0000312% and 0.0000108%. These are comparisons to an internal discrete reference, not measurements of physical force accuracy. Captured row residuals and friction-cone checks passed the predeclared gates, and each step reported use of the joint block. The complete data and definitions are in `runs/analysis.json`.

Witness instrumentation confirmed neighboring edge pairs in all steps: (1,2) on the five-node rod, (3,4) on the nine-node rod and (7,8) on the 17-node rod. An injected failure after the joint solve on the nine-node fixture rejected the step and restored exposed q/v, scene bodies, rod nodes and edges byte for byte against the ten-step control; an eleven-step negative control differed. Hidden caches, external owners and an actual retry were not established by this test.

The forced-denial shader is intended to exercise the split fallback. Its status sequences and acceptance counts matched the previous shader for the five- and nine-node fixtures, but the exposed traces contained 168 and 119 differing scalar values. The maximum numerical differences, across fields with mixed units, were approximately 5.96e-8. The predeclared byte-exact gate therefore **failed**; its cause has not been established. Moreover, forced denial does not demonstrate behavior on an actual out-of-band contact island. `fallback/analysis.json` preserves the comparison.

At step index 5 of the nine-node case, fifteen separate post-solve capture shaders reproduced the exposed uninstrumented trace and repeat checks. An independent FP64 reconstruction of the contact Jacobian and the captured 27-translation/eight-twist factors compared all 81 entries of the rod response against the GPU full-minus-rigid response. The maximum absolute difference was 0.000501954 velocity per impulse and the maximum normalized difference was 1.054e-7, passing the predeclared 0.01 and 1e-5 gates; a deliberately perturbed entry was rejected. See `operator/analysis.json`. The check validates the sampled factor action and witness geometry, not the underlying constitutive model or articulated response. Subtraction of two FP32 responses may amplify cancellation.

No end-to-end out-of-band island, hidden-state restoration, physical calibration, deformable interaction, arbitrary contact layout or performance gain has been demonstrated. Preserve the failed exact fallback result and continue to treat both contact and CCD experiments as disabled.

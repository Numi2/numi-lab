The paired-displacement revision passed **6/12** exposed one-step cases. Case 3, rejected by the centered-only candidate, now passes every physical gate. Cases 6 and 8, which previously passed, now reject at line search. The other five results retain their previous classifications. Therefore the predeclared prediction that all seven earlier passes remain passes is **contradicted**; this candidate is not an overall improvement and must not be promoted. All six rejected cases report candidate line-search status 4 without publication. Both full runs were byte-identical.

For the six passing cases, the largest independently evaluated local momentum residuals were 0.000229 m/s and 0.0000780 rad/s, and the two native gradient scales agreed within 1.03e-7 m/s and 2.28e-10 rad/s. The maximum reference differences were 5.82e-10 m in position, 5.82e-7 m/s in velocity, 9.36e-9 rad in twist and 9.36e-6 rad/s in twist rate; the largest support-force error was 1.69e-8 N. Perturbing velocity by 0.002 m/s triggered the local check in each of these passing cases. Per-case outcomes, status and complete accepted states are retained in `results.jsonl`.

The residual remaining in the rejected cases is predominantly linear, but the screen does not isolate its precise source. Paired displacement alone is not sufficient; cancellation or rounding in force accumulation is a further hypothesis to test independently. No looser threshold or rerun was used. This remains an isolated one-step Metal experiment with no persistent state, production publication, rollback, contact, calibrated material or benchmark qualification. The centered-only 7/12 candidate and the original 2/12 result remain available; neither repairs production. Directional CCD and AL contact remain disabled.

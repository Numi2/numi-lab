# Unused-direction and friction controls

After the first candidate screen, exercise the same current library and baseline/candidate shaders in a fresh parameterized probe. Use unused z rotations -0.55, -0.16, 0.13 and 0.49 radians, equal fixed geometry and positive 0.1 mm gap within the collision envelope; 1 ms step, no gravity, damping, CCD, angular motion or normal motion. For each angle test +x, -x, +z and -z at 1 m/s, with zero friction and then coefficient 0.6. Each case executes and repeats once. Analytically a frictionless rigid body translating parallel to the plane with zero angular motion has unchanged velocity and orientation. Require generated contacts, successful steps, repeat-identical q/v, normal/angular velocity changes below 1e-6, and no tangential velocity change in all zero-friction candidate cases. For nonzero friction the candidate branch condition must be inactive: require byte-identical baseline and candidate q/v and statuses for matched cases. The same normal check for baseline is an exploratory failure count. Add 1 cm clear-gap no-contact controls. Reject any mismatch; do not tune the solver or fit parameters to the outcomes. These are discrete fixed-plane rigid tests, not force, CCD, rod or moving-body qualification.

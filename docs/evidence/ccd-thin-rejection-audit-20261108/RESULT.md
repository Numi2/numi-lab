# Thin rotating-impact rejection diagnosis

This is an offline reanalysis of the previously retained, native hybrid-CCD full-step observations and event traces. It introduces no new native trial. For the legacy selector at both tested scales and search caps 16 and 128, each of the matching thicker impacts completes. The 250 rad/s thin impact rejects with an unresolved cast at both scales. The 177 rad/s thin impact rejects with event-pass exhaustion at scale 1 and an unresolved cast at scale 0.5. Raising the diagnostic search cap does not change these outcomes. Every trial was repeated identically; rejected q/v and the initially empty contact cache were restored. Detailed rows are in `analysis.json`.

In the final successful event boundary preceding each rejection, the reconstructed point normal velocities remain negative even after the recorded contact impulses. At cap 16 and scale 1 they are approximately -1.649 m/s (250 rad/s) and -0.995 m/s (177 rad/s); the corresponding recorded positive gaps divided by the configured 10 ms microstep give base target velocities of approximately -0.00528 and -0.00418 m/s. The target evaluator adds a nonnegative stabilization term. This is a strong indication that the captured candidate velocities and contact response warrant investigation before changing the cast search. It is not a certified solver residual: the old trace did not record evaluated rows, operator state or the final convergence certificate, and the contact point is a midpoint. These candidate boundaries belong to ultimately rejected steps, not accepted physical evolution.

The preregistered analysis initially assumed every thin rejection would have an unresolved-cast flag. That assumption was false: the 177 rad/s, scale-1 case exhausts the event-pass budget. `analyze-v1.py` and `analysis-v1.stderr` retain this failed assumption; the subsequent analysis classifies the first non-success status and retains the distinction. The unaltered captured trace and observation archive are referenced by SHA-256 in `inputs.sha256`. Recompute with `python3 analyze.py ../ccd-acceptance-resolution-20261107/retained-evidence.tar.gz` from this directory.

The next executable diagnosis should capture the selected contact rows, evaluated targets, post-solve Jacobian velocities, impulses and convergence certificate at these exact event boundaries, with independent reconstruction and matching clean near-misses. Any candidate correction must still pass whole-trajectory clearance, impact response, conservation and rollback checks at cap 16. Neither experimental advancement selector was enabled or tuned by this work.

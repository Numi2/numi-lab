# Continuous loaded mixed rod contact: research qualification

Before execution, use the same three-node rod, sphere, plane, materials and 1 ms step as the prior one-step fixture. Apply gravity -9.81 m/s^2 to both rod and sphere; start the sphere with 0.05 m/s tangential speed and zero normal speed. Run 20 uninterrupted control steps on the native GPU timeline with no CCD and friction 0.5. Compare identical inputs using the previously retained split solver at a fixed 64 sweeps and the unpromoted bounded solver requested at two sweeps with cap 64. A cap-2 candidate is a rejection control. Capture each post-publication contact status, rigid q/v, rod-node state and contact constraints using a read-only borrowed-command-buffer callback; compare instrumented versus callback-free final q/v, scene bodies, rod nodes/edges and status arrays byte-for-byte in each arm. The callback must never alter simulation state or determine acceptance.

Report accepted/rejected physical time, per-step contact counts, normal and tangent impulses and binned normal force, rigid and rod motion, and deterministic repeatability. Require all 20 control and bounded steps to complete, at least 10 accepted steps with both rigid and rod contacts, and every bounded normal residual <=0.002 m/s. Compare 5 ms averaged ground normal forces against the fixed-64 control's peak bin and total ground normal impulse relatively, retaining the previously specified 0.25% force and impulse limits. Independently sum final momenta and external gravity impulse with ground contact impulse when all external ground contacts are identifiable; evaluate force and impulse without time alignment. Capture and report contact impulse and a midpoint ground-contact work estimate, documenting its geometry and integration limits. Report all failed gates and data availability; never treat rejection as accepted evolution. This is a narrowed sustained mixed-contact experiment, not a force-qualified continuum reference, calibrated tissue or a benchmark. No production shader or experimental CCD/AL flag is changed.

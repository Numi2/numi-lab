# Velocity certificate sensitivity after the force-gate failure

The 64- and 128-sweep internal controls agree within the unchanged 1 ms force and impulse gates. The existing 0.002 m/s bounded result does not: its first-step ground impulse is 0.00179767 N s below the 64-sweep control, while its normal residual is 0.00179613 m/s for the 1 kg sphere. A velocity residual can therefore be small in absolute units yet be large relative to the impulse that the force requirement permits.

As a diagnostic, not production tuning, make two isolated copies of the same bounded shader with both normal and projected-tangent velocity thresholds set to 0.0002 and 0.00002 m/s respectively; keep the 64-sweep cap, requested two sweeps, initial conditions, model and timestep unchanged. Run the same instrumented 20-step case and report completion, exact capture transparency/replay, per-step normal residual, number of sweeps, 1 ms force error and total impulse error against the unmodified fixed-128 control. Require the previously specified 0.25% force and impulse limits. Retain any rejection or failure; do not promote any threshold or infer a general scale law from one fixture. A general solution must specify force/impulse tolerances and conditioning across changing mass, stiffness, geometry, timestep and coupled fields, and must pass unused cases and full transaction checks.

# Mixed contact full-response capture plan

The prior first-failure diagnostic found two contacts sharing the central rod node and a nodal-mass-only normalized cross response of 0.998815. That proxy omits the implicit rod inverse, twist, rigid response and friction axes. Determine the full 9-by-9 local discrete velocity-response matrix for the existing three-contact, three-node, one-articulation sustained fixture at its first rejected state; do not infer it from the proxy or prune contacts.

Use the retained isolated 2e-5 m/s, 128-sweep split-solver shader and identical current runtime and linked AIR inputs. After its final iteration, three capture-only shader variants, one per target axis, write each target contact response to all three source axes of all three contacts into otherwise unused contact evidence fields. The calculation must include the full rod Cholesky backsolve, twist inertia, articulated/rigid response, cross-contact coupling and per-row diagonal regularization. Restrict the diagnostic to exactly this fixture and fail rather than truncate other layouts. It must not mutate accepted or candidate physical state, solver, stopping rule or caches. Record dimensions, row identity/order, units, matrix values, and source/artifact hashes. Run a fresh unmodified baseline and each variant; require exact equality of exposed trajectory, status, contact geometry and impulses after excluding explicitly overwritten diagnostic fields, exact repeated captures, and the same 7 accepted steps and first failure at step 7. Preserve any failures and halt on mismatches.

Analyze all 9 axes at the first failure. Check finite entries, symmetry and positive definiteness numerically in FP64, report singular values and matrix scaling, with a declared relative symmetry screen of 1e-4 and minimum eigenvalue tolerance of -1e-5 times maximum absolute eigenvalue. Report normal-only submatrix and cross-axis blocks separately; do not equate conditioning with physical error. Independently validate the rod inverse action and full matrix against an FP64 reconstruction from captured factor and kinematic inputs or an independently applied velocity perturbation before treating it as the exact physical operator. If those inputs cannot be captured, retain the results as a solver-side estimate and explicitly identify the missing validation. A failed symmetry/positivity check is evidence, not permission to loosen the threshold. No runtime solver change, benchmark, frictional force qualification or new contact/CCD experiment is authorized by this instrument.

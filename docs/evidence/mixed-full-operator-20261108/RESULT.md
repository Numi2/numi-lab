# Full local response at the retained mixed-contact failure

**The captured local operator is highly coupled, including after diagonal scaling. This is a diagnosis, not a solver repair or a force qualification.** The bounded split solver is research-only; the directional/interval CCD and augmented-Lagrangian contact experiments remain disabled.

On Apple M6 over `ssh numi`, the retained three-contact sphere/rod/plane fixture and research shader were rebuilt from source revision `740d6082f`. With the correct two-sweep requested minimum and the shader fixed at a maximum of 128 sweeps, the fresh control exactly reproduced the earlier exposed trajectory: seven accepted 1 ms steps, followed by 13 rejected attempts, with first failure at step 7. All 14 post-solve capture arms reproduced those exposed fields, contact geometry, impulses, statuses, and repeated traces after excluding the deliberately overwritten scratch fields. This does not verify hidden cache or full owner restoration. The test is a synthetic fixture with imposed initial overlap; it is not a surgical or calibrated-force result.

The 9-by-9 response maps the three normal/tangent impulse components at each of three contacts to the corresponding relative velocity components and includes diagonal row regularization. The captured values are finite, relative maximum asymmetry is `5.27e-8`, and all eigenvalues of the symmetrized matrix are positive; both preregistered gates pass. The largest/smallest singular-value ratio is approximately `19,902`. As a post-hoc dimensional scaling diagnostic, dividing by the square roots of the diagonal entries gives a symmetric-eigenvalue ratio of approximately `10,143`; for the normal-only 3-by-3 block it is `2,034`, and the two rod normal rows have normalized cross response `0.999017`. These values describe this discrete matrix at this one candidate state. They do not establish the convergence rate or prove why a nonlinear frictional iteration stalls.

The rod contribution was independently reconstructed in FP64 from captured 9-by-9 translation and 2-by-2 twist Cholesky factors and the independently retained witness geometry. A CPU surface Jacobian includes interpolation, rotating-frame/radius response and twist. All 81 entries matched the GPU full-minus-rigid capture within `0.000503` velocity-per-impulse units, or `1.01e-7` of the largest reference entry; the preregistered gates were `0.01` and `1e-5`. Perturbing a captured entry by one unit was rejected. This independently checks response algebra for the captured factor and witness, not whether their underlying model is physically correct.

The first floating-sphere comparison **failed** its preregistered relative gate: maximum absolute difference `5.4363e-5`, relative difference `5.4308e-5`, versus `1e-4` absolute and `1e-5` relative limits.

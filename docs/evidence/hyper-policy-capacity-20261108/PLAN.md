# Stateful policy capacity-growth continuity

Before execution, use a real MetalWorld resident and a real compiled MetalHyperPolicy on the same deterministic Franka task. Disable CCD and use sufficient contact capacity, with one environment and no externally requested resets. Author a simple deterministic policy whose phase increases by 0.02 per accepted step, with a rate-limited constant nonzero action, so a reset of either phase or previous action is observable. The program is diagnostic and is not trained.

Compare three accepted physical steps as one resident submission partitioned 1+2 against 2+1 and 1+1+1. The 1+2 schedule alone grows control-step capacity after a stateful accepted step; 2+1 and 1+1+1 do not grow it after initialization. Capture phases, policy action outputs, reset/termination/status, final q/v and scene; repeat each schedule independently with identical initial state and seed. Check each accepted outcome and retain all data. Compare phase and action sequence at the same logical step and report any physical state difference; do not assume partition invariance before verifying this fixture. No performance claim, friction or learning-quality claim is intended. A mismatch signals a continuity defect or a partition-dependent confound to investigate, not by itself a qualified repair.

# Unused coupled-rod Metal states

The unchanged compensated-force Metal candidate accepted and passed all **12/12** preregistered unused one-step states, with identical outputs on two full runs. The seeds and fixed input generator were recorded before reference generation. All twelve FP64 reference steps independently passed the native-energy local momentum, two-gradient-scale and support-reaction checks before the Metal cases were run. These inputs are unused seeds from the same generating family, not a broad geometric or material holdout.

Across the twelve cases, the largest position and velocity differences were 4.16e-12 m and 4.16e-9 m/s; the largest twist and twist-rate differences were 1.09e-8 rad and 1.09e-5 rad/s; and the largest support-force error was 7.51e-9 N. Independently evaluated local linear and angular momentum residuals were at most 1.41e-7 m/s and 0.000103 rad/s, under their 0.001 gates. Both deliberately perturbed velocity and twist-rate controls were detected on every case. Exact candidate, reference, two-scale native results, commands and hashes are retained.

The comparison is an isolated 1 ms step without contact, gravity, damping or curved rest geometry. The two arms implement the same discrete rod energy, and the native gradients are a model implementation check, not physical calibration. No persistent device owner, full rollback, dynamic body coupling, performance or production solver claim follows. The earlier candidate failures remain retained; the production false acceptance is not repaired, and experimental directional CCD and AL contact remain disabled.

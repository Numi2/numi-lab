# Mixed articulated and scene-body angular momentum audit

Before execution: in zero gravity, use a freely floating articulated sphere and a dynamic scene sphere with equal masses, explicit spherical inertias, and no other nearby shapes. Give the scene sphere closing and tangential velocity and use one speculative step, captured contact rows, and independently repeated owners. Use zero gap and positive gap with friction, plus positive gap with friction disabled. Verify accepted status, a single expected sphere-sphere contact, repeated state, impulse direction and separately reconstructed witness positions. Independently compute total change in linear and angular momentum from initial and final body velocities and positions, and compare it with the angular impulse implied by the articulated witness and scene midpoint. Equal and opposite internal impulses at a shared point predict zero net angular impulse; the asymmetric points predict their separation crossed with the tangential impulse. Because position integrates with final velocity, compute the momentum about a fixed origin using initial centers and final velocities; also verify the equivalent final-position expression. An absolute 1e-7 N m s gate is the initial accounting tolerance. If prerequisites, inertial conventions, or physical state accounting do not hold, report inconclusive with raw evidence. This is a diagnostic, not authorization for an implementation change or performance claim. The fixed-contact CPU reference continues concurrently.

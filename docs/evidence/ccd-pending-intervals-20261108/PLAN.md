# Independent inspection of pending box-plane intervals

Before capture, instrument only diagnostic fields of an already unresolved
box-plane CCD record. Preserve the prior unpromoted four-contact shader, the
production cap of 16 and disabled experimental options. For both scale 1 and
0.5 of fixture 12, capture the first five pending normalized quaternion
intervals, projected sweep endpoint quaternions and vertical centers, fixed
plane normal and center, and the certificate tolerance and guard. The previous
run found at most five pending nodes. Multiple compile-time capture selectors
may be used; each must have the same outcome and all event trace fields as a
fresh uninstrumented control, except diagnostic pair-time fields on unresolved
records. No output from this observation affects selection or publication.

Use an independent FP64 quaternion/plane calculation on the captured endpoints
to evaluate each pending interval for corner 0 and all box corners; report
sample minima and conservative sampling enclosures with stated numerical
rounding allowance. Also evaluate the full remaining predicted sweep. The
fixture is a single freely rotating box over a stationary plane, not a general
articulated path. This is a diagnostic of the predicted remainder, not a
production geometric certificate or a claim about actual accepted motion.
Capture all selector inputs, binaries, traces and hash inventories. Do not
increase budgets or alter acceptance. If the observations differ or any data
is missing, fail the analysis and report that rather than drawing a conclusion.

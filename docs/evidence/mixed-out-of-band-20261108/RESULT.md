# Real out-of-band mixed-contact fallback

**The selected-inverse band guard was reached and denied the joint block on a real synthetic out-of-band island. The candidate remains isolated and unpromoted.** This checks one important fallback branch; it does not repair or qualify the production contact solver.

On Apple M6 via `ssh numi`, the nine-node, pre-bent rod contacted the same sphere through edges 0 and 6 while the sphere contacted a static plane. All 20 steps were accepted. On every step, the instrument captured one rigid and two rod rows, witness node span 7 and edge span 6, and a marker written only after the actual band predicate returned false. The candidate's ordinary and guard-instrumented traces match exactly after removing the scratch fields used for instrumentation. The ordinary trace also matches the prior node-limited shader on all exposed non-scratch fields. All arms report repeat, captured/uncaptured agreement and exact repeated captured traces.

The separate artificial forced-denial shader completed all 20 steps but **failed** the predeclared exact comparison against ordinary fallback, with 967 differing exposed scalar fields. The largest numerical difference over mixed units is 0.0001129848606, in the reported tangent diagnostic at step 12; this maximum must not be interpreted as a single physical error norm. The observed ordinary-versus-old equality does not erase this failed comparison or explain its cause. The synthetic guard finding must not be generalized to other geometries or compiler configurations.

The rod starts bent against a straight rest model, so internal elastic stress is intentional; this is not a measured material or physical force reference. The check did not independently validate tangential forces, all hidden caches, contact conservation, a failed-step retry, arbitrary topology or production behavior. A successful exposed trajectory and the solver-reported residual are not an independent physical-accuracy certificate. CCD was disabled in these fixtures, and neither experimental CCD nor augmented-Lagrangian contact was enabled.

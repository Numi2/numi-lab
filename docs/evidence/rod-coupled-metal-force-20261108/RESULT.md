# Compensated-force coupled Metal candidate

The predeclared screen completed and passed **12/12** exposed one-step cases, including all seven cases that passed the centered-only candidate and all five that it rejected. Two full independent executions were byte-identical. The only algorithmic change relative to the preceding paired-displacement candidate is the registered precision treatment of edge direction, stretch force products and residual accumulation; the combined result supports the force-rounding hypothesis for these inputs, but does not identify the contribution of every arithmetic operation separately.

The largest position difference from the checked FP64 reference was 1.32e-9 m; the largest velocity difference was 1.32e-6 m/s. Maximum twist and twist-rate differences were 8.09e-9 rad and 8.09e-6 rad/s. Maximum support-force error was 6.95e-9 N. Independently evaluated local linear and angular momentum residuals were at most 0.000184 m/s and 0.0000810 rad/s, both below 0.001. The maximum disagreement between the two native gradient scales was 1.03e-7 m/s and 4.25e-10 rad/s. Each passing case detected the registered 0.002 m/s perturbed-velocity control. The exact output for every case is retained in `results.jsonl`.

This is an exposed-case, single-step, standalone GPU research screen of straight-rest isotropic rods. It does not establish held-out generalization, long trajectories, production state ownership, rollback, contact, moving rigid coupling, calibrated material accuracy, or performance. In particular, it does not repair the published production rod path. The previous failed 2/12 and 6/12 screens and the partial 7/12 screen remain intact. Directional CCD and augmented-Lagrangian contact stay disabled. Next test an unchanged candidate on unused initial states and verify each FP64 reference independently with native energy gradients before evaluating any Metal results.

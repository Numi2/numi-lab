# Coupled Metal rod: resident commit and retry

The isolated resident owner completed the three preregistered ten-step clean trajectories and the corresponding three fault/retry trajectories on Apple M6, with no host state readback between attempts. The 63 GPU attempts in each complete run were encoded in one Metal command buffer. Each injected scratch edge produced candidate status 2 (degenerate geometry) before accepted step five; in all three cases the entire **752-byte** accepted record was identical before and after rejection. The retry and all subsequent accepted records, including edge and displacement pairs, velocity, centered twist, twist rate, last reactions and accepted-step count, matched their uninterrupted counterparts byte-for-byte. Both complete studies and their raw GPU/native traces repeated byte-for-byte.

All **30/30** clean physical steps passed the registered checks against the separately advanced native-energy FP64 reference. The largest differences were 2.51e-9 m in position, 4.02e-6 m/s in velocity, 3.74e-8 rad in twist and 2.84e-5 rad/s in twist rate. The largest support-force error was 8.00e-9 N. Local linear and angular momentum residuals, evaluated using the resident owner's published velocities, were at most 0.000210 m/s and 0.000146 rad/s, under the 0.001 gates. The reference residual maxima were 0.0000906 m/s and 0.0000842 rad/s. Moving-support work was -8.947994317e-7 J compared with -8.947994712e-7 J in the reference; maximum cumulative difference was 3.95e-14 J. Maximum kinetic-plus-elastic energy difference was 8.30e-14 J.

The compressed trace includes every native reference query and the complete GPU output, including accepted-state bytes before and after the deliberately invalid attempt. The device advances edge vectors and displacements relative to the immutable initial configuration; the diagnostic host retains that initial configuration to reconstruct world positions after the command completes. The reference uses independently computed native-energy gradients of the same model, not measured material data.

This is an isolated resident research owner for three previously observed, noncontact, gravity-free cases. It does not yet integrate with MetalWorld's rod state, checkpoint format, dynamic body or deformable contact ownership; it does not qualify command-buffer failure, device loss, general rollback, curved rest geometry, material calibration, or performance. Production's false-acceptance defect remains unfixed. The existing XPBD path and experimental directional CCD and AL contact options are unchanged. The next work must integrate paired state and atomic publication into the real MetalWorld owner and qualify full-step failure and retry there before considering promotion.

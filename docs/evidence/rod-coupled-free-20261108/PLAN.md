# Free-end coupled rod with gravity: preregistration

MetalWorld advances the rod without prescribing its endpoints; coupling constraints and contacts are later owned by the shared world solve. Test whether the existing coupled implicit rod model can instead advance a straight-rest rod with both endpoints free and uniform gravity, without changing the two-hard-support mode. This is a prerequisite for, not a substitute for, world coupling and shared reaction authority. Candidate remains explicit research-only. No contact, damping, rigid bindings or Matter are admitted, and no automatic fallback is permitted.

Implement a separate-free-end branch in the existing Metal kernel and model API, including all endpoint translational residuals and the body force in the same discrete equations. Preserve the paired geometry and transactional publication checks. Before evaluating observations, register a 3-node axial closed-form implicit system with free masses: three radii, three Young moduli, three timesteps, gravity vectors parallel to the rod, unequal initial axial velocities and asymmetric stretch (27 cases). Compute the full 3x3 FP64 reference independently, check every node and total momentum: position <=1e-6 m, velocity <=0.001 m/s, every local momentum residual <=0.001 m/s, and total momentum discrepancy <=1e-12 kg m/s; all 27 must complete. Run twice and require identical output. An undeformed, co-moving free-fall control must reproduce the semimplicit update and preserve edge lengths; a deliberately perturbed velocity must be detected by the momentum check. One attachment, invalid gravity and degenerate geometry must fail without changing the output. Rerun the retained 24 supported-end reference cases, the 27 moving-support axial cases and existing publication checks with the unchanged gates; no failures may be excluded.

The tests use native Metal on Apple hardware with source, executable and metallib identities pinned. Results will not establish general rest curvature, contact, friction, damping, material calibration, multistep world replay or performance. Both directional CCD and augmented-Lagrangian contact remain disabled. Follow-up should integrate the accepted candidate with MetalWorld state, contact impulses and checkpoint ownership, rather than introduce a second simulation owner.

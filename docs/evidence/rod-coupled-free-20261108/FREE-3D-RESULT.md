# Unseen free-end 3D result — 8 November 2026

Nine separately seeded free-end cases, with 3, 5 or 8 nodes and timesteps of 0.5, 1 and 2 ms, passed the preregistered native-energy reference gates twice with byte-identical records. Gravity was enabled. A first, unglobalized independent reference stopped at six indefinite predictors; that failed attempt and its original inputs and results remain retained. The revised independent FP64 reference starts at the same physical predictor, uses a bounded regularized search and recomputes the unshifted final tangent; it does not initialize from the Metal answer.

Across the nine cases, maximum errors were 4.01146646e-09 m in position, 2.00573314e-06 m/s in velocity, 4.82064384e-08 rad in twist and 2.41032181e-05 rad/s in twist rate. Maximum independently recomputed local linear and angular residuals were 2.95308528e-05 m/s and 0.000145486269 rad/s. The maximum global momentum error was 3.24154078e-13 kg m/s. Native-gradient agreement between two evaluation scales was 5.71336822e-08 m/s and 1.08752118e-09 rad/s; deliberate local-velocity perturbations were detected in all nine.

An additional independent finite-difference curvature check evaluated the unshifted full tangent at each actual published Metal state. All nine were positive at both derivative scales; the minimum inertially normalized eigenvalue was 0.99933601, and the largest entrywise difference between the two normalized tangent estimates was 6.4921739e-05. The first diagnostic attempt failed to start due to a missing Python namespace binding; that failure is retained, followed by a corrected retry.

The two references share a regularized-search idea, and this is a local numerical result for a homogeneous unloaded-contact research model. The checks do not demonstrate global uniqueness, agreement with physical suture data, a match between every Metal tangent entry and FP64, multi-step free-rod stability, MetalWorld integration, attachment reactions, contact, friction or transactional world-state restoration. Existing directional and interval-local CCD and AL contact remain disabled. No performance improvement is claimed.

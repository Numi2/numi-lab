# Bounded mixed-island research solver and failed-iteration reporting repair

The production change in [commit f6015eec1](https://github.com/Numi2/numi-lab/commit/f6015eec1) is a narrow diagnostic fix: Wave32 status reduction now includes the failing island's executed iteration count before returning its nonconvergence, excluding the invalid-packet sentinel. Previously, a first-island failure after two sweeps published zero sweeps. The native `wave32_status_reduction` regression deliberately failed on the old production metallib and passes on the repaired one. It covers a first-island failure, a failure after a longer successful island, an all-success maximum, the invalid-packet sentinel, and a previously failed environment. The native regression and existing contact probe pass on both the integration and main builds. The separately built mixed-island failure fixture confirms that status 11 now reports two sweeps rather than zero, with the same residual, exposed state restoration and unchanged neighboring environment. This is a failure-reporting repair, not a mixed-contact solver or rollback repair.

## Unpromoted solver research

The separately compiled candidate starts from the retained split-step shader and adds per-sweep normal complementarity and isotropic tangent projected-residual checks. A SIMD-wide reduction makes its early exit uniform across lanes and a cap produces status 11 for an uncertified mixed island. The candidate is not enabled in production and does not alter the CCD or augmented-Lagrangian flags. Its fixture-specific 0.002 m/s thresholds are not a generally qualified tolerance policy.

In the 60-case one-step screen, all 30 cases requesting two sweeps met the provisional normal gate after 2–24 actual sweeps (maximum normal residual 0.00166783 m/s), while all 30 requesting 64 sweeps ran 64 and also passed. A separate reconstruction of the tangential natural residual with a different positive step found maxima 1.259e-6 and 4.66e-10 m/s respectively. For active sliding rows, the maximum angular error against opposing slip was 0.008368 rad; cone, final-slip sign, independently reconstructed sphere velocity, reported momentum and exposed replay checks passed. Compared with the corresponding fixed-64 research result, the largest early-exit differences were 0.001668 m/s in generalized velocity, 0.002526 m/s in rod velocity and 0.001670 N s in a row impulse. These are observed differences, not a declared accuracy pass against an independent mechanical reference.

With the cap artificially restricted to two sweeps, 25 of the 30 distinct cases were rejected and five accepted. The original normal gate alone found 23 failures; the combined certificate adds tangential convergence. The retained probe repeats both requested settings against the same two-sweep cap, hence its file contains 50 rejected and 10 accepted records, not 60 independent fixtures. Every rejected record restored exposed q/v, scene body and rod node/edge arrays and repeated consistently; hidden caches and all owner state were not directly observed. The two-environment test placed an active mixed island beside a separate rigid environment. At cap 64 the mixed environment converged in 16 sweeps and the rigid environment exactly matched the unmodified two-sweep control. At cap 2 the mixed environment rejected and restored, while the rigid environment still matched the control. Queue flags did not establish that the two islands shared one packed SIMD packet, so cross-cohort barrier and arbitrary packet safety remain unqualified.

## Retained evidence and next checks

Plans, generators, hashes, build commands, probes, complete traces, baseline failure and negative controls are retained here. `candidate-sources.tar.gz` contains the generated research Metal files; `input-sha256.txt` validates the original two. `analysis.json`, `independent-natural.json`, `isolation.json`, and `isolation-count-fixed.json` preserve the numerical and transaction observations; `verify_isolation.py` reruns the paired assertions. `analyze-as-run.py` preserves the original remote reference path; `analyze.py` points to the identical retained baseline in the adjacent evidence directory. The initial generator's failed uniqueness assertion is described in `initial-generator-failure.txt`. Native libraries and executables are identified in `native-sha256.txt` but not committed.

Before considering production integration, the candidate still needs actual shared-packet/cohort tests, non-isotropic and capped-cone handling, nonfinite and dimensional-scale coverage, exact cache/owner restoration and retry, sustained multi-step work and force checks, stronger error gates against independent references, and review of dispatch budgets and barrier convergence. It must reject unsupported configurations rather than misrepresent them as converged. No speed claim, physical calibration, suture completion or full mechanics qualification follows from this screen.

The three original build stdout logs are retained byte-for-byte in `build-raw-logs.tar.gz`; `build-raw-logs.sha256` verifies them after extraction.

# Mixed and rigid environment isolation

Before this test, construct a two-environment batch with identical sphere/plane data but distinct rod state: environment 0 retains the original sphere/rod overlap, environment 1 translates its entire rod by 0.5 m to remove rod contact. Use the same source sphere model, materials, no gravity, 1 ms step, requested two sweeps, no CCD, and friction 0.5 as the prior fixture. Run the retained unmodified research shader, bounded cap-64 shader, and bounded cap-2 shader twice each. Require the isolated rigid environment's final q/v, scene bodies, rod nodes/edges and status to match the unmodified research shader byte for byte, including when its mixed neighbor is rejected. Require cap-64 to accept the mixed environment with normal residual below 0.002 m/s, and cap-2 to reject that environment with status 11 and exposed q/v, scene and rod restoration. Observe and record actual active contacts, islands and cohort flags; do not claim shared-packet coverage unless separately observed. Preserve raw output and any failure.

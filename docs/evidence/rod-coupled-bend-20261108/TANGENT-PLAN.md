# Coupled bend/twist tangent-vector follow-up

Use the local energy and gradient qualified in RESULT.md to derive its full coupled position/twist Hessian-vector product, including twist-to-position and position-to-twist blocks. This is a new diagnostic rather than a production solver. On 12 unused seeded straight-rest configurations (seeds 901–912; 3, 5 and 9 nodes), test deterministic coupled position/twist directions. Conditions use three-dimensional deformation, nonzero relative twist and offsets 0 and 0.5 m, with adjacent tangent dot product >= -0.5.

Compare the analytical Hessian-vector product with central differences of native DER component gradients at outer parameter steps 0.001 and 0.0005. Native gradients themselves use the retained finer central-difference scales. Require the two native directional estimates to agree and the analytical product to match the finer estimate componentwise, within max(1e-10 N for position or 1e-14 N m for twist, 0.2% relative to the larger compared magnitude). Also compare against independently differenced analytical gradients at the same outer steps; require the coupled Hessian bilinear symmetry `v^T H w = w^T H v` to max(1e-16 J, 1e-8 relative) on each case. Directions use position components of order 1e-3 m and twist components of order 0.5 rad. A negative control that suppresses the cross-coupling must fail at least one relevant comparison. Save raw native values and inputs, all per-case checks, exact source and instrument hashes, failed runs and a byte-identical repeat. Stop after this fixed set; no threshold or geometry changes after observing results.

This establishes only a CPU tangent comparison for this restricted energy. It does not qualify indefinite configurations, numerical Newton convergence, Metal precision, state publication, physical contact, material calibration or the production rod. Keep experimental CCD and augmented-Lagrangian contact disabled.

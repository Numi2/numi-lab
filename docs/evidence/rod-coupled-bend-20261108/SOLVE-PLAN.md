# Coupled straight-rest DER implicit-step diagnostic

Use the previously checked local straight-rest DER stretch, bend and twist energy and its coupled tangent to solve one implicit Euler step in FP64 with prescribed hard point anchors. No production runtime or contact law is changed. The inertial objective uses the actual circular rod node masses and edge rotational inertias from the native constructor, 1 ms timestep, no gravity or damping, and the unmodified EA, EI and GJ. Solve positions and edge twists together, with scaled Newton equations, line search, a positive-curvature check and explicit nonconvergence. A separate native energy finite-difference instrument evaluates the accepted state; it must include stretch as well as bend and twist. This is a reference-stage step toward Metal, not a replacement for resident execution.

Before execution register twelve cases: two 3-node previous-failure controls (0 and 100 nm transverse deviation), plus ten seeded cases 1101–1110 spanning 5, 8 and 9 nodes, 3D curved configurations, static and moving hard end anchors, offsets 0 and 0.5 m, nonzero twist and twist rate, and nonzero velocity. Rest geometry stays straight. All pairs must have positive nondegenerate length and tangent dot >= 0.5. Record each case and seed; no post-result adjustment or omission. Use at most 60 Newton iterations and 30 line-search reductions.

Require all 12 to converge to a positive-curvature stationary state with anchored positions exact and independently evaluated local linear momentum residual <=0.001 m/s and angular momentum residual <=0.001 rad/s on every unconstrained degree of freedom, using native component energy derivatives at two perturbation scales. Require the two native residual estimates to agree within 0.0001 m/s and 0.0001 rad/s respectively. Report per-anchor forces, impulses and prescribed-displacement work; compare forces to native energy gradients within max(1e-6 N, 1% of force) and reject nonfinite or structurally invalid inputs without publishing a state. Require the explicit negative control with a perturbed velocity or twist rate to violate the same local check. Repeat inputs and output byte-for-byte. Record all output, source and executable hashes, iterations, line searches, curvature, failures and statuses, with no performance claim.

This is a tightly scoped no-contact CPU diagnostic. Force/work traces, multi-step integration, friction, rod self-contact, moving-body reaction coupling, full rollback, FP32, GPU residency, curved rest geometry and calibrated suture behavior remain unqualified. If a gate fails, retain the result and do not promote the method. The experimental CCD and augmented-Lagrangian contact options remain disabled.

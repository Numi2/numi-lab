# Coupled straight-rest rod: corrected FP64 trajectories

The corrected follow-up completed 30/30 case-steps against a separately advanced reference based on numerical derivatives of the native DER energy. The cases are the three-node microbend, five-node curved/twisted rod with a moving endpoint, and eight-node curved/twisted rod with fixed endpoints. Both arms carry their own full state. This is a follow-up after observing an invalid initial run, not an untouched held-out confirmation; `TRAJECTORY-AMENDMENT.md` and `trajectory-initial-*` retain the fixed-anchor setup error and its complete initial output.

The maximum candidate/reference errors were 2.075e-9 m in position, 2.075e-6 m/s in velocity, 8.357e-11 rad in twist and 3.361e-8 rad/s in twist rate. The largest support-force error was 4.260e-9 N. Independent native-energy momentum checks on the candidate reached at most 1.146e-6 m/s and 1.397e-6 rad/s; the reference's largest corresponding residuals were 7.974e-5 m/s and 2.601e-5 rad/s. All were within their respective declared limits. The moving-support cumulative force-on-target work was -8.948259444e-7 J versus -8.948259469e-7 J for the independent reference; fixed-support work was zero. The maximum kinetic-plus-elastic energy difference was 3.054e-14 J. These are discrete-scheme comparisons, not a complete energy-conservation or physical-material assessment.

The corrected script initially recorded gate failures but would have continued that case, contrary to the plan. It was amended to stop on a failed gate; the earlier script is retained as `trajectory-corrected-prestop.py`. All 30 recorded checks had passed, and the native trace, per-step data and analysis remained byte-identical after this change and a further complete repeat. A separate test-only injected first-step gate failure returned a failure and stopped that case after one step while the other two completed ten each. Its first harness invocation failed because its source marker matched twice; the original script and log are retained, followed by the corrected passing stop control.

All native-reference inputs and component-gradient outputs are in the deterministic compressed trace; per-step errors, reactions and work are in `trajectory-analysis.json` and `trajectory-progress.jsonl`. Hashes bind the inputs, scripts, native executable and outputs. This is an FP64 CPU diagnostic with hard point anchors, no contact, no GPU residency, no production state publication and no calibrated tissue. It does not repair or qualify the production rod solver, Matter coupling, full rollback, or the continuous suture workflow. Both experimental collision/contact options remain disabled.

# Coupled bend and twist tangent

The independently coded analytical Hessian-vector product for the straight-rest scalar-bending law passed all 12 preregistered unused 3-, 5- and 9-node configurations. Its position/twist vector includes both cross-coupling blocks. Native DER gradients evaluated at two outer finite-difference scales agreed with each other and with the analytical product under the declared componentwise force and torque gates; independently differencing the analytical gradient also passed at both scales. The largest error was 0.001716 of its corresponding gate. Every Hessian bilinear symmetry control passed; the largest absolute discrepancy was 9.93e-24 J. Suppressing the position/twist cross blocks failed all 12 negative controls.

The minimum sampled adjacent-tangent dot product was 0.662, so these tests do not qualify sharp folds or antiparallel cases. Native output and analysis repeated byte for byte. The fixed generated inputs, all 48 native component-gradient records, per-case results, plan, source and checksums are retained. The reference uses finite differences of the existing native CPU energy, not a measured physical suture. This does not establish a stable coupled Newton iteration, GPU floating-point accuracy, contact/attachment reactions, state rollback or a production fix. Experimental directional CCD and augmented-Lagrangian contact remain disabled.

# Coupled straight-rest rod: sustained FP64 comparison

Run three independent 10-step, 1 ms trajectories from fixed cases in `solve-cases.json`: the three-node 100 nm microbend control, a five-node curved/twisted configuration with a translating second hard anchor, and an eight-node curved/twisted configuration with fixed hard anchors. Each arm carries its own prior positions, velocities, twists and twist rates. The moving target advances along the initial first-edge unit direction by 1 µm per step; all other targets remain fixed. Gravity and damping remain zero. Record all inputs and steps; a failed or missing step ends its trajectory and is retained.

The candidate is the registered coupled FP64 step solver. Independently advance the second trajectory from its own state by assembling a numerical Jacobian from finite differences of the native DER energy gradients and solving the same discrete implicit Euler equations; do not use the candidate positions as the reference initial condition. Use native gradient scale 1e-7 m / 1e-5 rad, outer Jacobian scale 1e-6 m / 1e-4 rad, at most 20 Newton iterations, and require independently evaluated free-node and twist-rate momentum residuals <=0.0001 m/s and rad/s. Require positive scaled Hessian eigenvalues before accepting a candidate step. Cap each native subprocess at 30 s and the study at 300 s, preserving partial progress and errors.

For all 30 steps require candidate and reference node positions <=1e-6 m, node velocities <=0.001 m/s, twist <=0.0001 rad, twist rate <=0.001 rad/s, and each support force error <=max(1e-6 N, 1% of reference). Check native-evaluated local momentum residuals on the candidate against 0.001 m/s and rad/s. Compare per-step and cumulative support work within max(1e-9 J, 2%) and final kinetic-plus-elastic energy within max(1e-9 J, 2%). The support-work sign is force on target dotted with target motion. Require completed/rejected statuses and valid finite states; repeat the whole fixed study byte-for-byte. Inputs are synthetic and no contact, bending rest geometry, biological calibration or GPU state is asserted. The experimental CCD and augmented-Lagrangian contact options stay disabled.

# Centered-twist coupled Metal candidate

The centered-angle revision passed **7/12** exposed cases, up from 2/12 for the first candidate. The original straight and microbend cases still pass; five previously rejected curved/twisted cases now also pass every registered state, reaction, independently evaluated local momentum and gradient-agreement check. The five remaining cases reject at line search without publication. Their internal linear residuals range from 0.000276 to 0.000540 m/s, above the conservative 0.00025 internal stop; their angular residuals are lower than in the first screen. This outcome supports the angular-representation hypothesis on these inputs, without proving it is the only cause.

Across the seven passing cases, maximum position error was 7.47e-10 m, maximum velocity error 7.47e-7 m/s, twist error 6.74e-9 rad, twist-rate error 6.74e-6 rad/s and support-force error 1.78e-8 N. Independently evaluated local momentum residual maxima were 0.0004175 m/s and 0.0000334 rad/s, below the 0.001 gates. The two native gradient scales agreed within 1.38e-7 m/s and 2.23e-10 rad/s. Each passing case detected the prescribed perturbed-velocity control. Both 12-case runs were byte-identical.

This is an isolated, one-step, CPU-submitted Metal research candidate with no production or persistent state owner, contact, rigid coupling, rollback or calibrated material evidence. These are exposed inputs, not a held-out qualification or performance benchmark. Five line-search rejections remain; an internal residual is not itself a physical publication certificate. The initial failed shader builds and the earlier 2/12 result remain retained. Experimental directional CCD and AL contact stay disabled.

# Local momentum invalidates the axial research candidate

**Do not promote either axial candidate.** The earlier vector candidate passed the stated position, velocity and support-force gates, but the new independent local momentum check found a failure that those gates did not capture. The production solver remains unchanged and its earlier false publication remains unresolved.

The fixed-end three-node native fixture varies the middle-node transverse offset from zero through ±1,000 nm at two orientations. The prior guarded candidate accepted 22 of 30 distinct cases; 17 accepted cases exceeded the 0.001 m/s local momentum-residual gate. At +100 nm, both orientations were accepted with residual approximately 0.00361 m/s. Its two zero-bend controls passed. Every distinct case was repeated with identical captured output.

For each accepted state, a separate FP64 CPU evaluation differentiated the actual rod stretch, curvature-binormal bend and twist energy at the published position. It evaluated `v_new - v_old + (h/m) ∇E` at the free node, not a summed body balance. Central differences at 10 and 5 nm differed by at most 1.8e-6 m/s over the first matrix, well within the predeclared 1e-4 m/s agreement limit. The analytical stretch gradient is retained alongside the finite-difference total; a deliberately perturbed residual is detected by the gate. This is an independent implementation of the local model check, not an external physical-material validation.

A separately compiled research variant adds a post-solve FP32 local stretch-momentum check and rejects nonconvergence before publication. It accepted the two zero-bend controls and rejected the other 28 microbend cases. It also rejected **all 24** five/eight-node straight moving-support fixtures that the prior vector candidate had passed under the earlier gates. This is containment with a serious useful-motion regression, not a solver repair. The check omits bend, twist, contact and coupling and cannot support production promotion.

The longer-chain diagnostic reran the earlier 24 vector cases and reproduced their exposed position, velocity and support forces exactly. Independent FP64 full-energy gradients show **all 24** exceed the 0.001 m/s local momentum gate, with a maximum of 0.2461 m/s. The two gradient estimates agree within 3.0e-6 m/s. Thus the earlier 24/24 result remains accurate for its stated gates, but is not evidence of local force consistency. FP32 position/force cancellation is a plausible contributor; this experiment does not prove its entire cause. A complete correction must reconcile local momentum, state representation, force transfer and useful motion, then pass coupled and rollback checks.

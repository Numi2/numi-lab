# Small-impulse residual correctness

The production temporal-cone status treats a normal impulse <= 1e-7 N s as zero for unilateral normal complementarity. That dimensional absolute cutoff may hide a positive separation residual under a real positive impulse on a small body. Test the actual MetalWorld owner with the existing oblique box/plane contact fixture, constant geometry and velocity but masses 1e-7, 1e-6 and 1 kg; scale inertia consistently. Zero gravity, friction and restitution; CCD disabled; normal speed -1 m/s; horizontal velocities -1, 0, +1 m/s; tilts -0.37 and +0.29; two and 32 velocity iterations. Reconstruct evaluated row complementarity independently from witness and body velocity; a positive impulse uses |v_n - target + regularization*impulse|, and zero impulse uses max(target-v_n,0). Record the observed status, impulses and exact repeats; compare old status with this equation before proposing a correction. Inputs, binaries, source and all results will be retained. The diagnostic concerns the solver status, not a force-accuracy certificate, a mixed-island convergence repair, or a performance experiment. Keep the two existing experimental options disabled.

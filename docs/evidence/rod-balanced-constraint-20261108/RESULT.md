# Accepted rod state: reproduced failure and coupled-system diagnosis

On the Apple M6, a freshly compiled copy of the retained three-node witness was linked against the integrated library and Metal shader at source `c6ff0346b` (the later commits before this report change only evidence). `baseline.log` reproduces the historical failure: at the ordinary 1e-7 m stopping tolerance, the symmetric prestretched case publishes a middle position of 0.0614376366 m and velocity of 0.4376359 m/s, instead of approximately 0.061 m and zero. The left/right reactions are +0.000621585/−0.000657152 N, compared with +0.089379112/−0.089379112 N from the independent axial implicit reference. The asymmetric and moving cases both reject at 128 iterations. The three physical gates fail. `status.log` additionally reports nine sweeps for the incorrect accepted case, a last net correction of 2.235e-8 m, and a maximum recorded constraint error of 0.00264477 in a mixed-unit diagnostic; that last value by itself is not a dimensional force certificate.

The executable `axial_projection_diagnostic.py` independently evaluates a simplified collinear FP32 version of the source stretch and hard-attachment projection order. It reproduces the accepted center position and nine-sweep net correction exactly for this fixture. Its last sweep includes an individual projection of approximately 0.00175237 m and post-sweep compliant stretch stationarity residuals of approximately 0.00262851 and 0.00175236 m. These residuals are `length - restLength + alpha * accumulatedMultiplier`, not physical stretch alone. The scalar model excludes bending and other runtime features; its intermediate values have not been read from the GPU. Agreement of the observable state and sweep count supports, but does not alone prove for every model, the cancellation diagnosis.

A separately compiled research-only Metal candidate augments the stopping condition with the predicted magnitude of a post-sweep stretch projection. It changes no production shader or executable. It rejects all three loaded cases instead of publishing the incorrect symmetric state; the unloaded straight control is accepted and meets its force and state checks. The control executable also runs two additional displaced cases, which reject; its process exit is therefore 1 even though the specifically named unloaded case passes. Candidate and control outputs repeated exactly. This is scoped containment evidence, **not a repair**: it does not complete any of the three loaded cases, establish an appropriate general residual policy, or certify bending, twist, contacts, force, rollback, or surgical behavior. No production promotion is justified.

The preregistered FP64 coupled stretch-plus-hard-attachment equations were compared with an independently assembled free-node implicit stiffness system. All eight straight-chain cases pass the stated gates for 3, 5, 9 and 17 nodes, including centered prestretch and displaced/moving interiors. Maximum position disagreement was 5.22e-15 m, maximum support-reaction disagreement 1.05e-13 N, and maximum multiplier-system residual 5.09e-15 m. The unscaled matrix condition number rose from about 5,223 at three nodes to 173,299 at 17. These are calculations of the same restricted discrete equations by different formulations, not independent physical measurements or a GPU implementation.

A subsequent preregistered CPU NumPy float32 screen of those systems passes the existing force and position gates for six of eight cases. Both 17-node cases fail the 1e-6 m maximum-position gate, at 1.399e-6 and 1.428e-6 m; their support forces nevertheless meet the 1% gate. The scaled algebraic residuals remain small, illustrating why residual size alone cannot establish position accuracy for this conditioning. CPU LAPACK/NumPy arithmetic is not a prediction of a particular Metal solve. This negative screen argues for analyzing scaling, conditioning, FP32 assembly and position reconstruction before implementing a coupled GPU solve.

`PLAN.md`, `CANDIDATE-PLAN.md`, `BLOCK-PLAN.md` and `FP32-PLAN.md` were committed before their respective screens. Raw outputs, nonzero exits, source, isolated patch, build commands and AIR input hashes are retained here. The probe's status is deliberately nonzero when any physical gate fails. Relevant rod source files are unchanged relative to public main `e045a554`; only the integrated binary was tested. The baseline and the research candidate were not benchmarked. Experimental directional CCD and augmented-Lagrangian contact remain disabled. The real earned suture bridge was not located or substituted.

Next, develop a generally applicable, dimensionally scaled coupled constraint method or preconditioner that preserves hard and compliant attachment reactions, then verify moving and bent rods, twist, contact and rollback against independent references. The full milestone also requires calibrated suture/tissue forces and an actual tissue-coupled workflow.

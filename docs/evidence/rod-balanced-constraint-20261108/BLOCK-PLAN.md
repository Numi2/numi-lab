# Independent axial coupled-system diagnostic

For the same fixed-end collinear model, form the coupled compliant stretch and hard-attachment multiplier equations in FP64. Compare the solved positions and endpoint reactions against a separately assembled free-node implicit stiffness system and equilibrium reactions. Check 3-, 5-, 9-, and 17-node straight chains with the same total length and cross-section, including a symmetric prestretch and a displaced/moving interior state. Require absolute position agreement within 1e-10 m, endpoint reaction agreement within 1e-8 N, and linear-system residual within 1e-9 m. Record the multiplier matrix condition number. This identifies a mathematical coupling direction; it does not implement a production GPU solve, prove FP32 stability, or address noncollinear rods, friction, twist or dynamically coupled attachments.

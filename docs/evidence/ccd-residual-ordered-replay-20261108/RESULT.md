# Residual-triggered ordered contact replay: negative diagnostic

**Decision: do not promote.** This isolated modification invokes an existing ordered contact replay when the preceding Wave32 island reports a normal residual above 0.002 m/s and that island has no rod. It is not an acceptance rule, a new solver, or a production change. It does not establish physical-force accuracy or safe behavior for mixed contact. The 0.002 m/s gate is the pre-existing fixture diagnostic, not a general solver contract. The preregistered experiment and limits are in `PLAN.md`.

Both shader variants were linked to the separately unpromoted monotonic box-plane certificate; both original experimental CCD selectors were off. The native 12-case hybrid fixture uses CCD cap 16, two configured velocity iterations, two scales and identical runtime/initial states to the prior controls in `docs/evidence/ccd-monotone-candidate-20261108`. The main variant uses the main Wave32 shader; the four variant also uses the separately unpromoted four-contact research shader. The replay uses the same configured iteration budget but adds ordered correction work. Only one floating box against a stationary plane is observed.

| Diagnostic | Main solver + replay | Four-contact research solver + replay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thin impacts, fixture 12/14 at both scales | 0/4 accepted | 4/4 accepted, as in control |
| Near misses, fixture 13/15 at both scales | 4/4 accepted with zero normal impulse | 4/4 accepted with zero normal impulse |
| Scale 1 thick-box fixture 1 | rejected, status 11; control accepted | rejected, status 11; control accepted |
| Normal residual gate over traced successful event solves | fails | fails |

Status 11 is `MR_STEP_DID_NOT_CONVERGE`; status 9 in one main trial is `MR_STEP_CCD_EVENT_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. For the four-contact scale-1 thick case the measured post-solve maximum normal residual rose from 0.4411288 m/s in the control to 4.3976307 m/s with replay, and the enclosing step was rejected. The trace shows the replay left a materially different internal candidate velocity and per-contact impulses. Reconstructing the normal complementarity residual from captured Jacobians, evaluated targets, impulses and final candidate velocities agrees with the reported residual within 2e-5 m/s. These are internal candidate observations, not accepted physical motion. The earlier accepted control also fails the 0.002 m/s diagnostic gate.

The four accepted thin impacts already had near-zero measured normal residual in the four-contact control, so their results provide no evidence that the replay improved those cases. In the main solver, both scale-0.5 thick impacts were accepted despite reconstructed event normal residuals approximately 2.052 and 1.282 m/s; the four thin impacts remained rejected. Main scale-1 thick fixtures 1 and 2 changed from accepted in the control to rejected. Detailed per-pass measurements and statuses are in `comparison.json` and the raw traces.

Trace-on and trace-off results match apart from initialization timing; repeated results and the exposed rejected q/v restoration checks pass for both variants. The separately executed trajectory observer passes on all accepted segments in these fixtures; this is a scoped geometric numerical screen, not a proof of general collision safety. It does not establish complete owner-state restoration, contact-force or friction accuracy, or safety for generalized and rod contact. The ordered pass is environment-wide and skips rod/generalized rows, so an island-local no-rod trigger cannot establish mixed-island safety. The replay's separate witness/lever-arm behavior also remains unqualified. The W component of the intermediate residual is repurposed as the replay signal in this diagnostic; it is not a convergence measure.

Because the targeted completion and residual gates failed, the preregistered 100-step and mixed-contact follow-ups were not run. No timing or performance claim is made. Next, inspect how the Wave32 impulse state, response columns, friction projection and per-contact velocity changes are consumed by ordered replay, using the retained thick-box candidate as a fixed state; qualify any correction against the current main solver, independent residuals, geometric near misses and mixed contact before considering it for production. Both original experimental options remain disabled.

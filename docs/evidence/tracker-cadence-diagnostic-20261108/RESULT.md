# Mixed-status tracker cadence diagnostic

The native tracker on Apple M6 has one host-side sampling clock for the batch. In this direct synthetic-status diagnostic, one environment is marked failed on the first of four observations and the other succeeds; both succeed on subsequent observations. With a 20 ms observation period and a 25 Hz camera, the renderer's batch frame sequence is `0, 1, 1, 2` for both the failure and no-failure arms. Both environments' last tracked positions change at the second and fourth observations and hold at the third. Two runs of each arm produce byte-identical logs.

This confirms the shared schedule and shows why globally rewinding that schedule would also alter an accepted environment. It does **not** establish an incorrect reset policy or prove the correct sensor output after a real rejected physical step. The test injects status codes directly, advances both tracked body inputs artificially, reads the observation output before any real task completion, and does not simulate, reset, or resume physical state. The restored private and actor histories are covered separately by [the preceding report](../tracker-actor-history-20261108/RESULT.md). A full fix needs a declared accepted-time versus reset-time sensor contract and a real mixed-failure continuation, along with per-environment ownership of any cached frame and randomness required by that contract. GPU command failure and output publication remain open.

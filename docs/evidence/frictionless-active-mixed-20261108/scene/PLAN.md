# Moving scene-body frictionless contact

Before execution, extend the two-body invariant to a dynamic scene-owned box and a kinematic plane, retaining the same 1 kg box, 0.3 radian tilt, positive 0.1 mm gap, 1 ms duration, no gravity, friction, damping, CCD, forces or torques. An inert floating sphere exists solely because the owning world has an articulation; place it far from the plane and box. The plane translates at 0.4 m/s in x, while the box has x velocity -1, 0, +1 m/s and optionally normal velocity -1 m/s. Run zero-normal sliding and closing conditions with two velocity iterations in both baseline and isolated candidate. Expect exact free translation of each body in non-closing cases; require contacts, velocity and angular changes below 1e-6, successful repeatable runs, and positive normal impulse for the closing controls. For active contact, independently reconstruct the normal equation from captured world witness, initial body pose and final scene-body velocity, then compare to reported residual within 2e-5 m/s, and check unit-mass momentum against normal impulse within 2e-5 N s. Require candidate normal residual below 0.002 m/s and no effect of tangential velocity on normal/angular response within 2e-5. Record any baseline or candidate failure and compare energy; do not treat a kinematic plane as an isolated two-dynamic-body momentum test. Follow with dynamic-dynamic and rod only if the controls pass. No production patch is implied by this experiment.

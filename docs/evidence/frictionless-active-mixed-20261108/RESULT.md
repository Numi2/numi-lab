# Active and moving-body checks; mixed-island gate fails

The isolated zero-friction candidate from [the prior failure](../frictionless-oblique-contact-20261108/RESULT.md) is **not promoted**. It passes the new active rigid and moving-scene checks, but fails the preregistered mixed rod–rigid normal-residual gate in two of three zero-friction cases. The accepted worst case independently has a 0.453348804 m/s rigid-row residual against the existing 0.002 m/s diagnostic limit. The runtime reports success. This is evidence of an unresolved accepted mixed-contact response, not a force-qualified simulation.

## Active free articulated box

At two tilt angles and three independent tangential velocities, the 1 kg free box approaches a static plane at 1 m/s from a 0.1 mm gap. The actual evaluated contact targets, impulses, witness points and final velocity were captured. An independent world-space point-velocity calculation reconstructs normal complementarity residuals within `5.14e-9` m/s of the candidate solver status and checks total impulse against the unit-mass normal-momentum change within `8.95e-8` N s. Maximum candidate residual is `8.821e-5` m/s at the existing two velocity iterations and `3.50e-8` m/s at eight or 32. The iteration sweep diagnoses the fixture; it is not a proposed global iteration increase. Varying tangent speed changes no candidate normal/angular output in this fixture. All runs completed and repeated exactly.

The baseline reaches normal residuals up to 1.9263 m/s in this set, and its normal/angular response varies with tangential speed by up to 5.658 in the mixed units of the recorded velocity components. The independently computed kinetic-energy change ranges up to +0.3554 J for the baseline; in the candidate cases it ranges from -0.1507 to -0.1396 J. This is an accounting diagnostic for the stated discrete fixture, not a general energy-conservation or force-calibration claim.

## Moving plane and scene-owned rigid box

With a kinematic plane moving at 0.4 m/s along x and a dynamic, tilted scene-owned box, three non-closing velocities are unchanged by the candidate, while three closing cases produce a normal response. The independently reconstructed candidate normal residual is `8.823e-5` m/s and the normal-impulse balance error is below `2.99e-8` N s. The original runtime changes the zero-normal-motion box velocity/spin in two of the three sliding conditions. This moving-plane fixture is not an isolated two-dynamic-body conservation test.

## Mixed rod and rigid contact

The retained mixed fixture has a free sphere, a rod, and a plane in one island with two rod rows and one rigid row. At incoming normal speeds -1, 0, and +1 m/s, the isolated candidate reports zero-friction residuals `0.453348815`, `0.004494271`, and `0.000777123` m/s. The first two fail the 0.002 m/s gate, despite accepted, repeatable steps. For the -1 m/s case, both rod impulses are zero and the rigid normal witness lies directly below the sphere centre; computing final vertical speed minus the evaluated target independently gives `0.453348804` m/s. This does not independently certify the other rod rows. All zero-friction tangential impulses are zero and the largest independently checked momentum-balance error is `4.47e-8` N s. Finite-friction candidate and baseline results are identical in all three matched cases. The initial geometry overlaps and has a nonzero stabilization target; zero initial normal speed is not a force-free control.

The first mixed run inadvertently inherited composition's default `(0,0,-9.81)` m/s² gravity, despite the component's zero-gravity setting. Its 0.00981 N s apparent momentum discrepancy was the unaccounted external gravity impulse, not proof of a contact defect. Those results are retained under `default-gravity-*`; [mixed/AMENDMENT.md](mixed/AMENDMENT.md) records the discovery and the explicit-zero-gravity rerun without changing the gate. The same worst residual remains in the corrected run.

The active, scene and mixed plans precede their respective tests. Their sources, raw outputs, analysis and logs are retained in [active](active), [scene](scene), and [mixed](mixed). The original solver source, candidate shader and runtime have the identities recorded in the prior report and this directory's `runtime-sha256.txt`; all tests ran on Apple M6 over `ssh numi`. Checks are limited to one-step fixture state and selected evidence, not full owner rollback, general rods, calibrated forces, or arbitrary contact paths. Both experimental CCD and AL contact options remain disabled.

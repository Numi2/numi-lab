# Multi-step stretch and attachment trajectories

Before considering the isolated Metal prototype for integration, run 20 consecutive 1 ms implicit steps on three fixed cases: a nine-node straight prestrained chain with a displaced/moving interior node, a nine-node curved chain with fixed hard ends, and a seventeen-node curved chain with a moving hard end and interior compliant anchor. Carry each accepted GPU position and velocity into the next physical step; do not reset the chain or substitute a reference state. Use an independently advanced dense FP64 full-Newton reference for each trajectory. Fail on a rejected step or unqualified reference. At every accepted step compare all node positions within 1e-6 m, each support reaction within max(1e-6 N, 1% of its reference norm), and independently recomputed local free-node force within 1e-5 N. Report cumulative support impulse, the sum of external support force dotted with target displacement, kinetic and stored stretch/attachment energy, and reference differences; accumulated work must agree within max(1e-9 J, 2% of the reference magnitude). Do not use energy dissipation as a substitute for the force and position gates.

Repeat the complete GPU trajectory from identical initial state and compare captured bytes. This tests only the standalone stretch/attachment prototype and host-carried state, not resident MetalWorld ownership, native GPU transactions, bend, twist, rod contacts, calibrated materials or surgical behavior. Do not promote it to production based on this result. Keep the directional CCD and augmented-Lagrangian options disabled.

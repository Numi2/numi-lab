# Vector coupled stretch and mixed-attachment diagnostic

Before any Metal promotion, extend the incremental FP32 approach to three-dimensional chain geometry. Solve the nonlinear implicit stretch energy with local block Gauss-Newton and a tridiagonal block solve, hard attachment elimination, and compliant point attachments. Use an objective-decreasing line search; reject singular, nonfinite, or nonconvergent cases. Check final node positions within 1e-6 m and each hard and soft attachment reaction within max(1e-6 N, 1% of reference norm). Independently recompute local force residuals and compare with a separate FP64 dense full-Newton solve, reporting maximum local residual and its chosen force scale. Numerical force gates are for this synthetic discrete system and do not establish measured material accuracy.

Preregister straight, rigidly rotated, curved and displaced/moving chains, 3/9/17 nodes, a moving hard target, one compliant endpoint, and an interior compliant anchor. Use the physical material from the axial witness, no contact and no bending or torsional energy; the latter omission is explicit. Hard constraints must stay at their targets, and support reactions include inertial and elastic contributions. Preserve failures, iteration counts and objective history. This is CPU research, not a production solver, a general rod, a performance result or a surgical qualification. Subsequent Metal work must add bending, twist, contacts, moving-body coupling and transaction checks before promotion.

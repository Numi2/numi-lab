# Two-phase candidate: moving-link friction and resident failure

The unchanged candidate from CCD_FRICTION_PREDICTIVE was tested on a freely jointed moving link with the COM-coincident parent and child from CCD_REVOLUTE: mass 1 and 0.5 kg, child z-inertia 0.05 kg m², a 0.2 m x-offset sphere and 0.01 m radius, friction 0.5, no gravity, 10 ms step and two sweeps. Initial x velocity is 1 m/s and two parent/joint speed splits each produce -1 m/s contact-normal speed. The independent planar impulse reference has `Wnn = 1/1.5 + 0.2²/0.05`, `Wnt = 0.2*0.01/0.05`, `Wtt = 1/1.5 + 0.01²/0.05`; positive sliding gives `Jt = -0.5 Jn` and `Jn = 1/(Wnn-0.5 Wnt)`. The work reference uses the initial contact velocities and this matrix. Before execution, the gates were 0.005 N s normal impulse, 0.0025 N s tangent impulse, 0.005 J work and the unchanged local maximum-dissipation gate.

Both impacts and the rest and separating controls accepted and replayed. Each impact produced normal 0.690865636 N s versus reference 0.691244240, tangential -0.345432818 versus -0.345622120 N s, normal residual about 0.000548 m/s, and work errors at most 0.000144 J. Friction impulse exactly follows `-0.5 Jn` at printed precision while final tangential slip remains positive. Rest and separation have zero impulse and work. These are single-contact planar, shared-COM and limited-material fixtures, not broad articulated friction qualification.

A separate, explicitly test-only shader injects an unsupported one-axis cone only when a sentinel target induces slip, after 33 or 257 manifolds have populated. The 33-contact queue flags are 0 and the 257-contact flags are 8 (`MR_ISLAND_WORK_DISTRIBUTED`); both failure steps return status 3, restore q/v/scene and complete allocated manifold count/header/point buffers byte-for-byte, and continue identically to independent controls. Separate normal-shader target controls accept and apply the target. The injected cone is not exposed by current production collision compilation; the test demonstrates these bounded failure paths, not production reachability.

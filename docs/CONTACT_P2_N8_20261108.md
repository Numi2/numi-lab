# Independent P2 n=8 spatial comparison

The order-18 n=2 and n=4 trajectories passed their temporal and sampled
quadrature/energy/work checks, but n=2→4 failed the unchanged 0.25%
force and impulse spatial gates. This n=8 study holds geometry, material,
density, gravity, initial gap, regularized continuum plane energy,
consistent mass and order-18 surface quadrature fixed. P2 and its quadrature
are a different spatial discretization from the production nodal P1 law.

## Prediction and gates registered before execution

Run complete 10 ms coarse/fine DOP853 trajectories with maximum steps 16/8
µs (larger than the n=4 study), rtol 1e-8/1e-10, displacement atol
1e-12/1e-13 m, velocity atol 1000 times greater. Time accuracy is not
assumed: the 0.25% force-bin, total-impulse and contact-work temporal
limits, sampled gap, positive Jacobian, momentum, energy and work checks
remain those of the n=4 plan. At all 0.1 ms snapshots compare order-18
force to independent Duffy-64 within 0.25%, and Duffy-48 vs Duffy-64 within
0.05%, each normalized by the fine n=8 peak 1 ms force bin. Reject
nonpositive order-18 gap and volume Jacobian at ODE evaluation stages and
require positive sampled Duffy gaps.

Only after the n=8 prerequisites pass, compare fine n=4→8 force bins and
total impulse to the unchanged 0.25% limits, normalized by n=8 peak bin
and total impulse. No spatial outcome is assumed. Passing one pair does not
prove converged spatial force, continuum clearance or production,
hard-contact, replay or material qualification.

The mesh has 3,072 tets and 4,913 P2 DOFs, eight times n=4 elements. Based
on n=4 104/237 s, element scaling and the doubled step cap suggest roughly
7/16 minutes, with considerable uncertainty. Run sequentially under
900/1,800-second process-group timeouts, stop on execution failure, one
BLAS thread and no GPU. Capture source/input hashes, stdout, stderr, process
RSS and host load/memory before and after. Preflight reports 16 GiB RAM,
85% system memory free and 25 GiB free disk; load 3.54/3.98/3.57 with
concurrent clang processes. These are resources, not performance evidence.

## Result

Both runs completed in 432.9 s and 892.8 s. Coarse/fine force-bin and
impulse differences were 8.005e-7 and 1.385e-7; contact-work difference was
1.085e-5 of its declared scale. The maximum order-18 versus Duffy-64 force
difference was 0.01226% of the fine peak bin; Duffy-48 versus Duffy-64
differed by 0.0000188%. These pass their declared checks. Minimum sampled
gap was 92.16 µm, and sampled Jacobian, momentum, energy and work checks
pass. The results do not certify clearance between quadrature points or
solver evaluations.

The prerequisite-gated P2 n=4→8 force-bin difference was **9.685%**, and
the impulse difference **1.383%**, normalized by fine n=8 peak bin and
impulse. Both fail the unchanged 0.25% spatial limits. The original n=2→4
failure remains retained. No pair alone proves a converged spatial force
reference, and no production, replay, hard-contact or material claim follows.

Peak RSS was approximately 779 MB. Host memory-free snapshots ranged from
84% to 86%; load averages at the start were 5.21/4.33/3.71 and at the end
6.29/5.18/4.38. Execution times are resource observations, not product
performance claims. [Evidence](evidence/contact-p2-n8-order18-20261108/)
retains all sampled states, inputs, hashes, gates, host snapshots and logs.
No GPU was used.

## Integration assessment

The research change touches the shared wave solver. Its extra bounded normal pivots apply only to rod-plus-articulation wave packets of at most 32 constraints for which every cone reports exactly zero friction and no normal-impulse cap. It retains the final normal certificate; other packet cases use the existing update. A separate guard rejects a mixed wave contact with nonzero impulse when either friction coefficient is positive, returning unsupported. That is a material behavior change: frictional loading which previously published a result can now reject, and zero-impulse cases alone are not evidence of valid frictional dynamics. Other routes, cap semantics, larger and distributed islands, and finite/negative cone admission need explicit audit and native qualification; no claim is made that the guard covers every mixed solver route.

The earlier resident rejection and neutral continuation compare captured q/v, scene, rod and manifold fields, but hidden state and general reset byte-exactness remain outside that result. The corrected 18-case run independently replays its emitted and captured resident state but does not exercise a newly rejected step or prove general rollback. Main integration would require a separately reviewed scope and API/status implications, complete supported-domain and queue routing audit, matched accepted and rejected resident tests with preexisting caches, noncontact and ordinary loaded controls, existing rigid/articulated/rod regressions, and force/work and friction qualification. The guard-only containment separately rejects useful approaching contacts; neither it nor this candidate completes the requested useful-impact repair. Keep both experimental CCD and augmented-Lagrangian options disabled.

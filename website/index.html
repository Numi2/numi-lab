Long-term research goals

Aims

Neural networks

Build learning systems that form useful world models through real consequence, not only static data.

Physics simulation

Make high-fidelity physical worlds fast and accessible enough to become a daily instrument of invention.

Biology

Understand how cells, bodies, and ecologies coordinate across scales by building testable living systems in silico.

Molecular systems

Connect molecular rules to development, behavior, and repair without losing the mechanisms in between.

Artificial life

Create open worlds where new forms of organization can persist, adapt, and surprise us.

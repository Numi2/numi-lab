# Ankle phase sweep with uniform contact-conditioned hip feedback

The uniform feedback screen showed continuous support through 4–6 s in eight closely related starts, but the observed median late-stance ankle interior-angle change was about +0.0037 degrees: effectively flat and not a meaningful related-corvid sign match. Without changing the other gait or hip feedback, retain two unchanged controls and test stance-phase ankle command amplitudes 0.08, 0.09, 0.11, 0.12, 0.13 and 0.14 versus the 0.10 control, with the same gentle contact-conditioned transition. Exact pre-intervention parity and controls are required. As an engineering screen, require no simultaneous foot unloading, no toe caps, no nonfoot ground impulse, at least 0.05 m forward travel, meaningful late-stance ankle median >0.1 degree and the other selected sign hypotheses, and <=1.1 times each matched foot's loaded contact-speed integral versus the uniform control. These are not crow-specific numerical targets, and the changing-contact speed integral is not tracked material-point slip. The one correlated start per setting and development seed cannot establish biological gait or mission qualification.


Pre-run refinement: a hallux-touchdown-to-digit-3-off analysis of 40 assessable bouts yielded lane medians of about +0.98 degrees late-stance ankle change on the left and -0.42 degrees on the right; the earlier near-zero pooled result included eight hallux-only bouts. Evaluate the toe-defined late-stance ankle median separately for each foot, requiring >0.1 degree on both for the engineering screen, and report incomplete bouts, ties and all data. The one-species speed/surface/anatomy differences and very similar starts still prevent a calibrated or independent gait claim.

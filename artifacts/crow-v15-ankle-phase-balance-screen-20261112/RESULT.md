# Ankle amplitude screen: no candidate passed

Two control lanes matched the existing uniform-gait trajectories exactly through all 300 endpoints and 4,800 contact substeps; every candidate matched its own reference through the first 200 endpoints and 3,200 substeps. Complete 4–6 second toe and contact coverage showed no toe caps, nonfoot contact or solver/overflow errors. The 0.09 amplitude lane produced positive median late-stance ankle changes on both feet (+0.421 and +0.161 degrees) with no simultaneous foot unloading, but its left loaded-contact-speed integral was 1.177 times its matched reference, exceeding the predeclared 1.1 limit. Other amplitudes failed the phase, support, forward-progress or contact-speed screens. No setting is promoted. This contact-speed integral uses changing collision points and is not tracked material slip. All settings are single correlated development starts, not independent animals; the 0.1-degree cutoff is an engineering choice, not a magpie or crow calibration.

Correction to biological interpretation: the reference describes renewed ankle flexion immediately before recovery after the mid-stance extension. This screen's positive late-minus-mid quintile gate was a partial phase proxy, not the complete corvid ankle target. A later ten-bin analysis of the original uniform gait retains the near-lift-off reversal separately.

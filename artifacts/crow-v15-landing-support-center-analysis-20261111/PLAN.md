# Support center and pitch: retrospective development analysis

Question: before the observed post-capture falls, where is the vertical-load-weighted perch contact center relative to the articulated whole-body center of mass, and how do pitch and pitch rate change? Compare the matched reference and rejected descent variant, including both initially opposed-toe-supported and non-opposed cases, all first episodes with landing-hold phase, and the temporarily supported non-failing lane. The prediction is that tipping may coincide with a sustained offset of support from center of mass; an association cannot by itself establish a cause or prove a corrective direction.

Use accepted endpoint q/v with the previously replay-checked v15 forward-kinematics probe, all reported 16 substep perch impulses, and the first-episode phase and failure bounds already archived. Compute vertical-impulse-weighted contact center and approximate net contact torque using the endpoint center of mass; compare late supported windows and pitch/rate. Require no missing substeps or overflows and independently check the reconstructed center for selected replay frames. Endpoint COM is an approximation for an interval and center of pressure can lie outside a purely geometric support estimate under friction; this is not full dynamics, muscle force, a causal intervention or a biological claim. Do not tune gains from a single falling lane.

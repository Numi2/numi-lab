# Landing pitch drift in retained failures

In the used-seed earlier-wing full-sequence run, all six tilt-terminated first episodes crossed 0.1 rad of pitch before later crossing 1.1 rad; four diverged negative and two positive. Five of these had previously recorded extended periods of opposing toe contact under the engineering support screen. Lanes 0 and 6 never crossed 0.1 rad but did not achieve a sustained supported landing, so low tilt alone is not success. The recorded endpoint pitch trajectory is evidence of a two-sided balance failure, not proof of its root cause. It does not identify applied joint torque or establish that toe friction is the limiting factor. Sequence/status fields are raw internal controller diagnostics and should not be interpreted as causal proof. Data come from the previously rejected earlier-wing development trial, not the new full-sequence trial or an unused layout.

# Staged foot-lift amplitude sweep

In preliminary staged-lift trace samples, the main left sole cleared but several distal toe boxes remained within about 0.1 mm of the perch; right-foot opposing contacts persisted longer than with abrupt lifting. These are preliminary samples, not a completed successful trial. With the same +0.22 rad hip shift, delay toe opening for 35 intervals and ramp knee bend over 20 intervals. Compare +0.55, +0.65, +0.75 and +0.50 rad knee offsets in two starts each, with ankle offset -0.5 times knee. These are within the nominal default joint position bounds. Measure full substep contacts, toe/sole clearance, unsupported duration, weight support, terminal regrip, endpoint speed caps and failures. This constructed stationary screen is not an autonomous or biological qualification.

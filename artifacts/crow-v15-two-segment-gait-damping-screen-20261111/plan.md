# Grounded velocity feedback screen

Neutral stance during the delayed start allowed three lanes to settle; one perturbed initial start remained in motion and failed walking. In the two-second standing interval only, test bounded joint-position feedback from the already observed forward and vertical body velocities, with no external forces. For odd lanes 1,3,5,7, use gains .8,.4,0,1.6 respectively; hip target opposes forward velocity and knee target opposes half vertical velocity, bounded to +/-0.25 and active only with accepted foot support. Even lanes reproduce the original gait and odd lane 5 is the matched neutral-stance control. Follow with the same two-second gait. Check every substep contact and calibrated whole-foot endpoint clearance, forward motion, full support and alternating swing; retain all failures. This is a parameter and mechanism screen on development starts, not robust gait qualification, a substitute for a readiness gate, or a full sequence.

# Right rear proximal toe effort counterfactual

Retained full-substep evidence shows the stance right rear proximal toe drive held its +0.02 N m configured cap throughout intervals 280–360 in the latest matched control and candidate, while the rear proximal joint opened and bilateral grip was lost. A prior validated observer independently found the same saturation near intervals 300–305; this does not by itself establish causation. Test a bounded plant-parameter counterfactual on the identical constructed perch state and controller: increase only the modeled right rear proximal toe effort limit from 0.02 to 0.04 N m in both identical lanes. All other joint limits, drives, friction, contacts, solver, targets and initial states remain unchanged; do not change poses or inject forces. The doubled limit is an exploratory sensitivity test, not a measured crow torque or a biologically calibrated model. Compare to lane 0 of the archived unchanged 0.02 N m trial. Since the parameter differs from the initial state, trajectory equality after the first loaded actuation is not assumed and differences are not a matched same-episode intervention.

Before seeing outcomes, require exact source/model mapping and initial q/v verification, 450 accepted states and all 32 substep toe velocities, drive efforts and contact records, no observer errors, no nonfoot impulse, no toe speed threshold, at least 44 consecutive qualified unilateral release intervals, then 100 terminal intervals of bilateral opposing supported grip and no root fall. Report torque saturation, actual joint angles, contact loss and all failed criteria. Preserve both identical treatment lanes, complete trace, source, binary and all failed results in Git. A passing constructed test would not establish walking, takeoff, aerial landing, biological realism or a full mission; contact/state epochs remain independently unverified.

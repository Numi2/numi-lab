# Two-segment toe free-space admission

The existing toe research body uses one rigid box and one hinge per digit; it cannot conform to a perch through multiple phalangeal joints. Explore two equal, independently driven rigid segments per digit with bounded flexion, preserving each toe's previous overall straight length and mass. Split each digit's rigid mass and inertia so the neutral model has the same aggregate mass, center of mass and inertia as the original v15 body; retain zero world damping on the added toe bodies. Compare neutral-state articulated kinetic/potential energy and momentum with the no-toe original for 36 moving reference configurations, verify repeated native GPU dynamics for 12 lanes against an independent CPU dynamics calculation, and require finite movement in all 16 toe coordinates. This free-space admission is not contact, grip, walking, flight, navigation or biological qualification. The precise dimensions and two-segment structure are exploratory rather than species-calibrated; no route controller or scene is changed by this prototype.

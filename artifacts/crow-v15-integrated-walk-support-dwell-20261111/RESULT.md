# Support-dwell gait rejected

On the same eight used development starts, three accepted opposite-foot load samples before swing removed the 30 unsupported contact substeps and two both-clear endpoints in lane 3. However, lane 5 regressed from zero to 135 unsupported substeps and eight both-clear endpoints, and its qualifying right-foot swing bouts dropped from three to two. Other lanes retained three bouts per foot and no unsupported substeps. Forward displacement was 3.33–4.05 cm, compared with roughly 3 cm in the reference. No nonfoot ground impulse or sampled toe speed cap occurred during the walking window. Coverage was complete at 16 substeps; endpoint clearance is not swept.

All eight first episodes survived the 600-step horizon, which ended before any waypoint. This candidate is rejected and does not qualify walking, takeoff, flight, landing, unseen layouts, or biological realism.

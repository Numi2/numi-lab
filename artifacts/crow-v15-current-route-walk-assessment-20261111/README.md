# Current route controller walking assessment

The earlier two-segment walking screen used a different, earlier gait. This assessment instead uses the retained current support-gate route run, whose ground gait starts at four seconds after an additional contact-gated settling period. It reconstructs foot/toe poses for every lane; lane 1 matches native replay positions within 4.92e-9 m and quaternion norm within 2.98e-8 after the verified observer/replay offset.

All eight development starts advanced 3.11–3.40 cm across the two-second gait window. Each had three left and four right qualifying swing bouts, with an unloaded swing foot, endpoint toe/sole clearance over 1 mm, and at least half nominal weight in upward impulse through the opposite foot. The recorded bouts interleave in these starts. No full control interval lacked foot normal impulse or showed nonfoot ground contact. However, 25–27 individual physics substeps per lane had no measured foot normal impulse; at step 291, reconstructed foot and toe boxes for both sides were more than 1 mm above the ground in every lane, confirming an unintended airborne phase at that sampled pose. Lane 7 also had both feet clear at step 293. The other zero-impulse substeps may include transitions or unloading, but all fail a strict continuous-positive-impulse requirement. These data do not establish continuous between-pose clearance, slip bounds, robustness to new starts, reliable takeoff or a complete autonomous route. They are teacher-guided development traces, not biological validation.

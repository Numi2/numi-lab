# Load-scaled leg swing on development walking starts

The original experimental gait gates a leg swing on whether the opposite contact group reports more than 0.05 N, a very small fraction of the approximately 4.4 N modeled body weight. Increasing knee swing led to frequent no-foot-contact substeps. The most recent 0.8 s cycle/short-pulse trial did not correct this. A boolean contact indication is not evidence that the stance foot is carrying the bird. Last-substep contact samples in one retained candidate lane show the opposite foot force varying from near zero to several newtons during swing; whether this is causal remains unproven.

Test a continuous load-scaled swing using the existing accepted opposite-foot force instead of its 0.05 N boolean. The multiplier rises smoothly from zero at 0.5 N to one at 2.2 N, approximately half modeled body weight; retain the original 0.50 s cycle, 0.60 knee multiplier, original hip and ankle ratios and gait time window. Apply the multiplier to the whole signed swing so hip, knee and ankle commands remain coordinated. These are exploratory control thresholds, not crow measurements. No other physics, sensor, flight controller, material, start, seed or route changes.

Use both previously exposed development seeds with eight lanes and compare to the original gait: require at least 15% greater bilateral knee range in at least six lanes, no more than 33 total no-foot-normal-contact substeps, no nonfoot contacts, no extra simulator errors or terminations, and forward net displacement in all lanes. Require logged pre-walk endpoint and contact parity through step 199. Preserve all native traces, contact coverage, and failure results. A pass would only justify wider development tests, not establish full walk/takeoff/landing, unseen-layout performance or biological realism.

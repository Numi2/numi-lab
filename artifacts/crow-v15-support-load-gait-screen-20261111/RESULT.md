# Opposite-foot force-scaled walking: failed development screen

The trial smoothly scaled the signed leg command using the prior accepted opposite-foot force from 0.5 to 2.2 N, retained the larger knee multiplier and original cycle, and kept the v15 model, materials, starts and frozen flight carrier unchanged. Both 300-step teacher-off native runs completed successfully; logged pre-walk states, actions and contact summaries matched the original through step 199 in all 16 lanes.

Despite greater than 15% mean bilateral knee span in all lanes, the candidate had 3,052 no-foot-normal-contact physics substeps in the walking windows versus 33 for the original, no qualified left or right swing bouts in any lane, and negative forward displacement in all 16 lanes. Neither arm reported nonfoot contact. This fails the preregistered support and displacement gates and is rejected. The observed joint span alone is therefore not evidence of walking. The data do not establish why the candidate failed; delayed or rapidly varying force feedback and inadequate weight transfer remain hypotheses.

The raw matched traces, shape and contact checks, body measurements, failed candidate shader, compiled library and source patch are retained. This is a development gait screen, not evidence of successful whole-journey walking, flight, landing, unused layouts or biological calibration.

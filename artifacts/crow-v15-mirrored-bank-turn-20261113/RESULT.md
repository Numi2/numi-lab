On the used development seed, both candidate first episodes remained airborne for all 1800 ticks and satisfied the registered contact, speed, height, effort and target-bound checks. Unchanged lanes and pre-command candidate prefixes reproduced the reference exactly. Neither meets the registered simultaneous 0.10 rad heading and 0.20 m displacement requirement. This shows a more stable bank response with large sideslip, not a qualified turn or frozen controller.

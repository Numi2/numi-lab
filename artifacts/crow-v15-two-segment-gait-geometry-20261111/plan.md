# All-lane accepted-state foot geometry

Qualify an offline instrument that reconstructs the two-segment model's accepted body poses from retained q/v using the native FP64 kinematics, without advancing the simulation. On the existing baseline run, compare its foot/toe poses against every corresponding replay pose in lane 1; require position and orientation absolute errors <=1e-5. Compute each foot's lowest vertical clearance above the flat ground from the foot box and all eight toe boxes. Combine those endpoint gaps with recorded all-substep foot normal and upward impulses. A qualifying swing interval needs >1 mm clearance, own-foot normal impulse <=1e-7 N s, other-foot upward impulse >=half the body weight integrated over .02 s, no nonfoot impulse, and a bout requires at least three consecutive intervals. Apply the validated instrument to every lane of the forthcoming uniform +.50 development gait. Report all bouts, alternating order, motion, support and calibration errors. Endpoint geometry is not continuous swept-volume clearance.

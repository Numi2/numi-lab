# Contact-conditioned hip placement screen

Static forward kinematics at the retained negative-pitch start predicts that increasing modeled hip pitch moves the foot center rearward. That is only geometry: a loaded foot may remain anchored and move the body instead. Test the hypothesis in the native contact simulation with the original baseline and three bounded incremental hip-target offsets 0.08, 0.16 and 0.24 rad, paired with each initial pitch +0.04 and -0.04 rad. Target ramps over 15 control intervals after step 10 and applies only while at least one foot is loaded. The original position-feedback drive remains active. This is an engineering diagnostic, not a measured crow joint trajectory.

All eight lane starts must exactly match the original reference before intervention. Record every interval and all 32 substeps, opposed toe normals, support, nonfoot contact, toe-speed caps, and falls. Each candidate must pass both initial pitch signs under the same final-100-interval support and pitch gate, without nonfoot contacts, speed caps, missing observations, or solver errors; later tests must examine new initial conditions and dynamic approach. No one start proves robustness and no static-start success alone proves autonomous landing or biological realism. Do not alter the baseline or promote from this screen alone.

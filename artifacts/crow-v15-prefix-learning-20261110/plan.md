# Exploratory current-body route-prefix learning

Before training, use only the first physically reached routing prefix of the development J recovery episode `crow-v15-student-recovery-20261110`, ending before its fifth-waypoint transition and excluding its landing segment. It contains a teacher-off student trajectory until step 1100 followed by privileged recovery; the accepted first 1099 physical and actor states exactly match the teacher-off control, and the recovery physically reached the later waypoints without a recorded failure. Label every sampled state using the guide's corrective command, weight student-visited samples fourfold and balance stages 0–4. Preserve the existing head/beak actor outputs, the v15 body and frozen lower-level flight controller. Exclude any failed episode and all terminal landing data from this exploratory training set.

Use every fifth eligible step, reserve every fifth such sample as an in-trajectory diagnostic (not an independent validation set), train the existing actor at fixed 0.0001 Adam for 120 epochs, and record initial and final losses and output pack. Afterward evaluate teacher off, one uninterrupted full start each on the already used development J and I seeds against the original actor, including waypoint progress, contact/solver failures, min clearance and landing support. Fitting accuracy alone is not success. A single source trajectory is inadequate for generalization or qualification; genuinely unused K/L layouts and seeds remain sealed until a development policy and its evidence justify final testing.

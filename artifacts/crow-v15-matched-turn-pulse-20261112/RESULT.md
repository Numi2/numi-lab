# Same-start wing-turn pulse

All eight experimental lanes and their same-lane obstacle-free controls matched state endpoints through trace submission 602; the six pulse lanes first differed at 603. The two zero-pulse lanes remained bitwise equal in recorded state and ground-contact traces through submission 1051. All episodes reached the 1050-transition horizon, without a reported contact failure, nonfoot ground impulse or recorded joint-speed cap after submission 600. Contact traces remained equal to each paired control because both birds were airborne; this is not a continuous obstacle-clearance measurement.

The +/-0.04, +/-0.08 and +/-0.12 differential flap pulses shifted final world y relative to matched controls by approximately -0.412/+0.419 m, -0.823/+0.834 m and -1.233/+1.248 m, respectively. At submission 650, their roll differences were +0.011/-0.012, +0.023/-0.025 and +0.034/-0.037 rad; by 1050 these roll differences were close to zero and lateral velocities were within 0.001 m/s of their matched controls. Final heading differences were only approximately +0.00035 to +0.00143 rad, so these pulses demonstrate sideways authority and recovery toward the original straight-motion state, not successful heading turns. They do not qualify a frozen flight controller.

The arms share launch, speed, altitude, empirical aerodynamics and start with their own same-lane control. They do not all share one another’s configuration: the retained wing shader has lane-specific unsteady-direction blends 0 for lanes 0–1 and 0.60 for lanes 2–7, and the speed/height commands differ across lane pairs. Empirical unsteady and additional yaw terms are not calibrated against crow data. Trace submission includes the separate pre-reset warmup; reported episode count does not.

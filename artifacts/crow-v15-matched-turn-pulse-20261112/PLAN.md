# Paired bounded wing-turn pulse on the continuous-launch v15 body

Use the retained obstacle-free trial as same-lane, same-seed controls, with identical launch, low-level controller, policy, inputs and per-lane speed/height commands. Introduce only a bounded antisymmetric flap command pulse after 12 s: lanes 0–1 zero, lanes 2–3 +/- 0.04, lanes 4–5 +/- 0.08, lanes 6–7 +/- 0.12. Smooth the pulse on over 12–12.5 s and off over 17.5–18 s; continue to 21 s to observe whether roll and trajectory recover. No direct body moment. Ground remains active; five course collisions are disabled. Confirm the full same-lane accepted-state/contact prefix before intervention, then measure paired path, yaw, roll, speed, height, speed caps and contacts. Counterfactual comparisons within each lane prevent random-start confounding; signs and gains across lanes remain exploratory. This is not a heading-target controller, qualification, autonomous navigation or biological validation. The policy-runner warmup creates a one-transition offset between trace submission and episode counter.

The retained research wing shader uses lane-dependent unsteady force directions: lanes 0–1 use blend 0 and lanes 2–7 use blend 0.60. The pre-existing commanded speed and altitude also differ across lanes. Each pulse is compared only with its own same-lane control, so the control pair shares these settings; results cannot be treated as samples of a uniform aerodynamic model. The wing unsteady and yaw-moment terms are empirical research approximations, not fitted crow aerodynamics.

# Temporary wing adjustment: first development seed

With teacher off, both 0.55-rad leg-push candidates remained unloaded from interval 377 through 500 without recorded nonfoot impulse, physical or observer errors, toe-speed threshold, or missing required substeps. In intervals 450–500 their minimum heights were 0.408 m (+0.04-rad boost) and 0.564 m (+0.08-rad boost), both above the registered 0.25-m floor; the unboosted earlier +0.55 candidate dipped to 0.223 m. Lane 6 matched the earlier unboosted state/action/contact stream exactly through interval 395, before the adjustment. Both push/held-leg pairs matched through step 368; all six untouched lanes matched all 500 steps.

The +0.04-rad held-leg control recontacted at interval 407 while the pushed candidate stayed unloaded. During the registered release window, wing targets were identical; a supplemental post-run check revealed later differences: greater than 0.00001 rad first at step 464 (lane 6) and 457 (lane 7), with maxima 0.000106 and 0.000178 rad respectively through 500. Consequently full-window command identity fails, and the short run alone is not proof of a strictly matched leg-only effect. The +0.08 held-leg control remained unloaded. Wings already moved about 0.549 rad through first release, and modeled aerodynamic force contributed, so this is a coupled leg-wing comparison, not isolated leg-powered takeoff.

The 500-interval result is a used-seed development screen, not qualified flight, landing or navigation. Observer state/contact epoch timing for these candidate lanes is inferred from independently checked other lanes. Full exact traces, replay, inputs and failures are retained.

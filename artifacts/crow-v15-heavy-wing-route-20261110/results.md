# Legacy-policy development route failures

On the estimated-wing body with the frozen research flight law and old actor weights rebound to the new contract, none of three continuous development trials reached the first waypoint. The previously used layout I trial survived 4500 steps, with minimum accepted-step sampled obstacle clearance 0.148 m. Two previously used layout J starts struck the left wing against slalom A at steps 2162 and 2241, with minimum sampled clearances 0 and 0.00272 m respectively. All runs reported zero solver failed steps. These are physical routing failures, not solver failures; the policy is out of distribution and was not retrained for the modified body. First-contact states, waypoints, approximate clearance samples, inputs and hashes are retained in analysis.json. Policy output vectors are not the low-level applied commands. There is no supported landing and no unused-layout evaluation.

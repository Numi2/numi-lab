# Carrion-crow-guided stroke-shape diagnostic

A physical asymmetric wing-position waveform shifted the median dominant tail-COM acceleration peak from 9.2 Hz to 4.6 Hz in all four five-second windows of the replayed lane. Against the predeclared training-only score based on eight carrion-crow individuals, the score changed from 5.7653 to 0.3357 (lower is closer on peak and spectral concentration). The waveform was an exploratory engineering intervention, not measured wing kinematics. It is a data-guided result worth retaining, not a biological fit of the whole bird: the real sensor was on a tail feather, its flight labels were broad, and this proxy is rigid tail COM of one simulated lane. Two other individuals had already been inspected and are no longer untouched for this intervention.

All six candidate lanes reached interval 1600, with no recorded physical failure, nonfoot contact after intervention, missing substeps or logged solver errors. All 48 pre-intervention sampled states and policy outputs matched the same-binary sham. However, all three left/right pairs failed the prespecified flight screen: sample intervals 800–1600 had body-forward speed 0.054–0.196 m/s, below 0.5 m/s; final heading errors were 1.300–1.435 rad, above pi/12. Sampled heights in that interval were 1.020–1.065 m, and absolute sideslip was at most 0.077 rad. The original body moments remained active before 9 s. The wingstroke is not promoted, and neither the flight controller nor navigation is qualified.

The first attempted candidate failed policy contract validation before simulation because the switch changes the task header. Its output is retained as warped-failed; a prior incompatible-host preflight failure is also retained. The candidate was rerun with copied policies rebound to the changed fingerprint after exact actor weights and observation/action transforms were verified; the matched prefix provides an additional dynamic check. The random seed was already used in development, and sampled states do not establish full-interval safety. The recordings and limitations are documented in the neighboring carrion-biofit artifact.

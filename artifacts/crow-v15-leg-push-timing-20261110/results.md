# Leg extension timing sensitivity

In the single used J start, a 0.08-second extension ramp produced a short physical jump but still flew backward; root velocity was -0.399 m/s at step 700. The 0.12- and 0.20-second ramps did not generate unloaded liftoff during the leg-push window; the bird left the ground only after the existing timed wing fallback. Their lower backward speeds therefore do not demonstrate an improved leg-powered takeoff. No variant is promoted.

# Stance toe joint and command audit

In the retained constructed return test, both lanes are identical through zero-based interval 304. The stance (right) foretoe command is 1.2 rad; three of its six segment angles first reach at least 1.199 rad at interval 305. The v15 research body defines toe position limits of -0.3 to 1.2 rad, a 12 rad/s velocity limit and 0.02 N m effort limit in `crow-v15-two-segment-toe-prototype-20261111/probe.cpp` (source hashes recorded here). At interval 300, right fore/rear signed normal impulses were about +0.00106/-0.00143 N s. By 304, fore impulse was approximately 2.2e-13 N s, and by 306 four foretoe angles were near 1.2 rad. The right rear proximal angle declined from 0.262 rad at interval 300 to 0.080 rad at 306 while its position target remained 1.15 rad.

These are endpoint observations from one constructed trial. They establish loss of opposing contact with joint saturation and rear joint target error, but do not establish whether geometry, drive effort, contact forces, whole-body drift, numerical behavior, or a combination caused it. They do not justify simply increasing a limit, force or friction. Check substep actuator effort, toe/perch geometry and body dynamics before revising the model or controller.

# v15 wing mass audit

The native model reports total mass 0.450040 kg. Each distal wing body has mass 0.000140625 kg, and each wing's two intermediate connector bodies add 0.000020 kg. These are the modeled rigid-body masses, not measurements of a real bird. The published eLife supplementary dataset (https://cdn.elifesciences.org/articles/89968/elife-89968-supp3-v1.pdf, table 3a, American crow row) lists 30.8 g for a single wing of its 387.6 g crow entry; its notes explain that some values use scaling and close relatives. The modeled distal wing is approximately 219 times lighter than that reference value, even before scaling for body mass. The code's prior flight qualification is therefore qualification of an estimated lightweight-wing model, not a biologically validated dynamic bird. Changing wing mass, center of mass or inertia changes the plant and requires fresh mass/inertia validation, controller qualification, retraining and navigation tests; no such change or biological calibration is claimed here.

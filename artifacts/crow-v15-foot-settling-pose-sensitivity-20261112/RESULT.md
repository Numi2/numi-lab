# Coarse frozen-pose sensitivity

No one of 56 sampled touchdown poses per development seed had a qualifying candidate in the -0.05/0/+0.05 rad knee-by-ankle grid. A +0.05-rad ankle change alone lowered the front box group by a median 1.61 mm but shifted the rear group upward about 2.18–2.32 mm, outside the 0.5-mm preservation screen. Offsets are native coordinates; boxes, thresholds, fixed torso and unmodeled compliance constrain the inference. Zero offsets reproduced the prior geometry exactly.

A coarse grid cannot establish impossibility, and it does not simulate motor response or support. A separate post-hoc refined grid explores intermediate opposing knee/ankle offsets.

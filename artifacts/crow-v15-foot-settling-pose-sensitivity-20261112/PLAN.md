# Constrained knee/ankle geometry sensitivity at rear-toe loading

For each retained negative-ankle touchdown event in two used seeds, sample its first detected rear-toe loading pose and run native forward kinematics for knee and ankle coordinate offsets -0.05, 0, +0.05 rad in a full 3x3 grid. Hold torso and all other joints fixed. Measure oriented-box clearance for the sole and all eight toe segments. A geometric candidate must reduce middle-front-toe group clearance by at least 1 mm versus the unmodified pose, keep rear-group clearance within 0.5 mm of that pose, and keep all boxes above -0.5 mm relative to the ground plane. Report each candidate and per-event coverage, including controls and rejected poses. Validate the zero-offset reconstruction against the prior geometry audit. Angles are native joint coordinates, not anatomical angles. This is instantaneous frozen-pose geometry, not an achievable motion, a contact solver prediction, a validated crow measurement, a supported gait or a controller trial. Joint limits, actuators, other foot, balance and relative phase remain unchecked. Do not promote any candidate without dynamic matched controls.

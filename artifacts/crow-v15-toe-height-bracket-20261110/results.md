# Intermediate height and toe response

All 16 teacher-guided development starts survived until step 4500, but no qualifying bilateral foot-load streak exceeded two accepted steps and no start entered the landed phase. The preregistered loading prediction is contradicted. Before the perch, sampled toe angles remained below 0.000312 rad. For the preregistered condition of 25 steps with no foot/toe contact candidate, the every-step observed maximum was 0.01810 rad. The original analysis counted only nonzero final impulses as contact and yielded maxima 0.06968 and 0.06063 rad; retained trace inspection shows those misses coincided with zero-final-impulse contact candidates. Both analyses are retained, and neither establishes deliberate release after a sustained grip. No autonomy or biological qualification is claimed.

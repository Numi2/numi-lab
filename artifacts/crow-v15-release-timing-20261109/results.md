# Grip release timing

The 45-second arm reached all five waypoints, sustained bilateral opposing toe loads up to and including endpoint 2249, and lost right and left opposing toe loads at endpoints 2252 and 2253 after release. It then failed tilt at step 2396 with no supported final state. The 46-second arm had already lost bilateral grip before its scheduled release and failed tilt at step 2403. Neither passed the full sequence. Layout J was exposed during development and the model parameters are estimates.

# Paused-descent trial: supported slip, no grip

One development-layout J, privileged-teacher full-start exploratory trial used the contact-gated toe controller and paused descent while horizontally misaligned. The trajectory matched the reference through step 1305, reached all five waypoints, and recorded a minimum accepted obstacle clearance of 0.13897 m. The observer covered all 16 substeps without overflow. There were 75 bilateral half-weight support intervals, including 72 consecutive intervals (1.44 s). No supported opposing toe-force interval was observed. No non-foot perch impulse was observed; the first episode ended at step 2258 in tail-ground contact after support was lost. Automatic episode restart subsequently occurred. There was no failed native environment step.

At step 2160 the landing controller transitioned to phase 2 using contact load and toe closure counters, although the rear toes had not supplied the opposing forces needed to establish grip. Flight and leg actions were then suppressed. This result does not establish a supported landing, grip, release, robustness, autonomous navigation, or biological realism. The target offset and descent rule remain exploratory and are not production-qualified.

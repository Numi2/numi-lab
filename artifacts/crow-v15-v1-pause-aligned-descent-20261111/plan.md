# Pause descent progress while re-aligning

The selected 0.24 hover calibration reached the development-J perch area and made a brief toe contact but timed out with no bilateral support. Its high-level descent phase repeatedly fell back when alignment was momentarily outside the 0.04 m / 0.08 m/s guard. Test only changing the guard's off-condition from retracting progress by 0.002 per tick to holding progress; continue descending at 0.001 per tick only while the existing alignment and speed guard is satisfied. Keep the selected offset, height bounds, V1 law, contact-triggered toe memory, wing relief, cutoff conditions, body, solver, course and controls unchanged.

Run one full-start development-J privileged case for 3000 ticks at 16 substeps using the validated full-perch observer. Require q/v parity through waypoint five and preserve the first failure or timeout, all substep contact impulses, first toe/foot and nonfoot contact, supported-opposed duration, endpoint overlap, release/regrip and obstacle clearance. At least 50 consecutive supported-opposed intervals, <=0.5 mm endpoint overlap, no nonfoot impact and observed release/regrip remain mandatory; a one-off touch or hover fails. This is exploratory development, not autonomous or a held-out trial.

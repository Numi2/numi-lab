# Post-hoc toe threshold and recontact audit

In the bounded pre-release push run, all six comparable lanes first hit the modeled toe speed threshold after their first three-interval release, in a substep with positive foot normal impulse. Four first events occurred inside the 348–375 high-resolution window: each was left rear proximal toe DOF 28, with ground contacts involving both rear distal toe bodies 26 and 34. In lane 5, that DOF was -0.00795 rad/s immediately before contact resolution and -12 rad/s at the recorded end of the substep; lane 4 changed from -5.59 to -12 rad/s, lane 6 from -9.76 to -12 and lane 7 from -10.69 to -12. The first events in lanes 2–3 occurred later, outside the retained pre-contact and body-pair detail window, so their pre-contact velocities and precise collider pairs are unavailable.

This is a post-hoc analysis of one used seed with correlated lanes. It demonstrates association with renewed foot contact and a substantial change after the pre-contact sample in the detailed lanes, not proof of which constraint, geometric feature, speed-limit mechanism or controller is solely responsible. Contact resolution and later substep processing are not independently separated by these measurements. It does not qualify toe grip, landing, or any mission.

# Intermediate teacher approach: partial supported contacts

On one previously used training layout and seed, the privileged teacher reached all five waypoint criteria in all eight first episodes. Three lanes (0, 4 and 6) ended with 25 consecutive 20 ms intervals having at least half nominal body-weight upward contact impulse, no nonfoot perch impulse, and opposing front/rear normal components on both feet. Their longest consecutive intervals meeting those criteria were 468, 116 and 91 respectively. All 16 physics-substep samples per interval were present, with no reported contact capacity overflow. This does not establish biological gripping force or reliable landing across starts.

An independent endpoint audit of the last 25 intervals measured maximum root linear speeds of 0.0135, 0.0384 and 0.0054 m/s for lanes 0, 4 and 6; lane 4 pitched from approximately -0.311 to -0.381 rad during that window. One first episode terminated for excessive tilt at interval 3437; the other four lanes did not meet the terminal support and opposition criteria. No controlled toe release occurred. The run is therefore a partially successful research diagnostic, not a qualified landing controller or an autonomous demonstration.

Accepted q/v, policy outputs and endpoint contacts matched the flare reference through each lane's fifth waypoint; the corrected approach and terminal balance terms operate afterward. The teacher remains privileged and the course was used for development. The frozen low-level flight law was unchanged.

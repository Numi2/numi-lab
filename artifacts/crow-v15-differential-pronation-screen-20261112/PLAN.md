# Differential wing-pronation identification

To investigate persistent body-to-path misalignment, repeat the matched obstacle-free open-flight diagnostic on used seed 20261126 with controls and zero heading demand, but apply antisymmetric ordinary wing-pronation position offsets +/- .10, .20 or .30 from 12–13 s in pairs. Direct body moments remain off. Check same-command prefix and controls, then measure body yaw, path, roll, height, speed, contact and joint caps. This is an open-loop model identification test, not calibrated bird aerodynamics or a qualified turn.

# Transient contact-gated toe closure trial

The current task increments toe closure only while a perch contact is observed at the last substep of each control interval and immediately opens when contact disappears. In the flare trial, contact is often transient and does not persist long enough for closure. Test a bounded 0.2-second closure window after an actual accepted foot/toe perch impulse, then gradual opening if contact is not regained. This uses no injected force. The two temporary counters occupy status bits 23-30, so this isolated variant must not be combined with the separate flight-toe ramp without redesigning and auditing the state allocation. The privileged teacher and partially successful intermediate approach remain development-only; no success claim follows without sustained physical support, opposing grip and demonstrated release.

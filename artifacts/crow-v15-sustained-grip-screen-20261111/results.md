# Sustained grip with the existing controller

The 1,000-tick development replay matched the reference through tick 85. The bird acquired 11 consecutive supported opposing-grip samples, with a worst estimated toe overlap of 0.449 mm while opposing grip was observed. Keeping toe targets engaged without scheduled release did not maintain actual opposing contact: only 14 grip-pose samples were observed, the longest supported run was 177 samples, and the bird fell with no terminal support. This failed the stringent sustained-grip gate.

This is not a controlled comparison of closing versus opening alone: the reference also ramps the leg balance gains and enables lean correction during its release phase, whereas the existing hold phase does neither. The next screen must match the leg balance law while changing the toe phase. The retained toe targets do not prove that the toes remained in contact. A departure release, full navigation and biological realism remain untested by this screen.

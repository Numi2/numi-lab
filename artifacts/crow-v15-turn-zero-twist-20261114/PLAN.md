# Zero baseline twist in the turn window

The 0.03-rad symmetric twist amplitude preserved both signed turns and reduced turn-window pronation saturation from about 66% to 48%, but missed the preregistered 33% effort and 0.08-rad tracking gates. Test the limiting one-variable ablation: smoothly reduce only the symmetric oscillating twist amplitude from 0.06 to zero rad during 10.0–10.5 s on candidate lanes 6 and 7. Keep the 0.08-rad turn signal, all other control, physical model and actuator limits unchanged. Removing symmetric twist may degrade aerodynamics or motion; the observed result, including failure, will decide.

Use the same used seed and 1800-interval training layout, teacher off, no scheduled resets. Require exact full q/v/action/contact parity to the original release-latch turn through 500 candidate intervals and unaffected lanes through their first episodes, complete unique 16-substep contact/toe/effort observations, matching host/policies and expected prebuilt shader, no errors, toe-speed threshold, nonfoot support or later ground contact. Require both signed heading changes >= 0.20 rad relative to matching straight, pronation effort saturation <33% in intervals 600–1200 on each lane, tracking error <0.08 rad, and height 0.25–1.30 m during intervals 450–1800. These fixed development gates do not establish biological realism, disturbed-start recovery, frozen flight, landing or the unused-layout mission. Preserve the full evidence and failures.

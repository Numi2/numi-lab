# Independent long replay of front-toe contact recovery

The independent single-environment run exactly matched all 750 saved state traces from the development batch. It maintained 941 consecutive supported samples (18.82 s at 20 ms per sample), including the final 100, and its final 100 samples all showed bilateral opposing toe impulses and support. The longest continuous supported-grip interval was 185 samples; opposing grip occurred in 732 of 1,000 samples and was intermittent earlier. Worst independent toe/perch overlap at grip poses was 0.449 mm, within the predeclared 0.5 mm limit. This meets the stationary single-start screening gate but is not robust landing qualification: perturbations, recovery from a real approach, release, flight and autonomous navigation remain untested. The overlap and support measurements are sampled and do not prove continuous geometry between observations. No reserved layout was used.

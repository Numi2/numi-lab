# Result: early leg-associated impulse without direct body moments or commanded flapping, but no qualified takeoff

Both first episodes reached 400 steps. The paired arms matched recorded q/v/action/contact through step 345 and had complete 335–400 contact, effort, toe and target records, with no reported errors or overflow. The direct yaw and pitch body-wrench requests were set to zero at the application point from episode start, and flapping-position amplitude was set to zero. These interventions do not immobilize all wing joints or remove passive aerodynamic forces.

In comparable lanes 2–7, the push generated +0.1320 to +0.1747 N s extra upward foot impulse relative to the active crouch arm in steps 348–355 and +0.0636 to +0.0959 N s in steps 348–357. The native whole-body FK accounting found +0.0559 to +0.0879 N s vertical center-of-mass momentum change in the push arm across 348–357, with about zero in the hold arm; its uniform-translation calibration passed. This is evidence for a modeled leg-command-associated early push under these controls, not an isolated measurement of muscle power or passive aerodynamic force.

The strict takeoff screen failed all six comparable lanes. Maximum root rise was only 0.0236–0.0261 m versus the 0.05 m criterion. Lanes 3–6 never achieved three consecutive unloaded control intervals in the observed window; lanes 2 and 7 reached that state at step 375 but recontacted at step 378 and stayed supported. Those two also had earlier support gaps. All six had 4–8 substeps at or above the modeled 11.99 rad/s toe-speed reporting threshold. No nonfoot ground impulse occurred during 348–400. The crouch arm also entered a later unloaded condition after its control transition, so that alone is not proof of propulsion.

The control intervention is limited to these two direct body-moment channels and commanded flap amplitude. This is one used seed with correlated lanes and empirical aerodynamic closures; it does not establish biological realism, sustained flight, controller qualification, autonomous navigation or landing. No controller is promoted. Preserve both full traces and input hashes.

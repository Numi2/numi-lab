# Rear-toe extension after accepted own-foot unloading

The earlier supported-swing rear-toe command improved clearance but halved forward travel. Test the same bounded -0.20-rad proximal toe target only once the current foot is also reported unloaded, while the opposite foot reports support and the same positive swing carrier is active. This accepted feedback is delayed by a control interval and cannot guarantee exact toe-off timing. Both arms use the baseline leg gait, one shader and passive contact observer, direct targets, same seed, policy weights, material and starting states. This is a physical command, not measured American-crow kinematics.

Predeclare exact q/v/action and contact parity before walking, complete error-free traces, no additional toe-speed caps or nonfoot impulses, at least two support-qualified clearance bouts per foot per lane, no more unloaded substeps than the sham, and all lane progress positive and at least 90% of matched sham. Require the pooled count of sole-clear but toe-low endpoint intervals to decrease at least 20% from a nonzero sham count. Candidate loaded-contact-point median and p95 tangential speed must each be at most 150% of sham. These are engineering comparisons, not biological targets or integrated slip. A pass would require different development starts, long gait and transition to takeoff.

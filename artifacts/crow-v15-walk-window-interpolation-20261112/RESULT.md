# Walk-window smoothing: invalid comparison

Each of the zero-, 5- and 10-ms lead candidates failed exact pre-walk state/contact parity in all eight lanes. In one inspected lane at interval 1, q differed by order 1e-10 and velocity by up to at least 1e-6 in reported units; contact records then diverged. These small initial numerical differences are not grounds to relax the predeclared parity rule, and the later results cannot establish the isolated effect of smoothing.

The 5-ms candidate met the other recorded engineering screen thresholds: all lanes retained 92.6–103.3% of control travel, neither foot was unloaded together in the recorded substeps versus 30 in control, and the four whole-substep motion-proxy 95th percentiles were 8.3–25.1% of control. This is promising but rejected due to the failed control comparison. Zero lead lost at least 31% of control travel in every lane; 10 ms lost at least 16.7% in every lane and accumulated 351 unloaded substeps. No variant qualifies walking or biological realism.

The next diagnostic tests a floating-point bypass when consecutive targets are identical; it must meet the original exact pre-walk parity requirement before its gait measurements are interpreted.

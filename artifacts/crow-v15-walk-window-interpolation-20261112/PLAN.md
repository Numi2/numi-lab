# Walk-window-only physical target smoothing

Earlier full-interval smoothing ran from startup, producing a different pre-gait state. Repeat it only in the experimental 4–6 s walking window by making the previous target equal the current target outside that window. During the window the existing physical implicit drive interpolates knee and ankle position targets over all 16 physics substeps; compare zero, 5 and 10 ms gait-oscillator phase lead. This is a scoped research intervention, not an animation or a measured crow gait. The experimental target-history buffer remains limited to the existing chunk-one diagnostic and is not reset/rollback safe for general use.

Require exact pre-gait q/v/actions and every logged contact against the sham, full observed substeps, no new nonfoot contact or toe-speed cap, no increase in both-feet-unloaded substeps, at least two support-qualified clearance bouts per foot, every lane at least 90% of its matching control travel, and all four mean control-boundary knee/ankle motion proxies at least 20% below sham. Report complete-substep mean, 95th-percentile and maximum proxies, and do not present the candidate as uniformly smoother if they worsen. One used seed and a two-second gait screen cannot qualify walking, neck dynamics, biological realism, takeoff or landing.

# Angular-momentum and damping cross-check

The passive 300-tick replay exactly matched the reference. An independently assembled whole-bird angular momentum agreed with the existing FP64 invariant API to printed precision at all samples. The observed torsional contact impulse was zero in the reported windows. Estimating body damping moment from control-endpoint kinematics explains much of the previous residual: in ticks 200–239 the mean residual changed from -0.000595 to +0.000043 N m s per control step, and in ticks 240–279 from -0.000999 to +0.000012 N m s. Mean absolute residuals after the endpoint approximation were 0.000079 and 0.000097 N m s, respectively.

The body-damping term is material relative to the approximately -0.0022 N m s contact moment in those windows; it is a modeled world-velocity damping load, not measured avian tissue or aerodynamic damping. This is not an exact closed balance because damping and COM use endpoints rather than all 16 substeps and other forces or solver details may remain. It does not by itself show damping caused the fall. Identify the contributing bodies and run matched physical controls before changing damping. The outcome does not qualify landing, flight or real-bird mechanics.

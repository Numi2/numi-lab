# Check angular-momentum accounting

The prior passive audit reproduced the original trajectory but its reconstructed momentum budget has a material residual. Repeat the same 300-tick replay with observation only, require exact trace parity and compare the independently reconstructed whole-bird angular momentum at each tick with the native FP64 invariant API. Record torsional contact impulse separately from the world contact-force moment, and estimate body damping moment from measured body velocities and authored damping coefficients at the control endpoint. Report residuals both with and without these terms. Endpoint damping and COM are approximations to substep integrals; a residual cannot automatically be called a solver defect.

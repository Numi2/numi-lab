# Smaller phase-gated turn command

With symmetric oscillatory twist reduced to zero, both candidates remained airborne and retained signed turns of about 0.29 rad, and modeled pronation saturation fell to 28.3%; however, maximum target tracking error remained about 0.0824 rad, above the pre-set 0.08-rad limit. Test one physical command change: reduce the phase-gated turn target from 0.08 to 0.07 rad for candidate lanes 6 and 7, leaving symmetric twist, launch, feedback, body, force model and actuator limits unchanged. A weaker command may reduce turning or have other physical side effects; do not promote it solely because tracking improves.

Use the same used seed and 1800-interval training layout, teacher off, no scheduled resets. Require exact full q/v/action/contact parity to the archived turn through candidate interval 500 and unaffected lanes through their first episodes, complete unique 16-substep contact/toe/effort observations, matching host/policies and expected prebuilt shader, no errors, toe-speed threshold, nonfoot support or later ground contact. Require both signed heading changes >= 0.20 rad relative to matching straight, pronation saturation <33% in intervals 600–1200 on each lane, peak tracking error <0.08 rad, and height 0.25–1.30 m during intervals 450–1800. Retain failures. This development screen does not establish biological wing realism, disturbed-start recovery, qualification, frozen flight, landing or the unused-layout mission.

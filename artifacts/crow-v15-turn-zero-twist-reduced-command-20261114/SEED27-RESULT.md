# Smaller turn command: second used-seed preliminary screen

On used seed 20261127, the two candidate lanes stayed unloaded through interval 1800 and met the preregistered preliminary actuator, height, continuity and substep gates. Modeled pronation saturation was 25.36% and 25.39%; peak tracking errors were 0.07238 and 0.07241 rad; heights during intervals 450–1800 were 0.416–0.877 m and 0.545–1.216 m. Relative yaw extrema versus the older same-seed straight reference were -0.2582 and +0.2566 rad, but that reference retains oscillatory twist while this candidate uses zero symmetric twist. These heading differences therefore do not isolate the effect of the turn command. A physically matched zero-twist straight reference is separately preregistered.

The candidate q/v, policy action and contacts matched the archived seed-27 turn through interval 500, and unchanged lanes matched through their first episodes. Teacher was off, the expected shader and shared inputs matched, target reconstruction and complete unique required substeps passed, and no candidate observer error, later ground/nonfoot contact or toe-speed threshold was recorded. The duplicated reset perturbation remains in this trial and first observed state is not an independently measured reset state. Contact/state epoch alignment in the candidate lanes remains inferred. This is not evidence of biological wing realism, leg necessity, disturbed-start recovery, a qualified or frozen controller, landing or unused-layout navigation.

# Full-window pronation observer and narrow turn screen

The read-only observer differs from the baseline host source in exactly one logging-window condition. Its rerun reproduced all 14,408 recorded q/v/action/contact states and all 64,128 overlapping effort samples exactly, with identical first-episode metadata and non-host inputs. The corrected analyzer checks all 9,616 substeps per candidate from ticks 600 through 1200; see ANALYSIS-CORRECTION.md for a retained earlier analysis draft that checked bounds on a shorter window.

On the same used development seed, the two candidates passed the preregistered narrow heading, lateral deviation, no-recontact, speed, height, target and full-window model-limit screens: relative yaw peaks were -0.1547 and +0.1542 rad, and maximum lateral deviations were 0.1737 and 0.1752 m. Pronation effort reached the model's 0.15 N m cap for 64.67% and 64.69% of the full window; maximum measured target-position error was 0.1131 rad. The matched baseline's earlier common-window saturation was about 58%, while the candidate was about 65% in that same window. This is one seed, two correlated directions, a phase rule chosen from development data and a relatively small heading change; the drives are heavily saturated. The result is not a qualified general turn, disturbed-start recovery, flight controller freeze, real-crow validation, autonomous navigation or landing, and the reserved layouts/seeds remain untouched.

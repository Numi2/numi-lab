# Bilateral-contact-triggered rear toe regulation

Hold-only adjustment preserved support but exceeded the overlap limit before hold began. Compare three exact controls with two candidates: track the original rear-toe targets until a prior interval has opposing contacts on each side, then latch pressure regulation and decrease each rear-toe target by 0.01 or 0.02 rad/tick when its opposing impulse exceeds 0.01 N s per 20 ms. Otherwise hold. Release remains bounded by the original decreasing target. The start, all other joints, contact solver, sensor, stiffness and force limits are unchanged. This threshold is an exploratory engineering value informed by retained development data, not a biological measurement. Require exact 200-state control parity, sustained bilateral opposing grip with support, release and terminal open-toe support, and worst grip-conditioned SAT overlap at most 0.5 mm to nominate broader tests. Retain all failures. Passing a single stationary test does not qualify the continuous task, untouched layouts, or crow biomechanics.

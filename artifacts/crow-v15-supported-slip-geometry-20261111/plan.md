# Supported-slip geometry audit

Post-hoc diagnostic of the archived paused-descent development run, not a prospective performance test. At selected control intervals spanning first contact, apparent support, phase-2 handoff and the fall, compare the accepted endpoint oriented boxes of all eight toe segments and perch; report signed separation, toe x position, root velocity, substep normal impulse, and controller phase. Confirm q alignment with the accepted preceding frame. The analysis is descriptive and cannot by itself prove continuous collision separation or grip.

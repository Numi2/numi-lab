# Integrated launch matched control

The unchanged leg-push and crouch-only trials have identical accepted states until step 651. At step 652, the push trial has forward and upward root velocity 0.197 and 0.144 m/s while the control remains supported, near stationary. The sampled final-substep ground impulse in the push trial points forward and upward. By step 655 the push trial is airborne, at forward and upward velocity 0.099 and 0.507 m/s; the crouch-only trial remains supported. Afterward the flight controller is engaged and root velocity becomes backward, reaching -0.731 m/s at step 700. These are one seed and one previously used layout; final-substep impulses do not represent the complete control interval, and late root velocities do not independently identify aerodynamic cause. This establishes the initial leg push is not itself a backward push, but does not qualify takeoff, prove momentum accounting, or qualify the full sequence.

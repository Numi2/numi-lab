# Vertical-speed-aware landing flare control

The prior development teacher reached the perch but two first-episode collisions had downward root speeds about 1 m/s. Add a bounded upward correction to the requested landing height proportional to measured downward speed, only after the existing landing phase engages. Keep the already-tested approach command, physical solver and low-level frozen flight law unchanged. Measure all-substep perch contacts, landing support, failures and toe speeds. This is a privileged teacher diagnostic, not autonomy or a final layout.

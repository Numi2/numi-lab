# Differential pronation authority: obstacle-free diagnostic

Bank and sweep trials without the two identified direct body-moment actuators after 9 s failed to turn reliably; translation was largely sideways. Test whether bounded differential physical wing-pronation position commands yield yaw authority, with zero, 0.12 and 0.24 normalized residuals across paired +/-90 degree heading demands. Preserve the prior launch and ordinary carrier, do not inject a new body moment, and compare the 48 logged pre-9-s q/v/action states against the matched earlier bank screen. Measure both turn directions, altitude, attitude, ground contacts, toe caps and first physical failure. This used-seed, simplified aerodynamic test cannot establish crow flight or generalization, and the earlier launch still contains assistance. Other aerodynamic closures remain.

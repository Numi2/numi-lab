# Walking contact-point slip observation

The contact-point observer matched all accepted q/v, endpoint contact fields and recorded substep aggregates for all eight first episodes through 600 intervals, excluding the added point field. Independent kinematics matched the retained lane-5 replay to 1.6e-8 m in position and 2.4e-7 rad/s in angular speed. Over positive-normal-impulse ground contacts during the eight-second walk, lane medians of tangential contact-point speed were 0.0026–0.0031 m/s and unweighted 95th percentiles were 0.0407–0.0470 m/s. Weighting sampled endpoint speeds by normal impulse gave 95th percentiles of 0.0303–0.0351 m/s and 99th percentiles of 0.0940–0.0956 m/s.

These are final-substep contact points combined with accepted endpoint kinematics, not continuously sampled slip or a biological target. Brief loaded sliding remains a limitation; this used development seed does not establish robust walking or a full autonomous sequence.

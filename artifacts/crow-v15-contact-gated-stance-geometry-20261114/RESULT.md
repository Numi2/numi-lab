# Contact-gated stance geometry: failed supported grip

The native v15 two-lane perch test retained all 900 states and all 28,800 contact and toe-velocity substeps, without observer errors or nonfoot impulses. The paired lanes' logged states and targets matched through zero-based interval 294; the experimental geometry latch and first differences occurred at 295, and the requested offset reached 0.02 rad. Both had a 44-interval qualified release. After return, the control achieved 13 consecutive fully qualified bilateral supported-grip intervals and the candidate 11; neither reached the required terminal 100. The root fell below 0.1 m at one-based interval 371 in control and 370 in the candidate. Both reached the modeled toe-speed limit before falling. The correction is rejected.

The same-run matched prefix supports the paired comparison, but the control diverged from the older reference at zero-based interval 122 before the intervention; the reason is unresolved. Thus this run does not establish exact cross-build control reproducibility. Contact/state epochs remain unverified. The static FK prediction did not translate to a sustained grip, and this constructed test does not establish walking, takeoff, landing or mission qualification. Preserve all raw data and the failed result.

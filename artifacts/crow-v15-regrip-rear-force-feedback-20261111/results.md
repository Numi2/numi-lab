# Rear-toe force-feedback isolation

The unchanged reference reproduced all 1,500 trace states, all fifteen candidates matched through the 877-state preswitch interval, and all requested the same step-777 release. All retained 39 clear, unloaded release samples and no non-foot ground contact over any substep. Every rear-only force-feedback candidate failed: none retained terminal support or any supported opposition in the final 100 samples; the unchanged reference retained support and had 93/100 opposing-force samples but exceeded the 0.5 mm overlap limit at 0.546 mm. The worst candidate reached 3.016 mm accepted-pose overlap during an opposing sample.

The chosen thresholds and rates are insufficient for this control scheme. This result does not show that contact force is unhelpful in general or diagnose the cause of instability. No candidate is promoted; this remains one development start with endpoint geometry, not robust landing, both-foot release or full navigation.

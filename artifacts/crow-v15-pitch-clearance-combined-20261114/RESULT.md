# Combined pitch and clearance: partial improvement, failed gate

Adding coordinated hip, knee and ankle clearance authority to the retained pitch-feedback candidate raised the longest qualifying release/support streak from 2 to 24 intervals, but failed the required 100 and remained below the earlier separate 34-interval baseline. The candidate passed intervals 241–264, then lost clearance and recontacted; first root height below 0.1 m occurred at interval 375 versus 363 for its matched control. At tick 265 the hip and knee clearance targets had reached their caps while ankle correction was still increasing. This motivates local kinematic inspection, not a claim of stable support.

The control exactly reproduced all 450 archived pitch-candidate states; the lanes shared an exact 225-state prefix. All 32 contact substeps and accepted states were present with no native or observer errors or nonfoot support. Reconstructed pitch and clearance feedback passed and the rebuilt probe hash matched the prebuild. The accepted-state stance toe reached the 12 rad/s threshold only after root-height failure in both lanes. Toe speed at substeps and drive effort were not established. Toes remained open; regrip and landing were not tested. This constructed perch probe has independently unverified state/contact epochs and is not walking or biological qualification.

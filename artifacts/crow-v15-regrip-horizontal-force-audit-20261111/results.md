# Post-release grip force audit

The independently instrumented replay reproduced all 1,500 reference states exactly. In the final 100 samples, signed horizontal impulses from the actual normal and friction forces showed opposing front and rear toe forces on both feet, with support, in 93 samples; the earlier normal-direction cone detected 16. The longest continuous supported horizontal-opposition run was 69 samples. The threshold was 0.0002 N s per opposing group, integrated across all 16 substeps.

The worst independent accepted-pose toe/perch separation during horizontal opposition was -0.546 mm, exceeding the predeclared 0.5 mm overlap limit. Consequently this diagnostic identifies a limitation of the original detector; it does not qualify the landing or replace its acceptance gates. The experiment covers one development start and makes no claim about continuous geometry between samples, other layouts, or successful regrip after perturbations.

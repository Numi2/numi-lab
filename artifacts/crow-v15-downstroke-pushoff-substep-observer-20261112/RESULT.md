# Substep observation of phase-adjusted launch

Observer and source trial match all 2,960 state endpoints and 47,360 contact substeps for 370 intervals in eight development lanes. All 3,968 expected q snapshots exist, final substep q matches endpoint exactly, with no reported errors or contact overflow. Forward kinematics agrees with the replay in position to 1.61e-8 m and orientation to 3.89e-8 in quaternion distance. Both modeled wing centers descend approximately 8.68 mm in torso coordinates before the final foot-loaded substep (352, 2) in every lane; both feet have no above-threshold load in intervals 353 and 354.

Contact and q are associated with the same native substep; causal order within a substep is unresolved. This does not measure feather shape, force, or full-body biological accuracy. The seed and layout were previously used.

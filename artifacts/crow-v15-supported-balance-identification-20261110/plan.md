# Open-toe supported balance identification

In the preceding exact-dimension supported bench, four of eight starts fell even in the open-toe control. An earlier, independent reached-state perturbation found positive symmetric hip position offsets moved the root forward (roughly 5.7 mm at +0.03 rad over 0.4 s) but also changed pitch and contact retention. Test whether a small measured-contact-gated symmetric hip position adjustment can stabilize the already-supported, open-toe bench without injected forces.

Keep toes at neutral in all arms. In eight identical starts, compare the original open-toe controller with hip correction gain +2.5, +5.0 and -2.5 rad/m. For arms 1–3, delta is gain times (-0.012 m minus root x minus 0.20 s times root x velocity), clamped to plus/minus 0.08 rad, enabled only when measured toe upward impulse exceeds 0.00005 N s. The -0.012 m target is a development-bench reference, not a general fixed-world navigation goal. No momentum or state is directly injected. Compare terminal 25 samples support, nonfoot impulse, tilt, speed, solver rollback and root displacement. Require exact original open-toe trace parity versus the preceding bench and at least 6/8 terminal-supported starts without solver rollback before calling an arm a candidate. This local single-perch setup and endpoint sampling do not qualify landing, grip, flight, substep validity or biology; unused layouts remain untouched.

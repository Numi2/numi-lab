# Joint toe mass and damping sensitivity

With a cleared reset, a 0.1 g segment mass preserved the walking gate in 8/8 development starts but still produced toe-speed caps in two. Test .012 and .030 drive damping with that mass and the same reset, gait, actions, shapes and limits. This is an uncalibrated sensitivity only. Require no all-substep toe cap and the unchanged walking gate in every lane; test fresh perturbations and the full flight and grip sequence before adoption.

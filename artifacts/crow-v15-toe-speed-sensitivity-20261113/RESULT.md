# Toe speed-limit sensitivity: paired comparison invalid

The first attempt failed before physics because the changed world was incompatible with the old policy contracts; its output is retained. A diagnostic compiler audit that omitted only the world fingerprint found matching remaining observation/action hashes, including contact radii. Fresh diagnostic packs were bound to the modified world's actual four fingerprints, and native readback confirmed identical actor layers, activations, transforms and clips. This establishes the documented diagnostic binding, not physical or semantic equivalence.

The bound 24 rad/s trial completed all eight first episodes and generated the required 368 target samples. However, the preregistered recorded-prefix parity requirement failed: comparable lanes 2 and 5 first diverged from the original model at endpoint steps 11 and 4, respectively, long before launch. Lanes 3, 4, 6 and 7 matched recorded endpoint and contact fields through step 345; control lane 1 also diverged at step 4. Because the whole paired design failed its precondition, do not interpret the trial as the preregistered matched push comparison or selectively promote matching lanes.

Descriptively, maximum recorded toe speeds across the six modified lanes were 15.0–24.0 rad/s; lane 5 reached 24 rad/s. Their root rises were 0.0252–0.0386 m and three had three consecutive unloaded intervals. Every comparable lane exceeded 12.1 rad/s, but the changed initial trajectories in two lanes invalidate the prespecified full paired sensitivity conclusion. The clamp setting is uncalibrated and its alteration is not a physical improvement, evidence for changing production limits, or leg-powered takeoff, flight or landing qualification. All model variants, failed preflight, binding audit, raw traces, source and per-lane outcomes are preserved.

# Related-corvid ankle phase hypothesis

The magpie study reports knee extension before ankle extension in recovery. The prior coarse, anatomically approximate observer predominantly finds the reverse or cannot resolve the order. This experiment applies ankle-carrier lags of 0, 0.3, 0.6 and 0.9 radians in individual development lanes while keeping the knee and hip laws fixed. The lags are engineering hypotheses, not direct fitted crow kinematics. The gait period remains 0.5 s, so these correspond to 0, 24, 48 and 72 ms.

Compare each lane against its own matched 0.05-rad toe preload run at the same previously used seed. Require 0-radian lanes to match exactly. Measure ankle/knee order with the existing contact-segmented observer, all rejected/fragmented cycles, both-feet-unloaded intervals, toe speed cap and contact-point motion. Do not promote a phase merely for matching a proxy; preserve failures and carry unresolved landmark, speed, surface and species differences.

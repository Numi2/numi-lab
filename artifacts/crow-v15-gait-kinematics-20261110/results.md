# Short-walk kinematic measurements

The native observer reproduced 32/32 accepted q/v samples exactly and its per-foot summed contact impulses agreed with the independently logged contact rows within 1e-9 Ns. On the 8 exposed starts during steps 100-200, the root advanced about 5.6 cm, with alternating foot support, no unsupported endpoints and no positive nonfoot-ground impulses. Each side showed several consecutive unloaded endpoints with more than 2 mm clearance. However, estimated stance contact-point slip was high: 95th percentiles of the per-endpoint impulse-weighted mean were about 0.070 m/s on the left and 0.282 m/s on the right. The observer combines final accepted endpoint twists and final-substep contact positions, so this is an approximation; it does not prove continuous slip or biological realism. The initial observer failed because it assumed box ground; the rejected traces and corrected plane method are retained. This short development test does not qualify a stable gait.

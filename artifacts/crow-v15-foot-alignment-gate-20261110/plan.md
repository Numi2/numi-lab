# Maintain foot alignment during the guided descent

The traced first catch reached the perch with the rear toes 67-85 mm short of the near face while the front toes carried the load. The guide checked alignment only once, then continued descending even after foot drift. Compare two guided command adapters on eight previously used J starts. Require foot-joint x error under 25 mm, lateral error under 120 mm, and planar speed under 0.065 m/s before further descent; otherwise maintain or raise the target to 0.31 m above the perch center. Keep the frozen flight law, collision geometry and all other physics unchanged. Compare its original forward command against a zero-bias proportional/damped command (0.6*x error - 0.5*forward velocity, bounded to [-0.08, 0.10] m/s). Keep toe targets neutral in both arms to isolate the foot placement and support problem; no grip or release is claimed.

Predict at least six of eight starts survive, at least two show ten consecutive bilateral loaded control samples, and at least one achieves 25 mm foot-joint alignment when first supporting both feet, with no shank/thigh impulse at that time. The low-speed command adapter has only coarse obstacle-free speed screening and no precision-hover qualification; these privileged experiments test that gap and are not autonomous or held out. Preserve failed and censored runs and do not promote on waypoint completion.

# Contact termination timing audit

The active-contact observer exactly reproduced the previously retained course states and ground-contact records, including the corrected terminating submission in all seven closed lanes. The native rollout binary performs one preliminary compiled-policy warmup transition, then resets the context before creating/recording `CrowEpisodeEvidence`; the host substep trace counts that separate warmup submission, while `steps_by_environment` counts the post-reset episode transitions. The episode itself does not omit its initial post-reset transition. Thus the terminal trace submission is `steps_by_environment + 1`. The earlier scripts in this artifact are retained as originally run but truncate the final submission; use `fatal-contact-join.py` and `compare-with-warmup.py` for the corrected join. The observed status counts matched `requiredConstraints` and `activeContacts` over the originally sampled range; the discrepancy was the time join, not evidence of a collision-solver defect.

All seven contact-rule terminations coincide with a nonzero obstacle contact on the last substep, where the task contact reducer reads its contact buffer. In lanes 2 and 3, respectively, the head hit slalom B at trace submission 942, and the left wing hit slalom A at submission 833. The other five final pairs are wing/slalom. The first-observed impacts, final-substep pairs and previous sampled positions, attitudes, velocities and policy actions are in `fatal-contact-join.json`. The recorded policy action is not the full applied actuator command: the high-level development schedule overrides the policy in flight. The task rule uses a force threshold; these paired final-substep contacts and configured body group are consistent with it, but no separate compact-buffer readback was added. The nonterminal lane also had an earlier wing contact, so a contact at another substep is not automatically fatal.

The independently rechecked obstacle-free comparison first diverges in every lane on its first observed obstacle impact, including these two formerly unexplained lanes. All eight obstacle-free starts survived the 1200-transition horizon under scripted speed/height and zero turn; seven of eight obstacle-course starts hit contact termination. This is evidence for a routing/clearance issue in this specific scripted trial, not qualified autonomous flight or biological realism. Starts are correlated and use a development seed/layout. Straight and turning tracking under perturbed starts, natural gait, grip/release, landing, and genuinely unused layouts remain open.

`run.stderr.gz` is split into byte-identical 32 MiB pieces listed in `stderr-parts.json`; concatenate in listed order and verify the recorded whole SHA-256 before running scripts that expect the gzip file. The other compressed evidence is retained in Git. Preserve the original failed and censored episodes.

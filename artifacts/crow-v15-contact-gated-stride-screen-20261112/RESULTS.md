# Contact-gated hip command: rejected

The unchanged control lanes matched the full six-second baseline, and all eight prefixes matched through four seconds, including 1,600 state samples and 25,600 contact substeps. The physical task used the previously used seed 20261127.

Scaling the .34 hip command to 25%, 50% or 75% during same-foot support gave forward movement of .071–.076, .100 and .128 m respectively over 4–6 s, versus .131–.139 m in matched baseline lanes. None met the preset 5% improvement. The 75% setting also produced 9 and 11 both-feet-unloaded substeps and one lane exceeded the per-foot speed gate. At 25% the right-foot p95 contact-point speeds exceeded the 1.5x baseline gate in both lanes. No 4–6 s toe speed caps or nonfoot ground impulses were recorded.

These contact-conditioned settings are rejected. The motion remains an exploratory controller rather than validated crow gait, and point speeds are not integrated slip.

# Reconstructed landing mass position

This diagnostic reconstructs the two-segment v15 body and its research wing modifications from retained joint states in the strong-balance development screen. An initial reconstruction omitted the research wing model and was rejected: wing positions differed from native replay by up to 0.0533 m. With the exact archived wing model applied, nine lane-1 reference poses matched the native replay within 2.41e-7 m in body position and 3.69e-8 quaternion norm; q alignment required a one-step observer-to-replay offset. The source and reproducible validation are retained here.

At sampled post-contact poses, total center of mass generally began roughly 1–3 cm behind the midpoint of the feet along the course. In some failing lanes it moved farther behind before backward tilt; another failing lane moved in front. The foot midpoint is not the perch center or the center of pressure, so these observations do not establish a contact stability margin, net torque or the cause of a fall. They motivate testing physically adjusted leg posture and damping, rather than treating toe closure alone as a balance solution. This is not a held-out autonomous landing or biological qualification.

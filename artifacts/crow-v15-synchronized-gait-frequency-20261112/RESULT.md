# Synchronized slower gait: rejected

The two controls exactly matched six-second baselines; all experimental prefixes matched through four seconds, including 1,600 states and 25,600 contact substeps. The validated substep observer also matched endpoint contact geometry and reconstructed body states, as recorded in substep-contact-speed.json.

At 1.0, 1.333 and 1.6 Hz, movement over 4–6 s was .070–.073, .093–.095 and .103–.107 m, respectively, versus .131–.139 m for paired 2 Hz baselines. Contact-speed-integral ratios versus each matched foot were .67–.84, .72–.88 and .87–1.01 respectively. The 1.0 Hz lanes had 19 and 21 both-feet-unloaded substeps, 1.333 Hz had 17 each, and 1.6 Hz had three and zero. No walking-window toe caps or nonfoot impulse occurred.

No pair met the preset requirement of continuous collective support and at least 25% lower contact-motion integral on each foot while moving at least .05 m. These model settings are rejected, and neither the metric nor the test establishes biological or mission qualification.

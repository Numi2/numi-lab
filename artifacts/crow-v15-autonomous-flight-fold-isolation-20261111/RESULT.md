# Autonomous flight fold isolation failed

The combined physical toe curl, gradual leg fold and bounded speed compensation was evaluated with release disabled on the used development course and seed. All eight teacher-off first episodes failed before their first waypoint. The first observed non-ground endpoint contact in every lane was right wing against the right gate; ordering at the fatal substep is not established. The experimental observer and no-fold reference matched accepted q/v, policy outputs and reported contacts at every sampled step 1–369 in all eight lanes before the earliest fold. Thus this observer comparison does not indicate an earlier mismatch, but does not isolate which of the three coupled changes caused the routing failure.

No positive-impulse crow self-contact was logged in the observed steps 380 through first termination, and no ground normal impulse was recorded from step 400 to first termination. All first-episode toe substeps were covered without contact-capacity overflow; no toe speed caps were observed after step 500, while early caps persisted in lanes 1, 2 and 3. The previously screened flight-only motions do not establish autonomous route performance. Reject this combined candidate; toe curl alone requires a separate control. This is not an unused-layout, landing, recovery or biological qualification.

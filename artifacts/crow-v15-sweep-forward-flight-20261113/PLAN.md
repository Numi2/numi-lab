# Preregistered wing-sweep forward-flight sensitivity

The corrected-gain model with synchronized 0.65-rad, 6.4-Hz flap and 0.18-rad pronation stayed unloaded in six correlated push lanes but moved backward by 0.99–1.08 m in ticks 400–500; mean simulated aero x force was negative. Test bounded bilateral fore-aft wing sweep as a physical joint-level sensitivity, rather than applying a direct body force. The research model gives opposite sweep axes to left and right so equal coordinates are mirror-symmetric, with modeled joint limits +/-0.20 rad; these are not measured crow joint limits.

Use the same used training layout/seed 20261126, 16 substeps, 500 first-episode ticks, source policies, research aero closure, 0.65-rad flap and +pi/2 0.18-rad pronation in all six candidate lanes. For pairs 2–3, 4–5 and 6–7 set smooth-entry sweep targets -0.16 rad constant, +0.16 rad constant and 0.16 rad sinusoidal at flap frequency with phase zero, respectively, starting at 7.04 seconds and smoothly entering over 0.2 seconds. Preserve authored joint limits, force/effort limits, leg and toe commands and no direct body moments. Run paired push and crouched hold, with no intentional resets, and record actual sweep motion and achieved forces. Retain inherited lanes 0–1 as controls only.

Check first-episode completeness, exact push/hold state/action/contact parity through tick 345, target history, source/model/observer hashes, finite states, target and actual joint ranges, maximum effort and load conditions. Report first failure, support/recontact, toe thresholds, nonfoot impulse, COM, world x displacement/velocity and sampled aerodynamic power. Exploratory success requires both lanes of a pair to remain continuously unloaded after release through tick 500, no nonfoot impulse, toe-speed threshold substeps <=1, whole-body COM rise >=0.05 m, displacement tick 400 to 500 greater than +0.15 m and mean world x velocity 0.15–1.0 m/s. Establish leg effect separately with wings held. A positive result is not qualified flight, navigation, landing or biological realism; keep final layouts and seeds unused and archive every failure.

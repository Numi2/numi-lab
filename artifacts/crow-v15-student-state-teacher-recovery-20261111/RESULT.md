# Student-state teacher recovery on a used development course

The trained student controlled eight first episodes until staggered teacher takeovers at steps 900 through 1600. Before each takeover, endpoint q, velocity, recorded policy outputs and contacts matched the teacher-off comparison at every sampled interval (899–1599 samples by lane). Seven lanes were still alive at takeover; all seven reached the five waypoint thresholds without a first-episode physical failure by step 3000. The remaining lane hit slalom A and terminated at step 1540 before its scheduled step-1600 takeover. None had landed by step 3000. This demonstrates recovery from those seven visited states on one used layout and seed, not reliable recovery near every impending collision, generalization, autonomous success, grip or release. Matching endpoint samples does not independently establish identical every-substep dynamics. Recorded policy actions are not actuator targets. Failed first-episode labels remain excluded from learning.

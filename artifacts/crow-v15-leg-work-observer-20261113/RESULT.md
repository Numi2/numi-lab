# Paired generalized leg-work observer

Both observation-only used-seed reruns exactly reproduced the corresponding original 3,208 endpoint state, policy-action and contact records, and all 5,888 recorded contact substeps in steps 335–380. Neither run reported an observer error; joint coordinate continuity within each control interval was exact at recorded precision. All 5,888 work records per run report implicit position drive mode 1, bounded-drive off and coupled-drive off. These parity checks cover the recorded fields, not every hidden simulator bit.

In the six comparable lanes over steps 348–365, the extension arm produced approximately 1.879 J positive and -0.708 J negative generalized-effort-times-coordinate-increment, net 1.1703–1.1706 J. The crouch-target arm produced approximately 0.000004 J net. The extension arm's summed signed upward foot impulse exceeded the matched crouch arm by only 0.00842–0.00856 N s in that window. In representative lane 5, the two knee-coordinate terms each contributed about 0.611 J net to the proxy. These measurements show substantial commanded leg work with little incremental upward ground impulse in this specific closed-loop setup; they do not identify where that work went or prove that changing its timing will work.

The sampled buffer is generalized working effort after the simulator's actuator path, which can contain non-muscle terms. The discrete effort-times-coordinate increment is not measured biological muscle work, an exact continuous integral, or evidence of crow-calibrated actuator strength. Contact and passive aerodynamic forces, prior direct-body-moment authority, and two active leg controllers remain in the contrast. The small impulse difference is not a complete force or energy balance. Lanes are correlated and the seed/layout were used for development. No leg-powered takeoff, flight, navigation, grip or landing is qualified.

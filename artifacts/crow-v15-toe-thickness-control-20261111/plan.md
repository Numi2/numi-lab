# Toe thickness sensitivity experiment

The experimental toe boxes are 18.4 mm high despite being 5 mm wide. Test 6 mm height with the same mass and recalculated box inertia, while the foot residual inertia is recomputed by the model builder. No specimen calibration is claimed. Measure speed-limit hits, physical clearance, gait and launch; successful walking alone cannot qualify flight or grip. Diagnostic policy rebinding changes the model contract and is not semantic policy validation.

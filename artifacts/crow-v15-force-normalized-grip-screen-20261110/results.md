# Force-normalized finer-step grip screen

The original 4-substep control exactly reproduced all 200 reference states. Both 16-substep arms and the 32-substep individual-toe arm failed to produce three consecutive force-normalized bilateral opposing-grip samples and failed terminal support. The force-normalized trigger did not resolve this one-start failure. The baseline passed the behavioral screen but overlapped 4.706 mm, failing the 0.5 mm geometry gate. Grip-conditioned overlap is unavailable for the failed finer arms and must not be interpreted as no overlap. Contact evidence in this bench is sampled at the final substep, so it may miss contacts within the control interval; a separate substep-accumulation experiment is needed. This is a development drop, not full navigation, a supported flight landing or biological qualification.

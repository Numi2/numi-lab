The original plan incorrectly inferred the active perch collision dimensions solely from RunProgram.cpp. The research host in build/crow-v15-heavy-wing-route-20261110/c_api.cpp overrides the half-width from 0.10 m to 0.025 m at runtime, leaving half-height 0.055 m. The actual collision shape is therefore 0.05 m wide and 0.11 m high; the existing toe span can cross its width. The wider rendered or authored layout alone does not establish the shape used by physics. The attempted alternative build failed at the existing runtime assertion before a rollout; its intended interpretation is invalid and no evidence of physical impossibility is claimed. The original false hypothesis and failed probe are retained. A future branch cross-section experiment must start from the active runtime shape and be identified as a new geometry.

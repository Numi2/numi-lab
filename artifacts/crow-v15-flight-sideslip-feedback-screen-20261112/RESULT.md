# Bounded sideways-motion feedback: failed flight screen

The candidate added accepted body-frame lateral-velocity feedback to the bounded wing-bank target after 9 seconds. The 48 retained pre-intervention q/v/action samples matched the earlier bank-plus-pitch control exactly; all expected contact substeps were present, with no logged solver errors or overflow. All six candidate lanes reached interval 1600 without reported physical failure or post-intervention nonfoot contact. In particular, positive-direction lane 4 completed this window while its corresponding prior control ended in physical failure at interval 1466; this comparison does not isolate a physical cause.

None of the three positive/negative pairs passed the predeclared sampled forward-flight and turn criteria. At interval 1600, heading errors remained 1.218–1.333 rad. Body-relative forward speeds were between -0.769 and +0.235 m/s; sideslip angles were between roughly -2.13 and +1.43 rad. The mild and middle four lanes had identical reported physical metrics to their controls at every retained post-intervention sample. The strongest pair differed; its positive-direction lane still had -0.176 m/s forward velocity and -1.787 rad sideslip at the end. The clamp can make weak feedback ineffective while the heading demand saturates, but these traces do not by themselves establish the complete cause.

This is one used development seed with snapshots 100 control intervals apart, an obstacle-free turn intervention and direct body moments still active before 9 seconds. It does not establish continuous flight safety, realistic crow aerodynamics, useful 90-degree turns, disturbed-start recovery, a qualified or frozen controller, navigation or landing. Do not promote.

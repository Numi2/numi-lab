# Bounded lateral-velocity feedback screen

The previous turn trials moved predominantly sideways relative to body heading. Hypothesis: feed accepted body-frame lateral velocity back into the commanded bank angle so a negative lateral velocity offsets the positive bank demand. Retain the tested bank limits and pitch feedback, use gains 0.15, 0.30 and 0.60 for paired positive/negative commands, and add no direct wrench. Compare the exact corresponding retained bank-plus-pitch-feedback development controls, including exact q/v/action prefix before 9 s. Different shader builds require explicit parity verification. Direct body moments still operate before 9 s, so this is a turn intervention screen rather than a controller qualification.

For a preliminary sampled engineering pass require all 16 contact substeps present, no error or overflow, no nonfoot contact after intervention, completion to interval 1600 without physical failure, all available 100-interval flight samples 500–1600 inside 0.65–1.15 m and abs pitch <0.35 rad, body-relative forward speed at least 0.5 m/s and abs sideslip at most 45 degrees at samples 800–1600, and final heading within 15 degrees of the requested +/-90 degrees. Both directions of a gain pair must pass. These are diagnostic engineering criteria, not biological data; even a pass does not establish full maneuver safety, unguided takeoff, perturbed recovery, altitude commands, navigation or landing.

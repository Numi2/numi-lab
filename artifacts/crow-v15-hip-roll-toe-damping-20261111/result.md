# Constant toe-damping comparison

On eight selected development starts, increasing all 16 toe-joint damping coefficients from 0.003 to 0.12 eliminated sampled 12 rad/s toe-limit hits over 500 intervals. It also disrupted the 0.80 gait: none met the combined criteria of three clearance bouts per foot, forward motion, no nonfoot contact and no fully unloaded substeps. Two starts did not achieve two rising unloaded intervals in the launch window. The constant-damping candidate is rejected. This model property is uncalibrated; reducing cap hits alone is not a physical or biological qualification.

The host property change produced different fingerprints. The initial rejected preflight and the exact diagnostic actor rebindings are retained. The flight law, task shader, toe effort bounds and contact material were held fixed; changing model damping still changes contact dynamics and cannot be treated as a matched trajectory.

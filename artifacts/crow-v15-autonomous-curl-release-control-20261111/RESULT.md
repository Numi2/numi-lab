# Toe curl with contact-gated release

On one used development course and seed, seven teacher-off first episodes reached five waypoints; lane 6 reached three and lane 4 fell after reaching five. Lane 5 showed 160 consecutive bilateral opposed-support intervals, followed by short independently replay-aligned left-foot clearance and right-foot support spans of 2 and 5 intervals, then 162 bilateral opposed-support intervals. It did not maintain that bilateral opposition to the end. The left-foot/toe clearance test uses estimated boxes at control endpoints; zero left normal impulse and opposing right-foot support are measured over all 16 substeps. It does not establish continuous clearance between endpoints or biological realism.

A separate substep audit found at least half estimated body-weight upward foot impulse in each of the last 29 intervals, although the bilateral opposition test failed in all 29. The release-enabled and toe-curl-only trajectories matched sampled q/v, actor outputs and contacts before first perch impulse; lane 5 first differed at step 3436. All substeps were accounted for and no contact-capacity overflow was logged. No lane ended with sustained bilateral opposition. The existing release abort path remains abrupt, the toe speed caps during early settling remain, and this is not an unused-layout result or a robust full-sequence qualification.

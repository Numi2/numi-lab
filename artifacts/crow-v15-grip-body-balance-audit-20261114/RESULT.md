# Body center of mass during failed return

The existing v15 FK executable reproduced both recorded foot-origin positions in all 26 sampled endpoints to within 0.60 nm; the modeled total mass was 0.45006 kg. In the no-recentering control, modeled COM x was 0.0311 m at zero-based interval 270, 0.0367 m at 300, 0.0458 m at 310 and 0.0819 m at 330. The perch top's positive x edge is 0.025 m, so the corresponding forward offset from that edge increased from 0.0061 to 0.0569 m. Both foot origins and toe impulses are retained alongside the COM results.

This endpoint geometry supports investigating forward balance before and during return. The top edge alone is not the bird's full support polygon: toes can exert forces against perch sides. These observations do not establish contact slip, instability cause, controller efficacy or biological validity.

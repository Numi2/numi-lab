# Two-segment contact-triggered grip: failed

The 350-step stationary perch screen matched the open-toe reference q/v through step 9, recorded all 5,600 substeps with no overflow, and had no reported native step failure. The left front target first advanced at interval 12 and the right at 142 in response to endpoint rear contact. There were 144 supported intervals after the additional tilt and speed criteria, with at most 97 consecutive; there were zero intervals of bilateral opposed front/rear forces. The front segments never made a positive perch impulse during this trial. The crow lost perch support and fell; by the last sample its root was below the perch. This is a failed isolated grip test, not a landing, release or autonomous-route result. A preflight aborted on an incorrectly sized kinematics output buffer and was corrected before the trial; its trace is retained.

# Autonomous contact-conditioned hip balance: development trial

On the matched teacher-free development run, six birds reached the perch but none finished with sustained two-foot opposed support; four fell after contacting it and two remained airborne at the horizon. Two others failed at slalom A. One bird achieved 185 consecutive intervals of the half-weight bilateral opposing-normal criterion, then fell; the approach-only reference had 169 intervals for that bird and also fell. Another lost the reference's transient opposition, so this is not a reliable improvement. All accepted endpoint q, velocity, policy outputs and contacts matched the approach-only run before each first perch contact; substep coverage was complete without overflow. The falling bodies pitched backward while still behind the longitudinal balance point; the lane-0 same-seed teacher reference reached that point and held. This is one development course and seed, not a biological, release, or final-layout qualification.

# Recovery-label imitation experiment

Continue from the initial imitation policy using all qualified original teacher demonstrations and qualified student-prefix recovery demonstrations. Exclude failed first episodes. Reserve the highest qualified lane in each recovery course for development validation; preserve the original lane split. Give student-visited prefix observations four times the weight of teacher continuation observations, and balance waypoint stages. Sample every fifth control step excluding the first. Train with fixed Adam learning rate 0.0001 for 120 epochs, retaining the previous head and beak outputs. Evaluate the resulting policy with teacher disabled from full starts on development layouts; validation imitation error is not a navigation success measure. These development layouts and seeds are not final held-out evidence.

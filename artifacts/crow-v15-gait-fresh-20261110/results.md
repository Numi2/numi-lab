# Fresh-seed bounded gait and push-off result

The predeclared screen passed on both fresh seeds with the research v15 body, hybrid SAT CCD and 16-event host: all 16 push lanes passed the stated 10-second gait limits and latched early; none of the 16 matched no-push lanes latched early. No native physical or solver failure was reported. The no-push lanes became airborne later, after the fallback flight sequence, so final airborne state alone does not establish push-off causation.

Forward progress was 0.11028–0.11437 m, the largest per-foot 95th-percentile sampled contact slip was 0.03134 m/s, and the largest individual sampled slip was 0.08178 m/s. Both feet achieved at least ten qualifying clear swings per push lane. Accepted endpoint support and slip samples do not reconstruct all substep contacts or peak instantaneous slip.

Paired states were identical at the sampled initial and pre-push steps. At the early latch, the push lanes rose at least 0.02037 m more than their matched controls, while sampled wing-angle change was at most 0.000000953 rad. This supports a leg-driven initial rise within this test, but does not measure all substep motion or establish biological realism. The starts produced very similar gait trajectories; the finite seed count and limited perturbation distribution do not establish broad robustness.

Repeating the two exposed seeds with a 16-event budget passed the same screen, documented separately in crow-v15-gait-budget-20261110.

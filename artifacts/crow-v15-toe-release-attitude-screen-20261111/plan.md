# Pitch feedback during physical toe release and return

The retained stationary perch runs released a foot but lost opposing grip while torso pitch grew from approximately 0.18 to 1 rad. Screen signed bounded pitch and pitch-rate feedback on both physical hip drives after release only, gains -0.75, -0.25, 0.25 and 0.75 with two constructed starts each. Preserve the .5 position balance controller and every contact, foot action, release rule and regrip rule. Compare pre-release states against the retained .5 control. Require the same 10 supported, clear-foot release intervals, 100 terminal intervals of bilateral opposing contact, no fall, no nonfoot impulse and complete substep observations. This is a constructed isolated perch trial, not a landed flight demonstration or calibration.

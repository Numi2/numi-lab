# Reduced stride development control

In the integrated baseline, one of eight starts briefly lost both-foot support during a rapid transfer. Holding targets by upward velocity or requiring three loaded samples degraded other starts. Test a 19 percent reduction of the existing sinusoidal walking joint-command amplitude, keeping timing, observed contact gating, toe drives, controller and launch unchanged. First run the same eight used starts for 300 intervals and measure all contact substeps, actual foot/toe shape clearance, individual swing bouts, forward displacement and toe speed caps. This is a controlled candidate, not a claim that support, takeoff, navigation, landing or biology are qualified. Preserve the unchanged baseline and reject a trade that harms other starts.

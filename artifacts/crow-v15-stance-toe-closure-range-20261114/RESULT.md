# Stance foretoe closure range: transient improvement, failed grip

The two constructed trial lanes had exact logged states and targets through zero-based interval 281. With post-return stance distal/proximal coupling ramped to 0.85 versus 1.0, the longest qualified bilateral-grip streak was eight versus eleven intervals; the first post-return stance opposing-contact gate loss was interval 302 versus 304. Both released and returned, but failed the terminal 100-interval grip requirement and fell below 0.1 m root height at one-based interval 370. Before falling both reached the modeled toe speed limit. All 450 states per lane and required toe substeps were present and finite, with no observer errors.

The larger target improved this narrow transient but did not prevent the subsequent body fall. Neither result qualifies gait or landing; contact/state epoch alignment remains unproven. Both raw arms and all failures are retained.

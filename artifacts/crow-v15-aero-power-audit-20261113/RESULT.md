# Result: modeled aerodynamic work in a failed training launch

Observer-only research run; no controller was qualified. Exact parity with the previous observer was confirmed for all eight lanes at sampled state, policy action and contact for ticks 1-400 and contact substeps 335-400. All first episodes reached 400; lane configurations 2-7 remain launch failures with foot recontact. The aerodynamic kernel input exactly matched the separately sampled substep-start state across ticks 348-400. The four recorded body forces account for the total to 1.2e-10 N. FP64 body twist uniform-translation check passed exactly for the sampled state; the ABI defines force and torque in world axes and torque about the COM. This does not validate the unobserved simulation state or the aerodynamic model.

Across ticks 376-400, step 0.02 s and 16 substeps, integrated modeled aerodynamic force and torque against corresponding substep-start FP64 body velocities gives signed work -0.614, -0.594, -0.704, -0.668, +0.395 and +0.797 J for lanes 2-7 respectively. The simultaneous wing effort times accepted joint displacement proxy is 2.48, 2.46, 2.64, 2.56, 2.42 and 2.02 J. These are computed quantities in the simulator, not measured biological muscle work or a complete energy budget. Lanes 0 and 1 use a separate high-effort baseline and are not comparable bounded-wing candidates.

The base shader contains a direction-fixed empirical unsteady term as well as lift, drag and yaw terms. Signed work alone does not determine which term caused the difference, whether a model assumption is appropriate, or whether the solver is defective. The actual run wind configuration has not yet been captured directly, so energy/passivity claims are not warranted. A same-state term ablation and actual dispatch capture are the next checks. Retain all failures and the existing plan; final unseen layouts and seeds remain unused.

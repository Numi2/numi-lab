Observe per-body world-frame force and COM torque around the existing wing-load kernel, and pair those records with logged substep-start body COM twists calculated with the existing FP64 model. Sum F dot v + torque dot angular velocity by body and over each modeled substep. Report wing effort times measured joint displacement separately as a proxy, not an independent muscle-work measurement.

Run exactly the previous supported-wing transition push seed 20261126, eight correlated lanes and 16 substeps, through tick 400, teacher off. Trace ticks 348-400. Prior observer records must match sampled state, actions and contact trace, and summed force must agree with the per-body force deltas. A mismatch, missing record, or failed episode invalidates the comparison. Per-body torques must be world-frame and COM-centered as defined by the engine ABI. Confirm sampled q/v timing against kernel input before interpreting power; absent that confirmation label as an approximation. Preserve source, input hashes, failure logs, analysis and outcome.

The audit is diagnostic, not calibration, and is not evidence of crow flight or flight-controller qualification. No final layout or held-out seed is used. Wind and substep timing must be verified from the actual run configuration. Do not infer a solver error or nonphysical energy source from signed power alone. No production model or controller changes are authorized by this audit.

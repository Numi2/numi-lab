# Contact status and body-velocity audit

The final task contact buffer was populated, with successful status and nonzero active contacts at every audited frame. Its body stride was 41 and its separate scene stride was 6: the earlier theory that body index 35 was out of range is contradicted for this run. The materialized root body state in the buffer used by task completion had zero linear velocity at every audited frame, whereas the accepted generalized velocity reached about 0.17–0.18 m/s in its largest root component. Body positions differed from accepted coordinates by up to 0.23 mm, consistent with a difference in representation or timing. This is a diagnosis of the sensor's input, not evidence that the contact solver failed.

The observer reproduced all 2,400 endpoint states and 38,400 contact substeps of the preceding run exactly. The zero-coded feedback therefore cannot be treated as a measurement of the real contact-point velocity. A correct experiment must materialize articulated body velocities from accepted q/v at the appropriate physical time, preserve existing task observations and reward semantics, and independently verify the resulting sensor before testing gait feedback.

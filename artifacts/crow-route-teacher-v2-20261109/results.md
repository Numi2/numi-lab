# Slower elevated route teacher

The collision-enabled v2 teacher failed the prespecified screen: 58/64 first episodes physically terminated and five reached all five waypoints; six survived to the horizon without completing all gates. Most candidate contacts were right-wing/gate-right or left-wing/slalom-A. Unlike v1, no final-published contact record included the body and perch, but most lanes did not reach the perch; this is not a proven safe approach. The full distribution, pre-contact states, shader and raw samples are retained.

The failure pattern is consistent with advancing lateral aim immediately when the root enters a waypoint sphere while the wings still overlap the previous obstacle. That interpretation needs a matched clearance-timed command experiment. The teacher is not qualified.

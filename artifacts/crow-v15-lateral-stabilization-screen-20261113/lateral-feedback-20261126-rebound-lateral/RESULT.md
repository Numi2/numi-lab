# Lateral stabilization: engineering screen failed

The corrected sensor interpretation and test were preregistered in commit c5f953cc63121948f4da40017d5f7a8db16953bb. On used seed 20261126, all four controlled lanes 4–7 survived 1200 ticks without observed post-release ground contact, nonfoot impulse or toe-speed threshold event; controls 2–3 matched their entire same-lane baseline exactly, and candidates matched through tick 450. Accepted states and declared contact, toe, target and flap-effort sample ranges were complete. Observer masks matched, there were no observer errors or duplicate accepted states, and candidate applied-target reconstruction from accepted state had maximum errors below 8e-8 rad. Flap effort reached the model limit of 3.0.

From tick 500 to 1200, maximum absolute sideways displacement was 2.833–2.855 m, compared with 10.756–10.784 m in same-run controls; the preregistered maximum was 1.0 m, so all four candidates failed. Sampled late maximum absolute sideways speed was 0.315–0.317 m/s, within the 0.4 m/s limit; x advance was 5.127–5.137 m, with root height 0.524–0.858 m. The matched controls 0 and 1 terminated physically, and all of their records are retained. This is a useful reduction in drift, not straight-flight qualification, maneuver competence, disturbed-start recovery, full mission success, biological realism or a frozen controller. The second development seed was not run because the preregistered selection gate failed; final layouts and seeds remain unused.

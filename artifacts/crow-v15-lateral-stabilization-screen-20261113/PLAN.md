# Lateral velocity stabilization, used-seed development screen

The posthoc audit of the retained 24-second runs found 10.75–10.88 m of sideways displacement over ticks 500–1200, despite 4.22–4.31 m of forward displacement. The original x-only straight-flight screen did not gate this, so its historical pass is not evidence of straight flight. The subsequent bank feedback test avoided ground contact but retained large lateral drift and did not achieve useful bidirectional heading commands. Neither controller is qualified or frozen.

Test a common physical position-target feedback law on the same research body, launch, no-obstacle scene, aero and seed 20261126. Controls 2–3 remain the exact baseline; all candidate lanes 4–7 use the same controller. From 9.0–9.5 s smoothly ramp in bounded flap asymmetry and maintain it through the 24 s test. Compute target roll as clamp(0.18*yaw-frame lateral velocity, -0.10, 0.10) rad, then differential flap amplitude as the onset times clamp(0.08*(target_roll - accepted_roll) - 0.025*accepted_body_x_angular_rate, -0.025, 0.025) rad. Apply opposite adjustments to physical left/right flap targets. This law damps lateral velocity; it is not a route controller or proof of position tracking. No body wrench, state reset, aerodynamic change or final layout use.

Require exact accepted q/v/policy-action/contact parity against same-lane prior baseline through tick 450 and full-horizon parity for baseline controls, unchanged masks and policy bytes, accepted states throughout the first episode, contact/toe/target coverage from tick 335 and flap effort from tick 348 through the end, actuator targets bounded as specified, no nonfoot impulse, no foot recontact after release, and no toe-speed threshold events. For this preliminary engineering screen require all four candidate lanes to complete 1200 ticks, sampled height 0.25–1.5 m over ticks 500–1200, positive world-x velocity up to 1 m/s over ticks 550–1200, x advance above 1 m from 500 to 1200, maximum absolute world-y displacement from tick 500 no greater than 1 m and absolute world-y velocity no greater than 0.4 m/s over ticks 550–1200. These are explicit development thresholds, not biological targets or physical qualification; first failures and full logs are retained. Only a pass justifies repeating on the other used development seed. Heading control, commanded turns, climb/descent, disturbed starts, takeoff, landing and final unused layouts remain unqualified.

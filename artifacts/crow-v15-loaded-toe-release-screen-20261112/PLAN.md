# Rear-toe release with supporting-leg stroke

The supporting-leg stroke increased movement but caused sustained rear-toe drag. In one retained development pose the sole was 5.68 mm clear while the rear toe was 1.18 mm below the ground clearance plane; the opposite foot carried much greater normal impulse. Frozen-pose forward kinematics indicates a -0.1 rad proximal / -0.075 rad distal rear-toe displacement moves the distal shape to about 1.71 mm clearance, but this is not an integrated dynamic prediction, a measured crow joint value or a biological target.

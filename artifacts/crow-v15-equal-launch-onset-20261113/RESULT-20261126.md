# Equal onset: first used-seed screen

On development seed 20261126, both candidate lanes first became unloaded at interval 375, remained unloaded with no foot or nonfoot impulse through interval 500, and completed the first episode without a reported physical failure or toe-speed threshold. All expected launch-aero, contact, leg and toe observations were present. Lanes 0–6 exactly matched their original same-seed states, velocities, actions and contacts through 500; lane 7 matched through 368, before the onset change. Other recorded host and policy hashes matched. Before initial release, the modeled net vertical aerodynamic impulse was +0.0000761 and +0.0000718 N s, while actual flap displacement was approximately 0.236 rad in each lane. Moving wings, the coupled model and the held-leg results prevent concluding that wings were irrelevant or legs were necessary for eventual flight.

This is a 500-interval test on one already-used seed. The other used-seed trial, long flight, turns, climb/descent, disturbed-start recovery, controller freeze and autonomous supported landing remain separate gates. Full raw diagnostics and replay are retained; the extracted window is reproducible and excluded as a duplicate.

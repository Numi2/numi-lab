# Preregistered full-foot contact feedback test

The preceding contact-prepared trial only knew about one rear and one front toe group; it therefore could trigger while another modeled toe or the foot remained loaded. This experiment adds a per-side indicator for ground contact on the modeled foot and all eight toe-segment collision bodies, using the same accepted-contact impulse threshold of 1e-7 Ns and the existing research buffer. A missing indicator still means only that no qualifying contact was observed at the capture instant; it does not prove the foot was airborne between samples. Sensor validity, one-step-old control feedback, cutoff, body/surface selection and checkpoint/rollback remain unqualified.

In the same two development seeds and 4–6s window, keep velocity quantization and toe bits, but use two bits rather than three per ramp and reserve two bits for the full-foot indicators. On candidate lanes 4–7, increment a three-level per-foot ramp when that foot has no observed full-foot contact while the opposite foot does; hold through observed rear-only contact; otherwise decay by one. Even candidate lanes rise one step, odd two. Offsets are (-.015,+.025) rad knee/ankle for lanes 4–5 and (-.030,+.050) for lanes 6–7. Retain the 4.02–4.22 second fade and existing physical position drives; no injected body force. Lanes 0–3 remain unmodified controls.

Audit bit-state transitions and compare the full-foot indicators with same-step logged final-substep per-foot contact impulse, explicitly reporting any timing mismatch. Require parity of control lanes and pre-intervention samples, the same support, toe-speed, nonfoot, changing-contact-speed, progress, recovery, knee-phase and >=15% toe-delay gates. Record every outcome and toe order. Two-second correlated development results are not a gait, biological, reset/checkpoint, takeoff, grip, flight, navigation or landing qualification.

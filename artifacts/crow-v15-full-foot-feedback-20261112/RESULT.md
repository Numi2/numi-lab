# Full-foot feedback: sensor exercised; gait rejected

All eight candidate lanes failed at least one previously defined physical gate, and none passed the combined physical and toe-timing screen. The only candidate with a >=15% reduction in median rear-to-front toe-loading interval was seed 20261127 lane 6 (ratio 0.82 relative to its same-lane no-placement control), which failed both the support and recovery-coverage gates. Other ratios were 0.92–1.01. A gait improvement on one correlated two-second development run is not a qualification.

Candidate lanes 4–7 had 1, 0, 1, 2 both-feet-below-threshold substeps on seed 20261126 and 3, 1, 1, 1 on seed 20261127. Seven of eight candidates therefore failed the strict zero-substep gate; tiny impulses and thresholding mean this flag does not itself prove a fall. Other failures included changing-contact-point speed, recovery coverage and knee timing. No candidate toe-speed caps or nonfoot ground impulses were recorded. The toe detector found 56 and 55 rear-first modeled touchdowns and no reversed complete touchdowns in the respective batches. This supports a modeled event-order observation only; it does not establish a biological gait fit.

The research buffer's per-side full-foot ground-contact flags were checked against final-substep logged summed impulses for 99 transitions per candidate side per seed: one of 1,584 comparisons disagreed. The sensor uses individually thresholded active contacts while the trace uses summed required constraints, so timing, aggregation and buffer differences remain. Its rise/hold/fall bits matched their prescribed rule for all checked transitions. The prior dedicated sensor’s cutoff, reset, one-step action timing and checkpoint limits still apply. Lanes 0–3 matched the negative-ankle controls in the recorded state, toe contacts and q/v, and pre-intervention recorded state/contact samples matched the uniform baseline. This validates only the checked records, not all simulator state or rewards.

No controller is promoted. The other-corvid knee and ankle measurements remain exploratory references: the model's anatomy, projection, speed, surface and measurement convention are not validated as matching the reference bird. Stable contact-driven walking, leg-only takeoff, grip/release, flight qualification, and uninterrupted autonomous navigation and supported landing on two unused layouts and seeds remain open.

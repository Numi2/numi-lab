# Supporting-hip pitch feedback during release and regrip

The contact-clearance hold gave one lane ten independently qualified release intervals but neither lane achieved a qualified regrip; after return, forward torso pitch and velocity grew before fall. The current model's supporting hip rotates about local +Y. If the right foot is fixed by perch contacts, a positive hip rotation tends to oppose positive torso pitch, but actual compliant contacts can slip, so test rather than assume that sign.

Starting with the identical 450-step clearance-hold controller and constructed-start lanes, latch the actual accepted torso pitch at the instant the release phase begins. During release and return, use the right hip's existing physical position drive to add a bounded correction proportional to measured pitch error and world-Y angular velocity: gains 0.4 and 0.8 in the paired lanes, rate coefficient 0.04 seconds, correction clamp +/-0.12 rad and slew <=0.005 rad per interval. Enable the desired correction only while measured right-foot upward support and opposing front/rear toe contact are present; otherwise slew it back toward zero. Keep existing sagittal balance, hip-lift hold, toe opening, lowering, contact-triggered grip, model, surface, solver and all other actions unchanged. Log the latched reference, pitch, rate, support, commanded correction and actual joint state, and require exact q/v parity against the prior trial through the interval before release begins.

Qualification is unchanged: complete 450 accepted intervals without reset or rollback, ten consecutive independently checked left-foot/toe-clear intervals with right support and opposing grip, then 100 final consecutive bilateral supported opposing-grip intervals, with no fall, nonfoot impulse or joint-limit violation. Check target/actual bounds and all 32 observer substeps; stop interpreting at first fall. An incomplete trial or failure to release/regrip is failure. World-Y rate is an approximation to pitch rate in tilted 3-D poses, and these gains are engineering hypotheses, not crow calibration. This is a constructed-perch test and does not use reserved layouts or seeds.

# Supported-hip pitch feedback: clearance, but no regrip

The constructed stationary two-lane v15 test ran all 450 intervals without native solver errors. Accepted q/v states exactly matched the prior control through the release decision (135 and 157 states); the independent substep observer matched accepted foot impulses within 6.21e-10 N s. The right-hip pitch target was bounded and slew limited; this does not establish substep torque bounds.

Neither lane passed. Lane 0 reached nine consecutive qualified clearance intervals and fell at step 336. Lane 1 achieved ten independently verified consecutive supported-clearance intervals (steps 199–208), began return at step 209, and fell at step 322. It achieved no interval satisfying all bilateral upward support, opposing toe contact, tilt and velocity gates before the fall. A separate aggregate analyzer reports 11 clearance intervals because it uses a different accepted-state convention; the independently checked ten-interval streak is the conservative result. The controller and this constructed start are not a supported landing or an autonomous mission. The test does not qualify a flight controller or establish biological realism.

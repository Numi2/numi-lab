# Knee-specific recovery phase advance: exploratory screen

Prior v15 800 Hz development measurements put the geometric knee minimum late in recovery, whereas the published magpie mean is earlier. Earlier ankle-delay screens regressed foot-contact measures. Test a different, bounded hypothesis: advance only the knee flexion waveform during physically gated swing, leaving stance target and ankle unchanged. This is a controller engineering test informed by a related species, not a direct crow anatomical fit.

Use common v15 exploratory gait and substep observer, two previously used seeds (20261126, 20261127), 300 20 ms transitions, eight correlated lanes per seed. Lanes 0–1 are exact zero-warp controls; lanes 2–3, 4–5 and 6–7 use cycle-phase warps 0.08, 0.16 and 0.24. Knee flexion uses sin(pi*(u + a*sin(pi*u))), for u in [0,1], only while the original other-foot-support gate and original lift are positive. Endpoints remain zero; a <= .24 preserves monotonic warp. The same warped knee lift modifies the existing two knee drive contributions, with no change to ankle or hip. Compare same-lane against previously retained uniform gait baseline for seed 20261127; verify controls exactly match through all observable steps, pre-intervention parity for all lanes, and archive source identities.

Exploratory candidate gate: complete run, zero foot-unloaded substeps, zero toe speed cap and zero non-foot ground impulse during 4–6 s; each side contact-speed p95 no more than 1.5x matched baseline, root-forward displacement >=0.05 m; at least one complete recovery per foot and median knee-minimum phase at least 5 percentage points earlier than matched baseline without fewer complete windows. The ankle proxy and contact-time definitions have unverified anatomy; incomplete cycles must be counted. Analyze both seeds and all declared arms regardless of outcome. Seeds and lanes are development samples, not independent animals; 50 Hz contact-point speed is not integrated slip. No promotion/freeze from this screen; do not infer species accuracy or whole mission success.

Before running the second seed or any baseline arm, add a freshly executed same-host same-shader baseline for both seeds. This supplies a same-lane baseline for seed 20261126 and independently checks the retained seed-20261127 baseline. The candidate already running on 20261127 is unchanged. The amended procedure does not revise any intervention or acceptance gate.

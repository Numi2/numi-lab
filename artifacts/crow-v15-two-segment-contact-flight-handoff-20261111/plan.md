# Contact-triggered wing handoff after physical jump

The matched wing-suppressed comparison produced a leg-powered jump in all eight development starts; seven then hit toe speed caps when falling back to the ground. Keep the same walk, bounded leg push, body, solver and frozen V2 flight source. In an isolated wrapper, hold wing actions and flapping off during grounded preparation. After the extension is armed, release the frozen wing controller only after three accepted consecutive unloaded foot-contact samples, positive vertical velocity above 0.05 m/s, and root height above 0.195 m. The low-level flight law and route policy weights do not change. Retain and compare accepted states with the wing-suppressed push run through interval 350 before the new release can activate. Observe 600 intervals without resets or teacher authority, all 16 contact substeps, toe speeds, root height, attitude and velocity. Report first release eligibility, time airborne, any ground/nonfoot contacts, toe cap hits and solver/termination failures; require at least 150 consecutive post-jump intervals with root height >0.20 m, no foot or ground nonfoot impulse, root tilt <=0.5 rad, no toe speed cap, and complete data. This is a fixed-time launch experiment, not a readiness-gated controller, comprehensive flight qualification, collision-free route or landing, and the height threshold is a development parameter.

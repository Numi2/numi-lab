# Conditional contact-triggered launch results

The conditional task activated the intended physical leg controller. The state observer matched 32/32 observed and unobserved q/v samples exactly. Across 24 perturbed development starts, all push-trigger trials latched on unloading, remained clear of ground after the latch and finished the final second airborne at least 0.15 m above the initial root. None of 24 no-push controls latched early; those controls became airborne later when the wing timeout restarted flapping. The fixed-timer push trials recontacted after the latch in all 24 cases, despite ultimately becoming airborne. There were no native physical or solver failures, and pre-extension q/v matched across each paired arm. The two additional seeds had been exposed in the invalid variant and are not untouched confirmation. This is a controlled launch transition in the simulated model, with aerodynamic forces after release; it is not a biologically validated takeoff, sustained walk or full navigation and landing.

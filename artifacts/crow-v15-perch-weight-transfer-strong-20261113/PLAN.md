At step 306 the stronger lane remained at 0.0234 m with roll target approximately 0.10 rad, well inside the unchanged 0.25 rad cap. Test gains 2.0 and 4.0 on the same paired constructed-start lanes, with all other body parameters, commands, slew rate, force sources, grip feedback, 32-substep solver and success/readiness gates unchanged. The hypothesis is that greater bounded proportional control reaches the existing readiness gate before release while maintaining bilateral opposing contact; the subsequent 10-interval contact-clear release and terminal 100-interval bilateral supported grip are the unchanged qualification requirements. Require exact pre-intervention q/v match against the archived original and prior transfer baselines, complete observer coverage, all target bounds, no fall, no reset and no solver rollback. Failure to release is failure; an unsafe release or any incomplete trace also fails. This is a constructed-start probe, not a full landing, a flight test or biological calibration.

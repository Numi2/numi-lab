# Wing-load observation during the failed bank screen

The instrumented 800-step development rerun exactly matched all 72 retained q/v/policy samples from the uninstrumented bank run. All 9,600 expected wing-wrench records were present in the two selected one-second windows, with finite values and no observer errors. The first build attempt failed because a private include path was missing; its log is retained, and the rebuilt observer passed the parity check. The observer copies the wrench buffer immediately before and after the existing flapping kernel; it does not modify the shaders. Each wing torque is about that wing body's COM in world coordinates. Root-kernel torque excludes the moment of wing forces about the root, and includes whatever the full kernel writes at the root; it is not the total aerodynamic yaw or proof of a particular closure's activation.

For the 0.12-amplitude turn lanes, mean left/right vertical wing forces over 9–10 s were 2.192/2.101 N and 2.101/2.196 N, respectively; over 14–15 s they were 2.151/2.160 N and 2.158/2.151 N. For the 0.24 lanes the corresponding first-window imbalance was about 0.21–0.22 N, while the later-window imbalance was about 0.01 N or less. For the 0.36 lanes the first-window imbalance was about 0.65–0.68 N and later values varied substantially; the prior screen recorded substantial altitude gain and steering in the wrong direction at its final sample. These are averages of fast-changing modeled forces, not measured crow data or proof that a specific controller or actuator saturated. The combined modeled vertical forces are roughly comparable to the modeled 0.45006-kg body's weight in the low-amplitude cases, but that alone does not establish force or power realism.

Full q/v ranges and all three vector wrenches are retained at native substeps in the compressed trace. The two windows do not establish the complete maneuver, net yaw/roll moment about the root, or the cause of reduced asymmetry. Computing that moment requires each wing COM position and orientation on the same pre-kernel source state, and correlating with actuator targets and body attitude. The old controller's direct moments were still present before 9 s, and no wing-only flight, biological, navigation or landing qualification follows.

# Result: bounded flap amplitude did not achieve launch

Both first episodes ran 400 ticks, matched push/hold state/action/contact records through tick 345, and target-history instrumentation verified that the previous target matches the preceding control-step target in every recorded tick 336-400 for lanes 2-7. The observer also matched exact aerodynamic input q/v with substep-start q/v, recorded total force within 1.4e-10 N of summed body forces, and passed its translation check. In the sampled still-air window ticks 376-400, no push or hold lane had positive total aero power above 1e-6 W.

At 0.25-rad target half-amplitude, neither of the two correlated lanes achieved three unloaded intervals. At 0.45 rad, both released at tick 375 and recontacted tick 378; at 0.65 rad, release was tick 358/359 and recontact tick 363. None passes the preregistered launch screen. Whole-body COM peak rise was 0.0128-0.0268 m. Toe-speed threshold counts were 3,3; 2,2; and 11,12 respectively. Early push-minus-hold measured ground impulse was 0.051-0.132 Ns through tick 355 and 0.080-0.146 Ns through 357, which does not isolate leg-only muscle impulse.

In the later half-second, target RMS errors were approximately 0.293, 0.431 and 0.572 rad for the three settings, and mean modeled upward aerodynamic force was 0.324-0.431 N for model weight about 4.42 N. These metrics describe this particular uncalibrated body, projected closure and controller; they are neither real-crow performance nor proof of a universal aerodynamic deficit. Lanes are correlated, the layout and seed are development data, and body forces, energy, muscles and motion remain unvalidated. The controller is not frozen or promoted; flight, landing and full-route qualification remain outstanding. Preserve all inputs, observer, code, binaries, paired logs and failed replay.

# Coordinated hip, knee and ankle lift: incomplete

The same-binary matched experiment completed all 300 steps without episode resets, physical failure flags, nonfoot contacts or toe velocity caps. State and contact parity held through the 200-step prewalk prefix. On previously used development seed 20261127, all eight lanes achieved three left and four right supported-clearance bouts. Forward travel was 1.11–1.40 times the no-extra-stroke held control. Median loaded endpoint contact-point speed was 0.00396 m/s versus 0.00276 m/s for control; p95 was 0.04059 versus 0.03484 m/s, within the prespecified 1.5x limits.

It failed two prespecified gates. Both-foot-zero-normal-impulse samples increased from zero to 4–6 per lane, clustered around steps 265–266 and 290–291; these short threshold events do not by themselves prove a fall. Sole-clear/toe-low endpoint counts increased from 49 to 201 versus the held control. The extra small-stroke sham had 686. Of the candidate 201 low-toe events, 132 had some normal impulse on that foot over the same 20 ms interval and 69 did not; control counts were 14 and 35. Consequently this count cannot all be called dragging: some may be normal toe support. Event time is an endpoint whereas normal impulse is integrated across the preceding interval; neither measures integrated slip.

During high positive carrier, median joint-angle differences against the matched small-stroke sham were hip -0.054/-0.060 rad, knee +0.116/+0.127 rad and ankle -0.063/-0.070 rad (left/right). These reflect diverged trajectories, not isolated actuator tracking. The candidate is not promoted. This short used-seed screen cannot establish real-bird gait or qualify takeoff, flight, landing or autonomous navigation.

# Returning-foot placement ablation: improved transfer, failed grip gate

In the 450-interval constructed paired trial, logged state and targets matched until zero-based interval 284. With the extra placement ramp disabled, the returning foot first established opposing contact at interval 295 and stance grip first failed at 300; control saw those events at 297 and 294 respectively. The candidate achieved four qualifying bilateral intervals, longest consecutive streak two, versus zero for control. Neither met the terminal 100-interval bilateral-grip gate, and the root fell below 0.1 m at one-based intervals 371 and 366 respectively. Both lanes reached the 12 rad/s toe-speed cap before falling. All substeps were present and valid; the detailed raw evidence is retained. Contact/state epochs have not been independently synchronized. This is not walking, landing, or full-mission qualification.

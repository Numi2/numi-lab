# Result: visible pitch and yaw disturbance screen

On the same used development seed, all eight first episodes survived 24 seconds; all pre-pulse state/contact samples matched their no-disturbance counterparts. No body, wing or toe velocity-cap events or nonfoot contacts occurred in the flight window. Signed pitch disturbances reached 0.01456–0.01457 radians and signed yaw reached 0.01631–0.01636 radians, exceeding the predeclared 0.01-radian observability minimum. Their average 20–24-second tracking differences in every measured attitude, height and horizontal-velocity component remained below the declared thresholds. This is a bounded test of response to a small externally imposed pulse, not broad recovery qualification. Signed roll reached 0.00858–0.00859 radians, so both roll arms were inconclusive, not passes. Early nonflight toe-speed caps were observed in lanes 1 and 2 and are preserved in the results.

The next experiment increases only the external roll pulse. These tests use a development seed, lack true perturbed starts and do not qualify commanded turns, route, landing or biological aerodynamics.

# Wings-held leg push: isolated development screen

On development seed 20261126, all eight correlated lanes passed the preregistered wings-held initial-push screen. The push arm first had three successive unloaded intervals at 376; the matched held-leg arm had no such event before interval 401. The push arm recontacted at 382 while its wings remained held. Across lanes the push-minus-hold vertical foot impulse over intervals 371–376 was 0.17087–0.17165 N s; integrated modeled vertical aerodynamic impulse before unloading was about -0.000295 N s and maximum pre-unload flap displacement 0.00219–0.00220 rad. No nonfoot impulse or toe-speed threshold crossing was recorded in the screen.

Within each pair the observed state, actions and contacts matched through interval 368, and in intervals 335–500 the wing, tail and toe targets matched: only the six leg targets differed, first at interval 373. Both arms used the same non-shader inputs. Recorded samples were complete with no observer errors. See `isolation-audit.json`, `all-command-audit.json`, `transition-audit.json` and `analysis-corrected.json` for the precise thresholds, clocks, all lane results and raw run references.

This is evidence of an isolated modeled leg-powered unload on a used development seed, not a biological validation or a sustained flight. Force/contact/state clocks have not been independently aligned and neutral wings can still generate passive aerodynamic forces. The distinct continuous transition and leg-necessity screens remain failed here. In the earlier flapping candidate the held-leg counterfactual also took off; this experiment does not repair that failure. The lanes are correlated rather than eight independent seeds. Neither landing nor navigation is qualified.

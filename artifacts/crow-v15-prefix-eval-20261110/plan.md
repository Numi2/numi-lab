# Teacher-off prefix-actor development evaluation

Evaluate the single-trajectory prefix actor from `crow-v15-prefix-learning-20261110` and its original actor, each from full physical reset with teacher entirely disabled, using the same v15 body, research host, frozen flight law and bounded-rebound shader. Use previously exposed development J seed 3193706597 and I seed 4038889268; one first 7000-step episode per pair, no intermediate resets. Candidate J is evaluated first, followed by its control and the candidate/control I pair. Record exact pack hashes, first-episode waypoint progress, first-failure collider/step, physical/solver failure, minimum accepted-step obstacle clearance, bilateral support and opposing toe impulses. The initial policy is not retrained between these evaluations. These are development results and do not establish unseen-layout generalization. K/L remain sealed.

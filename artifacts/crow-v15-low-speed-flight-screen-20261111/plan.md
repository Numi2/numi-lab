On the used development seed, compare six constant raw speed requests 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25 and 0.35 m/s, corresponding to effective speed-error targets -0.05, 0.00, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15 and 0.25 m/s after the journey adapter, with two starts per target. The screen retains the v15 two-segment toe body, walking recovery, frozen flight source and 16 substeps, and disables course collision only. It substitutes straight level commands for the student and disables terminal-goal override solely in this diagnostic. Run a two-step admission first and retain failures. On admitted runs measure actual world/yaw-frame speed, altitude, attitude, ground and toe contacts, speed caps and physical termination over the first 1600 intervals. This is not a trained route, does not cover all disturbances or the coupled terminal commands, and does not qualify a landing or real bird.

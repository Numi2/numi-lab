# Slow straight flight characterization

A two-step 12-lane preflight admitted the current two-segment toe-equipped journey model and collision-disabled diagnostic path. In the 1600-step obstacle-free screen, all 12 first episodes survived with zero solver-failed steps and no measured ground impulse or toe speed-limit samples from step 400 onward. Six constant effective speed-error targets from -0.05 to +0.25 m/s each had two starts. Their six sampled velocities during steps 1000–1500 averaged approximately 0.043–0.053 m/s below the algebraic target; the zero-target starts averaged about -0.047 m/s. Root-coordinate height in these sampled windows ranged approximately 0.927–0.948 m. These are discrete samples, not continuous stability or a precise altitude calibration.

Four starts recorded 3, 6, 11 and 14 toe speed-limit substeps during initial settling, before step 400. All toe/contact substeps were covered, with no reported capacity overflow or native substep error. Course collisions were disabled and fixed straight commands replaced navigation; only two starts per target used one development seed. The test does not establish flight tracking under turns, combined terminal commands, disturbed recovery, toe release, supported landing, biological realism or unused-layout success. The frozen flight controller was unchanged; the adapter setpoint algebra has not yet been independently instrumented.

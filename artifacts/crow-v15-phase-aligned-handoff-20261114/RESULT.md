# Phase-aligned wing-law handoff: failed

Aligning the phase changed the target during the blend, but did not pass the preregistered 800-interval screen. Every first episode ended on contact (reason 3), at intervals 587, 642, 689, 736, 595, 666, 696 and 734. The six .75–.85-rad candidate lanes first recorded foot recontact at 619, 675, none before end, 619, 675 and none before end; the two .70-rad lanes recontacted at 396 and 389. All six stronger lanes recorded a nonfoot impulse after unloading, and four of those reached the modeled toe-speed threshold. Positive lower-beak/ground contacts occur one recorded step after every reported endpoint, but metadata and observation epochs are not independently aligned, so these observations do not prove the fatal pair.

All eight lanes exactly reproduced the preceding blended candidate's logged states through interval 500 and targets over 335–500. The first recorded target difference occurred at interval 504 in every lane. For example, in lane 2 at interval 515 the phase-aligned flap target was -0.293 rad versus +0.278 rad in the earlier blend; its neutral is +0.35 rad. Thus the intended phase change is visible in the actual target and avoids the prior near-neutral command at that instant. It changes the subsequent trajectory but is not sufficient for sustained flight. State/target/contact/toe coverage through each reported endpoint was complete, finite and without observer errors.

# Slow rear-toe closure control

The immediate rear closure no longer detects opposing contact after toe world damping removal. Compare the same immediate command (arm 0) with a slower ramp (arm 1), the same ramp with contact sensing suppressed (arm 2), and the ramp with toe collision disabled (arm 3), on the same eight starts and unchanged 50 mm perch. The contact-gated transition and bilateral opposing-impulse hold/release criterion remain unchanged. This is a developmental already-supported pinch, not landing or whole-sequence qualification.

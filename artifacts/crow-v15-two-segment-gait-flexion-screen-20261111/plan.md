# Physical knee flexion walking screen

The +hip and +knee runs failed physical swing and support gates. Independent forward kinematics at a retained baseline pose shows positive hip, knee and ankle joint increments lower the ipsilateral foot. Test hip/knee pairs (-.30,+.40), (-.30,-.40), (-.30,-.80), (-.50,-.80) by lane modulo four, with the same eight starting states, gait window, 16 substeps and remaining actions. The first pair reproduces lanes 0 and 4 of the prior direction screen. Require complete observations, >=15 mm forward progress, no unsupported or nonfoot impulse intervals, bounded root tilt and speed, and >=3 alternating physically clear sustained swings each side. Observe every control endpoint and all substep contact impulses. This screen is development only; no qualification of takeoff, landing, full navigation or biological realism is implied.

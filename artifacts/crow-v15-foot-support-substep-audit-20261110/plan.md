# Full-interval foot support observation

The balance law gates its hip and pitch corrections using only the final substep's upward foot/toe impulse. Extend the existing research observer to sum the actual upward normal impulses of bodies 10 and 13 and toe bodies 17-24 against the perch over all 16 substeps, without changing controller inputs. Replay the reference and the geometry-placement gain-0.5 case for the known successful start; require exact 200-state trajectories versus their prior runs. Compare each final-substep gate (>0.0002/16 N s) to the full-interval mean-force-equivalent gate (>0.0002 N s), including the post-release period, and report missed and extra intervals. This measures temporal sampling in one development start, not whether changing the gate repairs balance, walking or a flight landing. Preserve failed parity and all data.

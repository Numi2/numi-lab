# Full-interval foot support observation

Both observed trajectories reproduced all 200 prior states exactly. Across 200 intervals in each arm, the full-interval and last-substep support gates agreed in every interval, including all 114 and 111 release-phase intervals respectively. The reference gate was on in 114/114 release intervals and the position-feedback gate in 108/111. Thus missed support gating does not explain the observed post-release drift in this development case. This observation does not establish whether support sensing is adequate in other starts or a flying landing.

# Negative hip-roll weight-shift exploration

Repeat the repaired 35-body stationary contact experiment with the same contact-gated release, but test negative hip-roll targets -0.10, -0.18, -0.26 radians and zero control in two starts each. This is a signed direction test, not a demonstration of flight, walking, autonomous landing or a qualified controller. The control will be checked for trajectory equivalence to the zero lanes in the positive-shift experiment, and full substep contact, independent foot geometry and complete failed/censored outcomes are required.

# Mechanical ankle phase depends on the event definition

This audit replaces the prior moving toe-landmark ankle proxy with the rigid modeled foot axis and the physical shank-foot anchor. It reconstructs the same retained 800 Hz v15 development gait and applies the same contact-based recovery-window selection and 21.25 ms centered smoothing. Of 25 selected windows from eight highly related simulated starts, the ankle angle minimum occurred before the knee minimum in all 25. However, the knee reached a sustained one-degree increase from its own minimum 6.25 ms before the ankle did in all 25. These are two different phase proxies, not direct estimates of extension onset. The windows are not 25 independent birds or held-out experiments.

The earlier audit's ankle-first minima therefore do not by themselves contradict the reported knee-before-ankle extension onset in walking black-billed magpies. The one-degree proxy also does not establish agreement: anatomical axes, filters, speed, substrate and species differ, and the simulated crow's phase is driven by an experimental controller. Joint-anchor and replay-position validation, event exclusions and all window values are in analysis.json. No unsupported crow head or neck trajectory was introduced. This finding informs further gait design but does not qualify physical walking.

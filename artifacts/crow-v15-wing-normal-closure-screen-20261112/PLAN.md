# Orientation-sensitive stroke-closure screen

Keep the original v15 model and force magnitude as controls in two lanes; in six lanes, blend the empirical stroke-force direction 0.15, 0.35, or 0.60 toward the live wing-surface normal (mirrored consistently), with a fixed equal-sign normalized pronation action of +0.20/-0.20 after 12 s. The same uncalibrated closure coefficient remains, and altered force direction applies throughout each trial, so matched intervention-prefix equality is not expected for candidates. Use the same used seed, obstacle-free controls, 16 substeps, enabled wings, and disabled direct action body moments; compare control parity, airborne survival, speed, height, bank and both turning directions. This is a modeling sensitivity study, not a measured crow aerodynamic law, final controller, or held-out navigation.

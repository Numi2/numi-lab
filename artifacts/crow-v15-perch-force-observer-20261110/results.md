# Results

Observer-on and prior observer-off matched all 3001 sampled accepted q/v states. The native FP64 mass-weighted COM combined with final-substep contact points and impulses found the upward support center 10-15 mm ahead of the COM during early perch loading, growing past 100 mm as the body tipped. The measured contact moment around the COM became increasingly negative. Endpoint COM and final-substep contact points are an approximation, not continuous force or biological data.

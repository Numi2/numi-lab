# Contact-gated front-toe closure: failed

In the matched 12-counter return test, withholding the early front-toe closure did not yield any interval meeting the bilateral opposing-grip, posture and speed gates. Both lanes fell below 0.1 m root height at one-based interval 329. The candidate stance toe reached the accepted-state 12 rad/s threshold at zero-based tick 322, before the fall; the control reached it afterward. The experiment therefore fails the original gates and is not promoted. Accepted-state speeds do not bound substep peaks or effort.

The control reproduced every q/v state and logged target from the previous trial exactly; the two test lanes were identical through tick 253 and first differed at the return command on tick 254. All 32 contact substeps were present, with no recorded native/observer errors or nonfoot support, and the run binary hash matched its preregistered prebuild. In both lanes the returning front toes had zero positive opposing normal before the fall; at the sampled tick 270 their nearest box gaps were about 9.56 mm and 10.62 mm respectively. These observations motivate examining placement, not attributing the failure solely to early closure. Contact/state epochs remain independently unverified. This constructed perch probe does not establish walking, airborne landing or biological motion.

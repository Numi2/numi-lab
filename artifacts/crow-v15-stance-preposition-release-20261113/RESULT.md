# Bilaterally supported stance preposition: failed regrip

Both candidates matched baseline lane 1 for 121 accepted states before the first stance command. They completed the +0.075/-0.15 and +0.15/-0.30 rad right knee/ankle targets, with increments gated by at least 50 supported intervals and a maximum 0.025 fraction per interval; recorded target and accepted joint positions remained within the modeled limits. The trials ran 450 intervals with complete 32-substep observations and no native errors; accepted foot impulses matched the last-substep observer within 6.21e-10 N s.

Neither candidate achieved an interval satisfying the combined bilateral opposing-toe grip, posture and speed test; their root heights fell below 0.1 m at intervals 306 and 290, earlier than the prior baseline's interval 322. The independent clearance audit found a longest qualifying streak of nine intervals in lane 0 and ten in lane 1, although the internal accepted-state gate triggered return in each case; lane 0 therefore also fails the independently measured ten-interval clearance criterion. The smaller stance candidate retained the right opposing-toe condition more frequently during return (38 of 87 pre-fall intervals failed that gate versus 89 of 100 in the anticipatory baseline), but this does not establish stable grip. Accepted pre-fall left-toe speeds peaked at 7.30 and 3.73 rad/s; substep speed and effort are not measured. This constructed test is not an airborne landing.

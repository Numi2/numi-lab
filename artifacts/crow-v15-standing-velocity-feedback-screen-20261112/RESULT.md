# Standing feedback screen: action masked during bounce

The eight-lane development screen tried bounded, support-gated vertical feedback through the knee action before 2 s, including a zero-gain parity control. The zero-gain lane exactly matched all 151 state samples and 2,416 contact substeps of its retained control. The other lanes changed recorded action state early, but none changed q or v until step 102 or later, and the first changed contact records were also after 2 s. Counts of unloaded contact substeps during the first 2 s were identical to the control in every lane. Some later measurements differed; this does not demonstrate that the proposed feedback can correct the initial bounce.

Source inspection explains the discrepancy: standing curriculum zeroes the non-independent posture action contribution to the position target. Action history can change while physical targets remain at their default. This test cannot establish the effectiveness of physical knee feedback. Any subsequent test must apply a specifically bounded physical joint target during the standing interval and verify that the target and q/v actually respond, while retaining a zero-gain parity control. These are development starts, not a qualified gait.

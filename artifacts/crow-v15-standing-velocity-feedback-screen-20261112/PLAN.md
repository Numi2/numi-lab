# Standing vertical feedback exploratory screen

Hypothesis: measured vertical body velocity, fed back through existing knee position targets only while prior contact indicates support, can reduce unwanted early standing motion. Screen eight gains (including zero and opposite signs) before 2 seconds while retaining the earlier crouch and walk after 2 seconds. This is an exploratory intervention, not a validated biological controller. Different lanes are distinct perturbed starts; cross-lane differences are not a causal estimate. A zero-gain lane must exactly match its prior control, and any promising result requires a matched all-start comparison and full-sequence tests. Record fall, toe speed caps and contact support as well as COM velocity; improvement on one metric cannot establish stability.

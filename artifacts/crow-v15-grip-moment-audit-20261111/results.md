# Preliminary angular-momentum audit

The passive 300-tick replay matched all stored reference state traces exactly. We reconstructed bird/perch contact impulses across 16 substeps, whole-bird momentum from authored body inertias and kinematics, and an approximate gravity moment using interval endpoint COM. The reconstructed angular-momentum budget does not close reliably: in ticks 120–159 the mean absolute residual was 0.000151 N m s per control step, and in ticks 240–279 it was 0.000999 N m s, compared with mean contact angular impulse magnitudes of 0.00211 and 0.00221 N m s respectively. The residual can include omitted damping, other external forces, torsional impulse interpretation, contact readback, discretization and/or reconstruction errors. Consequently no conclusion about the cause of the tilt is drawn from this audit yet. Next verify the reconstructed momentum against the existing independent invariant API and separate torsional from force moment before attributing the residual.

# Paired stance foretoe closure with bounded returning-leg placement

In the preceding physical trial, raising the supporting foretoe target improved opposing-contact coverage but did not restore bilateral grip. As supporting contact persisted, the returning-leg placement command reached 1.0; earlier fixed-state geometry and a separate trial suggest full placement can move the returning foretoe away from the perch, though that earlier trial lacked the improved stance support. Test the combination in a paired 450-interval constructed-state run. Both lanes use the exact prior candidate's 1.20 rad stance foretoe cap and all other commands; lane 1 alone limits the existing dimensionless returning-leg placement to 0.20 rather than 1.0. No kinematic override or external force is added.

Lane 0 must reproduce the preceding candidate lane 1 exactly, and paired states must match until the placement target first differs. Check command bounds and 0.05 per interval slew, independent foot/toe clearance and full contact substeps. Require at least ten supported release intervals and 100 consecutive final intervals of bilateral opposing grip, supported height, bounded tilt and speed, and no nonfoot support; retain every failure and speed-limit occurrence. The model's toe limits and acceptance criteria are unchanged. This is a local physical controller experiment, not proof of a walk, landing or mission.

# Extended hold diagnostic

The five-second landing-mode trial generated front and rear toe contacts on both feet, but the longest consecutive span observed in its final-substep contact snapshots was only 34 intervals; root tilt peaked at 1.215 radians during capture. Rerun the same saved start and constructed landing condition for 500 intervals to determine whether a longer hold develops. This does not repair or erase the sharp initial tilt, and the relocated perch and manually selected landing mode remain limitations.

Require 100 consecutive intervals with independently checked bilateral opposed toe impulses and adequate vertical support, root tilt at most 0.35 radians and speed at most 0.12 m/s. Inspect full substep data as well as sampled contacts, nonfoot and ground contacts. Report the capture transient separately from the hold. Preserve this result regardless of outcome.

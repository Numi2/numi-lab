# Extended negative-gain recovery test

The signed balance screen did not prevent the capture tilt and did not improve the onset of the original hold. The negative arm nevertheless ended its 600-interval trial with 82 consecutive intervals meeting the full-substep support and balance checks, while the positive arm had no qualifying intervals. To distinguish a transient recovery from a sustained hold, repeat the negative arm for 1,000 intervals from the same reconstructed checkpoint and relocated-perch fixture. There are no intermediate resets. This is a new developmental trial, not an independent seed or layout and not a cure for its initial tipping.

Before the run, require a terminal run of at least 250 consecutive intervals with all 16 substeps observed, bilateral vertical support and opposed toe impulses, no ground or nonfoot impulse, root tilt at most 0.35 rad, and root speed at most 0.12 m/s. Verify identical input, host and shader hashes and record actual source/run commands, every reset, error and retained sample. A shorter hold, later fall or lost support refutes durable recovery under this criterion. This criterion alone does not qualify the original autonomous mission, full flight, or biological realism.

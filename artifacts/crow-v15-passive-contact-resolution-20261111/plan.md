# Passive first-contact time-resolution control

The same controlled landing start acquired grip at 16 substeps but failed at 32, while the free-space toe drives closely matched. To isolate whether differing controller decisions are necessary for the early discrepancy, start the identical v15 body 15 mm above the development perch with the same initial velocities and fixed default joint targets, and run separate 16- and 32-substep 80-tick trials with no policy feedback or injected forces. Record root and toe motion and all-substep normal/friction impulses grouped by rear toes, front toes, feet and other bodies. Compare the first ten samples, when contact begins. The trials are passive diagnostic controls; differences do not by themselves establish a solver defect, convergence, biological realism or landing qualification. Preserve all outcomes and no reserved layout is used.

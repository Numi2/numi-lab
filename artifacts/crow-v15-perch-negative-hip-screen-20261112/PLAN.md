# Opposite hip corrections under perch load

The previous contact-conditioned positive hip offsets +0.08, +0.16 and +0.24 rad made the difficult -0.04-rad-pitch start fall progressively earlier (121, 114, 113 control intervals versus 136 with no offset). A static foot-placement calculation failed to predict the loaded outcome. Test the opposite bounded offsets -0.08, -0.16 and -0.24 rad under the same controller and exact initial states, together with a zero-offset control. This tests a directional hypothesis about a loaded articulated body; it does not claim an anatomical crow joint target.

Each of four offsets has one +0.04- and one -0.04-rad initial pitch lane, with the same actuation gating and ramp as the rejected positive test. Require exact first ten intervals against the retained reference, full 32-substep coverage, no solver or observer failure, no nonfoot contact, no toe speed cap and 100 final intervals of bilateral support, opposing toe forces and pitch below 0.25 rad for both initial signs. Test all signs and retain failures. Even if a gain passes this narrow stationary bench it must not be promoted without perturbation, contact timing, explicit release/regrip, dynamic landing and biological validation.

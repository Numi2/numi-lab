# Earlier clearance feedback

The 0.002 rad/interval feedback begun after twelve qualifying intervals started only as foot clearance was already falling below threshold and failed to avert recontact. Compare the unchanged no-return single-stance baseline to a candidate which begins clearance feedback as soon as the existing open-toe, support and posture conditions establish lift readiness. Use the same 3-5 mm clearance deadband and 0.15 rad target limit, with at most 0.005 rad target change per 20 ms, while preserving all physical drive limits and gates. It controls an existing hip position target from observed clearance; no body or pose override. This is a hypothesis, not an assumption of stability.

Require exact 450-state reference parity for control, paired prefix through first target difference, audited activation and target bounds, full substeps and no errors, nonfoot contact or early toe-speed threshold. Require at least 100 consecutive intervals satisfying the preexisting release, clearance, opposing stance contact, posture and speed criteria. Record all failures and timing caveats. A pass is not walking, takeoff, flight, landing or mission qualification.

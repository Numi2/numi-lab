# Overlapping leg and wing launch: incremental leg contribution, failed forward-flight gate

The first episodes reached 500 ticks and full detailed observations, gains, actuator limits, target history, matched wing commands and pre-leg target parity passed. In all six development push lanes, the three-unloaded interval began at ticks 375-376 and continued without recorded foot or nonfoot contact through tick 500: about 2.5 s after release. There were zero observed toe-speed threshold events and no sampled modeled sweep stop in these push lanes.

The matched wing-only holds also released and remained foot-unloaded, so the wings alone could lift the simulated body under these conditions. The push-minus-hold foot-normal impulse through each push release was 0.108-0.228 N s and upward COM speed was 0.233-0.489 m/s higher in the push. Matched modeled vertical aerodynamic impulse differences were -0.00793 to +0.00032 N s; the momentum-minus-ground-normal-minus-aero residual was -0.00722 to -0.00383 N s and is not assumed to vanish. All six passed the preregistered incremental-leg screen. These impulses are not measured muscle work.

All candidates failed the sustained forward-flight screen: world-x displacement between ticks 400-500 was -0.319 to -0.524 m, even though late forward velocity became positive. None is a qualified flight controller.

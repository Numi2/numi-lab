# Paired foretoe support plus bounded relative-COM balance

The foretoe-closure trial maintained stance opposing contacts longer but the bird's root continued pitching and both feet failed to regrip. The separate relative-COM balance trial modestly reduced early forward drift but lost stance foretoe contact. Test whether the bounded COM correction helps when paired with the stronger stance foretoe support: both lanes reproduce the foretoe-closure candidate, and lane 1 alone replaces the existing pitch correction after release with clamp(-1.5*(relative COM x-0.004 m)-0.10*relative COM vx, +/-0.02 rad), slew limited to 0.002 rad per 20 ms and gated on the same measured opposing stance support. All return commands, toe limits, model, solver, and starts are unchanged. This hypothesis is not assumed to work; it may destabilize either foot.

Require exact lane-0 parity with the archived foretoe-closure candidate and matched paired states until the hip target differs; verify correction bounds and both feet's accepted positions/speeds, independent clearance and all contact substeps. Require ten supported release intervals then 100 consecutive terminal bilateral opposing-grip intervals, bounded tilt/speed, support and no nonfoot support. Record all failures and precise state/contact timing limitations. This is a constructed-start contact screen, not a qualified landing, walking, leg-powered flight or mission.

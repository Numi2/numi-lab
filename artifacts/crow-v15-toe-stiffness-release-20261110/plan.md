# Toe drive stiffness sensitivity

In the larger-perturbation isolated test, the rear-first opening released opposing grip in all starts, but in two supported starts the measured terminal toe angle remained above 0.05 rad, and two other starts lost terminal support. The open-toe control stayed supported in all eight. Hypothesis: the prototype position drive cannot return loaded toes to neutral promptly. On the same eight perturbed supported starts and square perch, compare the original 0.10 N m/rad toe stiffness with 0.20 and 0.40 N m/rad; preserve toe mass, inertia, torque limits, damping, contacts, targets, release ordering and all other mechanics. Include the original open-toe control. Require exact 200-sample parity for the original stiffness against the saved rear-first arm, then at least 6/8 strict grip, release, support and toe-angle passes for one increased-stiffness arm. All test variants are uncalibrated research bodies and require flight and impact testing before integration; they are not evidence of real crow force values or autonomous landing.

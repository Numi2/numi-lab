See screen.json, effort-check.json and the retained substep logs for details and all lanes.

The birds recontacted around tick 382, roughly 0.12 s after release. This is a short, controlled leg-powered hop, not sustained takeoff or qualified flight. The other timing patterns failed, including toe-speed threshold hits. The two passing lanes are correlated trials on one used seed, not independent qualification. No controller is frozen by this result.

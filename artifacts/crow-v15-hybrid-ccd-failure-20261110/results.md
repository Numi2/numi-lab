# CCD box-pair failure and research-only correction

A protected capture of the first failed aligned catch, case 0 immediate closure, showed repeated zero-time events for the left rear toe and route perch. Replaying the captured input twice reproduced the original selected event. The generic GPU query reported a 1.368 mm positive gap. An independent double-precision separating-axis test instead measured a negative maximum axis gap of 3.329 mm at the initial pose; this is overlap, not Euclidean penetration depth. Across 257 interpolated poses the original query disagreed in overlap sign 60 times.

A research-only SAT box-box CCD branch uses the maximum signed separating-axis gap, padded conservatively, and leaves all other geometries on their prior paths. On the protected replay it correctly marked the initial overlap; two independent GPU replays agreed byte-for-byte. The independent SAT check agreed in overlap sign on all 257 sampled poses. A separately seeded 10,000-case GPU comparison with thin to broad boxes, random orientations, identical orientations and both pair orders found 2,844 definite overlaps and 7,154 definite separations, no overlap reported separated by more than the declared 10 micrometre band, no separation reported overlapped beyond 100 micrometres, and no failed query. The largest pair-order gap difference was 0.30 micrometres. The largest GPU-minus-double axis gap was negative (about -13 micrometres), but this finite sample does not prove conservative rounding for every pose or CCD tunneling resistance. Results and random inputs are retained in this directory.

This has not been merged into production: full moving-geometry, high-speed and non-box tests, contact dynamics, performance, autonomous flight and landing remain to be qualified. The broad-phase speculative-solver divergence is documented separately; hybrid CCD was used in this experiment and is a physics configuration change that requires its own qualification.

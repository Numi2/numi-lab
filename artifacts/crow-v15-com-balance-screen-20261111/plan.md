# Whole-body center-of-mass balance experiment

Passive dynamics showed that the support point was approximately x=0.013–0.020 m while whole-body COM moved through this region and away before the fall. Test the hypothesis that regulating whole-body COM rather than the root can maintain balance. After physical grip and release plus 20 ticks, smoothly blend the original root-position error over 100 ticks toward a COM target of x=0.015 m, inside the observed support region, with the same bounded hip and ankle drives, pitch and velocity feedback, and physical contact. Compute COM from the actual v15 articulated poses, without applying external force or changing states. This host-side computation is a research prototype, not a performance-qualified controller. Require exact reference parity through tick 106, then full 20-second support, grip/release, geometric overlap, tilt and speed gates; no promotion on one development start. The reserved layouts remain untouched.

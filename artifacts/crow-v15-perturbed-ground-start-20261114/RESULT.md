# Perturbed perch-start screen: passed registered screen, not flight qualification

On used training seed 20261126, all eight lanes passed the pre-registered combined turn, height and physical-observer checks over 1800 intervals, with the teacher off and no intermediate resets. First accepted states differed from the same-seed baseline by 0.063–0.148 rad in absolute roll and 0.078–0.081 rad in absolute pitch, meeting the audit's >0.02 rad threshold. These are post-contact-solve states; forward velocity differences varied and were not a registered gate.

Relative signed heading extrema were 0.258–0.260 rad in magnitude. Level, climb and descent window mean absolute height errors were respectively 27.9–38.7 mm, 8.4–10.4 mm and 7.9–9.4 mm; each met the 50 mm screen. Full per-lane gates, records, input hashes and raw observation streams are retained. The recorded trace was independently checked against the native-host SHA-256 values.

This is a scripted perch-start development screen, not midair disturbed-start recovery, a bounded command interface, a frozen qualified flight controller, leg-powered takeoff, toe-grip qualification, autonomous navigation, or supported landing. The unused layouts and seeds remain reserved.

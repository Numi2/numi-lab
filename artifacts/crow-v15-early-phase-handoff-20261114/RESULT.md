# Early phase-aligned handoff: development continuity screen passed in six lanes

All eight first episodes reached the 800-interval horizon without a reported physical termination. The preregistered .75–.85-rad candidate lanes 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 first unloaded for three consecutive intervals at 376 and then had no measured foot recontact, nonfoot impulse or 12-rad/s toe-speed threshold through interval 800. All six passed this short continuity screen. The .70-rad comparison lanes 0 and 4 first recontacted at 396 and 389 respectively and fail the no-recontact gate despite reaching the horizon. The lanes share one used development seed and are correlated, not independent validation.

All logged first-episode states matched the later-handoff comparator through interval 411, and logged targets matched through interval 411 over the available 335–411 window. The first logged target difference was interval 413 in all lanes. Required state, target, contact and toe substep records were present through 800, finite and without observer errors. This supports a causal comparison of moving the phase-aligned blend earlier, but is not proof of natural or biologically calibrated flight. The isolated wings-held leg-push experiment is separate, and a matched identical-wing-command leg-necessity gate has not passed.

This 16-second test does not qualify long straight flight, commanded turns, climb, descent or recovery from perturbed starts, freeze a controller, fix stance/toe grip, establish supported landing or complete the autonomous mission. The reported waypoint counts are not an unused-layout mission result. Source, native binary/input hashes, replay, all lanes and their full traces are retained in Git; the large compressed stderr is split into hash-manifested ordered parts.

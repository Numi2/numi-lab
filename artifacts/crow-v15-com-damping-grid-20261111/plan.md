# Center-of-mass and velocity feedback under physical grip

The 1,000-step exact replay of the 0.14-radian ankle candidate shows a later backward drift of the COM while previously near-continuous grip and support disappear; the endpoint grip overlap exceeds 0.5 mm. Test whether the chosen COM setpoint and limited forward/backward velocity feedback explain this unstable phase. Keep ankle and toe controls, body damping, physical limits, solver, initial state and early control fixed. In 16 parallel environments run the exact previous controller as lane 1, plus five COM targets (-0.03, -0.01, 0, +0.01, +0.02 m) crossed with three root-velocity coefficients (0.2, 0.6, 1.0). Blend changes in only after grip, as in the reference. Run 750 ticks without reset. Require exact 500-tick reference replay and all-lane early parity, terminal 100 bilateral opposed grip and support samples, no nonfoot support, bounded tilt and speed, and independent grip pose overlap at most 0.5 mm, with no solver failures. Any survivor is exploratory and requires long independent and perturbed replay, physical release, and whole sequence qualification. Keep all outcomes. No reserved layouts are used.

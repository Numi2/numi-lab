# Bounded velocity-heading feedback: partial response

Two unmodified controls and the six experimental prefixes exactly matched 5,100 state samples and 81,600 contact substeps. All eight first episodes remained airborne through 24 s with no nonfoot contacts or post-4-s joint speed caps; two early toe-cap events are recorded. Five course obstacle collision masks were disabled, ground remained active, and direct body moments were disabled.

For the +/- .35 rad velocity-heading requests and .15, .30, .60 feedback gains, the positive-target lanes had 20–24 s mean absolute errors .355, .266 and .199 rad; negative-target lanes had .098, .073 and .047 rad. Last-four-second mean planar speeds were .491–.540 m/s, with maximum .582 m/s. Over 12–24 s, maximum sampled roll in intervention lanes was .014–.035 rad, and body yaw still changed approximately -.166 to -.181 rad. These outcomes show a controllable path-direction response in the model but do not establish accurate bidirectional target tracking, body alignment or completed turns. Different lane starts and target heights limit inference.

No flight controller is qualified or frozen. This used-seed diagnostic does not validate bird aerodynamics, perturbed recovery, obstacle navigation or landing.

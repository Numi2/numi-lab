# Independent stance foretoe distal coupling

The fixed-state v15 kinematics check matched recorded toe poses within 10 nm. Near zero-based intervals 295–300, increasing the stance foretoe distal angles by 0.01 rad closed their small computed gap without changing rear-toe geometry; by 305 a larger 0.11 rad distal change was required, and by 310 the foot itself had separated. Test whether a bounded distal target adjustment can retain contact dynamically: both lanes disable the extra returning-leg placement ramp, and only lane 1 increases the stance-front distal/proximal target coupling from 0.75 toward 0.85 at 0.01 per interval after the sensor-qualified return transition. Existing proximal and rear-toe targets, plant, friction, collision, solver, effort and joint limits are unchanged. Require a matched state/target prefix before the coupling takes effect and inspect actual per-substep opposing contacts, whole-body stability, toe speeds and terminal 100-interval bilateral grip. Geometry does not prove force or reachability; archive failures. This is a constructed stationary test, not walking or landing qualification.

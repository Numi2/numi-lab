# Toe collision isolation results

The read-only contact instrument reproduced all 128 saved seed-20261120 baseline sampled q/v states exactly. On the unmodified body, positive recorded contacts after step 400 were absent; earlier positive contacts involved the feet/toes and ground. Eight of eight original-body lanes missed flight tracking with no physical failure.

Turning off articulated toe collisions without replacing their support caused all eight lanes to fail physically during ground preparation, so it cannot identify the airborne effect. The follow-up rigid-support surrogate preserved support and all eight completed 1600 steps but still failed the same speed gate, with errors 0.279-0.354 m/s. The rigid contact geometry is only an approximation to the original articulated contacts, so this does not prove exact equivalence. Together with the separate toe damping test, the results support continuing to investigate flight dynamics, rather than treating obstacle or toe-ground collision as a complete explanation. Neither experimental surrogate can grip and neither is promoted.

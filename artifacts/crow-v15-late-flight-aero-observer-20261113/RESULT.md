# Late launch momentum and aerodynamic observer

The extended observer reproduced the retained supported-wing push in every recorded state/action/contact endpoint through tick 400 and every recorded contact substep through tick 400. All first episodes and required modeled-force and wing-effort observations completed; uniform translational velocity in the COM reconstruction was recovered to 5.6e-17 m/s. This is parity of recorded quantities, not proof of identical unobserved internal state.

For the six candidate lanes, the 0.5 s interval from the end of tick 375 to tick 400 had recorded modeled upward aerodynamic impulse 0.569–1.574 N s. The modeled 0.45006 kg mass under assumed 9.81 m/s² gravity requires approximately 2.208 N s to balance weight over that interval. Foot-normal impulses were 0.382–1.436 N s as the bird recontacted, yet COM vertical velocity changed from +0.335 to +0.477 m/s at tick 375 to -0.040 to -0.115 m/s at tick 400. In the recorded momentum accounting, the remaining residual after foot impulse, modeled aerodynamic impulse and assumed gravity is within 0.009 N s for these lanes.

This establishes that the observed modeled aerodynamic support was insufficient to sustain the trajectory over that interval; it does not prove the aerodynamic model, wing actuation, impulse logger, energy expenditure or biological parameters are correct. The residual also does not rule out errors that cancel. This is a used-seed, correlated-lane development result, not qualification. Further isolate wing geometry, flapping kinematics and energy before raising force/effort limits or claiming realistic powered flight.

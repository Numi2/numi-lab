# Two-segment walking hip-direction development screen

The two-segment baseline yielded alternating supported swing bouts in the replayed lane, but displaced the root -1.2 mm and had eight wholly unsupported intervals; it fails forward walking. Test hip swing coefficients -.30, +.30, -.50 and +.50 with the same knee/ankle carrier, contact gate, gait onset, body, frozen V2 controller source, solver, policies and development seed. Assign lanes by index modulo four, two starts per coefficient; the -.30 lanes are exact baseline controls. Run 201 intervals, observe all 16 contact substeps and accepted states, and compare every shared baseline control q/v/action sample to the previous experiment. During gait intervals 101-201 require at least 15 mm forward root displacement, no wholly unsupported interval, no nonfoot impulse, tilt <=.35 rad, speed <=.5 m/s, no solver/physical failure, and complete observations. Report per-lane contact, motion and all failures. This is an exploratory directional screen with two starts per setting, not proof of foot clearance, generalization, takeoff, flight, landing or biological validity. No reserved layouts are used.

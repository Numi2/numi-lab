# Teacher-free student-state and approach imitation: development trial

On one previously used development layout and seed, six of eight first episodes reached all five waypoint thresholds by step 3800 without scheduled resets or a teacher. Two episodes failed at slalom A: the first observed endpoint pairs were tail/slalom and left wing/slalom. All physics substep records were present with no contact-capacity overflow. No first episode produced any foot/perch impulse; therefore there was no supported landing, grip or release. This longer run is not directly comparable to the previous 3000-step result as a final success rate, and no unused final layouts were used. The tested simulation used the prior toe controller, not the later physical flight-toe candidate. The separate stderr chunks reconstruct the exact gzip with restore-stderr.py.

All lanes matched their same-lane no-feedback obstacle-free comparator in accepted q/v, action and contact until the first target change; policy bytes and the disabled course/active ground masks matched. Offset -1 matched; offsets -2 and 0 did not. This validates the sampled physical position target and sensor timing, not the adequacy or biological realism of the controller. Only first-episode data support continuous-flight statements; records after termination may include resets. No late unobserved actuator or aerodynamic work is inferred.

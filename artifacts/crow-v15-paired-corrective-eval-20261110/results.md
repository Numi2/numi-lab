All four preregistered teacher-off full starts failed on their first episode. Difficult J struck slalom B with its left wing at step 2472 after two waypoints; difficult I struck slalom A with its right wing at step 2219 after two waypoints. Original J reached waypoint five at 2692 but struck the perch with its tail at 4827. Original I reached waypoint five at 2902 but struck the perch with its body at 4505. The I run had minimum accepted-step obstacle clearance 0.00309 m; J had 0.16839 m. These are development runs, not untouched-layout validation. Later automatic episodes following failure are excluded.

Each first episode walked approximately 0.1103 m from steps 100–600 with ten clear swings per side and no observed unsupported or nonfoot-contact control endpoints in that window; the push latch occurred at step 654. Matched no-push controls are reported separately. Toe deviations after 25 contact-free samples were below 0.000407 rad at sampled control endpoints. These endpoint findings do not establish full substep behavior, biological realism, reliable landing, or grip and release. None of the four reached the release event. No solver failures or nonfinite task-state samples were observed. See the bound policy, host, shader, first-contact details, full-sequence audit, and compressed replays.

The paired actor is rejected for final evaluation. Follow-up teacher recoveries are development-only and must reproduce the failed student physical prefix before takeover, finish every waypoint with sustained support, and pass all qualification checks before any routing prefix can be used. The flight controller stays frozen, and final reserved layouts remain unused.

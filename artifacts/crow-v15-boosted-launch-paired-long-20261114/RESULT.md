# Long paired launch development test

On used seed 20261126, both push candidates first unloaded at interval 377 and had no later recorded foot load through 1800. In the held-leg control, lane 6 first recontacted the ground at interval 407 while lane 7 remained unloaded through 1800. Push-lane height during intervals 450–1800 was 0.408–0.905 m and 0.564–1.407 m, respectively; the high lane exceeded the target height. Unchanged lanes 0 and 1 failed at intervals 760 and 764 in both runs. This is not robust flight qualification.

Both long runs exactly reproduced the archived 500-step q/v, policy actions and contact prefixes. The recorded full joint targets also match their respective shorter runs over intervals 335–500. Push and hold wing targets were identical at intervals 335–400; as feedback evolved, their maxima differed by 0.000106 and 0.000178 rad through interval 500. Logged target agreement does not mean wing force was isolated or identical: wings were active before unload, and both dynamics and contacts are coupled. These outcomes support a modeled leg contribution but do not establish that leg power is necessary or sufficient for takeoff; the held-leg lane that remained airborne is contrary evidence.

For the push lanes, in intervals 600–1200 pronation reached the modeled 0.15 Nm effort cap in about 47% of substeps, and the wing drive reached its 3 Nm cap in about 35%. All 16 required foot/toe substeps were present for the candidate lanes, with no accepted toe-speed threshold, nonfoot impulse or observer errors. These are modeled values and not biological validation. The observer's contact/state epoch alignment has not been independently verified for these lanes. No landing, teacher-off navigation course, disturbed-start qualification or controller freeze follows from this used-seed test. Complete traces and failures are archived.

# Results

Native FP64 central differences at accepted walking states expose geometry and the nonsmooth minimum box height. At step 106 left swing, hip/knee/ankle lowest-box sensitivities are approximately +0.0491/+0.0657/+0.0512 m/rad, while foot-center x sensitivities are -0.1317/-0.0707/-0.0091 m/rad. Two step sizes agree there; at near-flat step 100 the minimum-box derivative varies with step size because the minimum changes. Local kinematics is not a dynamical, force, walking, or biological validation.

# Crow foot geometry sensitivity probe

The smooth swing and increased knee/ankle experiments failed the unchanged sustained-walk slip/clearance gate. Before selecting more gait commands, inspect the actual v15 toe-body geometry. Starting from accepted lane-0 states at steps 100, 106, 112 and 120 of the prior long-walk development trial, calculate central finite differences of foot-center x/z and the lowest foot-or-toe collision-box z with respect to each corresponding hip, knee and ankle position, at 1e-4 and 2e-4 rad. Use native FP64 articulated kinematics and the actual revised model; no changes to physical states or dynamics. The two step sizes provide a numerical sanity check, but the minimum box vertex is nonsmooth at contact and is only a local proxy. Record accepted input q, DOF identities, outputs and exact binary; use this measurement to design a new dynamic test, not to claim a working gait, force model, landing, or realism.

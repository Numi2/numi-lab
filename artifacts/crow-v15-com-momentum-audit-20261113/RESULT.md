# Exploratory whole-body vertical momentum accounting

A native v15 FK observer computes mass-weighted COM position and velocity from retained endpoint q/v, using all body masses. A synthetic whole-root translation check produced COM velocity within 5.6e-17 m/s of the known translation. The source model's total mass is approximately 0.45006 kg; native trace headers give a 0.02 s control interval. State, contact and trial identity checks are inherited from the source trial analyses. This is a translation sanity check, not full sensor calibration or a full solver momentum audit.

For steps 348–365, the active-crouch lanes had approximately 1.5894 Ns of signed upward foot impulse, essentially balancing the assumed -9.81 m/s² gravity impulse over 0.36 s, with near-zero COM momentum change and residual. The hip-candidate lanes had approximately 1.5935–1.5942 Ns foot impulse and near-zero COM momentum change. The ankle candidate's six comparable lanes had approximately 1.5547–1.6372 Ns foot impulse and -0.0444 to +0.0430 Ns COM momentum change; their residual after subtracting foot and assumed gravity impulses was approximately -0.0097 to -0.0043 Ns. These are summed model signals and reconstructed endpoint momentum, not independent biological measurements.

The two ankle lanes with three sampled unloaded intervals (4 and 6) had COM vertical velocities 0.312 and 0.346 m/s at the first sampled interval, and peak COM rises of 0.0326 and 0.0344 m relative to step 350. Four other comparable lanes did not have such an unloading sequence. Toe-speed caps occurred in all six. The signals establish a promising but unreliable simulated response under this development setup; they do not establish leg-only causality because passive aerodynamics, earlier moments, actuator and contact solver effects remain. Unattributed residual cannot be labeled aerodynamic force without separately logging those forces. These correlated lanes are not independent birds. No launch, flight or landing qualification is asserted.

See analysis.json for all lanes and the exact input identity, kinematics source, executable and native library hash. Gravity is an explicit assumed value pending independent runtime dispatch verification.

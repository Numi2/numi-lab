# Support-gated return: failed

The stationary v15 perch trial completed with all 900 states, all 28,800 contact substeps and all 28,800 toe-velocity substeps recorded; no observer errors or non-foot impulses were reported. The control reproduced the prior control's logged states, commands and endpoint events exactly throughout all 450 intervals. The paired lanes were identical through interval 304 (zero-based); the candidate gate first closed and leg targets and states first differed at interval 305.

The candidate's returning-leg gate remained closed for the remaining 145 intervals and never resumed. The longest fully qualified bilateral grip streak was 10 intervals versus 11 in the control. The candidate fell at one-based interval 379 versus 370 for the control; both reached the 12 rad/s toe-speed limit before falling. The release phase had a longest qualified clearance of 43 intervals in each lane. Neither had the required 100 terminal supported intervals. A later fall is not a grip success; this gate is rejected and both raw trajectories are retained.

This is one deterministic constructed perch release/return trial, not a walking, landing or unused-layout mission qualification. The full-interval contact and endpoint sensor differ as established in the contact epoch audit; the result does not prove a unique cause of failure.

# Isolated settling-gain controls: no successful run

All four two-lane development processes ended in a solver-reported rollback before their 300-interval horizons. The 0.5 and 1.0 rad/m settings included falls before termination. The 1.5 and 2.5 settings rolled back without crossing the chosen root-height fall threshold first. The maximum bilateral supported streaks were insufficient to complete the shift and release protocol in every run. Toe-speed-limit samples occurred in three of the four runs; retained counts exclude the failed attempt.

These are two deliberately difficult constructed starts, not a general gain ranking or evidence of the cause of the solver failures. They use 0.4 s velocity damping, which differs from the original 0.2 s baseline. Every attempted setting and the full partial traces are retained.

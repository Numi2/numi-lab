# Joint-drive observer: knee speed limit invalidates launch work estimate

The diagnostic replays both matched launch conditions on eight used development starts for 370 intervals. For each arm, all 2,960 logged q/v/action/contact endpoints and 47,360 logged contact substeps exactly match the previously archived 500-interval run; each has 133,120 per-joint drive/velocity records. Pre- and post-substep velocity continuity is exact at the logged precision. The observer snapshots solver data without changing the flight model.

The extension condition hits both modeled knee velocity limits of 22 rad/s on 23 substeps per knee in the inspected lane (and repeatedly in the other lanes); those limits are enforced in the contact-state integration. The comparison withholding the extension command has much lower knee speeds. The previous toe-only cap check missed this leg-joint saturation, so the early-wing takeoff candidate must not be accepted as a smooth or leg-powered launch. This is an engineering model limit, not a measured biological limit.

The accompanying analysis retains an attempted implicit-drive work reconstruction and two quadratures for audit, but **those work figures are not accepted**: velocity projection impulses at the saturated knees are absent from that calculation, and contact/drive effects have not been independently energy-balanced. Do not use them to claim leg muscle power or leg-powered takeoff. Matched contact forces and forward displacement show an intervention effect only. Slow the leg motion, log every leg limit, and revalidate before reconsidering power.

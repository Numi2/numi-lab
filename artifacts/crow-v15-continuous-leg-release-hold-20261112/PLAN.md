# Continuous release handoff under delayed wing onset

The first delayed-wing test observed both knees hitting 22 rad/s during release. An instantaneous override switches the leg target from extending to crouched when the release flag is set. Test removing that reversal while retaining the existing bounded extension trajectory until 11.40 s, then blending from its extended target to the existing flight target over .20, .40, .60 or .80 s, two starts per setting. This continues the position drive after release and is an engineering intervention, not claimed bird kinematics. Keep the previously screened .04 s wing delay and all other body, contact, toe and aerodynamic properties. An exact same-seed, same-lane powered-crouch control uses crouched targets on both sides of the handoff. No root forces or changed joint limits are introduced.

On both used seeds, demand exact pair prefix through step 548, complete joint endpoints for steps 535–625 and toe/contact substeps through 625, no observed joint/toe caps, nonfoot ground impulse, solver error, overflow or both-foot-unloaded substeps during 501–515, and unloaded feet in all 80 final observed substeps 621–625. For development selection require each matched start of a setting to exceed its own hold by at least .020 N s vertical foot contact impulse over 549–556 and .030 m/s root vertical speed at step 556. Correlated developmental starts, uncalibrated aero and interaction with wings prevent interpreting this as measured muscle power, biological validation, full flight or mission qualification. Later behavior, unsupported control and any limitations must be reported.

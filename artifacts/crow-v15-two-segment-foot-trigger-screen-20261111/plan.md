# Pre-run exploratory foot-contact trigger screen

Use eight identical stationary starts and the 350-step 16-substep method of the toe flexion screen. Lane zero retains the original rear-contact trigger and 0.55 rad cap as an exact replay control. Lanes 1-7 use same-foot sole or toe contact to trigger rear flexion and require two consecutive contact observations to trigger front flexion; their corresponding caps are 0.65, 0.75, 0.85, 0.95, 1.05, 1.15 and 1.20 rad. Actuator targets are bounded, distal 0.75 times proximal. Require 50 consecutive supported opposing bilateral force intervals, tilt and speed limits, no nonfoot contact, and endpoint penetration at most 0.5 mm; retain every failed lane and force record. This is an exploratory constructed start, not flight arrival, release or biological qualification.

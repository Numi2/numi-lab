# Pre-run contact-triggered stagger screen

In the previous eight-start screen the +2 mm forward position and +0.02 rad roll cases fell; both had nearly simultaneous left and right toe closure and forward pitch, whereas the successful baseline began left closure later. Repeat only these two failed constructed starts. For each, test 0, 4, 8 and 12 control intervals of left-toe delay measured from the first real same-foot contact after interval 10; right toe and all leg controls are unchanged. The zero-delay lanes must reproduce their corresponding prior failures exactly. Do not force the bird or alter state. Preserve all substeps and any solver rollback. Require no fall, final 100 consecutive supported-opposed intervals, at least 50 consecutive such intervals, no nonfoot contact and worst opposed endpoint overlap <=0.5 mm. Any passing delays remain exploratory and must subsequently be tested as one fixed controller across the full perturbation set and real arrivals.

# Exploratory knee command range screen

The softer knee drive candidate was rejected: it did not increase knee excursion and increased substeps without foot support from 33 to 438 across 16 paired development lanes. The observed original controller commands only a short knee swing. Test the distinct hypothesis that the controller limits visible and physical knee movement. Keep original knee mechanics, all other joints, phase, support gate, contact, materials, flight controller and policy weights fixed; change only the positive knee swing action coefficient from 0.40 to 0.60 in the two-second walking window, using the native physical position drives and their existing limits. This is an uncalibrated control hypothesis, not anatomical measurement.

Use the same two used development seeds 20261126 and 20261127, eight lanes each, 300 steps and 16 physics substeps, with no scheduled resets and no teacher. Baselines are the same archived original-stride walks used in the knee compliance comparison. Before running predict at least a 15% increase in mean bilateral measured knee span on at least six of 16 paired lanes; require total no-foot-contact substeps no higher than the baseline 33, no additional nonfoot contact, no added termination or failed environment step, and positive forward displacement in at least the 16 baseline lanes. Also report hip/ankle and root angle/height excursions and individual swing bouts. Require logged pre-walk q/v and contacts to match baseline through the unmodified interval, and separately compare seed27 to the equally bounded baseline. If any gate fails, do not promote; preserve the run. These are repeated development starts, not body-deformability, grip, takeoff, landing, flight or biological qualification. Stop after both seeds.

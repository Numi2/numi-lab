# Six-second development walking previews

`development-walk-two-seeds.mp4` concatenates two six-second, 50-fps native replays from lane 5 of the 0.05-rad toe-preload development trials, seeds 20261126 and 20261127. Each shows approximately four seconds of standing/preparation followed by two seconds of walking. These are previously used training starts, not the two unused navigation layouts and not a full takeoff, flight or landing. The standing phase has known toe-speed-cap events; other starts had additional transient bouncing. See the matched trial and second-seed result artifacts for all lanes and limitations.

BirdFlow uses recorded accepted rigid-link states; the feathers, skinning, toe bridge, surface and lighting are estimated presentation. The camera removes root trajectory, and the video is cropped and enlarged for inspection, so it cannot establish forward displacement, slip, contact force or biological accuracy. Capture audits identify the training course, renderer overrides and exact replay payload.

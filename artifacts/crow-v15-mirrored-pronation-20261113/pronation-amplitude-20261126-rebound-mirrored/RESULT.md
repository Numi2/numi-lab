# Corrected-axis matched screen: failed flight envelope

On the corrected-axis v15 research body, all six candidate lanes reached tick 1800 without recorded post-release recontact or ground contact. Compared with the old-body amplitude screen, maximum lateral displacement fell from 1.90–2.65 m to 0.016–0.223 m, and sampled yaw remained within approximately -0.018 to +0.0013 rad. Both matched pronation joints showed almost identical modeled effort and position over ticks 650–1150. These comparisons are one used seed with correlated lanes; the changed model begins producing different physical states on the first recorded tick, so this is not a trajectory-identical counterfactual.

All candidates failed the registered height envelope: maximum sampled heights were 1.79–2.43 m versus the 1.5 m ceiling. All also exhibited some reverse forward velocity; lanes 4/5 had negative net forward displacement. The 0.18-rad pair spent about 93% of observed substeps at the modeled pronation effort limit; the 0.12-rad and 0.08-rad pairs spent about 69% and 47%, respectively. No sampled pronation effort exceeded the model clamp. The ground controls' first recontact occurred at tick 418 and their first episodes later failed; accepted pre-contact state and contact records are in matched-analysis.json.

The axis correction fixes a verified geometric inconsistency, and these data support its effect in this approximate simulator. It does not establish flight qualification, biological accuracy, proven leg-powered transition, turns, recovery, autonomous navigation or landing. Policies were rebound with identical arrays and transforms to the changed model fingerprint; the rejected original binding and both packs are retained.

# Teacher-off combined and airborne flight result

All 160 combined-maneuver lanes and all 80 perturbed airborne-start lanes passed their original sampled screens, with no physical or simulator failures. Every one of the 3,200 sampled q/v pairs matched its teacher-enabled counterpart exactly, and the teacher-off runs published no teacher-action labels. These are deterministic low-level flight tests with fixed diagnostic commands, not autonomous navigation or supported landing. The source start templates and limits described in the original studies still apply.

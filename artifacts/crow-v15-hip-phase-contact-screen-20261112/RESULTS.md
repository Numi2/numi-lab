# Hip phase variants: rejected

Both controls matched their six-second baselines, and every experimental lane matched the first four seconds, with 1,600 states and 25,600 contact substeps identical. Previously used seed 20261127 and the same contact model were used.

With .34 stroke, offsets -.65, -.80 and -1.05 rad gave .107–.111, .136 and .177–.186 m forward displacement respectively in the 4–6 s window. Matched baselines gave .131–.139 m. The -.65 lanes had 45 and 47 both-feet-unloaded substeps; -.80 had two in each; -1.05 had six in one lane and zero in the other. The faster -1.05 setting also exceeded the 1.5x per-foot contact-point-speed p95 gate on both sides of both lanes. No 4–6 s toe-speed caps or nonfoot impulses occurred.

None passed all planned gates. This timing screen is exploratory model tuning, not a measured corvid kinematic fit; the point-speed statistic is not integrated slip.

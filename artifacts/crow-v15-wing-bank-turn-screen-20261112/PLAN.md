# Differential wingbeat bank response, obstacle-free exploratory screen

The prior direct-yaw/pitch-moment ablation could not turn with additional differential wing sweep. Test bounded differential flapping amplitude using the existing physical wing position actuators and the current aerodynamic model, not an additional body moment. After 9 s, remove the two identified direct body moment actuators at their final application point and request alternating +90/-90-degree headings across six lanes. Apply increasing differential-wing amplitudes 0.12, 0.24 and 0.36 to paired lanes, while retaining common-mode altitude control. Screen 16 s of obstacle-free development runs. Compare all pre-9 s sampled q/v/actions to the retained six-lane baseline and record every physical failure, actual heading, roll, altitude, contacts and toe caps. A screen is informative only if the wing target affects actual motion; it cannot qualify the complete 90-degree turn from one perturbation, climb, descent, recovery, takeoff or autonomous navigation. Earlier takeoff and first 9 s still use the unqualified direct moments, and the wing aerodynamic force model has unvalidated closure coefficients. No controller is frozen or promoted by this experiment.

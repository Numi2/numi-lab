# Sampled height lowered 10 mm at reset: rejected

The prior experiment used the wrong height reference; this one lowers the already sampled reset height by 10 mm while retaining the measured non-root pose and perturbations. Initial responses are not a clean supported rest: at the first recorded 20 ms state, six of eight starts have upward velocity above 0.05 m/s, including 0.407 and 0.434 m/s in lanes 3 and 4. This is consistent with an initial contact transient; the retained endpoint snapshots do not establish the exact penetration or cause of the impulse. Across eight used-seed starts, both-feet-unloaded substeps during the first four seconds increased from 2,523 to 4,098. Individual starts improve or deteriorate, and five hit toe/neck speed limits early. All had continuous foot loading during walking at 4–6 s, no observed joint-speed caps from 4–10 s, and were airborne during 8–10 s without reaching a waypoint.

This is an unsafe and unreliable initialization candidate, not a repair. No unseen-layout, biological gait, takeoff, flight, navigation or landing qualification follows. The full first-episode contacts, substep velocities and source state are retained.

In the matched guided I recovery, both feet briefly supported the body, but around steps 4400-4470 sampled perch contacts showed forces on the feet and rear toes while front toes produced no normal impulse; the body slid forward, lost support and accelerated away. Before execution, increase only the lower bound on front-toe position target from 0.85 to 0.98 rad after the fifth waypoint, retaining the same bounded drives, ramps, no injected force, v15 body, flight law and guided student prefix through takeover at step 2200. Compare the same I seed and control. Measure actual front/rear opposing impulses, bilateral loaded durations, toe angles, root motion, any nonfoot collision and terminal support; require physical success and exact student-prefix parity. Toe-angle closure alone is not grip, endpoint samples do not prove uninterrupted substep contact, and this guided development experiment is not autonomous or held-out. Preserve failures and exclude them from demonstrations.

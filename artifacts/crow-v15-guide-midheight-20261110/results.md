# Result

All 16 privileged-guide starts reached five waypoints; 13 survived to 4500 steps and three had a physical failure, with no recorded solver failure. The longest bilateral loaded streak was three endpoints and there was no sampled bilateral opposed toe grip; the preregistered loading and grip predictions failed. Prior-to-perch angles stayed below 0.000312 rad, but toe reopening failed the 0.05 rad clearance threshold after 25 steps with no foot or toe contact candidates: maxima were 0.2051 and 0.2706 rad on the two layouts. The largest observed toe angle was negative with -3.66 rad/s joint velocity and no contact candidates. This diagnostic is a guided development run and does not establish autonomy, stable landing or real-bird validity. Retain the failure for a damping investigation; do not promote this descent variant.

# Neutral transfer walking experiment

The contact-gated phase stalled at a side-switch boundary while the stance-bound foot was still commanded upward. Add a neutral region to the knee and ankle lift so both feet can settle during transfer; leave hip swing, side-switch contact gate, physics, seed and timing unchanged. Test the same eight-second teacher-off extension with full substep contact and endpoint clearance. This is a used development course, not final qualification.

# Neutral transfer gait: insufficient foot clearance, rejected

The neutral lift band alone did not restore repeated clear steps: all eight used development starts produced only one left and two right qualifying clear swing bouts over eight seconds. Three lanes had 3, 3 and 14 substeps respectively with no measured foot normal impulse, and the last of these had one endpoint with both feet clear by 1 mm. No nonfoot ground impulses were recorded. Forward displacement does not establish alternating steps. Accepted state and substep-contact parity before gait is retained in comparison.stdout. A subsequent check of the active model confirmed that its existing foot contact groups already include all eight articulated toe bodies. Whether the gate stalls because of its force threshold, contact direction, phase updates, or another mechanism is not established. A direct upward ground-impulse gate is a separate experiment. Endpoint clearance is not swept clearance. No takeoff or landing was tested.

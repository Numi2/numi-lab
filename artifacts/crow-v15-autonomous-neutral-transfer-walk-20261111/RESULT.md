# Neutral transfer gait: stalls, rejected

The neutral lift band alone did not fix the contact-gated gait: all eight used development starts produced only one left and two right qualifying clear swing bouts over eight seconds. Three lanes had 3, 3 and 14 substeps respectively with no measured foot normal impulse, and the last of these had one endpoint with both feet clear by 1 mm. No nonfoot ground impulses were recorded. Forward displacement does not establish alternating steps. Accepted state and substep-contact parity before gait is retained in comparison.stdout. A subsequent contact trace showed that toe bodies can carry ground load when the foot-only gate is unloaded; adding toe support is a separate experiment. Endpoint clearance is not swept clearance. No takeoff or landing was tested.

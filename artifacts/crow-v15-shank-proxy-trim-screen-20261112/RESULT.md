# Shortened shank collision proxy: failed development screen

The 6 mm lower-shank collision-box trim is **not admitted**. All eight first episodes fell backward before the end of the 300-step used-seed run, at steps 168, 179, 183, 179, 174, 174, 170 and 235. The untrimmed reattachment control also failed all eight, and reproduced its retained predecessor exactly across 2,400 recorded endpoints and 38,400 substeps. No physics error or contact overflow was reported. The post-failure endpoints had body-ground contact in all eight; the precise first fatal collider pair was not recorded and cannot be inferred from that endpoint.

The native structural check found only the two intended collision boxes changed; 35 bodies, 34 joints, 40 DOFs, and two pre-existing torso-thigh collision exclusions were retained. Both toe-shank collisions were enabled. The changed collision bounding radii caused normal policy-contract rejection. For this diagnostic only, identical policy weights and action transforms were rebound after independent layout comparison; the two shank radius observation entries differed, so observations were not identical across these worlds. A two-step smoke preceded the full run. This is not a physically measured crow shank modification.

There were no recorded proximal rear-toe/shank contact pairs at sampled endpoints, but distal rear-toe/shank contacts remained frequent: 649 pair records in the first 100 sampled endpoints across lanes. Prior fixed-trajectory clearance estimates did not predict the new trajectory. Compared with the diagnostic model that excluded proximal toe-shank contact, median pitch by step 100 was -0.2945 versus -0.0145 radians. The models and resulting trajectories differ, so this comparison is not an isolated causal test.

A post-hoc one-dimensional support projection places whole-body center of mass behind the rearmost loaded foot/toe contact in 4/8 lanes at step 50 and 8/8 at steps 75 and 100 in the trimmed trial; median signed distances were +0.00079, -0.01411 and -0.02774 m. In the diagnostic exclusion trial, 0/5 sampled lanes with loaded contacts had negative values at each of those steps. This is not a support polygon, center of pressure, friction analysis, or a proof of causation; poses and contact timestamps can differ. At step 1 no loaded contact was sampled. See support-projection.json for lane-level values.

Gait scoring was not run because every first episode failed; the prepared gait and contact-order analysis scripts are retained but unused for the candidate. The two post-hoc analysis errors and corrections are retained. These used-seed diagnostic results do not qualify walking, takeoff, flight, grasping, landing, or either unused-layout full sequence.

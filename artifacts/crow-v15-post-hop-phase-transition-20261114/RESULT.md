# Post-hop phase screen: both push candidates failed continuity

The two wing-phase candidates were run with leg push and a matched held-leg control, teacher off, for 500 intervals on used seed 20261126. Both push candidates first unloaded in interval 375, then recontacted in 378 and 379 respectively. The held-leg lane 6 unloaded at 380 and recontacted at 386; held-leg lane 7 unloaded at 382 and stayed unloaded through 500. Push lane 7 also reached the toe-speed threshold on four substeps. Neither phase passes the registered continuous-transition gate.

For the push candidates the inferred maximum observer flap displacement through first unloading was approximately 0.0019 rad; modeled preceding vertical aerodynamic impulses were +0.0000043 and -0.0002003 N s in lane 6, and +0.0000043 and -0.0002015 N s in lane 7. This is consistent with a short, nearly wing-still hop under these model conditions; it is not proof of a sustained leg-powered takeoff. In the held-leg lane that remained unloaded, the flap had moved approximately 0.678 rad and modeled preceding net vertical aero impulse was +0.0897 N s. These are modeled forces, not biological measurements.

Candidate q/v, policy action and recorded contact states matched through pre-push interval 368, and all six unaffected lanes matched through 500. Paired wing targets and nonshader inputs matched, substep coverage was complete, and no observer errors or nonfoot impulse were recorded. The observer state/replay timing offset was directly checked in other lanes and applied here as an inference, not independently proved for these candidates. The complete raw failures and controls are retained. No flight controller, landing, navigation or unused-layout qualification follows from this screen.

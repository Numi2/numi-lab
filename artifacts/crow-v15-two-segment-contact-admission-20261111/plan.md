# Two-segment toe narrow-perch contact admission

In the separately documented 40-body isolated MetalWorld build, test one 33-body v15-derived model on a narrow box perch for 120 accepted control intervals of 20 ms, with 16 substeps, temporal-cone contact, bounded position drives and no state reset after initialization. Start from the previously used stationary-perch diagnostic pose with toe targets open. Record all accepted q/v, contact pairs, normal impulses, separation, required contact count and solver status. Require no failed native steps, finite q/v, no contact-capacity overflow, and at least one positive foot or toe contact impulse. This is a short endpoint-only admission, not a stability, continuous-contact, grip, flight, landing, performance or biological claim. Terminate and retain any failure.

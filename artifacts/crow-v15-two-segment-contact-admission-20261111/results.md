# Two-segment toe contact admission

The 33-body crow plus perch completed 120 consecutive 20 ms control intervals at 16 substeps without a reported native step failure or contact-capacity overflow. All q/v were finite. Accepted-endpoint readback contained 857 positive foot/toe contact records across 118 intervals; the maximum endpoint contact count was eight and the most negative reported contact separation was -0.318 mm. All positive endpoint contacts were soles or rear proximal toe segments against the perch. The root height changed from 0.1903 m to 0.1771 m. The first compile failed on incorrect field names and the first run rejected the old topology guard; both are retained. These are endpoint measurements from one stationary initial condition. They do not establish substep support, front/rear opposition, grip, release, dynamic landing, walking, flight or biological realism.

# Strong flight leg fold rejected

All five bounded-command obstacle-free first episodes ran 1600 intervals without physical termination or ground impulses after interval 400, but none met the existing screen. In lane-one accepted-state replays, forward speed at steps 1000 through 1500 was approximately 0.008–0.011 m/s for this fold versus 0.187–0.220 m/s in the matching original command screen. At step 450 the candidate moved backwards at approximately 0.605 m/s, while the reference moved forward at 0.369 m/s. These comparisons use actual measured velocities; raw target-error values in the screen are not calibrated to the flight adapter.

The physical rear toe joints also departed from their commanded curl, requiring a separate collision or actuation investigation. No conclusion about the cause is established from these observations. Early-settling toe speed-limit samples were unchanged in lane four; there were no toe speed-limit samples after flight began. Replay q/v, observations and actions matched for the first 370 frames before the new ramp. This physical fold is rejected despite its higher foot position. It was not tested for autonomous navigation, final approach, grip, landing or biological realism. The frozen flight law remained unchanged.

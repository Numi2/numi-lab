Reuse the exact previous release-gated push metallib, host, seed 20261126 and other inputs, with the teacher off, and extend the same full-start first episodes to 800 intervals. This is a used development seed, not held-out qualification.

Before observing results, require the six 0.75–0.85 rad candidate lanes to stay continuously foot- and nonfoot-contact-free after their initial three-interval unload through interval 800, with no toe speed cap and finite state and actuator values.

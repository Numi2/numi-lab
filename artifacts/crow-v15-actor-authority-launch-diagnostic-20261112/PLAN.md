# Remove scripted high-level flight commands

The prior v15 launch trials used a development-only time-scheduled speed, zero turn and height command overriding the route actor from 9.0–9.5 s onward, despite the teacher flag being off. Remove only this schedule from the source; retain the same .04 s wing delay, .40 s leg-release handoff, low-level flight feedback, physics and route/landing policies. The route actor's bounded commands then feed the flight law before waypoint five; existing explicit terminal guidance and flight-controller feedback remain and must not be mislabeled as a purely learned controller. Direct body moment actuators remain disabled. This is a developmental actor-authority test, not a frozen qualified flight controller.

For the same used seed and per-lane starts, record every nonroot joint velocity, full first-episode accepted states, foot contacts and scene contacts for up to 1200 steps with teacher off and normal obstacle collision. Compare state and contact prefixes through step 450 against the scripted-command course run; their first later divergence records the intervention effect. Report actual outcomes, any colliders with temporal context, flight caps, failure/waypoint status, and limits. No course event alone proves completion, supported landing, unseen-layout performance or realistic crow flight. A short-prefix match does not establish flight qualification.

Test the missing physical capability first. With wings suppressed through 5.4 s, compare symmetric hip extension targets -0.50, -0.30, -0.10 and 0.10 and ankle targets -0.30, -0.14, 0.02 and 0.18. Keep the loaded-foot gate, knee, crouch, latch, passive aerodynamic forces and all other dynamics and commands fixed. Test 16 variants on the same eight development starts with 16 physics substeps for 270 ticks. The -0.50/-0.30 arm must exactly reproduce the preceding wing-suppressed control in all saved q/v and substep contacts. All variants must match it through step 248, before extension.

A leg-only survivor must have no native failures, nonfoot contact or terminations; its first fully unloaded interval at or after step 249 must start at least two consecutive intervals with both feet unloaded and upward velocity >0.05 m/s, and attain >20 mm root-height gain before any recontact, in every lane. Report first unload, recontact, upward speed, height gain and all failures; include held-crouch control from the matching preceding experiment. This is a development screen, not a complete takeoff, independent validation, accepted geometry proof, landing or route. Surviving variants still require endpoint geometry, integrated flight and a held-out perturbation screen. No reserved layout or seed is used.

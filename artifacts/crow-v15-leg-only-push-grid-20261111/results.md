# Leg-only extension screen

The control arm exactly reproduced the prior wing-suppressed q/v and full-substep contacts, and every arm matched it through step 248. Four of the sixteen hip/ankle combinations passed the predeclared leg-only jump gate in all eight development starts: all four used the +0.18 ankle extension. Their first unloaded interval occurred at step 253, rising at minimum 0.489 to 0.632 m/s depending on hip target, before recontact at steps 260–262 with wing strokes intentionally suppressed. The other twelve arms failed the clean rising-unload gate; some first unloaded while falling. No arm recorded native solver failure or termination. Every passing arm needs its independently checked toe geometry, an integrated flight handoff, independent perturbed starts and the remaining navigation and landing tests. This screen is not an unused-layout or biological qualification.

# Constructed perch placement experiment

The +0.02 rad returned-hip placement adjustment (final target -0.02 rad) passed the predeclared release and regrip gates in two closely related constructed stationary starts. The foot was clear with the opposite foot supporting and gripping for 46 and 42 consecutive 20 ms intervals. Both then showed supported bilateral opposed toe contact for all of the final 100 intervals, with no nonfoot contact or fall. The baseline placement lost left toe opposition in all 100 terminal intervals. The -0.02 rad adjustment failed; lane 6 fell late.

Paired initial states, 3,484 endpoint states and 111,488 substep contact records matched through their respective intervention points with zero differences. All 4,400 endpoints had matching endpoint/substep total foot normal impulses to within 1.01e-9 N s; all required substeps and pose records were present. These are only two related starts on a constructed stationary perch with deliberately high, uncalibrated friction. The result is not a landing, realistic friction calibration, autonomous mission, or a robust qualification. Both normal opposition and force/support criteria are analyzed in `high/analysis-gates.json`.

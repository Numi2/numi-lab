# Extended continuous development-route landing

The teacher-off development run was extended from 3800 to 4400 control intervals, on the same used layout and seed. First-episode states and contact-substep lines matched the original record byte-for-byte in every lane through its original endpoint. This is a continuation test of the older flight and gait setup, not the newer candidate or a held-out qualification.

Lane 5 opened its left toes and had 16 consecutive intervals with no left normal contact while the right foot supplied at least half body weight; it then physically failed at step 3920. Lane 7 had 105 such open-toe/right-support intervals (3964–4068), followed by 309 intervals through step 4400 with both sets of proximal toes flexed above 0.60 rad, both feet in contact and combined upward foot impulse at least half body weight without nonfoot platform impulse. Contact coverage is complete. However, its torso tilt reached 1.016 rad (about 58 degrees) and its speed 0.136 m/s during the last 25 intervals. This is not a stable upright supported landing. Normal impulses and toe flexion do not prove opposed wrapping, low slip, or geometric clearance throughout the release; the reported toe threshold is a diagnostic, not a biological criterion.

Three first episodes suffered physical failure, including lane 5. Other lanes generally did not acquire sustained platform support. The original trajectory used a development scene and an older unqualified flight controller, and neither continuous unused-layout navigation nor reliable landing is established. The full log is split into exact-hash-verified compressed pieces for Git; scripts stream those pieces without restoring a raw log.

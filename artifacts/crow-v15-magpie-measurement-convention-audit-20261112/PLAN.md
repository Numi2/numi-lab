The prior related-corvid comparison defined toe-off from any front toe and used 3D angles; the study defined recovery beginning at digit 3 lift-off and measured from a side camera. Reanalyze the already retained 20261127 v15 baseline at 800 Hz using the same contact grouping, 1e-7 N s impulse threshold, missing-data rules, q/v and joint anchor reconstruction, but substitute the modeled middle front toe's two segments as a putative digit-3 proxy. Keep rear-toe-first contact as the putative hallux touchdown. Independently evaluate joint angles both in 3D and after projection onto the heading/vertical plane using the per-substep root heading. Preserve raw and existing 21.25 ms boxcar analyses and report all counts, rejected windows, phase-orderings and knee-minimum fractions, with the prior any-toe 3D reference beside them. Validate projection with known planar and yaw-rotated vectors and verify recorded body anchors/replay as before.

The middle simulated front-toe name and base are only anatomical proxies; the model is not a validated crow skeleton. Study side-view camera and measured world heading are not identical to this moving projection, and 3D joint extrema may legitimately differ from 2D. Segmented contacts, filtered phases, slow speed, surface, size and species differences prevent biological validation or direct parameter fitting.

# Experimental contact-gated toe release

This unqualified candidate extends the autonomous approach and balance controller with a one-shot contact-gated load transfer, left-toe opening, leg lift and return. It waits for repeated opposing bilateral toe contacts, gradually shifts hip-roll targets, requires measured right-foot upward and opposing impulses before and during release, aborts to regrip on lost support, and waits for consecutive no-left-contact observations before returning. The last-substep impulse is a control signal, not sufficient proof of support; external full-substep force, clearance, pose and first-episode audits are mandatory. The threshold assumes 16 physics substeps and estimated model mass. This uses status bits 16–19 and 28–31 after support and must not be combined with another controller using those bits. It is not qualified, calibrated, or safe for final-layout claims.

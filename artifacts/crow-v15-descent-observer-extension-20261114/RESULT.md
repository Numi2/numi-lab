# Observation-extended turn, climb and descent screen

In a full-start, teacher-off, no-scheduled-reset rerun of the 48-second used-seed training-scene trial, the six stronger launch lanes 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 met every preregistered fixed-profile physical and trajectory gate. The new observer was built by extending recording bounds only. All recorded first-episode states, including q/v, policy actions and recorded contacts, exactly matched the incomplete-observer run through 2,400; logged targets and contact substeps exactly matched through 1,800. The rerun has complete finite state, target and 16-substep toe/contact observations through 2,400, and no observer errors. None of the six recontacted after its three-interval release, produced observed nonfoot impulse, or hit the toe-speed threshold. The two weaker .70-rad lanes recontacted at 396 and 389 and failed the screen.

Relative signed turns against the straight reference reached approximately -0.257 and +0.256–0.258 rad; mean absolute height error was 8.1–14.4 mm in level flight, 8.0–8.3 mm during the climb window, and 7.9–9.1 mm in the descent window. All eight reached the horizon without a reported physical failure, but no lane completed the course. Pronation actuator effort was close to its modeled cap in approximately 25.4% of turn-window samples, with maximum target error near 0.0724 rad.

This result supersedes only the incomplete observation coverage of the earlier descent run; its invalid initial audit and missing-data failure remain archived. It is a correlated fixed-script development test on one used seed and scene, not arbitrary command, independent disturbed-start recovery, biological validation, a frozen flight controller, toe grip, supported landing or a continuous mission on unused layouts. Contact/state epochs remain independently unverified.

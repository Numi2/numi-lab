# Result: oscillation and no landing

All eight first episodes reached all five development waypoints with no recorded bird-to-course contact and complete physics substep coverage; all timed out without a perch contact or supported landing. Increasing the proportional forward command produced pronounced fore-aft oscillations; in individual retained samples speeds reached about 0.5 m/s. The first-episode final root x positions spanned 4.527 to 5.388 m and z positions 1.337 to 1.500 m. This teacher-guided development trial is not qualified autonomous flight, grasp, or landing.

# Phase-gated pronation: promising heading, unqualified

On used development seed 20261126, both all-body-wrench-ablated leg-launch candidates completed their 1,800-step first episodes without recorded post-release ground contact. Relative to each candidate's own independently restored straight-flight baseline, peak yaw changes through the command window were -0.1547 and +0.1542 rad; maximum lateral deviations were 0.1737 and 0.1752 m. All untouched lanes exactly matched the baseline through their first episode and the two candidates matched through tick 600. The physical pronation target delta matched the registered waveform within 8.1e-6 rad.

This trial **fails its full qualification gate**: pronation effort instrumentation covered ticks 650-1150 rather than the entire tick 600-1200 steering window. In the observed window the drives were at their model effort cap for 65.1% and 65.0% of sampled substeps, versus 57.9% and 57.8% for the matched straight-flight baseline. Maximum observed target-versus-position error was 0.1131 rad; observed target, position, speed and effort stayed within the registered model bounds. Saturation and tracking error call for investigation even within bounds. The phase rule was selected using development data; neither layout/seed holdout, disturbed-start recovery, full turn qualification, supported landing nor biological validity is established. Controller remains unfrozen. Exact raw traces, all gates, failed condition, native source and hashes are retained.

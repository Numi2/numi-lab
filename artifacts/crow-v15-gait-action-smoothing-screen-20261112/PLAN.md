The retained 1.25 ms joint traces show second-difference acceleration proxies concentrated at 20 ms command boundaries. The position target is held within each control interval; its existing first-order action response is updated at control rate. As an exploratory intervention, increase only the filtered response time for hip, knee and ankle actions during the 4–6 s walking interval to at least 50 ms. Keep actuator mechanics, limits, contact, body, gait amplitudes, toe controls, flight and landing unchanged. This is not a crow-muscle estimate.

First compare the same eight used-development seed-20261126 starts to the reference with the validated substep q/contact observer. Require exact q/v/action and contact parity through step 199, complete samples without solver or observer error, at least two support-qualified cleared bouts per foot per lane, forward travel at least 90% of its matched reference, no additional toe speed caps or nonfoot impulse above 1e-7 N s, and no increase in no-foot-loaded substeps. Require aggregate mean absolute knee and ankle q second-difference proxies at control boundaries to decrease by at least 20% for each of the four joints. Report within-interval values too. If any gate fails reject; if all pass, test the second development seed, longer walking and the complete sequence before considering promotion. These engineering thresholds are not biological calibration, and acceleration proxies are not torque or direct stiffness measurements.

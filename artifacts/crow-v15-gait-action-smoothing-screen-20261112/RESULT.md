# Walking action smoothing: rejected

The 50 ms minimum response-time candidate failed every lane's predeclared short walking gate. During the 2 s measured window, 200 substeps had neither foot above the normal-impulse threshold versus 30 in the matched reference. Seven lanes had fewer than two support-qualified right-foot bouts, and every lane fell below 90% of its matched reference forward displacement. There were no added toe speed caps or nonfoot impulse intervals in the measured window. Pre-intervention endpoint states/actions and substep contact records matched, and the native run reported no errors.

Mean absolute q second-difference proxies at control boundaries rose by 14.1% and 11.5% for left and right knees, and by 6.2% and 1.1% for left and right ankles. Within-interval means fell, demonstrating that lower between-update variation alone does not solve the boundary spikes. These are motion proxies, not measured torque or biological stiffness. The initial analysis script incorrectly assumed the native run emitted exactly 300 intervals; its failed log is retained. The corrected script explicitly bounds both runs to steps 1–300 and enforces full coverage.

This used-seed experiment does not qualify gait, takeoff, flight or landing. The smoothing candidate is rejected and not integrated; no second seed was consumed for this failed first screen.

# Exploratory contact distribution by modeled segment

Using the already observed seed-20261127 lane-4 baseline and 0.16-knee-warp 800 Hz measurements, summarize normal impulse and impulse associated with tangential point speed over 0.05 m/s by foot/toe body name. This is a post-hoc descriptive diagnosis, not a predeclared test or animal validation. Body names identify simulated rigid segments, not verified anatomical homologues. Contact-point and post-step state timing differ; speed is not accumulated slip and the threshold is an engineering cut. No new simulation is performed.

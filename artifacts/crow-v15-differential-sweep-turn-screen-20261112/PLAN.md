# Differential wing-sweep turn screen

Bank and pitch feedback alone produced substantial sideslip and did not complete either commanded 90-degree turn. Test whether bounded antisymmetric physical wing-sweep commands change the response: after 9 seconds add a mirrored + or -0.30 action residual to the existing bilateral pitch sweep, with opposite signs on left and right. Six lanes preserve the corresponding baseline bank/pitch settings; paired +/-90-degree requests use mode-paired residual directions -0.30, 0, and +0.30. The middle pair is an unchanged-code-path sanity control. All actuator limits remain in force. This compares each lane to its corresponding retained bank/pitch run; require pre-intervention q/v/action sample identity, all contact substeps and no solver errors. It is an exploration of this model, not a claim of biological yaw mechanism.

Require the same strict sampled engineering turn checks as the prior sideslip-feedback screen: no nonfoot contacts or physical failure, full 1600 intervals, sampled height 0.65–1.15 m and abs pitch <0.35 rad, sampled forward speed >=0.5 m/s and absolute sideslip <=45 degrees over intervals 800–1600, final heading within 15 degrees of the commanded +/-90 degrees. Both directions of a pair must pass. These values are not measured American crow targets. Direct body moments before 9 seconds, sparse samples and used development seed preclude flight qualification even on pass.

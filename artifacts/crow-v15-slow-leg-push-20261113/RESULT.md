# Slower wing-suppressed leg extension did not launch

The 160 ms development candidate met the pre-intervention data integrity checks: its eight lanes matched the active crouch control in every retained endpoint state, action and contact sample through step 345 and all recorded contact substeps for steps 335–345. All three compared first episodes ran 400 steps with complete recorded states, contact substeps and leg-effort samples; no contact-buffer overflow or observer error was reported.

In the six prespecified comparable lanes, the candidate's signed upward foot impulse over steps 348–375 was 0.2070–0.2116 N s **lower** than the crouch control. Sampled root height rose at most 18.43–18.58 mm relative to step 350, no lane had three consecutive unloaded intervals in steps 351–380, and root vertical velocity at step 375 was approximately -0.196 to -0.200 m/s. Both the prespecified impulse and lift screens fail. The slow arm showed no logged nonfoot ground impulse or toe-speed-cap substeps in steps 348–380. It is not a leg-powered takeoff.

The interval is a closed-loop contrast: the hold arm's surrounding controller changes after 7.4 s. A post-hoc split of lanes 2, 5 and 7 shows the slow-minus-hold impulse was +0.0090 to +0.0100 N s in steps 348–365, followed by approximately -0.216 to -0.221 N s in steps 366–375; do not treat the total as an isolated constant leg-force intervention. It does not explain where generalized work went.

A separate post-hoc native forward-kinematics audit verified selected lane-5 q and body poses against saved replay, then estimated model center-of-mass maximum rise in steps 351–380. The candidate's comparable-lane maximum rise was 17.89–18.02 mm. Its foot origins moved horizontally by up to roughly 25–29 mm relative to step 350; that is a whole-window foot-origin displacement, not a direct contact-point slip measurement, and the extrema need not occur while loaded. The first audit implementation rejected itself because it parsed the COM mass column as a position; that failed draft is retained as `kinematics-audit-initial-rejected.py`, and the corrected parser's replay checks and results are in `kinematics-audit.json`.

This is one exposed seed with correlated lanes, passive aerodynamics, active legs in both arms and direct body-moment authority before 6.3 s. The v15 physics and morphology are not biologically calibrated by this screen. Neither the leg-powered takeoff nor flight, navigation, grip and landing are qualified. Source, build hashes, full logs, replay and unmodified failed results are retained.

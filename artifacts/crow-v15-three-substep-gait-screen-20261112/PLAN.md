# Three-substep physical gait transition

In the used-seed paired screens, a two-substep ramp retained all eight lanes' travel and support but failed the predeclared 20% boundary-proxy reduction for both knees. A four-substep ramp reduced all boundary proxies but failed support and travel in several lanes. Test one intermediate three-substep transition with the same physical experiment and the unchanged baseline. This is adaptive development, not held-out evaluation.

Require the same complete evidence, bilateral supported clearance, at least 90% travel in each lane, no additional nonfoot contacts or toe caps, no increased total unsupported substeps, and at least 20% reduction of all four boundary proxies. Report full-substep distributions as post-hoc checks. A pass is still only a short engineering screen and needs other seeds, long-horizon walking and biological gait data before any promotion.

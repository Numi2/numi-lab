# Three-substep transition: rejected

In the eight dependent development lanes, all four mean boundary motion proxies improved by at least the predeclared 20%, and each lane retained at least 94% of its control forward travel with at least three supported clearance bouts per foot. However, there were 131 both-feet-unloaded substeps versus 30 in the matched control, so the predeclared support gate fails.

A post-hoc analysis of all substeps also found the 95th percentile ankle motion proxy was 146% and 143% of the control for left and right. This may reflect moving sharp motion away from the control boundary rather than reducing it throughout the step. No nonfoot contact or toe speed caps were recorded in the analyzed window. This adaptive, used-seed two-second screen does not qualify walking, biological movement or the chunk-one experimental implementation.

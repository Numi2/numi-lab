# Full-window modeled force observation of failed flight

The extended observer reran the same shader, policy packs, collider masks and v15 research body, and accepted q/v, policy actions and contacts exactly matched the prior first episodes through their respective terminal ticks in all six lanes. Its 16-substep observations were complete and finite from tick 500 up to (excluding) the first foot recontact, with no recorded normal impulse and no observer errors. Recontacts occurred at ticks 570, 570, 648, 648, 536 and 590. Model world-axis mean aerodynamic force over each corresponding window was [0.538, -0.109, 4.420], [0.539, -0.111, 4.420], [0.601, -0.135, 4.377], [0.602, -0.135, 4.377], [0.271, -0.051, 4.016] and [0.369, -0.087, 4.285] N for lanes 2–7. Approximate model weight was 4.415 N. Left and right modeled flap effort each reached the 3-unit bound in every lane.

Root world-x speed grew from 0.409/0.409/0.251/0.253/0.406/0.406 m/s at tick 500 to 1.044/1.045/1.798/1.799/0.587/0.866 m/s immediately before first foot recontact. Positive modeled forward force and mean lift near or below modeled weight are measurements, not a complete impulse or muscle-work budget. Root velocity is not COM velocity; mean force obscures periodic and transient forces. These observations do not prove the cause of contact, calibrate real bird biomechanics, or establish qualified flight. Full first-episode terminal contact evidence remains in the matched pitch trial; later reset samples must not be represented as a continuous trajectory.

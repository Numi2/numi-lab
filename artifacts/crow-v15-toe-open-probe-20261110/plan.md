# Reached-state toe-opening probe

Before execution, take the physically reached supported J step 5000 state from the guided bounded-rebound trial, including q, v and actual perch pose. From that identical state with a fresh solver cache, compare two independent 160-step, 5 ms single-perch arms: hold all initial joint targets, or ramp all eight simulated toe target angles to zero over 0.4 s while holding the other targets. Use the same v15 body, inertia, materials, SAT collision and effort limits. Measure per-toe angle, front and rear opposing impulses, vertical support, body motion, nonfoot contact, and any solver failure. Require the open arm to actually open relative to hold and reduce opposing force while remaining physically supported, without artificial forces; separately report loss of support. This is an isolated exploratory actuation test, not proof of integrated intentional release, takeoff from a perch, autonomy, long-term stability or biological realism.

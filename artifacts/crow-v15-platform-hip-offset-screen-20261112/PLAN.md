# Contact-conditioned hip position at development landing

An independently reconstructed full-body center-of-mass audit found that in one tipping lane the vertical-impulse-weighted foot contacts moved from behind the mass center to 0.022 m then 0.094 m ahead as body pitch declined; the sampled contact angular impulse was also negative. This motivates a bounded exploratory bilateral hip-target offset to change foot/body geometry through the existing articulated motor after terminal contact, not an applied body wrench. Keep two unchanged controls and test six signed offsets of 0.04, 0.08, 0.12 in individual lanes, keeping the existing hip command limit. Full continuous 4800-interval starts, no teacher, no scheduled reset; compare original first-episode parity and report true contact, root tilt/speed, toe release/regrip and every first failure. Require sustained bilateral support, no nonfoot contact, low tilt and speed and whole-sequence integrity, with all observer limitations stated. This used development layout, older unqualified flight, one start per setting and no direct slip measurement cannot qualify the task, even if an arm looks promising.

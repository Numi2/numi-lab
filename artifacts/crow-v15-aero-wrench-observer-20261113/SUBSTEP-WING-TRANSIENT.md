# Substep wing velocity exposes a handoff transient

The previous smooth-release report recorded maximum wing-flap speed only at 20 ms control endpoints (8.24–8.53 rad/s for lanes 2–7). The retained pre-solve full generalized velocities in the same parity-validated run show a larger transient: all six comparable lanes reach 37.68–38.00 rad/s at control step 369, physics substep 3. The bracketed aerodynamic kernel's vertical force at those samples is 34.6–36.9 N. Some of those samples also include positive foot-normal impulse; others do not. The authored model speed limit is 90 rad/s, but it is not a crow-specific physiological calibration.

This is a correction to the scope of the endpoint result: lower endpoint maxima did not bound within-step speed. The correlation of high wing velocity and high modeled aero force is consistent with a handoff transient but does not identify its physical cause or establish a real animal limit. Preserve the full substep force and velocity logs. Future wing-controller assessment must include substep joint velocity and target rate, not just endpoint samples, and must audit timestep sensitivity before using these force peaks for qualification.

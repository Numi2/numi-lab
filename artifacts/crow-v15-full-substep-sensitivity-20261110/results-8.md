# Eight-substep full-body development trial: failed

The exact-weight rebound policies passed the native compatibility gate. The original-J development start reached five waypoints at step 2761 without a reported physical or solver failure, but sampled minimum obstacle clearance was 0.03504 m (right wing / slalom B, step 2508), below the preregistered 0.05 m floor. The replay is continuous and teacher off.

After waypoint five there were 64 bilaterally loaded accepted endpoints, at most 27 consecutive, zero endpoints with simultaneous opposed front/rear toe contact on both feet, and no contact-conditioned release event. The bird was unsupported at the final endpoint. This is not a supported landing or grip/release success. During steps 5900–5999, which contain the longest intermittent support runs, independent accepted-pose box SAT found a worst toe–perch overlap of 5.606 mm. This selected window is not a pre-release window; no release occurred. The isolated-box suggestion therefore does not generalize to this full-body run. Sampled flight toe angles after 25 contact-free endpoints stayed below 0.000340 rad, which does not qualify all starts or substeps. Full trace, replay and reports are retained.

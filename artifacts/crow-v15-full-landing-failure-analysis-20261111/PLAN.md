# Landing trace analysis: development data

Question: among first episodes of the existing eight-lane teacher-off development run, do failures occur before first physical foot contact, during initial capture, or after supported grip? Are landing phases retained when physical support is lost? What nonfoot contact precedes each termination?

Prediction to check: lanes that never obtain lasting support remain in approach or enter contact without sustained bilateral opposition; lanes that fall after supported grip show loss of load or nonfoot contact before termination. A phase-2 flag alone is not physical support.

Before analysis, use the already-recorded phase snapshots, accepted root states, all physics-substep scene contact records, contact capacity and termination steps. Record first phase transitions, first foot contact, all supported grip bouts, last supported interval, and nonfoot contact pairs around failure. Verify full substep coverage and preserve episode boundaries; use the existing 0.5-body-weight and opposing-toe-normal criterion as a mechanical screen, not a biological claim. Samples are discrete, and correlation does not establish cause. Distinguish flight/approach, capture and post-capture failure; no controller intervention or held-out claim follows from this observation.

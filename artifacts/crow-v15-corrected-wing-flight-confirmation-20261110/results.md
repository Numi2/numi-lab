# Estimated-wing flight confirmation

The registered flight screen passed 80/80 lanes on each of the two prespecified seeds. There were no solver failed steps or recorded physical failures, and all lanes met the heading, lateral-speed, speed and height limits across all six evaluation samples. Maximum sampled toe joint speed was 0.03191 rad/s; maximum forward speed error was 0.1172 m/s and maximum height error 0.01523 m. Median climb-minus-descent height was approximately 0.400 m for each seed. Tested ground starts were perturbed and covered straight, left, right, climb and descent at 0.70 m/s. The model, control and force geometry remain experimental estimates; this result does not show real bird dynamics, airborne disturbance recovery, other speed commands, obstacle navigation, supported landing or reliable takeoff/walk. Seeds are not claimed as final layout tests.

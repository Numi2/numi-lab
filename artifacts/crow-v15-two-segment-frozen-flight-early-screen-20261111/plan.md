# Two-segment frozen-flight early admission

After the two-step preflight succeeds, run the exact frozen research V2 controller and research wing/aero model with the 33-body two-segment toe candidate, unchanged rebound zero diagnostic policy, serial widened ABA and 16 substeps. Test 80 matched development lanes on previously used seed 20261120 for 200 control intervals; all five prescribed flight modes are present, although this early horizon ends before the full steady-state tracking evaluation. Disable course-object collisions as in the original flight capability screen and retain ground collision. Record all first-episode outcomes, solver failures, trajectory samples and input/runtime hashes. No failure in all 80 is required to proceed to the separately specified 1,600-interval tracking and recovery screens. This screen does not establish full flight qualification, autonomous navigation, a landing, or biological realism.

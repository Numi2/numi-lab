# Result: no fall at cutoff, but no qualified grip

All eight teacher-guided development starts reached all five waypoints and physically touched the perch. All eight first episodes remained active at the 4200-interval cutoff; none can be counted as a pass. No lane had any interval meeting the bilateral opposing-toe, partial-support, upright and low-speed criterion. The separate nominal-weight criterion also had zero intervals. Each lane did have some rear-toe contact. Seven first episodes hit the toe velocity limit after ground handoff; none hit it before the first course-contact interval. No substeps were missing and no execution errors were detected. The runs match their aligned-descent q, v and policy actions over the tested pre-contact prefix.

The sustained-contact heuristic delayed wing folding and avoided the recorded early failures seen in some matched baseline starts, but these censored, unfinished trials do not demonstrate stable landing. The contact-normal threshold is not a measurement of vertical support. This is a development-only teacher screen with no explicit release and no held-out autonomous qualification; do not promote this controller on the strength of this result.

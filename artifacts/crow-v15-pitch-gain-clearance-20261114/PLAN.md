# Stronger pitch response during clearance

In the matched wider-hip trial, pitch increased from 0.166 rad at release to about 0.257 rad when clearance failed, while the stance hip pitch correction approached its existing cap; increasing roll range did not improve clearance. Test whether an earlier pitch correction reduces the coupled drift. Both lanes retain the prior wider-hip controller and existing physical forces, contacts, toe bounds, clearance feedback and slew. Lane 1 alone changes the pitch-change feedback gain from 0.50 to 0.80, retaining the same 0.10 rad correction cap and 0.002 rad per step slew. The sign and dynamic effect remain hypotheses.

Lane 0 must reproduce the prior candidate over 450 states and the paired prefix must match before the first target difference. Require at least 100 consecutive intervals satisfying the unchanged clearance, stance contact, posture, load and speed gates, no pre-fall accepted-state toe speed threshold, no nonfoot support or errors, all 32 substeps, and reconstructed feedback and physical bounds. Preserve failures. Toes remain open; no regrip or landing is tested. Contact/state timing remains independently unverified.

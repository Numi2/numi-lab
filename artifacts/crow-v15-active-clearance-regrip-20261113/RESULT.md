# Support-gated active clearance: failed

Both candidate q/v trajectories exactly matched the preceding supported-pitch control through 208 accepted states before intervention. The native run completed 550 intervals with all 32 contact substeps and no solver errors; last-substep foot impulse agreed with the independent observer within 6.21e-10 N s. Reposition increments obeyed support, posture, open-toe and clearance gates, and knee/ankle targets stayed within model limits. The feedback lift command increments respected the 0.003 and 0.006 rad per interval settings and reached the existing 0.15 rad cap.

The independently checked initial clearance streaks were twelve and eleven intervals. Subsequent knee/ankle repositioning stalled at 6% and 4% of the requested offsets; the hip lift reached its cap without restoring sufficient clearance for completion. Neither lane entered return or achieved regrip, and the birds fell at steps 340 and 343. This rejects the tested clearance feedback, not all possible physical controls. The result is a constructed stationary-start diagnostic, not airborne landing, full mission or real-bird validation. Full traces and unsuccessful states are retained.

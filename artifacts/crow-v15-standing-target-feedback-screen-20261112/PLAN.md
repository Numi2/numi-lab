# Physical knee target feedback: exploratory standing screen

The earlier action-path test was masked during standing. Test bounded additions to the actual position target of existing knee drives during the first 2 s only, gated by previous accepted foot support and proportional to accepted root vertical velocity. No externally applied force or altered contact parameter. Eight lanes receive different feedback gains, including opposite signs and a zero-gain control. Compare each to the same-start prior run; check zero-gain exact parity and q/v and contact path sensitivity. Judge normal support, peak center-of-mass velocity and joint speed caps, not any one metric. Lane-specific exploratory gains require matched all-start tests and later full sequence before any promotion. Starts are development data and the 3 s horizon cannot establish walking, takeoff, flight, or landing.

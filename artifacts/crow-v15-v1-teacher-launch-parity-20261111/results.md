# Matched-launch V1 teacher flight

All first 350 q and v samples exactly matched the existing teacher-off development-J run. The privileged teacher then reached five waypoints at step 1305 in that first episode without a recorded physical failure. It did not land: foot-support count was zero and the episode timed out. Full accepted-step obstacle-clearance records are in analysis.json.

This is one privileged development route, not evidence that an autonomous actor can follow it or that a teacher reliably completes a supported landing. It is not a final held-out evaluation or an imitation-training set. The next separate landing test must validate its own physical contact and the full uninterrupted sequence.

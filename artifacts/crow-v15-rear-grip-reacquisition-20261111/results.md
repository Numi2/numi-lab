# Bounded rear-toe contact reacquisition

The 1,000-tick development replay matched the reference through tick 85. The bounded feedback increased the number of bilateral opposing-grip pose samples from 45 to 118 compared with the matched-balance held-toe trial, and reacquired measurable rear impulse after contact dropped (for example tick 140). Worst observed toe/perch overlap during opposing grip was 0.449 mm. However, the longest supported run declined from 220 to 199 samples, both rear targets eventually reached the existing 0.85 rad limit, the bird fell, and terminal grip and support failed. The candidate is rejected; recovering contact by itself did not stabilize the body. The first 1,000 ticks are a single development start, not whole-sequence or biological qualification, and this costly host-readback prototype is not production control.

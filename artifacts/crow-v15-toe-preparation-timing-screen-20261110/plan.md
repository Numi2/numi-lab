# Toe preparation timing screen

The first actual foot contact in the separated development drops occurs around tick 2, while the baseline begins closing rear toes at tick 10. Compare the unchanged bilateral toe-regulated reference (arm 4) with otherwise identical closures beginning at tick 0 or tick 5 (arms 5 and 6), on known failed start 0 and previously passing start 1. This is an exploratory test of contact timing, using existing toe actuators, not a claim of natural biological timing; the initial toes remain open and no pose or force is injected. Require both unchanged arms to match all 200 prior states, all initial shapes separated, no failed runs, and both candidate starts to pass sustained opposing grip, release, terminal open-toe support and maximum grip-conditioned overlap 0.5 mm before wider perturbation screening. Preserve failures. The tests do not qualify a flying approach, unused layouts, or continuous navigation.

# Coordinated front-toe and stance-drive sensitivity

The front-toe support trial produced rear-first ordering in four of five qualifying stances in one lane with a 0.15 rad target, but that lane walked backward and exceeded low-toe thresholds. Test the engineering hypothesis that a modified supporting-leg stroke can restore forward travel with the same front-toe target, keeping original v15 body, other gait parameters, rear release, contacts and policy fixed. Use front peak 0.15 rad in all lanes and stance gains [.10,.20,.30,.35,.40,.50,.25,.60]; lane 6 is the unchanged prior 0.15/0.25 combination and must reproduce its same-seed predecessor. The range is a bounded action-position drive sensitivity, not measured corvid kinematics or assumed safe/realistic control.

Two-step smoke, then eight lanes, 300 steps, 16 substeps, previously used seed 20261127 and no resets. Require exact prewalk parity to the prior front-drive trial, full episodes, no reported physics errors, overflow, nonfoot contact or toe cap, >=3 supported clearance bouts per foot per lane, no extra both-feet-zero-normal substeps versus held per lane, >=90% held forward displacement per lane, <=120% held sole-clear/toe-low endpoints, loaded contact-point speed median and p95 <=1.5x held, and >=75% rear-first release among complete stances with both toe groups and >=2 such stances per lane/foot. Missing groups and partial stances remain excluded, not success. One initial lane per gain is a screening limitation; no biological or full-sequence qualification follows.

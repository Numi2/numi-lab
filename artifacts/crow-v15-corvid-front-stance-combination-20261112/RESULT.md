# Combined front-toe and stance-drive sweep: rejected

All eight first episodes completed 300 steps with no reported physical failure, solver error, overflow, nonfoot-ground contact or toe cap. The unchanged lane 6 exactly reproduced the retained 0.15 front / 0.25 stance trial over 300 endpoints and 4,800 contact substeps; all lanes matched the 200-step prewalk prefix.

Of 57 complete stances with both toe groups, 29 ended rear-first, 22 front-first and six in the same recorded 1.25 ms substep. Another 43 complete foot-contact runs lacked a toe group, so their relative release cannot be inferred; 31 runs were partial. Different gains used different starting lanes and are not independent estimates of an isolated effect.

The overall screen failed: 388 sole-clear/toe-low endpoints versus 49 held, and loaded contact-point median/p95 speed 0.00647/0.07899 m/s versus 0.00276/0.03484 held. Lanes 0 and 2 lacked three full-foot supported clearance bouts on at least one side. Every lane except lane 6 had extra both-feet-zero-normal substeps versus held; lane 6 moved backward. Lanes 2, 3, 6 and 7 traveled below 90% of held. Such thresholded substeps are not individually proof of falling, and sampled point speed is not integrated slip. The result cannot be promoted even where one contact-order or travel value improves.

The magpie ordering is a qualitative related-corvid reference, not measured American-crow joint angles or substrate response. These are short used-seed results and do not qualify realistic gait, takeoff, flight, grip, landing or continuous autonomous execution.

# Eight-second contact-driven walking screen

The extended observer and shader exactly reproduced the previous gait before second 2, and reconstructed foot poses matched the native replay. Each of the eight developmental starts produced 16 alternating physically clear, unloaded swing bouts per foot over eight seconds, advanced approximately 0.106–0.108 m, kept sampled root tilt below 0.034 rad, and had no nonfoot ground contact, native solver failure or termination. However, every lane failed the predeclared continuous-support gate: six successive physics substeps in the same control interval at step 128 had summed foot-normal impulse below 1e-7 N s. The impulses were small positive values, not necessarily zero; this observation establishes loss of support above the chosen measurement threshold, not proof of geometric separation. No arm is qualified as continuously supported walking.

This is an eight-second development start set with endpoint geometry and full-substep contact impulses. It does not establish contact-point slip, biological calibration, resolution convergence, perturbed walking or a full takeoff-to-landing sequence. The next experiment will examine a gradual contact-driven gait onset while preserving the failed run.

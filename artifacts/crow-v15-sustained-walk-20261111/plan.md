# Sustained contact-driven walking

The developmental 16-substep gait completed four alternating, physically clear swings per foot in two seconds, but long-duration stability and foot slip were not established. Extend the same contact-gated alternating leg commands from two seconds to eight seconds (episode seconds 2–10), defer launch preparation until after this trial, and suppress active flapping during the walking window. Do not change body, toe damping, geometry, solver, scene, control targets or contact forces. Extend the passive state observer to record every control interval and retain all 16 ground-contact substeps. Eight development starts, 500 control ticks, one continuous episode, no reset; this is not an independent final-layout test.

Require exact q/v and contact parity with the earlier gait before second 2; from steps 101–500, require in every lane at least 12 separate three-or-more-pose clear unloaded swing bouts per foot with opposite-foot upward impulse >=half bodyweight, every substep to retain at least one supporting foot, no nonfoot contact, >=0.05 m forward progress, bounded body tilt <=0.35 rad and speed <=0.5 m/s, and no native failure or termination. Independently reconstruct all sole/toe endpoint clearances and cross-check a lane against native replay. Report all failures, actual support loss, body motion and drift; accepted endpoints do not establish swept geometry, and foot-center motion is not a contact-point slip measurement. A pass still requires perturbed walking, temporal refinement, physical takeoff linkage and complete navigation/landing.

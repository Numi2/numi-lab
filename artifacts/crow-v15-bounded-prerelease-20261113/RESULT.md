# Result: bounded pre-release timing failed the strict screen

The two arms completed 400 intervals with all recorded state/action/contact prefixes exactly matched to one another and the prior bounded range push through 345. All observed flap working efforts obeyed their lane bounds and no errors or overflow were reported. Every comparable push lane recontacted after its first three-interval release, and every lane exceeded the one-substep toe-speed threshold allowance. Lanes 2–3 also had earlier support gaps and 12–14 toe events; lanes 4–5 released at 355 then recontacted 366–369 with 8–9 toe events; lanes 6–7 had earlier support gaps, recontacted 362–369, and recorded 4–7 toe events. First-stroke counter onset was recorded by step 355; counter timing is not direct physical downstroke measurement.

Peak flap speed was 23.51–23.85 rad/s at effort bound 4, 32.16–32.26 at 8, and 28.44–28.45 at 16 in the observed 348–375 window. These are model diagnostics, not measured physiological limits. Despite satisfying the two early foot-impulse thresholds, no bound passed in both lanes and the strict screen failed in all six lanes. This used-seed test does not qualify takeoff, flight, landing, or either final layout. Both complete traces, source, binary hashes and analysis are retained.

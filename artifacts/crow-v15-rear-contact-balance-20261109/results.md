# Rear-contact hip balance results

Both positive and negative trials reached five waypoints, then failed the physical tilt condition at step 2404, with 148 consecutive bilateral opposing toe-load endpoints and no release. Across all 3000 published frames, q and v are exactly identical to the no-balance baseline. Even the reduced one-increment gate produced no measurable intervention; contact-state instrumentation is needed. No trial passed the complete sequence. The layouts were previously used in development; anatomy and drive values remain estimates.

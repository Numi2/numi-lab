# Stance foretoe closure: improved contact coverage, failed regrip

The paired constructed-state trial failed the full screen in both lanes. The modified stance toe achieved 14 qualifying supported release intervals versus 11 in the unchanged control, but neither lane achieved any qualifying bilateral regrip interval; first root-height-below-0.1 m occurred at accepted steps 307 and 308 respectively. The control exactly reproduced all 450 archived baseline states, and the two lanes were identical until the first stance foretoe target difference at zero-based tick 121. The target's maximum step was 0.030000002 rad, within the registered 0.03 rad per interval tolerance. Observed accepted toe speeds reached the modeled 12 rad/s limit in both lanes; per-substep effort/speed was not measured here.

After return, the stance opposing-contact gate failed in 44 screened candidate intervals compared with 76 control intervals, while the returning-foot opposing contact failed in 87 versus 66. In this closed-loop trajectory the stronger stance contact let the returning-leg placement progress to its full command, so these counts do not isolate a single causal effect. Subsequent local timing and geometry analysis is required. Contact coverage was complete, clearance was independently cross-checked to 1.3e-8 m, and both failure traces, inputs and exact executable are preserved. This is not a supported landing or qualification.

# Exploratory damped stop transition

From +0.25 m/s to zero, an extra 0.8 velocity feedback gain led to 0/80 trajectories meeting the pre-existing +/-0.08 m/s limit over every sampled point from 20 to 30 s, with no recorded physical failures. Representative speed overshoot was larger than the undamped transition. Neither case establishes the desired stop performance; retain the unchanged flight law and explore the outer approach separately.

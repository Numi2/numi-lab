# Pre-handoff teacher parity candidate

Delaying privileged teacher commands and feedback until 8 seconds made the teacher-on and teacher-off accepted q/v, policy outputs and contacts identical for all eight development starts at observer steps 1–399. Candidate teacher-off samples also matched the original teacher-off run in that window. On replay lane 1, q/v, actor observations and accepted policy outputs were identical for the first 399 frames. This is a pre-handoff diagnostic, not proof of applied actuator identity, teacher safety after handoff, absence of future privileged leakage, landing or autonomy.

The original host and policy packs are archived in the walk-launch controls runtime; the corrected replay writer is archived in the replay-writer roundtrip runtime. The frozen flight source is unchanged.

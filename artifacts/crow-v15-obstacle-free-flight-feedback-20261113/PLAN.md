# Preregistered bounded flight-feedback development screen

The exactly matched obstacle-free control still recontacted ground, exceeded 2 m/s and terminated on contact before 900 ticks. Test whether bounded wing-target feedback can reduce speed growth and height loss. This is an uncalibrated, used-seed engineering experiment, not flight qualification or a change to the reserved mission layouts.

Retain the same obstacle-free diagnostic scene (five course colliders disabled, ground active), v15 research body, consistent wing gains, other controls, smooth launch target schedule and 16-substep first-episode 900-tick horizon. The same actor arrays/transforms and policy fingerprints are used. Instrument the task-state quantities consumed by the proposed controller: stored yaw-frame forward velocity, root height and vertical velocity. Verify them against the accepted root pose and velocity in the matching recorded control step (derive yaw-frame x velocity from the recorded quaternion); record errors, available sample coverage and any time offset. If the data do not establish the quantities or their timing, reject the run as an invalid feedback experiment rather than treating them as true sensors.

For candidate paired lanes 2-3, 4-5 and 6-7, after 8.2 s blend over 0.4 s from the existing targets to flapping amplitude clamp(0.70 + H*(0.65-height) - V*vertical_speed, 0.55, 0.85) and bilateral sweep clamp(0.10 + S*(0.35-forward_speed), 0.04, 0.12). Use gains (H,V,S) = (0.04,0.02,0.05), (0.08,0.04,0.08), and (0.12,0.06,0.10), respectively. Keep 6.4 Hz, onset schedule and 0.18 rad pronation unchanged. These are bounded position targets through existing joint drives, not added body forces. The prior no-feedback obstacle-free run is the counterfactual; compare each lane's accepted q/v/actions/contacts to it up to the first actual drive-target difference, and require exact equality before feedback changes the target.

Require unchanged model/scene mask and policy, observed sensor-source validation, first-episode and substep coverage, no missing target history, valid actuator bounds, and before-feedback matched-state parity. A pair passes this exploratory screen only if both lanes reach tick 900 in their first episode, remain foot-unloaded from first release with no nonfoot normal impulse, have at most one toe-speed threshold event in the observed transition, never reach a sampled modeled sweep stop, gain more than 0.5 m in world x over ticks 500-900, maintain root height 0.25-1.5 m throughout that interval and sampled x velocity 0-1.0 m/s throughout ticks 550-900. Record roll, pitch, angular velocity, actual target and joint ranges, first contact, termination and all failures. No unobserved late aero or actuator work claim. No pair can qualify flight, turn, climb, descent, recovery, navigation, landing or real-crow behavior from this one test; final layouts/seeds stay untouched.

# Isolated dedicated GPU contact sensor

The experimental contact-velocity publisher now writes a separate per-environment buffer and valid bit instead of overwriting the mechanical-power field. The sensor buffer is initialized, participates in the generic persistent-buffer checkpoint list, and is cleared to invalid by the publisher outside its test window. Host, shader, exact binary and source are retained. This is a research-only host with a changed buffer/checkpoint schema; no compatibility or production migration is claimed.

In two used seeds and eight correlated lanes each, the recorded 300-step state and toe-contact records plus the recorded q and v substeps match the aliased-sensor predecessor at logger precision. All 594 active-window sensor values per seed match. The retained lane-5 replay has 300 frames per seed and matches for q, v, reward, actor observations, accepted actions, body states, done, termination reason, timeout, tracking score and outcomes. This does not compare every GPU field, rewards in other lanes, or reset and rollback behavior. The checks include only the selected diagnostic window and do not show the earlier field actually changed rewards in those recordings.

The gain remains a non-robust exploratory gait: only two of twelve candidate starts passed the short screening checks, and matched repeats are not independent successes. The study data is related-corvid, slow simulated movement differs markedly from the study speeds, and this does not establish crow biological fit, powered takeoff, qualified flight, navigation, grip or supported landing. Tests of partial resets, checkpoint round trip and rollback plus schema compatibility are required before wider use.

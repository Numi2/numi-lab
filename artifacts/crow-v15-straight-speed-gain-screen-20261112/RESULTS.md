# Bounded speed-feedback gain screen

The two actor-command controls matched the previous 24-second flight run, and all other lanes matched its prefix through 9 s: 5,100 state samples and 81,600 foot/toe contact substeps. All eight remained airborne throughout 8–24 s without reported physical failure or nonroot velocity-limit hits after 4 s; some early limits remained. Course obstacle collisions were disabled and direct body moment actuators remained disabled.

For fixed .55 m/s and .95 m commands, body-yaw-frame mean forward speed over 12–24 s was .464–.465 m/s at gain .40, .492 m/s at gain .60, and .508 m/s at gain .80; corresponding sampled mean absolute speed errors were .092–.093, .072–.073 and .062–.063 m/s. In the same lane 4 on the preceding .24-gain run, mean speed was .431 m/s and mean absolute error .119 m/s, versus .492 and .072 at gain .60 here. World lateral speed and positive height error persisted (roughly .064–.066 m/s mean absolute lateral velocity and .084–.089 m mean altitude error for the candidate lanes). Values are 50 Hz endpoint estimates.

Gain .80 is a development candidate only: two previously used starts, one fixed target, uncalibrated aerodynamic forces, and no turns, unseen starts, recovery or landing. It is not frozen or navigation-qualified. Waypoint flags on this obstacle-disabled diagnostic have no mission significance.

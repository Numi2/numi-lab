# Early wing-pronation sensitivity

In one used J development start, neither of two opposite bounded launch-only pronation residuals prevented backward flight. At step 700 the negative residual yielded root forward speed -0.365 m/s, compared with -0.426 m/s for the same low-height command without a residual; the positive residual yielded -0.495 m/s. The temporary residual faded to zero by 16 seconds and used ordinary wing actuators; these samples are not a takeoff or biological qualification. No variant is promoted.

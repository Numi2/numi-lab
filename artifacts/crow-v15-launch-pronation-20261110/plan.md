On one used J start, test two opposing bounded 0.4 normalized pronation residuals during the contact-gated launch, fading to zero between 15 and 16 seconds. These use the existing ordinary articulated wing actuators and do not change aerodynamic coefficients or the frozen flight law; after 16 seconds the law receives no residual. Compare initial center-of-mass motion, takeoff clearance and contact against matched no-residual low-height command. The residual is an experimental launch controller and is not qualified flight, visual navigation, or biological evidence.

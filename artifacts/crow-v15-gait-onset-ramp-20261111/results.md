# Gradual contact-gated walking onset

The unchanged baseline exactly reproduced all previous q/v and full-substep contacts and again failed at six low-support substeps. All three gradual onset durations met the predeclared eight-second development gate in all eight starts: every physics substep had a foot-normal impulse above 1e-7 N s, with no nonfoot ground contacts, native solver failures or terminations. Each lane showed at least 14 alternating physically clear three-pose swing bouts per foot; all three arms exceeded 0.10 m forward travel. Independent reconstructed endpoint foot/toe poses matched the native replay.

The 0.5 s ramp yielded 15 left and 16 right swing bouts, with 0.105–0.107 m forward travel, and was selected after observing this screen as the smallest tested timing change for a connected takeoff experiment. The selection and subsequent integration are exploratory. These are eight development starts; the result does not establish contact-point slip, within-substep swept clearance, time convergence, disturbed walking, the complete route, landing or biological fidelity. The initial analysis variable-shadowing error and corrected recomputation are separately recorded.

# Height trim improved tracking but failed its combined registered screen

On used seed 20261126 the two candidate lanes completed their full 1800-step first episodes and passed the registered matched-prefix/reference, contact, coverage, target, speed, height and actuator checks. Over ticks 1250–1800, root-height mean absolute error was 0.00916 m against 0.80 m and 0.01232 m against 0.55 m, improving upon the prior approximately 0.10 m errors. Peak heights were 0.820 m and 0.603 m. This is a single used-seed paired trial tuned from those same development data.

The preregistered combined screen also required each candidate to show opposite signed 0.10 m height events against its own drifting straight-flight reference during ticks 650–1150. The high-target lane reached only +0.0463 m relative to its reference; the low-target lane reached -0.2233 m. Consequently the combined identification and tracking screen failed even though the individual 0.05 m mean-error tracking subcheck passed. Do not silently weaken or relabel the gate. Future switching-command tests can be separately preregistered to assess response in a way aligned with dynamic tracking. Flight, takeoff, toe grip, landing, navigation and biology remain unqualified.

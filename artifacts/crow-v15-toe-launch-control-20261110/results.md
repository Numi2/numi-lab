# Corrected toe-body launch controls

All eight matched pairs had identical pre-extension q/v. Holding the control crouch through flap suppression eliminated the confounding control hop: 0/8 control versus 1/8 push met the predeclared two-upward-endpoint unloaded criterion. A post-hoc secondary analysis measured one unloaded upward endpoint in each push lane and 22.4–22.9 mm root rise, versus approximately 0.2 mm and no such endpoint in controls. All 16 birds were airborne at 8 s after wing flapping resumed; this does not prove the leg push was necessary for subsequent flight, and passive aerodynamic forces were not eliminated. The two-second walking window advanced approximately 4.26–4.30 cm per lane with alternating measured foot loads and no nonfoot ground contacts, but contact-point slip and natural gait are unqualified. The layout and perturbations were developmental.

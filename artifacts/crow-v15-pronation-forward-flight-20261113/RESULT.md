# Bounded wing-pronation sensitivity: backward-flight failure

All push and crouched-hold lanes completed their 500-tick first episodes without physical termination. Recorded push/hold states, actions and contact substeps matched through tick 345; target-history and wing-drive metadata checks passed. This is one used training seed and layout with correlated lanes. The inherited lanes 0–1 are controls, not candidate replicates.

All six candidate push lanes had at least three unloaded intervals at tick 356–357 and no recorded foot recontact through tick 500. Peak whole-body COM rise was 0.082–0.097 m. However, root displacement over ticks 400–500 was -0.99 to -1.08 m along the course x axis, and mean x velocity was -0.49 to -0.54 m/s. None meets the preregistered forward-flight screen. Push lane 2 also exceeded the toe-speed threshold three times; other candidate push lanes had zero threshold events. There was no logged nonfoot normal impulse in the screen window. All matched wing-on holds unloaded and subsequently recontacted. Foot unloading or COM rise with wings operating is not proof of leg-powered takeoff.

Modeled late mean aerodynamic x force was negative in every candidate, approximately -0.087 to -0.117 N. In sampled lanes 6–7 over ticks 476–500, the observed total modeled aerodynamic work was approximately -9.28 J and the flap effort-displacement proxy approximately +24.1 J. These are model and numerical proxies, not validated crow muscle or aerodynamic measurements. Pronation targets stayed within the specified 0.18-rad amplitude; observed joint angles include response to coupled dynamics. No controller is frozen, and no maneuver, route, supported landing, biological or untouched-layout qualification follows. All failed data and provenance are retained.

# Bounded staggered-ankle gait screen

Question: can a small phase delay in an existing physical ankle drive separate the sampled knee and ankle angular minima without sacrificing the better retained development walk? The related-magpie study describes knee reversal before ankle reversal during recovery, but species, speed, body and joint geometry differ; this is a falsifiable engineering experiment, not an American-crow phase target.

Use the exact earlier development baseline, flight/launch/landing controls, two exposed seeds and eight lanes per seed, native physics and the prior contact and kinematic observer. In the existing 4–6 second walk only, delay the ankle half-sine carrier by 0.25 rad of its 0.5-second period (about 20 ms) while retaining its existing amplitude, envelope, contralateral-support gate, hip, knee and all remaining controls. Starting at step 200, this can also alter when the ankle begins moving; it is an exploratory candidate, not an established improvement. Compare states and contacts for every common sample through step 199.

Before considering promotion require, for each seed and lane: at least two support-qualified foot-clearing bouts per foot as defined by the earlier audit; forward displacement at least 90 percent of its matched baseline; no nonfoot normal impulse above the earlier threshold; no new toe-speed-limit or native solver failures; all expected samples present. Also require the ankle minimum to follow the knee minimum by at least one sampled 20 ms endpoint within at least 24 of the 32 side-lane comparisons, with the comparison restricted to supported clearance bouts that occur fully within the window. Report matched sample counts and any fewer or ambiguous bouts rather than imputing phase. These are exploratory engineering gates, not biological reference statistics. Reject if any gate fails. A pass would be a brief development-screen result only: an independent sustained walk, real-crow comparison, takeoff, flight, autonomous route and supported landing would all remain mandatory. Archive failure and full input/trace provenance.

For the 24-of-32 phase gate, a side-lane passes only if a strict majority of its complete interior clearance bouts have the sampled ankle minimum at least one endpoint later than the sampled knee minimum; tied minima do not pass. An empty or boundary-truncated set fails. Toe-cap comparisons count substeps whose absolute observed toe speed reaches 11.99 rad/s, paired per lane in steps 201–300; the candidate must not increase that count. The nonfoot threshold applies to summed normal impulse per control interval in steps 201–300. Positive net travel and matching prefix are required for every lane. No controller is accepted solely for meeting the phase metric.

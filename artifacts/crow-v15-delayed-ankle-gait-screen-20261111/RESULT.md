# Delayed ankle: phase separated, walking gate failed

This matched development screen compares 16 lanes across two already-exposed seeds, using the same v15 physical body and scene. Only the ankle carrier was delayed by 0.25 rad during the two-second walk. States and aggregate contact samples were identical to the baseline through control step 199 in every lane. All expected walk samples were present, neither seed reported native errors or contact overflow, and the lane-5 replay and native forward kinematics agreed within 1.84e-8 m for the inspected body positions.

The sampled ankle minimum followed the sampled knee minimum in the required majority of interior clearance bouts for 31 of 32 side-lane comparisons, satisfying the experimental phase gate. This proves neither substep ordering nor biological correctness. Six of 16 lanes failed the predeclared walking gates: in all six, one foot had only one support-qualified cleared-foot bout instead of at least two; seed 20261127 lane 0 also traveled only 0.0241 m versus 0.0315 m in its reference, below the 90 percent threshold. Every lane moved forward; no interval exceeded the nonfoot normal-impulse threshold or had a new toe-speed cap in the measured window. Per-lane and per-bout results are in comparison.json. These are sampled swing candidates, not proven complete strides or real-crow statistics.

Decision: reject this controller candidate and retain the earlier baseline. Separation of joint timing alone is insufficient when clearance and forward travel degrade. The native traces and compiled shader are retained for later analysis. This trial does not test sustained gait, takeoff, flight, landing or unused layouts.

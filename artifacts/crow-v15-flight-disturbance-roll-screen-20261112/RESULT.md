# Result: small-pulse flight recovery screen

All eight first episodes survived the 24-second development run; pre-pulse states and contact samples matched their no-disturbance counterparts exactly. Six signed external body-axis test pulses exceeded the predeclared 0.01-radian minimum: roll 0.01287–0.01288 rad, pitch 0.01456–0.01457 rad, yaw 0.01631–0.01636 rad. The mean 20–24-second attitude, altitude and horizontal-velocity differences were below the respective predeclared tracking thresholds, with no flight-period nonfoot contacts or observed body, wing or toe speed caps. Two lanes had early nonflight toe caps, retained in analysis. The roll pulse was 0.15 N m and the other pulses 0.10 N m, all lasting 0.10 s. These were prescribed external test inputs, not direct controller moments.

This demonstrates only small-pulse recovery in one obstacle-free used development seed with the existing uncalibrated aerodynamic force closure. It does not qualify real perturbed-start recovery, commanded left and right flight, speed response, navigation, landing or biological realism.

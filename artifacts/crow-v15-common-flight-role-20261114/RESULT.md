# Common role-6 straight-flight screen: six of eight lanes pass

Assigning all eight lanes the previously tested role-6 experimental settings does not pass the registered screen. Lanes 2–7 complete the 1,800-interval first episode and each meets the registered contact, actuator, height, forward, heading, tilt and lateral-drift development checks. Lane 6 exactly reproduces its corrected-reset reference in q/v, policy actions and contacts through all 1,800 intervals. Lanes 0 and 1 briefly unload at interval 375 but record ground contact again at interval 378 and physically terminate at metadata intervals 697 and 701; they do not achieve sustained flight. The finite audit found no invalid values in 14,408 logged states or 187,648 contact records. All lanes used the same source-level role selector, but other state and dynamics still differ; this result does not establish why the two launches fail.

The first recorded positive nonfoot contact after the launch window is tail–ground at observed step 698 in lane 0 and body–ground plus tiny tail–ground contacts at observed step 702 in lane 1. These are close to, but one observation after, the metadata episode ends. Metadata, state and contact epochs have not been independently synchronized, so these contacts are not identified as proven fatal causes. The retained failure inspection contains prior positions, attitudes, velocities, actions and joint targets. Do not treat contact itself as a solver defect.

This remains a fixed, lane-role-6 scripted controller on a used seed and training layout, not a general navigation command interface, disturbed-start qualification, frozen controller, leg-powered takeoff, landing, or an unused-layout mission. All six successful and two failed lanes and their traces are retained.

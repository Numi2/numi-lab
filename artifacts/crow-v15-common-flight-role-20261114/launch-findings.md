# Post-hoc launch inspection

Across intervals 371–377, the sum of the recorded model's vertical aerodynamic force times 0.02/16 s was +0.00495 and +0.00485 N s in failing lanes 0 and 1, versus +0.1801 to +0.1818 N s in lanes 2–7. At interval 375 the logged flap targets were 0.8996 rad in all lanes; flap positions were about 0.518 rad in lanes 0–1 and 0.895 rad in lanes 2–7. These correlations do not isolate a cause, and state/contact epoch alignment remains unverified.

The World.air actually used by this run embeds the source path of `crow-v15-intermediate-winghold-20261113/World.metal`. Recompiling the preserved source on the native host reproduced the exact SHA-256 of that World.air, `4decccd929a223714fca473adb54c58e4ec885cf1473e72fb7184c59f8a6e443`. That source applies an experimental target-velocity PD wing drive with a 3 N m cap only when `environment%8u>=2u`; lanes 0–1 follow the ordinary drive. The observed effort samples are consistent with this difference, but do not by themselves establish causal responsibility for failure. A separate prospective comparison applies the same experimental law across all lanes. This source-level configuration mismatch means the common-role task shader was not a common actuator screen.

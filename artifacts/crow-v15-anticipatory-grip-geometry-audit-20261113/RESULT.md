# Retrospective foretoe geometry after anticipatory closure

Using the same recorded-pose separating-axis box/perch calculation as the previous audit, the minimum foretoe gap at zero-based interval 230 was 8.17 mm in the baseline, 5.63 mm with medium anticipatory closure, and 4.80 mm with fast closure. At 250 it remained 3.18, 3.04 and 3.12 mm respectively. The accepted last-substep samples had a positive front normal in 13, 12 and 2 post-return intervals respectively, before the respective falls. No interval satisfied the combined opposing-grip and posture criteria in the two new trials.

This is a retrospective geometric and endpoint-contact diagnostic, not proof of why the controls failed; overlap and modeled geometry do not establish a sustained force or a complete contact history. The raw motion and contact observations are in the referenced trials.

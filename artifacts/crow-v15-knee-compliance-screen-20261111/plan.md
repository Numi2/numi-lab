# Exploratory knee compliance screen

Question: do the stiff v15 knee position drives materially constrain walking in the existing contact-driven gait? Competing explanations include the small requested knee excursion and support/phase control. Baseline drives are 65 N m/rad and 0.50 N m s/rad, with a 0.75 N m actuator effort limit; these are prospective uncalibrated model parameters, not crow measurements. The rendered torso is a single rigid link, and contact friction and geometry are separate hypotheses.

Change only the two v15 knee drives in an isolated host: multiply stiffness by 0.4 and damping by sqrt(0.4), retaining actuator limits, body/torso topology, contact/material, policies, gait and flight controller. Compare against the archived original-stride v15 walks, using the same used development seeds 20261126 and 20261127 and eight lanes for 300 control steps, 16 physics substeps, no scheduled resets. Each lane is a paired exploratory start, not an independent biological replicate. Do not use unused final layouts or seeds. Changing mechanics may change policy fingerprints; if needed, rebind only identical weights and transforms and report this does not establish policy validity.

Before seeing results, predict at least 15% larger physical knee angle span during the 4–6 s walk window on at least 6 of 16 paired lanes, with no increase in total unsupported physics substeps, no additional nonfoot contact, no added fall or solver failure, and positive longitudinal displacement in at least as many lanes as control. Reject as a gait improvement if any gate fails; report each metric and retain both arms even on failure. Same q/v before intervention is not expected: the softer drives act from initialization, so later effects are confounded by earlier dynamics and this is not a direct causal isolation of gait phase. Capture q/v, all 16 support substeps, nonfoot contact, knee angle and displacement. Stop after these two used-seed trials. This screen does not establish grip, takeoff, flight, landing, whole-body deformability, material calibration or biological realism; no default model is replaced.

# Coordinated wing-roll screen: rejected

All candidate/control pairs matched endpoint state and ground-contact histories through trace submission 602. The six controlled lanes first diverged at 603; untouched lanes 0–1 remained equal through 1051. All candidate episodes reached the 1050-transition horizon without reported contact termination or recorded post-600 joint-speed caps or nonfoot ground impulses. These were obstacle-free development trials on one seed.

The bounded flap correction produced signed roll and substantial additional lateral movement but did not materially improve the sampled heading response. At submission 850, candidate heading differed from its matched baseline by approximately +0.001 to +0.013 rad, while lateral displacement differences were roughly +0.22–0.50 m for positive targets and −0.22 to −0.48 m for negative targets. After target removal, the added displacement and some asymmetric residual roll persisted. This is an unsuitable turn-control improvement: it introduces extra sideways drift while leaving heading largely governed by the existing pronation loop. Do not promote it.

The current wing model and feedback remain empirical and lane-dependent. The test does not establish real-bird banking, robust recovery, supported landing, obstacle clearance or flight-controller qualification.

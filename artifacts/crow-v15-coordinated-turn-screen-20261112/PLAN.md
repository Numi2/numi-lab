# Coordinated wing roll and heading: matched screen

The existing low-level heading feedback responds but turns slowly with little observed banking. The paired flap-pulse study showed that antisymmetric flap inputs create signed roll and lateral velocity. Against the exact same-lane, same-seed heading-target trial, add one bounded differential flap term proportional to current heading error, limited to +/-0.06 normalized action, through the existing articulated flap drives; retain the original pronation feedback gain, output bounds, pulse targets, controller, body and empirical aerodynamic settings. Lanes 0–1 remain unmodified. Evaluate prefix identity, actual heading/target errors, roll direction and magnitude, height, velocity, joint limits, contact and recovery.

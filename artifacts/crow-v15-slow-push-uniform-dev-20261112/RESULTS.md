# Uniform slower push: two used development seeds

The 160 ms contact-initiated leg target transition and 100 ms early wing onset were applied in every lane, with direct body-moment actions 13 and 14 suppressed from the beginning. Sixteen teacher-off first episodes on previously used development seeds 20261126 and 20261127 ran 16 s with 16 physics substeps and no scheduled resets. All 16 reached only the first waypoint flag, remained foot-unloaded from 8–16 s, and had no reported physical termination by the horizon. They are not complete routes or supported landings. No minimum obstacle-clearance measurement was made.

Every non-root generalized velocity and foot-contact summary was logged for all 204,800 first-episode substeps. No joint velocity-limit hits were observed from 4–16 s; no both-feet-unloaded substeps or non-foot ground impulse occurred in the 4–6 s walking window. Five of 16 starts had toe or neck speed-limit hits during 0–4 s; several had long both-feet-unloaded sequences during initial settling. These are real failures of the current initialization and preclude a stable whole-sequence qualification. The specific locations and quantities are retained in the two analysis JSON files. On seed 20261127, lanes 4–5 match all 1,000 endpoint state/action/contact samples and 16,000 contact substeps from the earlier mixed screen through 10 s.

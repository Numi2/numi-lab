# Retained toe-off mechanics comparison

In the held gait, 14/40 complete stances with both groups ended rear-first; in the later driven gait, 0/40 did. Held rear-minus-front release time ranged -36.25 to +40 ms, median +18.75 ms. Driven ranged +5 to +13.75 ms, median +7.5 ms. The later gait had 12 additional complete foot-contact runs lacking a front-toe signal; these do not establish contact order. Each arm also had 32 partial runs.

Pairing ordinal stances by lane and side produced 40 numerical pairs, with median change in front-contact duration -7.5 ms and rear-contact duration -9.375 ms. At each gait's last front contact, the median driven-minus-held rear-proximal joint angle was -0.0495 rad and the ankle angle difference was +0.00161 rad. This is a post-hoc observational comparison of different gait controllers and targets. Missing-group stances may change ordinal correspondence, and the matched events are sampled at different times; the differences do not isolate a cause, actuator tracking error or biological kinematics. Complete event records, thresholds, and skipped categories are in comparison.json.

This audit makes no model change. The magpie's rear-first ordering is a qualitative related-corvid reference; no individual American-crow kinematics were measured here. Neither gait is biologically qualified.

# Contact-gated knee correction for rear-toe clearance

The early hip-lift feedback leaves the released foot and foretoes clear but its rear toe remains on the perch. Native FK at the retained candidate state showed that a small positive knee change locally increases rear-toe gap while decreasing the still-large foot/front gaps. Both lanes reproduce the prior early hip-lift feedback and keep the foot open. Lane 1 additionally applies a 0–0.08 rad correction to the existing left knee position target, with at most 0.004 rad per 20 ms change: while lift feedback is active and open-toe, stance-support and posture gates hold, it increases below 3 mm measured clearance and decreases above 5 mm. Physical joint/actuator limits remain unchanged and no pose or body force is overridden. Local static geometry does not guarantee dynamic success.

# Uniform-drive paired turn development screen

All eight lanes passed the predeclared turn screen on used seed 20261126 and the training layout, teacher off and without scheduled resets. All lanes exactly matched their corresponding straight-reference q/v, policy actions and contact records through interval 600, before the turn. During the turn window, the four even lanes reached -0.25788 to -0.25819 rad relative yaw and the four odd lanes reached +0.25783 to +0.25795 rad. Every lane completed 1,800 intervals without a recorded physical termination or later ground contact, satisfying the registered state, contact, height, posture, actuator and tracking gates. Maximum measured tilt was 0.2081 rad. Individual results and checks are in `analysis.json`.

This is a fixed scripted differential-pronation experiment, not a runtime-commanded or frozen controller. The 3 N m experimental wing-drive cap is not biologically calibrated. The screen does not establish disturbed recovery, continuous climb/descent qualification, leg-powered takeoff, toe grip, supported landing or unused-layout mission success. Source, build identities and all observations are retained; the raw native trace is split into hash-checked Git parts.

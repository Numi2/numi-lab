# Supported alternating toe release: substep observer

The observer replays the alternating-foot candidate on the original J and I development starts. Both matched all 7,000 reference frames exactly for step, q, v, actor observation, and accepted actions. All 444 expected substep status samples were present in each trace. Within the prespecified lift intervals, the longest runs of substeps with lifted-side toe impulse at most 1e-8 N s and opposite-side upward foot/toe impulse over 0.0000125 N s were J left 172, right 192; I left 180, right 184. Each exceeds the 12-substep gate. The separate accepted-endpoint audit checks lifted toe geometry and support; these observations do not establish continuous substep geometric separation or biological realism.

This is evidence for force-free toe release while the other foot supports the body on these two used starts. It does not qualify continuous gripping, navigation clearance, fresh layouts, or the full autonomous behavior. In the separate pre-release audit, simultaneous opposing contact remained intermittent. The development I route also passed too close to an obstacle. The frozen controller and untouched final layouts therefore remain unqualified.

# Substep supported foot-release verification

Extend the ranged toe observer with a read-only trace of each rigid foot/perch contact normal and impulse, so toe force-free intervals can be checked simultaneously with upward support impulses on the opposite foot at each physics substep. Preserve the original toe trace and the actual body, solver, task and controller. Require exact replay parity with the relevant full-start alternating-foot candidate before interpreting any trace. For each predeclared lift interval, require at least 12 consecutive observed substeps with no lifted-side toe/perch impulse above 1e-8 N s and with positive upward opposite-foot/toe/perch normal impulse above 0.0000125 N s. Record all status samples, signal missing data, and retain accepted-endpoint geometric separation evidence separately. One matched development case does not qualify held-out performance or biological behavior.

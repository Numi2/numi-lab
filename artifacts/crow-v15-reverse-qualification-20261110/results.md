# Results

All 40 obstacle-free perturbed lanes passed the existing coarse survival and flight screen. All 240 sampled settled speeds were negative, ranging from -0.173 to -0.106 m/s with median -0.142 m/s for the encoded -0.25 m/s target. This demonstrates directional reverse in the tested conditions; there is appreciable tracking bias and no claim of obstacle clearance, accurate hover, landing, or biological realism.

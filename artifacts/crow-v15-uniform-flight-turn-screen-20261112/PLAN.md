# Uniform experimental flight-controller turn screen

Test one common low-level configuration on the current experimental v15 body in the collision-disabled development course. This is a new candidate, not the previously labeled frozen controller. The prior turn screen used two different unsteady-force blends, three height feedback gains and two fixed-speed targets across lanes. Set the live-normal blend to 0.60 for every lane, height-feedback gain to 1.20 and speed target to 0.55 m/s. Command the same 1.05 m height in all lanes. Apply the same zero-heading feedback to every lane. From 12 to 18 s only, the commanded heading is zero in lanes 0/1, +/-0.15 in 2/3, +/-0.30 in 4/5, and +/-0.45 rad in 6/7, returning to zero afterwards. Do not modify unsteady force magnitude, synthetic yaw closure, launch mechanics or task-force ablation.

Use the same used seed and native physics, record accepted states and replay, and measure heading, height, speed and recovery in the full 24 s. The two zero-command lanes are controls. This is an exploratory ability screen; require unperturbed and perturbed straight, left, right, climb, descent and recovery; repeat seeds; full substep speed limits; physically calibrated aerodynamic forces and held-out tests before freezing a controller. Existing aerodynamic closures are not fitted to the jackdaw glide measurements and may be unphysical at this speed.

# Common-parameter turn screen: not qualified

On one used development seed, all eight related starts survived the 24-second obstacle-disabled test. The primary turning settings, speed correction, height gain and wing-normal blend were shared as specified in PLAN.md. Two lanes held zero heading; the remaining lanes received symmetric target pulses, then zero target. During 16–17 s, mean headings for +/−0.15 rad commands were +0.085/−0.138 rad; for +/−0.30 they were +0.186/−0.233 rad; for +/−0.45 they were +0.230/−0.282 rad. Controls averaged approximately −0.027 rad. During 22–23 s, after the turn pulse returned to zero, the +/−0.45 lanes averaged +0.048/−0.120 rad, while the controls averaged approximately −0.036 rad. Height and heading-normalized forward speed means are in `analysis.json`. The stronger turns undertrack and recovery is asymmetric. No minimum-clearance claim is possible without obstacle collisions.

The inherited contact diagnostic writes a lane-dependent packed velocity to `airReturnTracking.x` during 4–6 s even though that field is also used for mechanical power. Its diagnostic gains are zero, but the write leaves a task-state integrity concern; a separately matched intervention is prepared. There may be other lane-specific or empirical components, so sharing selected constants does not prove a uniform model contract. Direct task-body moment actions remain disabled, but uncalibrated aerodynamic force-direction and stroke-dependent yaw-moment closures remain. These motions do not establish biological realism. Each lane is one correlated development start; heading is absolute rather than a same-lane zero-pulse subtraction. Joint speed-limit substeps, perturbations, climb/descent tracking, independent starts, both layouts, landing and biological data fitting remain unqualified. Do not freeze this controller.

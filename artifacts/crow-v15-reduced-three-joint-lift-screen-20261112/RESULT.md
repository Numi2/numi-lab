# Reduced three-joint lift: one short development gate failed

The reduced 0.15-rad peak knee lift completed eight matched 300-step lanes with exact prewalk parity, no episode reset, reported physical failure, nonfoot contact, toe velocity cap, physics error or overflow. Every lane had three left and four right support-qualified clearance bouts. There were no both-foot-zero-normal substeps, versus 4–6 per lane in the matched 0.20-rad lift and zero in the no-extra-stroke held reference. Forward travel was 1.06–1.28 times the held reference. Loaded endpoint contact-point speed median was 0.00402 versus 0.00276 m/s and p95 was 0.04359 versus 0.03484 m/s, both within the prespecified 1.5x limit.

The sole-clear/toe-low gate failed: 244 endpoint cases versus 49 for the held reference and 201 for the matched 0.20-rad lift. Of the 244 candidate cases, 159 had some same-interval foot normal impulse and 85 had none, compared with 14 and 35 in the held reference. Some loaded cases may be ordinary toe support, and these counts do not measure slip; the endpoint and interval impulse are not perfectly synchronized. The predeclared criterion remains failed. Candidate is not promoted.

At high positive carrier, the endpoint median knee difference versus the matched 0.20-rad lift was -0.029/-0.032 rad (left/right), with corresponding hip +0.013/+0.014 and ankle +0.016/+0.017 rad differences. These are diverged trajectories rather than isolated tracking errors. The trial uses an already-used development seed and only two seconds of walking; it cannot establish biological gait or full-sequence competence.

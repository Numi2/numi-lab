# Stance hip-roll fixed-state sensitivity

Native fixed-root v15 forward kinematics at retained combined-candidate states show that a small increase in the stance hip-roll coordinate locally decreases center-of-mass lateral offset relative to the stance foot by about 0.122–0.124 m/rad at ticks 250–315. The nonstance hip's derivative is approximately +0.0037 m/rad. The reconstructed unperturbed relative x/y agrees with the next logged balance observation within 0.9 nm. At tick 290 the lateral offset was approximately 22.7 mm and the stance hip-roll target had reached its existing bound.

These are hypothetical fixed-root perturbations, not contact-constrained dynamics or an actuator-effort measurement. A prior increase of the roll limit worsened the release result, so the sensitivity does not establish that a larger roll command will stabilize the bird. Full state inputs, native binary, hashes and analyses are retained.

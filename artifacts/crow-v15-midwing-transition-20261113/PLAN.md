# 80 ms delayed wing onset paired transition screen

The 200 ms wing delay exposed a strong early paired ground impulse but four lanes recontacted before sustained climb; three were descending at the first recontact sample. The prior near-immediate flapping trial showed two lanes with no measured leg impulse. Test the single intermediate timing 80 ms after the 6.96 s push command, with the same lane-specific flap ramps, to look for an overlap that retains leg impulse and avoids recontact. This timing is an engineering hypothesis, not a biological calibration.

Use push and matched active-crouch arms, same v15 model, solver, policies, gravity and extended-logging-only host as the 200 ms trial. Use eight correlated lanes on development seed 20261127, 400 control steps, 16 substeps and no resets. Body moments are suppressed from 6.30 s only; passive aero remains. Lanes 2–7 are the predeclared development comparisons, not independent samples.

Require complete endpoint and 335–400 substep/target records, no errors or overflow, and exact sampled q/v/action/contact parity between matched arms and the earlier candidate through 345. Report early 348–357, later 348–365 and 348–375 impulse contrasts, COM momentum, actual flap target, first three unloaded intervals, recontacts through 400, maximum root rise, nonfoot impulses and toe threshold events. Screen only if all six comparable lanes have >=0.05 N s extra early upward foot impulse, three consecutive unloaded intervals by 375 with no later recontact to 400, >=0.05 m root rise and no nonfoot ground impulse. Keep both runs and negative results. A pass does not establish physical, biological or held-out mission qualification.

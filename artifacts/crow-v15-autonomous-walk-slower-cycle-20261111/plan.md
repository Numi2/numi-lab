# Slower gait cycle

One of eight starts on the original 0.50-second gait had a sharp upward excursion and foot-contact impulses up to 0.0821 N s after an unsupported interval. Hold the original physical action amplitudes and contact gate fixed while lengthening the cycle to 0.75 seconds. Assess alternate clear swings, support at all substeps, impact and forward progress in the same eight-second development test. Slower commands do not establish contact safety or biological realism.

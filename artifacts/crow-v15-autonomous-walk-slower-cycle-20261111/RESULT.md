# Slower walking cycle: rejected

The 0.75-second cycle maintained alternating clear-swing bouts in all eight development starts, but all had measured unsupported substeps: seven lanes had 30–39 and lane 6 had 315. Each of the seven had two sampled endpoints with both feet more than 1 mm clear; lane 6 had 15 and progressed only 0.063 m, compared with 0.102–0.112 m in the other lanes. No nonfoot ground impulses were recorded. Pre-gait q/v and substep-contact comparisons are retained. This does not qualify stable contact-driven walking, takeoff, landing, or biological realism.

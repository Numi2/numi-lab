# All-lane repaired-toe swing geometry

FP64 reconstructed foot/toe positions agreed with the retained native replay to within 4.9e-9 m and orientations within 3.0e-8 in quaternion norm. On every one of the eight development starts, the 16-substep two-second gait had four left and four right alternating bouts of at least three consecutive accepted poses with all foot/toe boxes >1 mm above the ground, that foot's normal impulse <=1e-7 N s over every substep, opposite-foot integrated upward impulse >=half body weight, and no other-body ground contact. Each lane moved approximately 2.58–2.61 cm forward.

This post-hoc reconstruction is constrained by the retained model and sampled states; it does not establish continuous geometry between poses, contact-point slip, longer walks, resolution convergence, reliable takeoff, landing or the autonomous course. No unused layout was accessed. Source hashes and exact kinematics executable are retained.

# Rear-toe release with coordinated lift

The reduced coordinated lift retained forward travel and full-foot clearance bouts without sampled zero-normal support, but exceeded the predeclared sole-clear/toe-low endpoint limit. The limiting geometry is the rear toe. Test the previously screened small, phase-led rear proximal toe extension, now in combination with this different leg trajectory: the extra position target is -0.02 rad at peak, only when the opposite foot is supported and the acting foot is not accepted as supported. Both arms use the same reduced 0.15-rad lift, 0.25 stance stroke and held upper body. This is an engineering hypothesis, not a measured crow toe trajectory. The same toe control failed a longer screen with the old leg waveform; this test does not erase that result.

Before inspection, run same-binary eight-lane 300-step 16-substep no-reset paired development trials on seed 20261127. Require exact prewalk parity through step 200, full first episodes, no errors, overflow, nonfoot contact or toe speed caps; at least three supported-clearance bouts per foot in every lane; no increase in both-foot-zero-normal substeps against the no-extra-stroke held control per lane; at least 90% of that control forward travel per lane; sole-clear/toe-low endpoint counts at most 120% and loaded contact-point median and p95 speeds at most 1.5x that control.

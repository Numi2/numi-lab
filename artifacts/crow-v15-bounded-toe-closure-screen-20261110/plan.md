# Bounded rear-toe closure screen

The 16-substep pitch-coordinated controller achieved grip and terminal support in one development start but its rear-first closure targets 0.85 rad and produced 0.949 mm worst rigid toe/perch SAT overlap. Compare its exact three prior controls with otherwise identical pitch-coordinated candidates whose rear-toe target is capped at 0.65 or 0.50 rad from closure through the hold. Keep 16-substep physics, original body, real interval contact signal, front-toe control, hip/ankle correction, release and start unchanged. All three controls must reproduce their 200 saved states exactly. Report grip with support, release, terminal support/open toes, failures and independent SAT gap. Require <=0.5 mm worst grip overlap and the complete support/release screen before a candidate may advance to perturbation and longer-duration tests. These settings are uncalibrated; one drop cannot qualify flight, navigation, unused layouts or real-crow biomechanics.

# Posthoc stronger-bank yaw-moment diagnostic

For the failed stronger-bank run, 8,016 substeps per lane over ticks 650–1150 yield mean modeled aerodynamic yaw moments of -0.0092055 and +0.0086578 N m about the instantaneous whole-body center of mass. Summed aerodynamic forces match the observer's total to better than 6e-11 N. The same retained FK/damping estimator gives integrated damping impulses of +0.09013 and -0.08480 N m s. With the same 0.00125 s sample interval, the aerodynamic impulses are approximately -0.0922 and +0.0867 N m s, so the estimated damping opposes roughly 98% of the observed aerodynamic yaw impulse in these two correlated development lanes.

The damping reconstruction assumes unit scales and the CPU damping convention; it is not an independent measurement of every GPU damping parameter and does not close all external wrenches, momentum, contact or integration. A previous separate instrumented experiment confirmed that reducing torso damping changes heading but still fails the turn gate; that change is not biologically calibrated and was not promoted. This audit diagnoses an area for further measurement, not a solver defect or a qualified flight model. Source trace hash, FK binary hash and results are in the JSON files.

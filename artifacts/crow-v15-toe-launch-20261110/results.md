# Toe-equipped launch exploratory screen

The two arms matched q/v before extension in all eight lanes. With flapping suppressed, the push arm met the two-sample unloaded and upward endpoint criterion in 1/8 lanes; the nominal no-push control did so in 8/8. Both arms were airborne at the end of 8 s after flapping resumed, and no native first-episode physical failures were recorded. Inspection showed that the control stopped holding the crouch at 5.2 s, so its return to the default knee position drove an extension before the wing suppression ended. This makes the control unsuitable for isolating the requested push. The result does not establish reliable leg-powered takeoff. All conditions used a development layout and estimated toes, and passive aerodynamics were not disabled.

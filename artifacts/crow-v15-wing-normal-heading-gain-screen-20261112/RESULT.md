# Stronger heading feedback: development screen

The experimental wing-normal force blend and direct-body-moment-disabled feedback remained airborne for 24 seconds in all eight obstacle-free development lanes. At the strongest 0.60 blend, mean absolute heading error during 20–24 s was 0.064 rad for the +0.35 rad target and 0.016 rad for the -0.35 rad target, versus 0.154 and 0.015 rad in the weaker-gain trial. At blend 0.35 errors were 0.165 and 0.014 rad. The 6000 pre-command/control states and 96000 contact substeps matched the prior closure trial exactly.

This is one previously used seed and fixed target per lane, with an uncalibrated force closure and remaining directional asymmetry. The observer in this run recorded toe speeds, but not wing-joint speeds. Neither flight qualification, biological validity, obstacle clearance nor a navigation or landing result follows from it.

The matched baseline remains loaded until substep 6 of interval 352, accumulating 0.787–0.791 N s upward ground impulse, exceeding body-weight impulse by 0.395–0.399 N s. This calculation uses the 0.45006 kg model mass and is not isolated leg-muscle impulse; passive and wing aerodynamics remain. It shows the early-wing candidate unloads before the baseline push-off develops, so this timing is rejected for the leg-powered objective despite the pre-release wing motion.

# Navigation assessment layouts

Layouts reserved-final-c and reserved-final-d were unused when first assessed. The preregistered 2026-11-09 assessment is preserved in crow-unused-layout-confirmation-20261109: 15/16 and 13/16 supported landings respectively, below the registered minimum for the second layout. They are now development layouts because those outcomes informed further policy work; their historical names and original records remain unchanged.

Layouts reserved-final-e and reserved-final-f are newly defined, distinct nominal geometries. They retain the existing collider types, deterministic episode perturbations and route mechanism. They must not be executed or used for training, teacher collection, policy fitting, or model selection until the flight and navigation controllers are frozen, a measurement contract is registered, and unused seeds are specified. If their outcomes motivate revisions they too become development layouts, and another untouched assessment is required.

# Invalid body-heading comparison: early intervention

This exploratory trial is invalid for its intended matched comparison. A missing time condition caused the candidate heading feedback to replace actor commands before 12 s, including during the launch prefix. The reference comparison recorded 8,405 state/action/contact differences; the first displayed policy-action differences appeared at step 3 in lane 2. The two controls were unchanged, but the experimental lanes cannot be interpreted as matched interventions.

All eight first episodes ran to 24 s in the obstacle-disabled diagnostic and the detailed raw outputs are retained; the results must not be used to claim a controlled improvement or flight qualification. A separate corrected trial preserves the reference commands until after 12 s and checks that prefix explicitly.

# Body and path heading feedback with ordinary wing joints

The small differential-flap controller moved velocity in both directions but the body yaw continued to drift and did not align to the commanded path. Test simultaneous heading feedback by driving the existing mirrored wing-sweep action from the negative wrapped body-heading error, with gains .5, 1 and 2 and two exact reference controls. Retain the .6 velocity-heading feedback and +/- .10 flap bounds, requested +/- .35 rad targets, used seed, no obstacle collisions or direct body moments. Compare control and preintervention states, final body alignment and path tracking, roll, speed, altitude, caps and contacts. Steering signs and aerodynamics remain hypotheses; no closed-loop obstacle turn, recovery or biological qualification is implied.

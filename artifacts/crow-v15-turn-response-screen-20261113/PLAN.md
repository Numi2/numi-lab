# Signed course-command turn-response identification

Only run this test after the previously selected position-gain 0.20 has passed both lanes on development seed 20261127; otherwise retain this plan as unrun. Hold the v15 research body, 36-second seed-20261126 launch, policies, obstacle-free masks, 16 substeps, observer, and existing position-hold law and limits fixed. Lanes 2–5 remain their exact same-lane position-hold references. In lane 6 command a +0.5 m world-y course target, and in lane 7 a -0.5 m target, smoothly enabled 12–12.5 seconds and disabled 23.5–24 seconds. Apply this target only through the existing position error and bounded actuator targets; no body forces or state edits. These are exploratory course-position commands, not evidence of perceptual navigation.

Require exact accepted q/v/policy-action/contact match to the same-lane seed-20261126 position-hold trial through tick 600 for lanes 6 and 7, through tick 1800 for lanes 2–5, and full first-episode equality for controls 0–1. Require identical host, policy bytes and scene masks, complete observer coverage, valid metadata and independent reconstruction of both targets from preceding accepted q/v and the declared command. Require the existing physical flight gates (no post-release ground contact, toe threshold or effort-bound violations, sampled height 0.25–1.5 m, sampled x velocity (0,1] and absolute y velocity <=0.4 m/s, x gain >1 m). Since deliberate turning displaces y, report but do not apply the straight-flight 1 m cross-track bound. Record all first failures and contacts.

For a signed response require each lane during ticks 650–1150 to attain a same-sign body-yaw difference of at least 0.10 rad and same-sign world-y displacement of at least 0.20 m compared with its own reference; these are small engineering identification thresholds, not biological calibration. Report maximum yaw, roll, lateral velocity, displacement and return-to-baseline after the command. A displacement without the corresponding yaw response is sideslip, not an established turn. Failure by either sign is retained and used for diagnosis. A pass is not qualified or frozen flight: it does not establish turn tracking, airspeed alignment, vertical control, disturbed-start recovery, leg-powered flight, autonomous route completion, final unused layouts, or supported landing.

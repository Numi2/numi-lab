# Walk recovery integrated into teacher-off development journey

On the used development layout and seed, seven of eight first episodes reached all five waypoint criteria; lane 6 reached three, and lane 4 suffered a physical fall after the fifth. Lane 5 had bilateral opposing toe support from steps 3609–3800, with no reported nonfoot perch impulse. Its accepted state/contact record matched the previous latch run throughout the first episode; its phase, toe-velocity, foot-impulse and individual scene-contact records matched exactly for steps 3500–3800. The corrected narrow-perch audit of that reference measured left-foot/toe endpoint clearance of at least 1 mm and zero left normal impulse in all sixteen substeps of each interval 3550–3559, followed by regrip. Swept clearance was not established.

No self-contact impulses above the logged threshold were recorded from step 380 onward and no toe speed caps after step 500; initial settling still hit caps in two starts. The other seven starts did not meet final bilateral opposed support. The walking recovery changed only lanes 3 and 6 relative to the matched latch run; six lanes matched its accepted endpoints through the shorter first episode. One development success is not reliable autonomous landing, an unused-layout result, or biological realism. The experiment does not satisfy the requested full sequence and no qualifying final video has been produced.

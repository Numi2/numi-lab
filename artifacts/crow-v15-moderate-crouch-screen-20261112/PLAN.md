# Moderate crouch versus foot contact

The 0.55-rad parameter improves selected modeled knee flexion and supports a 12-second development trajectory, but it fails the per-foot 1.5x sampled-contact-speed comparison in some lanes and seeds. Compare 0.40, 0.45, 0.50 and 0.55 rad in two same-seed lanes each against their exact baseline. Quantify joint minima, retained and excluded contact windows, per-foot speed, toe caps and unloaded substeps. This exploratory parameter sweep does not equate related-magpie angles with crow truth, does not test held-out starts or establish realistic gait.

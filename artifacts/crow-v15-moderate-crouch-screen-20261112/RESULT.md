# Moderate whole-leg crouch screen

On two previously used seeds, four lanes with a 0.45-rad crouch setting had selected knee minima of about 111.0–111.2 degrees versus 113.8–113.9 degrees in the 0.40 control lanes. No 0.45 lane had both feet unloaded, a toe-speed-cap substep, or nonfoot ground impulse in the 4–6 s walking window. Each affected foot's sampled p95 contact-point-speed ratio to its own matched 0.40 baseline lay between 0.73 and 1.37, within the 1.5 engineering screen. Point speed is not integrated slip. The 0.40 controls exactly matched their respective reference endpoints and substeps.

The 0.50 lanes recorded two or three toe-speed-cap substeps each, while one 0.55 lane exceeded the p95 comparison threshold by a factor of 1.83. All first episodes completed six seconds without reported physical termination or trace error. The 0.45 result is a small, selected development finding, not biological calibration or a substitute for testing the whole sequence; the directly measured American-crow gait is not available here and the related-magpie species, surface, speed and anatomical mapping differ. Independent starts, continuous takeoff, flight, grip and landing remain unqualified. A uniform eight-lane integration test follows separately.

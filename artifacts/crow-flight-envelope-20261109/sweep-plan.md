Test whether equal-sign bounded steering biases on the existing left and right wing-sweep action lanes add useful yaw authority. Compare +0.30/+0.30 and -0.30/-0.30 normalized offsets after 6 s against the roll-1.5 arm. Clamp each resulting action to [-1,1] before the existing actuator response filter. Preserve the roll loop, fixed +0.55/-0.55 pronation speed trim, body yaw limits, v15 mechanics and aerodynamic parameters, 32 matched native starts, seed 20261111, rate modes and 1600-step horizon.

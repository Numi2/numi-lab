# Exploratory articulated-wing authority probe

Compare matched lanes from the preceding gain-16 arm (zero added wing-pronation bias) with two new runs applying +0.30/-0.30 and -0.30/+0.30 normalized offsets to left/right pronation teacher commands respectively after 6 s, clamped to [-1,1] before the same actuator filter. Hold every other shader, task, controller parameter, initial seed, rate assignment and sample budget fixed. Existing wing actuators, v15 model and aerodynamic solver retain physical authority. Each arm is 32 lanes, 1600 steps, development seed 20261111; 2-second accepted-state samples and first-episode physical terminations. Paired lane index is the unit.

Prediction: at least one sign reduces median maximum absolute heading error in the -0.03 rad/s lanes by >=0.15 rad compared with zero bias, without introducing physical terminations in those lanes. Report +0.03 and +/-0.06 lanes, speed, height, termination and both signs as well. Missing samples or simulator failures invalidate the affected comparison. The comparison explores steering authority; a passing result does not qualify, freeze or deploy a controller. The study stops after both arms and reserves new seeds and layouts.

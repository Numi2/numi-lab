# Low-speed yaw authority

At t=30 s, median forward speeds were 0.794, 0.755 and 0.725 m/s for +0.55, +0.75 and +0.95 left/right opposing pronation trims. Median mean yaw rates over 20–30 s were -0.00954, -0.0094 and -0.0161 rad/s respectively. No first-episode physical terminations or failed simulator steps occurred. Neither larger offset reduced median speed by 0.10 m/s or achieved -0.03 rad/s; the preregistered prediction was contradicted. The generic per-arm heading screen assumed the prior five-mode commands and is not valid for these straight-reference yaw probes.

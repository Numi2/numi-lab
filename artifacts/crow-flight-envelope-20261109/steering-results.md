# Equal-sign pronation steering probe

The exploratory prediction was contradicted: median maximum heading error for -0.03 rad/s lanes was 0.654 rad for the fixed-speed control, 0.575 rad with +0.30/+0.30 added bias and 0.653 rad with -0.30/-0.30, below the required 0.15 rad improvement. No physical terminations or failed simulator steps occurred. No right-turn lane passed the strict screen. Positive equal-sign bias slightly reduced right-turn error but worsened speed tracking enough that only three lanes passed overall. This does not establish effective steering.

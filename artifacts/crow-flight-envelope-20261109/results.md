# Exploratory turn-envelope comparison

At development seed 20261111, neither yaw-gain arm met the preregistered all-sample screen in any of 32 lanes. Both had 0/32 physical terminations and zero failed environment steps. The median of the maximum absolute heading error over 12–32 s for +0.03/-0.03/+0.06/-0.06 rad/s commands was 0.196/0.747/0.723/1.579 rad for gain 3 and 0.041/0.606/0.585/1.501 rad for gain 16. Corresponding errors on straight lanes were 0.197 and 0.040 rad. The median lane maximum absolute forward-speed error was approximately 0.44–0.48 m/s across conditions for the 0.70 m/s target; median maximum absolute height error was 0.155–0.158 m. The stronger yaw feedback improves some heading tracking but still saturates on right turns and cannot support the candidate envelope. These are development results with two-second sampling, not independent validation. See arm JSON and compressed raw samples for all lanes and their failures.

Revision hypothesis: the separately commanded articulated pronation joints might change the yaw control balance without changing the bounded body-wrench actuator or altering aerodynamic coefficients. A matched paired bias probe is registered separately before its trials.

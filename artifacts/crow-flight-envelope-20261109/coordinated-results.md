# Coordinated flap and roll feedback

Increasing roll gain to 6 reduced the -0.20/+0.20 flap offset's median maximum -0.03-lane lateral speed from 0.418 to 0.104 m/s, but median maximum heading error worsened from 0.458 to 0.542 rad. The preregistered combined prediction was contradicted. The stronger -0.40/+0.40 offset gave 0.425 rad heading error but 0.609 m/s lateral speed and 0.614 m/s speed error. Both arms had no first-episode physical terminations and zero failed simulator steps; no right-turn lanes passed the original screen.

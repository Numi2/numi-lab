# Read-only yaw-moment observer

For all three yaw arms, observer enabled and disabled produced byte-identical selected-lane traces, identical first-episode outcomes in 32 lanes, and zero failed simulator steps. A Metal synthetic fixture recovered independently calculated moments 0.2, 3.3 and -0.2 N m. In every lane, all 100 sampled control transactions passed the preregistered 0.003 N m actuator-projection tolerance; maximum error over all arms was 5.58e-6 N m.

At negative saturated yaw, medians across 32 lane means over 20.02–22 s were approximately -0.019906 N m for the pre-aero root actuator wrench, -0.000199 N m for the summed wing moment about the observed root COM, and +0.000084 N m for root-aero-plus-tail moment. The prediction that one of the measured non-actuator terms would exceed 0.005 N m in magnitude was contradicted for the median lane. The actuator command therefore reaches the body-wrench buffer. These quantities do not include articulated joint reactions, inertial terms or the per-body damping applied later in the ABA dynamics. The pose buffer used to transform wing/tail moments may lag a physics substep; the force/moment aggregate is exploratory, not a calibrated full angular momentum balance.

Source inspection identifies a separate explicit 0.50 rotational body-damping parameter inherited from the estimated dove hybrid, while the fuselage aerodynamic model also has 0.010 N m s yaw damping. The comment says the body damping is a model closure for missing measured derivatives and was introduced for pitch stabilization. Its effect on yaw has not yet been isolated; it is not evidence of a solver defect.

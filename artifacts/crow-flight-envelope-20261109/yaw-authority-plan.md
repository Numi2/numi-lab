# Maximum yaw-action probe with roll stabilization and speed trim

Question: what bidirectional yaw rates can the current v15 body and bounded yaw actuator sustain under the improved longitudinal and roll feedback? Compare three matched 32-lane obstacle-free trials with identical straight heading, 0.70 m/s and 0.85 m targets, roll-1.5 feedback and +0.55/-0.55 pronation trim. Beginning at 6 s override only the normalized yaw action with -1, 0 or +1 before its normal actuator filter. Preserve all other actuation, constraints and aerodynamic parameters. Each arm uses the same development seed 20261111, native starts and 1600-step horizon; accepted state sampled every 2 s. This tests the closed-loop dynamics around a straight reference, not all possible controls or flight states.

Prediction: with -1 yaw action, at least 90% of lanes survive to 32 s and the median across first-episode lanes of mean wrapped heading change per second across t=20,22,...30 s is <= -0.03 rad/s. Compare +1 and zero actions and report individual lanes, speed, height, lateral velocity, failures and simulator status. Count only first-episode samples. Any missing required sample or failed simulator step invalidates the corresponding summary. Stop after the three arms; new seeds and layouts remain unused. This is exploratory characterization, not qualification or a claim of a fundamental body limit.

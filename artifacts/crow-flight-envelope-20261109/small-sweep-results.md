# Small sweep-offset test

The preregistered prediction was contradicted. In -0.03 lanes, the median maximum heading error was 0.559 rad without offset, 0.563 at +0.05 and 0.606 at +0.10. Median maximum speed error increased from 0.185 to 0.499 and 1.691 m/s, respectively. Both arms had no physical terminations or failed simulator steps, but zero lanes passed the existing strict tracking screen. Small equal-sign sweep offsets did not supply useful control in this setting.

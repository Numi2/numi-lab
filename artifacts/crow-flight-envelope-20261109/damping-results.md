# Diagnostic angular-damping ablation

The preregistered prediction was met at 0.05 root angular damping: median negative-action yaw rate over 20–30 s was -0.0432 rad/s versus -0.00954 at the original 0.50. At 0.15 it was -0.0210 rad/s. Zero-action rates were +0.0161, +0.0274 and +0.0294 rad/s at 0.50, 0.15 and 0.05. All four modified-damping arms had zero physical terminations in their 32 first episodes, complete required samples and zero failed simulator steps. At t=30 s median forward speeds in the negative-action arms were 0.794, 0.740 and 0.705 m/s, and root heights 0.754, 0.866 and 0.937 m. This isolates a large effect of the root damping closure on the tested yaw response; it does not establish the right physical damping or rule out other contributors. Rates and all lane data are in `damping-summary.json`.

The modified bodies and their rebound policies are diagnostic only. Original policy packs were rejected by the changed world contracts, and the actor tensors were verified identical after rebinding. Neither modified body has been promoted or used for current-v15 qualification. There are no dedicated perturbed-start, obstacle-course, landing or independent-layout results here.

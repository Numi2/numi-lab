# Differential flap authority

Relative to roll-1.5, the -0.20/+0.20 flap offset reduced -0.03-lane median maximum heading error from 0.559 to 0.458 rad, but increased median maximum lateral speed from 0.017 to 0.418 m/s, violating the preregistered <=0.20 m/s condition. The opposite sign worsened right-turn error to 0.680 rad. Both arms had zero first-episode physical terminations and zero failed simulator steps; each retained 14 screen passes, only straight and modest left. Differential flap authority is present but coupled to undesirable lateral drift.

# Pronation speed-feedback exploration

The fixed +0.55/-0.55 offset reduced straight-lane median maximum absolute speed error from 0.309 m/s for fixed +0.30/-0.30 to 0.181 m/s, with median maximum height error 0.108 m and no physical failures. The preregistered exploratory prediction was met. The feedback formula gave 0.206 m/s median maximum speed error and did not meet the 0.10 m/s improvement prediction. Both arms had zero physical failures and zero failed simulator steps across 32 first episodes. In the fixed arm, all seven straight and all seven +0.03 rad/s lanes passed the earlier three-metric sampled screen; all other turn lanes failed (0/6 each). Median maximum heading errors for -0.03, +0.06, -0.06 rad/s were 0.654, 0.541 and 1.546 rad respectively. No dedicated perturbed-start or recovery testing was performed. This remains a diagnostic teacher, not a frozen or independently qualified flight controller.

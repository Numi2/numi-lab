# Pronation-bias exploratory probe

The preregistered prediction was contradicted: median maximum heading error in the -0.03 rad/s lanes was 0.606 rad for zero bias, 0.660 rad for +0.30 left/-0.30 right, and 0.675 rad for the opposite bias; no physical terminations or failed simulator steps occurred in either new arm. Both bias arms failed all 32 preregistered tracking screens. The +left/-right bias reduced the straight-lane median maximum speed error from 0.475 to 0.309 m/s and height error from 0.157 to 0.130 m, whereas the opposite bias increased speed error to 1.071 m/s and height error to 0.251 m. At t=30 s median straight-lane forward speeds were 0.911, 0.839 and 1.744 m/s for zero, positive and negative bias respectively. These effects motivate a bounded longitudinal feedback test; the probe does not establish biological control or a useful turn envelope.

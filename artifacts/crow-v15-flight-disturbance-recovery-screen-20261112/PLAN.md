# Bounded external flight-disturbance screen

On the existing no-height-step obstacle-free 24 s flight setup, use two unchanged lanes and apply a single external torque of signed 0.005 N m for 0.10 s about body roll, pitch or yaw in two lanes each. This explicit wrench is a test disturbance, not actuator or controller authority; both normal task body-moment actions remain disabled. Compare identical-lane trajectories with the no-disturbance run, requiring exact pre-pulse states/contact substeps. For exploratory recovery, require at least 0.01 rad observed departure along the disturbed axis, survival, no post-8s toe/body/wing velocity cap or nonfoot contact, and mean absolute deviation from matched control over 20–24 s below 0.10 rad in each attitude component, 0.10 m height and 0.15 m/s horizontal speed. These thresholds are engineering screens, not measured biological limits; the uncalibrated closure, used seed, short impulse and lack of true perturbed starts or route mean even a pass is not full flight qualification.

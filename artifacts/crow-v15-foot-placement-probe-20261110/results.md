# Foot-placement probe: supported guided landing on development I

The adjusted foot target produced an exact match to all 2771 control frames before waypoint five. With the same guided takeover and physical body, the bird completed all five waypoints and ended with 1273 consecutive bilateral loaded control-step samples (25.46 s), compared with a 471-sample interval followed by loss of support in the matched control. Its longest sampled run of simultaneous bilateral opposing front-and-rear toe impulses was 1232 endpoints (24.64 s), versus 12 in the control. No physical or solver failure and no sampled nonfoot perch impulse were reported. After the measured impulse-free window, maximum flight toe angle was 0.000380 rad.

This is one guided test on a used layout. Opposing contact was not uninterrupted throughout the entire landing, endpoint sampling cannot establish all-substep forces, and toe release, teacher-off navigation, robustness on other starting states and unused layouts remain unqualified. The route prefix alone is eligible for development training; landing is excluded from successful-demonstration data under the stricter criterion. The underlying one-hinge physical digits are simplified engineering shapes rather than measured crow anatomy.

# Terminal descent damping: negative development screen

Increasing the coefficient on the accepted vertical-speed term of the final height target from 0.35 to 0.65, 0.95 or 1.25 did not produce a supported ending. All six modified lanes reached five waypoint flags without a reported physical termination by the 4400-step horizon, but none had foot support in the last 25 intervals, and none showed the measured one-foot release/regrip pattern. Their mean foot-contact normal and proximal toe state are in analysis.json. Those horizon-censored birds were not landed. One of two unchanged controls physically failed, and the other remained unsupported; both control states and contact substeps match the corresponding original development run through its original endpoint. Modified lanes first diverged at steps 2982–3081 after the terminal-guidance change became active.

The result rejects these settings for this developmental controller and horizon; it does not disprove the hypothesis that contact-speed control could improve a redesigned landing. There are only two correlated starts per tested coefficient, the controller and aerodynamics are unqualified, and the scene/seed are used development data. The previous supported-but-tipping lane used the unmodified coefficient, not this result. The archived replay is lane 7 and shows this trial rather than a successful landing.

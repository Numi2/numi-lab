# v15 ground/contact research checkpoint

This is an experimental checkpoint, not a completed end-to-end articulated-toe controller. The separately archived flight-controller source remains unchanged (SHA-256 in `native-runtime-index.json`). The frozen route and landing policies were evaluated with teacher off; the I/J layouts were already used during development. Plans in this directory were written before their corresponding tests, and failed outcomes are retained.

* The toe prototype has eight estimated single-hinge collision toes and unmeasured masses, inertia, armature and drives. Its initial 18-case and force-gated screens did not make an opposing grip. A subsequent nine-case perch-alignment screen found short opposing loads in four cases. At the selected centered 50 mm-wide development perch, a force-triggered closure and release was repeated against sensor-clamped and toe-collision-off controls. Raw identity evidence shows independently opposing fore/rear toe normal impulses on each foot for twelve sampled steps and their loss after release. All eight small initial-perturbation cases passed a predeclared per-foot hold/release screen. This is a short isolated test, not a disturbance-resistant landing grip or calibrated anatomy.
* The original rigid-foot v15 body used a load-gated alternating gait followed by a symmetric crouch and bilateral-load-gated extension. On the exposed training course, the wing-suppressed push arm achieved a two-sample upward foot-unloading hop in 8/8 settled initial-perturbation lanes; its paired no-push control did so in 0/8. At the selected replay the foot boxes cleared the ground by about 5–7 mm in the first two unloaded endpoints. This does not establish an uninterrupted flight caused by the legs, nor independent reliability. With wings active, both push and no-push arms lifted off. The sampled walking segment advanced 3.4 cm in two seconds in the inspected lane; endpoint foot loading alternated, but contact-point slip and natural gait are not qualified.
* Both push and no-push arms completed five waypoints and 25 final low-speed foot-supported samples in 2/2 lanes on each of the two exposed layouts, with no first-episode physical failures or failed environment steps. These are rigid-foot runs; they do not contain the separate toe experiment. The video shows these runs, not toe grip.
* The unmodified actor was correctly rejected when the experimental toe body changed its native contract. A conservative original foot bounding envelope left the narrowed physical sole in place and restored matching compared action/observation tables and inherited default coordinates. Separate diagnostic route/landing packs were rebound with identical tensors and new fingerprints; this is not a qualified replacement policy.

## Contact-logger correction

The earlier observer emitted no support row when a lane had no relevant contacts, so a missing row could not prove unloaded feet. Some earlier exploratory launch scripts interpreted a missing row as zero and must not be used alone as proof of lift or no-lift. `complete-observer.patch` records an explicit empty contact set for every lane at each boundary. Its selected replay matched the previous logger's 350 accepted states exactly. Claims in this checkpoint about the 8/8 leg-only hop use the corrected, complete observer (`launch-perturb-analysis.json` and `symmetric-launch-wings-off-analysis.json`); distinguish endpoint samples from continuous-time contact.

`native-runtime.tar.gz` and `native-runtime-index.json` preserve the actual binaries and reusable shader objects; actor source packs are already archived in `artifacts/crow-conditional-landing-native-20261109`. The exact experiment commands and environment are recorded in bindings and source. Do not promote any experimental body, gait or rebind on the basis of this checkpoint alone.

## First full-route test with articulated toes

After conservative-envelope admission, a neutral-toe full-route test on the two exposed layouts failed the requested end-to-end gate. Layout I: one lane reached five waypoints but subsequently had a body/perch collision at accepted trace step 2681; the other reached only waypoint three by the horizon. Layout J: one lane reached waypoint five but was airborne at the final sample; the other collided at waypoint one. In that collision the measured minimum clearance was zero between the left wing and slalom A at step 748. No solver steps failed. These are first-episode outcomes; later reset states do not count. The toes were held neutral in this admission test, so it did not exercise the isolated grip/release controller. Refer to `toe-neutral-route-*.stdout`, compressed traces and the actual replay for details.

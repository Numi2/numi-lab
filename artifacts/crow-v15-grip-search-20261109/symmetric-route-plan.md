# Full-sequence exploratory regression

Before running, compare the symmetric handoff with push and no-push on both previously exposed layouts reserved-final-i (seed 4038889268) and reserved-final-j (seed 3193706597). Two lanes each, 3000 control steps, four solver substeps, frozen flight controller, teacher off, no scheduled/intermediate resets. Record the whole first episode, actual support contacts, state trace, replay and sampled obstacle clearance. A first-episode success requires every waypoint in order, no physical failure or failed environment step, and 25 final accepted steps with low-speed upward foot-only support. Compare push and no-push; automatic resets after a physical termination do not count as successes. The body still has rigid feet and this is a regression on exposed layouts, not proof of toe grip, release, or held-out generalization. Do not infer wing-on leg contribution from airborne status.

# Toe pinch initial-state perturbation test

The central perch yielded opposing front/rear toe impulses independently on each foot during accepted steps 67-78; loss followed release at steps 84 and 88. Controls and a contact-identity rerun were checked. Before running, exercise the same controller on eight deterministic physically perturbed initial states: root x offsets of +/-0.004 m, roll +/-0.025 rad, and x velocity +/-0.015 m/s, all combinations, with the same center and width. No resets or externally applied state changes after initialization. Record all toe contact identities, actions and trajectory. Require each foot separately to have opposing front/rear normal impulses over the same three consecutive accepted boundaries during hold, then to lose opposing contact after the commanded release, without physical/solver failure. Report all lanes, including falls and false positives. These are settled-start and short-duration tests; they do not prove robust landing or biological toe parameters.

# Immediate accepted-contact toe response

The matched pre-impact continuation made front-toe contact at step 3, but the stationary-perch controller did not enable its accepted-contact latches until step 10. Those contacts ended before the latch was eligible, so all toe targets stayed zero and the bird slipped beyond the wide platform before the solver rollback. Test the identical states, geometry, solver, high-friction material and commands with only that ten-step delay removed. Contact is still read from the preceding accepted step; no contact is invented or pose prescribed. Compare the prefixes, contact timing, foot motion, failure and grip outcomes. This is a correlated used-state continuation, without original full controller or warm start, not an autonomous landing.

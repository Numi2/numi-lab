# Comparative evidence for turning

The physical screen requires both a heading change and corresponding displacement; a lateral displacement alone is not enough to qualify a controllable change of course. This is an engineering gate, not a claim that a real bird must yaw simultaneously with its first lateral acceleration.

- Ros et al., “Pigeons steer like helicopters and generate down- and upstroke lift during low speed turns,” PNAS (2011), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3250151/ . In measured low-speed pigeon turns, the birds rotate the aerodynamic force to redirect the trajectory and subsequently reacquire a preferred forward-facing body orientation. This cautions against assuming that initial lateral acceleration must immediately be accompanied by yaw. It is pigeon data, not a calibrated American crow model.
- Warrick et al., “Hummingbirds control turning velocity using body orientation and turning radius using asymmetrical wingbeat kinematics,” Journal of the Royal Society Interface (2016), https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2016.0110 . Free-flight experiments discuss body and stroke-plane banking and asymmetrical kinematics as routes to turning. Hummingbirds differ substantially from crows, so this does not specify crow coefficients.
- Sachs et al., “Exploring bird aerodynamics using radio-controlled models,” Journal of Experimental Biology (2011), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21098962/ . The model study demonstrates tail tilt and wing-tip effects relevant to control in gliding; it does not validate a crow flapping model.

Current code audit: the research aero kernel applies fuselage lateral drag at the root, a horizontal-tail lift/drag and pitch-damping model, finite-wing blade elements, and an empirical differential-stroke yaw moment. It does not model an independently actuated tail twist or detailed feather-resolved flow. Those limitations and the strong open-loop bank-and-sideslip failure motivate measuring real simulated forces, moments and time-aligned trajectory before changing coefficients; do not add an unvalidated yaw torque to force the existing gate to pass.

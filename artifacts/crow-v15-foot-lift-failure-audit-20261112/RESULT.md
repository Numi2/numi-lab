# Why the right-foot lift gate failed

The reduced support-phase candidate from the matched development-seed test had no right-foot endpoint interval satisfying all the existing lift checks: minimum modeled foot-and-toe box clearance above 1 mm, no right-foot normal impulse in that control interval, sufficient left-foot support impulse, and no nonfoot impulse. In the matched sham, 20–23 of the 100 sampled endpoints per lane satisfied all these simultaneous conditions. The existing gait audit groups at least three consecutive matching intervals into a qualifying lift bout.

In the candidate, the right-foot group exceeded 1 mm clearance at only zero to two of 100 sampled endpoints per lane, and none combined that clearance with all other required support conditions. Across each candidate lane, the right rear distal toe was the lowest box at 43–45 endpoints; in the sham, the lowest element alternated primarily between that toe and the foot. The gate's geometric box bottoms, 1 mm clearance, support impulse and three-interval duration are engineering diagnostics, not measured American-crow targets. Endpoint samples can miss brief movement and do not demonstrate continuous clearance or a biological stride. Left-foot details and all conditions are in analysis.json.

The finding narrows the observed failure to insufficient simultaneous clearance and support on the sampled states. It does not prove a defective joint, friction coefficient, renderer, or single causative mechanism. Input hashes, native kinematic reconstruction and the earlier independent replay validation are retained. Do not relax the gate or promote the candidate.

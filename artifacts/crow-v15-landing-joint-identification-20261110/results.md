# Results

All four 20-step joint pulses matched the unperturbed control exactly at the 100 recorded pre-pulse q/v samples. At step 2540, negative hip and positive ankle pulses were associated with 0.0052 and 0.0047 rad less pitch, respectively, than the control. All trials subsequently fell; positive ankle and negative hip pulses only modestly extended the longest opposing-grip run, from 117 to 120 and 121 samples. This is one used development start and does not qualify a controller or a supported landing.

# Development toe-contact audit

In the first episodes after waypoint five, the intermediate-height, ankle-extension, and load-responsive-wing development trials had zero accepted samples with both a front and rear toe delivering the specified opposing normal impulses on either foot. The audit uses the existing opposing-contact convention and threshold of 0.00005 N s per direction; it is a discrete accepted-contact test and does not establish continuous force between samples. No bilateral opposed grip or supported autonomous landing has been established.

# Frozen-pose lift direction

At 16 saved lane-5 poses per foot from the failed small-stroke development run, the paired +0.2 rad knee / -0.2 rad ankle perturbation shifts the foot-box center roughly 12.1–12.3 mm backward in world x while raising it roughly 2.7–3.7 mm. This foot placement may contribute to the failed live gait but does not establish causation.

A coordinated hip -0.1, knee +0.2, ankle -0.1 rad perturbation instead gives a foot-center x shift between -0.23 and +0.07 mm, a center height increase of 2.38–3.75 mm, and an all-foot minimum clearance of 2.12–4.62 mm in these samples. All quantities are model forward kinematics with the torso and all other joints fixed, not live actuator tracking, a dynamic prediction, or real-crow joint measurements. Dynamic contact testing is required.

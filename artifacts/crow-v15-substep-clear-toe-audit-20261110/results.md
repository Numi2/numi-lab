# Substep impulse-free toe audit

In the eight used-layout J diagnostic lanes with unchanged control, consider only post-waypoint-five sides after 25 consecutive control steps where the research sensor reported <=1e-8 N s of external foot/toe normal impulse across all four physics substeps. Align task-state and support streams by the documented one-step offset. Across 20,738 qualifying side-step samples, the maximum observed absolute toe angle was 0.000319 rad. The larger apparent 0.27 rad clear-flight value was preceded by the previously recorded missed substep impact and therefore does not satisfy the stronger impulse-free criterion. The accumulator was independently checked for one lane and a 35-step interval, not all possible contacts; zero normal impulse is not identical to absence of geometric candidates or proof of continuous clearance. This is an encouraging bounded diagnostic, not flight-wide, real-bird, grip-release, or full-sequence qualification.

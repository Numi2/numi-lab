# Delayed, contact-gated flight handoff

Five of six arms passed the predeclared gate in all eight development starts. In each passing arm, the executed trajectory and all substep contacts exactly matched its independently tested wing-suppressed leg jump through the first two unloaded rising intervals. Active-wing trajectories then remained airborne through step 500, gained at least 0.15 m by step 353, met the forward-speed and tilt limits at both steps 353 and 500, and had no native solver failures or terminations. Independently reconstructed sole/toe endpoint geometry also met the two-pose clearance condition on all six leg-only arms. The sixth arm failed the paired-flight gate and is retained.

Of the passing arms, hip -0.50 / ankle 0.18 had the smallest observed initial rearward excursion on lane 0, about 0.223 m from the pre-push position; it reached 0.248–0.249 m/s forward at step 353. This identifies a candidate for independent perturbation and clearance tests, not a frozen production controller. A backward excursion of this size may matter near obstacles. These are eight development starts, not continuous swept clearance, a landing, unused-layout navigation or biological validation.

# Same-run substep contact observation through grip transition

All 120 endpoint states exactly matched the uninstrumented 16-substep reference, and all 1,920 substeps were observed. In this one development trajectory, 11 rear-toe/interval pairs had horizontally oriented opposing normal impulse whose interval-averaged force exceeded the selected threshold, while the corresponding final-substep signal was below its threshold. Several appeared during the controller's attempted grip transition. Two additional generic toe/interval pairs met that missed-signal criterion without the opposing normal condition. These observations establish temporal sampling loss in this trajectory; they do not show that an interval-accumulated controller will succeed or that every impulse is sustained support. Test any such controller without changing the 16-substep physics or using resets, and retain its failures. This remains a stationary drop, not full-route, held-out, or biological qualification.

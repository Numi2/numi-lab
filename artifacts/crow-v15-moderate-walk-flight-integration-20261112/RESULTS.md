# Moderate crouch: full eight-lane development screen

**Decision: do not promote or describe as a biologically qualified gait.** This is a 12-second, teacher-off development trial on previously used seed 20261127. It compares the uniform 0.45-rad crouch candidate with the matched uniform 0.40-rad baseline, including the 0.185 m contact-gated release and the two direct body moments disabled only after 7.10 s. It is not an unused-layout mission, landing, or flight qualification. Early launch still has artificial assistance and the other aerodynamic closures are uncalibrated.

All eight candidate lanes ran 600 control intervals and reached two waypoint criteria without a reported physical termination, but were horizon-censored, not mission completions. During 4–6 s there were no both-feet-unloaded substeps, recorded toe-speed caps, or non-foot ground impulse. Combined upward foot impulse during intervals 348–353 was 0.725–0.745 N s; this includes passive and aerodynamic contributions and is not an isolated leg impulse. There were no recorded toe-speed caps after 7.4 s and no loaded feet from 8–12 s. Baseline and candidate match at all 800 first-100 interval endpoints and 12,800 corresponding substeps; their first divergence is interval 103.

The candidate fails the predeclared 1.5-times per-foot sampled contact-point-speed gate: lane 0 right-foot p95 is 0.0379469 m/s versus 0.0252182 m/s for its baseline, a ratio of 1.50474. This speed is sampled at loaded contact points; it is not integrated slip distance. Other lanes cannot cancel this failure. The limited magpie observation motivates testing greater flexion; species, surface, scale and coordinate differences prevent treating this as a measured American-crow target.

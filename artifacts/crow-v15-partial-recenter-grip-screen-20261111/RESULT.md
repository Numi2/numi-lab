# Partial post-release shift: two stationary development passes

The candidate reduced the existing hip-roll shift from 0.22 to 0.11 rad over 75 control intervals after the supported-release trigger, keeping the higher-traction foot material and all other tested model, controller and solver settings. Eight constructed development lanes were matched against the retained higher-traction reference. Logged q/v states matched before the first finish signal in all lanes; states of the six lanes that never finished matched for all 550 intervals. The native run completed with no errors, full substep observer coverage and maximum endpoint normal-impulse check difference 1.01e-9 N s.

Two of eight lanes met the existing combined gate: a sustained qualified single-foot release followed by 100 consecutive final 20 ms intervals with individual upward support from both feet, opposed toe contacts, adequate total load, no non-foot contact and bounded speed and tilt. The reference achieved zero of eight. Two candidate lanes fell, exactly as in the reference; the remaining four did not complete release. The preregistered partial-screen prediction was met, but reliability remains inadequate and the candidate is not promoted to the journey controller.

The original full recenter restored bilateral loading but lost left toe opposition; this intermediate shift retained both during the final 100 intervals in the two promising lanes. Sustained release measurements use the existing analyzer, which may continue counting qualified clearance after the release-finished signal; the pass gate requires at least ten qualified intervals. Full per-lane outcomes and substep evidence are in `comparison.json`, `high/analysis.json`, and `high/terminal-diagnostics.json`.

This is a constructed, stationary, teacher-directed release probe using exploratory friction, not a walking, takeoff, autonomous landing, object pickup, unseen-layout or species-calibrated result. The candidate must be retested on new starts and integrated into the v15 autonomous sequence before any broader claim. Source, binary, native input hashes, observer, full traces, failures and comparison are retained.

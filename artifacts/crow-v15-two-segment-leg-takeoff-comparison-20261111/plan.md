# Paired two-segment leg takeoff control

If the second-seed walking screen supports further work, start from the retained uniform walking controller and compare two runs with identical eight development starts and seed 20261126. Keep wing flapping disabled and upper wing and tail targets neutral from 6 to 9 seconds in both arms. The push arm uses the existing bounded bilateral crouch and contact-gated leg extension starting at 6.3 seconds; the control uses neutral leg targets in the same period. Do not inject forces, change collision geometry or reset. Require identical accepted q/v, action and contact samples through the first 300 intervals before treatment. Per lane record both feet's integrated impulses, root height and upward speed, three consecutive wholly unloaded intervals, nonfoot impulses, all-substep coverage, toe joint speed-cap hits and simulation failures. A leg-powered jump requires physically applied prior foot impulse, upward motion, >=3 cm rise relative to the pre-push root height, >=3 consecutive unloaded intervals, no nonfoot impulse before the qualifying rise and absence of comparable airborne motion in its matched control. This wing-suppressed controlled jump is not a qualified wing handoff, full navigation or landing. It also does not establish a contact-driven readiness gate or biological realism.

# Fixed-state geometry around failed regrip

For the lane with delayed front closure at zero-based tick 270, native FK gave foot, nearest-front and nearest-rear box gaps of 2.41 mm, 10.62 mm and -0.27 mm. A local positive hip change had estimated derivatives of -141.5, -142.1 and +116.2 mm/rad for those gaps; knee and ankle changes had the same sign pattern with smaller magnitudes. Thus a hip advance could bring the foretoes closer while moving the already touching rear toe away, but it also brings the foot itself toward the perch. That tradeoff must be tested under dynamics, since a static gap is not a contact force or grip.

Across both lanes at ticks 250, 270 and 290, unperturbed box gaps matched the independently reconstructed recorded body poses within 3.8 nm. All tested +/-0.02 rad perturbations stayed within model joint position limits. Individual digit derivatives use the minimum gap across all front or rear colliders, so a zero derivative can simply mean a different digit is currently nearest; they are not a reliable estimate of simultaneous coordinated closure. The exact input states, model source, binary and library hashes, derivatives and failed dynamic trials are retained. No reachable motion, landing or biological validity follows from this diagnostic.

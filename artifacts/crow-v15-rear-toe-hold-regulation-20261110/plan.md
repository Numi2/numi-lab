# Hold-phase rear toe pressure response

In the preceding reference, the maximum measured overlap at bilateral grip occurred at left rear toe step 81 after grip was detected at step 76. Earlier contact-responsive regulation disrupted acquisition. Compare unchanged controls with two exploratory candidates preserving the exact acquisition targets and, only during the 10-step hold, lowering each rear target by 0.005 or 0.010 rad per tick when its measured opposing impulse exceeds 0.015 N s over the 20 ms interval. Otherwise hold that target; during release use the lesser of that target and the original decreasing release target. This threshold is an engineering probe informed by the retained development trace, not a biological measurement. Require all-three-control exact 200-state parity, supported sustained bilateral opposing grip, release, terminal open-toe support, and independently measured grip-conditioned overlap at most 0.5 mm before broader testing. Keep all failures; a single stationary start cannot qualify a route, walking or real-crow biomechanics.

# v15 wing actuator coordinate conventions

The current research model has mirrored sweep, flap and pronation joint axes: left +z/+x/+y and right -z/-x/-y respectively. Both sweep actuators have scale 0.2, both flap actuators 1.2, and both pronation actuators 0.3. These are model conventions, not measured bird joint axes. The audit prints the model generated by the same v15 research-model builder and is a coordinate reference for interpreting differential commands; it does not establish the resulting force or correct steering sign.

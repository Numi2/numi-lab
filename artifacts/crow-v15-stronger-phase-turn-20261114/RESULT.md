# Stronger phase-gated turn: development screen

Both candidate lanes completed the 1,800-interval teacher-off used-seed screen without recorded contact after first full unloading or physical failure. Relative yaw extremes against the matched straight run were -0.2909 and +0.2908 rad, exceeding the preregistered 0.25 rad screen; maximum lateral deviations were 0.352 and 0.338 m. All original checks passed, with exact pre-onset and unchanged-lane parity. Pronation effort saturated for approximately 66.3% of measured samples, and maximum tracking error reached approximately 0.131 rad, so this does not establish a responsive general-purpose turn controller.

The support observer has a separately identified one-interval q/v versus contact timing mismatch in a reproduced launch. That was not independently verified in this specific run; interval-labelled yaw and displacement results must be interpreted with that limitation, and any paired state/contact interpretation requires correction. This is one fixed turn profile on one development seed with an existing flight carrier, not flight qualification, a disturbed-start recovery, a frozen controller or a navigation result.

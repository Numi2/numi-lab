Its states and logged contacts matched the earlier held trial through interval 410, and its six recorded targets stayed at the specified crouch through intervals 412–500. Comparison uses the immutable push arm from that earlier trial; the push was not rerun here.

The recorded state and target prefix and substeps were complete, and no observer errors were reported.

This is a system-level control-rule result, not leg necessity under identical wing commands. The lanes share one used seed and are correlated.

# Every-substep toe velocity observer

Accepted control-endpoint readings can miss transient toe joint speed-cap hits. Extend only the isolated host-side contact observer with a GPU buffer copy of the committed generalized velocities at every physics substep and log the 16 toe coordinates for each lane. Do not alter world, solver, controller or actions. Replay the selected retained standing/walking controller and require every accepted q/v, policy action and contact sample to exactly reproduce the original, plus complete 16-substep velocity coverage and agreement between the last substep and accepted endpoint. Report speed-cap hits by toe, lane and phase; do not infer no hidden hits without successful coverage and parity. This is an observer validation, not a fix or biological calibration.

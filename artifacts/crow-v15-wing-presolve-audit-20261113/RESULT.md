# Where the observed flap-speed transient develops

In the earlier parity-validated smooth-release push run, inspect step 369, substeps 0–2, lanes 2–7, both flap DOFs. This interval was selected after the peak was observed and is descriptive, not a preregistered test. Each flap gained 8.79–12.96 rad/s between its recorded substep start and pre-contact velocity. The subsequent pre-contact-to-next-substep-start difference was at most 0.049 rad/s in magnitude across these samples. The sample period is 1.25 ms.

Thus, for this window, the major increase is already present before contact resolution; the subsequent change is comparatively small. This does not distinguish drive torque, aerodynamic force, gravity, other pre-contact effects, or numerical integration, and does not establish physiological validity.

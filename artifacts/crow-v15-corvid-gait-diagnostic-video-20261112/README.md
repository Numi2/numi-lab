# Walking diagnostic, not mission footage

These are native v15 physical replays of one already-used development start, rendered at 50 fps over the 4–6 s walking interval. Baseline and candidate use the same lane, seed, model and camera; the candidate applies the gentle corvid-informed knee/ankle stance drives. Both side and front views are supplied. The candidate maintained collective foot support but failed the predeclared contact-motion reduction test. This clip is not a successful full autonomous sequence, held-out layout, takeoff qualification or landing.

# Gait phase observer: clock advances, feet do not clear

The read-only observer matched accepted q/v, endpoint contacts and all recorded substep aggregate contacts through 450 intervals in all eight development lanes. During steps 301–400, the gait clock advanced on all 99 transitions and visited all 25 phases in every lane: the phase did not stall. In lane 3 the maximum whole-foot/toe clearance over those endpoints was 0.633 mm for the left and 0.873 mm for the right, and neither foot was unloaded over a full interval. These are sampled endpoints and interval impulse measurements, not swept geometry. Positive knee-angle perturbations lowered foot height in an independent local kinematic query; the knee sign test is an unqualified hypothesis. No full-sequence or unused-layout qualification follows.

# Same-start leg-extension control for the integrated takeoff

On the same two used seeds and eight closely related starts, compare the existing toe-bypassed integrated takeoff against an otherwise identical arm whose commanded extension target is replaced with the existing crouched leg target. The control actively holds crouch; it does not remove all leg actuation. Both arms retain the same wings, body, contact conditions, controller and .20 s release handoff. No extra force is introduced. Require exact same-lane state and contact prefixes through the first possible change at 10.96 s. Retain all outcomes and measure per-foot vertical normal and total normal impulse, load duration, and root vertical speed/height over prespecified windows 549–556, 549–560, 549–575 and 549–600, plus later survival, contacts and joint speeds to step 625. Same-lane differences indicate the effect of a leg-target change in this simulated coupled system; they do not measure muscle work, isolate aerodynamic impulse, prove legs alone achieve liftoff, validate against American crow forces, or qualify flight/landing/navigation.

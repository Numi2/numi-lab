# Perch settling feedback sweep

Two +4 mm fore-aft perturbed starts in the earlier development trial drifted forward and fell before the bilateral grip gate, without entering weight shift or release. On those two fixed starts compare pre-release hip balance gains 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 4.0 rad/m, with 0.4 s velocity damping and the existing ±0.16 rad bound, then use the same staged release and 80-interval return. Record the full contact, fall, release, toe velocity and regrip outcomes; this privileged stationary experiment is not a final controller.

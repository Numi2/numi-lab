# Feedback controller flight envelope

All four preselected 80-lane tests passed the plan's ground-start, five-mode sampled screen: 160/160 lanes at 0.70 m/s and 80/80 each at 0.40 and 0.85 m/s. Each had zero solver failed steps, zero recorded physical failures, climb/descent median separation around 0.40 m, and no sampled toe at the 12 rad/s limit. See the individual analysis files for maxima and bindings. This is an obstacle-free research-body result; its generalized mass partition, wing planform and aerodynamic closure remain estimated rather than biologically calibrated. The runtime flight controller, changed body and observation contract have not yet been integrated and frozen for autonomous navigation, and this screen does not test the full walking-to-landing task.

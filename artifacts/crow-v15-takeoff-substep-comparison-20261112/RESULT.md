# Launch timestep sensitivity: force peaks remain unresolved

The initial 32-substep smoke was correctly rejected by the full policy contract because the world, task, observation and action fingerprints changed. A separate diagnostic host compared the compiled observation/action hash inputs while excluding the world fingerprint; including contact radii, these hash inputs matched between 16 and 32 substeps. Copied diagnostic policies retained identical network weights and transforms and were rebound to the 32-substep fingerprints; the normal policy contract was not weakened. The rebound smoke completed. The input route pack and landing pack are already retained respectively in the near-slalom policy artifact and hip-roll runtime archive; their SHA-256 hashes match the recorded input metadata.

All eight 32-substep first episodes completed interval 360, with all 92,160 substep records present, no reported physics error, overflow or nonfoot ground impulse in intervals 340–360. The recorded perch-release sequence bits remained zero through the test. This is only a ground-start takeoff diagnostic; the 32-substep perch grip threshold has not been calibrated or tested.

In the 16-substep retained run, each foot's largest raw upward-impulse/dt peak was 7.14–7.93 bodyweights, and the 25 ms box-average peaks were 2.09–2.26. At 32 substeps, raw peaks were 6.93–7.25 and box-average peaks were 1.35–1.39. Total upward impulse over intervals 340–360 was 1.493–1.497 N s at 16 and 1.485–1.493 N s at 32. Last detected ground load was step 352 substep 6 at 16 versus step 353 substep 10 at 32. Both arms used the same model, seed, host binary, shader, actor weights and nominal commands, but their earlier evolution was not identical: root positions differed by 7–94 mm at step 340. The different force profiles therefore cannot be attributed to timestep alone or treated as a same-state convergence test.

These inferred vertical impulse rates and box averages are not the 10 kHz, 40 Hz-filtered measured ground-reaction vector in the related-crow preprint. The substantial peaks and changed averaged waveform warrant a restored-state temporal-resolution study with equivalent contact and controller history, followed by the exact empirical filter if available. This result does not qualify crow takeoff or the rest of the sequence, and no controller or 32-substep perch behavior is promoted.

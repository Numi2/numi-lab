# Contact-gated balance deadband with grip

The previous combined trial exactly reproduced the original control, but continuous gain-5 hip feedback reduced the 8-start supported grip/release result from 4/8 to 1/8 and disturbed previously centered starts. The measured root pose during a prior used-development supported grip was near -0.012 m relative to the perch. Test whether avoiding hip actuation near that reference preserves stable starts while correcting displaced ones.

Use original 0.10 toe drive and original rear-first closure/release, the same v15-shaped supported scene and eight perturbations. Arm 0 has no hip feedback; arm 1 uses the previous continuous contact-gated gain-5 rule; arms 2 and 3 use the same rule and 0.08 rad cap only when absolute root-to-reference error exceeds 0.004 m or 0.008 m respectively. Feedback must be zero inside the deadband even if velocity fluctuates. No force or state is injected. Require exact saved-trace parity for arms 0 and 1, at least 6/8 with simultaneous opposed grip and bilateral support for three endpoints, later release and all final 25 endpoints supported with neutral toes, and at most 0.5 mm worst accepted-pose overlap during opposing grip. Report every rollback and failure; no tuning to reserve layouts. This development bench is not a complete landing, walking, takeoff, flight, substep or biological qualification.

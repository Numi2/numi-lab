# Balance deadband screen: rejected

Both reference arms reproduced their retained traces exactly. The no-balance and continuous-balance reference arms achieved the simultaneous supported grip/release/terminal-support sequence in 4/8 and 1/8 starts. The 4 mm and 8 mm deadband arms achieved it in only 2/8 each; each retained final bilateral support in 2/8. Their worst accepted-pose toe–perch overlap during opposed grip was 0.984 mm and 5.600 mm, above the 0.5 mm gate. Neither arm qualifies. The original control reproduced its previously recorded rollback in case 4.

This bench used default speculative collision and a default library/shader, whereas the complete crow host uses hybrid CCD with all bird shapes flagged and a research SAT collision shader. Its results must not be attributed directly to the full system. The next isolated comparison must use the full host's collision settings and shader, and any candidate still requires full autonomous validation.

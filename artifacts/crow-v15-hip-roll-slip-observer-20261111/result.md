# Measured walking contact velocity

In the selected eight-start development gait, final-substep contact-point tangential speed medians are 2.84–3.76 mm/s; p95 values are 38.7–43.3 mm/s and individual maxima are 109–111 mm/s. Logging reproduced the pre-existing trajectory and contacts exactly; independent kinematics matched the replay. These are contact-count-weighted endpoint samples, not force-weighted, cumulative slip, substep-continuous motion, or a biological benchmark.

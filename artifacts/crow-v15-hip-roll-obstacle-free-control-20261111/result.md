Across their first episodes, the substep observer recorded 29, 19, 28, 14 and 12 toe speed-cap samples respectively; this alone does not establish airborne toe behavior. The trace also includes later data after first-episode termination and must not be treated as one uninterrupted successful flight. An intentionally interrupted initial attempt lacking body-state samples is retained.

The course collisions were deliberately disabled for this control, while ground was retained. The newer task shader changes walking and takeoff scheduling compared with the earlier flight-envelope trial; therefore this is not a matched comparison and does not isolate the frozen flight law or the body as the cause. It is not autonomous navigation, a supported landing, or biological validation. The named terminal contacts are sampled at termination and need not identify the first fatal contact.

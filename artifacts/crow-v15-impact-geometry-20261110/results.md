# Result: rear toes could not reach the perch at the traced catch

The replayed GPU traces matched all 120 sampled q/v summaries exactly across the three matched controls. In the first 0.2 s of case 1, projected world-x bounds of each rear-toe box remained separated from the near face of the perch. At the first sampled state the left and right gaps were 71.3 and 69.8 mm; at 0.15 s they were 67.2-85.4 mm across the arms and sides. A positive separation on one axis proves those boxes were not touching the perch at those snapshots. The supported open control carried load on the front toes; closing while the rear toes were out of reach was therefore not an opposed grip.

This identifies a necessary alignment problem in this reached-state probe, not a complete all-lane explanation. It does not prove how to achieve a reliable approach or establish whole-route autonomy, supported landing, toe release, or real-bird anatomy.

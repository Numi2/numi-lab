# Same-execution substep contact observer

The split-submission observation changed the simulation trajectory and cannot show what happened inside the original 16-substep execution. Adapt the existing research substep buffer-copy observer to log all contact body indices, normals and impulses during the first 40 control submissions of the separated case. No contact or state buffer is mutated. Compare every q/v trace sample with the previously retained same-input 16-substep single-submission control; any mismatch invalidates the observation for that trajectory. Resolve toe and perch body IDs from the compiled model. Compare per-toe normal impulses over the full interval with final-substep impulses, distinguishing all contacts from horizontally opposing grip contacts. Record all failures and paths. This observes a short development drop, not a controller fix, supported landing, full flight, or biological validation.

# Upward ground support gait: insufficient foot clearance, rejected

The direct upward ground-contact gate did not restore repeated clear steps: all eight used development starts produced only one left and two right qualifying clear swing bouts over eight seconds. Four lanes had 3, 3, 7 and 14 substeps respectively with no measured foot normal impulse; one had an endpoint with both feet clear by 1 mm. No nonfoot ground impulses were recorded. Accepted pre-gait q/v and substep contact parity is retained in comparison.stdout. The active contact groups already include toes; this test compares a ground-specific upward impulse criterion with the original aggregate force signal. A read-only task-state observer is needed to identify the mechanism behind the insufficient clearance. The gait is not qualified and takeoff and landing were not tested.

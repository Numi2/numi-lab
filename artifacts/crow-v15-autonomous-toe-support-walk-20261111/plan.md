# Ground upward support gating

The active contact groups already include the articulated toes, so the hypothesis that they omit toe support was wrong. Compare the existing aggregate contact force gate with a direct accepted upward ground impulse summed across each foot and its eight toe segments, using a quarter-weight last-substep threshold; self and perch contacts do not qualify. Keep the neutral region and all other dynamics unchanged. Measure sustained support, clear alternating swings and forward travel on a used development seed before considering full-sequence testing.

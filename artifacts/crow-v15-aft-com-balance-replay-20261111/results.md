# Independent aft-COM replay

The 1,000-tick single-environment replay matched all 500 stored state traces from batch lane 2 exactly. The result repeated 377 consecutive supported samples, 54 consecutive supported opposing-grip samples and a maximum estimated 0.449 mm overlap during grip, then tipped and fell; no terminal support or grip remained. This fails the gate. Actual hip and ankle positions closely tracked their commands; ankle pitch correction reached its existing bound around tick 400. Opposing rear-toe impulses became intermittent by tick 200 and disappeared for long intervals while both rear targets stayed fixed; the center of mass subsequently drifted beyond the 0.025 m perch half-width. These are observations, not proof of a unique failure cause. This one-start prototype has not been qualified for release, flight or navigation.

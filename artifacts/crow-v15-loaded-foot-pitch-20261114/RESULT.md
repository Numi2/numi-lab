# Loaded stance-foot pitch correction: failed

Continuing the bounded pitch correction while the stance foot carried vertical load, even after opposing-toe support was lost, did not produce a qualifying bilateral regrip. Both the control and candidate had zero qualifying regrip intervals and a longest strict release of 41 intervals; this test's early return prevents reaching the 100-interval release gate. The root first dropped below 0.1 m at one-based interval 361 in the control and 362 in the candidate. There were no recorded nonfoot support impulses. This is a constructed stationary perch test, not walking or landing.

The control matched the preceding feedback experiment's control exactly over all 450 logged state, command and event intervals. The candidate first differed from its matched control at zero-based interval 289. Both runs have complete, unique, finite contact and toe-velocity substep coverage and no recorded native or observer errors. Before the detected fall, the control and candidate had 13 and 84 substeps respectively at or above 11.99 rad/s, reaching the configured 12 rad/s toe-speed limit. Contact and state epochs remain independently unverified, and these speeds are not actuator effort. The candidate is not promoted.

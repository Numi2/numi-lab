# Equal-sign pronation command screen

The paired command changed the actual bilateral wing orientations: in the 13.02–15 s sample the ±0.3 normalized-action arms produced 0.115/0.122 rad mean sagittal reflection error versus 0.049/0.050 in the controls. Reconstructed wing poses were checked against the retained lane-5 replay. Yet both signs produced similar body yaw, rather than opposite turns: over 12–24 s the +0.1/−0.1 yaw changes were −0.155/−0.158 rad; +0.2/−0.2 were −0.122/−0.130; +0.3/−0.3 were −0.091/−0.104. Controls were −0.172 rad. All candidate arms also substantially changed forward travel. The model needs more investigation before these joint commands can support qualified steering.

All 5,100 matched state records and 81,600 contact substeps agreed exactly through the intervention and in the unchanged controls. All eight lanes remained airborne for 24 s and had no nonfoot-ground contacts or post-4 s toe-speed caps. The wing-coordinate samples did not exceed 0.34 rad, but actual target saturation was not recorded. This is collision-disabled development flight on a used seed; waypoint flags here are not navigation successes, and orientation symmetry does not validate aerodynamic forces or biological realism.

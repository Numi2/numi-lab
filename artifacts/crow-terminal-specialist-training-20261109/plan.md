# Landing-only specialist

Train only on observations at or after physically reaching waypoint five, since the stage selector does not execute this actor earlier. Use successful teacher episodes, successful teacher-approach/student-landing recoveries, and successful full-student recoveries. Exclude failed episodes. Reserve the highest eligible lane per recovery dataset and course for development validation. Weight full-student terminal states before teacher intervention sixteenfold, deterministic terminal-only student states twelvefold, and earlier terminal student states eightfold. Subsample every fifth step except the first; retain head and beak behavior. Fixed Adam 0.0001 for 300 epochs. Freeze the route actor during evaluation and compare teacher-off execution on matched starts.

# Research-only wing aerodynamic alignment

The existing load kernel computes the distal wing center from the sweep joint alone. With the revised flap hinge, it ignores the flap-dependent translation and can apply a wrench at an incorrect lever arm. In 4,000 random bilateral states the old expression differed from the independent FP64 articulated kinematics by as much as 0.07115 m. A specialized expression for this research-only v15 topology matched the reference wing center and velocity to below 5e-16 and arbitrary wing-point position and velocity to below 7e-16. This independently validates the mathematical expression, not the execution of every GPU shader operation or the aerodynamics themselves.

The experimental shader moves blade stations around the shoulder and computes sweep/flap velocities relative to that shoulder and pronation velocities relative to the distal origin; it converts the resultant moment to the true wing COM. Geometry assumes the modeled shoulder, a 0.35-radian neutral flap, zero distal local COM, and a neutral planform extending from the shoulder to twice its center span. It is specific to the experimental modified body, not a generic model, and the planform, unsteady closure, torso inertia, tail and coefficients are not calibrated to a particular crow.

In a paired obstacle-disabled, fixed-head, zero-actor, 8-lane exploratory screen, the old flight law produced 8/8 physical failures with the corrected aerodynamic geometry. First-episode fatal contacts were beak/ground at steps 524–698; pre-impact pitch was 0.976–1.008 radians and world forward velocity 1.75–3.33 m/s, versus a 0.70 m/s speed command. The solver reported no failed environment steps. The retained policy action trace is the zero actor output, not the executed flight-controller action. Subsequent states after termination are not valid first-episode evidence. These findings do not qualify the revised body, controller, landing, or biological realism.

# Matched-perch initial overlap

The first twelve endpoint states exactly reproduced the prior original-control run. Independent rigid-box SAT found the left rear toe initially overlapped the perch by 1.317 mm before the first simulation step; the right rear toe was initially separated by 1.626 mm. The left overlap was 1.869 mm after the first endpoint and 2.573 mm by step 10; the right toe became overlapped by step 10. Thus this stationary supported-start bench does not begin from nonintersecting rigid shapes and is unsuitable for a clean landing-contact qualification. Its other results remain useful only within that limitation. This does not establish that the independently observed full-route landing overlap has the same cause, nor does it identify a solver defect. A new separated-start test is needed.

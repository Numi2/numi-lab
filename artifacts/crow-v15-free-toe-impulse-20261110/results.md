# Result and measurement limitation

All arms fail the preregistered zero-contact-candidate requirement because the engine reports many candidate constraints, including internal body pairs even at a 100 m free-fall start. A separate first-step diagnostic reported no positive normal impulse among the recorded candidate pairs, but does not establish that subsequent steps are impulse-free. Thus these runs cannot qualify any damping value as a contact-free response test. The observed worst angle after step 25 was 0.000129 rad for baseline damping, 0.00101 for 0.009, 0.0705 for 0.015 and 0.0683 for 0.030, but they are descriptive only under this measurement limitation. Preserve the initial and diagnostic traces and explicitly record impulses throughout a follow-up. This is not a full-body flight result or a biological calibration.

# Open-toe impact drive ablation

Starting with the identical separated development pose from case 1, let the bird fall for 40 steps without ever commanding toe grip. Compare (0) original 0.10 N m/rad stiffness and damping, (1) 0.01 stiffness with original damping, (2) zero stiffness with original damping, (3) toe position drives disabled, retaining all other body parameters and implicit driven joints. These are numerical sensitivity tests, not biologically calibrated motors. Arm 0 must reproduce the first ten original separated-start endpoint states, before the original begins closing. Independently calculate toe/perch SAT gaps and measure toe angles and normal contacts, including first arrival and maximum overlap. A passive toe may change posture or contact, so improvement alone is not a valid grip or landing, and disabling a toe drive is not a proposed flight fix. Report all solver failures. The full-route issue, navigation and held-out layouts remain unqualified.

# Open-toe impact drive ablation

The original arm reproduced all first ten retained separated-start states. With no closure command for the first 40 endpoints, original stiffness 0.10, stiffness 0.01, and zero stiffness with original damping all completed and showed maximum toe/perch rigid SAT overlaps of 2.684, 2.678, and 2.677 mm, respectively. For each, overlap first appeared at endpoint 2 and exceeded 0.5 mm at endpoint 3. Thus reducing this toe stiffness alone did not prevent penetration during impact in this one case. The fully disabled-drive configuration failed model validation before stepping; no dynamic result can be inferred for that arm. The other joints and the zero-stiffness toe damping remained driven, and no conclusion can be drawn about completely passive articulation or a solver fault. These are uncalibrated short-drop controls, not grip, landing, flight or biological qualification.

# Leg drive tracking audit

For candidate lanes 4–7, across the two previously used seeds and 100 sampled control steps per lane, median absolute final-substep knee-coordinate error relative to the start-of-step logged target was 0.0026–0.0034 rad; ankle error was 0.0017–0.0020 rad. The corresponding 95th percentiles were 0.0150–0.0287 rad for the knee and 0.0049–0.0069 rad for the ankle. Results for matched no-placement controls are retained alongside them. This makes gross, persistent target tracking error an insufficient explanation of the measured median mismatch; it does not explain toe contact timing, exclude transient errors, or measure drive torque, saturation or exact foot slip.

The target is recorded at substep zero, and the joint coordinate is recorded at substep 15 of the same control step. Target constancy within the interval was not independently checked. The coordinate-to-target mapping assumes the seven-coordinate free root versus six free velocities; indices and method are recorded in audit.py. Short correlated development starts, model anatomy and changed trajectories preclude biological or causal qualification. The input run logs are independently hash-verified in GitHub archive commits f3a7198 and b6748ce; consult the retained input artifacts for exact provenance. No controller is promoted.

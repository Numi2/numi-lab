# Walking and leg-launch controls on the experimental hip-roll body

With the gait amplitude at 0.80, all 16 selected starts across development seeds 20261126 and 20261127 had three alternating clearance bouts per foot during the two-second walk, forward root displacement about 3.1–3.3 cm, no nonfoot ground impulses and no substep with both feet unloaded. In the matched active-wing-suppressed controls, the accepted states and substep contacts matched through the first two rising unloaded intervals, both feet contributed upward impulse, and the body rose 3.90–4.37 cm before recontact. Passive aerodynamic forces remained active. All matched wing-on starts remained clear of ground through step 500 and met the stated short-handoff thresholds. Endpoint clearance and reconstructed geometry are checked against a native replay.

The wing-on 16-substep runs had no recorded toe speed-cap hits during walk, launch or after observer step 400, but six of the 16 starts hit the cap during initial settling. Wing-suppressed recontact also generated toe speed-cap hits. Doubling to 32 substeps did not fix this: on the first seed it produced 303 sampled cap hits and none of the eight starts achieved three qualifying alternating bouts per foot. The first 32-substep attempt was rejected on policy fingerprints; the retained retry uses re-bound diagnostic weights, not a claim of policy semantic equivalence.

These tests are teacher-guided, selected development starts on a v15-derived 35-body model with estimated hip drives. Foot slip, full flight, arrival, landing, active toe re-grip, teacher-off navigation, held-out layouts and biological calibration remain unqualified. The first exploratory 0.80 and wing-off runs did not capture their runtime hashes, so the separately hashed repeats are the cited evidence.

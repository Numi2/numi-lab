# Bounded differential-sweep identification (development only)

After both 24-second baseline runs complete, use the same v15 research body, launch, controls, observer, aero, masked course and seed 20261126. This is an exploratory actuator-identification experiment, not a navigation controller or qualification. Keep lanes 0–1 as their existing no-push/wing controls, lanes 2–3 as same-controller baseline controls, lanes 4–5 and 6–7 as opposite signed interventions. During 12–18 s only, add an antisymmetric differential sweep position target of +0.015 or -0.015 rad to actions 2 and 3, with a 0.5 s smooth entrance and exit, clamped to the existing sweep target range [-0.09, 0.025] rad; no direct body force or moment. Preserve the common flap, pronation and forward-speed feedback.

Require exact same-lane accepted state, action and contact histories to the 24-second baseline up to the first intervention, and exact full-horizon match for unaffected candidate controls. Report signed yaw change against the same-lane baseline, roll, pitch, height, planar velocity, minimum/maximum joint positions and efforts, substep contacts and first failures including any terminations. Treat sign, asymmetry, tracking and recovery as measurements, not guaranteed success. Report the entire result including failed lanes. This screen does not qualify turning, disturbances, climb/descent, leg-powered takeoff, landing or real bird mechanics. The reserved layouts and seeds remain untouched. Do not freeze this controller based on this identification.

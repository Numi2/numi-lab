# Supported anticipatory foretoe return

In the prior test the returning foretoes waited for two intervals of foot contact before closing, while the measured foretoe geometry remained separated from the perch for much of return. Starting from the exact same constructed supported state, keep the ten-interval open-foot clearance requirement and the same return, hip and rear-toe controllers. During return only, permit the left foretoe target to close without waiting for its own foot-contact latch, provided the opposite foot has measured opposing grip and posture remains bounded. Compare 0.045 and 0.060 rad per 20 ms ramp, respecting the existing 1.05 rad cap and all original modeled joint and material limits. This tests a sensor-gated anticipatory motion, not injected force, attachment or an airborne landing.

Require pre-intervention q/v parity against the supported-pitch lane 1, 32-substep complete contact observation, ten qualified clearance intervals, and 100 continuous final intervals of bilateral upward support, opposing toe normals, posture and velocity gates. Record accepted toe speed and actuator limits, recognize that unobserved substep effort cannot be certified, and preserve both failures and traces. Reject if either lane falls, loses the required support or cannot regrip. A constructed perch start is not whole-mission landing qualification.

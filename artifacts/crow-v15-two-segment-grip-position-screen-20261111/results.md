# Eight-position two-segment grip screen

All eight development starts completed 350 control intervals without native step failure; all 44,800 physics substeps were observed with no gaps or capacity overflow. Lane 0 matched every accepted q/v and control sample of the prior single-lane test exactly. None of the eight starts produced bilateral opposed front/rear toe forces at any interval; every bird's root fell below 0.1 m by interval 200. The longest bounded-speed, bounded-tilt and bilateral half-weight support run ranged from 55 to 101 intervals. One lane had a nonfoot perch impulse. The more rearward starts touched front segments but did not sustain the rear contact needed to close the fronts; the forward starts contacted rear segments but did not form opposed front contact. This rejects the sampled positions for this controller, not the toe model or the general possibility of gripping. No release or autonomous route was tested.

# Earlier wing transition: recontact persists

The 7.50 s and 7.52 s wing-onset push lanes first achieved three unloaded intervals at 375–377, then both recontacted at 378; they became unloaded again at 380 and 382 respectively. The matched held-leg controls first achieved three unloaded intervals at 381 and remained unloaded through 500. The matched controls and push used identical wing targets, unchanged lanes matched through 500, and candidate states matched through interval 368. All expected contact, leg, toe and launch-aero substeps were observed with no observer errors.

In the push lanes, modeled net vertical aerodynamic impulse before initial release (371–374) was -0.000196 and -0.000197 N s; flap displacement was about 0.00190 rad in each. Nevertheless the subsequent recontact fails the continuous leg-to-flight transition. Two and three push substeps, respectively, reached the 11.99 rad/s toe-speed threshold; the held lanes had none. These are paired development lanes on one used seed and the modeled forces are uncalibrated. This is not a qualification of flight, landing or a mission.

The original streams, replay, FK inputs and results, binaries and analysis are retained. Reproducible compressed extraction windows are excluded from Git to avoid duplicates.

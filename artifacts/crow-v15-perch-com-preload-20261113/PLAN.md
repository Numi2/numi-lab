# Supported center-of-mass preload before toe release

The accepted-state FK audit found COM x at +0.0254 m during stable support and +0.0334 m moving forward at the return latch on a perch with x range [-0.025,+0.025] m. Foot-position correction after release failed and often accelerated the fall; prior root-position screens and post-release hip-gain screens also failed. These correlations do not prove a cause. Test whether a physically supported preload toward COM x=+0.015 m before release permits a more stable transfer.

Two retained constructed starts per gain (.25, .5, 1, 2) reproduce the original physical controls until 50 consecutive bilateral opposing-support intervals have occurred. Then use bounded actual hip target feedback from measured whole-body COM x and velocity, with target +0.015 m, velocity coefficient .2 s and existing ±.16 rad correction bound. Maintain actual contact-driven toe logic. Permit foot release only after 20 consecutive intervals of qualifying bilateral opposing support with COM within 5 mm of the target and |COM vx| <= .02 m/s. Continue the same COM feedback through release and return. No force, state reset, support fixture, teleport, or changed friction is allowed. Record COM, velocity, readiness, physical hip target and joint limits every interval.

Compare q/v and controls with the retained reference exactly until each start's original preload-entry interval. Require all 32 substeps, ten qualified clear-foot release intervals, final 100 consecutive bilateral supported opposing-grip intervals, no fall or nonfoot impulse, and all hip targets within declared limits. Missing records, failure to reach readiness or solver rollback fail. The observer's effort and integration with corrected wing-axis flight remain unqualified even on a pass; this is not a flight landing or a biological validation. Preserve all failures and final layouts/seeds.

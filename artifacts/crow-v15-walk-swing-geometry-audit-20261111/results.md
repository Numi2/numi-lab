# Retained walking swing geometry audit

The archived toe-body control replay is environment 1 and matches contact-observer lane 1, not lane 0. Its accepted frame t matches observer sample t+1 to within 5e-10 in generalized positions across replay steps 100–200. The original same-number pairing failed; this correction limits comparisons in the earlier walk-contact audit which mixed replay kinematics labeled lane 0 with aggregate lane contact counts.

In this single development lane, independent rotated-box geometry puts the entire left foot and its toes more than 1 mm above the flat ground at 35 of 101 accepted poses (longest run 10), and the right foot at 42 poses (longest run 11). At each such pose, the opposite foot also had over 0.001 N s sampled normal impulse in the paired final-substep contact record. The largest minimum-foot clearances were 3.53 and 4.15 mm, respectively.

These are accepted-pose measurements with final-substep contacts, not continuous clearance, a full-interval support measurement, contact-point slip, or robust full-sequence walking qualification. The toe boxes and sole have estimated geometry. Source hashes, the offset check, failed initial pairing, and reproducible analysis are retained.

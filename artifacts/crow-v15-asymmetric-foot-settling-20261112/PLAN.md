# Faster-on, gradual-off foot placement, preregistered

The earlier abrupt paired drive shortened the toe-loading delay but lost support in all eight candidates. A later symmetric one-level-per-step ramp reduced support loss but failed to shorten the delay. On the exact same development starts, retain each lane's offsets and ramp capacity, but increase the ramp by two levels per 20 ms step while rear-only contact is detected; decrease by one level per step otherwise. Lanes 4-5 use seven levels and offsets -0.03/+0.05 rad; lanes 6-7 use four levels and offsets -0.04/+0.06 rad. The dedicated state and one-step-old contact are research-only, and these arms differ from the preceding symmetric-ramp run only in rise rate. Lanes 0-3 are unchanged. No artificial body force is introduced.

Require exact pre-intervention parity and controls 0-3 matching recorded state/contact/q/v; check ramp updates against published contact bits. Apply all existing support, joint/toe-limit, nonfoot, sampled contact-speed, forward-progress, recovery coverage, knee-timing and >=15% toe-loading-delay gates. Compare each lane to its no-placement baseline and to the earlier abrupt and symmetric-ramp trials. Save every result. A passing short trial would not establish normal-speed gait, rollback/checkpoint compatibility, real-crow measurements, grip, takeoff, flight, navigation or landing.

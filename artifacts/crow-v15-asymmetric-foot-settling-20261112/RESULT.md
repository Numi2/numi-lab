# Faster-on gradual-off foot placement: rejected

All eight candidate trials failed at least one physical gate, and none achieved the required 15% reduction of rear-to-front loading delay: the median ratios were 0.96–1.03 relative to the same-lane no-placement runs. Compared with the corresponding symmetric ramp, this faster onset reduced the observed both-feet-unloaded substeps in six lanes, kept one at zero and increased one from zero to one; the eight counts were 0–4. Several foot-speed ratios improved, but three still exceeded the 1.5 limit. Some candidates also failed knee timing or recovery coverage. These are short correlated development starts and tiny-impulse support flags; they are not evidence of a reliable or biologically realistic gait.

All recorded control lanes 0–3 matched the no-placement state, toe-contact, q and v samples across both seeds, and recorded pre-intervention states and contacts matched. Contact-ramp levels matched the prescribed +2/-1 rule on 99 checked transitions per candidate side per seed. Sensor validity, timing mismatch, changed checkpoint schema and other limits remain as in the preceding trial. Sampled changing-contact speed is not integrated material slip. No physical controller is promoted, and supported landing, leg-powered takeoff, qualified flight and autonomous navigation on unused layouts remain open.

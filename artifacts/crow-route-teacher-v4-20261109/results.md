# Earlier cross-slalom transition

The v4 matched route trial failed the zero-physical-failure requirement: two lanes contacted the second slalom with the right wing. Fifty-two of 64 first episodes reached all five gates, meeting the waypoint-count part of the screen; all five-gate completions occurred at steps 1513–1600, leaving essentially no time for a supported landing within the existing 1600-step horizon. No gate or first-slalom contacts were recorded. Seven additional surviving lanes reached four waypoints and three reached three; all failure and timeout records are retained.

The earlier transition substantially improved this development case but does not qualify the teacher. It needs a safer margin, earlier arrival, actual landing control, and genuinely unused-layout evaluation.

# Takeoff comparison development preview

This comparison uses native recorded states from the same development seed 20261127 and lane 5. Before: the integrated walking-to-takeoff run rises and falls back. After: the contact-gated release threshold test stays airborne through its eight-second horizon. The two tests match through the push; neither is a qualified full flight, unused-layout navigation or supported landing. The latter flight still uses artificial body-moment actuators. The renderer estimates feathers, skin, toe bridge, surface and lighting and its camera follows the root, so it does not demonstrate real-bird appearance, displacement, slip or force. No success claim is made beyond the measured short screen. Rendering uses Numi2/numi-gpu-physics-pigeon-sim revision d7718eae2e56ef94ec8ee9ce725032bf09458414.

The paired clip covers simulated 5.2–8.0 s. Its old renderer shows a foot appearing detached during takeoff. At one inspected frame the v15 shank and foot joint anchors differ by only about 2e-8 m in the recorded physical poses, so that visual symptom is not evidence that the joint physically separated. The anatomy display needs correction and validation from multiple views. The scripts used to add labels and combine the clips are included as a record of the local presentation; their local paths may need adapting to reproduce them elsewhere.

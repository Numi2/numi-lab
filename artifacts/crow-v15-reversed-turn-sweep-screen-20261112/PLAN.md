# Reversed differential wing sweep for bounded turns

The prior heading screen responded with opposite body yaw for positive demand, while negative demand produced weak positive yaw and both paths drifted in the same direction. Retain its ground, open-flight setup, .8 speed gain, two exact straight controls, bounded +/- .15 heading demands and no direct body moments. Use paired positive/negative demand at .55 m/s; add reversed differential mirrored sweep feedback with gains .75, 1.50 and 2.25 to the ordinary joint targets after 12 s. Require identical controls and matched preintervention trajectories, then measure signed yaw, path heading, roll, altitude, speeds, joint limits, collision and first failure. The resulting commands remain bounded; this is exploratory used-seed system identification, not a target-heading controller, biological calibration or qualified navigation.

# Reversed differential sweep: insufficient turns

The two unchanged controls and six experimental prefixes reproduced 5,100 state samples and 81,600 contact substeps. All eight first episodes remained airborne through 24 s, with no post-4-s joint speed caps or nonfoot contacts. Five course obstacles had collision disabled; the ground remained enabled. Direct body moments were disabled.

At a constant .55 m/s request, +.15 relative-heading demand combined with sweep gains .75, 1.50 and 2.25 produced path angles -.148, -.169 and -.190 rad respectively; corresponding -.15 requests produced -.038, -.017 and +.002 rad. Positive-demand body yaw changed -.174 to -.178 rad and negative-demand yaw +.082 to +.086 rad. The two zero-demand controls also drifted negative. These results do not demonstrate consistent steering in the requested direction, let alone target-angle turns. Different initial lanes and alternating requested heights limit comparisons.

No flight controller is qualified or frozen. These used-seed development results do not establish real-bird aerodynamics, perturbed-flight recovery, navigation or landing.

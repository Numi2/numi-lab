# Reference-shaped goal approach evaluation

With the corrected route actor on the same used development course, seven of eight teacher-off first episodes reached all five waypoints and none physically terminated before the 3800-step horizon. No lane achieved sustained bilateral support; five of the seven arrivals never made a recorded foot impulse at the perch, and the two with contact did not sustain it. The altered phase-one and speed guidance is rejected as a landing solution. It was not tested with toe release and is not a result on unused layouts.

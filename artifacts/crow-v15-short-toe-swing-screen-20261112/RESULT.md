# Shortened rear-toe swing: rejected

The candidate shortened the same contact-switched -0.02 rad rear-toe target to a 0.6–0.95 phase envelope. In eight matched lanes on development seed 20261126, all retained three opposite-foot-supported clearance bouts per foot, no nonfoot contacts or toe-speed caps, and zero unloaded substeps versus one in the sham. The count of sampled sole-clear/toe-low endpoints decreased from 41 to 3, and sampled loaded-contact point-speed median/p95 decreased from 0.00299/0.03813 to 0.00236/0.02872 m/s.

Every lane failed the predeclared forward-travel requirement: candidate travel was only 56.0–72.5% of its same-binary no-toe sham. The prewalk q/v/actions and contacts matched, all expected substeps were captured and no errors or overflow were recorded. The result is worse for travel than the preceding broader envelope and is not promoted. Clearance alone is not sufficient. Because this failed the first development seed, the predeclared second-seed check was not run.

These are engineering diagnostic thresholds on a two-second simulation window, not measured crow gait statistics or evidence of sustained walking, takeoff, landing or navigation. The relationship between phase-window width and whole-body progress is not monotonic here; further arbitrary narrowing is not justified by this result.

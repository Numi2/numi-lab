# Shortened rear-toe swing command

The 0.4–0.8 phase envelope improved clearance and passed one development seed but failed the >=90% travel threshold in two lanes of the other. A posthoc audit found negative right-rear targets occasionally overlap weak foot contact, without evidence of half-weight support. Test a shorter 0.6–0.95 envelope at the same -0.02 rad peak, preserving the same contact switches and physical actuators, first on the failed seed 20261126. The timing and thresholds are engineering choices, not real-crow measurements. The paired sham uses the same compiled host/shader but no added toe command.

Before accepting a result require identical prewalking states and contacts, complete substeps and no errors/overflow/nonfoot contacts or added unloaded substeps or toe-speed caps; at least three support-qualified clearance bouts per foot per lane; forward displacement >=90% of paired sham in every lane; sole-clear/toe-low endpoint counts <=80% of sham; and median/p95 loaded point speed <=1.5 times sham. If it passes, check the other already-used development seed separately. This short research screen cannot qualify biological gait, stable sustained walking, transitions, flight or navigation.

# Foot-load wing relief: no grip

The full-substep perch observer reported complete first-episode data with no overflow or observer errors. Before first physical foot contact, q/v matched the deeper-target comparison exactly, and all five waypoints were reached with 0.139 m minimum sampled obstacle clearance. There were only two consecutive bilateral half-weight support intervals, no supported-opposition interval, no nonfoot perch impulse and no failed native environment step. The first episode timed out without a supported landing, toe grip or release. The geometry threshold could not be evaluated because no opposed interval occurred.

This is a single privileged development trial, not autonomous or held-out evidence. Scaling wing requests on accepted foot force did not solve the landing.

# Hold height on sensed touchdown

The faster contact-aware guided descent reached the perch repeatedly with no nonfoot collision, but never sustained more than two bilateral-load samples. On several touchdowns the feet were aligned over the perch, yet the supervisor immediately increased height target by 5 cm in response to load; the body then rebounded upward, interrupting grip. Test the same used-J seed, v15 body, frozen flight law, hybrid SAT CCD, 16-event budget, foot sensor and toe reflex, changing only the touchdown response from current height +5 cm to current height: hold or raise the target to the measured body height but add no positive height jump. Continue the same gated descent otherwise. Record all contacts, approach velocities, opposed toe impulses, support duration and any physical or solver failure; require at least 25 consecutive loaded samples, opposed front/rear grip, stable support and no intermediate reset. This is a privileged development trial and cannot qualify autonomous navigation or the unseen layouts.

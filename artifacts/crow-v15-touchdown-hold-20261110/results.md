# Touchdown-height hold result

On used J seed 3193706597, the privileged teacher reached waypoint five at step 2787 and ran 5000 steps with no reported physical or solver failure and no recorded nonfoot/perch contact. There were 81 sampled perch/foot-contact endpoints and 11 bilateral-loaded endpoints, but the longest consecutive bilateral load was two samples; there were no bilateral opposing front/rear toe contact samples and the supported landing phase was never entered. The predeclared landing gate therefore failed.

Contact inspection shows the first contact at step 3658 involved front toes, and later approaches often contacted either front toes or rear toes alone. Toe angles increased after some contacts, so this trace does not support attributing failure solely to an unresponsive toe actuator. The first feet were substantially behind the commanded perch-relative position, indicating that alignment and approach dynamics merit a separate test. The later samples include upward body motion after contact; a single cause of the rebound has not been established. The flight-toe angle result in analysis.json uses endpoint contact observations, not full all-substep impulse accounting.

This is a guide-on development result, not an autonomous landing, a release test, or a result on an unused layout.

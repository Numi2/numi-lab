# Toe damping revision: independent flight results

All 160 lanes across two previously unreferenced seeds passed the original straight/turn/climb/descent flight gates with no physical or solver failure. Maximum speed error was 0.111 m/s, maximum sampled toe speed 0.073 rad/s, and climb-versus-descent median height separation was 0.400 m for each seed. Source fingerprints and all lane traces are retained. The fixed head and zero-output diagnostic policy isolate low-level flight, while course obstacle collisions were disabled. The frozen flight law was unchanged. This does not qualify obstacle navigation, near-hover, landing, contact-driven walking or biological realism; toe inertia and damping remain estimated. The exact tested host library and policy are archived with the toe damping isolation experiment.

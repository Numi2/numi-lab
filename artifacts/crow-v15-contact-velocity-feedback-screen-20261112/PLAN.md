# Accepted-contact velocity feedback gait screen

Development-only eight-lane screen on used seed 20261127, walking 4–6 s. Controls 0–1 use .05 rear-toe preload; lanes 2–7 test paired signed gains 0.5, 1, 2 on fore-aft relative contact velocity, clamped to 0.12 normalized hip action. The final-substep, impulse-weighted sensor is one 20 ms interval old; its collision geometry and accepted velocity may be staggered up to one physics step. It is a noisy proxy, not a biological slip measurement. Body moments disabled. For this isolated experiment two 10-bit velocities temporarily reuse the mechanical-power lane after rewards; verify the effort kernel restores it before the next reward. This is not a production interface. Require exact prefix/control parity, complete observation, forward progress, support, toe limits, and improvement in 800 Hz contact motion. Even a pass requires full-sequence and whole-gait qualification.

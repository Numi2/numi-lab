# Contact-triggered flare experiment

The reached-state grip test held non-toe joint targets while the toes closed and obtained opposed contact. Continuous guided approaches maintain full wingbeat until 25 consecutive supported samples, but they have never exceeded two; the wing-to-perch handoff can therefore be circular. Test whether a bounded, reversible wingbeat pause on sensed bilateral foot load near the target gives the existing physical toe reflex time to catch. Start from the touchdown-hold supervisor, with unchanged body, frozen flight law, toe drives, friction and collision. Only enter an intermediate contact-flare phase when both accepted foot loads exceed 0.2 N, foot-position error is under 4 cm, lateral error under 10 cm, horizontal speed under 0.15 m/s, and vertical speed under 0.4 m/s. Set flapping amplitude to zero during that phase; leave normal actuator limits and the rest of the body unchanged. If both toe-reflex timers decay to zero without renewed load, resume flight; retain the original 25-sample supported-phase gate. No contact or force is synthesized.

On used J seed 3193706597, stop at the first 5000-step episode boundary. Require 25 consecutive bilateral loaded samples, opposing front/rear contact on both feet, no nonfoot perch impact, stable support, and no reset. Record any actual failure or absence of qualifying contacts. This does not establish autonomy, biological realism or performance on unused layouts.

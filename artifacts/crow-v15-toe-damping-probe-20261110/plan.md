# Physical toe damping hypothesis

In successful development landings, opposing toe contact is intermittent despite sustained bilateral support; front contact is the missing component. Hypothesis: increasing only the toe joint damping from 0.003 to 0.0045 in the isolated research body reduces contact oscillation without changing toe mass, stiffness, dimensions, control or frozen flight controller. Build the isolated host first. Before adopting, check exact contract compatibility and compare matched original J and I full starts against the archived unchanged-body teacher-off runs. Assess earliest failure, flight toe deviation, walking, leg push, grip duration and duty cycle, toe opening, support and obstacle clearance. If compatibility, stability, grip or release fails, retain the result and reject the candidate. This is an exploratory mechanical change, not biological validation; K/L remain untouched.

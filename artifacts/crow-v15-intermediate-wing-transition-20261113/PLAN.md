The wing-held matched experiment demonstrated an isolated modeled leg-powered hop; wing onset at 7.50 and 7.52 s left an airborne window at intervals 375–377 but the bird recontacted at 378. Wing onset at 7.42 s produced continuous flight but moved the wings substantially before initial release. Test the intermediate 7.46 s and 7.48 s wing onsets, in candidate lanes 6 and 7, respectively, with identical matched held-leg controls on already-used seed 20261126. Keep other physical bodies, actuator limits, task-body-wrench ablation, teacher-off state and input policies unchanged. Retain complete rollout, all substeps, aero, joint and contact records, and rendered replay inputs.

Require exact unaffected-lane q/v/action/contact parity and candidate pre-push parity with matched hold, matched wing commands, complete substeps without overflow or solver errors, no toe-speed cap, and continuous clearance from the first unloaded interval through 500. Measure pre-release modeled aerodynamic impulse and flap displacement as well as foot-normal impulse and body center-of-mass velocity using the identified forward-kinematics executable. Compare a physically matched held-leg control and report every failure, recontact, and any wing contribution. Thresholds and timings are engineering diagnostics, not biological calibration; even a pass does not prove takeoff necessity, robustness, landing or full mission success.

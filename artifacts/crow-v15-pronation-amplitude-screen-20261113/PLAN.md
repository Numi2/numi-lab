# Reduced symmetric wing-pronation carrier screen

The exact matched 36-second load audit found that pronation drives were at their modeled +/-0.15 effort clamp for 92–99% of ticks 650–1150 and the right wing ran asymmetrically near its modeled stop. Test whether a smaller bilateral cyclic pronation target can reduce that saturation while preserving physical flight. This is a bounded engineering investigation, not a measured crow wingbeat or a change to actuator force limits.

Use the same v15 body, leg/wing launch, aero, position-hold controller, policies, obstacle-free masks with ground active, 16 substeps, seed 20261126 and 36-second horizon as the position-hold reference. Lanes 0–1 remain original launch controls; lanes 2–3 retain the full 0.18 rad carrier; lanes 4–5 use 0.12 rad and lanes 6–7 0.08 rad, beginning at the same 7.44-second onset. Keep the same 6.4 Hz waveform and all physical drive and joint limits. Use the passively instrumented host from the matched force audit, recording actual pronation effort and substep state plus aero wrenches during ticks 650–1150. No body wrench, state, drive capacity or model property is changed.

Require exact accepted q/v/policy-action/contact equality to each same-lane position-hold reference through tick 372 for the changed lanes, through 1800 for lanes 2–3 and full first episodes for controls 0–1. Require unchanged masks and policy bytes, correct current model metadata, observed target reconstruction, finite complete records and no observer errors. For each candidate pair, require both full 1800-tick first episodes with no post-release foot or other ground contact, toe-speed threshold events or flap-effort-bound violations; sampled height 0.25–1.5 m at ticks 500–1800, x velocity in (0,1] m/s and |y velocity| <=0.4 m/s at ticks 550–1800, x gain >1 m and maximum |y(t)-y(500)| <=1 m. Require no sampled pronation position at the modeled +/-0.35 rad limit and both pronation drives at their modeled effort bounds in <=75% of substeps during ticks 650–1150. Require full matching and coverage for 2–3 before candidate comparisons; report all lanes, any fatal contact and rejected trials. When both pairs pass, select the one with lower worst per-drive saturation fraction, then greater minimum height margin; if neither passes, no candidate is selected.

These thresholds measure one used-seed development screen, not animal physiology or a qualified/frozen controller. Even a pass requires an independent used-seed repeat, controlled signed yaw/turn authority, climb/descent, disturbed recovery, proven leg-powered transition, autonomous route, unused layouts and seeds, and supported landing. If all candidates fail, investigate the force model and targets rather than changing the rule. Reserved final layouts and seeds remain untouched.

# Matched rear-toe preload with standing feedback

On eight identical development starts compare 0.05-rad preload to zero and to the already retained 0.20-rad condition, keeping the same physical standing feedback, crouch, hip and leg commands. Test 0–6 s, and compare exact screen parity for the corresponding zero lane and 0.05 lane. Check every 4–6 s toe-speed cap, foot load, support-qualified swing, travel, contact-point speed distribution and nonfoot contact, with deteriorations reported. Require the 0.05 arm not to exceed the established 1.5x pooled contact-point p95 and median of the zero-dose arm and no additional toe caps or loss of support to consider it for follow-up; a gate is an engineering filter, not proof of biological validity. Endpoints are not integrated sliding. Retain all failures and do not promote without perturbation and complete-sequence evidence.

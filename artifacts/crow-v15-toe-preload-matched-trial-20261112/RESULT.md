# Matched small toe preload: promising, not a complete gait

The 0, 0.05 and previously retained 0.20-rad conditions completed all eight matched development starts for 300 intervals with no reported physical failures or trace errors. All arms matched the standing-feedback trace at all 1,208 sampled states and 19,328 contact substeps through 3 s. Zero lane 0 and low lane 1 reproduced the corresponding dose-screen records exactly through the full six-second trace.

During 4–6 s, the 0.05 arm had no toe-speed-cap substeps versus 2–3 per lane at zero, no both-feet-unloaded substeps in either arm, and 3 left and 4 right support-qualified clearing bouts per lane. Candidate travel was 130.7–139.3 mm versus 130.7–132.8 mm at zero. The pooled sampled contact-point median/p95 was 0.00319/0.04301 m/s versus 0.00354/0.04019 at zero; it passes the previously specified 1.5x filter. The 0.20 arm had p95 0.07698 m/s. Point speed is not integrated slip, and a narrowly passed engineering filter is not evidence of grip, biological validity or stability across unseen starts.

With a two-substep gap rule, all 48 complete 0.05-arm contacts containing both toe groups showed rear-group-first touchdown and release, matching the related-magpie qualitative ordering within the observer's limits. Seven other contact runs lacked one group versus two at zero and none at 0.20. All seven lasted under 20 ms; they disappear when bridging up to 16 silent 1.25-ms substeps, which may merge physically distinct contacts. Their interpretation is unresolved, not a reason to discard them. Under the coarse 20 ms phase observer, the 0.05 arm yielded 25 complete recovery windows: 16 ankle-proxy-first, 9 same endpoint, none knee-first. The proxy landmark mapping is unvalidated, equal endpoints are unresolved, and many fragments were excluded; this does not establish or refute the magpie finding that knee extension precedes ankle extension.

For the 0.05 arm, root pitch span was 3.57–3.70 degrees, COM vertical span 2.14–2.33 mm, left-knee coordinate span about 27.2 degrees and head movement relative to the torso in the forward axis 0.250–0.261 mm over the 2 s window. These are measured simulation diagnostics, not real-crow targets, and no lifelike whole-body or neck motion is established. Toe speed caps remain earlier in the 0–3 s preparation, and the test ends before takeoff. Independent starts, anatomical phase mapping, genuine grip, wing-driven flight and supported autonomous landing remain unverified; no controller is promoted.

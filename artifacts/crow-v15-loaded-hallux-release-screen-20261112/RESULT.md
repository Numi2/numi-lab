# Loaded hallux release: failed corvid-order and toe-clearance gates

The same-binary matched eight-lane development trial completed 300 steps without reset, reported physical failure, solver error, overflow, nonfoot contact or toe velocity cap. Prewalk state and contact parity held through step 200. Candidate support-qualified bouts were three left and four right per lane, with no both-feet-zero-normal substeps. Forward travel was 1.09–1.28 times the no-extra-stroke held control. Aggregate loaded endpoint contact-point median/p95 speeds were 0.00392/0.04353 m/s versus 0.00276/0.03484 for held control, within the prespecified 1.5x thresholds.

The intervention failed the corvid contact-order criterion: 0/40 complete candidate contact runs had rear-first toe-off, and 40/40 had front-first toe-off. The matched unloaded-only release had the same order. This comparison treats any front toe as the front group and uses the documented contact threshold and gap bridging; model toe anatomical identity remains unvalidated. The sole-clear/toe-low gate also failed: 183 candidate endpoints versus 192 matched sham and 49 held. Of the 183, 140 had accepted toe-ground contacts; the point-speed median/p95 for those contacts were 0.0442/0.0705 m/s. Those sampled velocities do not establish integrated slip, and accepted points and post-step body velocities can differ slightly in time.

The small -0.02-rad target, permitted earlier during loading, did not produce the related-magpie sequence; it is not promoted. This short test uses a previously used development seed and cannot establish American-crow biology, longer walking stability, takeoff, flight, landing or navigation.

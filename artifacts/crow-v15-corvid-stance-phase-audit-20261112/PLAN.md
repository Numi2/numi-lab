# Physical stance-phase comparison with related-corvid gait

Reconstruct hip, knee and ankle segment geometry at 800 Hz over the retained v15 development walk. Segment each foot's loaded stance and compare early, middle and late fifths with the reported qualitative magpie pattern: hip extension, knee flexion, and ankle flexion then extension. The modeled hip/ankle definitions, threshold, speed, species, surface and measurement protocol differ; report the contrast as directional evidence only. Closely related simulation lanes are not independent animal observations.

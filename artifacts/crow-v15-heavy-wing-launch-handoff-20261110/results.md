# Experimental walk to fixed-command flight handoff

The observer-on/off run matched all 72 sampled q/v pairs. Across two prior development seeds and eight matched starts each, all 16 push lanes passed the 10-second walking screen, all 16 latched early and remained airborne at the 900-step gate, with zero reported physical or solver failures. Walking advanced approximately 0.14 m; each foot showed repeated loaded stances and clear swing intervals under the predeclared endpoint-based measurements. The no-push controls also flew later after wing fallback; this result alone does not establish that legs caused the early lift, nor that flight is autonomous route navigation. The command here is fixed straight at 0.70 m/s and 0.85 m height, the revised body and aerodynamic closure are uncalibrated estimates, and neither landing nor final layouts were tested.

# Pre-lift foot and toe clearance geometry

At the two physically reached states immediately before gradual lift, native FK reproduced all 126 logged left-foot and toe position/orientation components within 5.53e-10. The 125 registered joint-offset combinations per lane were all within model joint limits. The starting minimum box-to-perch gaps were 0.000055 m and 0.000005 m. In both lanes, a -0.15 rad left hip-pitch offset with zero knee/ankle offset was the smallest tested offset norm predicting at least 0.001 m clearance: predicted minima 0.00270 m and 0.00347 m. Larger offsets predicted more static clearance; full candidate tables are retained.

These calculations hold root pose and other joints fixed. They do not test contact forces, stance stability, joint actuator tracking, leg/body motion, self-collision or biology. The offset is a hypothesis for a bounded physical trial, not evidence of actual release or an optimal bird gait.

# Results

All 16 privileged-guide first episodes reached five waypoints and survived 4500 steps without recorded solver failure, but no lane had more than one consecutive bilateral loaded endpoint and none entered the landed phase. No lane produced even one bilateral opposed toe-contact endpoint. Thus the predeclared loading and grip predictions failed. Before waypoint five the maximum sampled absolute toe angle was 0.000312 rad; after 25 steps without any foot/toe contact candidate the maximum was 0.02286 rad. These are endpoint measurements, not continuous substep evidence. The foot alignment changed landing feedback but did not produce a qualified landing or autonomous behavior; it remains an experimental diagnostic, not a promoted controller. The old impulse-only absence analysis is retained because it marks some zero-final-impulse contact candidates as absent.

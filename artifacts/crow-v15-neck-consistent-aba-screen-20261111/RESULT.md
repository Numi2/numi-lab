# Result: isolated free-motion neck feasibility passed

The isolated host/Metal build completed with an expanded, matching ABA capacity. Across 16 locked-pitch cases, the maximum sampled neutral body-position difference was 5.56e-17 m and the largest absolute energy/momentum numerical difference was 3.81e-9 in its respective units. Opposing 0.4 rad pitch joints displaced the head center 9.93 mm and preserved head orientation in the static test. The measured displacement was -1.97 mm in the local forward axis and -9.74 mm vertically: this pose retracts and lowers the head and does not itself demonstrate forward walking extension. The straight neutral linkage requires a suitable flexed rest and further reach-envelope study.

All 12 isolated free-motion Metal lanes ran and repeated identically. Maximum CPU-versus-GPU scaled acceleration error was 1.19e-6, below the preregistered 5e-5. Device: Apple M4 Pro. The failure-capture size assertion is 20,208 bytes for the private header; this experiment did not dispatch a bounded-drive failure capture or run any contact-world operation, and does not establish host/shader ABI for those operations.

This is an engineering mechanism only. The proximal mass remains located at the old head center for neutral mass equivalence and is not anatomically calibrated; no cervical collision shape, walking or flight control, contact, full-world ABI, renderer, takeoff or landing was tested. The prototype has not replaced the current bird or policy. Further work needs physically and biologically plausible mass placement, a meaningful flexed neutral configuration, contact-aware head/body control and new flight and sequence qualification.

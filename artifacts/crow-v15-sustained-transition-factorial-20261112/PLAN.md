# Six-second transition factorial (exploratory, preregistered)

The prior matched six-second walk produced the same toe velocity caps at step 302 in all lanes, shortly after the crouch term ends abruptly at six seconds; an independent contact-feedback publisher also stops at six seconds. Test whether either transition causes those caps and the concurrent loss of foot contact. Retain the original trajectories as same-lane controls. Within one used-seed eight-lane run: lanes 0–1 preserve both original cutoffs, 2–3 extend only the crouch command through 10 seconds, 4–5 extend only the already-active contact-feedback publisher through 10 seconds, and 6–7 extend both. Before attributing effects, require exact same-lane accepted states and contact-substep parity through step 300, full substep coverage through step 500, no solver errors or overflow. Compare each lane against its corresponding prior same-seed hip trajectory. A promising engineering signal requires no recorded toe caps, reduced both-unloaded substeps, no nonfoot impulse and at least 0.15 m forward displacement. Also report toe events and support by second, including lane-specific failures. These starts are correlated and used for development; changing contact feedback publication extends the existing physical signal rather than adding root forces. This test diagnoses transitions and does not establish whole-mission, physical or biological qualification.

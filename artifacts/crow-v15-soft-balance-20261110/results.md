# Low-authority balance result

The guided used-J episode reached all five waypoint triggers and entered supported phase at step 3819. The crow then lost support and returned to flight phase at step 4058. It reached the 5000-step episode boundary without a reported physical failure or nonfoot/perch contact, but ended airborne without a supported landing. The screen therefore failed despite avoiding the baseline body collision. Its longest continuous opposing toe-contact streak was 21 samples, with 113 opposing-contact endpoints in total; the longest bilateral-load streak was 232 samples. These signals are sampled endpoints and do not establish persistent grip between samples.

This low-authority pitch-only correction is an exploratory guide-on trial. It did not demonstrate intentional release, stable landing, autonomy or unused-layout performance. The separately running pose-hold test investigates the abrupt leg target change seen in the previous baseline.

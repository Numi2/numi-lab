# Low-authority pitch damping after catch

The first hip balance experiment caused a large transient and failed at step 3889 with a right-wing/perch collision, versus body/perch at step 4054 in the uncorrected catch. The accepted support-point code varied markedly on consecutive control steps during contact; feeding it directly alongside high pitch gains may have amplified the motion. Test a distinct lower-authority alternative: ignore that noisy signal and use symmetric hip target corrections based only on pitch and pitch rate with gains 0.12 and 0.03, bounded to 0.05 normalized action. Keep the baseline contact flare, toe grip, frozen flight law, solver and geometry. Evaluate the same used-J first episode, comparing body pitch, support duration, opposing grip and first failure with the uncorrected catch. Require held bilateral grip, stable support and no nonfoot collision or reset for a successful screen. This is guided exploration, not completion or an unused-layout result.

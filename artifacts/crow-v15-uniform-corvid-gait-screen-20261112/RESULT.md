# One corvid-informed gait setting across eight development starts

The same .125 hip modulation, .10 knee stance target and support-gated .10–.115 ankle target with 4.5–5.5 s smooth transition were applied in all eight correlated starts. All retained some foot contact through the 4–6 s walking window, with no observed toe cap or non-foot ground contact. Five of eight passed the predeclared per-foot contact-speed proxy and progress criteria. Three narrowly exceeded the 0.75 proxy ratio in one foot. Four of those five also have the expected qualitative sign in each of the four median joint-phase comparisons; each median uses six segmented stance intervals in that lane. Before 4 s, 1600 states and 25600 contact substeps match the baseline exactly.

The gait is exploratory, not qualified. The proxy is not accumulated slip, stance segments and starts are correlated, and the magpie data differ in speed, anatomy, animal, surface and measurement method. The observed simulated forward travel is approximately .073–.076 m over 2 s. This isolated gait shader deliberately differs from the full-journey launch timing and cannot establish a walking-to-flight handoff. A separate matched full-launch test is required.

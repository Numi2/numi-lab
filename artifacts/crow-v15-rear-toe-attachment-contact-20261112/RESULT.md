# Rear-toe reattachment: contact trial rejected

The switch-off host exactly matched the retained equal-toe current-body run at 2,400 lane endpoints and 38,400 contact substeps. As expected, the changed body initially rejected the original world-bound policies. A diagnostic audit found two changed foot contact-envelope radii; with the world and radii excluded from the diagnostic hash only, the remaining checked observation and action inputs matched. Copies of the source actors with identical weights and transforms were rebound to the actual modified-body contract. This diagnostic comparison does not establish equivalent sensor values or valid policy generalization.

A two-step changed-body smoke preceded the longer run and had no reported physics error, overflow, reset or termination. Its initially copied descriptive caveat was wrong and was corrected with the original retained; see SMOKE-METADATA-ERROR.md. In the subsequent 300-step run, every one of eight development lanes physically failed with forbidden external contact after 156–230 first-episode steps; there were no reported solver errors. All failures occurred before or early in the 4–6 s walking evaluation window, so no post-failure gait scores or successful navigation are claimed. The root was pitched backward by approximately 0.74–0.79 rad at the reported end. The recorded following endpoints showed ground contact with body in five lanes and tail in three; the exact first fatal collider pair is not established by these endpoint records.

The changed model immediately produced accepted contacts between the shank and proximal rear toe in all eight lanes. Such a pair was present in 800/800 sampled lane endpoints during the first 100 intervals, versus 0/800 for the unchanged control. The old attachment shifts the rear toe 34 mm backward relative to this candidate, so correcting the sole gap creates a self-collision regression in the current thick collision proxies. These recordings also show the changed support geometry and motion, and do not by themselves prove that self-contact alone caused the backward fall. Do not disable collisions to label this an improvement. All failed traces, smoke, parity, signatures, body source, logs and audits are retained. The prototype is not promoted and does not qualify standing, gait, takeoff, flight, grasping, landing or navigation.

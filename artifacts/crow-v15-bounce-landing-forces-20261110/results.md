# Results

The contact observer matched all 2743 accepted q/v states through the first failure. On this used development start, the sampled support point was about 11–23 mm behind the articulated COM for much of the initial grip, and the final-substep contact moment about COM increasingly accelerated forward pitch as the bird tipped. Toe joint angles remained near their closed targets as contact transferred from toes to the foot. These are endpoint estimates, not continuous-time force integrals or a biological finding.

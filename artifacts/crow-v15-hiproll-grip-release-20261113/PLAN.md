Run the prior two-start contact-triggered release/regrip controller on that exact topology as a baseline, with hip-roll drives held at their defaults. This is an exploratory constructed-state screen, not a complete flight landing.

Smoke test one step before the full run. Require 32 substeps, ten clear supported release intervals, final 100 bilateral supported opposing-grip intervals, no fall or nonfoot impulse, no solver failure and successful observer check.

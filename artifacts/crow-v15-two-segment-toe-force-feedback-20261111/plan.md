# Pre-run exploratory force-feedback screen

Use the 16-substep foot-trigger experiment with identical stationary starts and original joint effort. Lane zero is the original 0.55-radian rear-trigger exact-replay control. Lanes 1-7 use same-foot trigger and dynamically increase or decrease toe target when endpoint opposing normal impulse is below half or above a threshold. Thresholds are 0.000005, 0.00001, 0.00002, 0.00004, 0.00008, 0.00016 and 0.00032 N s per final substep; targets remain in [0,1.2] radians and distal target is 0.75 proximal. Log endpoint feedback plus all 16 substep forces. Require 50 consecutive supported bilateral opposing intervals, no nonfoot impulse, and at most 0.5 mm endpoint overlap during opposed intervals. The 32-substep failure remains unresolved: this screen is exploratory, not qualification or evidence of timestep convergence.

# Slower, stronger flight toe flexion candidate

The physical airborne toe target was increased from 0.35 to 0.70 rad by extending the same liftoff-gated ramp from 14 to 28 intervals, without increasing its per-interval command increment. In five obstacle-free bounded-command first episodes, all survived 1600 intervals with no ground impulse after interval 400 and no toe speed-limit samples after flight began. Four passed the strict screen; lane four retained nine initial-settling speed-limit samples also present in the reference. All contact-substep samples were present. Lane-one replay matched reference q/v, actor observations and recorded actions for the first 370 frames; at frame 450 proximal/distal physical toe angles were about 0.70/0.525 rad.

Commands replace the actor, course collisions are disabled, and a single replay lane was examined. Raw command-tracking errors in flight-analysis.json are not calibrated to the flight adapter; speed-comparison.json records actual measured velocities instead. Multi-angle visual inspection, perturbed recovery, physical approach opening, contact release, autonomous navigation and landing remain untested. The frozen low-level flight law was unchanged. The compressed replay was checked against original bytes.

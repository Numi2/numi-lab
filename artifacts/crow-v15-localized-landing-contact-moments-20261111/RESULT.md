# Contact moment proxy in two falls

The full-substep contact records and endpoint states of the localized-route development trial were inspected for lanes 0 and 6, which fell, and lane 5, which met the last-25-interval bilateral opposed support gate. During lane 0 steps 3400–3449, the mean contact pitch-moment proxy about the sampled root was +0.00865 N m s per control interval from rear toes, -0.00173 from front toes and -0.00482 from foot pads; the body pitch rose from 0.217 rad and its sampled pitch rate was +0.169 rad/s. During lane 6 steps 3750–3798, the corresponding proxy was +0.01203 rear, -0.00202 front and zero foot, starting at pitch 0.538 rad and angular velocity component +0.904 rad/s. The contact records had complete substep coverage in all examined lanes.

These are associations, not proof that rear toes caused either fall. The moment is about the endpoint root, not the whole-bird center of mass; positions change between substeps and torsional, gravitational and aerodynamic moments are omitted. The source body-group mapping and contact sign conventions are those of the retained observer. This analysis motivates inspecting the grip and approach together; it does not establish a stable controller, continuous nonpenetration, held-out success or biological fidelity.

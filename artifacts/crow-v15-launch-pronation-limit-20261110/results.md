# Near-saturated launch pronation sensitivity

On one used J start, all four variants ran 1000 steps without first-episode physical or solver failure. The two 0.30 m height-target variants had minimum sampled forward root velocity -0.155 m/s during steps 655–850; no active endpoint contact was reported within this interval. They still moved backward briefly and flew low. The 0.50 m variants reached -0.242 m/s in the same interval. The residuals saturated the existing bounded wing commands and faded to zero at 16 seconds. These endpoint observations do not establish continuous wing clearance, stable perturbed-start takeoff, complete navigation, supported landing or biological realism. The m16h30 arm is being evaluated separately on development routes; no variant is promoted.

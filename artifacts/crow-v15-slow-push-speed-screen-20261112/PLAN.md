# Slower knee-extension launch screen

Hypothesis: the prior 20 ms position-target switch causes hard 22 rad/s knee-velocity projection during the launch. Screen paired 40, 80, 160 and 320 ms smooth target transitions, with the same 100 ms early phase-inverted wing onset in all eight lanes. The bilateral-contact latch initiates the push; the direct body-moment actuators are disabled throughout. The 7.40 s push window and release logic remain unchanged. Used development seed, 16 substeps, teacher off, 10 s horizon.

Before interpreting the result, require no hip, knee, ankle, hip-roll or toe velocity-limit hits in the 6.8–7.6 s observed window, no non-foot ground impulse and no contact overflow; record actual foot unloading and recontacts, root height, attitude and motion. A lane meeting this screening gate still requires independent perturbed starts, sustained flight, biological validation and mission tests. The onset choices are engineering probes, not measured crow parameters.

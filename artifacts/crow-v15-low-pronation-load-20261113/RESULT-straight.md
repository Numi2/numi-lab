# Reduced-pronation straight-flight control: development screen passed

On the previously used 20261126 seed, the no-turn 0.06 rad symmetric wing-twist carrier passed all registered checks in both candidate lanes for the full 1800-step first episode with teacher off and task body-wrench ablation. Release began at steps 375 and 380, with no later ground contact. The unchanged comparison lanes matched the prior baseline over their complete first episodes; candidate lanes matched through step 310. Drive-target reconstruction, 16-substep contact and actuator-observer coverage, toe-speed, wing effort, height, forward speed, and full-window twist joint bounds passed. Two other control lanes experienced physical failure; the candidate outcomes do not erase those failures.

Across the full measured twist window each candidate had 9,616 substep records and spent 46.99% and 46.97% of measured actuator samples at the 0.15 N m limit; maximum tracking errors were 0.0591 and 0.0590 rad. This is less than the approximately 64.7% recorded in the earlier *turning* candidates, but the required comparison is the registered paired low-twist turning trial, which has not yet been run. The apparent improvement is not a turn result and is not flight qualification, biological validation, disturbed-start recovery, navigation or landing. The unchanged course and seed are development data. Full raw diagnostics are byte-reconstructible from the recorded parts and SHA-256 manifest.

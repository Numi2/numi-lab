# Reduced-gain eight-second walking test

The matched teacher-off eight-second walking control had 334 unsupported substeps and 18 >1 mm airborne endpoints in lane 3, with another six unsupported substeps in lane 6. Change only the sinusoidal leg drive multiplier from .80 to .60 to test whether contact impulse and bouncing reduce while preserving physically clear alternating swings and forward progress. Verify exact pre-gait parity and all-substep support, toe clearance, tilt and displacement for all eight used development starts. This is not a biological or final-layout qualification.

# Whole-gait audit of retained crow walking trials

Exploratory reanalysis, not a fresh physical trial or biological confirmation. Inputs: the two previously exposed coordinated-foot-lift development rollouts and their matched baseline traces, 8 lanes each. The independent unit is a lane, with two shared development seeds; lanes are not wild animals. Analyze only the existing 201–300 control-step walking interval and retain the inputs and outputs by hash. Do not tune or promote a controller on this audit alone.

Use the previously replay-validated native forward-kinematics observer and per-substep normal impulses to report per foot: loaded fraction and supported swing candidates, 1 mm full-foot/toe geometry clearance at endpoints, persistent clearance runs, touchdown/liftoff timing where resolvable, stance slip, and alternating physically cleared swings. Report root roll, pitch, height, leg and neck joint spans; head motion in world axes and in torso axes; whole-body COM motion; forward travel and speed. Treat frame-only geometry clearance as a sampled lower-resolution observable, never continuous clearance. Root motion is not flexible torso motion and head position is not neck stretch.

Thresholds are instrument definitions, not biological calibration: normal impulse >1e-7 N s per substep for support, full-foot and toe box clearance >1 mm at endpoint, opposing-side summed normal impulse >= half of the nominal weight impulse within the same control interval, no nonfoot impulse >1e-7 N s, and at least three consecutive endpoint samples per swing (matching the prior gait screen). No threshold represents measured crow statistics. Missing data, missing shapes, time gaps, nonfinite values, invalid replay validation or insufficient contacts must fail closed. For results with no repeated complete cleared swing cycles, cadence, duty factor by stride, phase offsets and stride length are null, not zero. Raw whole-window loaded fractions are not stride duty factors.

Verify the clearance/contact code on synthetic supported swings, toe dragging, insufficient support, window truncation, and missing data. Cross-check existing lane/seed swing outcomes and head/toe kinematics with accepted replay; record every discrepancy. References for future biological comparisons: Hayes and Alexander 1983 DOI 10.1111/j.1469-7998.1983.tb05784.x (crow gait categories; no species-specific parameter values extracted); Fernandez-Juricic et al. 2010 DOI 10.1007/s00359-010-0570-0 (American-crow scanning, not stride timing); licensed Cornell American-crow videos ML469899 and ML416478 (no footage annotations measured yet). Different individuals and footage must be reserved before fitting a model.

# Matched gait comparison

comparison.mp4 shows earlier baseline on the left and the rejected coordinated-foot-lift candidate on the right, seed 20261127, lane 5. The video is slowed to half speed. Both panels show the same two-second control interval from the matching accepted-state replays, frame indices 199–298 of 300 at 50 frames per simulated second. The panels are cropped identically from baseline.mp4 and candidate.mp4, resized and arranged side by side. Rendering uses the user’s BirdFlow repository Numi2/numi-gpu-physics-pigeon-sim, revision d7718eae2e56ef94ec8ee9ce725032bf09458414; the release binary SHA-256 was 8316472abd1f25ede1118ecd1a13ae7136d12331e5eaf1bb204943ef75ab5072. Lighting is overcast; camera yaw -1.25 rad, pitch 0.20 rad, distance 1.05 m. Toe visuals use the estimated v15 toe bridge.

The camera tracks the root and removes global translation; the video is unsuitable for judging displacement or slip. It shows a rendering of accepted rigid-body states, not measured crow footage and not an exact reconstruction of feathers, skin or collision shapes. Support and clearance results come from physics traces, not the appearance of the video. These are short development-gait excerpts, not takeoff, flight, waypoints or landing on either held-out layout.

The unslowed renderer inputs are retained as baseline.mp4 and candidate.mp4 with renderer audits and logs. The combined video was created on the desktop using ffmpeg from those inputs, identical crop 720:405:280:145, scaling each panel to 960x540, setpts=2*PTS and horizontal stacking; no synthesized motion or interpolation was introduced.

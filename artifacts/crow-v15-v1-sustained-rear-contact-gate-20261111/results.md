# Rear-toe contact gated handoff

In one matched development J teacher trial, the first q/v difference from the +25 mm approach was step 2261, after the original false handoff. Requiring ten consecutive endpoints with bilateral rear-toe and foot load prevented phase 2 from triggering in this trial. Full substep observation found two isolated bilateral half-weight intervals and no supported opposing toe-force interval. The crow drifted away and the first episode failed when its tail contacted slalom A at step 2508. No non-foot perch impulse occurred; this is an obstacle contact, not a perch solver failure. The controller did not achieve grip, release, supported landing, or autonomous navigation. The counter is a conservative endpoint condition and would not itself establish opposing forces.

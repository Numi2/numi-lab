# Uniform lateral stabilization confirmation on two used development seeds

In the preregistered two-lane-per-gain screen, the .60 gain produced 1.60 m of maximum lateral drift and failed the 1.0 m engineering threshold; 1.20 gain with +/-0.18 rad target bank produced 0.989–0.995 m in two correlated lanes and met that exploratory threshold narrowly. Select 1.20 as specified by that prior plan, without changing the physical body, launch, aerodynamic model, scene masks, baseline policies, feedback source, PD gains, 9–9.5 s onset or +/-0.025 rad differential flap-amplitude bound. Apply one identical controller to all six candidate lanes 2–7, keep lanes 0–1 as their prior no-push/wing controls, and run used development seeds 20261126 and 20261127 separately for 1200 ticks at 16 substeps. These seeds/layouts are development inputs; do not consume reserved final layouts or seeds.

Require all six candidates on each seed to reproduce their same-lane 24-second baseline accepted q/v/policy-action/contact records through tick 450. The no-push/wing controls must reproduce their same-lane baseline through their first-episode endpoints. For seed 20261126, lanes 6–7 additionally must match their entire first-episode records to the selected-gain screen; any mismatch is investigated before interpreting the result. Require masks and policy bytes unchanged; complete first-episode accepted states and declared contact/toe/target coverage from tick 335 and flap effort from tick 348; per-step reconstructed actuator feedback; no post-release ground contact or nonfoot impulse, no toe-speed threshold event and flap effort within the modeled limit. Require every candidate to reach tick 1200, sampled height 0.25–1.5 m over ticks 500–1200, x velocity (0,1] m/s and |y velocity| <=0.4 m/s over ticks 550–1200, x displacement >1 m and max absolute y displacement <=1 m from tick 500 to 1200. These are development engineering screens and are not biological reference data. Preserve complete results including controls and failures.

A two-seed pass remains insufficient to freeze or qualify flight: heading and commanded left/right turns, ascent/descent, disturbed starts, recovery, actuator speed limits and longer operating horizons remain untested. Leg-powered continuous mission, contact-driven toe grip/release, landing and genuine final-layout trials remain outstanding.

# Retained toe-body walking contact audit

Post-hoc analysis of the previously archived 8-lane no-push control, steps 100 through 200. At a 0.001 Ns normal-impulse threshold, every lane had 44 left-only, 39 right-only, 18 bilateral and zero neither-foot support endpoints. The earlier retained analysis reports approximately 4.3 cm forward travel in two seconds. Lane-zero foot-body origins moved vertically about 5 mm; their planar origin speeds are not contact-point slip. These endpoint observations do not establish clean swing clearance, long-duration gait stability or biological realism.

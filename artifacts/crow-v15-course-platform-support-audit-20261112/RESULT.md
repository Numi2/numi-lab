# Wide course-platform contact audit

Re-analyzes the full-substep contact data from the already used development-layout and seed route. Four of eight episodes have at least 25 final control intervals meeting the declared half-weight upward foot impulse with no nonfoot normal contact. Two of those four episodes are marked physical failures and are not successful landings; the other two have no reported physical termination within the recorded horizon. Four remaining lanes have no such final support. Waypoint flags and these support intervals are not a whole-mission qualification. The source audit documents substep coverage and toe-speed caps.

The platform is 0.20 m wide, unlike the separate narrow-branch grip setup. These impulse records alone neither establish zero slip nor demonstrate intentional toe release. Summed tangent magnitudes do not determine a friction coefficient. The analysis streams compressed Git-archived source parts and does not duplicate the raw contact log.

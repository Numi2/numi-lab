# Result

All 400 baseline q/v/candidate samples matched the preceding probe exactly. At baseline damping 0.003, all four free-fall starts had zero observed endpoint normal impulse, and the maximum toe angle after step 25 was 0.000129 rad. Damping 0.009 also passed all four with maximum 0.00101 rad. The 0.015 and 0.030 arms passed only two and one cases respectively; their endpoint records included normal impulses and the largest post-step-25 angles were 0.0705 and 0.0683 rad. These are impulse observations at the final physics substep only, so they cannot establish that all intermediate substeps were contact-free. The isolated baseline settles after the chosen impulse in this setup; this does not clear the near-perch route episode, establish whole-flight release, or justify any damping promotion.

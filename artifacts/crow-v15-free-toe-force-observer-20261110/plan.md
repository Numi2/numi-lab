# Free-toe response with force observation

The preceding free-fall probe recorded internal contact candidates, including zero-impulse pairs, so it failed the stated zero-candidate criterion. Repeat the same four cases, damping values, initial angles and rates, motor commands and native solver. Record the largest actual normal impulse over all candidate pairs at every accepted step, as well as candidate counts. Require every observed normal impulse <=1e-8 N s across all 100 steps, all q magnitudes <0.05 rad after step 25, and no solver failure. Also require the new baseline q and v to match the prior probe exactly for all 400 samples. This only observes the final physics-substep contact record at each accepted control step: it cannot rule out unsampled substep impulses and does not qualify flight or landing.

# Toe-only lift after release: rejected

The candidate changes only the post-release toe drive from neutral to -0.12 rad. On both original-body, teacher-off development starts it reproduced every checked q/v, observation and action frame up to the unchanged release event (4432 frames on J and 5564 on I). Both reached all five waypoints and remained bilaterally supported through step 7000 after release, with no observed physical or solver failure or nonfoot perch contact. Previous grip remained intermittent: opposing bilateral impulses in 86/100 and 74/100 sampled endpoints immediately before release.

The candidate failed the preregistered force-free release condition on both starts: front-toe/perch impulses above the endpoint threshold were recorded on both feet in every one of the 100 late post-release endpoints. Separating-axis analysis found all toe boxes separated by small positive gaps at those endpoints, illustrating that sampled geometric gaps do not imply no solver impulses throughout the interval; these are speculative/contact solver outputs, not proof of penetration or direct biological contact. The change is rejected and the original controller remains the baseline. This is previously used development data and does not establish continuous grip, a physically cleared release, autonomous generalization or biological realism.

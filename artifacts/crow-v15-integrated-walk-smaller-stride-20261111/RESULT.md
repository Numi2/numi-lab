# Smaller stride rejected

The 0.50 scale eliminated the 30 unsupported substeps in the original problematic start, but six of eight used starts instead had 37–477 no-foot-normal substeps and most lost one or both qualifying foot swing patterns. Three starts moved backward by approximately 1.1, 1.1 and 2.8 cm. The 0.65 candidate retained three clearance bouts per foot in all eight starts and limited unsupported substeps to 10 in one start. No nonfoot ground impulse or sampled toe speed cap occurred in the 0.50 walking window. Logged accepted endpoint and aggregate contact records match the reference before gait intervention through step 199.

The 0.50 candidate is rejected. All measurements are from a short used-course development test, with shape clearance sampled at endpoints; neither launch nor landing is evaluated.

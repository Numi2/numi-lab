# Ankle extension: partial unloading, failed screen

The candidate changed only the normalized symmetric ankle endpoint from 0.18 to 0.83 relative to the prior hip trial. All eight first episodes completed 400 steps. Required recorded state, policy actions and contact fields matched the prior hip and active crouch through step 345, including contact substeps 335–345; all expected work/contact samples were present with no logged overflow or substep error. This covers recorded fields, not every hidden state.

All six prespecified comparable lanes failed both preregistered screens. Root rises were 0.0242–0.0353 m; lanes 4 and 6 had three consecutive unloaded control intervals starting at step 358, while lanes 2, 3, 5 and 7 did not. Over steps 348–375, foot upward impulse was 0.0194–0.1483 Ns below the active crouch. Toe-speed cap events occurred in every comparable lane (5–7 substeps); recorded nonfoot ground impulse was zero. Generalized leg-work proxy was 0.769–1.398 J. The proxy contains solver effort terms and is not isolated muscle work.

The earlier window, steps 348–365, avoids the active crouch's 7.4 s transition: impulse differences versus that control ranged from -0.0347 to +0.0478 Ns, and versus the prior hip trial from -0.0388 to +0.0431 Ns. These lane-level variations and the two unloading events are exploratory, correlated observations from one used seed. They cannot establish reliable leg-powered takeoff, flight or biological realism. Retain all lanes, including 0–1, and do not promote this controller.

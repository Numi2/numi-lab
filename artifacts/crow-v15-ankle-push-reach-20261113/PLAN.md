# Ankle extension versus the prior hip push: bounded development experiment

Question: with wing amplitude suppressed, can increased ankle extension transfer the modeled leg push to the ground more effectively? The previous hip-only candidate produced 15 mm root rise and less impulse than active crouch in six comparable lanes. A posthoc coarse native-FK search about that used-seed posture suggests that increasing ankle pitch can extend foot reach while retaining a feasible joint endpoint, but it assumes stationary feet/body attitude and contains no contact forces. It is an engineering hypothesis, not crow biomechanics.

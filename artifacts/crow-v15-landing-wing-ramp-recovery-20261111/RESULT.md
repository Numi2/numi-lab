# Physical wing-amplitude ramp rejected

All eight teacher-off first episodes reached five waypoint criteria without physical termination by the 3800-step horizon on the used development course. The two recovered starts had not landed, ending at root x 22.02 m and 10.22 m versus final goal x approximately 5.17 m and 4.96 m. Lane 0 reached a sampled forward speed of 2.75 m/s at step 3550. Only lane 5 met the final 25-interval bilateral opposed support criterion.

The two recovery starts had 67 and 4 sampled toe speed-cap substeps after step 500, whereas staged opening without the physical wing ramp had zero in either. Two other lanes retained initial-settling caps. Full substep coverage was present with no capacity overflow, recorded substep error, nonfoot perch impulse, or logged crow self-contact after step 380. This experiment is rejected; the records do not prove a unique cause for the overshoot or speed caps. It is not full-sequence, unused-layout or biological evidence.

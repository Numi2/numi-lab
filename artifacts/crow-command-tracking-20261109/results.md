# Closed-loop command-tracking screen

The preregistered 80-lane run produced zero failed simulator steps. All 16 sampled lanes in each of left (+0.03 rad/s), right (-0.03 rad/s), climb (1.05 m target), and descent (0.65 m target) passed the prescribed individual tracking screen; the climb and descent median heights differed by 0.39989 m. The straight group passed 15/16 and therefore failed the group screen. The failing first episode crashed at step 970: the final-substep contact was crow_tail / locomotion_ground. Retained state shows that at step 849 this particular lane had already crossed the task's fifth waypoint, which switched the experimental teacher blend to zero. The unqualified actor then took over and the lane crashed. This is an observed controller handoff in the flight-bench harness and a real first-episode failure, not evidence that the active flight controller remained stable for that lane. The other 79 lanes did not hit that handoff. The first fatal microstep collider and full continuous clearance remain unproven.

This experiment does not qualify the controller. A matched follow-up must hold the low-level test controller active throughout the flight-only bench, keep the same starts, and report the previously failing lane and all other outcomes. This is a diagnostic flight-bench change only; it does not count a waypoint as a landing or solve the real landing handoff. See tracking-results.json, tracking.patch and compressed samples.

# Matched takeoff COM and foot impulse

Using the retained used-seed 0.55 rad wing-amplitude trials and native v15 forward kinematics, summed modeled foot-normal impulse across intervals 371–390 was 0.6556 N s with leg push versus 0.5537 N s with the held-leg control, a +0.1018 N s difference. Computed whole-body COM vertical velocity at the first three-interval unloaded window was 0.455 m/s versus 0.270 m/s. The center-of-mass velocity translation sanity check error was below 6e-17 m/s. These are model outputs; the impulse is a sum of contact-normal magnitudes, not a directly isolated vertical leg impulse.

The wings move substantially during both trials and their dynamics, contacts and body trajectories are coupled. The difference is compatible with a leg contribution but does not isolate its causal impulse or prove a leg-only takeoff. The separately archived wing-held hop provides the bounded leg isolation comparison. Candidate q/v versus contact alignment here is inferred from the independently verified observer convention in other lanes. This one-seed analysis does not qualify long flight, landing or navigation. Full states, inputs, binary hashes and analysis are retained.

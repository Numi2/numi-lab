# Differential flap steering identification

Matched open-flight study on previously used seed 20261126, ground retained, five obstacle masks disabled, no direct body moments. Preserve controls 0–1 and the .55 m/s flight command; lane pairs receive antisymmetric flap offsets of +/- .10, .20 or .30 from 12–13 s, with zero heading demand. Measure signed path and yaw response, roll, height, speed limits, contact and failure against matched preintervention trajectories. This is a physical-joint diagnostic, not a closed-loop turn or biological qualification.

# Contact-responsive rear toe targets

In the one development start with supported grip and release, measured rear-toe opposing impulses commonly exceeded 0.01 N s per 20 ms interval, while rigid overlap exceeded the independently prescribed limit. Compare three unchanged controls with two exploratory candidates that independently back off each rear toe target by 0.01 rad per control tick when its measured opposing impulse exceeds respectively 0.01 or 0.02 N s; otherwise track the original target at up to 0.02 rad per tick. During release the target can only fall and is bounded by the original release target. Front toe control, original stiffness/effort, 16 substeps, pitch control, separated start and contact observer remain unchanged. These impulse thresholds are engineering probes, not biological measurements or a calibrated crow. Require exact all-200-state replay for all three controls, sustained opposing grip, supported release and terminal open-toe support and independent grip-conditioned overlap at most 0.5 mm to nominate broader tests. Preserve failed trials. A stationary start cannot establish walking, flight, route completion, held-out layout success or biological fidelity.

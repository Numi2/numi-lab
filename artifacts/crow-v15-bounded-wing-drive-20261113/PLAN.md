# Bounded flap working-effort trial, preregistered

The single-step matched flap effort ablation eliminated approximately 12.95 rad/s of first-substep acceleration. The actual current v15 flap drives have gains 800 and 8 and a 120 effort bound; these are not crow-calibrated. Test a deliberately conservative, engineering-only symmetric working-effort cap of 2.0 for each flap DOF in comparable lanes 2–7, applied at both preparation and refresh of the existing implicit position drive. Lanes 0–1 keep original settings as context. No q/v state clamps, body or solver changes, direct body wrench, or hidden support are added. The cap is not asserted to be a biological muscle torque. Apply from episode start; this can change the pre-launch walking trajectory, so require recorded parity through 345 between matched push and active-crouch arms, and separately report whether parity with the historical uncapped push persists. The experimental shader changes the runtime actuation despite retaining the model descriptor fingerprint; the metallib hash and source identify it, and no promotion is allowed on that fingerprint alone.

Use the smooth-release leg and wing-target programs, instrumented host, one used development seed 20261126, eight correlated lanes, 400 control intervals, 16 substeps, no resets. Require complete logs and no errors/overflow. Require zero flap working-effort cap exceedances in the observed substeps 348–375 in lanes 2–7, and matched state/action/contact parity between arms through step 345. Report early push-minus-crouch foot impulse in 348–355 and 348–357, pre-release support gaps, three-interval release, later recontacts, root rise, nonfoot impulse, toe threshold counts, substep flap speeds and model aero forces. Apply the prior strict launch screen per lane: both impulse gains >=0.05 N s, no unsupported gap before release by step 375, no recontact to 400, rise >=0.05 m, zero nonfoot impulse and at most one toe threshold substep. All six must pass. Improvement in speed or passing one used seed cannot qualify physiology or flight. Preserve source, binaries, hashes, both full traces and failures.

# Approach contact-gate proxy audit

In 275–798 observed phase-one endpoints per lane in the localized-route development run, both logged foot loads exceeded the 0.05 threshold only zero to three times per lane. Other position and velocity proxies were simultaneously satisfied without the two-load proxy at 11–103 sampled endpoints per lane. This is consistent with inadequate bilateral contact during the repeated approach, but does not establish why the feet fail to load or prove the actual landing predicate failed for the same reason.

The logged task state and endpoint root velocity are proxies: the production predicate reads compact contacts and task state in different kernels, so timing and contact-group mapping have not been independently aligned. Some samples immediately after entering hold are excluded. These data motivate direct predicate instrumentation and approach/foot-placement analysis rather than relaxing a support threshold. No final-layout or biological claim follows.

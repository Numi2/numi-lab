# Development-route approach states at first perch contact

This audit compares the preceding 20 ms control endpoint with each lane's first recorded foot impulse against the course platform. The one lane that also finished the original 3800-step development run with 25 bilateral opposing support intervals had pre-contact downward velocity 0.051 m/s. The other seven lanes had downward speeds 0.215–0.352 m/s at their respective previous endpoints; two later physically failed. This suggests a testable slow-descent hypothesis, but it is a small correlated development batch and cannot establish causation or predict touchdown velocity at the exact contact substep. The endpoint positions are world coordinates, and support, collision and phase must be interpreted with the source substep audit. The result is not a qualification or proof of grip release.

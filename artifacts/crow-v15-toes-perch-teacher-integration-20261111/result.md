# Flight-toe and teacher approach integration: rejected landing

All eight first episodes reached five waypoint criteria in this privileged teacher diagnostic on a previously used training course. Perch toe impulses appeared in all eight, but none achieved even one interval of bilateral opposed toe contact carrying at least half body weight. Four first episodes physically terminated. Among the four surviving to interval 3800, none completed a supported landing. The per-substep observer reported complete contact coverage and no contact-capacity overflow; body stability and contact geometry require separate analysis.

In the lane-one accepted-state replay, toe joints that were curled during flight opened by the final approach. This establishes a physical joint response in that lane, not a successful landing or a multi-lane visual validation. The other early toe speed-limit samples and later contact-phase hits are retained in perch-analysis.json. The stronger teacher approach was already rejected in isolation and this combination is also rejected for landing.

The controller is privileged; this is not a teacher-off autonomous or unused-layout test. Flight source was unchanged. Earlier archived journey artifacts contain the host, policy packs and corrected replay writer. See inputs.json and runtime-index.json for exact hashes.

# Development teacher on the experimental body

All eight first episodes reached two waypoints by 1600 control intervals, with no physical failure or failed environment step. All eight were horizon-censored and neither completion nor landing was observed. This is a privileged teacher on one previously used development course and seed, not autonomous navigation, a supported landing, or a qualified full-route demonstration. Endpoint contacts do not bound all substep collisions.

Teacher-on versus teacher-off comparisons show q/v first diverged at observer step 102 in all eight starts, with differences at every subsequent step through 400. Consequently the teacher trial does not reproduce the autonomous gait or launch, and its route cannot be presented as starting from an identical physical prefix. The recorded actor actions are policy outputs, not applied actuator targets. A longer horizon, teacher-off parity through handoff, and safe physically reached landing remain required.

The exact host, shader and policy packs are archived in crow-v15-hip-roll-walk-launch-controls-20261111/runtime.tar.gz; the corrected replay writer is archived in crow-v15-replay-writer-roundtrip-20261111/runtime.tar.gz. The per-run input hashes bind this run to those files.

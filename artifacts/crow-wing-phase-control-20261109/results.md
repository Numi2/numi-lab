# Exploratory phase-dependent feathering results

The unchanged v15 body and the preregistered 72-lane native bench completed without physical failures or failed simulator steps. Three of nine groups met the prespecified screen: group 3 (+0.60 sin phase) median -0.04448 rad/s, group 5 (+0.30 cos phase) -0.03695 rad/s, group 7 (+0.60 cos phase) -0.04252 rad/s, versus the zero-modulation group's -0.00940 rad/s. Maximum sampled absolute lateral speed in these candidate groups was 0.0504, 0.0441, and 0.0469 m/s, respectively; maximum speed and height errors are in phase-results.json. The screen covers only samples 20–30 s, forced maximum right yaw after 6 s, nominal starts, and the development training course with obstacle collisions disabled. It does not establish a command-following controller, recovery, collision clearance, autonomous navigation, or landing.

The groups were interleaved by lane rather than paired starts. All groups and individual outcomes, exact source and shader identities, complete first-episode records and compressed raw observer samples are retained with the preregistration and source patch. The next prediction is that modulating the +0.60 sin component according to negative closed-loop yaw demand can enable tracking a moderate right turn without impairing straight, left, climb and descent. That prediction must be tested independently; the forced-yaw study does not establish it.

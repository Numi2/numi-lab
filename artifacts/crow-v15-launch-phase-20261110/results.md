# Early wingbeat phase sensitivity

On one used layout and seed, phase offsets of one-quarter, one-half and three-quarters of a cycle all left the crow flying backward after its leg push. At step 700 their root forward speeds were -0.601, -0.589 and -0.604 m/s, respectively, versus -0.620 m/s with the unshifted early-flight command. Phase is restored smoothly by 16 s. These short diagnostic trials do not establish a qualified takeoff or navigation and none is promoted.

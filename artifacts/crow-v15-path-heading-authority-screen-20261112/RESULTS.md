# Bounded velocity-heading authority: improved, still incomplete

Two unchanged controls and six experimental prefixes exactly matched 5,100 state samples and 81,600 contact substeps. All first episodes remained airborne through 24 s, with no nonfoot contacts or post-4-s joint speed caps; two early toe-cap events are recorded. Five course obstacle collision masks and direct body moments were disabled.

At +/- .35 rad velocity-heading targets, 20–24 s mean absolute heading error for positive/negative requests was .197/.028 rad at a .10 differential-flap limit, .110/.029 at .15, and .110/.028 at .20. Experimental-lane mean planar speed was .491–.566 m/s and maximum .610 m/s during that window; maximum sampled roll was .034–.061 rad over 12–24 s. Body yaw nevertheless changed approximately -.16 to -.18 rad in both requested directions. The bird therefore moved toward the requested paths but did not align its body to them.

These used-seed tests do not establish realistic turns, perturbation recovery, obstacle avoidance, frozen control or autonomous landing. The settings are exploratory and the opposing initial lanes have different height requests.

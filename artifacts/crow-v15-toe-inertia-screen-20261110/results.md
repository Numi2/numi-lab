# Reduced toe inertia screen: rejected

Neither predefined candidate passed any of the eight preliminary lanes; neither produced solver rollback or native physical failure in this flight-only screen, but all missed the speed gate and hit the 12 rad/s toe speed limit. All 128 recorded lane/time samples for each candidate contained at least one saturated toe, with median sampled absolute toe speed 12 rad/s. For comparison, the 5e-5 baseline had no saturated toes among 1280 sampled lane/time points on seed 20261120 and a maximum sampled toe speed of 0.071 rad/s. Neither lower-inertia variant is eligible for broader qualification, and changing that parameter alone did not explain or fix flight tracking. Samples are every 100 control steps; they do not prove continuous-time bounds. Neither variant was tested for grip, walking, or landing.

# Pre-run small-perturbation grip screen

Use the same foot-contact-triggered control, physical hip balance, 1.05-rad front cap, 1.15-rad rear cap, 32 substeps and 12/6 solver iterations as passing lane 6 in the previous stationary test. Lane zero must replay it exactly. Hold the controller fixed across lanes: vary initial root x by -2 or +2 mm, height by +2 or -2 mm, forward speed by -0.01 or +0.01 m/s, or initial roll by +0.02 rad, one change per lane. Each runs 350 intervals. Record all substeps, failures, lane-zero parity, full sustained bilateral support and opposition, nonfoot contact and geometric overlap. Require no fall, final 100 consecutive supported-opposed intervals, at least 50 consecutive such intervals and worst opposed endpoint overlap <=0.5 mm. These seven small constructed perturbations are development only, not a flight arrival, release, unused-layout evaluation or biological qualification.

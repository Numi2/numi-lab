# Matched obstacle-disabled mixed-controller control

All eight correlated development starts survived all 1200 post-reset transitions with no reported physical failure when course-object collisions were disabled, while all eight matched collision-enabled first episodes terminated on the contact rule. Both runs used the same development controller after the explicit command schedule was removed, including remaining fixed-speed feedback, zero-heading feedback in lanes 2–7, lane-dependent height control and uncalibrated aerodynamic terms. The source audit correction in the paired trial is essential: actor commands were not the sole speed or steering authority.

For every lane, the complete logged state/policy-action records are identical to the collision-enabled course run up to the first observed course contact, and first differ on the trace submission that contains that impact: lanes 0–7 respectively 1140, 801, 942, 901, 947, 924, 954 and 926. The scene observer sees nonzero impulses at those submissions in the course run. A separate one-transition policy warmup occurs before reset; the trace-submission numbering includes it. The control and collision-enabled policies were rebound to their respective world fingerprints, and this observed prefix identity checks their matching behavior through contact. See `state-parity.json` and the paired trial’s contact analysis. This supports clearance/contact as the immediate cause of the mixed-controller course failures, not a general solver failure. It does not show that subsequent flight is controlled accurately, or that every contact is fatal.

With collisions disabled, the waypoint counts were [2,2,0,0,2,0,0,0]. They do not represent course successes. This was one used seed and eight related starts. The lighter observer did not record full joint substeps; no conclusion about absence of speed caps, flight qualification, meaningful minimum obstacle clearance, toe grip, supported landing, unseen layouts or biological accuracy follows.

The first attempt was interrupted before completion after an observer gate omission; its exit -2 and partial files remain in `aborted-observer-disabled-20261126`. The complete control uses the corrected trace settings. `compare.py` restores the previously pushed course log by streaming its Git objects and requires their availability. Both raw state replay and compressed replay originate from the same native rollout; the compressed replay and state trace are retained here.

# Phase-lead smoothing screen: all rejected

All three candidates failed the predeclared exact pre-walk comparison in all eight lanes. The research interpolation was active from startup rather than only during the walk, so the phase-lead comparisons are confounded by changed pre-gait states. No causal claim about phase lead alone is justified.

At 5, 10 and 20 ms lead, both-feet-unloaded substep counts were 0, 283 and 497, versus 30 in the sham. The smallest candidate-to-control lane travel ratios were 0.557, 0.414 and 0.821 respectively, each below the 0.90 gate. Despite lower boundary and whole-substep knee/ankle motion proxies, none is an acceptable walking improvement. No observation here qualifies the crow's gait or compares it with measured American-crow stride kinematics. A separate follow-up is being prepared to keep the physical state unchanged before walking.

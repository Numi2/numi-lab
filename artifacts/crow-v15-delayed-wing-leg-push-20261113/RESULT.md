# Delayed-wing leg push: short hop, recontact, then flight

Both paired 500-interval trials ran without reported physical failure, observer errors, missing contact, leg, toe or launch-aero substeps. Unchanged lanes matched exactly through 500; candidate lanes matched through 368; the compared wing targets matched. Whole-body COM kinematics used the recorded and hash-identified FK executable, with a successful translation-velocity check.

With wings commanded to start at 7.56 s, both push lanes first had three unloaded intervals at 375–377, but recontacted at 378, then became unloaded again at 384 and 382 respectively. During 371–377 the modeled net vertical aerodynamic impulse was -0.000439 and -0.000441 N s, and maximum flap displacement was 0.00190 rad; COM vertical velocity at release was +0.291 and +0.290 m/s. The held-leg controls first had three unloaded intervals at 384 after wing onset and remained unloaded through 500. The measured hop is therefore consistent with a leg-driven initial release in this modeled setup, but the recontact fails the registered continuous-transition criterion. Full-push-minus-held foot-normal impulse over 371–390 was 0.0223 and 0.0237 N s; that window includes recontact and wing effects and must not be treated as isolated leg impulse.

These are paired lanes on one used seed, not a robustness test, biological validation, flight qualification or landing. See analysis.json for exact values and limits. Raw observations and executed inputs are retained; compressed window extracts are reproducible with extract.py and are not archived as duplicate data.

# Retrospective stance-foot separation

In the baseline, medium anticipatory closure, and medium return-placement trials, the right stance foretoe had a small modeled overlap at zero-based interval 209; at interval 212 its closest foretoe-box gap was respectively 1.37, 1.46 and 1.82 mm. At interval 220 the gaps were 4.82, 4.81 and 5.24 mm, while the right foot center moved from approximately x=28.3 mm at 209 to x=36.4–36.9 mm at 220. The stance rear toe still had negative modeled gap at those samples. Positive front-normal endpoint samples after return were infrequent across all cases.

These are recorded-pose box/perch calculations and accepted last-substep contact samples; they do not prove causation, friction failure or a complete absence of force during the full interval. The result motivates testing stance placement and support before releasing the other foot. The full raw traces remain in their respective Git evidence directories.

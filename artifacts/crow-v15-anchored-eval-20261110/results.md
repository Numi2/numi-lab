# Anchored actor teacher-off development results

The fixed actor was evaluated from full starts without a teacher on the same used J and I seeds. Neither completed the course.

| Layout | Reached | First fatal contact | Minimum sampled clearance |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| J | 2/5 | Left wing / slalom A at step 2248 | 0 m |
| I | 2/5 | Head / slalom B at step 2409 | 0 m |

The anchor restored two-waypoint progress relative to the second actor's one waypoint on each course. It did not improve the first actor's waypoint count; unlike that actor, which timed out on I with no fatal contact, this one struck I's slalom. These outcomes are not successes. Full first-contact position, attitude, velocities and high-level policy outputs are retained in route-diagnostics.json; those outputs are not measurements of actual low-level actuator efforts. After the measured impulse-free window, maximum toe angles were 0.000563 rad on J and 0.000422 rad on I. Neither landed or demonstrated autonomous toe release. Final K/L remain unused.

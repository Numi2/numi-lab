# Damped perch approach: no arrival

On the used training course with privileged teacher control, all eight first episodes reached five waypoint criteria and survived through 3800 intervals. No lane made perch contact or achieved supported landing, so this candidate is rejected for this trial horizon. Substep coverage was complete with no reported contact-capacity overflow. In lane-one replay the bird remained airborne roughly 0.14 m behind the target near the end of the run; this single trajectory illustrates slow arrival and is not a general equilibrium measurement.

The candidate changed position and velocity gains of the bounded final approach and corrected the position-error inversion for its post-contact balance term. The frozen flight law was not changed. Matched prefix comparison and all failures are retained. This is not teacher-off autonomy, biological validation, or a reserved-layout test.

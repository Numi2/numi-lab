# Isolated revised-body grip and release

All 32 matched starts completed the 150-step isolated test without a reported solver failure. With contact-driven toe closure, 8/8 starts on each of the two cross-sections produced at least three consecutive accepted samples with opposing front/rear impulses on both feet; neither set of eight open-toe controls did. In every closure start the bilateral opposing-contact condition was absent after the commanded release began. This is a test from already-supported starts, not a landing.

The toe-only terminal-support threshold held over the final 25 samples in 2/8 original-section and 5/8 square-section closure starts, so the preregistered 6/8 requirement was not met. Some remaining weight may be carried by the soles, but this probe recorded toe contacts only; it cannot determine total foot support or rule out nonfoot contact. Several failed toe-support cases also show a substantial root-height loss or pitch. Full stable support, deliberate release with verified nonfoot clearance, and the continuous autonomous sequence remain unqualified. The 0.4 m isolated perch length differs from the 1.44 m route perch; both top surfaces were at the same initial height.

# Rear-toe preload and contact-motion screen

The substep observer found substantial rear-toe normal loading in the used walking baseline. Keep the baseline .30 stroke, 2 Hz leg/toe phases, .40 crouch and other physical parameters fixed; use two .05 preload controls and paired rear-toe preloads 0, .025 or .10 during the existing contact-conditioned walking phase. Require exact controls/prefix, at least .05 m forward movement in 4–6 s, no both-feet-unloaded substeps, toe-speed caps or nonfoot impulse, and at least 25% reduction on each foot in the normal-impulse-weighted contact-speed integral versus the matched baseline. Use passive substep observations and report segment loads. These preloads are uncalibrated model hypotheses and this is not a tracked-point slip or full-gait qualification.

# Rear-toe preload: rejected as a contact-motion fix

Two controls matched the six-second reference; all prefixes matched through four seconds, including 1,600 states and 25,600 contact substeps. The validated substep observer and the same eight used starts were used.

Preloads 0, .025 and .10 produced .127–.136 m forward progress in the 4–6 s window. The normal-impulse-weighted contact-speed integral ratios versus the matching baseline were .82–1.08, .94–.97 and .97–1.07 respectively across feet and starts, so none approached the required 25% reduction for both feet. Zero preload had two and three toe-cap substeps, .025 had one in one start, and .10 briefly unloaded both feet for one substep in each start. No nonfoot impulse occurred.

These values are rejected for the stated gate. The speed integral is a changing-contact proxy rather than tracked slip, and the physical toe parameters are not calibrated to a real crow.

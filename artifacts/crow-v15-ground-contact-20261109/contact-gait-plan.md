# Contact-gated v15 gait: exploratory, pre-registered

Question: can bounded position targets gated by the preceding accepted solver foot loads produce a nontrivial walking step on the existing v15 body? Use two simultaneous lanes, band 1, same 350 x 20 ms horizon, four substeps, actor, model, observer, seed and exposed course as walk-band baseline. The experimental shader changes only band-1 leg targets; the frozen V1 source is unchanged. Beginning after two seconds, a 0.5 s alternating carrier requests a swing only when the other foot measured >0.05 N in the preceding update. The control arm is identical except both feedback readings are forced to zero. Other body forces and the solver remain unchanged. The feedback uses compact solver contact force, not the geometric animation or a contact oracle. This simple clock-plus-contact rule is a first experiment, not a claimed state machine or trained controller.

Prediction and gate for the observed lane 1: >=0.05 m net horizontal displacement; each foot has positive ground impulse; at least one left-only and one right-only load sample; no reported physical failure or failed simulator step. The feedback-zero control should produce no commanded gait and less travel than the candidate. Retain both arms, all failures, source patches, metrics and bindings. Contact traces are sampled at accepted step endpoints; they cannot establish integrated force, continuous slip, stability across unseen starts, or takeoff/landing. Missing or censored data cannot pass. A result on this exposed course is not held-out validation. No control or body is frozen based on this experiment.

# Walking-to-navigation spawn alignment: single developmental hypothesis

The initial full-route gait transfer failed in both lanes on both already exposed layouts, at the right-wing/gate or slalom contact. The walking segment translated the launch state approximately 0.052 m forward relative to the frozen navigation training start. Test one explicit hypothesis: moving the starting position 0.052 m backward along world x, before simulation starts, lets the same physical walk reach approximately the original launch location. The walking and flight actions, actor, body, obstacles and dynamics are otherwise unchanged. There are no mid-episode position edits or resets in the first episode. This changes the initial condition and must not be conflated with the earlier navigation qualification.

Run the same two lanes, 3000 control steps, exposed layouts and seeds as full-handoff-plan.md. Evaluate only first episodes: five waypoints, no physical failure or failed environment steps, and 25 terminal foot-only supported perch samples per lane. Retain collisions and first-failure traces, and do not infer transfer to untested starts or toe grip. These experiments are not held-out validation.

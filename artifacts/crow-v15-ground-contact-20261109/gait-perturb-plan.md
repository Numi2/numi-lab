# Perturbed-start walking screen

After the initial gait and development-course screens, run the same contact-gated walking law in eight simultaneous lanes with deterministic episode-seeded perturbations: up to 0.06 rad initial roll, up to 0.04 rad on each of six leg joints and up to 0.025 m/s on each horizontal root velocity. The perturbations apply to the initial physical state, not kinematic replay. Use 350 x 20 ms steps, four substeps, existing exposed training course and seed 20261126. Contact-load and actor/controller code otherwise match the previously fingerprinted live-feedback shader. Save the complete physical result, patch, source and contact traces. Lane 1 replay is retained; the per-lane endpoint contact observer provides q at the earliest and final loaded samples for every lane.

Each lane must retain first and last accepted ground-contact samples no later than step 10 and at step 350, progress >=0.05 m in the world-horizontal plane between these samples, exhibit both left-only and right-only positive-load samples, incur no nonfoot ground contact and no physical failure. There must be zero failed environment steps. Measure maximum sampled tilt from quaternion and report per-lane displacement and initial roll, rather than assume a seed changes the body. This is a bounded development test; success alone is not certification of biological realism, terrain generalization, continuous contact-point slip, takeoff, or grip.

# Gait candidate replication, preregistered after first screening

Run the exact previously fingerprinted live-feedback shader on three additional development configurations: training/20261122, reserved-final-i/20261123, and reserved-final-j/20261124. These layouts are all previously exposed; this is robustness screening, not held-out qualification. Each configuration uses two lanes and observes lane 1 over 350 steps. Retain every run and report completed steps, physical errors, root displacement and height/tilt range, each foot contact count, one-foot-only contact counts, foot-center clearance range and the distribution of planar foot-center speed while solver-reported ground load is positive. Contact-center velocity is a conservative diagnostic, not the velocity at the actual contact point; toe geometry and contact slip are not resolved. Displacement >=0.05 m, both exclusive-load patterns, zero physical errors and 350 observed steps are necessary, not sufficient, for a walk. Do not assert static friction or sustained stable locomotion from this gate. Flight controller remains frozen; this test does not cover launch, landing or articulated grip.

# Contact-gated gait to autonomous flight: developmental handoff screen

Pre-register transfer of the existing live-feedback gait to difficulty band 10, the v15 navigation run. Keep its timed 2-5 s walking window, its accepted-foot-load gating, all flight outputs and the frozen flight source unchanged; this is a distinct experimental Metal library, not the frozen controller. Use the original navigation actor, teacher off, no reset, two lanes, 350 steps, four substeps, on already exposed courses reserved-final-i (seed 4038889268) and reserved-final-j (seed 20261124). Observe lane 1 replay and both lanes contact endpoints.

Necessary screen: each lane has alternating left-only and right-only ground load and no nonfoot ground contact, the observed lane moves >=0.05 m horizontally before the end of the walking window, both feet subsequently release and the bird climbs above 0.5 m by the end of the run, with no physical failures or failed environment steps. Preserve failures. These previously used courses are not held out. This first screen does not test all waypoints, final landing, grip, continuous slip or perturbed walking-to-flight starts; any later full-route test requires its own preregistration.

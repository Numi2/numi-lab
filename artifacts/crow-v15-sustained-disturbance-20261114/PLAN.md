# Sustained bounded pitch-disturbance development test

The measured +/-0.020 N m pulse for 0.5 s produced a roughly 0.01 rad pitch response, below the registered measurable-response gate; during the forcing period the pitch difference was still growing. Test the same existing bounded external actuator for 2.0 s, rather than increasing its physical torque limit. This is a sustained in-flight test disturbance, not bird control or hidden assistance; it does not replace disturbed-start qualification. The pre-aerodynamic observer is widened to intervals 895–1020. First run a no-disturbance control and require exact archived first-episode q/v/action/contact and failure parity, expected inputs and zero applied wrenches in all lanes and observed substeps.

Then use opposite +/-1 normalized pitch requests for 100 control intervals beginning at episode tick 900 in candidate lanes 6 and 7. Require exactly 1600 measured active substeps per candidate, correct signs, maximum 0.020001 N m, zero force, no observed pulse outside the registered observation window, zero other-lane wrench, exact matched prefix and unchanged-lane parity, continuous candidate flight and all existing physical, teacher-off and observer checks. In the 150 intervals after onset, require at least 0.04 rad pitch or 0.10 rad/s world-y angular-rate difference relative to each matched control; at interval 1200 require absolute pitch difference below 0.06 rad and rate difference below 0.15 rad/s. Preserve complete raw runs, controls and failures. A pass would be a limited used-seed sustained-disturbance screen, not general recovery, perturbed-start qualification, navigation or landing.

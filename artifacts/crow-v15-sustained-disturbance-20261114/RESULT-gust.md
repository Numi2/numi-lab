# Sustained pitch disturbance: response threshold failed

On used development seed 20261127, both candidate lanes ran all 1,800 first-episode intervals with continuous recorded flight from first full unloading and passed the registered parity, contact, effort, speed, height, teacher-off, wrench and observer-coverage checks. Both received the expected opposite-sign 0.020 N m pitch torque for exactly 1,600 substeps, with no applied force or observed out-of-window wrench. Unchanged lanes matched the control, including its failures at steps 760 and 762.

Both candidate screens failed their preregistered measurable-response gate: peak absolute pitch differences were 0.03554 and 0.03515 rad, below 0.04 rad; peak world-y angular-rate differences were 0.08034 and 0.09862 rad/s, below 0.10 rad/s. Differences at the later evaluation point met the specified recovery bounds, but this cannot qualify recovery because the disturbance response was below the minimum. The original failed analysis is retained unchanged.

A supplementary accepted-frame timing recheck gives the same peak responses and gate failures. Independent native replay-to-observer comparison for environment 5 in both arms demonstrates the one-interval q/v convention; application to candidate environments 6 and 7 is inferred from the shared observer and has not been directly verified by candidate replays. These are correlated lanes on one used seed under a sustained in-flight test torque, not perturbed-start qualification, frozen flight, leg-powered takeoff, navigation, landing, or biological validation.

# Rear-toe preload at push-off

The coordinated handoff retained contact in the 10.0–10.3 s transition, but both rear proximal toes struck the configured -12 rad/s limit at step 552 during the push, with endpoint positions near their extension limit. Test whether changing physical rear-toe position targets while the foot is loaded can avoid that event. Use the same late gait fade (10.30–10.55 s), continued crouch and support-conditioned toe handling uniformly in all eight used seed-20261126 lanes. Lanes 0–1 use no extra preload; lanes 2–7 use target maxima 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.40 and 0.50 rad respectively. Apply a smooth 10.50–10.80 s onset and require the corresponding accepted physical foot load; stop at 11.15 s. These values are exploratory engineering controls, not measured crow targets, and no speed limit, contact force or root force is altered. Compare exact same-lane state and contact records through step 485 with the prior coordinated run; full toe/contact coverage and no solver/overflow/nonfoot ground impulses are prerequisites. Require no toe caps through step 575, no unloaded substeps at steps 501–515, positive initial launch impulse and no foot load at the end. Report all arms and timing. The test does not measure muscle work, true slip, crow toe forces, qualified flight or full mission. Separate matched baseline and second-seed replication are required before promotion.

## Control correction

Source inspection after this screen found that the branch applies max(proximal, preload * ramp) even when preload is zero. Thus a loaded rear toe with a negative walking target is clamped to zero between 10.50 and 11.15 s; the zero-target lanes are an active toe-target floor, not untreated controls. It also means the +0.05/+0.10 lanes differ from their earlier reference by the floor as well as the positive target. Their first difference at step 527 is consistent with the target branch becoming active but does not isolate positive preload. A true no-change control must bypass the branch altogether when preload is zero. All observations remain valid; neither the floor nor positive preload has been causally qualified.

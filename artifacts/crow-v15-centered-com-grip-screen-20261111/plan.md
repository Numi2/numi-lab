# Centered whole-body balance during held grip

In the matched-balance held-grip development replay, measured COM x drifted from 0.0249 m at tick 100 to 0.0408 m by tick 280, beyond the 0.025 m half-width of the perch top, while body damping opposed rotation. Test the geometric hypothesis that progressively targeting the center of the perch, rather than a fixed root position, improves support: after hold tick 10, blend the existing hip position error over 100 ticks toward minus the actual articulated COM x, with the same bounded hip and ankle, toe and leg laws. No state resets, external force, increased damping, altered body or perch. Require exact parity through tick 85, 1,000 simulated ticks, terminal 100 samples of bilateral grip and support, speed/tilt and nonfoot-contact gates, and no grip overlap exceeding 0.5 mm. Compute COM through host observation for this research only. A prior COM target of +0.015 m in an opening-toe trial worsened support; this separate centered target and held-toe configuration is a different, prespecified counterfactual, not proof of improvement. One development start, no final layouts.

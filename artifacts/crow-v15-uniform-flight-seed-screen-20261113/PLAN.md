# Preregistered uniform-candidate two-seed straight-flight screen

Two paired groups passed an exploratory forward-flight screen with different lane-specific sweep gains and flap-onset times on one used seed. Before promotion, test each of those gains in an environment-independent candidate configuration on two development seeds. Six candidate environments (lanes 2–7) use the same 7.44 s onset and gain within each run; lanes 0–1 remain the explicit no-push/wing controls and are not counted as candidate successes. Their launch body state and toe control continue to arise from the same v15 research-body dynamics. This is not final flight qualification.

Run both fixed-gain candidates K=.08 and K=.12, with the same physical target law clamp(-.04+K*(.35-yaw-frame speed),-.09,.025), flap and pronation settings, research v15 body and aero, active ground, disabled course shapes, policy fingerprints and 16 substeps; use seeds 20261126 and 20261127 in separate full 900-step runs. Both are development seeds, not the reserved final tests. Keep the 7.44 s onset fixed in all candidate lanes and both candidate laws; this matches the earlier K=.08 lane 4–5 law at commit 9c3854f4054c963276fd3715427c42784ea1bec6 for seed 20261126, which must reproduce q/v/action/contact histories for their whole first episodes before treating the comparison as equivalent. K=.12 uses a different onset than in the prior screen, so do not claim exact parity for that variant. Validate each full-blend target against accepted quaternion and velocity and its timing, masks and policy bytes, full trace coverage and actuator bounds where observed.

Use the prior per-lane 900-tick development thresholds without revision: no foot recontact after release, no nonfoot normal impulse, at most one toe-speed event, no sampled sweep stop, height .25–1.5 m throughout ticks 500–900, world-x speed 0–1 m/s throughout ticks 550–900, and >.5 m world-x progress between 500 and 900. Require all six candidate lanes to pass on each of both seeds for a candidate to pass this screen. Report all 24 outcomes, failed gates and observer limitations. Do not treat lanes sharing a seed as independent replicates. Neither a pass nor selection constitutes a frozen or qualified controller; later require longer-duration straight flight, left/right turns, climb/descent and perturbed-start recovery, then navigation and a contact-supported landing, on appropriately reserved inputs. Final layouts/seeds remain untouched.

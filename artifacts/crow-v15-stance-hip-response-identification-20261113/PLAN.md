# Identify supported stance-hip response

The stricter supported-return trial shows COM-relative posture already outside the return-placement gate, while the stance foot remains loaded. Rather than guessing the sign of a stabilizing feedback law, use the same constructed stance-preposition state and apply +0.03 versus -0.03 rad right-hip target pulses for up to four 20 ms intervals, only after at least three clearance intervals and while opposing stance-toe contact is measured. All other controls, limits, materials and forces are unchanged. Both lanes start identically and the 260-interval diagnostic is teacher-free, with 32 physics substeps per interval.

Verify q/v equality before the first pulse, actual target differences, contact and substep coverage. Measure the signed paired COM-relative-to-stance-foot position and velocity at onset and over the following ten intervals, while reporting support loss, posture, any toe contacts and native solver errors. If the states diverge before the pulse, support is lost, observations are incomplete, or response is inconsistent, do not infer a usable feedback sign. This is local system identification on a constructed perch state, not a successful release, landing or biological claim.

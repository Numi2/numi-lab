# Result

Thirteen of 16 privileged development runs survived to 4500 steps with all five waypoint regions reached and no recorded solver failure. No bilateral averaged-load streak exceeded three control steps, no run entered landed mode, and no sampled bilateral opposing toe grip appeared; the preregistered loading and grip predictions failed, so this reflex is not promoted. The all-substep normal-impulse sensor classified 20,288 and 22,385 side-step samples after 25 consecutive impulse-free steps; the largest toe angles were 0.000589 and 0.000582 rad respectively. This is a bounded release observation conditional on the research sensor, not a deliberate release from a sustained grip, an all-substep contact-geometry clearance proof, or full navigation qualification. Endpoint-only absence analyses show larger post-impact motion because they miss intermediate substep contacts, and are retained for comparison.

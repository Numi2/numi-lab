# Result: stronger gains did not qualify landing

All eight teacher-guided development starts reached all five waypoints and physically touched the perch. Four first episodes failed before 4200 intervals and four remained active at cutoff; those survivors are censored, not passes. One failed lane showed 328 consecutive intervals (6.56 seconds) of bilateral opposing toe impulses, upright low-speed posture and at least half nominal weight in upward perch impulse before contact termination. The nominal-weight version of the same sampled criterion lasted at most five consecutive intervals in any lane; instantaneous impulses alone cannot rule out aerodynamic assistance or prove sustained full weight bearing.

Seven of eight first episodes hit the toe velocity limit after ground handoff; lane 1 hit it at 945 substeps. No recorded first-episode hits occurred from handoff through the interval before first scene contact. No substeps were missing and no execution errors were detected. Gains and starts are confounded; all runs match their aligned-descent q, v and policy actions over the tested pre-contact prefix. The screen is teacher-guided, development-only, and does not test explicit release. These gains are not promoted and no held-out autonomous completion is claimed.

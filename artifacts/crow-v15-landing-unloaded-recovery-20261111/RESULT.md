# Contact-loss recovery did not prevent landing failures

All eight teacher-off first episodes reached five waypoint criteria on the used development course. Lanes 0 and 6 fell at steps 3475 and 3798; only lane 5 met the final 25-interval bilateral opposed toe-support criterion. Lane 6 had 154 consecutive qualifying support intervals before falling. Lanes 0 and 6 were still loading feet while pitch increased before falling. No lane transitioned from landing hold back to approach during its first episode: sampled phase reached at most 2.06, short of 2.20. Thus the no-load, descending timer was interrupted and did not test whether an actual return to approach could recover a fall. This candidate is not an improvement and is rejected for the current sequence.

Compared with the localized-route reference, accepted endpoint state, policy output or contacts differed at only 3, 5, 3 and 3 sampled endpoints in lanes 0, 5, 6 and 7, respectively; the other four matched throughout their common first episodes. These comparisons do not establish matching at every substep. Flight observer recorded four toe speed-cap substeps near landing in lane 6 and initial settling caps in lanes 1 and 2; no logged crow self-contact after step 380, no missing or duplicate substeps, no capacity overflow and no substep errors. Results are not unused-layout evidence, biological qualification or continuous-clearance proof.

# Calibrated touchdown: no supported landing

The first 1305 q/v samples matched the matched-launch teacher and all five development-J waypoints were reached. Full perch-substep records were present without overflow or observer error. A first foot/toe impulse occurred at step 2268, but there were no bilateral half-weight supported intervals, no opposed grip and no nonfoot perch impulse. The bird remained in the descent/alignment phase and the episode timed out; there were no failed native environment steps. The toe-overlap criterion could not be evaluated without an opposed interval.

The selected hover offset did not by itself establish physical landing, toe release or reacquisition. This is one privileged development trial, not an autonomous or held-out result. Its descent progress repeatedly decreased when the strict alignment guard was not met; a separate trial is needed to test whether pausing rather than retracting is useful.

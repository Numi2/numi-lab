# Student-state I recoveries

Both teacher-assisted recoveries exactly matched the anchored student's accepted states, observations and actions up to takeover (1,849 and 2,449 frames). The original-I recovery reached waypoint five at step 2,794, maintained 0.075804 m minimum sampled obstacle clearance, and ended with 1,270 consecutive bilateral support samples. The harder-I recovery reached waypoint five at step 2,832, maintained 0.101486 m minimum sampled clearance, and ended with 2,837 consecutive bilateral support samples. Neither had a recorded physical, solver, or nonfoot-perch failure; both qualify under the preregistered development routing-prefix gate.

These are teacher-assisted development recoveries, not autonomous or held-out results. Only pre-waypoint-five routing labels may be used; landing labels are excluded. Clearance and support are sampled at accepted control steps, not continuously certified.

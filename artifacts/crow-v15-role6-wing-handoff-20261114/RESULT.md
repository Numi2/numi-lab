# Smooth handoff to role-6 wing law: failed

This full-start development diagnostic did not pass the 800-interval continuity screen. All eight first episodes ended on contact (reason 3) at intervals 631, 653, 677, 708, 594, 679, 663 and 715. No lane reached the horizon. The six .75–.85-rad launch lanes first recorded foot recontact at 531, 631, 688, 531, 631 and 688; the two .70-rad lanes first recontacted at 396 and 389. Six of eight lanes reached the 12-rad/s toe-speed threshold. Positive lower-beak/ground contact is observed one recorded index after each metadata endpoint. Because metadata and contact epochs are not independently aligned, those contacts do not by themselves establish the fatal pair.

Every lane's recorded state through interval 500 and logged targets over intervals 335–500 exactly matched the unchanged handoff control. Substep contact and toe records and state/target coverage were complete through each metadata endpoint; no observer errors or nonfinite state/target values were recorded. The blend transplanted only the prior role-6 wing target law, not its entire flight or launch controller. Its earlier success in six different launch episodes therefore cannot be transferred to this trial. That earlier common-role run also used an experimental flap-drive law only in lanes 2–7, as documented in the prior launch findings; the current trial uses that drive law in all lanes.

Full source, compiled metallib, binary/input hashes, replay, recorded observations, analysis and the exact original compressed trace split into manifest-verified ordered parts are retained. The eight lanes share a used seed and are correlated. Neither flight, toe grip, landing, the full mission, nor a genuinely unused layout has been qualified. This trial is rejected; it does not establish biological realism.

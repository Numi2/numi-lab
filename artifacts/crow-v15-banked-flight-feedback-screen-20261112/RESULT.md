# Bounded bank-feedback flight screen

All six lanes completed the 1,600-interval obstacle-free development run without a reported first-episode physical failure, and all 48 pre-intervention sampled states and policy actions exactly matched the same-lane baseline. Recorded contact coverage was complete, with no logged contact overflow, and no ground contact or toe-speed cap from interval 400 onward.

Against the matched baseline, final absolute heading error improved only 0.61%, 1.33%, 1.04%, 2.30%, 0.96% and 2.87% in lanes 0–5 respectively; the predeclared requirement was at least 15% in both turn directions. Sampled post-intervention roll remained within 0.049 rad and sampled height within 0.928–1.010 m, but those sparse samples cannot prove continuous limits. Reject the candidate for inadequate steering. It does not qualify flight, landing, or any unseen layout, and the pre-9-second controller still includes direct body moments.

# Bounded bank-feedback flight screen

Open-loop differential flapping showed a small bank response at moderate input and loss of flight at the strongest setting. Test whether live roll and body angular-rate feedback can produce a bounded bank without the two identified direct yaw/pitch moment channels. This is a diagnostic feedback candidate, not a qualified or frozen controller.

The candidate shares the exact six starts, seed, policy, goal commands and model with the retained baseline from `crow-v15-differential-wingbeat-screen-20261112`. From 9 s it adds an opposite wingbeat residual driven by heading error, accepted roll and body angular velocity. The three bilateral +/-90 degree lane pairs use maximum bank targets of 0.04, 0.06 and 0.08 rad; the normalized added residual is clamped to ±0.28. This is a proposed engineering control, not measured crow kinematics. Launch before 9 s retains the old law and potentially direct moments.

Require exact pre-intervention sampled q/v/actions for each lane against the retained baseline. Record all first-episode terminations, ground and toe events, substep coverage and sampled heading, roll, height and velocity; exclude later continuation. Report all pairs and compare against their exact same-lane baseline. A control candidate cannot pass if either matched direction physically fails, has incomplete evidence, exceeds a 0.2-rad sampled roll or departs the engineering altitude window 0.65–1.15 m after the intervention. Seek at least a 15% reduction in final absolute heading error in both directions; a favorable result still requires tighter tracking, perturbed starts, climb/descent and recovery qualification, credible aerodynamic validation, and full autonomous navigation. No final layout is consumed.

# Six-second continuous walking screen

Extend the identical physical walking and phase-led toe commands from 4-6 seconds to 4-10 seconds; extend airborne hold and postpone the launch crouch. Retain the same physical body, substeps, contact gates, no-toe sham, and 0.5 s leg period. This is an isolated walking diagnostic, not an attempt at launch or navigation. Before examining outcomes, run eight matched lanes on the already-used seed 20261127 with no intermediate resets.

Require exact matched prewalk states and contact substeps; complete endpoint and contact-substep records for every lane; no recorded errors, overflow or non-foot contacts during the walking window; candidate unloaded substeps no more than its paired control in each lane; at least ten support-qualified clearance bouts per foot in every candidate lane; strictly positive forward displacement and at least 90% of paired sham in every lane; and aggregate sole-clear/toe-low endpoint count no more than 80% of sham. Record joint velocity caps wherever observed and report coverage rather than claiming uninstrumented substeps. Audit full-run episode resets and relevant forces, and treat unexpected modes or missing data as failure. These are engineering thresholds, not measured crow targets or evidence of biological fidelity. If it passes, repeat on seed 20261126 and proceed toward takeoff and landing integration only after further review.

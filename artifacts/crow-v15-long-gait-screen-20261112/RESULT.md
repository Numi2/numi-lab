# Six-second continuous gait: failed

On used development seed 20261127, each arm ran all 500 steps without recorded episode reset or physical-failure flag. Prefix state/contact samples matched exactly through step 200; replay validation and all 300 walking-window endpoint and contact-substep records were complete. Every candidate foot had at least eleven support-qualified clearance bouts and no recorded toe-speed caps in the full 4800 substeps per lane. No walking-window non-foot contacts or reported errors occurred.

The intervention failed its preregistered gates. Lane 0 achieved 87.1% of matched sham forward travel; candidate no-foot-contact substeps exceeded sham in lanes 0, 2 and 6. Sole-clear/toe-low endpoints were 97 versus 95 in the sham, worse than the required <=80%. Do not promote. The no-foot-contact condition is a contact-impulse threshold and may include transient contact fluctuations; it alone is not proof of a fall. All eight lanes first encountered it at step 305; post-hoc phase analysis shows all such events in the middle two-second section. Root forward speed was only about 0.01-0.02 m/s in most two-second sections, far below the speed range discussed in a related-corvid treadmill study; this does not set a species-specific minimum, but it exposes a serious performance/biological-comparability gap.

The shader still changes actions 0-6 at six seconds, and the retained body trace shows wing/tail joint motion at that transition. Therefore the trial cannot distinguish cumulative toe effects from a simultaneous body-control transition. An isolated matched transition experiment is needed before concluding causality. The first attempted long run incorrectly requested only 300 steps; it is retained with SETUP-ERROR.md and is not used in the result.

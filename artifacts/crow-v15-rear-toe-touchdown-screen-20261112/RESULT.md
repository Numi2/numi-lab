# Rear-toe touchdown screen: not admitted

All eight first episodes completed 300 intervals with no reported physical failures or logged contact overflow. The zero-preload lane 4 reproduced its previous 0.4-rad crouch candidate exactly and all lanes matched before crouch onset. Several preload settings on individual starts avoided toe velocity caps during walking; the 0.2-rad lane 3 and 0.4-rad lane 6 had zero recorded caps or both-feet-unloaded substeps across preparation and walking, at least three supported-clearance bouts per foot, and six recorded complete rear-first toe release runs with no missing toe group. They traveled 158 and 152 mm respectively in two seconds. These are single different starts and different controls, not paired evidence of a treatment effect.

The whole screen fails: three lanes lost accepted foot normal load during preparation, a fourth had brief losses, and two had toe speed caps. These preparation events precede the toe preload, which begins at 4 s. Across all eight different preload settings, endpoint contact-point speed median/p95 was 0.00332/0.05890 m/s versus 0.00276/0.03484 held; this pooled comparison misses the predeclared p95 criterion and is not integrated sliding. Some contact fragments lack toe groups. The sampled 0.2 and 0.4 settings warrant matched control runs on all starts and further preparation stabilization; neither is promoted or established as a biological toe position. Full landing and flight remain unqualified.

# Paired knee-ankle swing clearance screen

Frozen-pose geometry showed that rotating the ankle alone can exchange rear-toe drag for front-toe drag, whereas a knee increase paired with the opposite ankle rotation can lift all toe boxes. Test whether this can occur under real contact and actuator dynamics. Both arms use the same small supporting-leg stroke and held upper body. In the candidate only, when the opposite foot is supported during the positive swing carrier, add position-target offsets of +0.20 rad knee and -0.20 rad ankle at peak carrier, smoothly ramped with the existing gait ramp. Use the actual 0.75 and 0.65 actuator scales. These are engineering targets motivated by model geometry, not measured corvid joint trajectories. No added force, state correction, or extra toe target is used.

Before inspecting results, run paired eight-lane, 300-step, 16-substep no-reset tests on previously used development seed 20261127 with identical binary and weights apart from the research switch. Require full first episodes, exact pre-walk state and contact parity, no physics errors, overflow, nonfoot contact or toe speed caps; at least three supported-clearance bouts per foot in every lane; no increase in both-feet-zero-normal-impulse substeps against the no-extra-stroke held control in any lane; at least 90% of that control forward travel in every lane; and sole-clear/toe-low endpoint counts <=120% and loaded contact-point median and p95 speeds <=1.5x that control. Report also the matched small-stroke sham and achieved angles. These conservative engineering screens do not establish real-bird gait. A passing result still requires independent starts and longer tests.

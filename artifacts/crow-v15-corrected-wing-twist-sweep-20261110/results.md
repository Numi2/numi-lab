# Bounded wing twist screen

The estimated wing body and research-only shoulder-aligned force model were tested for 1200 steps with fixed twist offsets -0.50, -0.75 and -1.00 after six seconds; each arm used the same eight lanes and seed, no obstacles, fixed head, zero actor and an experimental copy of the flight law. Each arm had 0/8 physical failures and no solver failures. That is a short survival result, not flight qualification. For illustration, lane 0 at step 1200 had heights / forward world speeds of 0.404 m / 0.965 m/s (-0.50), 0.453 m / 0.751 m/s (-0.75), and 0.926 m / -2.229 m/s (-1.00). The first two were too low and the third traveled backward; the commanded cruise was 0.85 m and 0.70 m/s. The estimated body and aerodynamic closure are not biologically calibrated.

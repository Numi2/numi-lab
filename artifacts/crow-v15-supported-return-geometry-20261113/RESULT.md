# Supported return placement reached target but failed to grip

The trial and control had identical q/v through 220 pre-placement states. The trial returned the cleared foot with measured opposing stance contact and reached the complete bounded +0.15 rad knee / -0.30 rad ankle target; progress advanced by no more than 0.050001 per 20 ms interval. Both lanes completed 450 intervals with every expected 32-substep contact record and no recorded solver, observer or contact-capacity errors.

Neither lane produced any interval satisfying the combined bilateral opposing-toe, support, tilt and speed grip criteria. The root fell below 0.1 m at interval 306 in the control and 310 in the placement trial. Independent geometry showed the minimum returning foretoe box-to-perch gap at interval 250 was about 13.2 mm in the control and 7.5 mm with placement; neither had a positive foretoe contact normal at any sampled endpoint after return. The trial moved the foretoe closer but did not establish contact or sustained grip. Geometry uses retained poses and box shapes; sampled endpoint normals do not establish whether contact occurred at every intervening substep.

Both trajectories, substep records, commands, executed binary, model inputs and analysis are archived. This constructed stationary release and return is not an airborne landing qualification.

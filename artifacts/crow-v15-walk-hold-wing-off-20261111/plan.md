# Matched takeoff with wing actuation suppressed

On the used development seed, suppress wing actuation after six seconds using the integrated walking recovery, keeping toe, leg and passive aerodynamic dynamics active. Require physical contact and q/v parity with the wing-on integrated trial through consecutive unloaded rising intervals, both-foot upward impulse, no nonfoot ground impulse and sampled clearance. Compare the subsequent rise and wing-on flight. Passive aerodynamic forces remain, so this isolates leg contribution, not biological realism or complete qualification.

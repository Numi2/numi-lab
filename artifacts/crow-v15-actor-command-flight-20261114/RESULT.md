# Actor-command flight handoff: physical screen failed

On the used training seed 20261126 with the teacher off, all eight lanes completed 1800 recorded intervals without later ground contact or toe-speed-cap hits, and matched the preceding same-seed flight through interval 500. However, the registered wing-pronation tracking-error limit of 0.08 rad failed in two lanes: maximum errors were 0.11183 and 0.09360 rad. Those worst substeps were at the modeled 0.15 N m pronation effort cap. One commanded pronation target reversed by 0.12586 rad in one 20 ms interval; other rapid reversals also occurred. Therefore the actor handoff did not pass the registered physical screen. Speed remained fixed; height and turn inputs were clamped.

The command reconstruction compares the observed differential targets with several possible recorded actor-head offsets and observer lags. The best fixed head/lag hypothesis matches many but not all samples; because the actor head can switch by waypoint and observer epochs are not independently aligned, this is not a complete command-provenance proof. The old scripted target, signed turn and height-cycle checks do not apply to the actor stream. Full traces and failed checks are retained. This is not navigation, flight qualification, landing or held-out mission evidence.

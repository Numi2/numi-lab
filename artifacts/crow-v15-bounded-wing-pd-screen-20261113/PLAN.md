# Bounded wing-drive feedback sensitivity: preregistered

The current flap position-drive settings are 800 stiffness and 8 damping, with a model effort limit 120 and an additional experimental effort clamp of 3.0. In the continuous wingbeat test, the 3.0 bound was saturated in over 99% of late substeps, while targets were not tracked and support was insufficient. The original settings and proposed gains are not calibrated crow physiology. Test whether reducing the flap feedback gains can improve tracking and modeled lift without increasing the 3.0 bound.

Use fixed continuous 6.4 Hz wing target and the same v15 body, aerodynamic model, gait, leg commands, no direct pitch/yaw body moments, used seed 20261126, 16 substeps and 400 ticks. Research-only wing gains (kp,kd) for lanes 2–3 are (20,0.6), lanes 4–5 (80,1.2), lanes 6–7 (200,3.0); retain the model's predicted-step proportional error and velocity damping structure, per-lane controller multiplier and bounded effort. The gains are not biological measurements. Applied gains are active throughout, so this is a matched-start sensitivity, not a state-matched intervention against earlier baseline.

Run paired push and active-crouch hold arms, both using identical gain settings. Require complete first episodes, trace coverage, exact state/action/contact parity through tick 345 between paired arms and effort <=3.0. Record COM rise, toe events, support, recontact, nonfoot impulse, early push-vs-hold impulse, wing tracking RMS, saturation, modeled lift and work proxies. Preliminary launch pass requires both lanes of a setting to remain unsupported after a three-tick release through step 400, COM rise >=0.05 m, no nonfoot impulse, <=1 toe threshold substep, continuous support before release and early push-minus-hold impulse >0.05 N s in both windows. A tracking or lift improvement alone is not qualification; no promotion without calibrated energetics, flight envelope, contact, landing and held-out route tests. Preserve all failures.

# Wing-held leg push: failed isolation screen

The matched push and crouched-hold runs used the corrected research-only v15 wing-drive metadata, projected aerodynamic closure, a development layout and seed 20261126. Neutral wing actuators remained active. Push and hold matched through tick 345; both first episodes reached tick 400. The full recorded configurations and checks are retained here.

All six exploratory lanes failed the preregistered isolation screen. The push-minus-hold upward foot impulse was +0.081 to +0.113 N s over ticks 348–355, but only +0.011 to +0.047 N s over ticks 348–357; the required threshold was greater than +0.05 N s in both windows. At tick 365, push-minus-hold whole-body COM vertical velocity was -0.056 to -0.053 m/s, versus the required greater than +0.05 m/s. Peak push flap speeds were 0.786–0.850 rad/s; the absolute modeled vertical aerodynamic impulse in each push was below 0.00035 N s. Remaining wing motion and body aerodynamics mean this is not a wing-free experiment. Foot impulse difference and the effort-displacement proxy are not direct measurements of muscle work.

The result does not establish leg-powered takeoff, flight qualification or biological accuracy. See the companion timeline audit for the transient positive vertical velocity followed by loss of momentum. No held-out layout or seed was consumed.

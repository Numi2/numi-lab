# Preregistered wing-held leg push comparison

Question: does the current v15 bilateral leg extension produce a meaningful ground impulse and upward center-of-mass velocity when wing joints are held to their default positions instead of executing the launch flapping command? The preliminary short launch with wings on cannot isolate this, because its no-leg-extension hold also became unloaded. Starting at 6.30 s, replace flap, sweep and pronation targets for actions 0-5 with their existing neutral default coordinates; keep those actuators active and bounded. This is active wing holding, not removal of wings, passive aero, body momentum or all possible wing forces. Measure residual wing motions and aero forces, and do not claim truly wing-free motion if residuals are large.

Compare push and crouch hold under the same corrected v15 gain metadata, projected research aero closure, used seed 20261126, training layout, eight correlated lanes, 16 substeps and 400 first-episode ticks. No teacher, no direct body pitch/yaw moments, no deliberate reset. Require exact push/hold sampled state/action/contact parity through tick 345 and contact substeps 335-345; compare against the earlier wings-on run only before the wing-hold intervention, not afterwards. Check corrected drive flags/scales, all records and finite states. Record peak wing flap/sweep/pronation velocity, modeled vertical aero impulse, normal foot impulses, contact intervals, COM momentum and height, applied leg effort/displacement proxy, and nonfoot/toe threshold events. For an exploratory leg-push signal, both lanes of a setting must show push-minus-hold upward foot impulse >0.05 Ns over 348-355 and 348-357 and push-minus-hold COM vertical velocity >0.05 m/s at tick 365, while peak flap speed over the window is <=1 rad/s and absolute upward aero impulse <=0.02 Ns. These thresholds are operational isolation criteria, not biological standards. Passing this short counterfactual does not prove flight transition or biological validity; a failure must be archived. No final layout or seed is used.

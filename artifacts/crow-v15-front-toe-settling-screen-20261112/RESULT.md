# Front-toe settling target screen: rejected

None of the twelve candidate lanes across two previously used seeds passed the predeclared joint physical and toe-settling screen. Pooled median hallux-proxy to middle-front-toe loading delays ranged from 0.87 to 1.02 times the same-lane previous values; none met the required 0.85 ratio. Every candidate also failed one or more physical gates. In the first seed, all six candidates had both-feet-unloaded substeps and right-foot sampled contact-speed ratios from 1.64 to 3.16 relative to the uniform baseline. On the second seed, one .10-rad lane stayed within the support and speed limits but lacked the required recovery coverage; the other five failed contact speed or support and other gates. Unwarped control lanes matched their previous reference and all recorded pre-intervention states and contacts matched.

The alteration modifies a target floor only while support is detected; a zero floor is still active and not a no-operation control. Toe loading uses modeled two-segment groups and a 3-substep persistence filter. The ratio is not measured material slip or an anatomical crow validation. Results are correlated development starts at very slow speed. The related-corvid toe order remains an exploratory qualitative target, and neither the original knee/ankle candidate nor any toe change is promoted. Reliable whole-body gait, takeoff, qualified flight, autonomous waypoints, grasp and supported landing remain open.

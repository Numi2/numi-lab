# Substep contact observer

The toe in a used J lane moved from neutral to -0.27 rad shortly after passing within an accepted-sample 2.4 mm separating-axis gap of the perch. Endpoint contact candidates were absent and targets were neutral, so an unobserved intermediate contact is plausible but unproven. Add a read-only diagnostic host that copies native contact constraints and status after each of the four physics substeps in a narrowly bounded control-step window and logs toe/perch pairs. Keep the original body, shader, actor tensors, solver, guide, 8 lanes, seed, and all controls exactly as the saved midheight run. Require exact replay q/v parity over the whole lane-0 first episode and compare final-substep records against the existing endpoint contact record. If parity or status indexing fails, no causal conclusion may be drawn. This is a used development layout and does not qualify navigation or landing. Copies must not modify native buffers or physics state.

# Contact-triggered walking recovery: improved development behavior

On the same used development course with two seeds, all 16 teacher-off starts recorded 15 strictly alternating, qualifying clear-swing bouts per foot over eight seconds and moved forward by approximately 0.15 m; no nonfoot ground impulse was recorded. Seed 20261126 had 19 unsupported substeps and one endpoint with both feet more than 1 mm clear in lane 3, plus three unsupported substeps in lane 6; the other lanes retained measured support. Seed 20261127 had four unsupported substeps in lane 3 and no sampled endpoints with both feet clear. Endpoint clearance is not swept clearance.

Compared with the matched original on seed 20261126, the recovery changed only lanes 3 and 6, beginning at steps 242 and 254. Lane 3 peak upward impulse over steps 210–300 dropped from 0.0821 to 0.0296 N s, and its walking-window toe-speed cap hits dropped from three to zero. No lane hit a toe-speed cap during either walking window; settling hits persist, including on the second seed. This is a development improvement, not qualification on unused layouts, a complete walk-to-flight sequence, or biological validation.

# Differential pronation: fixed-state screen

The zero-perturbation native Metal evaluation matched all 96,768 archived per-body force/torque components exactly. All 4,032 retained states and five matched perturbations were present and within the declared pronation limits. The native source, executed binary, compiled metallib and runtime hashes, full outputs and analysis are retained here or identified by the input manifest.

For each radian of equal-and-opposite pronation offset, the mean modeled yaw moment was -0.01179 and -0.01172 N m/rad in the two bank-turn lanes; the accompanying mean roll moments were -0.1110 and -0.1114 N m/rad. Instantaneous yaw derivatives changed sign throughout the wingbeat (about 64% positive), so the negative mean alone does not describe its phase-dependent behavior. These values reflect fixed-state perturbations at already used development states, not the achieved tracking or yaw of a flying bird. In particular, differential pronation strongly couples to roll here and would require actuator-effort, stability and flight testing; this is not evidence of qualified steering or real-crow aerodynamics. No reserved layouts or seeds were used.

# Fixed-state differential pronation sensitivity

The corrected-axis flight body has failed heading screens with bank and sweep, despite large lateral drift. To assess an alternative physical steering channel before a new dynamical trial, perturb the two wing-pronation positions at the 4,032 retained native bank-turn states used by the prior independently checked aerodynamic observer. The right pronation axis is mirrored to +Y in this research model; evaluate equal-and-opposite offsets (+.02,-.02) and (-.02,+.02), plus equal offsets (+.02,+.02) and (-.02,-.02), in radians, with q indices 11 and 12. Preserve velocities and every other state and physical parameter. Recompute the whole-body center of mass and wing moment arms by native forward kinematics for each perturbed state; dispatch the same archived Metal aerodynamic kernel. Report force and torque vectors about COM and central-difference differential and symmetric yaw and roll moments, by lane and wingstroke sample. Reject states outside the declared .35 rad pronation target limits, nonfinite output, missing or duplicate samples, or a baseline discrepancy over 1e-5 in any one of 96,768 retained wrench components. Record input, binary and library hashes, counts, extrema, and all failures. This evaluates instantaneous modeled aerodynamic response only: it does not test actual joint tracking, actuator saturation, stability, biological accuracy, turning, or full flight. It uses only already-used development states and does not expose the reserved layouts or seeds.

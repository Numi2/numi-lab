# Early flight speed and height sensitivity

The previously screened 0.70 m/s forward command, applied with a 0.50, 0.70 or 0.90 m height target, did not eliminate the post-liftoff backward drift in this used-layout, single-seed diagnostic. At step 700 the respective root velocities were -0.426, -0.536 and -0.654 m/s. The lower target lessened backward speed but did not solve it. The 0.70 m/s obstacle-free flight screen did not include the ground transition; none of these variants is promoted or qualifies the full sequence.

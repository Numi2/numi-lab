# Contact-aware bilateral pitch: rejected

The candidate continued the existing pitch feedback while the returned foot had measured opposed support and applied the correction through that foot's existing hip drive. Same-run states, targets and contact substeps matched the control before the first changed target at zero-based interval 308. All 450 states and required substeps were present, with no observer errors; contact/state epoch alignment was not independently verified.

Both lanes achieved 43 qualified release intervals and only an 11-interval consecutive bilateral supported opposed-toe grip streak. The candidate briefly regained one more isolated qualifying interval but fell at interval 368 versus 370 for the control, and both reached the 12 rad/s toe-speed cap before falling. Neither met the terminal 100-interval grip gate. Reject this candidate and retain the original controller; this stationary test does not establish walking or landing.

# Eight-second walking comparison video

The four quadrants are native replay renders of the earlier 0.75-second-cycle development walking test, seed 20261126, frames 201–600 (the 4–12 second walking window). The top two show lane 1, where the contact observer reported 34 consecutive substeps of zero normal foot support across steps 221–223. The bottom two show lane 5, for which that observer reported no both-feet-unloaded substeps in the same window. Side and front are different views of the same saved trajectory, not independent simulations. No-load is a contact observation, not proof of a fall. No takeoff, flight, autonomous mission, held-out layout or landing is shown. Lane 1 is the exact recorded-state/contact reproduction documented in ../crow-v15-long-walk-failure-replay-20261113/RESULT.md; lane 5 is the original saved replay in ../crow-v15-walk-hold-slower-cycle-20261111/.

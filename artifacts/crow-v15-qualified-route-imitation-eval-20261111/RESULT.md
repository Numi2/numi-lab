# Teacher-off route imitation: development control

On a previously used development layout and seed 20261126, the route actor trained on successful privileged-teacher prefixes ran from eight physical starts with no teacher and no scheduled reset. By 3000 control intervals, three first episodes had reached all five waypoint thresholds, three were still at four, and two had failed after reaching two and one respectively. The first observed non-ground contact for both failing lanes was left wing versus slalom A; endpoint sampling cannot establish the first substep impact. No lane had completed the required supported landing at the end of its first episode. The original student had reached zero first waypoints by 1600 intervals on this development course and seed; these trials have different durations and are not evidence of held-out generalization. The actor has not been tested on reserved final layouts. Only the first episodes are counted. The physical toe-flexion candidate is not included in this run.

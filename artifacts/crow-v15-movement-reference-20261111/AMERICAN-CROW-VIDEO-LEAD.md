# American crow gait reference lead: not yet qualified

[Cornell Macaulay Library ML416478](https://macaulaylibrary.org/video/416478) identifies an adult American crow (*Corvus brachyrhynchos*), filmed on the ground while foraging in Montana in April 2000, contributor Timothy Barksdale. The archive page does not establish calibrated frame rate, camera geometry, the duration of unobscured walking, or a measured gait. A browser attempt on 2026-11-12 encountered an automated access challenge, so the footage itself was not inspected. Its usefulness for whole-body gait, stride timing, perspective or ground-contact analysis remains unknown. Do not extract numerical targets or claim comparison with this video without first obtaining authorized access, inspecting the footage and recording appropriate measurement limitations. The copyright remains with its rights holder; this note links to the reference and does not reproduce media.

The paper [Hayes and Alexander (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7998.1983.tb05784.x) reports film-based gait analysis of four crow species, with walking at low speed and out-of-phase hopping at higher speed. Its accessible abstract does not identify those species, so this is comparative corvid evidence rather than measured American-crow stride data.

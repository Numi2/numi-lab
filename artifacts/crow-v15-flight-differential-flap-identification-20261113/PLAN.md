# Bounded differential-flap identification

The preregistered differential-sweep test changed lateral velocity but produced at most 0.00244 rad mean heading departure during its pulse; this is insufficient to establish useful steering. Test a distinct bounded physical joint-target intervention on the same v15 research body, aero, no-obstacle host, seed 20261126, launch and frozen comparison inputs. Use the same six candidate lanes: 2–3 remain baseline controls, 4–5 receive +0.07 rad amplitude on flap action 0 and -0.07 rad on flap action 1, 6–7 receive the opposite signed intervention. The nominal amplitude is 0.70 rad; amplitudes are therefore 0.63–0.77 rad. Ramp from zero during 12–12.5 s and back to zero during 17.5–18 s. Do not change symmetric sweep feedback, frequency, pronation, other actuators, forces, collider masks or body state.

Run continuously for 24 seconds with the same 16-substep observers and exact first-episode comparisons. Require exact accepted q/v/action/contact parity with each same-lane 24-second baseline through tick 600 and full-horizon parity for controls. Report all interventions and failures, signed yaw and roll versus same-lane baseline, velocity, height, joint target and effort, toe and ground contacts, coverage, and response after removal. This is open-loop actuator identification, not closed-loop maneuver qualification, and cannot freeze the controller. Late aero is unobserved; the two reserved layouts and seeds remain unused.

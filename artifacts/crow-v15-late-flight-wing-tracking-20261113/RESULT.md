RMS requested-target minus observed start-position error is 0.80–1.05 rad, and peak absolute observed starting flap speed is 13.1–22.0 rad/s. The positive sum of working effort times accepted joint displacement is 8.9–21.2 in the model's effort/displacement units, with 6.4–19.0 in negative-magnitude contributions over the same half-second. This is a timing-sensitive proxy, not verified full muscle work or a measured crow power. Saturation and large target error show that the commanded wing motion is not being closely followed in this simulated transition; they do not uniquely identify incorrect controller gains, inertia, aerodynamics or target phasing. Do not simply raise the bound or claim biological realism. The parent observer matched recorded state and contact traces but does not rule out hidden-state differences. The development seed and correlated lanes are not held-out qualification.

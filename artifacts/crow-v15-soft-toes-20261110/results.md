# Softer toe drives results

At 16 substeps, neither candidate passed any of eight flight lanes. In each candidate at least one toe reached the 12 rad/s speed limit at every one of 128 sampled lane/time points. The 5e-6 armature candidate had no physical or solver failure; the 5e-7 candidate had one physical failure and no solver failures. Lower stiffness and damping therefore did not eliminate the observed speed saturation in these tests. Neither candidate is promoted; no claim is made for grip, walking, takeoff, landing or navigation.

# Front-toe friction causal ablation: preregistered

Retained supported-push traces show that front-toe ground normal and tangential impulses rise during the same time as rapid backward front-toe motion; this does not prove friction is the cause. Using the exact v15 push controller, development seed 20261126 and observer, set only the effective tangential coefficients and cached tangential impulses to zero for front-toe vs ground contacts in lanes 2–7 from observer step 351 onward. Keep all normal contacts, toe articulations, other friction, leg and wing inputs and rest of model. The observer passes its already established trace-submission counter only to the research collision scatter shader. This intentionally unphysical ablation is not a candidate for promotion.

Before interpreting results, require exact retained baseline state, action and contact parity through step 350, full 400-interval first episodes, intact observer coverage, and logged front-toe ground friction x/y and tangential impulses zero after the gate while other contacts retain their coefficients. A failure of any prerequisite invalidates the ablation. Compare original and ablated front-toe speed, normal/tangential impulses, support, recontact, whole-body COM rise and whole-body movement, with same-lane comparison; do not use separate runs as independent biological trials. Reduced toe caps would show sensitivity to this intervention but not prove a solver defect or justify a frictionless real foot. Additional tests, including walking, takeoff and landing, remain necessary. Preserve failure and all provenance.

# Individual-toe contact feedback, hybrid matched bench

Aggregate toe impulses can trigger the former controller while one foot lacks opposing contact. Compare the unchanged, parity-checked baseline against three exploratory per-toe feedback laws in the same matched single-start perch bench. Each toe gets a separate position target, advances only while its own appropriately oriented contact impulse is absent, and holds when contact is present. The three rates are 0.005, 0.01 and 0.02 rad per control step. A grip transition requires three samples with rear and at least one front contact on each foot; after a ten-step hold all targets open, with a 120-step release timeout. This uses simulated contact information, with no prescribed forces or state resets. Physical parameters, hybrid SAT collision, timestep and body remain fixed. Prespecified outcomes: exact control trace parity, all solver failures, per-foot grip with load-bearing support, full release and sustained support, and independently measured toe-perch SAT overlap. One-case improvements must be confirmed on all eight starts before integration; no held-out layout is used. These numerical choices are uncalibrated.

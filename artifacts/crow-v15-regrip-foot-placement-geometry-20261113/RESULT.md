# Fixed-state geometry of failed foot placement

The native FK executable matched all 315 logged foot/toe position and orientation components at five retained lane-1 states within 9.27e-10. The left foot center was x=0.02783 m at the last pre-release sample (zero-based 157) and x=0.03713 m at the end of clearance (208), with the perch centered at x=0. The same-state isolated +0.15 rad hip-pitch offset moved its center back about 20.5 mm; +0.15 rad knee pitch moved it back about 11.0 mm. At tick 208 these fixed-state changes also predicted negative minimum foot/toe box separation, approximately -7.0 and -5.2 mm respectively. All 125 tested combinations were within declared joint-position limits for each state.

These are hypothetical fixed-root, fixed-other-joint configurations, not dynamic actuator movements or measured contacts. Overlap does not establish a valid placement or opposing grip and demonstrates why jumping directly to the calculated configuration would be invalid. The 9.3 mm observed foot shift motivates smaller rate-limited physical placement tests with actual contacts and support checks; it does not establish a cause, a successful landing, or biological fidelity. The complete table is retained.

# Contact-triggered leg-to-flight handoff

All eight development lanes first fully unloaded both feet at step 253 and remained free of measured ground contact for at least 103 consecutive 20 ms intervals; no native failures or terminations occurred, and each had gained roughly 1.05 m in height by step 353. The q/v trajectories matched the suppressed-wing leg-jump reference through step 252, then diverged during the first fully unloaded interval when wing actuation began. Therefore the predeclared exact-match-through-unloading gate failed, although the ground-supported portion of the push matched.

The trial also failed its forward-speed gate: at step 353 the birds moved backward at approximately 0.18–0.19 m/s, despite low sampled tilt. The representative lane reached roughly 0.70 m behind its start before returning to positive forward speed by step 400; its forward speed at step 500 was approximately 0.37 m/s. The actor requested a high initial flight height while the body was near the ground and outside the previously demonstrated flight-speed envelope. This observation motivates bounded high-level launch commands, not a change to the frozen flight law. This is not qualified navigation, landing or biological realism; all outcomes are retained.

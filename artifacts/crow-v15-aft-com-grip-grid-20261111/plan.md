# Contact feedback with aft-COM posture

The independently replayed aft-COM candidate maintained support longer but lost both rear-toe contacts while the target remained fixed. Test 15 bounded contact-reclosure variants against that exact controller in 16 concurrent isolated development environments. Lane 0 is unchanged; other lanes form the full cross product of lower impulse thresholds 0.0004, 0.002 and 0.005 N s with (target cap, rad/tick) pairs (0.85,0.004), (0.85,0.008), (1.0,0.004), (1.2,0.004), (1.2,0.008). Reclosure starts only after bilateral contact and 10 held ticks; existing high-impulse relief, actual 16-substep opposing impulses, joint effort limits, leg motion and physical body remain unchanged. Require every lane to match reference through tick 85 and unchanged lane to match the independent 500-tick replay exactly. Run 500-tick short screens with no resets; require last 100 ticks of continuous bilateral opposed grip and physical support, no nonfoot support, speed/tilt gates, no solver failure and grip toe overlap no worse than 0.5 mm. Any candidate needs independent 1,000-tick and perturbed-start tests, release, flight and full-course qualification; no reserved layouts are used. Contact thresholds are simulation diagnostics, not calibrated biological forces.

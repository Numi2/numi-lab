# Bilateral rear proximal toe damping: perturbed perch screen

The retained cap was in the right rear proximal toe during support transfer. Test whether small, symmetric changes to the two rear proximal toe-joint damping values help the complete release-and-regrip bench: baseline 0.003, candidate 0.006, candidate 0.009 in the existing native drive units. The settings apply from the start and change model dynamics and fingerprint; they are not matched post-intervention trajectories or biological calibration. Do not change gains or contact material. Preserve the same eight constructed initial q/v starts, controller, 32 substeps, collision setup and native solver. Compare baseline with its retained predecessor; report any nonparity.

Before evaluation require all 550 intervals, all contact/toe substeps, no errors or overflow, at least ten consecutive support-qualified left-foot clearance intervals and return, final 100 intervals of bilateral opposed supported contact, no falls/nonfoot impulses, and zero sampled toe-speed caps in all eight lanes. These are the existing bench gates. Even a pass would require walking, takeoff, flight-toe and continuous landing tests on the same changed model, independent starts and physical data. No damping value is an American-crow measurement.

# Rear-toe damping: no passing setting

The baseline exactly reproduces the previous release bench, both as decompressed streams and as compressed-file hashes. In all three arms, all eight lanes completed 550 intervals with all 140,800 contact and velocity substeps per arm, no reported solver errors or overflow, and no fall or nonfoot impulse. This is a constructed perched-arrival bench, not a complete landing or biological calibration.

| Rear proximal toe drive damping | Passing starts | Failed gates |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 0.003, baseline | 6/8 | Lane 0 lacked the final bilateral-support criterion for one interval; lane 3 hit the velocity cap twice. |
| 0.006 | 6/8 | Lanes 5 and 7 hit the cap two and three times respectively; all lanes satisfied terminal bilateral support. |
| 0.009 | 4/8 | Lanes 3, 5 and 7 each hit the cap twice; lane 6 did not satisfy the terminal bilateral-support criterion. |

Every capped sample in these arms was the right rear proximal toe at -12 rad/s, during intervals 250–277. The initial q/v configurations are identical but damping is changed from the beginning, so subsequent trajectories are not matched interventions. Sampled cap timing alone does not identify the cause. None of the changed settings is accepted or integrated. These gate outcomes do not establish walking, takeoff, a continuous landing, or real-crow mechanics.

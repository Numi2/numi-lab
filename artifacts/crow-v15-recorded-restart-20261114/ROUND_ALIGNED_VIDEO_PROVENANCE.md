# Aligned round-perch failure video

`round-aligned-video/crow-48s-plus-aligned-perch-failure.mp4` is a 53.6-second, five-view presentation of the original 48-second recorded flight followed by the first 280 recorded frames of `round-position-midpoint-yaw-front-400`. This fixture was a stationary 0.025 m radius, 0.695 m half-length capsule relocated to x=17.770 m and yawed +0.2 rad at the reconstructed checkpoint; landing phase was forced. The renderer presents that actual collider shape using `capsule-render.patch`, not naturalistic branch texture. The original flight had course collision masks disabled. This is not continuous original-course autonomous navigation and excludes the reset after first termination. The qualifying bilateral grip lasted 166 intervals, 3.32 seconds, but tipping led to termination at step 279 (5.58 s); no stable landing is demonstrated.

The renderer and compiler identity, collision caveats and pre-roll reconstruction are documented in `ROUND_VIDEO_PROVENANCE.md`. `make_limited_render_splice.swift`, `render_limited_round_views.sh` and `compose_aligned_video.py` produce this video. The first 210-trial-frame-specific splice script recorded in the earlier video provenance is not used here. Presentation frames are renumbered; the five original pre-roll frames are hidden, and renderer camera-relative framing is not a global path. The source simulation and contact trace remain in `round-position-midpoint-yaw-front-400/`. Renderer logs report emitted accepted steps 6 through 285.

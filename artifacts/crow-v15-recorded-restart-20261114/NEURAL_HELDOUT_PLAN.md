# Neural balance v1 evaluation plan

After the four-update development training series, compare its final deterministic deployment policy with the initial zero-output policy on two untouched seeds 20310111 and 20310129. Neither is used for training, development rollouts, or hyperparameter selection. Use the same task, randomization code, v15 body, static round collision geometry, physics timestep and 16 substeps, with no teacher. For each seed/policy pair retain full first-episode physical replay and all-substep contact evidence; no reset may count as a continuation. The test uses a reconstructed forced-landing start and development branch geometry, not unused navigation layouts.

Report first termination, qualified grip intervals, duration of final consecutive supported opposed-toe hold, body tilt/speed, and ground/nonfoot contacts. Require no done/reset and the last 250 consecutive intervals satisfying the predeclared first-episode gates for every tested seed. Compare to the zero-output baseline; a trained policy must improve physical outcomes, not just PPO reward or KL. Any change motivated by these results consumes these seeds for development; reserve new seeds for a later final test.

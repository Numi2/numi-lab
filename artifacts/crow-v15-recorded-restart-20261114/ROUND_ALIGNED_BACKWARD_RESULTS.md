# Matched two-millimeter backward offset

The x=17.768 m aligned capsule produced 111 consecutive qualified intervals (36–146) and first termination at step 222. The x=17.770 m control had 166 intervals and first termination at 279; x=17.772 had 22 intervals and first termination at 172. All three fail sustained landing, and all used the subsequently identified experimental out-of-range toe command. These matched geometry perturbations show local sensitivity, but neither identify a solver defect nor establish a realistic model. Later automatic restarts are excluded.

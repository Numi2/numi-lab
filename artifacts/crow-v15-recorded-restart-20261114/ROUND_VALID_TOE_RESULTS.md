# In-range front-toe target result

Capping the experimental front proximal target at the modeled +1.20-radian joint limit on the x=17.770 m +0.2-radian aligned fixture produced 278 consecutive first-episode intervals satisfying the declared bilateral contact and posture checks (38–315, 5.56 s). Tilt then exceeded 0.35 rad and the episode terminated at step 393 for tilt. It fails the required final 250 qualifying intervals and no-termination condition. The result is a useful development observation, not a stable landing or trained neural controller. No later reset is included.

The measured toe angles and targets, first-episode replay and all-substep contact trace are retained; actual motor limits and the rest of the feedback loop still require a full audit. The scene was reconstructed, the branch was moved, and landing mode was forced.

# Round-perch first-episode video provenance

`round-video/crow-48s-plus-round-perch-failure.mp4` is a 52.2 s presentation: the original synchronized 48-second, five-view recorded flight followed by 4.2 seconds from the reconstructed, forced-landing, moved-capsule combined development trial. It contains no frame from the automatic reset after the first termination. The original 48-second run disabled course collision masks; no waypoint or original-course landing success is implied. Cameras are bird-relative. The overlay suppresses a renderer waypoint HUD that would incorrectly suggest completed navigation.

First-episode source is `round-position-forward-front-closure-400/replay.json`; full-substep evidence and analysis are in the same directory. `make_round_render_splice.swift` makes a presentation-only replay with five hidden original pre-roll frames plus the first 210 trial frames; frame numbers and digest are regenerated. Renderer logs confirm accepted frame steps 6–215, corresponding to the 210 emitted trial frames. It is not an unbroken native simulation state history and the reconstructed checkpoint does not restore controller, random or solver warm state exactly. The capsule was relocated before continuation.

Renderer base revision `1d40006ecfd5865f9d8be0f56d151b706237b43b`, with `capsule-render.patch`; binary SHA256 `bac877e6f90613957e1a0850cf1f01bd91b10875a1e779d717725ca8cf339d4b`. For this opt-in diagnostic it renders the physical collider as a y-axis capsule with 0.025 m radius and 0.695 m half-length. The physical host selects the same shape and dimensions; colors are presentation only. Five frame clips and logs are generated by `render_round_views.sh`, assembled with `compose_round_video.py`. Final video SHA256 `95015218916792f443e7dee59a657d2e7d422c8694609c4252bf955e73b93ff4`.

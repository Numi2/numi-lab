# Neural balance v2 training plan

The first policy does not pass both paired starts. Its 128-step training horizon also ends well before the observed late losses. Continue from the retained v1 policy and optimizer on the same 16-substep articulated v15 plant, but use 512-step rollouts and a wider reset neighborhood (x/y ±0.006 m, x velocity ±0.05 m/s). Train four updates with four environments, fixed learning rate and no adaptive KL schedule. Retain all policies, optimizer states, rollouts, measurements, sources, seeds and failures.

Replace the stale bilateral ramp reward proxy with a per-tick signal computed from actual final-substep contacts between each foot/toe and the round branch, rewarding upward load and opposed front/rear normal components, and penalize tilt and motion. This is an imperfect noisy training signal, not the physical success criterion: the latter still sums all 16 substeps, rejects ground/nonfoot support and checks the last 250 consecutive intervals. Evaluate development seeds before reserving new unseen seeds. Preserve and independently evaluate v1 even if v2 is worse. The scripted terminal carrier remains in charge of the rest of the body; no claim of autonomous landing follows from this training.

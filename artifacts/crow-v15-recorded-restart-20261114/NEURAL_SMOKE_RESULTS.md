# Neural balance smoke and initial paired evaluation

The first attempt to use the conditional waypoint expert was rejected by the existing Swift trainer guard because it requires a masked objective. A subsequent isolated masked-trainer attempt was rejected by the native deterministic stage-selection guard. The successful dedicated task uses the existing `v15-hierarchical-navigation` task without a base policy, retains the unmodified production guards, and starts from the reconstructed terminal state. Its isolated experimental shader applies the first four neural action slots as bounded hip/ankle residuals, randomizes starting root x/y and x velocity, and uses a round fixed collider and modeled 1.20-rad front-toe target limit. The frozen carrier and forced landing phase remain active; a policy pass here would not demonstrate original-course autonomy.

One two-environment, 64-step rollout produced 128 transitions and two PPO minibatch updates. The behavior likelihood was re-derived on the four executed action dimensions; the maximum all-18-dimension native log-probability parity error was 4.1723e-6. Four-action post-final-step KL was 0.01708, not the pre-update average KL of 0.000664. The actor gradient norm averaged 1.923 before combined clipping and 0.800 after clipping; critic norm was 1.440 before and 0.581 after. Actor mean shift RMS was 0.01251. These are smoke diagnostics, not a claim of useful skill or a target-KL success.

A paired deterministic rollout at seed 20261208 showed that the trained outputs altered physical q from the first step. The initial policy had a 304-interval qualified streak and the smoke-updated policy 301 intervals, but both lost the tilt criterion before the end of 400 steps. Neither meets the terminal-hold gate, and this comparison does not demonstrate improvement. Seed 20261208 is consumed development data and will not be described as held out in later evaluations. First-episode contact/replay evidence is retained. The actor observation contract and policy action parity still require independent inspection.

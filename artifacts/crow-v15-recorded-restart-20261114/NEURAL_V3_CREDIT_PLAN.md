# Neural balance v3: longer failure attribution

Retained v2 rollouts contain 48 first-failure neighborhoods with 100 earlier samples. At 100 steps before termination, the actual GAE(.99,.95) advantages average +0.082 while the terminal-safe lambda=1 estimate averages -1.959; their signs differ in 35/48 cases. At 50 steps the means are -0.587 and -3.511. These are descriptive estimates on failed development rollouts, not causal proof or a universal calibration. The multiplicative terminal-TD factors are 0.00217 and 0.366 at distance 100.

Test the specific hypothesis that extending temporal credit improves the learned correction. Restart from the retained v1 policy and optimizer, keep v2's same wider reset distribution and last-substep physical contact reward, four environments, 512 decision steps, fixed 0.0003 learning rate and 16 substeps. Set gamma=.99, lambda=.995 and run eight updates on new development seeds 20270211 through 20270218. Keep raw rollouts, policy and optimizer checkpoints, final-step per-action KL, separate actor/critic norms after combined clipping, and likelihood parity. Do not use adaptive learning rate or count training reward as success. Compare the resulting deterministic policy on the two consumed development starts against v1/v2 using the same narrow-reset evaluation shader. Only if it improves both starts should a separately registered new-seed holdout be used. This remains a reconstructed branch-start fixture with a scripted carrier, not autonomous landing.

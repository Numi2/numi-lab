# Earlier contact flare

The 0.2 N-per-foot flare never activated and was bit-identical in its reported contact outcomes to touchdown-hold. At the promising contact around step 3773, left/right accepted loads were 0.569/0.122 N and vertical velocity was -0.331 m/s; at the following recorded step the impact had already driven upward velocity near 0.79 m/s. Test whether requiring 0.05 N per foot, while keeping all alignment and velocity gates, enables the contact transition before that rebound. This is a sensing-threshold experiment with the same physical body, flight law, toe reflex, solver, actions and 5000-step used-J first-episode boundary. Require 25 consecutive bilateral loaded samples, simultaneous opposing toe contacts, stable support without nonfoot collision or reset. Count the actual flare activations and report any failure. A transition based on transient load is not itself a landing, autonomy, or biological qualification.

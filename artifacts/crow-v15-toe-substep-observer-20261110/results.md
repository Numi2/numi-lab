# Original J substep grip audit

The configurable observer reproduced all 7000 frames of the original J development replay exactly for q, velocity, actor observation and accepted actions. Every one of the 880 requested substep-status samples was present. During the 100 control steps immediately before the endpoint release event at 4433, opposing front/rear impulses on both feet exceeded the predeclared per-substep threshold in 368 of 400 substeps; the longest uninterrupted opposing run was 49 substeps. In the early post-release window, zero of 400 substeps had opposing bilateral impulses, while 320 of 400 still had a toe/perch impulse above threshold. These results support intermittent opposed contact and opening of the grip, not uninterrupted grip or fully lifted toes.

The threshold is a diagnostic 0.0000125 N s per substep, one quarter of the earlier endpoint threshold, not a biological force threshold. No impulse does not establish geometric separation. This is one previously used development J trajectory with a programmed timed release; the unchanged body and controller have not met the full two-layout physical goal. The observer source changes only recording, and its complete accepted physical trajectory matched the previously archived reference in the checked fields.

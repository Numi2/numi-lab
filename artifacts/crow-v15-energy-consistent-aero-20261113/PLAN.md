# Preregistered energy-consistent closure research trial

Hypothesis: the fixed body-up unsteady force can inject positive mechanical work in still air without a modeled wake energy store, as found in the same-state audit. Test a deliberately conservative research-only closure: for each blade element, retain its prescribed unsteady force direction only after removing the component that would do positive work against the local incoming flow. Keep any component that removes mechanical energy. Leave the regular lift, drag, body, tail, controller and articulated body unchanged. This makes the algebraic term passive in still air at the element level; it is not a validated unsteady aerodynamic model and may remove real wake-mediated energy recovery. Do not use it to assert bird realism.

Run the previously observed push and matched hold for seed 20261126, eight lanes, 16 substeps, no teacher, 400 ticks, on the same training layout. Use the identical task shader, v15 body, policy packs and observer; replace only the flapping-wing air object in the metallib. Preserve original shader and explicit diff. Compare each variant against the archived baseline, using records rather than assuming trajectories stay matched after the first aerodynamic difference. Check push versus hold input equality through the point their controllers diverge, first-episode coverage, finite state, observer errors, signed aerodynamic work and force. Compute body twists at kernel input and report projected-term power behavior; also report release, recontact, COM rise, foot impulses, actuator effort and threshold events. Any missing or incompatible logs invalidate the result. Failed launches are retained and no alternate variant is promoted based on this trial. No unused layouts or seeds will be used.

Prediction: the resulting term has no positive instantaneous work in still air by construction, so total integrated aerodynamic work should be non-positive up to numerical/timing tolerance if the model kinematics and wrench conversion are consistent. A violation triggers geometry or timing investigation, not a solver-defect claim. Physical flight qualification still requires independent force/kinematic calibration, powered launch, flight maneuvers, and continuous supported landing.

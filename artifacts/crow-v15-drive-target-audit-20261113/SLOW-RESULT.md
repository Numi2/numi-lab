# Drive-target audit: earlier -0.70 action request

The logging-only run matched the retained slower-extension run for all 3,208 recorded endpoint fields and 5,888 contact-substep fields, with no recorded overflow or substep errors; 368 target rows were logged. In comparable lanes 2–7, both knee targets equaled the lower bound -0.10 rad at every sampled first substep from control step 358 through 372. The target range over all lanes and steps 335–380 was -0.100000001 to 1.02999997 rad. The observed -0.10 request condition remained between 0.205 and 1.030 rad. In both conditions the respective experimental push failed its launch screens.

The copied target is after the task shader's binding clamp and before the solver's joint-limit clamp, so equality at the limit does not reveal the unclamped candidate. We do not infer how far past the limit it would have gone. The 160 ms ramp nominally ends at 7.12 s; with 20 ms steps, the first observed lower-limit target at step 358 is about 7.16 s. The prior assertion that it must have reached the limit early during the ramp was not supported. The command value -0.70 is a normalized action, not radians. Results are observational, six correlated lanes, one used seed; they do not qualify takeoff or flight. Full per-step records and parity checks are preserved.

# Tiny unloaded rear-toe command: rejected

The -0.02 rad proximal command was compared with a same-binary sham in eight lanes of one used development seed. The 2,400 accepted states and 38,400 contact substeps per arm are present, with exact pre-walk state and contact parity; no logged solver errors, overflow, added toe-speed caps, nonfoot contact or unloaded substeps. Both feet show three support-qualified clearance bouts per lane.

Sole-clear but toe-low endpoint counts decreased from 49 to 21. Median loaded contact-point speed decreased from 0.00276 to 0.00235 m/s and p95 from 0.03484 to 0.02935 m/s; these are dependent sampled velocities, not integrated slip. Forward displacement was 21.26–21.81 mm versus 25.07–29.71 mm for the corresponding sham lanes; all failed the required 90% progress threshold. The candidate is rejected and not integrated. This single two-second development screen is not biological gait validation or a physical takeoff/landing transition.

# Matched autonomous leg push-off

In all eight first-episode development starts on a used course and seed, the wing-off run matched the wing-on autonomous replay in accepted q/v and all substep contact aggregates until the feet were unloaded and rising. Both feet contributed upward contact impulse, no nonfoot ground impulse was recorded in the launch window, and foot/toe boxes cleared the ground by at least 1 mm at two sampled endpoints. With commanded wing flapping suppressed, the body rose 4.2–4.4 cm before recontact; with wings enabled it reached sustained flight through step 500. Two starts had 14 and 6 toe speed-limit substeps during initial settling. Passive aerodynamic forces remained; this is evidence of leg contribution, not a controlled physical bird validation or qualification across unseen starts/layouts.

# Contact-gated release following autonomous approach

On one used development layout and seed, eight teacher-off first episodes were observed with no scheduled resets. Lane 7 reached five waypoints, landed, held bilateral opposed support for 156 consecutive intervals, then released the left foot while the right foot supported at least half estimated body weight. Left foot and toe box clearance exceeded 1 mm at 13 successive control endpoints with all-substep zero left contact and right opposed support. Bilateral support later returned, including the final 129 intervals; no nonfoot perch impulse or missing substeps occurred in the audited interval. This is one of eight attempts: two collided with slalom and five hovered without final supported landing. Endpoint clearance does not prove continuous separation between endpoints, physical parameters are estimated, and this is neither an unused-layout result nor biological validation. The experimental abort path can abruptly reset targets after loss of right grip; shared packed state conflicts with the separate flight-fold controller. Do not combine or promote without redesign and requalification.

# Bounded support evidence observer

Long stable landings generate thousands of nearly identical contact records per lane. Preserve raw support evidence for the first 50 consecutive qualifying steps, every 100th subsequent step, every step of the final 25-step episode window, and all contacts after any interruption until 50 qualifying steps recur. A qualifying observation uses the same upward foot normal impulse, absence of non-foot perch contact, and velocity limits as the landing screen. Contact failures and q/v sampling remain unchanged. Before use, compare this read-only observer against the full observer on identical 16-lane 3000-step first episodes: sampled q/v and first-episode outcomes must match exactly, and terminal supported-finish classifications must agree. This bounds disk and Git use without deleting existing evidence or weakening the final 25-step measurement.

# Bounded wingbeat bank-attitude feedback screen

The constant differential-wingbeat screen had weak or wrong-way heading response and the largest input disturbed altitude. Test a bounded feedback term at the existing physical wing position actuators: heading error requests a small signed bank angle, and measured bank and roll rate bound the differential wingbeat command. Three bank targets (0.08, 0.16, 0.24 rad) are tested in paired +/-90 degree lanes after 9 s, with feedback term capped at 0.28 in normalized action. Keep common-mode height/speed control, and remove the two known direct body moments only after 9 s as in the matched baseline. Use the already parity-checked full-state/wing-load observer in two one-second windows. Compare pre-intervention q/v/actions with the retained baseline and compare heading and attitude with the prior wingbeat screen. One lane per setting/direction is only a development screen; earlier motion still uses the old unqualified controller. Require no physical failure, no post-9 ground contacts or toe caps, bounded attitude and altitude, and meaningful improvement in both turn directions before any broader testing. No controller is frozen, no unseen layout is used, and wing aerodynamics remain unvalidated.

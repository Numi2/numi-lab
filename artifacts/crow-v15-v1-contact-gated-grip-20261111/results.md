# Whole-foot-triggered grip and delayed handoff

The first 2419 q/v samples matched the prior alignment-gated run, including all five waypoints, and the full first episode had complete 16-substep perch records with no overflow or observer errors. The trial did not repeat the prior beak impact and recorded no nonfoot perch impulse, but it timed out without a landing. It had two nonconsecutive bilateral half-weight support intervals, no opposed toe-support interval and no demonstrated grip or release. The toes received only brief endpoint load signals while the active flight controller kept the bird mostly hovering. No native environment step failed.

The absence of an opposed interval means the predeclared toe overlap check could not be evaluated; missing grip is not a pass. This is one privileged development trial, not visual autonomy, robust landing, biological realism or an unused-layout result.

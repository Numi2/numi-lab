# Stance foretoe geometry: potential distal-control hypothesis

For six retained control states, zero-offset native v15 forward kinematics matched the independently recorded stance foot and toe box gaps within 9.4 nm. At intervals 295, 297 and 300, the smallest sampled change placing both front and rear minimum gaps in the registered -0.6 to 0 mm band was +0.01 rad on the three front distal joints, with no proximal change. At 305 the minimum sampled distal change was +0.11 rad; by 310 no sampled toe adjustment met the band because the foot itself was separated. Exact grids, model executable and input states are retained.

These calculations hold the body fixed and do not predict dynamic reachability, contact normal, impulse, grip stability or biological realism. They motivate a separately controlled physical trial, not a success claim.

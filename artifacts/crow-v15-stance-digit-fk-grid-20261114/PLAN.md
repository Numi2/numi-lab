# Stance digit geometry screen

Use six retained matched-control v15 states around returning-foot contact and stance-front grip loss (zero-based intervals 290, 295, 297, 300, 305 and 310). At each fixed full-body state vary all three stance foretoe proximal joints together and distal joints together over +/-0.15 rad in 0.01 rad increments within existing -0.30 to 1.20 rad limits; retain all other body and toe coordinates. Compute native oriented-box foot, stance foretoe and rear-toe perch gaps. Check zero-offset gaps against the independently recorded body poses before interpreting results. Report the smallest offset giving front/rear gaps in [-0.6, 0] mm and foot penetration no deeper than 0.2 mm. This fixed-state geometry screen is a hypothesis generator only: it cannot establish reachable motion, load, stable grip, walking or landing. No physics or material changes.

# Low-speed flight screen

Each preselected 80-lane development test at 0.15, 0.25 and 0.35 m/s passed all sampled flight gates, with zero solver failed steps or first-episode physical failures. Across the three arms, worst sampled speed error was 0.0836 m/s, worst heading error 0.0416 rad, and maximum sampled toe rate 0.0317 rad/s. The +/-0.20 m/s speed tolerance is relatively wide at these low speeds; these results do not establish stationary hover, reverse flight, a transition to landing, obstacle clearance, or biological realism.

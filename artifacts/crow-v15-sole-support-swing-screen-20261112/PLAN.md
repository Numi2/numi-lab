# Coordinated swing-lift sensitivity with sole-gated toe release

A single sole-gated front-drive lane achieved forward motion and rear-first toe-off but failed toe clearance; 28/38 of its low-toe endpoints were lowest at a rear segment. Test whether altering coordinated swing lift can improve clearance without losing forward progress, support, or release order. Fix front-toe peak at .15 radians, other gait and model settings unchanged, and use swing-lift amplitudes [.10,.175,.20,.225,.25,.30,.15,.35], preserving lane 6 as the original .15 drive. These are bounded experimental position-drive inputs, not measured crow movements. Keep the sole-contact flags in diagnostic scratch bits, mask flight-curl output throughout 4–7.4 s and clear scratch state on exit. No implementation is admitted and the boundary remains subject to separate testing.

First two-step smoke, then 300 steps with 16 substeps, 8 lanes of previously used seed 20261127, no resets. Require exact lane 6 q/v/policy/contact and substep parity to the previous sole-gate run, all-lane prewalk parity, first episodes completed, no errors, overflow, nonfoot contacts or toe caps, at least three supported foot-clearance bouts per foot, no extra both-feet-unloaded substeps relative to held per lane, >=90% held forward travel, <=120% held sole-clear/toe-low endpoints, loaded contact-point speed median and p95 <=1.5 times held, and >=75% rear-first complete stance runs with at least two qualifying runs per foot per lane; separately report missing toe groups. It is a sensitivity screen with one start per amplitude, not biological or cross-seed qualification.

# Smaller negative hip offsets: rejected

All eight lanes ran the full 550 intervals with complete contact and toe-velocity substep coverage and no logged overflow. The first ten sampled intervals before intervention matched the same-pitch baseline. Lanes 6 and 7 are duplicate zero-offset controls, not additional independent initial conditions.

With negative initial pitch, the zero-offset baseline fell below 0.1 m at interval 136. The -0.08 and -0.16 rad candidates delayed that event to intervals 158 and 200, respectively. However, with positive initial pitch, the baseline remained supported through interval 550 while those candidates fell at 519 and 463. Both candidates subsequently hit the toe speed cap in both starts. Neither satisfies the predeclared requirement for both starting poses; neither is promoted.

The explicit toe-release phase was disabled in this stationary test. This result does not establish grip/release, autonomous landing or biological realism.

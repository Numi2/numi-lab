# Differential wingbeat authority: not qualified

Three matched six-lane obstacle-free runs compared the same baseline and two signs of bounded differential wingbeat command at three strengths. Each arm used the same seed, lane starts and ±90-degree heading requests. All 48 sampled pre-intervention states and policy outputs matched for each candidate. The two identified direct yaw and pitch body moments were suppressed only after 9 s; earlier launch still used the old law. The simulated wing loads used the experimental v15 corrected-geometry kernel, not biologically validated forces.

At the intermediate 0.22 residual, the positive-sign candidate survived through interval 1600 but ended with approximately 1.204 and 1.316 rad heading error for the two turn directions, compared with 1.229 and 1.368 rad for the matched baseline. The opposite sign produced approximately 1.266 and 1.408 rad error. Neither direction supplied adequate turn authority. At 0.32 residual, one lane physically failed at interval 1029 in the positive-sign arm and a different lane at interval 1020 in the negative-sign arm. One non-failed high-residual lane reduced its sampled error to 0.713 rad but reached 1.637 m height; it does not establish a controlled turn. The positive failed lane had ground contact observed near failure; the retained logs here do not identify its exact fatal collider pair. The other failed lane had no observed post-400 ground contact, so a ground collision must not be assumed.

All first-episode substep records were complete and the run reported no solver failed steps or contact overflows; toe-speed caps occurred in some lanes before interval 400 but none afterward. First-episode analysis excludes logged post-failure continuation. The action was open-loop and these are used development starts; it does not qualify flight, walking, takeoff, realistic aerodynamics, navigation or landing. None of the candidates is promoted. Commands, model/binary fingerprints, traces, replays, analysis and unsuccessful cases are retained.

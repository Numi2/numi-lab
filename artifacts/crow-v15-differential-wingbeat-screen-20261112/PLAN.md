# Differential wingbeat authority screen

The matched differential-sweep experiment produced only small turning response with two known direct body moments removed after 9 s. Test whether bounded left-right asymmetry of the existing flap amplitude produces useful bank and turn with those same moments suppressed. This is an exploratory actuator-authority experiment, not a trained or frozen controller.

Three six-lane arms use the same seed, lane identities, initial state, no scheduled reset, corrected experimental aerodynamic kernel and +/-90-degree target commands. In each pair of lanes the direction of the target is mirrored. The baseline has no extra wingbeat asymmetry. The two candidate arms apply equal and opposite normalized wingbeat residuals from 9 s onward, in magnitudes 0.12, 0.22 and 0.32 across the lane pairs; the intervention sign is reversed in the second arm. Actuator limits, live physics and normal response filters remain active. It is intentionally open-loop after target selection to discover response signs and is not a navigation policy. Earlier walking and launch retain the old law and can still include the direct moments before the intervention.

Require exact matching sampled q/v and policy before 9 s; record all first-episode terminations, substep coverage, ground contact, toe speed caps, heading, roll, pitch, altitude and speed. Compare per-lane against the matching baseline, not between different environments. Inspect actual wing coordinates and both direction responses; one seed and one start per case cannot establish stability. Do not promote or freeze on this screen. Require separate feedback control, perturbed starts, climb/descent, the full capability suite and biologically grounded validation later.

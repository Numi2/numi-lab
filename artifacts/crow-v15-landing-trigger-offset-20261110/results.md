# Landing offset with sustained-foot trigger

Each of three used J original trials reached five waypoints but fell before the 4500-step terminal gate. The 25 and 35 mm foot offsets produced no opposing bilateral toe grip and failed at steps 1851 and 1858. The 45 mm offset produced a longest 56-endpoint opposed-grip run and failed at 1983. These are endpoint samples, not continuous support, and the direct accepted-state alignment and legacy visual policy are developmental only. None is a supported landing or is promoted.

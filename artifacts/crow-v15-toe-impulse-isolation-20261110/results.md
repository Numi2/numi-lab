# Isolating the aligned toe impulse

The exact original aligned catch produced a 97.10 N s toe normal impulse and -136.28 m/s root vertical velocity in one 5 ms step before any nonfoot contact. Restarting from the retained pre-step state with a fresh contact cache still produced 25.84 N s and -37.97 m/s; holding toe targets at their current values did not eliminate the instability. Removing friction suppressed the explosion in this counterfactual but is not a valid physical fix. More temporal-cone iterations worsened the divergence. The default speculative broad margin generated 588 constraints, including many well-separated pairs; hybrid CCD reduced this captured case to 12 and prevented the one-step explosion. A distributed-block damping experiment remained materially under-solved and was not promoted. Full evidence, patches, commands and sampled results are retained here; see the separate hybrid CCD results for subsequent failures and the SAT experiment.

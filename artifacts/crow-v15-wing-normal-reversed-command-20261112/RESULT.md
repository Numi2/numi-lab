# Matched-start reversal confirms directional authority in the modified model

For each of six candidate lanes, reverse only the equal-sign pronation offset after 12 s while retaining the same altered wing closure and start. All 6,000 pre-command/control endpoint states and 96,000 contact substeps exactly match the earlier run. Each trajectory reached the 24 s screen horizon. At blend 0.35, the same-start +0.20 versus -0.20 command produced yaw changes of approximately -0.420 versus +0.168 rad; at blend 0.60 the changes were approximately -0.603 versus +0.398 rad. The effects reproduced across both lanes at each blend, with approximately 0.588 and 1.001 rad signed yaw separations respectively. Trajectory angles also responded, though less than body yaw and asymmetrically. At blend 0.15 both signs still produced negative yaw despite a measurable separation.

This establishes directional control sensitivity for these same-start model tests, not a reliable closed-loop turn, real crow flight law or qualified/frozen controller. The force closure is estimated, not experimentally calibrated. The runs use an already-used seed, disabled course obstacles and direct action body moments, with other empirical aerodynamic closures active. The observer did not emit body/wing joint-speed samples, so their speed limits remain unverified here. Roll remained very small and needs physical assessment. Continuous navigation and supported landing remain untested.

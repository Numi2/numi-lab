# Foot placement recovers five of six difficult constructed captures

At the two previously failed +4 mm forward starts, .015 and .030 rad hip capture offsets passed for both +2 and +4 mm height. The .045 rad offset passed at +4 mm height but failed at +2 mm height: it stayed upright but achieved only nine consecutive release intervals and never completed regrip. All five passing candidates met the strict supported-release and terminal 100-interval bilateral opposed-grip gates; both original controls passed.

Controls exactly matched 1,100 earlier endpoint states and 35,200 substep contacts, and the normal observer agreed within 7.4e-10 N s. These fixed offsets were deliberately screened on known failed starts; known initial perch/body geometry and high uncalibrated friction are supplied directly. No continuous capture rule, actual flight-to-landing transition, held-out layout, or biologically qualified grip is demonstrated.

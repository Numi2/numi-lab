# Isolated diagnostic teacher labels: matched student trajectory

On one previously used training seed, eight first episodes with teacher labels enabled at full student authority exactly matched teacher-off runs in recorded q/v, policy outputs and endpoint contacts at every first-episode control sample. First-episode lengths and outcomes matched, and lane-one replay q/v, actor observations and recorded actions also matched for all 1507 first-episode frames. This covers accepted-state samples, not direct proof of all low-level actuator targets.

The retained rollout pack contains finite, nontrivial labels, different from student latents in the inspected post-eight-second window. That does not prove the teacher would safely recover those states. Three first episodes failed physically and no lane reached the first waypoint by the 1600-interval horizon. These data are not qualified demonstrations and must not be used as evidence of navigation or landing. Further full-route causal checks and teacher recovery qualification are required. The frozen low-level flight law was unchanged. All compressed replay, trace and rollout streams were checked against their original bytes.

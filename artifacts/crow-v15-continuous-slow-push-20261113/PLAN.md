# Slower continuous push and bounded flapping screen

A continuous leg-target push reduced support interruptions from six of six to one of six comparable lanes on development seed 20261126, but failed its preregistered 348–357 impulse criterion in three lanes. A posthoc split shows early ground impulse then compensating support from the control. Test whether extending the existing coordinated joint ramp from 160 to 240 ms, while keeping the continuous-target gate, can maintain support and preserve useful early impulse. This ramp is an engineering intervention rather than measured corvid physiology.

Compare the new push with a same-source matched crouch control: fixed v15 body, solver, contact coefficients, passive aero, policies, extended observer, 80 ms wing onset, 400 steps, 16 substeps, used seed 20261126, no reset. Prior direct moments remain through 6.3 s; six action drives are position controlled. Lanes 2–7 are correlated comparisons, lanes 0–1 context.

Require full episode, substep, target and input records with no errors/overflow and paired/past reference parity of recorded q/v/action/contact through step 345. Report push-minus-hold foot impulse in both 348–355 and 348–357, final release, pre-release support gaps and later recontact through 400, max root rise, nonfoot impulse, actual leg/flap targets and toe-speed threshold incidence. A missing record or failing lane fails. This is not flight, leg-power isolation, biological or held-out qualification. Preserve all results.

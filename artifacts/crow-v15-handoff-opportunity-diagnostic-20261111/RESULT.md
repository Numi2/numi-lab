# Landing handoff opportunities in one development lane

In lane 7, the centered-handoff run contained 14 intervals with normal impulse from both feet on the perch during steps 3000–3800. Every one was outside the tested 10 mm root-position tolerance; no sampled interval met all reconstructed position, velocity and attitude proxies. One later interval was within a 20 mm tolerance and met the other reconstructed checks. All substep records were present in the window.

This uses accepted endpoint values and summed substep impulses; the controller uses a different compact load signal and the samples may differ by one control step. It does not directly observe the internal phase, prove continuous contact, validate bird biology or establish unused-layout performance.

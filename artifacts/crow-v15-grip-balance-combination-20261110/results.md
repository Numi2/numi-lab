# Combined hip balance and grip: rejected

The original-closure control reproduced its retained trace exactly. Across eight supported starts, original closure without balance achieved the grip/release/terminal-support sequence in 4/8. The same closure with hip balance achieved 1/8, while the rear-limited and front-plus-rear contact-limited closure with hip balance each achieved 2/8. The combined cases had at least three consecutive simultaneous bilateral opposed-grip and bilateral supported endpoints in no more than three starts. Their worst accepted-pose toe–perch overlaps were 1.598, 1.020 and 0.926 mm, respectively, each above the 0.5 mm gate. The original control again rolled back in case 4; all balanced variants completed. No balanced grip variant passed the preregistered 6/8 and overlap gates.

The open-toe balance candidate therefore did not transfer directly to grip. In particular, the hip response shifted the contact configuration in starts where the original grip was stable; contact settling, grip acquisition and balance must be coordinated and independently retested. The rigid-pose SAT is endpoint-only and this stationary development bench is not a full autonomous landing or biological qualification. All outcomes, including the failed and incomplete cases, are retained.

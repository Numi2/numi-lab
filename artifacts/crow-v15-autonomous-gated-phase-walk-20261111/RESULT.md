# Contact-gated gait: insufficient foot clearance, rejected

The candidate exactly matched accepted states and recorded contact substeps through step 200 in all eight development starts. Over the following eight seconds, each lane had only one or two qualifying clear swing bouts per foot despite substantial forward displacement. Three lanes had 2, 3 and 6 substeps respectively with no measured foot normal impulse; no sampled endpoint had both feet clear by 1 mm. The movement after the early bouts therefore cannot be accepted as stable alternating walking. No nonfoot ground impulses were recorded. A later read-only observer matched this run through 450 intervals and showed that the gait phase advanced on all 99 transitions from steps 301 to 400 in every lane. The phase did not stall; later swings failed the foot-clearance and unloading criteria. See `artifacts/crow-v15-gait-phase-observer-20261111`. Endpoint shape clearance is not swept clearance. Used development seed and course only; no takeoff or landing tested.

# Contact-gated gait: stalls, rejected

The candidate exactly matched accepted states and recorded contact substeps through step 200 in all eight development starts. Over the following eight seconds, each lane had only one or two qualifying clear swing bouts per foot despite substantial forward displacement. Three lanes had 2, 3 and 6 substeps respectively with no measured foot normal impulse; no sampled endpoint had both feet clear by 1 mm. The movement after the early bouts therefore cannot be accepted as stable alternating walking. No nonfoot ground impulses were recorded. The joint trajectories are consistent with the phase waiting while the next stance foot remains commanded upward; a neutral transfer region is a separate hypothesis, not a proven cause. Endpoint shape clearance is not swept clearance. Used development seed and course only; no takeoff or landing tested.

# Contact-latched front closure prototype

The full-interval contact signal advanced the existing 16-substep grip state machine but did not grip or support in the separated start; its front target retracts whenever the measured rear force drops. Compare the same baseline final-substep controller (arm 0), unchanged accumulated-impulse controller (arm 1), and two bounded front-closure variants after the existing rear-contact transition (arms 2/3). The latter retain the rear target reached at transition and advance the front target monotonically by 0.01 or 0.025 rad per control step until the existing grip/hold/release rules apply. Both use actual full-interval opposing impulses. No force, pose, reset, or initial condition is injected; body and physics are unchanged. Both control arms must exactly reproduce all 200 respective states from the preceding experiment. Measure opposed grip with support, release, final support and open toe angle, plus independent SAT overlap; retain failures. At most 0.5 mm grip overlap and complete support/release are needed even to nominate further eight-start testing, and full flight, navigation, held-out and biological qualification remain outstanding.

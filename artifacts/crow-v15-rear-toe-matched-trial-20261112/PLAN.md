# Matched rear-toe touchdown intervention

Compare two otherwise identical full eight-start native runs: the previous 0.4-rad crouch and hip phase -0.9 with either zero or 0.2-rad rear-toe preload during the specified support-gated walking phase. All 200 endpoint and 3,200 contact-substep samples per lane through 4 s must match. The zero-preload lane 4 and 0.2-preload lane 3 must each reproduce the previous screening trace. Only differing accepted physical outcomes after 4 s are paired intervention evidence; preparation failures in either arm are retained and cannot be attributed to the intervention that starts later.

Predeclare rejection on physical failure, contact overflow, toe speed caps, increased unloaded support versus same-lane control, lack of repeated supported foot clearance or missing/reordered toe contacts; measure forward travel and sampled contact point velocity without conflating them with biological qualification. The zero-preload arm is an experimental control, not a qualified reference. The prior very slow held gait remains a separate comparison. If any arm fails, do not promote. All starts and this layout were used during development; no full-sequence, takeoff or landing claim can follow from this 0–6 s trial.

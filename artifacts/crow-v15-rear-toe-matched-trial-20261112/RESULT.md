# Matched rear-toe intervention: promising but not admitted

Control and 0.2-rad preload runs exactly matched through all 200 pre-walk endpoints and 3,200 contact substeps per lane. Control lane 4 and preload lane 3 also exactly reproduced their respective prior screen records for the entire run. Each first episode completed 300 intervals without reported physical failure or logged overflow. An initial analysis script referenced a nonexistent path; that failure is retained, and the corrected script produced these results.

During the 4–6 s intervention, every matched start had zero recorded toe-speed-cap substeps with preload, versus 2–3 per lane in the zero-preload control; both arms had zero both-feet-unloaded walking substeps and at least three supported-clearance bouts per foot. Each lane moved 156.6–158.1 mm versus 130.7–132.7 mm for its matched control. The preload produced 48 complete both-group contact runs with rear-toe-first touchdown and release and none missing a toe group under the two-substep gap rule; the control had 48 such both-group runs and two missing. This is a development result, not a biological or held-out qualification. Release of a ground contact is not evidence of gripping a perch.

Preload failed the predeclared contact-point-speed gate: pooled sampled p95 was 0.07717 m/s versus 0.04054 for matched control, though median was 0.00325 versus 0.00360. These are instantaneous endpoint velocities at accepted contacts, not integrated slip; faster travel may be relevant but does not prove the cause. The earlier standing/preparation phase had the same unloading in both arms, including 202, 158, 433 and 70 no-foot-load substeps in four starts. Three starts hit toe-speed caps before the intervention in both arms. Thus the controller remains unqualified even though a narrower walking comparator reports a pass; that comparator does not include the preparation or contact-speed gates. This trial used development starts, ran only six seconds, did not test perch grip or full flight and landing, and is not promoted.

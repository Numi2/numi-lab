# Measured pitch disturbance: limited development screen passed

The observation-only control and gust reruns exactly reproduced their respective original runs' recorded q/v/action/contact states, targets, foot-contact substeps and toe-velocity substeps through the 2,400-step horizon. There were no observer errors. The added pre-aero observer recorded every expected control wrench sample as zero and every unaffected-lane gust sample as zero. In lanes 6 and 7, it measured 1,600 active substeps over 100 consecutive intervals, torque magnitudes 0.039999997–0.040000001 N m with opposite signs, zero force, and no out-of-window pulse in the observed window.

Both perturbed lanes completed the episode without post-release recontact, nonfoot impulse or toe-speed cap and passed the registered height, attitude and recovery screens. Peak pitch response relative to their matching control was 0.0703 and 0.0697 rad; at accepted frame 1200 the pitch differences were 0.00431 and -0.00236 rad and world-y rate differences were -0.0180 and 0.00752 rad/s. The directly recorded replay, lane 5, independently confirmed the observer's one-interval q/v offset for both arms; applying that offset to lanes 6 and 7 is a shared-host inference, not a direct replay check of those lanes. The original same-label analysis and its initially missing-wrench result remain archived.

Unperturbed weak launch lanes 0 and 4 recontacted after initial unloading. This is one used-seed, already-used-layout, fixed-profile in-flight pitch-pulse test, and the observed return could include passive dynamics. It is not perturbed-airborne-start recovery, general flight command qualification, a frozen controller, demonstrated walking, toe grip, supported landing or continuous autonomous mission. Contact/state epochs are not independently established for every observation.

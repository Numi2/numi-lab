# Expanded bounded pitch correction on development I

The previous increase from -0.18 to -0.30 rad in the hip/ankle correction limit extended the longest bilateral-load interval from 471 to 689 control steps, but the bird pitched beyond 0.30 rad, slid and fell. Before execution, test a fixed -0.45 bound on that same position-feedback correction with the same v15 body, toe targets, frozen flight law, seed and guided takeover at step 2200. Compare the original and previous 0.30-bound controls for accepted prefix parity, physical failure, root pitch, foot load, actual opposing toe impulses and terminal support; measure rather than assume success. Failed runs and all landing samples are excluded from training. This is a used-layout guided probe, not autonomous or held-out qualification.

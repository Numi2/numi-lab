# Foot alignment sensitivity

On both previously used J starts, all four trials reached five waypoints but eventually fell. The 15 mm behind-goal foot target produced no opposed bilateral toe-grip endpoints, with failure at steps 2622 and 2615. The 45 mm ahead target produced temporary bilateral opposed toe grip on both starts (longest endpoint runs 83 and 75); failure occurred at steps 2876 and 2826, and the strict terminal support/grip test failed. These endpoint-contact counts do not establish continuous force throughout each interval. The target enters both approach control and landing-mode alignment; causality cannot be attributed to geometry alone. This privileged simulator-state diagnostic is not autonomous visual navigation or a qualified supported landing, and no change is promoted.

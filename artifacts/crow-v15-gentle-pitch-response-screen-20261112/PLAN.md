# Gentle ramped perch pitch response

The original stationary-perch bench stayed upright after +0.04 rad initial pitch but fell after -0.04 rad, with forward root translation preceding the fall. The +0.15 hip response failed both signs, and -0.15 caused a native rollback in the first 32 attempted steps. The large negative response had produced a strong negative pitch rate before rollback. This motivates, but does not establish, a smaller and gradually introduced response in the opposite direction from +0.15.

In the same native bench, exact body, grip, solver, two perturbation states and eight duplicate lanes, compare -0.03 and -0.06 times (-pitch - 0.4*pitch-rate) added to the hip targets. Ramp linearly from zero at step 10 to full contribution at step 60 and retain the existing contact condition and command limits. Run each gain separately; use the archived zero-gain trial as the matched baseline and compare every logged state through step 10. Do not change body mass, friction or force limits. Record all substeps and reject an arm for any native rollback, missing data, toe speed cap, nonfoot contact, falling, or failure to sustain bilateral opposing-normal support with |pitch| <=0.25 rad during the final 100 of 550 intervals. Both signs must pass. These are constructed perch starts and development parameters: no result here qualifies grip release, flight-to-perch landing or avian anatomy. Retain all failures.

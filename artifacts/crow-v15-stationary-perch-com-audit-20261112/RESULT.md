# Translation during stationary-perch pitch perturbations

In the retained zero-extra-gain stationary-perch bench, the +0.04-rad and -0.04-rad starts both have an estimated center of mass at x≈0.0049 m after the first interval. By interval 40 their pitch is similar (+0.055 vs +0.045 rad), but their centers of mass have moved to x≈0.0063 vs +0.0224 m. By interval 100 they are at +0.0259 vs +0.0925 m; the negative-start bird subsequently falls below the bench's root-height threshold at interval 136. The perch box spans x=-0.025 to +0.025 m. The forward translation difference precedes the fall and supports examining placement and fore-aft velocity alongside pitch, but does not identify the cause of the translation or prove that crossing the box boundary caused the fall: the toes can exert forces outside the top surface and momentum and angular motion matter.

COM is reconstructed from the same estimated v15 model and native kinematics used in the related COM contact audit; that audit checked two first-episode lane-5 replay frames, not this separate stationary bench. These are two constructed initial states, each duplicated in the original batch, not independent bird measurements or an autonomous landing. All recorded event fields and sampled states are retained.

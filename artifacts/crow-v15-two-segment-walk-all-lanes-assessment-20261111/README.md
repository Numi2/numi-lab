In lane 1 the reconstruction matched native replay foot/toe positions within 4.13e-9 m and quaternion norm within 2.98e-8, after the independently verified one-step observer/replay alignment. All eight starts had at least three qualifying swing bouts per foot under the recorded endpoint-clearance and all-substep opposing-foot-load criteria, with no recorded nonfoot ground contacts in the measurement window. The bout counts alone do not establish alternation or continuous clearance.

This start fails a forward-progress, continuous-contact walking requirement. The ground height for every lane is inferred from the common authored task and lane 1 replay, not independently replayed for each lane. These are selected development starts, not independent robustness, slip, takeoff, landing, or biological validation.

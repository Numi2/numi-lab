# Bounded hallux release amplitude screen

The -0.02-rad rear-toe target enabled during loaded late stance did not change the rear-last release order. In a representative lane the candidate changed the rear proximal angle by only about 0.001–0.002 rad near toe-off relative to its matched control; contact and trajectory divergence limit that observation. A prior native frozen-pose test found that a -0.10 rad proximal / -0.075 rad distal rear-toe perturbation raised its tip by about 2.9 mm at a different recorded pose. That geometric result is not a dynamic or biological prediction. A larger -0.10 rad loaded toe target failed under a different gait. Test one intermediate -0.05 rad peak target against the current -0.02 rad target, using exactly the same late-stance gate and the reduced three-joint gait. This is an engineering amplitude probe, not a measured crow target.

Before inspection, compare same-binary paired eight-lane development trials on used seed 20261127, 300 steps and 16 substeps, without resets. Require exact prewalk state and contact parity through step 200, full first episodes, no physics errors, overflow, nonfoot contact or toe velocity caps; at least three supported-clearance bouts per foot per lane; no increase in both-foot-zero-normal substeps against the no-extra-stroke held control per lane; at least 90% of held forward travel per lane; sole-clear/toe-low endpoints at most 120% and median/p95 endpoint contact speeds at most 1.5x held. Also require rear-first toe-off in at least 75% of complete runs with both front and rear toe contacts, with at least two complete runs per foot per lane. Report matched target-control and toe-contact point velocities. No candidate is promoted on this short used-seed test alone.

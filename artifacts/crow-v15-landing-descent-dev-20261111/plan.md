Test a descent command near the perch: enter the descent phase within 0.20 m and 0.20 m/s, and request an altitude of perch center plus 0.15 m. This is within the height range of earlier sampled flight tests, but those do not establish maneuver qualification at this site or guarantee safe descent. Preserve the low-level law, development starts, physical course, contact observer and 5000-step first-episode horizon.

# Bounded relative center-of-mass stance balance

An earlier paired 0.006 rad right-hip target difference produced a +0.424 mm initial difference in COM position relative to the stance foot and +0.0123 m/s relative forward velocity, though later closed-loop dynamics changed sign. In the current baseline, relative COM x drifts from about 0.002 m at release to 0.016 m at return and the stance foretoe opposing contact then disappears. Test lane 1 using the measured relative COM position and velocity in place of the baseline pitch-angle correction at the same right hip: clamp(-1.5*(relX-0.004 m)-0.10*relVx, -0.02, +0.02) rad, with target slew at most 0.002 rad per 20 ms. Preserve the original full opposing-contact support gate, all other commands, target bounds, physical drives, and lane 0 as an exact baseline. The sign and gain are hypotheses informed by a short local pulse, not a claimed stable transfer function. No kinematic override or external force is added.

Use 450 intervals and 32 substeps from identical constructed starts. Check actual target and q/v parity before intervention, archived baseline identity, correction magnitude/slew, hip and toe bounds, complete contacts and independent geometry. Require at least ten release intervals and 100 final bilateral opposing-grip intervals under existing tilt, speed, support and nonfoot-contact criteria. Record falls and all failed gates; accepted-state speed is not substep effort or speed. This is a constructed-state controller experiment, not flight, walking, landing or biological qualification.

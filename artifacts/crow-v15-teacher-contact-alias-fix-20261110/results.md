# Teacher contact-command isolation, development only

The foot-contact monitor overwrote the privileged guide commands in shared state. This experiment stores bounded guide velocities in a separate packed channel and updates the revised-body mass approximation. The packing has not been proven invisible to the student and is not authorized as imitation data.

On reused development layouts, the guide reached all five waypoints in 8/8 J starts, and 6/8 I starts; the remaining two I starts hit the perch before waypoint five. All J starts flew past the perch: no supported landing is claimed. These are privileged guide trajectories, not autonomous navigation and not held-out tests. Failed demonstrations are excluded from training. See analysis.json and retained traces.

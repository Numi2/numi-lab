# Larger return placement reached the foretoes but failed bilateral grip

The medium and larger placement lanes matched q/v exactly for 220 pre-placement states. Both achieved eleven consecutive independently checked release intervals and all 450 control intervals completed with complete 32-substep contact records and no observer or capacity errors. The larger target advanced only 20% before its stance-support/posture gate blocked further motion; the accepted knee and ankle positions stayed within their recorded limits. Accepted-state joint velocities are reported separately and are not substep effort measurements.

The larger-placement lane produced positive returning-foretoe normals at 20 accepted endpoint samples, whereas the medium control produced none. At interval 250, independently reconstructed nearest foretoe-perch separation was about 3.75 mm versus 7.48 mm in the medium control. Despite this partial foretoe contact, neither lane had a single interval satisfying the combined bilateral opposing-toe support, tilt and speed grip criteria. Root height fell below 0.1 m at intervals 310 and 308 in the medium and larger lanes respectively. By the time the larger lane's foretoes first showed a meaningful opposing normal around zero-based interval 263, its body tilt was already approximately 0.50 rad, above the 0.35 rad grip gate, and the stance foretoe normal was absent in that endpoint sample.

The results and full traces are retained, including the failed medium and larger trials. This constructed stationary return does not establish walking, a supported landing or biological calibration.

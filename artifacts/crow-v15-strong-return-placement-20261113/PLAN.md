# Stronger bounded return-foot placement

The prior supported-return trial completed its +0.15 rad knee / -0.30 rad ankle targets while stance opposing normals remained available, but its nearest returning foretoe remained about 7.5 mm from the perch at interval 250 and no sampled foretoe normal appeared. Compare the same medium target in lane 0 with +0.30 rad knee / -0.60 rad ankle in lane 1, using the same measured stance-contact and posture gate. Both targets remain inside the existing joint-position limits from the constructed pose; progress is limited to 0.05 per 20 ms and the physical implicit drives and force limits are unchanged. There are no artificial body forces or kinematic overrides.

Run 450 intervals at 32 substeps from identical constructed states, retain all control and trial observations and reject missing or overflowed contact records. Verify exact pre-placement q/v parity, measured target and accepted position limits, independent toe/perch geometry, ten qualifying release intervals and 100 final bilateral opposing-grip intervals under the established support, tilt, speed and nonfoot-contact gates. Report all failures and falls. This development test is not a landing or walking qualification and larger joint targets are not assumed biologically calibrated.

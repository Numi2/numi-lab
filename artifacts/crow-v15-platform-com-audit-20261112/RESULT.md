# Full-body center-of-mass landing diagnostic

For the retained lane 7 development landing trajectory, reconstructed v15 body centers agreed with 595 stored replay body positions to within 0.000000246 m. The reconstructed mass was 0.45006 kg. At sampled intervals 4000 and 4100, the vertical-impulse-weighted foot contact x coordinate was about 0.022 m and 0.094 m ahead of the total body center of mass as pitch fell from -0.156 to -0.493 radians. The sampled summed foot-contact pitch angular impulses about that center were negative, -0.00195 and -0.00692 N m s. This establishes a consistent geometrical and contact-impulse observation, not causation: the total angular momentum budget includes other forces and links, and the weighted contact coordinate is a diagnostic. The trajectory used the older unqualified controller and a used development layout. No stable landing is claimed.

# Result: 85% step strength merits confirmation

In the selected development seed, the two starts assigned 0.85 gait strength each advanced 3.27 cm, had three qualified swing bouts per foot, no recorded nonfoot ground contact, no all-substep gap in foot normal impulse, and no sampled pose with both feet more than 1 mm above the ground. Maximum tilt was about 0.027 rad and speed about 0.115 m/s. The 0.70 setting passed in one start but failed severely in another; the two 0.55 starts failed the swing or contact requirements. Both unmodified controls reproduced their matched reference q, v and policy actions exactly through step 355, including the known airborne phase. Modified lanes matched the prior baseline through step 202 and first differed at step 203. Geometry reconstruction matched the native lane-1 replay within 3.97e-9 m and 2.98e-8 quaternion norm.

Scale and start are confounded and only two starts used 0.85. These results are a selected two-second teacher-guided development screen, not confirmation across starts, continuous geometric clearance, toe-speed qualification, takeoff, release, landing, full autonomous route or biological realism. Do not promote until the same setting passes all starts and fresh seeds and is integrated and retested end to end.

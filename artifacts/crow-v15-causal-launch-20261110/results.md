# Contact-triggered takeoff transition

The predeclared exploratory prediction passed on the development course. All 8/8 push-trigger lanes recorded both support groups at zero and latched at accepted episode step 254, then were airborne for the final second at more than 0.15 m above their starts with no first-episode physical or solver failures. No-push controls latched before timeout in 0/8 lanes. In post-hoc analysis, all eight push-trigger lanes remained clear of ground from the sample following the latch through 8 s; the fixed-timer push arm recontacted the ground in all eight lanes before subsequently taking flight. Pre-extension q/v matched the independently saved prior run in all lanes. The task-state logger is a read-only shared-buffer diagnostic and its q/v prefix was verified. The controller and prototype remain unqualified for full-route execution, unused layouts or biological realism; flapping and other aerodynamics were active after release.

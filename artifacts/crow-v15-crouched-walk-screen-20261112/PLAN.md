# Crouched walking screen

The measured model knee stays relatively straight; test a gently ramped symmetric knee flexion bias of [0, 0.1, ..., 0.7] radians with equal and opposite half-biases at the hip and ankle to approximate foot orientation. These are exploratory position-drive offsets, not measured crow anatomy. Begin at 2 s, ramp by 3.5 s, retain the previous walking control and do not change the joint limit or inject forces. Lane 0 has zero bias and must match all previous recorded states and contacts; every lane must match before crouch onset. Different biases use different starts, so no cross-lane causal estimates; repeat candidates as matched trials.

Check physical failures, toe-speed caps, support, travel, toe release, contact velocities and reconstructed knee angle. Assess the entire 2–6 s interval for stability and include the walk, rather than hiding preparation failures. This 0–6 s diagnostic is not integrated with takeoff, flight or landing and must never be promoted directly. The prior experimental flight curl masking has not been qualified beyond 6 s. Corvid reference joint angles are phase- and speed-dependent, not a pass threshold for the mismatched study.

# Bounded stronger hip roll shifted weight but did not complete release

All eight constructed-perch runs finished 550 intervals with 32 valid contact observations per interval, no native errors, and a named-contact/observer normal-impulse discrepancy below 8e-10 N s over 4,400 last-substep comparisons. At sampled pre-release endpoints, the +0.10 rad arm moved the center of mass toward the right foot from 36.46 mm to 23.83 mm in one start and from 36.47 mm to 26.75 mm in the other; negative targets moved it away. Reconstructed feet matched native positions within 8.4e-8 m. Foot center is not center of pressure, and these are endpoint samples.

The positive and negative target arms never produced a qualified release interval; the zero-target controls achieved at most six and eight consecutive intervals, less than the required ten. None returned to bilateral grip; all roots dropped below 0.1 m in intervals 346–384. Every lane had at least one toe speed-limit hit at an observed control endpoint, including one at step 222; substep velocity was not recorded, so the true count may be higher. This test leaves the toe-flight/contact regression and full autonomous sequence unresolved. The trial used a constructed perch and estimated, non-specimen-calibrated parameters.

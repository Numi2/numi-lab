# Stronger measured disturbance remains inconclusive

The widened no-disturbance observer reproduced the archived straight-flight reference exactly before this trial. In lanes 6 and 7, the pre-aerodynamic observer measured opposite approximately 0.020 N m torques at every one of 400 substeps over intervals 902–926, with no applied force, correct signs, no other-lane wrench, no missing samples and no observed pulse outside the registered observation window. Shared inputs, pre-pulse and unchanged-lane parity, teacher-off mode, candidate continuous flight, physical bounds, substep coverage and observer checks all passed.

Nevertheless the maximum absolute pitch difference from the matched control was approximately 0.01182 and 0.01121 rad and peak world-y angular-rate difference approximately 0.03234 and 0.02590 rad/s. Both fall below the predeclared measurable-response gate of 0.04 rad or 0.10 rad/s. Differences at interval 1100 were smaller, but the initial response is insufficient to qualify recovery. The two noncandidate first-episode physical failures are retained with the complete control and gust traces, replay, source and binaries. This is not a perturbed-start test, flight-controller qualification, navigation or landing result.

# Contact-gated release pitch feedback screen

In matched constructed stationary-perch experiments, the unilateral-release strategy drifted into forward pitch and sideways roll after the return while its two-foot-hold controls remained upright. Test signed small bounded pitch and pitch-rate feedback on the existing physical hip position drives during release and regrip, with gains -0.5, -0.25, +0.25, +0.5 in two starts each. Keep the selected 0.4 s fore-aft velocity damping, -0.04 rad return placement, contact gates, toe drive bounds and 32 substeps; preserve all substep and state traces. Require ten consecutive clearance/opposite-foot support intervals, no fall or nonfoot support, and 100 terminal intervals of bilateral opposed supported grip. This is a narrow, constructed stationary test, not a landing, autonomous sequence, toe-speed-cap qualification or biological validation. Gain and start remain confounded.

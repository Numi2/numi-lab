# Development progress video, not a full mission

The 17.98-second video starts with the archived eight-second walking comparison: top views show a used-seed lane with an observed no-support interval, bottom views show a second lane without that observation in the same window. Those are development traces and the no-support observation alone does not prove a fall. The next ten seconds show side and front native renderer views of the same 500-frame reproduced environment-7 takeoff candidate from used seed 20261126. Both views are a simulation replay, not two independent trials. They end before a full waypoint mission or landing. A course obstacle partly obscures the side view at the end; the front view remains available.

The rendered mesh is an estimated crow driven by native accepted rigid-link and toe poses, not an exact animal reconstruction. Camera, feather registration, soft deformation and presentation are estimated. The native q trace and shifted observer agreement and their caveats, including lack of independently checked final velocity and body-transform forward kinematics, are documented in ../crow-v15-launch-candidate-replay-20261114/RESULT.md and ../crow-v15-observer-timing-audit-20261114/RESULT.md. This candidate has substantial pre-release wing motion and its matched held-leg control also flies; do not call it leg-only takeoff.

# Toe mass sensitivity with cleared starts

The exploratory v15 toes use 0.05 g per segment. Double and quadruple each segment while retaining the constructed foot total mass, center and inertia by the existing compensation code, using the 10 mm cleared reset. Damping, contact geometry, drive limits and gait are unchanged. This is a parameter sensitivity, not an anatomical calibration. Require all-substep toe speed and unchanged walking gates in every lane; any candidate must also pass new perturbations, takeoff, flight, grip and landing before adoption.

# Post-hoc contact sensor discrepancy localization

For previously retained 20261127 knee-plus-feedback development data, independently reconstruct fore-aft, normal-impulse-weighted foot contact velocity from last-substep q/v and loaded points and compare against the packed published sensor. Report per-side errors, loaded impulse and neighboring-step comparisons for all lanes, and list large discrepancies rather than hiding them in means. This is a diagnostic of the observed signal; it cannot establish ground-truth timing or causation. Offline loaded points use cutoff 1e-7 N s whereas the shader includes >1e-9 N s; q/v and contacts may differ in time. Treat small impulses, differing samples, event selection and quantization as possible explanations. No new controller or physical result is produced.

# Used-seed height response replicated; tracking remains imperfect

Without changing the compiled controller, development seed 20261127 replicated the registered identification: candidate lanes 6/7 completed their full 1800-step first episodes with matched same-seed reference prefixes, unchanged reference lanes 0–5, target reconstruction, physical/contact/effort coverage, height and speed gates. Height differences from same-lane straight-flight references over ticks 650–1150 reached +0.111 m and -0.133 m respectively. Baseline lanes 0/1 failed and are included.

In the posthoc ticks 1250–1800 window, mean root heights were 0.8963 m and 0.6496 m versus targets 0.80 m and 0.55 m, with mean absolute errors 0.0963 m and 0.0996 m. The control is therefore biased high even though the response identification passed. Both seeds and configurations were used in development; this is not unseen-layout or flight-controller qualification, nor evidence of takeoff, landing or biological realism.

# Walking stabilization counterfactual

The matched counterfactual temporarily enabled the inherited direct root yaw and pitch moment actions only during the 4–6 s gait interval. Against the integrated candidate with these actions disabled throughout, 1600 state endpoints and 25600 contact substeps matched exactly before 4 s. All eight no-moment candidates had 26–27 unloaded substeps, while all eight moment-enabled counterparts had none under the same contact threshold. This identifies a strong sensitivity to the permitted artificial stabilization; it does not show which axis matters or prove the applied moment values from policy outputs.

The intervention is not a physical solution and is not promoted. It is a development diagnostic on closely related starts, with uncalibrated aerodynamics. The next test isolates the two axes; a real fix must generate stabilization through physically modeled contacts, joints and forces and then pass the full handoff again.

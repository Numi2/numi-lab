# Contact-driven walking stride screen

Test whether modest hip-stroke increases produce physical forward progress without undermining support, toe speed or contact quality. Keep the prior 0.40 crouch, toe preload, unloaded-foot leg timing, 2 Hz gait and standard contact mechanics; use paired hip stroke coefficients .30 baseline, .40, .50 and .60 on eight previously used seed-20261127 starts, first six seconds with no scheduled reset. The 0.30 controls must exactly match their retained same-lane baseline. Measure 4–6 s root displacement and velocity, contact-point speed per foot, both-feet-unloaded substeps, nonfoot contacts, toe velocity limits and joint/body trajectories. Reject a candidate with any 4–6 s toe caps, new both-feet-unloaded substeps or per-foot p95 contact-point speed above 1.5x its same-lane baseline. This is a mechanical development screen, not an assumed crow stride-length target, integrated-slip measurement, or biological qualification.

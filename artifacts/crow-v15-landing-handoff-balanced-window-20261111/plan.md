# Wider stable landing window

The centered handoff diagnostic found 14 two-foot impulse intervals in lane 7, all just outside or farther from the 10 mm root-position tolerance; one later interval was within 20 mm and met the reconstructed velocity and attitude bounds. Test a 20 mm tolerance while retaining the pitch and pitch-rate limits and all other centered-control settings. Compact contact load and one-step alignment differ from this proxy, so it is not guaranteed to trigger. This is a development-data-selected sensitivity test, not an unused-layout evaluation or biological validation.

# Release trigger ablation

On the same used development seed and course, inhibiting only the release state transition left the route actor, wing law, body, approach and other landing-shader code fixed. All accepted q, v, actor outputs and endpoint contacts matched the release-enabled reference through first perch contact in all eight lanes. Lanes 2 and 7 first differed after contact at steps 3508 and 3350; without release both still later tipped and ended in physical failure at steps 3723 and 3509. Their longest qualifying half-weight opposing bilateral foot-support runs were 212 and 190 intervals, respectively; neither had qualifying support in the last 25 intervals. Seven lanes reached five waypoints and one stalled after three. All first-episode substep records were complete, with no recorded contact-capacity overflow.

This isolates release initiation at source level and indicates the unsupported outcome is not explained solely by initiating release. Normal directions are geometric support indicators, not proof of biological grasp. First fatal collider, continuous clearance, physical parameter calibration and performance on unused layouts remain unestablished.

# First toe-speed threshold during supported wing transition

In four of six push lanes (2, 3, 4, 7), the first recorded modeled toe-speed threshold occurred at tick 355, before that lane's first three-interval release. These first events were front proximal toe DOFs 26, 30 or 32, with positive foot normal impulses; pre-contact velocities were already approximately -11.4 to -11.5 rad/s before the recorded substep ended at -12 rad/s. Thus those first events cannot all be attributed solely to impact after a completed release. Lane 5's first event was a rear proximal toe after release; lane 6's first event also involved a rear proximal toe and occurred with rear distal toes contacting the ground, with its recorded pre-contact velocity near zero and end velocity -12 rad/s.

These are posthoc first-event associations in one used seed with correlated lanes. Pre-contact and detailed collider-pair logging covers only ticks 348–375; the post-precontact difference can include contact resolution and subsequent processing, and does not identify a unique solver or controller cause. Threshold events are simulator diagnostics, not measured crow speed limits or by themselves proof of a biological injury. This finding does not qualify takeoff, toe grip, landing or flight; preserve the source logs and analysis.

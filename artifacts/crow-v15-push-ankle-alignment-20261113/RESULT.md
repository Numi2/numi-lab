# Supported ankle-alignment screen: rejected

The 400-tick candidate and retained active-crouch control have matching sampled state/action/contact records through tick 345, complete first episodes, complete substep observations and no reported observer errors. The six tested lanes retained support until their first three unloaded intervals, then every lane recontacted beginning at tick 362. No setting satisfies the preregistered improvement or preliminary launch gates.

With normalized ankle end requests 0.40, 0.15 and -0.10, respectively, the lane pairs recorded toe-speed threshold counts 6/6, 11/8 and 20/19. The earliest push-minus-hold ground impulse deltas were 0.039/0.049, 0.081/0.084 and 0.072/0.100 N s. Whole-body COM rise from tick 350 through tick 400 was 0.0176/0.0176, 0.0535/0.0419 and 0.0430/0.0397 m. The 0.15 pair preserved early impulse but did not avoid toe events or recontact. The height measurement for one lane does not establish sustained flight. Wing working effort stayed within the common 3.0 bound.

These are correlated lanes on one used seed, with simulator position requests and uncalibrated anatomy. The recorded foot impulse is not isolated leg-muscle impulse. No candidate is promoted, and this does not qualify takeoff, flight, landing or biological realism. The referenced retained hold arm, logs, analysis and binaries are archived in Git.

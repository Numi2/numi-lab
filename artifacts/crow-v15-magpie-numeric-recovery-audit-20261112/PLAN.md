# Exploratory numeric related-corvid recovery comparison

Question: where do reconstructed v15 knee and distal ankle-proxy minima occur in the fraction of each recovery phase, compared with published black-billed magpie walking observations? Reanalyze the existing 800 Hz seed 20261127 eight-lane development record, using the existing contact segmentation and geometry unchanged. Compute minimum indices in each recovery divided by recovery substeps, for raw and 21.25 ms centered boxcar signals; report counts, side, lane and rejected windows, and compare descriptively with published means and SD. The knee geometry is a 3D interior angle; the distal ankle proxy and rear/front toe contact boundaries have not been anatomically validated and do not guarantee equivalence to the study. Their measurement was side-view 2D, with close-ups digitized at 250 Hz and complete strides additionally filmed at 50 Hz, 0.5–1.2 m/s, three magpies; this simulation is much slower. The reference values are not target tolerances, cannot be extrapolated as a crow fit, and observations in a lane are not independent birds. These previously observed data are exploratory, with no retrospective significance or controller qualification. Retain source and hashes.

Reference: Verstappen et al. 2000, DOI 10.1242/jeb.203.14.2159, Table 1: knee minimum in recovery 27.3 ± 7.9% (16 observations); ankle minimum 52.3 ± 9.3% (16 observations). Both values are percentages of recovery duration.

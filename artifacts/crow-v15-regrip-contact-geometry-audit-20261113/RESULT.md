# Retrospective return-foot contact and geometry audit

In the matched baseline lane, the minimum gap between a left foretoe collision box and the perch was approximately 12.6 mm at zero-based tick 209, the first accepted return state; at tick 240 it remained approximately 6.5 mm. The earlier pre-release pose had a small negative geometric gap and recorded foretoe contact. After return, the baseline had 13 of 112 pre-fall accepted last-substep samples with a positive front normal, but none of the intervals met the stricter combined bilateral opposing-grip and posture screen. Faster closure showed 7 and 10 such samples; the two hip-placement candidates showed none. These are accepted endpoint samples and geometric calculations, not a complete substep contact history or proof of cause.

The smaller and larger positive-hip placement candidates moved the supported system rather than producing the simple fixed-root backward foot shift: at tick 230 the foot centers were x=0.0393 and 0.0380 m versus x=0.0370 m in the control. A separate fixed-state search predicts that +0.15 rad knee and -0.30 rad ankle offsets at tick 208 could bring the foot from x=0.0371 to x=0.0295 m while maintaining about 1.4 mm geometric clearance, assuming every other joint and the root are fixed. That is an unexecuted geometric hypothesis, not a contact or stability result. It motivates a gradual physically simulated swing while clear with measured support and regrip gates. No results establish landing, flight qualification or biological fidelity.

# Retrospective regrip geometry and contact audit

Across the archived supported-pitch control, rate-limited hip-return test and contact-gated faster-toe test, inspect physically reached pre-fall states after return on the constructed perch. For the returning foot and each of its eight front/rear toe links, compute the same separating-axis box/perch gap used in the validated clearance analyzer, preserve their recorded centers, and tabulate accepted last-substep contact impulses and front/rear normal components. Compare the matched starting pose, selected return/post-return times and first-fall boundary. Quantify whether front toe collision boxes reach the perch and whether recorded contact normals meet the opposing test; distinguish a sample with geometric overlap from a measured contact and do not infer causality or assume an unmeasured contact substep. This is a posthoc model diagnostic on used constructed starts, not a qualification or real-bird finding.

# Walk-to-launch handoff screen

Extending the crouch command prevents the six-second transition spike in eight closely related seed-20261126 starts, but that command and the walking gait still stop at 10 s. In the same used starts, test four handoff settings over 575 steps: lanes 0–1 preserve the hard 10-second cutoff, lanes 2–3 retain the crouch through 10.30 s, lanes 4–5 fade it from 9.70 to 10.30 s, and lanes 6–7 fade from 9.70 to 10.50 s. All other controls, including the existing launch and unqualified flight controller, remain unchanged. Require same-lane accepted state and contact parity to the uniform-crouch trajectory through step 485, full toe and contact substep coverage through the observed first episode, no caps, overflow or nonfoot ground impulses, and at least one loaded foot at every substep from 10.0 to 10.3 s. Report cap timing, landing/failure, normal impulse, and any later airborne status without assuming the jump is leg-powered. An engineering screen does not qualify the flight law, prove real bird mechanics or establish takeoff. These are correlated development starts, and the model includes hip pitch and roll but lacks an explicit hip yaw coordinate, so it does not reproduce all measured 3D magpie hip mechanics.

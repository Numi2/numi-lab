# Terminal supported landing screen

Use the measured 0.45004 kg toe body and final-substep impulses with 0.005 s substeps. For each first episode, require all five waypoints, 3000-step survival, no physical or solver failure, and 25 consecutive final accepted endpoints with each foot/toes on the perch at >0.001 Ns, upward normal impulse at least half the body weight times substep duration in aggregate (0.5*0.45004*9.81*0.005), no non-foot perch contact, root tilt at most 0.35 rad and root speed at most 0.12 m/s. Also require at least three consecutive endpoints of opposing fore/rear normals on both feet in the terminal interval, with each component over 0.00005 Ns. This conservative normal-only support proxy omits vertical friction and samples only the final substep; it is not continuous-time proof. First episode and exact source/runtime remain authoritative; failures are not dropped.

# Autonomous walking and launch audit

The privileged gait control diverged from the teacher-off release replay at endpoint 102 in every lane, so it cannot certify the autonomous sequence. Direct audit of the teacher-off replay instead found three alternating left and right foot clearance bouts in each of eight lanes over the selected two-second walking window, about 3.2 cm forward progress, no nonfoot ground-contact intervals, and no simultaneous endpoint clearance of both feet. The summed vertical foot impulse was below half estimated body-weight impulse in two intervals per lane; this is not a full stability or biological qualification. All eight had two unloaded rising intervals at 353, no ground recontact through step 500 and wing-on rise, but this alone does not prove the legs caused takeoff. A matched wing-suppressed counterfactual is separate. Replay geometry was validated against the accepted kinematics with a one-step observer offset.

# Reduced baseline twist: failed actuator gate

On used seed 20261126, reducing symmetric twist amplitude from 0.06 to 0.03 rad retained continuous unloading through interval 1800 in both candidate lanes and produced signed heading changes of -0.290 and +0.289 rad relative to the matching straight trial. Heights during intervals 450–1800 were 0.413–0.857 m and 0.547–1.214 m. However, modeled pronation effort remained at the 0.15 Nm limit in 47.9% and 48.1% of the turn-window samples, and peak absolute tracking errors were 0.103 rad in both lanes. Both fail the preregistered <33% saturation and <0.08 rad tracking gates and are not promoted. The prior full-twist test had about 66% saturation, so the result motivates a separately registered ablation but does not justify changing the gates.

The candidates matched the original turn's q/v, policy-action and contact stream exactly for 500 intervals, before the new ramp; unchanged lanes matched through their first episodes. Lanes 0 and 1 failed at intervals 760 and 764, as in the prior run. The teacher was off, expected shader and shared inputs matched, and candidate foot, toe and actuator substep coverage was complete with no reported observer errors, later ground contact, nonfoot support or toe-speed threshold. The logged target reconstruction passed. Contact/state epoch alignment in the candidate lanes remains inferred. The used-seed experiment is not a disturbed-start test, general flight qualification, landing, frozen controller or unseen-layout navigation.

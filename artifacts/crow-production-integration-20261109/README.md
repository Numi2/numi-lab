# Integrated v15 hierarchical navigation, development status

The native rollout and training commands expose a separately fingerprinted hierarchical v15 task. It uses the frozen flight law and bounded high-level actor commands; a deterministic route actor is selected before the last waypoint and an independent deterministic landing actor afterward. Teacher labels are enabled only explicitly, and the policy selector rejects a stochastic or critic-bearing primary actor. This selector is for deterministic execution, not PPO likelihood evaluation.

The four targeted runtime and embodiment checks passed. Legacy v15 behavior, both with and without its teacher, reproduced exactly for the checked 800-step trajectory and first-episode summaries. A safety guard rejected an incompatible stochastic/critic policy; the initial guard attempt is retained and failed earlier argument validation rather than exercising that guard. The generated production source reproduces from the specified ancestor and saved experimental source.

Production and the earlier experimental build were not physically identical: small numerical differences grew and changed two first-episode results. Their measurements must not be combined. A production-derived, opt-in observer matched all first-episode outcomes and one full sampled state trajectory exactly against the ordinary production library; see the observer parity report. The observer records commands selected by the policy, not the downstream filtered joint actuation. The obstacle clearance measurement is Euclidean box distance sampled at accepted 20 ms control-step endpoints, not a continuous-time minimum.

Integrated development evaluation of the first gate-corrected route and landing specialists found 10, 10 and 13 supported finishes of 16 per course. The second route correction found 12, 3 and 15, respectively, showing course-specific regression. These are teacher-off, first-episode trials on development layouts; they do not establish held-out layout success. The user-reserved final layouts and seeds remain untouched.

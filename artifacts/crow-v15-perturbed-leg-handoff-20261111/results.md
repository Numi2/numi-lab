# Bounded start perturbations for the physical takeoff handoff

The unperturbed control lane reproduced the prior candidate exactly for all 500 recorded q/v and substep-contact intervals. Across two newly selected development seeds and all 16 bounded initial conditions, the paired wing-suppressed controls met the leg-only jump criterion, and each executed flight matched its paired leg-only q/v and contact observations through the first two unloaded rising intervals. Both toe and sole endpoint boxes cleared the ground by at least 6.29 mm in those intervals. All 16 continued airborne without ground recontact through step 500, met the predeclared height, forward-speed and tilt gates, and had no recorded native failures or terminations. Independent reconstructed body poses matched the native replay within 7.0e-9 m.

These are perturbations at episode initialization, not disturbances applied at takeoff. Their forward-velocity spread had mostly settled by the push: approximately 0.00596–0.00649 m/s across the paired starts. They do not establish robustness to disturbances immediately before takeoff. The minimum root x was about 0.223–0.224 m behind its pre-push position, which must be accounted for in route clearance. Endpoint geometry does not prove swept clearance. Neither seed was a reserved final layout; full navigation, grip, supported landing and biological realism remain unqualified.

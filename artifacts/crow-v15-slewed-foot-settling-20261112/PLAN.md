# Slewed contact-triggered foot placement, preregistered

The previous paired-offset candidate improved toe loading delay in six of eight trials but all lost support and exceeded the foot-speed limit. Its rear-only condition switched 10–17 times per side over the two-second window, and directly switched the position target. Test whether a bounded per-foot contact-driven ramp improves the coupled behavior. Lanes 4-5 ramp up/down one seventh of their prior -0.03/+0.05 rad knee/ankle offsets each 20 ms control step; lanes 6-7 ramp one quarter of their prior -0.04/+0.06 offsets. The publisher stores each 3-bit level with the experimental dedicated sensor and clears it when the sensor is invalid. No extra body force, visual animation or synthetic reset is used. Lanes 0-3 are unchanged controls; use the same two prior development seeds and full 800 Hz contact record.

Require exact pre-intervention parity and recorded state/contact/q/v parity for controls 0-3, check the actual ramp level changes by one per valid step against the recorded contact bits, and apply all prior support, toe-limit, nonfoot, contact-speed, progress, knee, recovery and >=15% toe-lag gates. Compare each lane to its same-lane no-placement baseline, and report the earlier abrupt-drive trial separately. Preserve all failures. This experimental state and checkpoint schema, one-step sensor delay, correlated starts, short slow walk and model toe proxies preclude production, biological or complete mission qualification even if a lane passes.

Keeping the existing balance and placement feedback active when the stance foot retained the measured upward load delayed the first root fall from one-based interval 360 to 365, but neither lane achieved even one interval of simultaneous bilateral opposing-toe grip, support, posture and speed. The candidate reached the accepted toe-speed threshold on both feet before falling, whereas the matched control did not. Neither lane produced a positive front-toe normal impulse in the logged post-return observations. Both had only 41 strict release intervals before the intentionally early return. The intervention fails the preregistered grip and speed gates and is not promoted.

The control exactly reproduced all 450 prior original-placement state, command and event records; the candidate matched before the first altered support decision at zero-based tick 289. All 32 contact substeps were present with no reported native/observer errors or nonfoot impulse, and the runtime binary matches its prebuilt hash. Contact/state epochs remain unverified; accepted toe-speed samples do not bound substep speeds or drive effort. This is a stationary constructed-perch failure, not walking, airborne landing or biological qualification.

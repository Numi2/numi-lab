# Result: mixed partial support, no qualified landing

All eight teacher-guided development starts reached five waypoints and physically touched the perch. At the 4200-interval cutoff, three first episodes had failed and five remained active; the latter are censored, not passes. Four lanes showed 80–103 consecutive intervals with bilateral opposing toe impulses, upright low-speed posture and upward perch impulse of at least half nominal weight. This is partial support only: wings or other forces may supply the rest. Three of these four episodes terminated or had already terminated by the cutoff; the fourth was still active, without a qualified landing.

Compared with its own prior aligned-descent start, lane 2 improved from a failure at 3799 with no qualified partial-support interval to 101 consecutive intervals and remained active at cutoff; lanes 0 and 7 instead failed before cutoff where the prior runs remained active. Lane 3 fell later but its partial-support interval shortened from 106 to 80. Gains and starts are confounded, so these are exploratory paired observations, not a gain ranking. All starts matched their prior q, v and policy actions through the tested pre-contact prefix. Four first episodes still reached the toe velocity cap after ground handoff; no missing first-episode substeps or execution errors were detected. No explicit release was tested. This is neither autonomous nor held-out validation, and no candidate is promoted.

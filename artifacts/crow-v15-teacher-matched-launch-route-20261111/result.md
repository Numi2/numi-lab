# Matched-launch teacher route

With teacher-on, the frozen flight law, the 35-body development crow and no scheduled resets, all eight first episodes reached all five waypoint criteria on the development course, between steps 2567 and 2709, without reported physical failure through step 3000. Accepted q/v, actor output and endpoint contacts through observer step 399 match the original teacher-off reference for every lane. These are privileged-teacher results, not autonomous actor performance.

There was no foot-support step and no observed perch contact. At the 3000-step horizon all eight remained airborne, so waypoint completion is not a supported landing; the trial is incomplete as a full physical demonstration. Contact records are accepted endpoints rather than complete substep contact records, and the actual low-level actuator stream was not recorded. This is one previously used development course and seed, not either reserved final layout.

The exact host and policies are in crow-v15-hip-roll-walk-launch-controls-20261111/runtime.tar.gz, the parity shader is in crow-v15-teacher-launch-parity-candidate-20261111/runtime.tar.gz, and the corrected replay writer is in crow-v15-replay-writer-roundtrip-20261111/runtime.tar.gz. Input hashes bind the run to these files.

# Toe contact-sensor isolation

Neither removing the rear-toe wrench groups nor removing those groups plus toe members from foot-support groups restored flight tracking: both arms passed zero of eight lanes and had no physical or solver failures. Each arm matched the full-sensor baseline q/v at all 128 available sampled lane/time points. These tests do not support contact-group layout as the cause of this flight regression, within the tested fixed-head flight setup. The sensor-removed models are diagnostic only and cannot establish contact-driven grip or release.

# Reduced descent compensation: rejected

On the same used development layout and seed, the changed controller exactly matched recorded q, v, actor actions and endpoint contacts before phase-one entry in all eight lanes (2980–3116 samples per lane). All eight reached five waypoint criteria. Five first episodes physically fell versus two in the reference; no lane finished with the final 25 consecutive bilateral opposed-toe half-weight intervals, versus one reference lane. The altered descent is rejected.

Five candidate lanes versus four reference lanes obtained at least ten consecutive half-weight foot-support intervals, but all five candidate lanes subsequently fell; more initial support did not translate to stability. Lane 3 achieved 135 consecutive opposed-toe support intervals before falling. The trial therefore does not isolate a beneficial acquisition fix or establish its cause. Full first-episode records, early resets, toe caps, commands, all 16 substep contact records and the source/metallib fingerprints are retained. There were no missing recorded first-episode substeps, observer errors or contact-capacity overflows. Contact is sampled at substep boundaries, and this development run does not demonstrate biological realism, reliable release or unused-layout performance. The frozen flight source and all other body and controller settings were unchanged.

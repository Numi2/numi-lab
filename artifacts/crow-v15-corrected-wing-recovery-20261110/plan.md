Restore full 31 q and 30 v values, observed filtered normalized controller actions, flight clock and phase, and recomputed feedback; the rest of hidden state is reconstructed, so this is not continuous execution. Cross each source with ten deterministic perturbations: none, roll +/-0.15 rad, pitch +/-0.10 rad, yaw +/-0.15 rad, lateral velocity +/-0.15 m/s, and the prior combined roll/pitch/yaw/lateral-velocity/roll-rate perturbation. With obstacles disabled, 0.70 m/s and 0.85 m targets, run 800 ticks and require zero solver/physical failures, then after 6 seconds every lane's sampled heading <=0.15 rad, lateral speed <=0.20 m/s, speed error <=0.20 m/s and height error <=0.15 m at steps 300,400,500,600,700. Missing data fail. Record all outcomes. Only development seed 18721911 is used; final layouts/seeds remain unconsumed. A pass is a bounded simulated recovery diagnostic, not real crow validation, walking, takeoff, route or landing qualification.

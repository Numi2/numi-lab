# Matched obstacle-free control: flight instability persists

The five course collision masks were disabled while locomotion_ground retained its full collision mask. Fail-closed mismatch and exact actor-layer/transform rebinding are retained. Every candidate lane matched the obstacle-present long run exactly in accepted q, v, policy actions and contacts through the control interval before its first scene collision; see counterfactual-parity.json. No course collision was used for this control.

All six first episodes still terminated with CONTACT reason 3 at ticks 637, 635, 655, 654, 634 and 657 (lanes 2-7), well before the planned tick 900. Foot recontact occurred at ticks 553, 553, 585, 586, 520 and 534 respectively. Sampled later forward speeds reached 2.23-2.40 m/s, above the 1 m/s flight screen, while root heights reached about 0.164-0.166 m. Toe-speed threshold events ranged from 66 to 91 per lane. Each termination trace contains a lower-beak/ground contact record; its normal impulse was positive for lanes 3, 5 and 7 and zero in the other three terminal traces. Other toe/ground records and exact pre-contact q/v/actions are in fatal-contact.json. Do not equate a zero-impulse contact record with a measured impact, or infer a solver defect.

This matched control establishes that disabling the obstacles alone does not produce stable flight in these trials; the obstacle-present run additionally hit course objects. All pairs failed the preregistered long-flight screen. Later dispatch samples may belong to reset episodes and are not continuous execution. Aero and individual flap-effort measurements after tick 500 were not captured. The result concerns a research actuator/aero model, one used development layout/seed and correlated lanes, not biological realism, qualified or frozen flight, navigation or landing. The untouched final layouts and seeds remain reserved.

The original compressed stderr stream is stored as ordered 40 MiB parts, with exact byte size and hash in stderr-parts.json. Run restore-stderr.py before reanalysis. Exact host and observer sources, binaries, policy packs, scene-mask patch, observations and failures are retained here.

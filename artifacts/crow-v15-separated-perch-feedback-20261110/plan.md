# Separated-start hybrid perch test

The previous stationary bench included an initially overlapping rear toe. Test all eight existing perturbations with the whole root raised 15 mm, retaining initial pose/shape data so clearance is measured before any step. This is a drop-and-settle developmental test, not a replay of a real flight approach. In each start compare original closure with per-toe 0.01 rad/step contact-limited closure, under the matched hybrid/SAT collision and original body, drives and timestep. The arms now have identical starts and must have no initial toe/perch overlap; other body shapes are separately audited. The original previously retained trace is not expected to match because the initial height differs. Record all simulation failures, bilateral opposing grip with support, release, sustained open-toe support, and grip-phase SAT overlap. Require all eight initial poses to be separated, at least 6/8 complete bench passes, no rollback and worst grip overlap at most 0.5 mm to nominate a candidate for further work. No reserved final layout is used, and passing does not establish realistic biology, flight, walking, takeoff or full autonomous landing.

# Separated-start hybrid toe feedback result

Independent SAT checks of all authored bird collision boxes against the perch confirmed all 16 arm/start combinations were separated before simulation. Minimum gap was 13.093 mm for all boxes and 13.203 mm for toes. All runs completed without a reported solver rollback. With the original controller, 4/8 starts passed the grip, release and terminal-support check; with individual-toe feedback, 2/8 passed. Maximum grip-conditioned rigid-box overlap was 4.920 mm and 5.582 mm respectively. Neither meets the prespecified 6/8 and 0.5 mm screen. These results are development drop tests, not flight approach or biological qualification. In case 1, overlap first appeared at endpoint 2, during the open-toe settling phase before grip commands, exceeded 0.5 mm at endpoint 3, and later increased; this cannot be explained solely by the closure command or the previous bench's pre-existing intersection.

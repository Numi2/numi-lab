# Ankle target range under physical grip

The independently replayed 1,000-tick ankle candidate tracked its physical joint targets but spent most of the supported phase at its 0.12 rad ankle-correction cap before tipping. Test whether additional bounded joint-target range can sustain both support and grip, while keeping authored ankle limits, existing effort/velocity limits, body damping, actual contact feedback, hip law and aft-COM target unchanged. In 16 isolated lanes, retain three exact reference controllers (no rear feedback, original rear feedback, and previous 0.12-cap candidate). Other lanes compare six correction caps 0.14, 0.16, 0.18, 0.20, 0.24 and 0.28 rad at gains -1.25 and -2, plus cap 0.32 at gain -2, smoothly blended as before after grip. Target displacement is relative to the -0.16 rad default ankle and remains within the existing [-0.9,0.7] rad joint limit. Compare all lanes over 500 physical ticks with no reset; require all to match early samples and all three reference traces to match exactly. Require terminal 100 bilateral opposed grip/support samples, overlap at most 0.5 mm, no nonfoot support, tilt/speed gates and no solver failure. Any survivor needs independent long, perturbed, release, flight and full-route validation; no held-out layout is used. This is not a claim that these controller gains are biologically calibrated.

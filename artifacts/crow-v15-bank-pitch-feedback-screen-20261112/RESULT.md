# Wing-sweep pitch feedback during stronger-bank trials

All 48 pre-intervention q/v/action samples exactly matched the corresponding bank-only control. All first-episode contact substeps were present, with no logged solver errors or overflows. At the strongest 0.24-rad bank setting, positive-direction lane 4 first had a nonfoot ground-contact substep at interval 1381 and terminated for physical failure at interval 1466. The bank-only control had its first such contact at 1215 and failed at 1302. Feedback delayed these events in this one development trial, but both failed; the observer does not establish that contact caused termination.

The mild-bank pair reached interval 1600 and passed the predeclared sampled height, pitch, minimum-speed and 15%-heading-improvement engineering screen. Their final heading errors were still 1.218 and 1.308 rad to ±90° requests; the matched bank-only errors were 1.220 and 1.312 rad. That is a very small change, and neither bird completed the requested turn. At the middle bank, one lane exceeded the height limit; at the strongest bank, the opposite lane exceeded it and retained 1.417 rad heading error. The middle and strong parameter pairs therefore failed. Earlier direct body moments were suppressed only after 9 s.

This is one used seed, no obstacles, sparse endpoint samples and arbitrary engineering bounds. It does not establish biological flight, useful commanded turning, disturbed-start recovery, climb/descent, a qualified or frozen controller, navigation or landing. Do not promote the controller from the partial mild-bank pass.

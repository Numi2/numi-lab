# Bounded wing-sweep feedback: stable but no demonstrated gain

In six obstacle-free development lanes, with the old direct yaw/pitch body moments disabled after 9 s, bounded bilateral wing-sweep pitch feedback reached interval 1600 without physical failure, nonfoot ground contact, missing contact substeps or logged errors. All 48 sampled pre-intervention q/v/action states matched the same-lane control exactly. At the 12 sampled times from intervals 500–1600, candidate height was 0.918–0.972 m and absolute pitch was at most 0.077 rad, passing the predeclared sampled bounds for all gain pairs.

However, the matched calm controls also passed the same sampled height/pitch bounds: heights 0.910–0.960 m and absolute pitch at most 0.082 rad. At the final sample the feedback lanes moved at only 0.176–0.197 m/s and retained 1.222–1.361 rad of error to their ±90° commanded headings. The experiment therefore does not demonstrate improved flight stability relative to the control, a useful turn, or meaningful route flight. The old direct body moments were still active before 9 s. A follow-up must compare the same feedback against the strong-bank intervention that previously lost altitude and failed, and separately measure speed, heading and perturbed recovery. These are sampled engineering limits, not biological flight validation, a qualified/frozen controller, an unused-layout test, or a supported landing.

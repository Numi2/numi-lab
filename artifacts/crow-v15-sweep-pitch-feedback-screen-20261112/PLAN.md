# Bounded wing-sweep pitch feedback

A matched open-loop screen found that equal-sign sweep residuals changed pitch in the same direction at the first shared sample; holding ±0.20 caused later excessive-tilt failures. Test closed-loop correction using accepted pitch and pitch rate through both physical sweep drives only, from 9 s onward, with gains 0.20, 0.40, and 0.80 and a ±0.12 normalized residual bound. Pairs use the existing two opposite 90-degree development commands. The pitch target is zero for this diagnostic; it is not a complete speed/height/turn controller. Before 9 s, the older controller still uses direct body moments; both identified moments are suppressed after 9 s.

Require exact 48 sampled pre-intervention q/v/action records, complete first-episode contact substeps and no logged errors or overflow. For an engineering stability pass, both lanes in a gain pair must run to interval 1600 without physical failure or post-intervention nonfoot contact, with all available 500–1600 sampled root heights in 0.65–1.15 m and absolute pitch below 0.35 rad. Also report matched height, pitch, speed and heading throughout; sparse samples do not prove continuous bounds. A pass would be a preliminary obstacle-free development result, not biological validation, qualified turns, perturbed recovery, a frozen controller or route/landing success.

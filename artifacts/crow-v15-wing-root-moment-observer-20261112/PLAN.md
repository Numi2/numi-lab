# Reconstruct per-wing aerodynamic moment about the root

Extend the already parity-checked before/after flapping-kernel wrench observer to record all source-state q and v at the same substeps in the same two one-second windows. Replay the same obstacle-free bank screen with the same source and policy. Check complete sparse q/v/action parity, recorded wrench parity against the earlier observer, and CPU forward-kinematic wing/root poses against the native replay at a matching source state. Then sum kernel-applied wing and root wrenches shifted to the root COM, retaining left/right contributions in world axes. This is the moment of the modeled flapping-kernel loads, not a direct measurement of biological forces, actuator torque, whole-body angular momentum rate, or all external forces. Its accuracy depends on body index/frame and source-phase validation. No controller is promoted.

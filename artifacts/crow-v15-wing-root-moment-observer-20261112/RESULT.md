# Modeled wing moments about the body

The root-moment observer reproduced all 72 sparse q/v/policy samples and all 9,600 previously recorded flapping-kernel wrench samples exactly. Its CPU forward-kinematic root and wing COM positions, using the same source q/v, matched one native replay frame to 5.52e-8 m; source q matched to 4.75e-9. This validates that sampled frame and alignment, not every state or the biological model. Each per-wing COM torque was shifted to the root body COM using its simultaneous force and COM offset; the root wrench written by the same kernel was added. Values are world-axis modeled moments, not net moment from all forces or measured crow aerodynamics.

In the +/-0.12 wingbeat-asymmetry lanes over 9–10 s, the left/right mean yaw moments about root were -0.0363/+0.0317 and -0.0328/+0.0376 N m. The combined kernel yaw moments, including the root contribution, were -0.00465/+0.00480 N m, opposite the respective requested heading signs. During 14–15 s they were -0.00123/+0.00123 N m. The 0.24 lanes showed combined means -0.01031/+0.01000 N m initially and -0.00246/+0.00247 later. Wing contributions partly cancel, so evaluating either wing alone would be misleading. Mean roll moments also changed substantially over these windows; the strong 0.36 settings produced large moments and the previous test showed altitude gain and wrong-way final heading.

These observations do not show that moment sign alone predicts the heading trajectory: body orientation, inertia, angular momentum, remaining external forces and controller response interact. The old direct body moments remained before 9 s, the sampled windows are not the whole maneuver, and the aerodynamic closure lacks empirical calibration. Next compare per-substep moment and angular acceleration under controlled one-sided wing perturbations and no direct moments, including both signs and attitude/speed limits, before fitting a bounded physical turn controller. No controller is frozen or promoted.

## Scope correction: tail contribution omitted

The reported `combined_kernel_about_root` sums the left wing, right wing and root-body wrench only. The simulated crow tail is a separate body (index 7); its flapping-kernel force was not recorded in this observer and is omitted from that sum. It must not be interpreted as the total aerodynamic or whole-kernel moment. The per-wing contributions and root-body contribution themselves remain as recorded. A tail-inclusive observer and replay-frame checks are needed to determine the full kernel moment.

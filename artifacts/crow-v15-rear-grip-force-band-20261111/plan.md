# Rear-toe impulse bands for supported grip

Actual rear-toe impulses in the reacquisition trial were often close to the 0.0004 N s lower threshold even as the body tipped. With the same contact-driven bounded rate, 0.85 rad target limit, 0.010 N s high-impulse relief threshold, leg controller and body, compare two independently initialized 1,000-tick development runs using lower thresholds 0.002 and 0.005 N s per 20 ms. This tests whether earlier force regulation helps; impulse is not a direct measurement of muscle force and no parameter is claimed biologically calibrated. Require exact reference parity in both runs through tick 85, opposing toe contacts, supported stance with no other-body support and speed/tilt limits, maximum 0.5 mm independent toe overlap during grip, and 100 terminal samples of bilateral support and grip. Separate processes avoid the diagnostic observer counter issue. Failed and excessive-penetration candidates will be retained and rejected. This does not establish walking, departure, full navigation or held-out performance.

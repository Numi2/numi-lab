# Integrated development route with hybrid CCD

One prior-used J layout and seed ran from the ground through 4500 control steps under the privileged guide with the research v15 body, toe controller, wing inertia, hybrid CCD, and SAT box query. All five waypoint triggers were reached by step 2787, with no reported failed environment steps. This does not qualify navigation or landing: the guide was on, the layout and seed were already used in development, and no sustained supported perch was achieved by the horizon. After waypoint five, logged contact had just one sample meeting bilateral load criteria and five endpoint samples with any foot/toe-perch impulse. No sample showed opposed contact on both feet, and the supported-landing phase was never reached.

In flight before waypoint five, after at least 25 consecutive endpoint-observed contact-free samples per foot, the maximum toe-angle magnitude was 0.000380 radians across 3,928 foot-samples. This is a bounded endpoint observation, not a proof of no intermittent substep contact or of intentional release after a successful landing. The gait and launch portions here have not yet passed the independent matched no-push, slip, and contact qualification on this changed body and collision configuration.

An initial all-body CCD attempt overflowed the old authored CCD candidate capacity; see initial-capacity-overflow.md for the retained failure and logging loss. A topology-derived capacity allowed this full screen. Policies were rebound byte-identically in learned tensors for diagnostics only, and their fingerprints document the changed world. This is not an autonomous-policy success and it does not touch the reserved K/L layouts.

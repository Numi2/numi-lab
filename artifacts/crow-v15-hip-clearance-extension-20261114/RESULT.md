# Wider released-hip correction: improved clearance, failed support

Widening the released hip's clearance correction cap from 0.21 to 0.26 rad increased the longest consecutive interval satisfying the preregistered released-foot clearance and opposite-stance gates from 24 to 34 (steps 241–274). It did not meet the 100-step requirement. The foot recontacted, and root height fell below 0.1 m at step 378 versus 375 for the matched control. The candidate's lateral roll target reached its 0.25 rad control cap by step 270; body pitch subsequently grew and the stance hip correction reached its 0.10 rad cap by step 300. These associations do not establish causality.

The control reproduced all 450 archived baseline states, the paired prefix was exact for 255 states, the input binary matched its prebuild hash, feedback and accepted joint bounds reconstructed, and all contact substeps were present without recorded errors or nonfoot support. The accepted-state stance toe reached 12 rad/s only at or after root-height failure; substep toe extrema and effort were not measured. Toes remained open; regrip and landing were not tested. State/contact epochs remain independently unverified. This constructed stationary perch trial does not establish walking, flight, or biological realism.

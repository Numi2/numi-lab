# Frozen-pose front-toe coordinate sensitivity

Starting from six recorded hold-arm step-347 poses in the larger-ankle-offset test, forward kinematics changed only the three left proximal front-toe coordinates. At +0.10 rad the middle front-toe group had estimated clearance between approximately -0.9 and +0.6 mm across these poses, versus +1.5–2.4 mm without the perturbation. At +0.20 rad the estimate penetrated the ground by approximately 2.9–4.4 mm. Negative perturbations in this range did not lower the closest middle toe. The baseline states are the same poses for which the preceding audit checked FK against native replay; this perturbation itself has no replay validation.

This is frozen-pose geometry, not a physically simulated motion; reaction forces and posture changes could invalidate the prediction, as earlier ankle trials demonstrated. It is not a measured crow angle or a command recommendation. The +0.10 rad change lies within the source model's -0.3 to +1.2 rad joint limits for the sampled initial values, but joint-limit compliance does not establish safe contact, natural toe articulation or launch success. A bounded matched dynamics test with gradual commands is needed before interpreting contact effects.

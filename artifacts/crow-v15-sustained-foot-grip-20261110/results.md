# Sustained-foot front-toe trigger

In the used J original development trial, both feet remained loaded while the baseline front closure counters stayed zero through step 1800. With the new trigger, front counters reached 48 after sustained current foot load and prior load debounce. This establishes the intended trigger response in that trial, not a reliable grip: neither J original nor I original established sampled opposing toe grip, and each fell at steps 1848 and 1998 after reaching five waypoints. The other used J start hit slalom A at step 1108 after waypoint two, before landing, unchanged from the matched launch candidate. No trial passed the terminal support and grip gate. The toe rule preserves counter decay when load is absent before phase-two latching, but general in-flight behavior and deliberate supported release are not qualified. The implementation is experimental and is not promoted.

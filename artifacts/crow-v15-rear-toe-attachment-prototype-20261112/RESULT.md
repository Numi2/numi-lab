# Isolated rear-toe attachment prototype: native free-space checks

The current 40-DoF model is copied and both rear-toe assemblies are shifted forward until their proximal neutral collision envelopes meet the rear edges of the existing sole boxes. The result closes the measured 34 mm collision gap but is an engineering topology correction, not a measured American-crow hinge position. Toe shapes and extents, joint drives, DOF defaults, all non-foot body properties and the remaining joint fields are unchanged. The corrected geometry is unpromoted.

The articulated solver treats a body origin as its center of mass, so the accepted prototype shifts the foot frame, compensates the incoming and all outgoing joint anchors and sole local shape, and recalculates the foot inertia. The initial centerOfMass-field-only prototype failed the momentum check; its source and output remain in first-attempt files. At neutral articulated toe pose, the revised assembly keeps mass unchanged, each foot first-moment difference below 4.0e-12 kg m, and its y/z moments below 3.1e-14 kg m²; the remaining neutral envelope gap is within 3e-9 m. Across 36 common rigid-foot states the kinetic-energy difference was 1.1e-13 J, linear momentum difference 1.3e-12 and angular momentum difference 9.8e-13; unchanged body positions differed by at most 6.99e-10 m. The initial 1e-10 m position assertion failed and was revised after seeing the float-scale result; ROUNDING-DEVIATION.md retains the deviation.

On the modified 40-DoF model, 12 free-space native GPU lanes were deterministic and agreed with CPU forward dynamics to maximum scaled acceleration error 2.71e-8 using the matching explicit metallib. This does not test contacts, policy compatibility, standing, walking, takeoff, flight, grip or landing. A preflight and short contact smoke must precede any longer changed-body test, and the existing controller cannot be considered qualified for this new model.

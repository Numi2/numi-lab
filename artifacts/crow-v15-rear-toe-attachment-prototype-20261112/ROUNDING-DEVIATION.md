The revised prototype passed momentum and energy checks at ~1e-12 but failed an initially coded 1e-10 m unchanged-body position assertion with max difference 6.99e-10 m. The preregistered plan required floating-point tolerance rather than that numerical value. Because 32-bit anchor compensation itself quantizes at this scale, the code threshold was changed after observing the result to 2e-9 m. The first output and failure are preserved in probe2.stdout/stderr; this post hoc threshold is not a prespecified pass.

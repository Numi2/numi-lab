# Refined frozen-pose sensitivity

In a post-hoc finer grid on the same used touchdown poses, 46 of 56 events per seed had at least one offset that lowered the middle front-toe box by 1 mm, kept rear-group displacement within 0.5 mm and kept all foot boxes above -0.5 mm. The fixed pair knee -0.03 rad and ankle +0.05 rad satisfied this geometric screen in 42 of 56 events on each seed; knee -0.04 and ankle +0.06 satisfied 39 of 56. Zero-offset geometry exactly reproduced the earlier audit. Repeated events and related starts are not independent, and the offsets were selected using these same data.

The body and other joints were frozen, and these are native joint coordinates. Actuator feasibility, joint limits, support forces, other-foot stability, contact delays, biological data, and loaded slip were not tested. The result provides an experiment hypothesis only; no walking or other controller is qualified.

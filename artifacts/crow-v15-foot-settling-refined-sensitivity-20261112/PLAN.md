# Refined foot-settling pose sensitivity, post-hoc

The coarser -0.05/0/+0.05 rad knee/ankle grid produced no pose meeting all three geometric criteria. Because those data suggest opposing knee and ankle changes may reduce rear-toe displacement, test the 3x3 grid knee -0.04/-0.03/-0.02 and ankle +0.04/+0.05/+0.06 rad, plus zero controls, on the exact same previously used touchdown poses. Apply the same requirements: front group lowers >=1 mm, rear group remains within 0.5 mm and all foot boxes are >= -0.5 mm. Save all poses and compare the zero controls to the prior geometry audit. This selection is post-hoc and starts are correlated, not an independent optimization, reachable actuator result, biological fit or stable supported gait. No candidate is promoted without a full physical trial.

# Early hip authority for initial perch contact

All four unchanged references exactly reproduced their 500 prior states, and all 16 lanes matched the previously audited initial shapes. No candidate passed the declared acquisition gate. Increased early feedback gave at most three consecutive samples of simultaneous supported opposing grip, and none held terminal support; some improved a brief support interval but did not acquire grip. Stronger bounded hip response alone does not resolve the failed starts and is rejected. No reserved layout was used.

# Stance ankle phase test

Retain the .125 hip swing and .10 knee stance target which passed contact criteria in four of six development starts, but showed inconsistent late-stance ankle extension. Test ankle sinusoid magnitudes .10, .15 and .20 across matched used starts, with two unchanged original-gait references. Require the same per-foot contact-speed, progress, support, toe and non-foot gates, and report actual geometric hip, knee and ankle phase patterns from substeps. This is qualitative related-corvid comparison at a different speed, not a numerical or species validation.

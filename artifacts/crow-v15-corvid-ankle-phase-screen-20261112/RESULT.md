# Larger stance ankle targets fail stability gate

The two unchanged original references and the .10 ankle-reference lanes matched the earlier repeat exactly across 1204 state and 19264 contact records. Increasing stance ankle magnitude to .15 and .20 made late-minus-middle ankle geometry positive in the lane medians, but all four lanes had 33–52 simultaneous unloaded substeps, and neither foot pair met the contact-speed requirement. The .20 cases also failed the minimum forward-progress gate. No candidate passed.

The original .10 amplitude is kept for comparison; these results do not establish physical walking, biological validity or mission qualification. Stance phase signs are qualitative related-corvid evidence, not species- and speed-matched numerical targets.

# Separated open-toe impact substep sensitivity

The 4-substep arm reproduced all ten reference states before grip. All four 40-endpoint drops completed. The worst accepted toe/perch SAT overlaps were 2.684 mm at 4 substeps, 0.748 mm at 8, 0.318 mm at 16 and 0.127 mm at 32. This shows a substantial numerical sensitivity in this single open-toe impact, but endpoint gaps do not measure substep maxima and these tests did not command grip or demonstrate supported landing. The changed timestep also changes the motion; full-route 8- and 16-substep trials previously failed separate clearance, support or flight criteria. No setting is qualified for use in the autonomous sequence. An adaptive landing-only setting would still require demonstrated seamless state continuity, grip, release, support and fresh end-to-end flight/navigation gates.

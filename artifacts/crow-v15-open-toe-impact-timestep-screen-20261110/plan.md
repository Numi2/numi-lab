# Separated open-toe impact substep sensitivity

The separated drop developed rigid toe overlap while the toes remained open, even at zero position stiffness with damping retained. Compare 4, 8, 16 and 32 physics substeps per identical 20 ms control interval during the same 40-step no-grip drop. Leave body, drives, controls, iteration counts, hybrid collision, shader and initial state unchanged. The four-substep arm must reproduce all first ten states from the separated-start reference. Record failures, individual earliest toe overlap, maximum accepted-pose SAT overlap, toe angles and contact endpoints. This screens numerical sensitivity, not substep-level nonpenetration; any setting that reduces overlap still requires separate flight, gait and full-route requalification and no claim of realistic or stable landing follows from this case. The 8- and 16-substep full-route outcomes have previously failed independent gates. No reserved layout is used.

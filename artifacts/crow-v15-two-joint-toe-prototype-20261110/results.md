# Exploratory two-joint toes

The neutral split passed 36 CPU kinetic/potential energy and momentum invariants and, with the intended isolated 40-body Metal library explicitly selected, a 12-lane free-space GPU comparison (maximum scaled acceleration error 4.29e-5). The earlier GPU rejection had loaded the old 32-body shader. On the isolated contact path with neutral joint targets, both single-joint controls completed 50 steps at 4, 8, 16 and 32 substeps. The two-joint body failed at 4 and 8 with toe collision disabled, passed disabled at 16, failed enabled at 16, and passed both enabled and disabled at 32. This is only a short stationary numerical screen; it does not establish walking, grip, landing, flight stability, long-horizon robustness, or biological validity. The research body/controller remains the original single-hinge version.

# Two-joint toe ground-contact screening

After free-space neutral invariants and CPU/GPU comparison, run a matched one-second native ground test for the original and two-joint toe bodies, each with toe collision disabled and enabled. Hold all joint targets at their neutral pose with four physics substeps. Record solver status, contacts, toe normal impulses and final root height; preserve all failures. This narrow stationary test does not demonstrate gait, dynamic grip, release, flight, landing, long-term stability or biological realism.

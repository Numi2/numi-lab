# Two-joint physical toe free-space prototype

The current v15 research foot has one rigid segment per digit, and supported landings show intermittent opposing contact. Before changing the working v15 body, prototype an additional distal hinge per physical toe in an isolated build. Split each original rectangular toe into two equal half-length bodies with mass, COM and inertia chosen to reproduce the original rigid toe when each new joint is neutral; preserve the existing proximal joint indices and append eight distal DOFs. Test model validity, body and joint counts, neutral-pose energy and momenta over varied root and existing-joint states, and native GPU dynamics against the CPU oracle. Check new joints actually respond. This is a geometric/physical prototype, not proof of grip, biological anatomy, flight qualification, contact performance or compatibility with existing policy/renderer. Do not change the current controller or use this new body for final runs until it passes those later gates.

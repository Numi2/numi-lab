# Contact-gated toe release screen

Starting from the selected faster-closure grip controller, use a baseline and +2 mm forward start for each of four bounded left-knee target changes (+.15, -.15, +.30, -.30 rad, with half opposite ankle adjustment). Trigger opening only after 50 consecutive accepted intervals of bilateral upward support and opposing front/rear toe impulses measured at the final substep. Once triggered, ramp only the left front and rear toe targets to zero at their original bounded per-interval rates; retain right grip and the existing contact-dependent balance feedback. No state resets or external force. A release passes only if after trigger there are at least ten consecutive intervals with every left toe and foot collision shape at least 1 mm clear of the perch at accepted endpoints, no left foot/toe impulse, at least half body weight supported by the right foot, right opposing toe impulses, no nonfoot impulse, tilt <=.35 rad and speed <=.12 m/s. Compute support from all 32 substeps; check the endpoint geometry and report first failure. This is a constructed stationary grip-to-release probe, not a flight arrival or autonomous landing.

# Smooth ankle transition: short development screen

Three 0.015-radian additional ankle modulation ramps were tested, with two closely related lanes per ramp, against matched original starts on previously used seed 20261127. The modulation is gated by accepted support of the opposite foot and acts through v15 joint targets. The 4–6 s interval contains no both-feet-unloaded substeps in any candidate, unlike the abrupt support-gated screen. Five of six satisfy the predeclared per-foot contact-point-speed proxy, no-toe-cap, no-nonfoot-contact and progress screen. The remaining lane has a right-foot ratio 0.762 against the 0.75 threshold. The contact-speed integral is a proxy, not tracked material-point slip.

The median sign of each magpie-informed hip, knee and early/late ankle measure matches the expected qualitative direction in three of six lanes. Only two lanes satisfy both that sign screen and every numerical contact/progress gate. Those medians are based on six modeled stance segments per lane, and the groups differ in ramp timing. The 0.5–1.2 m/s magpie observations differ from this slow model, its body proportions and surface; they are not measured crow targets. This two-second exploratory result cannot establish sustained walking or biological realism. Subsequent work must repeat each identical candidate across starts and integrate the walk with the independently tested launch.

# Contact-conditioned pitch feedback rejected

The exploratory bounded negative pitch/rate hip correction did not stabilize landings on the used development seed. The release-inhibited reference and candidate matched accepted endpoint state, actor outputs and contacts through first perch impulse in all lanes; first later differences occurred at steps 3420 and 3268 in lanes 2 and 7. Seven first episodes reached five waypoints and one stalled after three. Both supported lanes fell, at steps 3703 and 3388 versus 3723 and 3509 for the matched reference. Lane 7 tipped backward faster, had no interval meeting the bilateral opposing support criterion, and had two toe-speed-cap substeps. Its reference had 190 consecutive bilateral opposed support intervals. Lane 2 had 172 versus 212 such intervals. Substep coverage was complete and no contact overflow was logged.

This correction is rejected; the sign and hip geometry need further isolation. These results use an estimated model and development course, not held-out layouts, continuous-clearance proof or biological validation. An initial local build used the wrong variant label and produced no requested binary; the corrected binary hash and successful rollout are in the manifest.

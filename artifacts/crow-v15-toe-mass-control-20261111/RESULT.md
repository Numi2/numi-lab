# Toe mass sensitivity: inconclusive and not adopted

On the used development seed 20261126, doubling each estimated toe segment mass while redistributing mass from the parent foot did not eliminate initial toe speed-cap events: lanes 1 and 2 recorded 10 and 6 capped substeps, compared with 14 and 6 in the prior 16-substep baseline. Eight teacher-guided lanes each had three left and three right clearance bouts in the walk window, with about 3.0–3.2 cm forward displacement and no measured non-foot ground impulse in that window. All reached airborne flight without recontact by step 500 under wing actuation. This diagnostic is not a matched causal test of independent leg-powered takeoff, autonomous navigation, landing, anatomical calibration or stability across starts. The changed mass is not accepted as a repair. Observer/replay state alignment and step coverage are reported in the included analyses.

# Toe mass sensitivity experiment

The estimated two-segment toe links are 0.05 g each and experienced transient speed-limit hits on contact. Test doubling their mass while the existing model builder subtracts the redistributed mass and inertia from the parent foot, preserving the assembled zero-angle foot mass and inertia if validation passes. This is not anatomical calibration. Compare all-substep toe speeds, physically supported steps and takeoff; reject any setting that degrades them. The flight law and collision shapes stay unchanged.

# Forward to stopped flight transition

The fixed-command bench transitions from +0.25 m/s to zero at 16 s. No physical failures occurred in 80 starts, but 0/80 met the predeclared +/-0.08 m/s limit over every sampled point from 20 to 30 s. Representative velocity oscillated around zero and overshot the tolerance, so this transition has not passed. The retained samples and lane metrics contain the exact measurements. The frozen controller and estimated body remain research-only.

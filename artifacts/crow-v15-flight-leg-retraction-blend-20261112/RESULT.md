# Leg-to-flight blend screen: partial result

Eight closely related developmental lanes, seed 20261126, reached step 625 without recorded termination, contact overflow or nonfoot ground impulse. All reproduced their previous sustained-walk state and contact through step 485, and each had loaded feet in every substep of the 501–515 transition. Each was unloaded over all 80 measured final substeps (621–625). There were no observed toe-speed-limit hits, but toe contact at a very small impulse threshold is not proof of stable support or low slip.

All 58,240 joint-drive observations per lane were present for steps 535–625. The two hard-cutoff controls hit both knee limits three times each at step 573. The 0.10, 0.20, 0.40, 0.60 and 0.80 s handoff lanes had no observed authored joint-speed-limit hits in this window; the 0.30 s lane hit both knee limits once at step 557, before the blend itself. This is a short, single-seed, closely related-start engineering screen, not evidence of an optimal or biologically calibrated duration. The step-557 hit cannot be attributed to the later handoff.

Against the previous wing experiment, all lanes reproduced same-lane state and contact through step 548; the 0.10 s lane matched through 571 before its first state difference. These arms share a 0.05 s wing ramp and the loaded rear-toe target zero floor; that floor is not a no-change toe control. Flight beyond 625, measured material-point slip, isolation of leg work, disturbance recovery, complete autonomous navigation, supported landing and unseen-layout qualification remain open. Candidate selection requires repetition and a proper untreated toe control.

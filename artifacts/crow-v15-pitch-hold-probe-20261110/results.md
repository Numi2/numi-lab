# Bounded pitch correction lengthened, but did not stabilize, support

The test matched 2199 accepted student prefix frames and reached all five waypoints without a reported physical or solver failure. Raising the permitted hip/ankle pitch correction bound extended the longest bilateral loaded interval from 471 to 689 accepted steps (9.42 to 13.78 seconds), but support was lost before step 7000. The longest sampled simultaneous bilateral opposing-toe impulse interval remained 12 accepted steps, identical to the control. The body pitch continued to grow, reaching approximately 0.30 rad before sliding and falling. This fails the terminal-support and grip gates and is excluded from successful training. It is one guided used-layout result; endpoint counts do not prove uninterrupted substep contact.

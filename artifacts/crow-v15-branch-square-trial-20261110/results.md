# Exploratory branch cross-section trial

All 16 teacher-guided starts survived through step 4500, but no lane sustained qualifying bilateral foot load for more than two steps or entered the landed phase. The preregistered loading prediction was contradicted. Candidate-free 25-step toe angles stayed below 0.035 rad and pre-perch sampled angles below 0.000312 rad. This is a different estimated collision shape from the original research course; it is not a complete landing, grip/release, autonomous navigation, genuine unused-layout test or biological qualification.

# Revised gait, leg-only launch and frozen-flight handoff

The 0.85 gait candidate met the selected two-second contact and forward-step screen in 16 development starts; six had toe-speed cap hits during prior settling. For each of the same two development seeds, execute 500 continuous control intervals in eight lanes with the frozen V2 flight law and revised gait. Pair each flight-on run with an otherwise identical wing-suppressed control from six seconds onward. Require q/v and all substep contacts to match through the wing-off control's first two consecutive unloaded rising intervals; measure physically grounded leg push, rising clearance and height gain before ground recontact, and continuous flight after handoff. Record all toe-speed caps, nonfoot contacts, failures, censored states and replay geometry. This is teacher-guided development only: no controller is promoted and no held-out route, landing, release, biological validation or complete ground-sequence qualification is implied.

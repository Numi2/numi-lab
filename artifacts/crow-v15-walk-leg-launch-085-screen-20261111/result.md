# Result: matched leg push and short flight handoff on development starts

Across two development seeds and eight starts each, both feet produced measured upward impulse during the push. In wing-stroke-suppressed controls, every start had two consecutive unloaded rising intervals, endpoint-reconstructed soles and toes at least 10 mm clear, and 4.80–5.24 cm maximum root-height rise before recontact. Each flight-on run exactly matched its paired wing-suppressed q/v and all physics-substep contact records through those first two unloaded intervals. The flight-on runs then avoided ground contact through control step 500, reaching at least 0.75 m root-height gain, at least 0.33 m/s forward speed and at most 0.043 rad tilt at the endpoint. The 16 on-run walking prefixes exactly matched the earlier separately assessed 0.85 gait q/v, policy actions and all contact records through step 355. Two replayed lane-1 samples per seed validated foot/toe geometry within 4.43e-9 m and 3.1e-8 quaternion norm. No native execution errors or terminations were recorded.

The stroke-suppressed control does not eliminate passive aerodynamic forces, so this does not measure the wings' total contribution. Six of 16 starts reached the toe speed cap during initial settling; none did so from push-off step 348 through step 500 in the flight-on runs. These are selected teacher-guided development starts and a short flight-handoff check, not complete ground-sequence qualification, a held-out route, supported landing, release, or biological validation. The candidate is not promoted on this evidence alone.

# Passive substep contact-velocity observer

Existing walking comparisons measure contact-point speed at 50 Hz endpoints, not the path of slip. Add a passive observer of each loaded foot/toe contact location and the accepted joint velocity vector at all 16 substeps during the two-second walk window; existing coordinates are already copied. Compute contact-point velocity from independently validated articulated kinematics, integrate a normal-impulse-weighted magnitude across loaded contacts as a sampling-aware proxy, and report support coverage. Contact points may change identities and are sampled at collision time while q/v are committed at the end of the substep; this metric is not a tracked-material slip distance. Require exact q/v/actions and contact-summary parity against a reference replay, complete substep coverage, finite contacts, forward-kinematics checks and static-ground confirmation before using the observer. The observer does not control the solver.

If paired J and delayed I recovery runs satisfy their predeclared qualification, add their successful teacher routing prefixes before waypoint five to the previous qualified development data. Start from the earlier fresh-corrective actor, which completed difficult I, and preserve its output on its own actual successful difficult-I pre-landing observations with an explicit distillation loss.

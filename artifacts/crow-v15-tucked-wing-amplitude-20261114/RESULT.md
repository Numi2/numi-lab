# Early flap amplitude: continuous push transition, but no necessity

In the eight-lane matched development-seed screen, all lanes first reached three unloaded intervals at 376 after an approximately +0.171 N s matched excess upward foot impulse in intervals 371–376. Before this unload, maximum flap movement was approximately 0.0022 rad and modeled vertical aero impulse approximately -0.000295 N s. Both 0.70 rad control lanes recontacted by intervals 389–396. For each of the 0.75, 0.80 and 0.85 rad target amplitudes, both correlated push lanes had no observed foot recontact through interval 500, no nonfoot impulse, no toe-speed-threshold hit, and passed the registered continuous-transition screen. The held-leg controls also became airborne without later recontact in all lanes. Therefore none passes the preregistered leg-necessity screen: these data establish a matched early leg impulse and a candidate continuous transition, not that the leg push is required for takeoff.

Accepted endpoint flap angles and velocities remained inside the model's limits; this does not rule out substep limit hits. Starting states vary across lanes, contact/state/aerodynamic epochs are not fully synchronized, and the test uses only the development seed and time-scheduled toe behavior. Neither v15 flight qualification nor supported landing, unused-layout navigation or the full mission is established. Retain both counterfactuals and failures.

# Contact-triggered paired placement experiment

For the research v15 body, extend the dedicated accepted-contact sensor with rear and middle-front toe contact bits from the previous physics step. Between seconds 4.02 and 6, while a foot has rear-group contact and no middle-front-group contact, add bounded paired position-drive targets: lanes 4-5 knee -0.03 and ankle +0.05 rad; lanes 6-7 knee -0.04 and ankle +0.06 rad. Lanes 0-3 retain the prior negative-ankle screen and serve as exact same-lane controls. These two offsets were chosen post-hoc from frozen-pose geometry; this is an exploratory dynamic test on two previously used seeds, not independent or biological validation. Sensor data is one control step delayed; groups use a 1e-7 Ns normal-impulse threshold. No root force or prescribed pose is added.

Record contact bits, driven targets, 800 Hz states, all contacts, toe limits and replay. Require pre-intervention parity and complete state/contact/q/v parity for unchanged lanes 0-3 against the earlier same-seed run. Apply the existing support, toe, nonfoot, foot-speed, forward-progress, knee-recovery and coverage gates. Compare the filtered rear-to-middle-front contact lag with the earlier same-lane condition and require >=15% median reduction with >=2 touchdown events per foot in both arms. Verify the new rear-only bits actually occur and compare against observed contact records; preserve mismatch and filter/timing limitations. Passing any lane is a limited screen, not a stable or natural gait, supported landing, qualified flight or completed mission.

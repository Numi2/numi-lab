# Third-digit load and release contact screen

In the prior 4–6 s development gait, all 40 assessable bouts had the hallux contact before any front toe and front digit 3 leave at the last observed time or tied; none had digit 3 uniquely last, and eight additional bouts involved hallux-only contact. The related magpie paper reports third-digit-last release but does not provide comparable contact delays. Test bounded signed changes to digit 3's normal contact-gated flexion target (-0.08, -0.04, +0.04, +0.08, +0.12, +0.16 in one closely related lane each); keep two unchanged controls, all other toe targets, uniform hip balance and direct body-moment ablation. Require controls and exact pre-4-second state/substep parity, no simultaneous unloaded foot substeps, no toe cap or nonfoot ground impulse, at least 0.05 m progress, each side's contact-speed integral <=1.1 times its uniform no-change counterpart, and assess any uniquely-last digit-3 events and hallux-only bouts with full counts and ties. Per-lane locomotion, gait phase and force must remain visible; no biological calibration or tracked material slip is claimed from these starts.

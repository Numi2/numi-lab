# Faster toe closure: failed development trial

Both 450-interval lanes completed from identical constructed stationary states. Both achieved 11 independently verified release intervals (one-based 210–220), but neither achieved any interval meeting all bilateral opposing-grip, support, tilt and speed criteria after return. The root fell below 0.1 m at interval 308 in both lanes. Exact state/control parity held through the 224 intervals before the first differing front-toe command; measured target increments respected the 0.045 and 0.090 rad/interval limits and both targets stayed at or below 1.05 rad. Faster closure did not solve the support and stance-grip loss. Accepted-state toe angular speed reached the 12 rad/s reporting threshold in both lanes. Substep toe speeds and actuator efforts were not observed; this is not a safety or biological qualification. Contact observations covered all substeps, and no nonfoot impulse occurred. These constructed release tests do not establish walking, airborne landing or full mission performance.

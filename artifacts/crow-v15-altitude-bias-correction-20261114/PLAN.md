# Height-bias correction: paired development test

The prior eight-lane altitude cycle rose and descended but missed the predeclared 0.05 m mean-absolute-error gate, with late descent means about 0.058–0.061 m above the 0.55 m command. As a testable calibration hypothesis, retain that exact controller and physical model but subtract an additional 0.0048 from its bounded flap-amplitude correction, ramping this change in at 16–16.5 s. This is 0.08 times 0.06 m, derived from the observed offset and the existing height gain. It may be wrong because the dynamic response, saturation and aerodynamic model are nonlinear.

On the same used seed and training layout, run teacher-off for 1,800 intervals with no scheduled resets. Apply the prior cycle's unchanged 0.05 m mean-absolute tracking, 0.12 m rise/fall, finite/coverage, contact, posture, speed, actuator and command-reconstruction gates.

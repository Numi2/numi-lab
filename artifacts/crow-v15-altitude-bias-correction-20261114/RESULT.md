# Height bias correction on the development seed

The completed trace and replay were recopied after an early copy had started while replay was still being written. Their SHA-256 hashes match the finished native files. The final analysis passes the registered ascent and descent windows and physical gates in all eight lanes, on the same seed and layout used to derive the correction. Mean absolute error across lanes is 8.1–10.4 mm during the ascent tracking window and 7.9–8.9 mm during the descent tracking window. The pre-command level-flight window has mean absolute error 79–89 mm; it was recorded but not part of this cycle's pass criterion. Thus this result does not qualify level-height tracking, general command following, disturbed starts, takeoff, landing, or navigation. See both analysis logs and complete retained trace.

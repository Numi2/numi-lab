# Smaller strides: contact-driven gait screen

The previous .15-radian hip swing improved the foot-contact proxy by 15–18% without toe speed caps or loss of collective support, but missed the predeclared 25% improvement criterion. Test .07, .10 and .125, each with no or .25 contact-speed feedback, under the same matched controls and gentle stance-phase joint commands. Preserve the same progress, support, cap and contact-speed gates. Record whole-leg stance kinematics separately. Development starts and a contact-speed proxy cannot establish real-bird gait.

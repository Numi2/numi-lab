# One-foot physical toe release and regrip

A stationary attempt to open both feet fell despite balance adjustments. Test a natural alternative on the exact single-start development grip: after 50 consecutive samples of bilateral opposed grip and measured right-foot load, open the left toes and lift the left leg using the previously exercised walking-joint directions and existing position drives, gated by real opposing-foot upward load. The 70-tick smooth lift starts after 20 ticks of opening; left toe feedback then resumes to seek contact and grip. The other foot retains its contact-driven grip. No body state, support force, joint limit or flight controller is overwritten.

Run 1,500 ticks. Require exact trace parity with the held-grip reference until release; at least three consecutive accepted samples with all four left toe boxes separated from the perch by more than 1 mm, all left toe normal impulses at most 1e-7 N s across every substep, the right foot carrying at least half body weight over that control interval, no other-body support, and bounded tilt/speed; then require 100 terminal samples of renewed bilateral opposed grip and support, no other-body contact, no solver failure and measured grip-pose overlap at most 0.5 mm. This is a one-start development test, not a full-route, perturbation, contact-resolution or biological qualification. Preserve every outcome and use no unused layouts.

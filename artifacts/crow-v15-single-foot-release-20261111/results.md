# One-foot physical toe release and regrip

The release replay exactly matched all 777 held-grip reference states before the contact-conditioned command. The left toes cleared the perch by more than 1 mm at accepted poses, exerted no measurable normal impulse across all 16 substeps, and the opposite foot carried at least half body weight for 39 consecutive samples; the strict release condition held in 43 samples overall. No other-body contact was observed, and terminal support held through all last 100 samples. However, only 16 of the last 100 samples showed renewed bilateral opposing grip, and the worst grip-pose overlap was 0.546 mm, over the 0.5 mm limit. Thus this demonstrates one contact-gated single-foot release in one stationary start, but fails the complete release-and-regrip gate. Toe geometry was sampled at accepted poses, not continuously between substeps. Perturbed starts, alternate foot, takeoff and the complete autonomous route remain unqualified. No unused layout was used.

# Substep velocity repeat: speed caps observed

The 8-lane stationary experiment repeated all 4,400 end-of-control-step states exactly against the initial run. All 140,800 substep velocity records were present; all final-substep velocity records exactly matched native published endpoints. Named endpoint contact-normal sums also agreed to within 8.22e-10 N s. Four lanes hit the 12 rad/s toe velocity cap after release: lane 2 in 15 substep samples, lane 4 in 9, lane 5 in 9 and lane 7 in 5. The two -0.20 rad lanes both hit the cap. This invalidates a no-cap claim based only on control endpoints.

Most spikes occur during abrupt return; one -0.28 lane has a later supporting-toe spike. These are constructed stationary trials and do not demonstrate or disprove the airborne toe regression. The recording captures substep endpoints, not continuous within-substep extrema.

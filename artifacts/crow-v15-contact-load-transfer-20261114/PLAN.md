# Contact-triggered load transfer

In the paired 30-clearance return, the stance-foot front grip disappears just before the returning foot acquires opposing contact. The controller continues to aim the center of mass over the stance foot even after the returning foot is loaded. Test a contact-triggered target change: both lanes retain the same 30-clearance staged return and all original joint targets; once the returning foot generates more than 0.0001 N s of measured upward contact impulse, lane 1 latches a new lateral center-of-mass goal 0.025 m toward the returning foot. Keep the existing bounded 0.005 rad-per-interval roll target slew. Lane 0 retains the stance-foot goal throughout. No external force, body, friction, geometry, solver or joint-limit changes. Verify matched logged states and commands before the first contact-triggered change; report actual latch timing, impulse and both feet's opposing contacts, root stability, toe-speed substeps, and the full terminal 100-interval bilateral-grip gate. Keep failures. This is a stationary constructed development test, not walking or landing qualification.

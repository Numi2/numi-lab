# Staged body weight transfer and leg swing screen

Three isolated v15 walking trials failed support gates: reducing knee-drive stiffness, enlarging knee movement, and an instantaneous force-scaled larger movement. The latter moved all 16 starts backward. The periodic gait does not explicitly transfer weight before commanding a leg. Whether missing lateral transfer is the cause is not proven.

Test a finite-state candidate only in the same development walk window. Command both hip-roll drives to shift toward the stance foot over 15 control ticks, wait for the stance group to report at least 2.2 N, then execute a bounded 20-tick knee/hip/ankle swing; return the roll toward center over ten ticks, shift toward the opposite foot and repeat with the other leg. Knee command uses the 0.60 multiplier, swing is a smooth half-sine, and shift is bounded at 0.12 rad. A waiting stance does not advance the next swing until its force threshold is met. Thresholds and ranges are exploratory, not measured crow values. The swing itself is time bounded and does not claim continuous force-based fall prevention. Use task-state sequence bits only during the walk and clear them before launch; preserve the existing flight carrier, physics, materials, starts and remaining phases.

On the same two previously exposed development seeds and eight lanes each, require at least 15% larger mean bilateral knee range in six lanes, at least one qualified swing bout from each foot in at least six lanes, no more than 33 aggregate no-foot-normal-contact substeps, zero nonfoot contacts, positive forward displacement in all lanes, no extra native errors or terminations, and exact logged prefix states and contact summaries through step 199. Retain all outcomes, including failed or incomplete steps, and reject promotion if any condition fails. This is a constructed two-second controller screen, not a qualification of takeoff, flight, autonomous landing, held-out layouts or biological realism.

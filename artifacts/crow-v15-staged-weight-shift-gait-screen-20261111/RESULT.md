# Staged weight-shift gait: contact preserved, walking failed

The candidate used contact-gated, finite-time hip-roll weight transfers and smooth knee swings during the two-second development walk, without changing the body, materials, flight carrier, or starts. Both 300-step teacher-off native runs completed without reported error; logged pre-walk endpoint, action and contact-substep summaries matched the original through step 199 in all 16 lanes.

The candidate increased mean bilateral knee span at least 15% in all 16 lanes. No observed physics substeps lacked foot normal contact (the original had 33), and neither arm reported nonfoot contact. However, zero lanes achieved a qualified swing from both feet, and all 16 moved backward over the walking window. The predeclared swing and forward-motion gates failed. This is supported weight shifting, not a qualified walk, and is not promoted.

`kinematic-sensitivity.json` records one fixed-root forward-kinematics diagnostic at an observed configuration: individual positive hip, knee and ankle perturbations moved the foot in different directions. It is a local geometry observation rather than a dynamic contact result or biological measurement; it motivates checking full-leg coordination rather than increasing knee motion alone. Full source, compiled shader, traces, per-lane support and body-motion comparisons are retained.

No autonomous takeoff, flight, landing, unused-layout performance, biological realism or production behavior was established by this test.

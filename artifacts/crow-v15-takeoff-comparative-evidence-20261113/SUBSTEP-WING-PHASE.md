# Posthoc substep wing-body phase audit

The retained continuous-push trial already contained wing coordinates and foot impulses at all 16 substeps per control interval during the launch. Reconstructing the relative wing-body center-of-mass height with native model forward kinematics at the 1.25 ms substep interval places its local maximum 47.5 ms after the last recorded positive foot-normal impulse in lanes 2–3, 22.5 and 31.25 ms after in lanes 4–5, and 25.0 ms before in lanes 6–7. Last contact is defined only within steps 350–365, as normal impulse >1e-7 N s in a substep; the peak search is restricted to steps 355–360. Full definitions and per-lane values are retained in substep-wing-audit.json.

This refines the previous 20 ms endpoint observation; it is still a body-center proxy, not the wingtip or aerodynamic-force direction. Body attitude affects relative world height, the search window and threshold are posthoc, and one used seed with correlated lanes cannot validate crow takeoff. It does demonstrate that the current lane-timed wing motion is not consistently synchronized to the observed last foot contact. A future contact-triggered experiment must predeclare its measurement and verify individual actuator dynamics and stability.

# Initialization height sensitivity

Both zero-offset controls matched all 1,000 sampled q/v/policy/contact states and 16,000 contact substeps of the single-perturbation reference. Each nonzero-offset lane was compared to its own same-seed reference. The 2 mm offsets produced 609 versus 623 and 39 versus 51 both-feet-unloaded substeps in the first 4 s. The 4 mm offsets produced 45 versus 53 and 1,158 versus 182; the latter is a substantial deterioration. The 6 mm offsets produced 98 versus 131 and 188 versus 273. One 6 mm lane had 0.004 N s upward foot/toe impulse in the first 20 ms; all other lanes had none in that interval. The long 4 mm bounce, and only two lanes per setting, rule out a reliable initialization fix. Detailed joint cap and trajectory records are in the analysis.

No offset was promoted. These are used-seed model tests, not a validated bird gait, flight controller or completed mission.

# Sole-gated front-toe diagnostic: not admitted

The candidate completed all eight 300-step first episodes and the smoke test with no reported runtime or physics failure. All 2,400 endpoint samples and 38,400 contact-substep samples for lane zero, and all 1,600 prewalk endpoint samples and 25,600 substeps across lanes, exactly matched the earlier front-drive run; the fields compared are the logged q/v, policy actions and contacts, not the intentionally modified status bits. All lanes had at least three supported-clearance bouts per foot and no sampled toe-speed caps or nonfoot-ground intervals.

The physical gates fail. Across the screen, 14/40 complete stance runs with both toe groups were rear-first, 24 front-first and two unresolved at the substep resolution; two more runs lacked a toe group. The held reference was 14/40 rear-first. The candidate had 247 sole-clear/toe-low endpoints versus 49 held, lane 5 had 15 both-feet-zero-normal substeps versus zero held, and lanes 5 and 7 traveled only 85.5% and 64.2% of held, respectively. Overall loaded contact-point speed median/p95 was 0.00394/0.0460 m/s versus 0.00276/0.0348 m/s held; sampled point speeds are not integrated slip.

Lane 6, with 0.15-rad peak, illustrates a useful but unreplicated result: 5/5 complete stances were rear-first and the bird advanced 26.27 mm versus 26.07 mm held, with no zero-normal substeps. The earlier aggregate-gated lane instead moved backward 8.33 mm. However, the candidate lane had 38 low-toe endpoints against six held, so it still fails clearance. These lanes share a development seed and each gain has a different start; no population performance or crow-specific anatomical fit is established. The ground geometry, binary threshold and related-magpie contact-order comparison are limited proxies.

The scratch support bits overlap the flight-curl field. The experiment was stopped at 6 s and does not validate the transition to flight: the action just after the window can read the previous scratch bits before the transition clears them. The plan's statement that behavior outside the window is preserved is therefore not established and may be false at the boundary. A subsequent design must provide independent state or explicitly guard and test this boundary, and requalify the whole sequence. This research shader is not production code and is not promoted.

A follow-up geometric attribution for lane 6 finds the lowest toe box is a rear segment at 28 of its 38 low-toe endpoints; the remaining ten are front distal boxes. Twenty occur on the positive carrier and 18 on the negative carrier. This is post-hoc lowest-box attribution, not proof of dragging or an independent biological measure. The original box geometry and FK limitations still apply.

# Sole-gated front-toe diagnostic

Test whether replacing the aggregated own-foot support gate for front toe drive with last-substep sole-ground contact improves rear-before-front release and gait. Use the unchanged v15 body, policies, rear toe action, and all other controls, same 8 used-seed lanes and front target amplitudes as the prior front-support sweep. Last-substep sole contact requires a positive normal impulse above 1e-7 from the corresponding sole and ground; this is a thresholded control signal, not a measurement of pressure or sustained support. This is a causal engineering intervention, not measured crow toe geometry or a biological claim.

For this isolated bench only, hold two sole-contact bits in the normally flight-curl field during 4-6 seconds. Mask the flight target during that window and clear the scratch bits after it, preserving flight-field behavior outside the window. These bits are not a production design and must not be promoted; flight revalidation is necessary even if the short test passes. Test two-step smoke then 300 steps with 16 substeps, seed 20261127, no resets. Require unchanged zero-front-drive lane q/v, policy actions and per-substep contacts against the previous front-drive run throughout, and all-lane prewalk parity; ignore intentionally changed status bits. Use the predeclared front-support experiment's physical, support, travel, toe clearance, contact speed and release-order gates. Record missing-toe stances separately. This single-seed gain sweep does not establish generality.

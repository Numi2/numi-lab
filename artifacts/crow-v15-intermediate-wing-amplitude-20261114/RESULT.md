# Intermediate wing amplitude: a limited leg-push development result

On used seed 20261126, both 0.55 and 0.60 rad initial-amplitude push candidates stayed unloaded from interval 377 through 500. The matched 0.55 rad held-leg bird first unloaded at 377 but recontacted at 406; the 0.60 rad held-leg bird also stayed unloaded through 500. Thus the 0.55 rad comparison demonstrates a modeled difference associated with the push under this specific development condition. It does not prove leg-only takeoff, a necessary leg contribution across starts or a qualified controller.

In the 0.55 rad pair the observer flap displacement through first unloading was approximately 0.549 rad in both arms; preceding modeled vertical aero impulses were +0.0400/-0.00046 N s for push and +0.04045/-0.00069 N s for held. Wings already moved substantially before release, and observer q/contact alignment was directly verified in other lanes but inferred here. Both push lanes had complete registered substep coverage, no toe-speed threshold, nonfoot impulse, observer error or recorded physical failure. Matched candidate q/v, action and contact records were exact through interval 368; all six unaffected lanes matched through 500, and paired wing targets and nonshader hashes matched.

This passes only the preregistered 500-interval continuity screen on one used seed. It does not establish perturbed-start robustness, long-duration straight flight, turns, climbs, descents, recovery, frozen flight, toe grip, landing, navigation or unused-layout performance. Full controls and observations are retained.

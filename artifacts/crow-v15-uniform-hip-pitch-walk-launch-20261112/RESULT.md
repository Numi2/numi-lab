# Result: uniform contact feedback through walking and launch

In eight closely related starts on used development seed 20261127, all matched the same-lane no-feedback integrated control for 200 state endpoints and 3200 contact substeps before four seconds. The same -1 pitch / 0.05 pitch-rate loaded-hip feedback eliminated the controls' 26–27 consecutive both-feet-unloaded substeps during the measured 4–6 s gait window in all eight lanes. All progressed approximately 0.081 m in that window, with no recorded toe-speed caps or nonfoot ground impulse. Relative to their matched no-feedback counterparts, left/right loaded contact-speed integrals were 1.031–1.043 / 0.996–1.003; this is a changing-contact-point proxy, not measured material slip. No lane met the previous 25-percent reduction gate against the different older configuration; both comparisons are retained.

All first episodes survived the 12-second horizon and were foot-unloaded after eight seconds, with no observed toe-speed caps after 7.4 seconds or sampled nonfoot contacts. In the six-interval launch window, total upward foot normal impulse was 0.430–0.441 N s; this is not leg-only work and does not alone prove a leg-powered takeoff. Earlier matched leg-extension controls separately show a contribution, but do not qualify takeoff for this configuration. Wing/body speed caps were not recorded by this host.

The related-corvid hip and knee phase signs were reproduced, but the median late-ankle change was only about +0.0037–0.0039 degrees, effectively flat rather than a meaningful biological match. These starts are nearly identical and use one development seed; sustained walking, a second seed, actual slip, realistic whole-body motion, toe grip/release, flight qualification, route and landing remain unproven. The next experiment uses matched control and candidate on a second development seed. Do not promote or claim completion from this screen.

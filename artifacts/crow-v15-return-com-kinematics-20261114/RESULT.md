# Returning-leg local geometry

Native FK finite differences on retained COM-feedback states cross-check the independently recorded body-pose box gaps to within 8.1 nm. For candidate lane 1 at tick 270, the foretoe gap was 2.69 mm and rear-toe separation -0.33 mm; increasing hip angle locally changed the gaps by approximately -155.5 and +126.0 mm/rad respectively. The knee and ankle showed the same opposing sign. At tick 240 the corresponding foretoe gap was 10.19 mm and the rear gap -0.27 mm. Thus a simple hip correction toward the foretoe can move the rear collider away; this is a local geometric tradeoff, not a prediction of contact impulses or reachable motion.

The exact input states, native executable and library hashes, all differences and comparison script are retained. These estimates do not demonstrate regrip, walking or landing and should guide only a separately tested physical control.

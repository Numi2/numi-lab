# Landing contact slip observation

Adding final-substep contact-point logging reproduced all 2876 accepted q/v states exactly through the first physical failure of the used J 45 mm alignment trial. Independent native articulated kinematics reconstructed approximate contact-point tangential velocities. During steps 2600–2649, 254 of 433 sampled loaded toe contact points had a tangent-to-normal impulse ratio above 0.59; their median reconstructed tangential speed was approximately 0.0081 m/s. During steps 2650–2699 the corresponding values were 195 of 413 and 0.0074 m/s. Contact points can be from the previous detection boundary, so these are endpoint estimates, not continuous slip trajectories, integrated dissipation, or proof that friction alone causes the fall. The one development trial did not produce a supported landing.

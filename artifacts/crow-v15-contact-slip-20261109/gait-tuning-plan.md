# Three bounded lower-slip gait variants

The initial contact-point diagnostic showed intermittent high tangential contact speed and tangential/normal impulse ratios near 0.6. Test three fixed variants of the previously retained live-feedback band-1 gait on the same exposed layout, seed, two lanes and 350 steps. The variants are (period seconds, hip amplitude, knee lift, ankle lift): (0.50, 0.20, 0.32, 0.12), (0.70, 0.30, 0.40, 0.15), and (0.60, 0.20, 0.32, 0.12). The same accepted contact feedback and 2-5 s walking window apply. No forces, body parameters, friction, wing law or policy are changed. Run all three regardless of early results and retain all outcomes.

For a candidate to clear this exploratory gate, selected lane 1 must move >=0.03 m over the 2-5 s window; have both left-only and right-only loaded endpoints; have a 95th-percentile contact-point tangential speed <=0.04 m/s for each foot using only contacts with positive upward endpoint normal impulse >0.0001 N s; show no non-foot ground contact; and have zero physical failures and zero failed simulator steps. The original diagnostic rollout is scored using the same formula as a control. Endpoint contact location and body velocity may differ in substep timing; these values do not prove continuous no-slip. Such a pass would only identify a developmental candidate and would still require full walking-to-navigation and landing retesting.

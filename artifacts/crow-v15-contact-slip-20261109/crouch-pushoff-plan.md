# Contact-gated crouch and rapid leg extension

Both previous straightening candidates raised the body but failed to lift both feet in the wing-suppressed control. Test one distinct, preregistered leg preparation: during 4.30-4.80 s, ramp bounded bilateral hip/knee/ankle targets toward a deeper crouch, gated per leg on previous solved foot load; then transition continuously from the crouch toward extension during 4.80-4.90 s and hold while the foot remains loaded until 5.20 s. No external force is injected. Use the isolated host library that materializes articulated foot velocities; the frozen flight law and v15 body remain otherwise unchanged. Run four matched arms: push off/on and wings normal/suppressed from 4.4-5.4 s, two lanes, identical exposed seed and 350 steps. Retain all outcomes.

As in the first push-off test, require the wing-suppressed push arm to exceed no-push peak upward root speed by >=0.03 m/s, exhibit positive solved foot loading and then at least two both-feet-unloaded endpoint samples, no nonfoot ground contact, no physical failures and zero failed simulator steps. Compare normal-wing launch separately; do not attribute aerodynamic acceleration to the legs. Contact endpoints and velocity are not integrated impulse, energy or continuous flight. A pass would not qualify a full walking-to-flight handoff or real-crow biology.

# Grounded foot velocity feedback: exploratory candidates

The three open-loop amplitude/period variants failed the preregistered slip screen. Test a different mechanism: when a foot is in the nominal stance half-cycle and the previous accepted solver load is positive, use estimated forward velocity of the loaded foot contact patch to correct that leg hip target. The patch velocity is computed on device from accepted foot center velocity and angular velocity, solver center of pressure, estimated 0.008 m sole half-height and root yaw; it is a one-step-lag estimate, not an exact physical contact sensor. Keep the original gait period and amplitudes, model, actor, friction and support gating. Test all three bounded hip corrections, gains 0.5, 1.5 and 3.0 normalized action per m/s, with estimated foot speed clamped to +/-0.15 m/s. Retain all outcomes, including instability or failure.

Use exactly the previous lower-slip gate on the same exposed layout/seed: >=0.03 m net travel, left-only and right-only support, no nonfoot contact, no failures, and <=0.04 m/s 95th-percentile sampled contact-point tangential speed per foot for contacts above 0.0001 N s upward endpoint impulse. Compare the previously scored original candidate using the same diagnostic. A pass remains a development screen, not a biological or long-horizon qualification.

# Smaller hip-stroke changes under the existing contact gate

The .40, .50 and .60 hip-stroke screen improved displacement but all failed the preregistered contact gate. Test .325, .350 and .375 in paired lanes, alongside exact .30 controls, using the same 0.40 crouch, foot/toe model, 2 Hz gait and previously used development seed 20261127. Require exact controls, no new both-feet-unloaded substeps, no toe-speed caps in 4–6 s, no nonfoot impulse, and each foot's 50 Hz contact-point speed p95 no higher than 1.5x its matched baseline. Also require at least 5% greater 4–6 s forward displacement in each candidate lane. A passing screen requires repetition on a second used seed and full-sequence compatibility before any controller adoption; it is not a real-crow gait or integrated-slip validation.

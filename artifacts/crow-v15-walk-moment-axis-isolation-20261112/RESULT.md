# Isolate pitch versus yaw stabilization in walking

In a matched 4–6 s counterfactual, even lanes temporarily enabled only the inherited direct yaw moment while odd lanes enabled only the inherited direct pitch moment. All 1600 pre-4 s states and 25600 substep contacts matched the no-moment integrated gait. All four yaw-only lanes retained the original 26 unloaded substeps; all four pitch-only lanes had none, versus 26–27 in the matched no-moment lanes. This isolates a strong sensitivity to the pitch channel in these development starts. The experiment does not establish applied moment magnitude from policy outputs or biological validity.

Direct body moments are diagnostic artificial authority, not a physical solution. They remain disabled in the next screen, which tests bounded contact-conditioned hip feedback instead. These starts are closely related, and no sustained walking, takeoff, flight or held-out mission is qualified.

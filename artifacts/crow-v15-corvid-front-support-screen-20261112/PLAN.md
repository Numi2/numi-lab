Test the engineering hypothesis that limited front-toe position drive, while the foot is accepted as supported and the rear-toe release phase is active, can sustain front contact through rear unloading. Keep original model, mass, joints, contact, reduced-lift gait, stance stroke, -0.02 rad rear target and all other controls fixed.

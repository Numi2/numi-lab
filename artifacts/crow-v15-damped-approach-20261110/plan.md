# Damped low-speed approach experiment

The tighter precision gate produced no perch contact during the first 5000-step episode: sampled forward speed remained as high as roughly 0.18 m/s in both directions as the bird oscillated around the target. The 7000-step invocation reset after 5000; only its first episode is used. Test whether reducing position gain and increasing velocity damping in the bounded forward command improves low-speed settling without changing the frozen flight controller. Use a moderately relaxed alignment gate, the same toe reflex and height response, SAT physics and event budget. On the same development J seed, require 25 continuous bilateral-loaded samples, opposing front/rear toe contacts and supported posture, no nonfoot collision and no reset. Stop at the 5000-step episode horizon. This is exploratory guide-on evidence only.

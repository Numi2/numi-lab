# Bounded supported front-toe closure

The clear-j-hard development trial completed the course and remained supported, but its final 100 pre-release samples contained only 62 simultaneous opposing toe-contact endpoints and a longest uninterrupted run of six, below the prior nine-endpoint isolated-grip screen. Test a smaller supported-phase front-toe target floor of 1.05 radians (the earlier 1.08 floor degraded a different J trajectory). Preserve the v15 toe mechanics, low-level flight, routing, landing state machine, gait, push-off and alternating leg release. This is an empirical hypothesis, not a claim of improved grip.

First replay the matched clear-j-hard full start with teacher off and no reset. Require exact q/v, actor observation and action parity until the supported-phase change begins; complete every waypoint with no physical, solver or nonfoot-perch failure; minimum sampled clearance at least 0.05 m; at least nine consecutive opposed grip endpoints in the last 100 before release; both supported releases and final bilateral support. Record the fraction of opposed endpoints as well. If it passes, freeze the candidate and run the other three development starts without retuning, measuring the same criteria and any regressions. This does not certify substep force continuity, biological realism or reserved-layout behavior.

# Shifted stationary start: no grip

This single constructed start shifted the root by -10 mm. It brought the front toe segments into contact, but neither foot accumulated the rear endpoint contacts needed to activate front closure. Full observation recorded 90 consecutive supported intervals and no bilateral opposed-force intervals; the bird lost support and fell. All 350 native steps completed and all 5,600 substeps were observed without overflow. This is a failed stationary test, not a supported landing, release or autonomous flight.

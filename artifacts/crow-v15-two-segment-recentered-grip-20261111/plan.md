# Shifted stationary start for two-segment grip

The prior single stationary grip screen produced rear but no front-toe perch impulses; at the initial accepted pose, a front proximal segment was centered at x=+0.042 m while the perch's forward edge is +0.025 m, leaving little geometric reach. Test a single initial root x shift of -0.010 m relative to that screen, keeping all other initial values, model, bounded contact-triggered toe commands, solver and 350-interval observation unchanged. This is a deliberately constructed stationary diagnostic, not a flight arrival or autonomous landing. Require at least 50 consecutive supported, bounded-tilt and bounded-speed intervals with bilateral front/rear opposing normal forces and <=0.5 mm independent endpoint toe/perch overlap; report all failures and retained contacts. This experiment cannot establish release, robust starts, walking, takeoff or full navigation.

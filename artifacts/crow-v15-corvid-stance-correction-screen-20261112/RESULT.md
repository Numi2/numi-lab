# Corvid-informed stance drives change joint phase but fail walking gate

The v15 model received bounded physical joint-position targets during reported stance, with no visual pose overrides. The unchanged controls and 1,600 pre-intervention endpoint states plus 25,600 contact substeps matched the retained baseline. Across selected 800 Hz modeled stance windows, the baseline median knee interior-angle change was +3.49 degrees. The .10 knee/.10 ankle candidate shifted the median to -2.71 degrees and the early-to-middle ankle change from +1.50 to -1.44 degrees; its middle-to-late ankle change remained -0.11 degrees, so the complete magpie-like qualitative ankle pattern was not achieved. It moved 0.162 m versus its own 0.131 m baseline over the two-second window with continuous collective support and no toe caps, but its left/right contact-point-speed integrals rose to 1.049/1.073 times baseline. This proxy is not tracked material slip or a biological target.

The .20/.20 and .30/.30 candidates showed median knee flexion and early ankle flexion followed by late extension in the selected windows, but lost collective foot support for 41 and 43 substeps and hit toe-speed caps 13 times each. All six candidates failed the predeclared two-foot contact-motion reduction and safety screen; none was promoted. Knee-only settings increased contact motion further, and the largest also unloaded and hit toe caps. Full lane metrics, event exclusions and angles are in the archived analyses. Windows from closely related starts are not independent animal observations, and simulated American-crow morphology, speed and substrate differ from the reference magpie experiment.

# Earlier bounded stance centering

The combined candidate's center of mass drifted laterally from its stance foot and the existing roll target saturated before the fall. Fixed-root native kinematics show the stance hip coordinate has the sign needed to reduce the relative offset locally, but an earlier higher roll limit worsened the physical result. Test a modest earlier correction without raising that limit: both lanes reproduce the combined candidate; lane 1 changes only the existing lateral-offset target from 10 mm to 5 mm. The same gain, +/-0.25 rad target cap, 0.005 rad/interval slew, physical joint/drive limits, pitch feedback and contact logic remain unchanged. This does not presume that fixed-root geometry predicts contact dynamics.

Require exact 450-state control parity to the combined candidate, paired prefix, reconstructed roll and other feedback, valid targets and positions, 32 complete substeps, no native/observer error, nonfoot support or accepted-state toe-speed threshold before failure, and 100 consecutive unchanged clearance, stance-contact, posture, load and speed gates. Retain failures. Toes remain open; walking, regrip and landing are untested, and contact/state epochs are independently unverified.

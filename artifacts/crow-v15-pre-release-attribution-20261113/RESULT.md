# Retrospective pre-release comparison

In the already-archived used-seed 20261126 paired experiment, lane 6's push condition became unloaded at interval 375 and remained unloaded through the original 1800-step flight screen; its held-leg control became unloaded at 377. Over intervals 371–374, foot-normal impulse was 0.68047 N s with the push versus 0.43297 N s held, a difference of 0.24750 N s. The validated observer replay measured only +0.0000761 N s net modeled vertical aerodynamic impulse in the push condition during that same window. However, the wing had already moved 0.236 rad, and these are coupled dynamics; the result supports a modeled leg contribution to initial release, not a claim that wing motion had no effect or that the legs were necessary for eventual takeoff. The held control also flew.

Lane 7's initial unloaded period was interrupted by recontact, as shown in the recorded contact transitions. These are retrospective measurements on correlated development lanes, not independent qualification or biological calibration. Results draw from the archived launch-aero observer and leg-wing attribution raw traces; reconstruct their window extracts with their documented extraction scripts before reproducing this analysis.

# Pre-registered exploratory leg/wing launch factorial

Question: does the programmed bilateral extension materially change contact impulse and the subsequent rise, with and without commanded wing flapping? The prior unassisted push plus early-wing screen had no matched no-extension arm. This factorial leaves the v15 body, ground, initial state, leg crouch, passive aerodynamics and other empirical closures unchanged; both identified direct body-moment actions stay disabled from 6.30 s as in the source. It does not establish leg-muscle work or a real-crow biological model.

Four shaders: push+wing (original); no-push+wing (replace the three-joint post-crouch extension target with the crouched target); push+wing-off (suppress flapping-position amplitude beginning at 6.30 s); no-push+wing-off (both edits). Wing-off suppresses commanded flapping amplitude; it does not immobilize every wing joint or disable passive aerodynamic forces. The early-wing trigger, controller state and body may respond differently after intervention; a whole-run outcome is therefore a closed-loop intervention effect, not a decomposition of independent forces. Lanes 2–7 retain the original .04/.10/.20 s onset timings in pairs; lanes 0–1 do not receive the original early-wing intervention and are negative context rather than six independent replicates.

Use the same previously used development seed 20261127, 8 lanes per shader, training layout, 400 control steps, 16 substeps, teacher off, no scheduled resets, exact same host and model inputs. All four runs start from the same reset mechanism; no claim of exact restored-state branching. Hold the reference shader and hashes, all outputs and failed trials. Compare like-indexed lanes. Require no physics error/contact overflow, 400 endpoints and 6400 contact substeps per lane, and exact q/v and contact parity in the pre-intervention prefix through step 315 (6.30 s); require no-push versus push parity through step 345 (before extension at 6.96 s). Treat a missing or invalid arm as inconclusive. Log first divergence. Report per-lane integrated signed upward ground impulse per foot during steps 348–365 and 348–400, body vertical speed and height at steps 345, 365, 380, 400, first sustained unloaded interval and subsequent recontact; record nonfoot contacts and toe-speed caps. Prediction to explore, not a qualification threshold: with wings suppressed, commanded extension increases combined upward ground impulse by >=0.05 N s and later upward body velocity by >=0.05 m/s in at least four of the six paired early-wing-design lanes. Predeclare both outcomes; a failure of either contradicts that proposed large effect for this screen. Do not count lanes as independent birds or use them to infer population statistics. A materially different result with wings on is an interaction to inspect, not a claim that the wing force was isolated.

All this is exploratory on a previously exposed seed and layout, with overlapping dependent lanes and an arbitrary analysis window. No candidate is promoted. The 8 s horizon cannot prove sustained flight, controlled maneuvers, grip/release, autonomous route execution, or a supported landing. Do not use untouched mission layouts or seeds in this development test. Bound output to these four runs; archive exact source, inputs, logs, outcomes and failures in Git and independently verify before retiring local duplicates.

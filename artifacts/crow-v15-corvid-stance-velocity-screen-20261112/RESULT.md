# Gentle corvid-informed stance plus contact feedback: no passing setting

Six candidate lanes combined fixed .10/.10 knee/ankle stance targets with hip contact-velocity feedback gains 0, .25, .5, 1, 1.5 and 2. None passed the predeclared two-foot contact-motion, continuous support, toe-speed, nonfoot-contact and progress screen. Gain .5 yielded left/right contact-speed integrals of 0.988/1.030 times baseline, maintained collective support, but hit a toe speed cap in four substeps. Gain .25 maintained support without toe cap but increased the two ratios to 1.065/1.185. Higher gains also hit caps and some lost support. The changing-contact metric is a physical velocity proxy, not tracked material slip.

The median modeled knee/ankle phase changed in several candidates toward the related-magpie qualitative pattern, but that does not override the failed physical gate or establish biological agreement. Two unchanged lanes and 1,600 pre-intervention states plus 25,600 contact substeps matched the reference. The host logs contacts at a finer cutoff than the baseline; PRELIMINARY.md records the corrected comparator filter and the unchanged physical run. The sensor audit uses the finer cutoff. The seed and starts were used in development. No controller was promoted.

# Preregistered contact-prepared foot placement

The swing-clock trial sometimes started applying placement while both observed toe groups were already loaded and failed all eight candidate physical screens. Hypothesis: initiate the bounded knee/ankle placement only when neither instrumented toe group of that foot is loaded and at least one instrumented group of the opposite foot is loaded, then hold through observed rear-only contact and decay after front-group contact. This tests observed toe-group feedback instead of assuming the clock identifies unloaded swing. Those group bits cover only a subset of possible bird-ground contacts and are one accepted step old; absence of these two observed groups is not proof the foot is airborne or the opposite foot is stably supporting the body.

Same two development seeds, walking window, test body and policy as the matched trials. Unchanged lanes 0–3 are controls. Lanes 4–5 use paired knee/ankle offsets (-0.015,+0.025) rad; lanes 6–7 (-0.030,+0.050). A seven-level per-foot research state rises by one per control step in even lanes and two in odd lanes while the triggering observed contact condition holds, holds at rear-only contact, and falls one per step otherwise. Retain the 4.02–4.22 second intervention fade. Existing joint drives and real contacts remain authoritative; no body force is injected.

Require unchanged control and pre-intervention recorded parity; audit recorded contact bits against level updates; evaluate all existing support, toe-speed-cap, nonfoot, sampled-contact-speed, progress, recovery coverage, knee phase and >=15% toe-loading-delay gates. Report every failure and toe-event order, including reversed events. Short correlated development trials cannot qualify long-term walking, unmeasured contacts, biological fidelity, checkpoint/rollback, leg takeoff, toe grip, flight, navigation or landing.

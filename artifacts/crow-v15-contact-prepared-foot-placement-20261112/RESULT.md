# Contact-prepared placement: rejected

Of eight changed candidate lanes across two correlated development seeds, seed 20261126 lane 7 passed the previously defined basic physical gates but its median rear-to-front toe-loading interval was still 0.96 of its same-lane no-placement control. The other seven candidates failed at least one basic physical gate; no candidate achieved the prespecified 15% interval reduction. Candidate toe-loading ratios were 0.94–1.01, and no candidate passed the combined physical and toe-timing screen.

Across candidate lanes 4–7, both-feet-below-threshold substep counts were 0, 13, 0, 0 for seed 20261126 and 0, 1, 0, 11 for seed 20261127. The sampled changing-contact-point speed p95 exceeded 1.5 times the uniform baseline in seven of eight candidates on at least one side. That quantity is not a measurement of integrated foot slip, and a both-feet-below-threshold sample does not by itself establish a fall. No candidate toe-speed caps or nonfoot ground impulses were recorded. Corrected toe-event detection found 54 rear-first and one complex touchdown in seed 20261126, and 54 rear-first and one front-first touchdown in seed 20261127; this is a model proxy, not an established biological fit.

The separately published experimental sensor and ramp followed the recorded rise/hold/fall rules for 99 checked transitions per candidate side on each seed. Unchanged lanes 0–3 matched the no-placement trajectories in recorded state, toe-contact, q and v samples; pre-intervention state and contact records also matched the uniform baseline. Sensor contact indicators cover only selected front and rear toe segments and use a threshold; other contact groups and the one-step timing mean absence of those indicators does not prove flight or stable support. A geometrical match to the other-corvid reference remains unvalidated and these two-second correlated trials are far below the study's speed range.

No controller is promoted. Long-term contact-driven walking, leg-only takeoff, articulated grip and release, flight qualification, and uninterrupted navigation with supported landing on two unused layouts and seeds remain unproven. Keep the rejected outcomes and source to guide a proper coupled whole-foot controller.

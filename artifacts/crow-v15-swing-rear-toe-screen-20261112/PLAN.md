# Contact-gated rear-toe extension during walking

The prior support-phase change failed and is not included. The existing unmodified gait sometimes lifts the sole while toe boxes remain below the 1 mm diagnostic clearance; the right rear distal toe was commonly the limiting box in the failed candidate. Test an experimental negative rear-toe proximal target up to 0.20 rad (distal scaled by 0.75) during the positive swing carrier only, and only when the opposite foot reports accepted support. This is a physical bounded motor command, not a visual animation or measured American-crow toe kinematic. Both arms retain the same baseline leg gait, same shader and host with passive contact observer, direct target steps, same initial state, policy weights and material.

Predeclare exact prewalk q/v/action and contact parity, complete traces without error, no extra toe-speed caps or nonfoot impulses, at least two support-qualified endpoint-clearance bouts per foot in every lane, no more both-foot-unloaded substeps, and every lane positive progress at least 90% of matched sham. The pooled sham-vs-candidate count of intervals where the sole is clear but some toe remains below the clearance threshold must decline at least 20%; sham count must be nonzero. Median and p95 endpoint loaded-contact-point speeds must not exceed 150% of sham. These diagnostic values are not biological targets. A pass remains a used seed/two-second screen requiring other starts, longer walk and takeoff transition.

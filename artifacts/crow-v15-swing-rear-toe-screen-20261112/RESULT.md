# Supported-swing rear-toe extension: rejected

The matched used-seed candidate commanded bounded rear-toe extension during the positive swing carrier when the opposite foot reported support. All prewalk q/v/action and contact samples matched, and both arms had 2,400 endpoint records and 38,400 contact substeps, no logged solver errors, no additional toe-speed caps, no nonfoot contacts and no fully unloaded substeps. Each lane had three support-qualified clearance bouts per foot.

Across both feet and eight lanes, the count of sampled intervals with the sole clear but at least one toe still below the 1 mm threshold fell from 49 to zero. Loaded endpoint contact-point speed also fell: median 0.00212 versus 0.00276 m/s and p95 0.03024 versus 0.03484 m/s. The samples include contact transitions and do not establish integrated slip or biological accuracy.

However, candidate root travel was only 12.48–14.11 mm in two seconds versus 25.07–29.71 mm in the matched sham. All eight lanes failed the predeclared 90% progress requirement. The unchanged gait has a substantial movement cost associated with this early toe command, but these data do not establish the precise physical cause. A follow-up may test waiting for accepted own-foot unloading, with identical physical gates. This is one used seed, not a reliable gait or whole-sequence qualification; no controller was promoted.

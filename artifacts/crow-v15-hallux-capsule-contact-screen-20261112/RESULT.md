# Rounded rear-toe collision proxy: failed physical screen

The candidate is **not admitted**. All eight first episodes completed 300 steps with no reported physical failure, physics error, contact overflow, nonfoot-ground contact or sampled toe-speed cap. The same-binary unchanged-box control reproduced the retained box-amplitude sweep exactly across 2,400 endpoint samples and 38,400 contact substeps. The short two-step candidate smoke passed before the full trial. Structural audit found only the four intended rear toe shapes changed; 35 bodies, 34 joints, 40 DOFs and two inherited collision exclusions remained identical. Shape-neutral longitudinal extent and plantar height were preserved; mass and inertia were deliberately unchanged and are not inferred from the new shape.

The normal policy contract rejected the changed world. After diagnostic-only layout inspection, actor layers, biases and transforms were copied exactly to the new fingerprint for this trial. All non-world/non-radius observation and action hash inputs matched, but eight radius table entries covering four bodies changed from 13.11 to 15.70 mm, and sensor values and physical trajectories can differ. The geometry remains an engineering proxy based on existing model dimensions, not a measured corvid foot.

Contact order did not improve: all 40 complete stances with both toe groups had front-group release before rear-group release, while another 49 complete foot-contact runs had no detected front group and cannot establish ordering. The rear target was recorded before or at last front contact in 40/40 qualifying stances, with median 40 ms lead; a position target is not a measured force. Contact and stance thresholds, digit identity and surface differences limit the magpie comparison. No rear-toe self-contact pair with another crow body was recorded at sampled endpoints; this is not a proof of no transient substep contact.

Against the held box-body walking reference, all lanes achieved at least three supported clearance bouts per foot and at least 90% of its forward displacement. Two candidate lanes had 1 and 2 both-feet-zero-normal substeps versus zero held. Candidate had 186 sole-clear/toe-low endpoints versus 49 held, failing the 120% gate; 92 candidate endpoints had accepted toe contact. Loaded contact-point sampled speed median/p95 was 0.00355/0.03145 m/s versus 0.00276/0.03484 held. Point velocities do not measure integrated slip, and a thresholded no-load substep alone does not prove a fall. Capsule-specific clearance geometry is used in the saved gait analysis; different body geometry makes candidate-versus-held prewalk state parity inappropriate as a gate.

This 4-6 s used-seed diagnostic does not qualify whole-body gait, biological realism, leg-powered takeoff, flight, grasping, landing, or autonomous navigation.

# Result: 80 ms coordinated leg extension failed the development screen

The candidate changed only the extension ramp from 160 to 80 ms. All eight first episodes reached 400 control intervals, with no reported substep errors or contact overflow. For the candidate, baseline and active crouch, recorded endpoint states, actions and contact samples matched through step 345, and all required substep, effort and toe records were present. All 368 drive-target observations were available; rear walking-toe targets remained zero during the push.

Every comparable lane 2–7 failed all-lanes acceptance. Maximum root rise was 0.0292–0.0342 m, below 0.05 m. Three lanes never had three unloaded intervals; two first reached that condition at step 375 and one at 358. All six had 6–8 substeps with at least one toe at or above 11.99 rad/s. Upward impulse relative to crouch over steps 348–375 ranged from -0.1523 to -0.0003 N s, and over the earlier 348–365 window from -0.0214 to -0.0054 N s. The later control window crosses the crouch controller transition, so the earlier comparison is reported separately. Five release outcomes changed compared with the 160 ms candidate, but this one correlated batch does not establish general performance. No candidate is promoted.

Whole-body FK momentum analysis passed its uniform-translation calibration and found negative vertical momentum change over steps 348–365 for all six lanes; its residual includes unmeasured force and numerical contributions and is not an aero measurement. Root height is not whole-body COM. The run has passive aerodynamics and earlier body-moment action, and it uses one development seed: it does not establish isolated leg power, flight, landing or mission qualification.

# Physical clearance of the clean first leg jump

The fast-push latched-retraction variants show first unloading while rising, without the rebound that invalidated the earlier height-only result. Independently reconstruct both feet and all toes for all eight development lanes and validate reconstructed lane-one poses against native replay and final-substep impulses against published contacts. Require at least two consecutive accepted poses after the first push where every foot/toe box is >1 mm above ground, full-interval 16-substep foot normal impulse is <=1e-7 N s, root vertical speed is >0.05 m/s and there is no other-body ground contact. Report later contact and root motion separately. This is an accepted-pose geometric check of development starts, not continuous swept clearance, sustained flight, routing, landing or biological validation. No reserved layout is used.

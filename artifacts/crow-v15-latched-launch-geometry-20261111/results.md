# First-jump foot clearance

For both successful fast-push thresholds, all eight development lanes had three consecutive first-jump accepted states in which both sole boxes and all eight toe boxes were clear of the ground by more than 1 mm, no foot normal impulse was measured over the entire 16-substep interval, root upward speed exceeded 0.05 m/s, and no other-body ground contact occurred. Minimum foot clearance at the first such state was approximately 14 mm. The reconstructed poses matched the retained native replay and last-substep contact evidence within the predeclared tolerances.

The root's horizontal velocity during the clear rising samples was backward, and the bird subsequently returned to ground before suppressed flapping resumed. This is not yet sustained takeoff, flight handoff, full-route or landing qualification. Accepted-pose geometry does not establish swept within-step clearance; passive aerodynamics were not disabled. No unused layout was used.

# Uniform longer stride: stability gate failed

On eight previously used seed-20261127 starts, the .34 hip-stroke, .05 rear-toe-preload variant moved forward .1545–.1564 m over 4–6 s versus .1307–.1393 m for its matched .30 baseline. It had no both-feet-unloaded substeps, toe speed-cap events or nonfoot ground impulse in that interval. The first 4 s matched exactly, including 1,600 state samples and 25,600 contact substeps.

However, right-foot contact-point speed p95 rose from .0249–.0349 m/s to .0520–.0600 m/s, exceeding the preset 1.5x per-foot gate in every lane. Left-foot ratios exceeded the limit in some lanes as well. These are sampled contact-point speeds rather than integrated slip. This setting is rejected and is not a qualified or biologically calibrated gait. The two-second diagnostic does not establish the full walk-to-flight transition.

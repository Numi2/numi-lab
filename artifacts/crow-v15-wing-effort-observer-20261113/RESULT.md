# Result: observer reproduced run and captured wing effort-buffer saturation

The logging-only host matched the retained aero observer in all recorded q/v/action/contact states across all 400 steps and every recorded contact substep in steps 335–400. It produced all 3,584 expected wing records and no detected error or overflow. In lane 2 at control step 369, flap DOF 8 had target 0.34197 rad. During substeps 0 and 1 its working-effort buffer was 120, while its pre-contact velocity changed from 2.7935 to 15.7388 and then to 28.6963 rad/s. The buffer was 82.8567 at substep 2, then -36.4654 at substep 3 as velocity began to decrease. The other comparable lanes have the same qualitative transient; per-lane peak records and the complete log are retained.

This observes correlation in an internal buffer, not a controlled intervention. In particular, we have not established that the buffer alone equals the complete actuator torque, nor inferred a bird-specific torque bound or whether the drive, aerodynamic closure or numerical coupling is the dominant cause. The previous pre-solve audit bounds only the selected pre-contact versus subsequent velocity changes. The experiment is a used-seed diagnostic; it qualifies neither flight nor the mission.

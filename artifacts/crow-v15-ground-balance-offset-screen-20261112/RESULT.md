# Standing offset screen: failed, with control masking identified

All eight lanes failed the first episode on the reattached-foot and shortened-shank experimental body, at steps 148, 167, 190, 179, 178, 183, 185 and 197, respectively. The internal zero-offset lane reproduced the preceding trimmed trial exactly at all 300 recorded endpoint states and 4,800 contact substeps. No reported substep errors or constraint overflow occurred. Different offsets were assigned to different initial lanes and are not a replicated estimate of any offset's effect. These results do not qualify walking, and the body alteration remains unadmitted.

A post-hoc target audit substantially limits the screen's interpretation. During the first two seconds the standing-curriculum gate suppressed position residuals. Despite differences in the logged policy-action field, every nonzero lane's physical q, v, and contacts matched its own no-offset reference through step 101; the earliest q/v differences occurred at step 102. The instrumented replays matched each retained physical trajectory exactly across 880 endpoints and unchanged lane 3 across 110 endpoints. The sampled drive targets were identical at steps 98-101; at step 102 the two hip targets changed by -0.21 rad in the -0.30 lane and +0.21 rad in the +0.30 lane, alongside other small state-dependent target changes. These are commanded targets, not measured actuator torque. Thus this trial tests recovery after the onset of a backward lean, not an early standing response.

The initial two- and six-step target snapshots and the later transition-window follow-up are all retained. The later target-observer patches correspond to each sampled window; reconstruct the previous observer from the substep-target-interpolation study, apply the relevant patch, then build using build-target-observer.py. The two short run scripts were later changed to six steps; each run's saved command.json preserves its actual invocation. The build.py-generated hashes.json uses an inherited host field for the prior matched observer; use each run's inputs.json for the actual host and shader hashes. The target-host-hashes.json records only the last observer rebuild.

Neither the mask bypass nor a balance controller was included in this trial. The related-corvid anatomical data remain provisional for American crow, and a successful standing experiment would not establish a complete sequence or independent biological validation.

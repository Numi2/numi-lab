# Retained autonomous toe-command audit

Decoded the packed landing sequence, left toe-contact ramp and flight-fold ramp from phase samples in the eight first episodes of the retained phase-adjusted full development run, then evaluated the corresponding current shader expression. Only lane 3 supplied any samples with sequence 170–229: two samples, neither requesting a negative perch target or selecting the flight-fold floor over a perch target. The other seven lanes supplied none. The shader expression *can* floor a negative extension request at the nonnegative flight target, but this retained run does not show that it did so, and provides no basis for attributing its failures to that interaction. The more immediate observed problem remains loss of pitch stability before the release sequence in the failed lanes.

These are decoded endpoint phase samples from one used seed and layout, not a direct measurement of applied target, toe motion or force. Exact timing between the sampled state and shader pass is not established. The expression must be instrumented and evaluated in a matched test before changing or blaming the interaction. Failed and absent release cases remain part of the record.

# Wing-held push timing audit

This post-hoc timeline examines lane 6 of the matched wing-held development test. The push COM vertical velocity rose from about -0.002 m/s at tick 350 to +0.208 m/s at 354, fell to +0.028 m/s by 357 and was -0.056 m/s at 365. Both feet remained supported during the early impulse. Normal foot impulse rose above the crouched hold at ticks 352 and 354, then fell below it at 356–357. This establishes a transient change in whole-body motion and contact impulse, not an isolated muscle force or a successful launch. The next experiment should investigate contact, geometry and actuator timing under matched controls.

Inputs and their hashes are in input-hashes.json. No final layout or seed was used.

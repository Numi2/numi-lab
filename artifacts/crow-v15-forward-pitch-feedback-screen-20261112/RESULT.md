# Forward-speed pitch feedback: rejected preliminary turn screen

The predeclared forward-speed-sensitive wing-sweep feedback did not meet the turn screen in any of three left/right pairs. All six candidate lanes reached interval 1600 without recorded physical failure or post-intervention nonfoot contact; the 48 retained pre-intervention comparisons matched, with no recorded solver errors or missing contact substeps. The highest-bank positive-turn reference failed at interval 1466, so its later state cannot be compared.

At interval 1600, candidate body-forward speeds were -0.129 to +0.327 m/s, lateral speeds -1.735 to +1.831 m/s, absolute velocity-to-body angles 1.220 to 1.645 rad, and absolute heading errors 1.218 to 1.402 rad. These are sparse endpoint measurements, not whole-interval extrema. The predeclared thresholds require forward speed at least 0.5 m/s, absolute sideslip at most pi/4, and final heading error at most pi/12, alongside height, pitch and contact bounds. The candidates fail; survival alone is insufficient. One lower-bank final forward speed improved from -0.105 to -0.019 m/s compared with its matched control, but remained backward and predominantly sideways.

This is one used development seed and a sparse sampled screen, not an independent qualification. Direct body moments remain active before nine seconds. Wing aerodynamic coefficients and resulting motion are not biologically calibrated. The reserved final layouts and seeds were not used. Raw replay, contact output, commands, source patch and analysis are retained alongside this result.

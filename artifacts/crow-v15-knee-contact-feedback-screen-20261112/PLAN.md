# Matched knee timing plus measured foot-contact feedback

Prior controlled screens found that the 0.16 knee-phase warp improved the modeled knee timing but increased foot contact motion; reducing the hip swing alone failed to fix it. The earlier separately audited materialized contact sensor responded to fore-aft contact motion and modestly reduced right-foot motion in another gait. Test whether its bounded, one-control-step delayed signal compensates for the knee-warp effect, without claiming this engineering signal measures stance-integrated slip or biological force.

Use v15 experimental research world and materialized sensor host, 16 substeps, 300 transitions, same 20261126 and 20261127 development seeds, eight correlated lanes. Lanes 0–1: zero knee warp and zero feedback (uniform-gait controls). Lanes 2–3: knee warp .16, gain zero. Lanes 4–5: knee warp .16, hip stance feedback gain +0.5; lanes 6–7: same knee warp, gain +1. Feedback is the pre-existing bounded fore-aft point-velocity hip term, |action| <= .12, faded in 4.02–4.22 s and applied only when the leg reports load. The separate sensor is computed after task completion and uses a diagnostic power-state lane that an effort pass restores; its timestamp, transient state alias, reset/rollback and production behavior are unresolved. This trial is strictly isolated and cannot be promoted as production.

Compare with the same-seed same-lane uniform-gait baseline and, for lanes 4–5, the existing knee-warp-only trial. Validate the newly recorded sensor against independently reconstructed final-substep contact velocities, count unique values, missing values and large errors, and audit unchanged controls and pre-intervention state/contact parity. Apply identical prior exploratory gates: all episodes complete, zero both-feet-unloaded substeps, zero toe cap, zero non-foot ground impulse, each foot p95 endpoint contact speed <=1.5x same-lane baseline, at least .05 m forward movement, at least one complete recovery per foot without reduced count, and knee minimum >=5 percentage points earlier than baseline. Record both seeds and all lanes regardless of outcome. Single short development trials cannot establish full gait or species validity.

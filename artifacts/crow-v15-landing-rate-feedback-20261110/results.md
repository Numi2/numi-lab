# Result: both rate-feedback trials failed

All 16 runs reached the five waypoint regions but all 16 had a physical termination before the 4500-step horizon, compared with six of eight surviving on the matched J baseline. Each final diagnostic contained a positive-impulse shank or thigh contact with the perch. Neither setting produced more than three consecutive bilateral loaded samples, a sampled bilateral opposed-toe grip, or a supported landing. Root posture was pitched forward near failure. The preregistered predictions failed and neither controller is promoted. Waypoint completion alone is not landing success.

Across the two trials the research all-substep impulse sensor classified 12,416 and 12,011 side-step samples after 25 impulse-free steps, with maximum absolute toe angles 0.000533 and 0.000513 rad. This does not establish deliberate grip and release or absence of all geometric contacts; endpoint-only noncontact angles in the other analysis can include hidden intermediate contact. These are privileged guided runs on a development layout, not autonomous or held-out tests. The shader modifies the teacher's bounded command and leaves the separately fingerprinted flight law unchanged.

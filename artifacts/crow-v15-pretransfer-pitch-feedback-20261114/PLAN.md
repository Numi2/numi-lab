# Earlier pitch feedback during one-foot support

In the failed 1.0 distal-coupling trial, the matched native FK audit found the modeled center of mass already 6 mm beyond the perch top's positive x edge at interval 270, before the foot return, and drifting forward as the stance grip degraded. This edge is not the full support polygon, and the observation does not establish cause. Test stronger bounded pitch feedback during the earlier one-foot phase rather than only after contact is lost. Both lanes retain the same 1.0 post-return distal coupling and existing support gate, hip target clamp and slew. Lane 0 uses the existing pitch proportional/damping coefficients 0.8/0.04; lane 1 uses 1.6/0.12 as soon as the existing pitch reference and support gate become valid. Position and velocity terms, other joint targets, solver, contact, friction, effort and limits remain unchanged. Log command/state prefix and the actual pitch feedback, monitor toe speeds, full contact substeps, release/return, root drift, tilt and terminal 100-interval supported bilateral grip. Preserve outcomes and treat the single constructed trial as neither gait nor landing qualification.

# Related-corvid stance knee-flexion hypothesis: rejected in this form

The unmodified controls matched six-second baselines and all prefixes matched through four seconds, with 1,600 states and 25,600 contact substeps identical. This is a used-seed model test, not a measured crow kinematic fit.

The .05 normalized stance knee-flexion setting gave .160–.162 m forward movement over 4–6 s with continuous collective support and no toe caps or nonfoot impulses, but right-foot contact-point speed p95 rose by 2.06–2.17x versus the matched baseline. The .10 setting also exceeded that 1.5x gate and had 12 both-feet-unloaded substeps per lane. The .20 setting exceeded the contact-speed gate and did not meet the 5% progress criterion in either lane. No variant passed the predeclared combined gate.

A separate finer observer subsequently detected substantial within-step contact motion in the baseline; the endpoint contact-point metric used for this comparison is not a material-point slip distance. Further development must use the finer observer and cannot promote the baseline as realistic merely because the earlier endpoint test passed.

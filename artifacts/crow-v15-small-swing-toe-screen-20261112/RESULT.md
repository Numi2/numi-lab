# Small unloaded rear-toe command: rejected

A -0.05 rad rear-proximal toe command during the existing supported and own-foot-unloaded swing was compared with its same-binary sham across eight lanes of one previously used seed. All 2,400 accepted states and 38,400 contact substeps per arm were captured; pre-walk states and contacts matched, and there were no reported solver errors, contact overflow, added toe caps, nonfoot impulses or unloaded substeps. Both feet completed three support-qualified clearance bouts in every lane. Native forward kinematics and retained replay agree to within 4.2e-9 m in position and 7.3e-9 m/s in linear velocity.

Sole-clear but toe-low endpoint counts fell from 49 to 13. The loaded near-vertical contact-point median speed fell from 0.00276 to 0.00238 m/s, and p95 from 0.03484 to 0.03053 m/s; these dependent endpoint samples are not integrated slip distance. However, forward root displacement was 21.65–22.46 mm versus 25.07–29.71 mm in corresponding sham lanes, below the predeclared 90% threshold in all eight. The experiment is rejected and not promoted. It is a two-second development screen, not a biologically validated gait, prolonged walk or physical transition to flight.

# Walking target observer: retained result

An instrumented replay exactly matched 12,800 joint-state and 12,800 contact substeps from the retained, teacher-off, eight-lane development walk; 800 target samples were recorded. During the 2-second analyzed window, signed changes in the knee target correlated 0.912–0.924 with the first-substep joint-position second difference across lanes, and ankle target changes correlated 0.965–0.981. The knee target advanced by 0.0152–0.0167 rad and the ankle by 0.0050–0.0056 rad on average per observed update.

The target is sampled after substep zero. The second difference is a motion proxy, not torque or biological stiffness. Correlation is not proof of causation; coupled contacts and joints remain possible contributors. One used development seed and a short trial do not qualify gait or represent measured American-crow kinematics. A causal intervention with matched contacts and full physical qualification is still required.

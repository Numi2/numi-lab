# Staged toe opening and foot lift

The immediate +0.50 rad knee bend lifted the left foot in preliminary traces, but the supporting foot's opposing contact became intermittent. Compare the immediate +0.50 comparator, a +0.50 lift delayed 10 intervals and ramped for 20, a +0.50 lift delayed 35 and ramped for 20, and a +0.30 lift delayed 35 and ramped for 20; each has two constructed starts and the same bounded +0.22 hip-roll shift. Toe opening remains contact-triggered and occurs over approximately 35 intervals. This isolates transition timing only approximately: toe drives and physical contact still interact. Require independent substep forces, clearance, upright/slow state, supported release and bilateral terminal grip. Position targets are physically actuated, and the fixed delays are experimental, not a proven autonomous sensor-based state machine. Retain failures and solver rejections.

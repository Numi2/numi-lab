Build a separate observer that copies the destination velocity buffer at each existing contact capture point and logs the 16 toe velocities by lane and substep. It must not modify solver equations, contact, control, limits, integration, or the original observer. Use the existing 35-body/40-velocity hip-roll mapping and retain source patch and library hashes.

Replay the previous original-placement control under the new observer. Require exact q/v, command and contact-event parity through all 450 intervals and identical per-substep contact records against the original observer, complete unique velocity records, correct lane and 16-joint indexing, and finite values. Compare the final captured substep against the corresponding accepted-state toe velocities and explicitly report whether epochs match; do not assume they do. Report maxima and limit hits before the first fall, retain failures and all input identities. Passive observation and a constructed perch do not establish grip, walking, landing, or biological realism.

# Bounded stance-only assistance

The full supporting-leg stroke moved farther but produced increasing torso pitch, near-continuous rear-toe drag and high contact-point speed. The original controller suppresses the negative carrier when the opposite foot is unloaded. Test a reduced intervention: while that condition holds and the current foot is accepted as supported, add one quarter of the existing negative carrier; otherwise preserve the old carrier exactly. This quarter-scale value is an engineering hypothesis motivated by the excessive pitch in the full-stroke trial, not a measured crow kinematic target and not a linear prediction. Disable the extra toe target in both arms to isolate leg assistance. Use the same shader and physical host, with switch off/on and policy contract rebound without changing policy weights; retain the existing upper-body hold.

Before inspection, run eight paired lanes on used development seed 20261127, 300 steps, 16 substeps, no scheduled resets. Require exact prewalk state and contact parity, full records, no physical failure/reset/error/overflow/nonfoot contacts or toe speed caps, no additional both-foot-zero-normal-impulse substeps versus paired sham in any lane, at least three support-qualified clearance bouts per foot per lane, >=90% paired forward travel in every lane and >=120% in at least six lanes, sole-clear/toe-low endpoint count <=120% of sham, and loaded endpoint contact-point speed median and p95 <=1.5 times sham. Report failures and continuous values; these engineering criteria are not biological qualification. A pass would require another development start and a longer continuous test before any promotion.

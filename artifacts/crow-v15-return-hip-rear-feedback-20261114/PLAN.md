# Contact-gated return hip and rear-toe test

The previous returning-foretoe trial failed, and independent FK finite differences on retained COM-feedback states identify a local geometric tradeoff: hip advance moves the returning foretoe closer but moves the rear toe away; rear toe flexion tends to bring its collider closer. Test a small combination using physical position drives: lane 1 permits the rear target to reach the existing 1.20 rad joint maximum and adds at most +0.01 rad left hip target, slewed by at most 0.001 rad per 20 ms and gated on returning rear-toe contact after release. Lane 0 reproduces the archived COM-feedback candidate. All other targets, starts, physical limits and solver stay fixed. The local linearization does not guarantee reachability, stability or contact.

Require exact control parity against the archived 450-state COM run and paired prefix until first changed target, verify hip bounds and gate, both feet and full substeps. Require at least ten supported release intervals, terminal 100 consecutive bilateral opposing-grip intervals, bounded tilt/speed, no nonfoot support, no early toe threshold and no GPU/observer failure. Preserve all failures. State/contact epoch alignment remains unverified for this constructed perch probe. A pass is not qualification of walking, takeoff, flight, landing or mission.

# Continuous wingbeat sensitivity: preregistered

The matched late observer shows flap drives saturating and tracking errors of 0.80–1.05 rad RMS, with mean modeled vertical aero support below model weight and recurrent ground contact. Test whether a continuous sinusoidal flap target at a fixed effort bound of 3.0 improves tracking and sustained support, using 4.8, 5.6 and 6.4 Hz in paired lanes 2–3, 4–5 and 6–7 respectively. The target clock starts at 7.04 s, preserves the pre-stroke envelope and continues through 8.0 s instead of blending to the inherited controller at 7.30–7.40 s. These are developmental sensitivities, not individually calibrated crow kinematics; 6.4 Hz is retained as a data-informed comparison and no lower frequency is presumed biological. All direct pitch/yaw body moments remain disabled, with identical v15 body, used seed 20261126, leg push, unmodified contact, two wings and physically modeled aero.

Require complete first episodes and observer records, exact sampled state/action/contact parity with the retained 3.0-bound hold arm through step 345, and observed working wing effort at or under 3.0. Report tracking RMS, percentage of saturated substeps, positive and negative working-effort-displacement proxies, modeled mean aerodynamic lift, whole-body COM rise, foot recontact, toe threshold events and early push-minus-hold impulse. A preliminary physically modeled launch requires both lanes of one setting to remain unsupported after a three-tick release through step 400, COM rise >=0.05 m, no nonfoot impulse, no more than one toe-speed threshold substep and early push-minus-hold impulse >0.05 N s in each measurement window. A promising tracking result is not a launch pass; such a pass would still not qualify biological realism, flight maneuvers, landing or navigation. Preserve all outcomes and do not use held-out layouts.

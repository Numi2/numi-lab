# Earlier foot return: rejected

The candidate returned the released foot after 15 consecutive clearance/support intervals rather than 30. Before its return transition (zero-based interval 257), the two same-run lanes had identical logged states, targets and contact substeps; contact/state epoch alignment has not been independently established. All 450 state samples and required substeps were present with no observer errors.

The candidate achieved 30 qualified release intervals, but its longest subsequent bilateral supported opposed-toe grip streak was only 3 intervals, versus 11 for the matched control. Root height dropped below 0.1 m at interval 355 versus 370 in the control, and both reached the 12 rad/s toe-speed cap before falling. Neither met the prespecified terminal 100-interval grip gate. Earlier return therefore did not solve grip; retain the original controller and do not infer successful walking or landing from this stationary test.

The plan, build identity, raw compressed traces, substep observations and analysis scripts are retained with this result. Intervals in different audit files may use one-based versus zero-based indexing; labels in those files identify the convention.

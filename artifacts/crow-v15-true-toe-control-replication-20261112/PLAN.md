# Replicate the flight-leg handoff and isolate the rear-toe floor

Use two already-used development seeds with eight closely related starts each. All lanes receive the same 0.20 s contact-released leg handoff and 0.05 s wing onset. Lanes 0–1 apply the inherited zero floor to the contact-loaded rear-toe target, lanes 2–3 bypass that operation entirely, lanes 4–5 use a 0.05 rad floor and lanes 6–7 use 0.10 rad. This is a true bypass of the tested operation, not a zero-valued clamp. All other gait, controller and physical parameters remain fixed. The .20 s candidate was selected from a single exploratory seed; this replication is developmental, not an unseen holdout.

Record accepted states and per-substep contacts/toe speeds through step 625, all driven joint endpoints for steps 535–625, and compare each same-lane walk prefix through step 485 to the previous two-seed study. Require complete coverage, no solver errors, overflow, nonfoot ground impulses or authored joint/toe speed-limit hits in the monitored window, and contact continuity during steps 501–515. Report actual support, impulse, release and failures separately. No tested arm is presumed to produce biological walking, leg-powered takeoff, qualified flight, full navigation or supported landing.

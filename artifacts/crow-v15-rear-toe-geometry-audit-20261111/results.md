# Retrospective rear-toe geometry comparison

Recomputed oriented-box separating-axis gaps from stored toe and perch poses in the matched-balance hold and the bounded reacquisition trials. In the unmodified hold, rear-toe gap became positive (separated) by tick 140, reaching approximately 0.53 mm at tick 180 and 1.48 mm at tick 220. The feedback case maintained slight modeled overlap through tick 200 and was separated by approximately 0.02 mm at tick 220, consistent with restored rear contact during part of the trial. Both fell and neither qualified support. Solver contact impulse may be zero even when the endpoint SAT gap is slightly negative; endpoint geometry is not an interval-integrated force or proof of load-bearing. The comparison cannot by itself establish why body balance failed or prove realistic toe anatomy.

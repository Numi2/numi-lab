# Center-of-mass motion before failed regrip

Native model FK evaluated each accepted q/v record in the retained eight-lane partial foot-centering trial. In the paired even-start lanes, at zero-based step 100 while bilateral grip was established, whole-body COM x was +0.02538 m; the perch spans x = -0.025 to +0.025 m. At the return latch, step 150, COM x was +0.03338 m and COM velocity was +0.09489 m/s. Thirty intervals later, COM x was +0.07060 m for the zero-gain lane and +0.07736, +0.09894 and +0.08752 m for gains .25, .5 and 1.0. COM includes every modeled link; the logged root position alone is approximately 0.018 m behind COM at the first two samples.

These are accepted endpoint kinematics on constructed starts, not a complete force or balance analysis. A projecting COM outside the perch's box footprint does not by itself prove insufficient support because gripping toes can exert moments. The chronology supports studying whole-body balance and gripping geometry jointly, and it does not establish a calibrated crow behavior or an airborne landing. Input and binary hashes are in `input.json`; the source trace and runtime library are archived separately.

# Matched height-command control

With the requested height step removed, all eight lanes reproduced 6000 pre-command/control states and 96000 contact substeps exactly. From the same starts, the step run changed actual root height by approximately +.20 or -.20 m relative to this control in the final window. For example lane 6 was identical at step 600, differed by .021 m at 625 and .182 m at 650, with different vertical velocity and physical flap angles during the transition. All lanes survived the same 24 s; no body or wing speed caps were observed, with two early toe cap events in the broader eight lanes.

These are used-seed, obstacle-free tests of an uncalibrated estimated aerodynamic model with task body-moment actions disabled; other empirical aerodynamic closures remain. Rapid, symmetric convergence requires model calibration and should not be interpreted as real-crow flight. This does not qualify recovery from perturbations, the control envelope, navigation or landing.

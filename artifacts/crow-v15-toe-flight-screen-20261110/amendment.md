# Amendment after visual-binding discovery

The original benchmark policy's fingerprints differ on the toe body, and the corrected camera assets change observations. The original plan therefore prohibits rebinding its weights as if observations were equivalent. Instead, construct an explicitly zero-output diagnostic policy with the new contract and hold all non-flight actions (head, beak and extra channels) at zero in the experimental benchmark adapter. The separately frozen flight-law source remains unchanged and drives its original first 15 actions. The fixed-head and passive-toe setup differs from the original benchmark and from the learned navigation task; results will be a new toe-body flight-only screen, not a matched replay or qualification for a moving head, landing or visual navigation. Use the same two development seeds, five commands, eight perturbations, and original tracking gates. Preserve a one-lane admission smoke test before the two 80-lane tests. If the model cannot admit the screen or the diagnostic policy influences flight actions, report the limitation rather than forcing a result.

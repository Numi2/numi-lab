# Fixed-head toe-body screen: obstacle collisions, not flight isolation

The two 80-lane tests failed the original straight/turn/climb/descent gates (0/160 passed). They used the corrected toe-body visual packs and a constant zero diagnostic policy, with the frozen flight law controlling the first 15 actions and the head fixed. Of 160 lanes, 154 had physical contact terminations. Their retained first termination contacts all involved course objects; wing/slalom contacts dominated. The remaining six lanes reached the time limit but did not pass the sampled flight criteria. No solver steps failed. This test retained physical obstacles and cannot separate flight instability from collision/routing; it does not establish failure of obstacle-free flight or qualify flight. See contact-analysis.json for collider pairs and preceding positions, attitudes and velocities. The logged zero policy outputs are not applied flight-actuator commands, which this logger did not record. A matched obstacle-collision-off experiment is required.

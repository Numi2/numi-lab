# Bounded launch command screen

All six candidates matched the preceding contact-triggered handoff exactly through step 252 in all eight development lanes, then first unloaded at step 253 while rising. None had ground recontact through step 500 or native solver failures or terminations. All failed the prospective launch-to-flight gate: at step 353 the forward speeds ranged 0.035–0.038 m/s for the 0.4 m/s speed, 0.65 m height target, and were lower in all other conditions (down to -0.180 m/s). At step 500 all recovered to at least 0.330 m/s. Lower initial height substantially reduces backward drift, but no candidate qualifies and no acceptance threshold is changed. Detailed per-lane observations and hashes accompany this result.

These are eight development starts on one training course, with 16-substep ground contact observations. They do not establish obstacle clearance, landing, grip, unused-layout navigation or biological realism.

# Crow-referenced wingbeat-period probe, preregistered

The pre-raised wing test failed all six lanes; two later remained airborne only after a support interruption. Its temporary cosine carrier has period 0.36 s (2.78 Hz). Jackson and Dial (2011, DOI 10.1242/jeb.046789) reported 6.4 ± 0.1 Hz pooled for American-crow escape wingbeats excluding the first. Test a 0.15625 s (6.4 Hz) period for the temporary cosine carrier while preserving the pre-raised envelope, push-triggered phase, 4/8/16 engineering effort bounds, 160 ms leg push, transition and every other model/solver/contact setting. The study does not measure the first stroke at that frequency, and a sinusoidal single-DOF target is not a measured wing trajectory: this is a sensitivity probe, not a fitted or biologically qualified first stroke.

Run matched push and active-crouch arms on used seed 20261126, eight correlated lanes, 400 intervals, 16 substeps, no resets. Require complete records and no errors/overflow; exact recorded state/action/contact parity between arms through 345 and against prior pre-raised push through 345, before the counter starts; require effort bounds on all recorded substeps. Report support from 340–347, both early impulse contrasts, pre-release support gaps, three-interval release and later recontact, rise, nonfoot impulse, toe threshold events, actual wing speed and simulated aero peaks. The strict screen remains: >=0.05 N s in both windows, continuous foot support from 340 until first three-interval release by 375, no subsequent recontact through 400, >=0.05 m root rise, zero nonfoot impulse and at most one toe threshold substep. Each bound must pass in both assigned correlated lanes. No one-seed pass qualifies flight, biological realism, landing, or the held-out mission; preserve raw results including failures.

# Matched leg-powered push controls

The hip -0.50, ankle -0.30 extension arm passed the eight-start 500-step development launch screen. Test whether its jump is produced by the leg extension: compare the unmodified candidate with (1) identical extension and wings suppressed through 5.4 s, and (2) the same crouch held without extension, with contact-driven wing release unchanged. Keep all other physics and control fixed, on the same eight starts for 270 steps at 16 substeps. No artificial force or state reset is allowed. Include a geometry audit of the candidate using independently reconstructed toe and sole box poses, cross-checked against the retained native replay.

Require exact q/v agreement in every lane through step 248. Report each arm's full-substep foot and nonfoot contacts, first unloading and vertical speed, maximum upward speed and height gain from step 247 before 270, and first recontact. For the claim that the legs produce the initial jump, require the wing-suppressed push to unload while rising with at least two consecutive rising unloaded intervals, >20 mm root gain before any recontact in every lane, no nonfoot support and no failures, while held-crouch has no such rising unloaded interval in the matched window. Report contrary results rather than changing criteria. For geometry require both feet's sole and all toe boxes to be >1 mm above the ground at two consecutive accepted unloaded rising poses and no ground impulse in those intervals. This comparison is developmental only and does not validate a landing, unused course, continuous swept geometry, robustness to perturbations or biological realism.

# Matched leg-power controls

The integrated candidate cleared the ground in all eight lanes; independent reconstructed foot and toe endpoint poses were more than 24 mm above the plane at first fully unloaded rising interval, with a replay position cross-check error below 0.000000015 m. Its observed initial upward speed there was about 1.36 m/s. The wing-suppressed push control, however, first unloaded with only about 0.017 m/s upward speed and failed the two-rising-interval criterion in all eight lanes, then recontacted. The held-crouch control remained grounded with minimal vertical motion. Both matched the candidate before the push, and no non-foot support or native failures occurred. Thus the candidate's successful integrated launch substantially depends on the wing contribution and does not meet the predeclared leg-only control gate. Geometry was checked at accepted poses, not continuously swept between substeps.

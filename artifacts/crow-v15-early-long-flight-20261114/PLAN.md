# Longer full-start flight screen for the early handoff

The same full-start v15 candidate stayed contact-free through interval 800 in six correlated .75–.85-rad development lanes; the .70-rad comparisons recontacted. Extend the identical native host, early-handoff metallib, body, solver, 16 substeps, policy, training layout and used seed 20261126 to 1,800 intervals with teacher off and no scheduled resets. Run from the beginning, not a reset at 800. Compare every recorded first-episode state through 800 and logged targets over 335–800 with the retained short trial. Any mismatch invalidates a direct continuation claim.

Before looking at outcomes, require each of the six stronger lanes to reach 1,800 in its first episode, without later foot or nonfoot impulse after the initial three-interval unload, without the nominal 12-rad/s toe-speed threshold, with finite complete recorded state, action, target and contact/toe substeps. Over intervals 450–1800 additionally require root height 0.25–1.30 m, upright tilt at most 0.35 rad, yaw deviation from interval 450 at most 0.35 rad and lateral displacement at most 0.50 m. Over intervals 550–1800 require world-x velocity greater than zero and at most 1.0 m/s. These are conservative engineering screens and do not establish real-bird biomechanics. Record all eight lanes and each failing bound, first contacts and preceding states. Preserve both failures and passes in Git.

This tests a longer full-start first episode on a used seed in the existing training scene; route-policy inputs may affect the non-wing controls, so even a pass does not establish a separate commanded-straight capability. Qualification also requires independent bounded-command straight, left/right, climb, descent and disturbed-start recovery demonstrations; subsequent unseen layouts/seeds, toe grip, supported landing and the continuous full mission are separate. Do not freeze the controller or consume reserved layouts on the basis of this screen.

# Privileged foot-guidance landing experiment

The passive diagnostic was rejected as an exact current-state sensor (up to 2.63 mm mismatch); it can still be evaluated as a delayed approximate signal, not represented as exact or visual perception. Test the existing measured signal without compensation. After reaching waypoint five, a supervisory command uses the signed horizontal goal-to-foot-midpoint difference, with bounded proportional speed (gain 1.2/s, range -0.18 to +0.18 m/s). It holds requested flight height at 1.20 m until the feet are within 2.5 cm of target x, body y within 8 cm, and planar speed below 0.08 m/s, then hands landing height back to the actor. The frozen low-level flight controller is unchanged; no injected force or reset. One full start per previously exposed layout, identical seeds and the baseline contact-triggered takeoff. Retain all failures. Success requires all waypoints, no physical or solver failures, sustained opposing toe contacts, subsequent release, and stable foot-only support, not waypoint completion. Report collisions, first failure, clearance and gripped/support states even if it fails. The goal and foot body positions are privileged simulated quantities and are not visual navigation.

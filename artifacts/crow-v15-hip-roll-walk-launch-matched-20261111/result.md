# Modified body: matched walking and wing-on launch screen

Eight teacher-guided development starts completed 500 intervals with no reported GPU step or first-episode physical failure. Each had at least three qualifying alternating foot-clearance bouts per side during steps 201–301, with 3.27–4.48 cm root advance, no observed nonfoot ground impulse, and no sampled endpoint with both feet over 1 mm clear. Kinematics were checked against lane 1 replay. Four lanes nevertheless had 5–17 individual gait substeps with zero recorded foot normal impulse, so continuous load-bearing walking is not established.

All lanes had two consecutive rising intervals with no recorded ground impulse beginning at step 353 and remained without recorded ground recontact through step 500; root height gain from step 347 was 0.759–0.869 m. There were no toe speed-limit substeps during gait, launch or the observed flight window, but two lanes hit the cap during initial settling. This is a wing-on result only; it does not establish leg-powered causality without a matched wing-suppressed control, continuous toe clearance, perturbed flight recovery, supported landing, autonomous navigation or biological realism. The new release controller is not integrated.

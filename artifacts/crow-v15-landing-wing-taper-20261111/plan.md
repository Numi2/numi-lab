# Physical wing-stroke taper at landing handoff

In the extended used-course reference, a bird began pitching backward immediately after handoff as toe load rose and the wing stroke was switched off. Test a 0.5-second reduction of the actual wing-stroke amplitude from its existing computed value to zero after the same loaded-foot handoff; keep the frozen flight action law active during that taper, and retain the staged toe-release controller afterward. This changes ordinary actuator targets and aerodynamic response only; no forces, teleports or teacher are added. The timing association does not prove causality, and continued flapping could destabilize or snag the feet. Run the same eight used starts for 5000 intervals, compare the prefix before handoff, physical failures, support, toe caps and contact substeps. Do not equate avoiding a fall with landing; assess continuous support, release and regrip and do not use reserved layouts.

# Finer-step grip screen

The 4-substep baseline reproduced all 200 saved endpoint states and passed the behavioral screen in this start, but overlapped the perch 4.706 mm during grip. None of the three finer-step arms achieved three consecutive bilateral opposing-grip samples or terminal support. Thus none qualifies and their grip-conditioned gap is unavailable; this is not evidence of no overlap. Scoring accounted for final-substep duration, but the controllers themselves still used fixed impulse triggers, which correspond to larger force thresholds at 16 and 32 substeps and may have prevented grip transitions. This confound needs a separate comparison with force-equivalent controller thresholds. The original baseline itself fails the 0.5 mm overlap gate. This was one development drop, not a full landing or biology qualification.

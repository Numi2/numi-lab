# Finer-step grip screen

The separated open-toe impact improved below 0.5 mm accepted-pose overlap at 16 and 32 substeps in one case. Test the same development start for the entire 200-step grip/release/support sequence: (0) original controller at 4 substeps, (1) original at 16, (2) individual-toe feedback at 16, and (3) individual-toe feedback at 32. All other settings and state are unchanged. Arm 0 must reproduce all 200 states from the separated baseline. Report completion/rollback, bilateral opposed grip and support, release, terminal open-toe support, and maximum independent grip-conditioned SAT overlap. Any candidate must satisfy the combined support/open-toe condition and at most 0.5 mm observed grip overlap, then pass the eight-start screen and a separately designed landing-only timestep switch with full flight and autonomous navigation requalification. Both 8- and 16-substep whole-route attempts previously failed. No held-out layout is used and this stationary-drop test cannot qualify biological realism.

To compare equal force thresholds, scale the reported impulse thresholds and weight-support threshold by the relevant substep duration; the four-substep control is unchanged.

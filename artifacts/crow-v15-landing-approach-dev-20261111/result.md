# Result: no perch contact

All eight first episodes reached five waypoints with no recorded bird-to-course contact over 5000 control intervals and complete substep coverage. All timed out without perch contact or supported landing. Endpoint-sampled minimum obstacle clearance ranged from 0.071 m to 0.276 m; this is not a continuous geometric bound. Final root x ranged 4.899–4.977 m, approximately 0.3 m short of the development perch. This was teacher-guided on development starts, not an autonomous or held-out evaluation. The added forward command bias and longer horizon did not solve landing.

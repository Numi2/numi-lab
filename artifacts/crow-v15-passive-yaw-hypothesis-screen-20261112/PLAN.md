In the current model, fuselage side drag acts at the root center, so that term cannot directly produce a yawing moment. Related-bird studies motivate testing passive body/wing realignment but do not determine American-crow force coefficients or centers of pressure: Ortega-Jimenez et al. 2019, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6660782/; Ros et al. 2011, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3250151/. This is a research-only model-form counterfactual, not a validated crow parameter or a replacement for wing kinematics.

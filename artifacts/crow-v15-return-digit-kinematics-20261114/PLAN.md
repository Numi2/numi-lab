# Returning digit geometry sensitivity

Use retained COM-feedback accepted states at zero-based ticks 220 to 290 by tens to compute fixed-state +/-0.02 rad finite differences for the left front proximal, distal, both and left rear proximal, distal, both. Enforce model joint bounds for every perturbation and compare baseline collider gaps with the independently cross-checked returning-leg FK. This identifies only a local geometric response, not reachable dynamics, contact forces or grip; do not promote a controller on this basis.

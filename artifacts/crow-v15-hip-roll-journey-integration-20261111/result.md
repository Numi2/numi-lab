# Experimental 35-body journey preflight

An isolated host inserts the experimental hip-roll joints into the v15-derived two-segment body, keeps the research flight law unchanged, remaps the five scene-visual bindings and contact observer indices, and updates the task shader for the new body count and contact indices. The initial attempt failed the old topology guard; a second failed because the separately generated visual assets were in the wrong location. Both failures are retained. After correcting those issues, changed world/task/observation/action fingerprints rejected the old policies as expected. Diagnostic rebinding preserved both actors’ weights and transforms as equal arrays. A two-step teacher-enabled native preflight then completed.

The new toe release/return control is not integrated into this task shader. This preflight does not prove observation semantic equivalence, correct contact classification in a full course, policy validity, flight, landing, or autonomous navigation. Flight and contact observer behavior require independent tests; the added joints change dynamics even with the same frozen flight source.

# Explicit climb and descent on the v15 body

Keep the same obstacle-free host with ground retained, no direct body moments, smooth launch, fixed .8 speed gain and continuous first episodes. On previously used seed 20261126, controls 0–1 retain their prior command throughout 24 s and must reproduce it. Lanes 2–7 use .55 m/s and zero lateral/heading command, with .95 m target through 12 s, ramp to 1.25 m during 12–13 s, ramp to .80 m during 16–17 s and ramp to 1.00 m during 20–21 s. Other state, actuators and bounds are unchanged. Verify pre-command and zero-control parity, then measure actual climb/descent magnitudes, overshoot, vertical rates, speed error, altitude, attitude, contact and speed-limit hits. No individual waypoint flags count as a mission with obstacle collisions disabled. This is used-seed development evidence, not biological or unseen-layout qualification.

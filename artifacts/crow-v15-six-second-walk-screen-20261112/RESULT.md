# Six-second development walk: limited matched pass

On development seed 20261126, all eight smoothed candidates and matched unsmoothed controls completed 500 intervals, including six seconds of walking, with complete contact and toe-velocity substep coverage, no observed physical failure, solver error, nonfoot ground impulse or toe-speed cap. The two arms matched exactly before walking; both also matched their respective preceding two-second runs through interval 300. Every candidate lane had three qualified endpoint-clearance bouts per foot in the first two-second window and four per foot in each subsequent window. Each window had positive root displacement; summing the three within-window displacements gives approximately 77–81 mm across six seconds. This is a very slow scripted, support-gated physical gait, not a learned or biologically validated crow walk.

Across the full walking window, the candidate had zero substeps with neither foot recording a positive normal impulse; the matched controls had 1,131 such substeps across eight lanes. This is specific to this used seed and longer-walk setup, which deliberately delayed the launch crouch and toe-off detector. The same smoothed gait failed its shorter matched screen on development seed 20261127, so it is not robust enough to promote. Endpoint clearance does not prove continuous separation; the prototype history is not safe for arbitrary chunks, reset or rollback. The experiment did not test the transition to takeoff, the route or landing.

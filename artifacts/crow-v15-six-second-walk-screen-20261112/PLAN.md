# Six-second physical walking screen

The 5 ms phase-led, physically smoothed gait passed a two-second matched development screen. Test whether those results persist across a six-second walking window (episode seconds 4–10) in the same v15 body and same-binary paired experiment. Delay the takeoff crouch and toe-off detector until after this window; do not add a visual motion or force. The start and first two seconds must be independently checked because the longer-duration shader is a new build, and both arms must match exactly before walking. Same seed, actor and starting conditions; this is a used development seed, not the reserved layouts.

Require 500 intervals without first-episode termination, missing substeps, solver errors, toe-speed caps, nonfoot ground impulse or body fall. For each of three two-second windows require at least two independently computed, opposite-foot-supported endpoint-clearance bouts per foot in each lane and positive forward displacement; report unsupported substeps and compare matched travel without hiding failed lanes. Contact-point endpoint clearance is not continuous separation. A pass remains a limited slow walk, not biological validation, general reset/rollback, leg-powered takeoff, landing or autonomous navigation.

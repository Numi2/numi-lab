On used seed 20261126, teacher off, no scheduled resets, 16 physics substeps, candidate lanes 6 and 7 remained unloaded from the first zero-foot-load interval (377 and 376 respectively) through interval 1800. Their first episodes lasted the full run; no nonfoot or later ground contact, reported observer error or toe-speed cap was seen. Forward displacement after release was 11.39 and 11.27 m; heights over the measured flight window were 0.413–0.832 and 0.547–1.212 m. The controller alternated height targets 0.55/0.80 m; the second lane's peak exceeds 0.80 m by 0.412 m. Pronator effort was at its limit for about 47% of the measured 600–1200 interval window. These are substantial tracking and actuation limitations. Unchanged lanes 0 and 1 had physical first-episode failures at 760 and 764; do not call this an eight-lane success.

The 450-step launch prefix matches the independently retained short launch exactly in logged q, v, policy actions and contacts in all eight lanes. The straight analyzer's comparison against itself checks parsing completeness, not independent reproducibility. It reconstructs commanded targets and checks substep coverage, effort, contact and finite-state limits; observer state/contact epoch alignment remains inferred. The wing already moved before unloading and the matched leg-held controls from the earlier test sometimes remained airborne; this is not proof of leg-powered takeoff. This scripted, used-seed, obstacle-free development screen does not qualify or freeze flight, test disturbance recovery, demonstrate landing or establish navigation. The paired turn run is separate.

# Result

With unchanged frozen-flight shader and rebound legacy tensors, all 16 privileged development starts reached all waypoints and survived to 4500 steps, with no solver failure. Bilateral load streaks reached at most two endpoints, with no opposing bilateral grip and no landed-phase handoff. Pre-perch sampled toe angles remained below 0.00025 rad, but after 25 steps without a foot/toe contact candidate they reached 0.2387 rad on I and the -0.3 rad joint limit on J. This fails the predeclared 0.05 rad reopening requirement; the 0.009 damping change is rejected and the prior research body is not replaced. Different damping changed trajectories, so the improved survival is not a controlled landing success or an autonomous result. The task actor tensors were rebound identically to the changed contract; they are not trained for this body.

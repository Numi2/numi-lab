# Standing feedback integrated into experimental walk

All eight development starts ran 300 intervals without reported physical failure or trace errors. Before the walking preload activates, the integrated run exactly reproduced the matched -0.5 knee-feedback trace at all 1,208 recorded samples and 19,328 contact substeps through the 3 s diagnostic. The reference is the same-start prior 0.2-rad toe-preload walk without the initial knee feedback.

During 2–4 s preparation the four starts with 202, 158, 433 and 70 unloaded substeps in the reference had zero in the candidate; the other four already had zero. During 4–6 s, both arms had zero both-feet-unloaded substeps and zero recorded toe-speed-cap substeps. Every candidate lane had 3 left and 4 right endpoint-resolved support-qualified foot-clearing bouts, and forward displacement was 156.7–158.2 mm, comparable to the reference's 156.6–158.1 mm. These bouts do not prove clearance between endpoints or biological gait. Earlier toe caps during 0–3 s remain in the inherited -0.5 matched trace, so the zero walking count is not a full-sequence pass.

The reconstructed instantaneous accepted contact-point-speed median/p95 was 0.00326/0.07698 m/s, versus 0.00325/0.07717 for the same preload reference. The p95 remains above the previously declared 1.5x gate against the zero-preload comparator (0.04054 m/s), so the overall walking candidate remains unqualified. These endpoint velocities are not integrated slip, and the comparison also changes movement speed. COM, ankle/knee phase, joint dynamics, grip, and unseen-start robustness remain unresolved. The test ends before takeoff and establishes neither autonomous flight nor landing; no controller is promoted.

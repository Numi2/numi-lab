# Support-aware stance test

All variants failed the unchanged 8-lane gait gate, but the 25% stance gain reduced lane-0 left/right slip p95 from 0.0704/0.2816 m/s to 0.0377/0.0529 m/s, while preserving 2 mm swing clearance and 5.37 cm forward travel. The result supports further investigation of the contact-gating discontinuity; it is not a qualified gait.

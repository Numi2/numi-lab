# Route-prefix imitation candidate

The supervised actor was trained on full-start teacher prefixes from five non-failing training lanes, with two additional non-failing starts from the same used layout and seed reserved for development validation. Physically failed lane 2 was excluded entirely. Samples span waypoint stages zero through four. Across the four bounded command outputs, development-validation mean absolute errors changed from [0.0661, 0.4552, 0.0803, 0.6492] before training to [0.0189, 0.0762, 0.0259, 0.0515] after training. See training.json for per-stage values and hashes. This measures teacher fit on correlated states, not autonomous navigation or generalization.

The original actor-only policy was wrapped with an unrelated legacy critic solely because the training API requires one; actor layers and transforms were verified identical before training. The supervised loss does not use critic outputs, and the critic was stripped from the resulting policy with an exact actor-layer and transform comparison. The initial attempt without a critic failed and is not a training result. The output policy is an unqualified candidate: teacher-off physical first-episode tests are required. The frozen low-level flight law was not changed. The compressed actor-only policy was verified against original bytes.

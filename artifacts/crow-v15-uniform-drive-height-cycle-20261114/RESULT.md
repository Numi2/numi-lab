# Uniform-drive altitude cycle: tracking failed

All eight lanes stayed in continuous flight through 1,800 intervals and passed the preregistered base physical and observer gates. Their logged q/v, policy action and contact records exactly matched their own straight-flight references through interval 790, before the programmed height change. Every lane rose and subsequently descended by more than 0.20 m in window means, so the commanded direction was visible.

The tracking gate failed in every lane. During the commanded 0.80 m climb window, mean heights were 0.8480–0.8563 m; during the subsequent commanded 0.55 m descent window, mean heights were 0.6080–0.6107 m. Seven lanes exceeded the 0.05 m mean absolute error limit in the climb window, and all eight exceeded it in the descent window. Detailed errors, target reconstruction, coverage and parity are retained in the analysis files. No controller is promoted on this basis.

The run used an already-used development seed and training layout, teacher off, with no scheduled resets. This fixed schedule does not qualify arbitrary flight commands, disturbed recovery, leg-powered takeoff, toe grip, supported landing or mission execution. The raw trace, split Git parts, source and build identities are retained.

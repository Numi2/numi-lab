# Moderate physical leg fold: short flight screen

No body-to-body contact above threshold was recorded in five lanes during the step 380–600 self-contact window. The rejected stronger fold produced bilateral rear-toe contacts in all five. Lane 1 matched the reference in q, v, actor observations and recorded actions for the first 370 frames, but the moderate fold still changed flight substantially: at frame 450 forward velocity was -0.127 m/s versus +0.369 m/s for the reference, and at frame 600 the bird was approximately 0.994 m behind the reference. This is insufficient flight qualification; the moderate fold is not accepted for navigation or the final video. Later self-contact, other lanes longitudinal performance, approach, landing and biological realism remain unverified.

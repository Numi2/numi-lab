# Full-substep flight joint-speed observation

The instrumented host exactly reproduced all 9600 retained states and 153600 contact substeps in the stronger-heading-feedback test. All 8 lanes supplied 19200 samples each for body, toe and contact observations. No body or wing joint speed reached the current research-model limits in this used-seed obstacle-free run. Two lanes had early toe limit events (three and six substeps); none were observed during the walking, takeoff or later-flight windows. No non-foot ground contact was recorded.

Engineering speed limits and an uncalibrated aerodynamic closure are not biological validation. Fixed headings at one used seed do not qualify the controller for perturbations, climb or descent, navigation or landing. Exact replay parity demonstrates that this passive observer did not alter the retained trajectory; it does not validate missing physical dynamics.

# Preregistered supported leg-launch ramp

The wing-held matched handoff audit shows that the original crouch override ends abruptly and creates a second, previously unaccounted leg extension at ticks 373–374. This produced late lift and toe-speed threshold hits even in the supposed no-push control. Test whether an explicit, slower supported extension can generate a useful leg-driven release without that hidden jump.

Use the corrected research-only v15 model, projected aerodynamic closure, neutral active wing hold, used training layout/seed 20261126, 16 substeps, 400 ticks, no intentional resets. Extend the crouch window through 8.2 seconds in both matched arms. Preserve the existing bilateral-support initiation and extend its existing push latch through 8.2 seconds in both arms; the latch must initiate under both accepted foot loads, cannot inject force and is cleared by its existing navigation guard. In the push arm, only while supported or latched and not in the existing released state, smoothly change bounded normalized hip/knee/ankle targets from crouch (-0.85, 1.00, -0.20) to (-0.25, 0.29, -0.09), approximating the old policy handoff's final physical targets, starting at 7.4 seconds. Lanes 2–3, 4–5 and 6–7 use 0.12, 0.24 and 0.36 second ramps respectively. The hold arm keeps crouch. All authored actuator limits, effort bounds, toe behavior, contacts, aerodynamic terms and no-body-wrench settings remain unchanged. Lanes 0–1 remain inherited controls. The airborne action-history gate and release logic remain as authored.

Require first-episode completeness, matching accepted state/action/contact prefix through the first target divergence, valid drive and target histories, and measured actual target/effort limits. Record normal and tangential foot impulse, support, toe-speed events, modeled aerodynamic impulse, whole-body COM rise and velocity, nonfoot contacts, and time of release. For an exploratory pass both lanes of a pair must have three consecutive unloaded control intervals before tick 398 with upward COM velocity at first unload, positive push-minus-hold upward foot impulse over the preceding push window, zero nonfoot normal impulse and zero toe-speed threshold substeps through release, peak flap speed <=1 rad/s and absolute modeled vertical aerodynamic impulse <=0.02 N s through release. A pass does not establish real-bird calibration, a wing transition, flight, navigation or landing. Archive all failures and unused final layouts remain reserved.

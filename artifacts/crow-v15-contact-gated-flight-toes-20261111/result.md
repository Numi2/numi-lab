# Contact-gated flight toe candidate

The candidate gradually commands the real proximal and distal toe joints after a contact-derived liftoff latch, and schedules gradual opening when the final approach waypoint activates. Against the matching obstacle-free five-command development screen, all five first episodes ran 1600 intervals without physical failure or ground-contact impulse from observer step 400 onward. No sampled toe speed-limit hit occurred during that flight period; the nine hits in lane 4 occurred during initial settling. The original and candidate native replay q/v, actor observations and recorded actions matched for the first 370 frames in lane 1, before the toe ramp. At frame 450 its proximal toe joints were approximately 0.350 rad and distal joints 0.263 rad. Other lanes have substep speed and contact observations but no independently replayed joint poses.

Commands replace the actor and course obstacles are disabled. Approach-triggered opening, landing grip, route execution, recovery and biological calibration are not tested here. The filtered start includes the same early toe speed-limit issue as the reference. The tracking diagnostic errors use the raw command and q-height despite a flight adapter offset and must not be interpreted as exact tracking error. Frozen flight source is unchanged.

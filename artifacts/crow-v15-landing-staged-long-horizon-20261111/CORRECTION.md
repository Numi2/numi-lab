# Self-contact observer coverage correction

The previous flight-analysis scope incorrectly said the self-contact observer covered each first episode to its end. The host observer records crow-versus-crow self-contact only from steps 380 through 3800. The earlier zero counts therefore say nothing about steps 3801–5000. This correction preserves the prior commit and relabels the same counts. Perch and ground contact observers and their 16-substep coverage have a different, longer window; the extended landing failure and support findings are unchanged.

# Result: reduced hip pitch-rate feedback

All lanes matched corresponding no-feedback integrated runs for 200 pre-4-second states and 3200 contact substeps. The three -1 pitch-gain arms, with rate multipliers 0, 0.05 and 0.10, each retained at least one loaded foot throughout 4–6 seconds, made at least 0.077 m forward progress, and had no recorded toe speed caps or nonfoot ground impulse in that window. Their matched left/right loaded contact-speed integral ratios were 1.059/1.055, 1.033/0.998 and 1.070/1.106; all selected related-corvid stance-median signs held, although the +0.0037-degree late-ankle change in the 0.05 arm is effectively marginal. Positive-gain arms still had 20–24 unloaded substeps. No arm passed the earlier 25-percent reduction gate measured against the different older configuration; the matched ratios are separately reported because they isolate the intervention.

This is one correlated start per gain and a development seed, not an established gait. Speed integrals are impulse-weighted changing-contact proxies, not tracked slip, and related-species signs are not crow-specific calibration. The 0.05-rate setting is being retested uniformly through walking and launch across eight starts before any promotion.

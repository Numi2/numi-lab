# Contact-conditioned hip rate damping screen

The integrated screen found pitch feedback can eliminate simultaneous foot unloading but increases the loaded contact-speed proxy. The +0.8 arm's endpoint values show pitch-rate excursions and short corrective commands during the unload window; this is a hypothesis, not proof of causation. Test signed pitch gains -1 and +0.8 with angular-rate multipliers 0, 0.05, and 0.10 against unchanged no-feedback controls. Direct body moments stay off; feedback is applied through the loaded hip only. Require matched pre-feedback parity, zero simultaneous unloaded substeps, no contact caps or nonfoot contact, forward progress, related-corvid phase signs and less loaded contact-speed proxy. The impulse-weighted proxy is not tracked slip; the seed and starts are development-only, and positive findings require repeat tests.

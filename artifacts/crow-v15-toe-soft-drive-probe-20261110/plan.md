# Exact-dimension supported toe-drive screening

The 4-substep full v15 trial had up to 4.975 mm rigid toe–perch overlap during grip; an 8-substep continuous run had up to 5.606 mm during intermittent support and no release. Test whether lower toe drive stiffness reduces this in an isolated already-supported bench using the exact full v15 toe and perch box half-extents. Keep mass, toe geometry, torque limits, damping, friction, closure and release law and 4 substeps unchanged. Compare existing 0.10 N m/rad to 0.05 and 0.025, plus an open-toe control, over the same eight previously defined position, roll and velocity perturbations. This changes an uncalibrated research parameter, not measured crow anatomy.

Retain all starts and failed cases, normal impulses, rollbacks and state. Reconstruct each toe center and orientation independently using FP64 articulated kinematics and report accepted-pose OBB SAT against the static perch for supported grip, plus opposing grip, terminal support and release. A candidate requires at least 6/8 strict full bench passes (three consecutive bilateral opposed grip samples, a release, and all final 25 samples supported with toes within 0.05 rad), no excluded failures and worst accepted grip overlap at most 0.5 mm. A bench success is only a hypothesis for full continuous landings; numerical and contact validity, full navigation, and flight/walking requalification remain necessary. Do not use reserved final layouts.

The initial pose instrument queried the combined compiled scene and failed in all cases at step 10. Preserve those results as instrumentation failures, then repeat with the original robot mechanics for the independent FK query; this does not change the simulated model or controls. First check one case before executing the complete batch.

This bench uses its own stationary ground-scene setup and default MetalWorld library/shader, with v15-shaped boxes and attached toe model. It is not asserted to reproduce the exact full-route scene, collision filtering, solver configuration or arrival dynamics. Geometric agreement of shape extents does not establish identical contact physics; all findings remain bench hypotheses until a matched full-body test.

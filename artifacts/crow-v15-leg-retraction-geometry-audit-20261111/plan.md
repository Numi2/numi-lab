# Independent foot-clearance audit of leg-push candidates

The two late-trigger full-crouch variants achieved three consecutive rising intervals with no measurable foot normal impulse in all eight development lanes. Reconstruct each foot and toe box from every lane's retained accepted q/v, first validating the FP64 reconstruction against all matching replay-lane native poses and checking final-substep normal impulses against the published contact record. In the leg-only window require at least two consecutive accepted states in each lane where both complete feet and toes have >1 mm plane clearance, their full 16-substep summed normal impulse is <=1e-7 N s, root vertical speed is >0.05 m/s, and no non-foot ground contact occurs. Report first such states, clearance and root velocities. This checks accepted poses and entire-interval contacts but not continuous swept geometry, perturbation holdouts, transition to flight, all-waypoint navigation, landing or biological realism. No reserved layout is used.

# Pre-run solver-iteration convergence screen

The 32-substep rear-toe cap trial maintained supported bilateral opposition through the final 100 intervals at caps 1.15 and 1.20 radians, but worst rear-toe endpoint overlap remained 0.97 and 0.94 mm, exceeding the predefined 0.5 mm limit. Repeat the same eight conditions with velocity iterations increased from 4 to 8 and final velocity iterations from 2 to 4. Keep all other model, controller, starts, thresholds, timestep and instrumentation identical. The old reference trajectory is expected to differ. Record all 89,600 substeps and native failures; require no fall, 100 terminal supported-opposed intervals, 50 consecutive supported-opposed intervals, no nonfoot contact and maximum 0.5 mm endpoint overlap while opposed. This checks sensitivity to solver iterations, not flight arrival, perturbations, biological calibration or release.

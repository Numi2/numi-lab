# Bounded bank feedback: fewer contacts but inadequate straight flight

The prerecorded plan and binary are at commit 1b30d2ad536701e110b2380c413df311929bcca5. On used seed 20261126, all six candidate lanes ran to tick 1200 with zero observed post-release foot or nonfoot contact and zero toe-speed threshold events. Controls 2–3 matched their same-lane baseline exactly for all 1200 ticks; candidate lanes 4–7 matched through tick 600. The accepted state, toe, contact and flap effort observations were complete; no observer errors were recorded. The flap effort reached the modeled limit of 3.0. Reconstructed flap targets agree with the previous accepted-state roll/rate feedback to within 8e-8 rad for the sampled reconstruction points.

This is not a satisfactory turn or straight-flight controller. During ticks 500–1200, controls drifted −10.76 to −10.78 m sideways; +0.10 rad target lanes drifted −6.65 m, and −0.10 rad target lanes drifted about −3.29 m. The corresponding x advances were 4.85–5.12 m for feedback lanes. During ticks 800–850, mean rolls were about +0.151 rad for +0.10 target and +0.052 rad for −0.10 target; heading changes versus matched baseline were only −0.0033 to −0.0026 rad. The bank feedback changed the response and prevented the previous wing strikes but neither achieved both requested signed banks nor useful heading tracking. Treat both signs as failed maneuver identification, not flight qualification.

The original x-only 24-second screen did not test lateral movement; a separate posthoc lateral audit records the gap. These trials remain used-seed, obstacle-free and uncalibrated. No controller is frozen; disturbed starts, climb/descent, leg-powered full mission, landing and final layouts remain unqualified.

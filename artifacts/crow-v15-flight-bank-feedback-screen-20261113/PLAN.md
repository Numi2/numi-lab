# Bounded bank feedback identification

The +/-0.07-rad differential flap pulse produced wing/ground contacts on all four intervened lanes, with up to 0.70-rad roll and over 2.5 m/s lateral speed. Do not promote it. Test whether feedback from the existing accepted-state roll and body-x angular velocity can limit this observed instability on the same used seed, body, launch, force model, obstacle-free host and observers. Candidate lanes 2–3 are unchanged baseline controls. In lanes 4–5 the bank target is +0.10 rad and in lanes 6–7 it is −0.10 rad; smoothly ramp the target up during 12–12.5 s and back to zero during 17.5–18 s. Begin the stabilizing feedback smoothly at 12 s and keep it active through 24 s. Use one identical formula in the four lanes: differential flap-amplitude adjustment = clamp(0.08*(target_bank - accepted_roll) - 0.025*accepted_body_x_angular_rate, -0.025, +0.025) rad, multiplied by the 12–12.5 s smooth onset. Apply opposite adjustments to existing left/right physical flap amplitudes; no body wrench or state edits. The bank target is a controlled experimental input, not an autonomous route command.

Use 16 substeps and record all samples; require full-horizon exact accepted q/v/policy-action/contact parity for controls and exact same-lane baseline parity through tick 600 before intervention. Count first wing, body and toe contacts including the first termination-boundary substeps, toe caps, control effort and coverage; report roll, yaw, height, velocity and physical joint targets against same-lane baselines during pulse and recovery, excluding any post-termination states. Report each failure. A contact-free response with both signed targets would justify further tests only; this used-seed experiment does not qualify turns, disturbed starts, climb/descent, takeoff, landing, navigation, or biological realism, and does not authorize a freeze or consume final layout/seed reservations.

# Local rear-toe rate screen

The prior one-start rear-toe rate screen had one arm with 98 of the last 100 samples showing supported opposing force and 0.5132 mm maximum endpoint overlap. Its missed samples were brief rear-toe force dropouts, and peak overlap was on the released rear toe. To test a bounded local control change, retain the normal-cone feedback and all physical geometry, drive limits, front-toe law, release schedule and 16-substep mechanics unchanged. After the release, independently vary rear relief rate 0.006, 0.008, 0.010, 0.012 or 0.014 rad/tick and rear reclosure rate 0.006, 0.008 or 0.010 rad/tick across 15 lanes; lane 1 is the unchanged original reference. The 0.010/0.008 arm must reproduce the prior best lane exactly for all 1500 recorded states; lane 1 must reproduce the original reference and every arm must agree before the control change. Capture actual horizontal impulses and nonfoot contacts over all 16 substeps.

Require the same prespecified single-foot release, terminal 100 supported bilateral opposed-force samples, <=0.5 mm maximum toe-box SAT overlap during opposed grip, no other-body contact on any substep, bounded body speed and tilt, and no solver failures. Publish every arm. This is one exploratory developmental perch start, not a complete walk, autonomous arrival, unused layout, other-foot grip or biological qualification. A survivor must be repeated, tested on other-foot release and perturbed actual arrivals, and refined in time before any integration claim.

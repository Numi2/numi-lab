# Local rear-toe control rate screen

The unchanged reference, the previous best arm and every candidate before the rate switch reproduced their saved state traces exactly. All 16 candidates retained the 39-consecutive-sample physically clear, unloaded left-foot release with opposite-foot support, and none had nonfoot ground contact across any substep. The 0.010 rad/tick relief and 0.010 rad/tick reclosure arm retained 100 consecutive terminal samples of bilateral opposing force and support, but its worst independent endpoint toe-box overlap across opposed-grip samples was 0.5230 mm, exceeding the predeclared 0.5 mm limit. Every other arm also failed at least one gate; the previous best retained 98/100 and 0.5132 mm. No arm is promoted.

This is one development start with sampled endpoint geometry, not a landing, independent perturbation, alternate-foot release or biological qualification. Full detailed results, all failed arms, runtime and hashes are retained.

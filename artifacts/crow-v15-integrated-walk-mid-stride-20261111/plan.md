# Intermediate stride development control

The original 0.80 scale had 30 unsupported substeps in one of eight used starts; 0.65 reduced this to 10 while retaining three qualifying swings per foot. Scale 0.50 badly degraded support, walking and forward motion. Test the intermediate 0.60 scale on the same eight starts, 300-step horizon and contact gating; compare physical contact substeps, swing clearance, displacement and toe speed limits, without assuming a monotonic relationship. This is development evidence, not full-sequence or biological qualification.

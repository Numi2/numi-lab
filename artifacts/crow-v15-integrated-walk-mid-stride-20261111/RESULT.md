# Intermediate stride rejected

At the 0.60 walking-command scale, all eight used development starts experienced 19–181 no-foot-normal substeps, compared with zero in seven starts and ten in one for the 0.65 candidate. Six starts lost at least one qualifying swing bout and two starts had two and three toe-speed-limit substeps. The 0.60 scale produced no nonfoot ground impulse; forward displacement ranged from 2.35 to 5.13 cm. Logged accepted q/v, actor outputs, reported contacts and printed aggregate contact substeps through step 199 matched the reference.

This candidate is rejected. The 300-step test ends before takeoff, shape clearance is sampled at endpoints and these are used development starts. The 0.65 candidate remains unqualified for continuous walking or other starts.

All six development candidates exactly reproduced their archived q/v/actions/contact samples through tick 500. None reached the 900-tick horizon in its first episode. First-episode termination reason was CONTACT (3) at ticks 574, 636, 584, 585, 587 and 587 for lanes 2-7. The separate contact observer recorded the corresponding events one trace step later. The named collider pairs, contact impulses, preceding q, v and policy actions are in fatal-contact.json. This is a modeled scene-contact failure, not a solver-defect conclusion.

Before termination, some lanes had already recontacted the ground with a toe at ticks 520-553; two had no detected foot recontact before terminal scene contact. The lanes accelerated beyond the 1 m/s exploratory speed bound, with sampled late x velocities reaching roughly 1.75-2.30 m/s, while heights fell and pitch increased. All six failed the long-flight screen. Later states in the overall 900-step dispatch can belong to later reset episodes and must not be counted as uninterrupted flight. The absolute-root trajectory, complete first-episode ground/toe coverage, terminal pre-contact actions and attitude ranges are in analysis.json and fatal-contact.json. Detailed aero and individual actuator-effort logging after tick 500 was not captured and is unknown.

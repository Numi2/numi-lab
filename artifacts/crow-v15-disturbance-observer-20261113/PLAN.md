# Matched in-flight pitch-disturbance diagnostic

This development experiment probes recovery after an external disturbance at a physically attained flight state. A new observational host prints the pre-aerodynamic body-wrench sum for every lane and substep across the disturbance window; it must reproduce the established straight flight and first-episode state/action/contact traces exactly with the unchanged straight task binary before any recovery result is interpreted. The observational host does not modify integration or controller state.

In the gust task binary, leave the task-body-wrench ablation in place except for opposite normalized pitch requests of +0.5 and -0.5 on the existing bounded physical pitch actuator in lanes 6 and 7 for five 20 ms intervals starting at episode tick 900. All other lanes and task logic remain unchanged. This is an externally imposed diagnostic disturbance, not bird control or flight assistance; no task-body wrench may remain after it. Run both 1,800-interval teacher-off trials on the already-used seed 20261127 from full start with no resets and retain all observations and failures. Compare to the matched observational-host straight control, including exact q/v/action/contact prefix and other-lane parity. Verify measured pre-aerodynamic wrench direction, substep timing and absence outside the window rather than assuming the request was applied.

To count as a measurable recovery screen, both candidates must stay airborne and within the registered straight-flight physical limits, show at least 0.04 rad absolute pitch deviation or 0.10 rad/s world-y angular-velocity deviation in the 50 intervals following onset relative to the same-lane control, and have absolute pitch and world-y angular-velocity differences at interval 1100 below 0.06 rad and 0.15 rad/s respectively. The check is a limited same-seed recovery after a measured force pulse, not qualification over disturbed starting states, general flight or any unused layout.

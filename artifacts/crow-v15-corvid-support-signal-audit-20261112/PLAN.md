# Foot-support feedback attribution from retained traces

The own-foot support flag aggregates sole and toe contacts, and front-toe drive is conditional on that flag. Inspect the prior front-drive and combined-stroke trials during walking. For each lane, side and 20 ms interval with a positive captured front-toe target, sum sole, front-toe and rear-toe normal impulses over 16 substeps, and join the preceding recorded support flag. Count intervals with no sole normal, a loaded toe, and the flag set, including those with no measurable opposite-foot load. Compare backtracking lanes to controls, and retain all interval rows. The preceding logged state and interval targets may not align exactly with the gate evaluation or contact impulse, so this is an observational test of a possible self-support feedback loop, not proof of causality or a safety guarantee. No new physical controller or biology claim.

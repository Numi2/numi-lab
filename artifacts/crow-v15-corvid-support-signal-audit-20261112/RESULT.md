# Support-signal attribution audit

The own-foot support flag includes the sole and all toe segments. In the front-toe drive screen, 235 lane/side/control intervals had a positive captured front-toe target; all 235 had the preceding own-foot flag set. In 53, toe impulse was measurable but estimated sole impulse was not; 51 of those had front-toe impulse. The two highest front-drive lanes accounted for 15/43 and 25/51 such intervals and previously moved backward. In the combined front-toe and stance screen, the corresponding counts were 302 active intervals, 84 toe-loaded without measurable sole impulse, and 81 front-toe-loaded without measurable sole impulse.

These are interval-summed observations using a 1e-7 impulse threshold and a sole estimate obtained by subtracting toe normals from the recorded whole-foot normal. Tiny negative subtraction residuals are numerical rounding. The recorded prior flag, captured target and contact interval are not guaranteed to be coincident, so the association does not prove that toe feedback causes the gait failure. A controlled intervention is needed. No controller is promoted and no species-specific toe forces or timing are inferred. The source and complete rows are retained here; the input trials and their failed outcomes remain in their respective committed artifacts.

# Uniform terminal hip offset: failed generalization

The +0.12 bounded bilateral hip-target intervention was repeated in all eight same-seed development starts. Lane 7 exactly reproduced the previous candidate: all 4,800 accepted endpoint records and 76,800 contact-substep records have identical hashes, with four left-foot release and bilateral re-contact episodes and an upright supported last 25 intervals. All eight recorded five waypoint flags. Lanes 0, 2, 5 and 6 suffered physical failure at steps 3,442, 4,618, 3,987 and 3,811 respectively; lanes 1, 3 and 4 remained airborne at step 4,800; only lane 7 passed the final-25-interval support, speed and upright engineering screen and exhibited release/re-contact. Lane 5 also showed a release and later bilateral contact, but fell. The candidate does not generalize and is not a qualified landing controller.

The old no-offset baseline comparison in prefix-parity.json is deliberately through each prior episode's endpoint, not a pre-intervention-only test. Differences after intervention are expected; the four exact matches and four differences cannot establish pre-intervention parity. Exact within-lane full-trajectory replication against the earlier +0.12 lane is separately recorded in lane7-parity.json. The simulation uses the older flight law, an already-used layout and correlated starts; no unused-layout claim follows. Contact and flexion observations do not measure toe wrap or material slip. These outcomes and all first-episode data are retained.

The baseline and intervention match exactly for all eight lanes for the first 370 accepted endpoints and 5,920 substep contact records per lane, before toe-flight onset and terminal hip-offset action. See pre-toe-flight-parity.json.

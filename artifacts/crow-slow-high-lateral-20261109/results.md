# Slow, high lateral flight screen

All 80 perturbed-ground-start lanes passed their assigned sampled combined-command screen with no physical failures or failed simulator steps. The four lateral groups used +/-0.30 m/s lateral command with 0.15 m/s forward command and height targets 1.15 or 1.30 m. Median lane-mean heights were approximately 1.039 and 1.189 m, respectively. This supports trying those bounded commands in a development route teacher; it does not establish safety between samples, obstacle clearance, a stop, or landing.

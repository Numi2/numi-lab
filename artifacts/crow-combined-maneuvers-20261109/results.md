# Combined maneuver result

All 160 first episodes across two development seeds passed their assigned coupled-command screens, including each prescribed start perturbation. There were no physical failures or failed simulator steps. Commands combined 0.40 m/s forward speed with lateral velocity up to +/-0.30 m/s, yaw rates up to +/-0.02 rad/s, and altitude targets between 0.75 and 0.95 m. These tests used no obstacles and do not show route completion, continuous clearance or landing.

The automatically inherited JSON field named climb_minus_descent_m compares mode 3 with mode 4 in this study and is not a climb-minus-descent metric. For the actual high- and low-altitude groups, median heights were approximately 0.846 and 0.648 m. Original JSON and raw samples remain retained; this note corrects that inherited label.

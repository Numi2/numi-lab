# Perturbation screen for physical toe release and regrip

Test the -0.28 rad extension candidate, which passed the prior two initialized starts, on eight constructed starts: root longitudinal offsets of -12 and -8 mm with pitch ±0.04 rad, and root offset -10 mm with pitch ±0.02 and ±0.06 rad. Initial root x velocity remains -0.025 m/s. These are used development conditions, not an autonomous approach or reserved layouts. Match every lane to a release-disabled control with the identical initial state, and verify q/v and all contact substeps through its first support-triggered shift.

Use the same predeclared physical release/regrip gates and the independently checked box-clearance calculation from the preceding screen: complete 550 intervals, all contact and toe-velocity substeps, at least ten consecutive endpoint clearances of all moving-foot boxes while the other foot is supported and opposed, a return, final 100 intervals of bilateral supported opposed contact, no toe-speed caps, nonfoot ground impacts, falls, solver errors or overflows. Report each failure and any absence of a release trigger. Endpoint geometry cannot establish continuous clearance. A pass remains a bench robustness screen, not biological, walking, takeoff, flight or full-course qualification.

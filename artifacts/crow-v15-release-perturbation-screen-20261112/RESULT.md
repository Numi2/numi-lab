# Perturbed perch release: six of eight development starts passed

The -0.28-rad extension candidate was tested from eight constructed supported starts against matched release-disabled controls. All eight had exact q/v and 32 contact-substep parity through the beginning of their support-triggered shift. All 140,800 contact and toe-velocity substeps were present, with no logged solver errors or contact overflow. The independently computed box clearance agreed with the logged sensor to within 1.36e-8 m.

Six starts passed all prespecified release and final-support gates. Every lane had at least ten consecutive qualified endpoint-clearance intervals. Lane 0 failed because its left net upward impulse fell below the support threshold for one of the final 100 intervals (step 524); opposed front/rear impulses remained present in that interval. Lane 3 achieved the final support gate but its sampled toe velocity reached the -12 rad/s cap at two substeps of interval 262, after release began at interval 200. The velocity-slot index is retained, but the contact and actuator cause has not been established. No fall or nonfoot impulse was recorded. These two lanes fail the prespecified gates; neither is silently excused.

The trials are a matched *initialized perch bench* with root velocity -0.025 m/s, not continuous flight arrivals, a walking qualification, unused-layout evaluation or biological validation. Geometric clearance is established at endpoints, not continuously between them. The result rejects promotion as a reliable release controller, while identifying two distinct conditions to investigate.

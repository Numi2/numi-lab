# Intermediate contact-conditioned hip feedback screen

The previous controlled test found no continuously loaded foot support at absolute gain 0.5, while gains of 1 and -1 retained support but increased both feet contact-speed proxies versus matched controls by 23–47 percent. Screen signed gains 0.7, 0.8 and 0.9 to locate this tradeoff; unchanged control lanes and pre-feedback parity are required. Feedback only changes loaded hip drives, with all direct body moments disabled. Judge both support and contact speed along with leg phase signs, contact caps and forward progress. This is one closely related start per setting on one development seed; any apparent improvement needs independent, uniform repeated tests and is not gait qualification. Contact speed is an impulse-weighted proxy, not tracked material slip.

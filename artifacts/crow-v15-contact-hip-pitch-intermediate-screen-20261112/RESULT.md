# Result: intermediate hip feedback gains

All lanes matched the corresponding unmodified integrated gait for 200 pre-4-second states and 3200 contact substeps. Zero-gain control contact-speed integrals matched exactly. Of the six exploratory signed gains, +0.8 alone had zero simultaneous-foot-unloaded substeps, no toe-speed caps or nonfoot ground impulse, and all four selected related-corvid stance-median signs. Its loaded contact-speed integrals increased 23.8% and 33.2% relative to the respective matched no-feedback feet. No lane passed the full earlier gait proxy screen. The other gains had 6–42 both-feet-unloaded substeps. Each arm is one correlated start from one development seed, the contact-speed integral is not tracked material slip, and related-species sign hypotheses do not establish crow-specific gait realism. None is qualified for walking or a full mission. Further diagnostic testing changes pitch-rate feedback instead of accepting this tradeoff.

# Four-substep contact feedback in the landing reflex

The diagnostic substep accumulator reproduced the independently copied toe impulses within 6e-9 N s at the inspected bilateral impact without changing the full lane-0 recorded state or actor observation. Test using each foot's accumulated normal impulse divided by the 0.02 s control timestep as the contact load in the existing toe reflex and support-duration calculation after waypoint five. Before waypoint five, preserve the prior sensor, gait and takeoff. Keep the 25-consecutive-step support requirement and the frozen flight law; no action is directly caused by a nonexistent persistent support force, no force is injected, and contact loss returns the sensed load to zero on the next control step. Match the two used 8-lane development starts and 4500-step horizon. Predict at least 12/16 collision-free first episodes and at least four episodes with ten consecutive averaged-load steps above 0.1 N per foot and 2.2 N total. Also record first failures, substep grip, and sampled toe release; a one-step impulse does not qualify as support. This is a research teacher-guided test, not autonomous navigation or biological validation.

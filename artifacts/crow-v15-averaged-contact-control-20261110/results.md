# Result

The per-control-step impulse sensor was used as feedback in the existing landing reflex. Thirteen of 16 used-layout first episodes survived 4500 steps; all reached five waypoints and no solver failure was reported. The longest bilateral averaged-load streak was three steps, none entered landed mode, and there were no sampled bilateral opposing toe grips. The prediction of four 10-step loading streaks failed; this variant is not promoted. Toe extremes after 25 endpoint-candidate-free samples were 0.2028 rad and 0.0596 rad; the validated substep observer has shown that this endpoint-only criterion can include intermediate impacts and cannot establish free flight. A correct contact measurement alone did not produce a successful landing. These were privileged guided development runs, not autonomous qualification or biological evidence.

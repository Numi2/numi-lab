# Supported-tip recovery test

The matched contact-loss trial showed that lanes 0 and 6 continued loading their feet as forward pitch and pitch rate increased; a 0.2-second unloaded condition never fired. Test a bounded hypothesis: after 0.1 seconds with pitch above 0.12 rad and positive body pitch rate above 0.04 rad/s during landing hold, reopen the existing frozen flight/approach controller for one second before re-arming hold. Clear the existing release sequence on exit. The measured tipping is the trigger, not a claim of biological recovery. No force is added and the actor, walking feedback, body, contact solver and frozen flight law remain unchanged. Compare teacher-off first episodes, toe-speed caps, actual contact and sustained support against the retained localized-route run. This is a development course and the previously failed sign-flipped hip feedback is not reused.

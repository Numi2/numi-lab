# Passive perch-contact refinement

The 16- and 32-substep runs reproduced all 80 reference samples exactly. The first ten accepted control samples differed by up to 1.510 mm in root x and 0.02311 rad in rear-toe angle between 16 and 32 substeps; 32 versus 64 still differed by up to 1.370 mm and 0.01804 rad. Summed rear-toe upward impulse increased from 0.454 to 0.534 to 0.617 N s at 16, 32 and 64, while sole upward impulse decreased from 0.429 to 0.344 to 0.262 N s. These are integrated contact impulses across the first ten control samples, not per-step forces.

This fixed-target one-start passive-contact experiment shows resolution sensitivity and changing load distribution; it does not identify its cause or establish a solver defect. It does not validate walking, landing or biological realism. The full per-resolution traces and the tested runtime are retained.

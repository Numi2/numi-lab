# Height-aware capture did not generalize

Both original controls and all three repeated difficult starts passed the strict release and final 100-interval opposing grip gates. Of three new nearby combined position, height and speed starts, only the +4 mm forward, +1 mm high start passed. The +4 mm forward, +2 mm high and +4 mm forward, +4 mm high starts fell at intervals 140 and 138 before a qualifying release. This is 6/8 overall and 1/3 new combined starts, so the fitted height gate is rejected as a reliable general landing rule.

The two controls exactly reproduced 1,100 prior endpoint states and 35,200 substep contacts; all 140,800 substeps were observed. Endpoint normal impulses agreed within 7.6e-10 N s. These are constructed stationary perch starts with known geometry and uncalibrated high friction, not an actual landing or independent mission evaluation. No controller was promoted.

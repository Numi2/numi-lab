# Height-aware initial perch capture

Use the development-tuned bounded fore-aft geometric capture rule except disable its extra hip offset when the supplied initial height exceeds the nominal constructed start by 1.5 mm. Test three prior difficult starts and three new nearby height/position/speed combinations plus two original controls, with identical controller and strict release/regrip gates. This threshold is fitted from development outcomes, depends on known root/perch geometry and uncalibrated high friction, and is not an independent landing or general perception result.

# Contact-gated clearance feedback: failed single-support test

The candidate adjusted its lift target in 0.002 rad steps within the existing 0.15 rad cap after twelve clearance intervals, gated on open toes, observed stance support and posture. A post-run reconstruction from prior recorded observations found no feedback mismatches across 221 active records. The control exactly reproduced all 450 states of the archived no-return single-stance lane; paired states matched for 229 records before their first target difference. Contact substep coverage was complete, with no native/observer error or recorded nonfoot impulse.

Neither lane exceeded 12 consecutive intervals satisfying the registered release and single-support gates, far short of 100. The control and candidate first failed clearance at interval 230, and first reloaded the released foot at 232 and 233 respectively. Root height first fell below 0.1 m at interval 333 and 329; the stance toe reached the 11.99 rad/s accepted-state speed threshold before both height failures. The bounded correction therefore does not fix the physical behavior and is not promoted. Exact traces, control, binary and audits are retained. State/contact timing in this constructed probe remains independently unverified; accepted-state speeds do not establish substep maxima. This is not walking, flight or landing qualification.

# Release-gated leg-to-wing handoff: rejected development trial

The preregistered system-level handoff screen failed in all eight correlated lanes on used seed 20261126. The push arm first unloaded at interval 376; after release, its wing targets first differed from the held-leg arm at interval 378. Six 0.75–0.85 rad push candidates stayed foot-clear through interval 500, while the two 0.70 rad candidates recontacted at 389 and 396. Before unloading, the measured push-minus-hold foot impulse was 0.17087–0.17165 N s, modeled vertical aero impulse was about -0.000295 N s, and measured flap displacement was about 0.0022 rad. No nonfoot impulse, toe-speed threshold crossing or endpoint wing limit hit was observed in that window. These are development screens, not flight qualification.

The held-leg arm first unloaded at interval 414 in every lane. Inspection of the recorded targets shows that its leg hold expired at the configured 8.2-second boundary: targets changed at interval 412, prior to that unload, and its wings began flapping at interval 415. Thus this late held-arm event does not establish autonomous wing-only launch; the counterfactual stopped holding the legs. It also cannot establish the preregistered no-unload control. Any follow-up must explicitly hold the physical leg targets through the full diagnostic while preserving this failed result. Wing commands were intentionally different after release, so this control-rule trial cannot establish leg necessity under identical wing commands. Contact/force clock alignment, substep wing limits, perturbed-start flight, landing and full mission remain unqualified.

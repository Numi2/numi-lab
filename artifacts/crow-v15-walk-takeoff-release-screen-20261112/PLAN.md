# Supported unloading and wing-enable screen

The integrated gait-launch test showed leg push in both feet, but several lanes rose briefly and settled back while the wing-enable latch required 0.195 m height, upward motion and three consecutive unloaded intervals. The observed relationship suggests a threshold/timing mismatch, not proof of the cause. Test a 0.185 m threshold with all other logic and the three-unloaded-interval requirement preserved. Compare every lane to its matched initial state on two previously used seeds, including loading, impulse, wing motion, toe speed, airborne interval and return contact. The earlier-wing candidate preempted leg push and was rejected; this later release experiment must preserve the actual push rather than merely increase height. A short successful rise cannot qualify flight, landing or crow takeoff.

# Exploratory leg travel geometry

Question: can a small symmetrical leg posture change extend the feet below the body without a large horizontal offset? Apply the existing native FP64 forward-kinematics probe to one retained state from the modified-wing v15 development flight, holding the root, wings, other joints and velocities fixed. Vary each of the three bilateral leg coordinates by -0.2, 0 or +0.2 radians, 27 candidate pairs, without asserting these static poses are dynamically reachable, within drive limits, stable, or biological. The toe/leg kinematic chain is unchanged by the wing-inertia prototype. Report foot-height and fore-aft changes against the same accepted state; a later dynamic trial must establish actual movement and contact. Retain probe input, source and results.

# Standing restitution diagnostic: inconclusive

On eight development starts, the rebuilt control exactly matched the retained control through 3 s at all 1,208 recorded state samples and 19,328 contact substeps. Forcing the two selected new-impact restitution terms to zero changed none of those records. A deliberately non-biological positive control forced those same terms to coefficient 1 and threshold 0; it too produced exactly identical sampled states and contact substeps. All three runs completed the 150-step diagnostic without reported physical failure; they do not demonstrate locomotion, takeoff, flight, or landing.

The positive control therefore failed to demonstrate that these shader branches affect this scene. The null result cannot establish that restitution is absent from the actual contact path or is not contributing to bounce. The positive control is diagnostic only and is not a candidate for adoption. Identifying the executing contact path and energy transfer remains necessary. Commands, source patches, input hashes, compressed native logs, analysis and failures are retained here. See the preceding standing-bounce audit for the validated COM reconstruction and its limits.

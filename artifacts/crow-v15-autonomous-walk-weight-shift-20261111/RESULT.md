# Walking hip-roll weight shift: rejected

On the used development seed, the added 0.08-rad gait-synchronous hip-roll target worsened support: all eight lanes had unsupported substeps, ranging from 85 to 1,223, and each had at least two endpoints with both feet more than 1 mm clear. Seven lanes did not strictly alternate qualifying swing bouts; lane 1 had no qualifying bouts at all. No nonfoot ground impulses were observed. The pre-gait q/v and substep-contact comparison is retained. This open-loop weight shift is not a qualified walk, takeoff, landing or biological model.

# Matched physical standing feedback: development improvement, not qualified

Both three-second arms completed all 150 intervals on all eight development starts, with no reported physical failures or trace errors. The -0.5 arm exactly reproduced the earlier -0.5 screen in lane 1, and the +0.5 arm exactly reproduced the earlier +0.5 screen in lane 5, at all 151 state samples and 2,416 contact substeps each. An initial analysis invocation failed due to a stray here-document terminator; the analysis source was corrected and rerun, without rerunning or changing the native trials.

For the four starts with prolonged baseline unloading (lanes 1, 2, 5, 7), -0.5 reduced unloaded substeps during the initial 0–2 s from 1,342/1,302/1,272/1,300 to 655/648/235/188, and during 2–3 s from 202/158/433/70 to zero. Their toe-speed-cap substeps over 0–3 s decreased from 15/12/8/13 to 9/8/2/0. Peak absolute COM vertical speeds in 2–3 s fell from 0.204–0.241 m/s to 0.018–0.019 m/s. On lane 0, initial unloaded substeps increased from 91 to 110, while lanes 3, 4 and 6 matched the original counts. +0.5 was less consistent and increased toe caps in lanes 1 and 5.

These are matched observations on a previously used seed, with feedback applied only before 2 s. They do not establish initial peak velocity reduction, energy correctness, biological realism or robustness on unseen starts. An unloaded substep is not inherently a failure. Nonzero toe-speed caps remain in three bouncing starts. The trial uses zero rear-toe walking preload and ends before walking; it does not establish contact-driven walking, takeoff, wing-only flight, navigation or supported landing. Continue with integrated walking and matched contact-point speed, then assess toe dynamics and the full physical sequence before promoting anything.

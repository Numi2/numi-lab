# Contact gait sensitivity screen

The revised body showed real alternating contacts and 2-4 mm swing clearance in its 2 s developmental walk, but its impulse-weighted average contact-point slip reached a per-side 95th percentile of ~0.070 and 0.282 m/s in lane 0. This is too much for a stable stance. With the native endpoint-kinematics observer, hold the physical model, support-load gating, task, frozen flight law, launch, solver, 8 perturbed starts, 20261126 development seed and two-second 2-4 s walk interval fixed; test the archived baseline against four explicit variants. Variants jointly reduce leg oscillation amplitude and change the half-second step cycle to 0.65, 0.80 or 1.0 s; exact coefficients are in build_shaders.py and the patches. Predict a useful candidate must complete all eight lanes without physical/solver failures or nonfoot ground support, make >=0.02 m forward progress between steps 100 and 200, keep at least one foot above 0.001 Ns at all 101 endpoints, keep per-side stance impulse-weighted mean contact slip 95th percentile <=0.05 m/s, and demonstrate >=3 consecutive endpoints with >=2 mm clearance for each unloaded swing foot while the other foot bears load. The derived slip uses final-endpoint twist and a final-substep contact point, not a continuous ground-truth trajectory; thresholds are engineering screening limits, not biological calibration. Evaluate all candidates and retain failures. A passing 2 s developmental screen is not long-duration gait, held-out qualification, launch, landing or full navigation; no gait is promoted without follow-up on unseen conditions.

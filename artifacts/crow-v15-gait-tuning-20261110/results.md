# Contact gait sensitivity results

All four reduced-amplitude/slower-cadence variants failed the preregistered eight-lane gait gate. They retained forward motion but did not simultaneously satisfy the <=0.05 m/s stance slip 95th-percentile and >=3 endpoint 2 mm swing-clearance criteria on both sides. No candidate is promoted. All raw accepted-state observations, source patches, binaries and per-lane outcomes are retained. A follow-up tests the more specific support-gating hypothesis.

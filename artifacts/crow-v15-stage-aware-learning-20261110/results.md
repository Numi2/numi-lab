# Stage-aware training diagnostic

The active control block was decoded from teacher-label duplication in each qualified rollout. Four samples disagreed with transition-reported stage at boundaries, so the native action-label block rather than the post-transition outcome selected the trained outputs. In-trajectory validation mean absolute errors decreased from 0.0253, 0.1767, 0.1552, 0.1233 to 0.00339, 0.0595, 0.1164, 0.0232. These are correlated development samples and do not establish full-start navigation; teacher-off trials are separate.

# Quarter-strength support-phase command: rejected

The same-binary seed 20261127 candidate failed the declared physical gait and contact-point gates. All eight lanes matched their sham q/v/action and contact samples before walking; 2,400 accepted-state records and 38,400 contact substeps were captured per arm, without logged solver errors or overflow. Native foot/toe kinematics agreed with 100 lane-5 replay frames to within 8e-9 m position, 8e-9 m/s linear velocity, and 2.4e-7 rad/s angular velocity. Ground was static.

Although two-second candidate root displacement was 64.21–71.18 mm versus 25.07–29.71 mm in the sham, the candidate had only one qualifying left-foot and zero qualifying right-foot clearance bouts in every lane. It recorded 103 neither-foot-loaded substeps versus zero in the sham, plus one toe-speed cap in lane 3 versus zero for that matched control. No candidate nonfoot contact intervals were reported. These observations reject the configuration regardless of apparent forward progress.

Among loaded, approximately vertical endpoint contacts, candidate tangential point-speed median was 0.00369 m/s versus 0.00276 m/s sham, while the 95th percentiles were 0.05251 and 0.03484 m/s respectively. The candidate p95 narrowly exceeded the prespecified 150% matched-control bound. Contact samples include transitions and are correlated, so this is neither integrated slip distance nor biological validation. The stance interpolation fraction is not monotonic evidence: the prior full-strength experiment was a different shader and trajectories, and both configurations failed. No controller was promoted.

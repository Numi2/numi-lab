# Hysteresis release: rejected; deep toe penetration discovered

On the used development layout and seed, the lower hold threshold kept the release phase active but the left foot did not achieve 1 mm clearance and the controller stalled at sequence 220 without terminal bilateral grip. Seven lanes matched the latch control throughout their first episodes; lane 5 first diverged at step 3575. All substeps were covered and no contact capacity overflow was reported. Reject the hysteresis candidate.

Independent replay geometry found the left front distal toe more than 5 mm inside the perch at 197 sampled endpoints; at 52 of those endpoints there was neither an endpoint contact pair nor positive pair impulse in any of the 16 substeps. The deepest SAT separation was -22.86 mm. An independent FP64 CPU collision query on the step 3540 snapshot also reported deep box-box overlap while the live endpoint reported no pair. Toe shape type, half extents, local transform, perch extents and observer/replay q/v alignment were checked. The discrepancy persists before the latch and hysteresis diverge, so the earlier apparently supported grips are not physical qualification. A fresh-cache GPU restart is a separate pending diagnostic; the mechanism is not yet identified.

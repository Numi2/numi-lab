# Passive foot position sensor, preregistered

Question: can the accepted simulator foot positions and explicit final goal be carried to the next control step without changing the existing route? Instrument only, no change to controls. On each accepted state after waypoint five, encode final-goal x minus the mean x of the two foot bodies in millimetres, saturated at plus/minus 0.5 m, into experimental task-state high bits. Compare the decoded signal against independently recorded body and perch positions, allowing one control-step synchronization and millimetre quantization, and compare all first-episode q/v against the corresponding uninstrumented route. The explicit scene goal and exact body poses are privileged simulator state, not demonstrated visual sensing. A parity or sensor mismatch rejects use. Run both previously exposed layouts with their same starts; no claim of held-out generalization.

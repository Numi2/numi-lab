The selected development gait uses a support-aware stance command and 14% stance hip gain. It passed all 8 short development lanes with direct ground yaw/pitch request and wing flapping suppressed, though the right-foot slip margin was small. Extend walking from 2 to 12 seconds and shift the crouch/leg push and contact-triggered wing handoff 8 s later. While grounded and before the unload latch, command wing/tail actions and direct yaw/pitch requests to zero and additionally suppress the two direct body-wrench actuators in the physics actuation pass, so those actuators cannot prop up or move the body. Existing physical gravity, leg/toe drives, collisions, contact, and passive aerodynamics remain. No reset or pose teleport occurs. Resume frozen flight commands after accepted unloading or at the fallback time. The test uses 8 starts per seed for two new seed identifiers 74419361 and 74419417, which were absent from tracked artifacts at preregistration; paired push-trigger and no-push control arms each run 900 x 20 ms steps on a development course with course collisions disabled and ground enabled, corrected visuals and teacher-off zero actor/base actor.

Each foot must have stance-mean-contact-slip 95th percentile <=0.05 m/s, and at least eight separate swing runs of >=3 consecutive endpoints with clearance >=2 mm and opposite support. At least 6/8 push trials per seed must trigger the unload latch before 13.4 s and stay ground-clear for the final 51 endpoints while ending >=0.15 m above the start, while <=2/8 no-push controls latch early. Missing measurements fail. Assess no-push separately; preserve failures and actual ground contact rows. Slip uses accepted endpoint twist at final-substep contact points, so it is a screening approximation. This is an extended simulation screen, not proof of biological realism, final toe grip, route execution, obstacle navigation or supported landing.

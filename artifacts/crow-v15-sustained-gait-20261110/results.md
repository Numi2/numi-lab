# Sustained physical walk and launch results

The 10-second test on 16 perturbed starts covered two new seeds. The observer reproduced all 72 sampled q/v states exactly. All push trials advanced about 0.236 m, stayed upright, retained support, had no nonfoot ground impulses and achieved at least 20 clear swing runs per foot. All 16 push trials latched during unloading and ended airborne; no-push controls did not latch early and later became airborne after wing fallback. However, zero of 16 walking trials passed the unchanged <=0.05 m/s contact-point slip p95 gate: lane-0 left/right values were approximately 0.070/0.054 m/s on each seed. These limits use accepted endpoint twists and last-substep contact points; they are not continuous trajectories or biological reference data. The run therefore fails the full sustained-gait gate. No physical or solver failures were recorded. Direct ground body yaw/pitch actuation and wing flapping were explicitly suppressed until unload/fallback. Not a navigation, grip, landing or biological qualification.

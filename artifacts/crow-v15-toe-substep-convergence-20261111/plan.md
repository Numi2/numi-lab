# Toe contact convergence check

Repeat the cleared-reset, 0.1 g toe-segment test at 32 rather than 16 physics substeps with all other properties, actions, seeds and controller unchanged. Observe all substeps and evaluate the same two-second walking gate. Require no toe speed-cap hit and gait success in every lane; compare trajectories and impacts against 16 substeps. A change in step size alone is not biological calibration or full-sequence validation.

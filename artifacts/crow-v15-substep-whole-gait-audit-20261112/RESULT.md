# Whole-gait substep audit

The development baseline was reconstructed at 800 Hz from the retained, passive, exactly matched substep recording. The joint-anchor reconstruction agreed within 2.08e-9 m; replay body-position validation and the individual event boundaries are recorded in analysis.json.

Across the eight two-second windows, torso height spanned 2.26–2.43 mm, roll .0406–.0419 rad and pitch .0631–.0654 rad. The head's position relative to the torso spanned approximately .267–.277 mm forward/back, .360–.376 mm sideways and .875–.909 mm vertically. These are observations of the model; the reference data do not establish a corresponding American-crow head trajectory.

Of 25 selected recovery windows, raw knee and ankle-proxy minima were ankle-first in 16 and tied in nine; after a centered 21.25 ms boxcar, all 25 were ankle-first. Segmentation excluded partial contacts, missing toe groups and incomplete pairs. The proxy uses a modeled toe landmark whose anatomical correspondence is not verified, and minimum timing is not extension-onset timing. Thus this is a useful diagnostic contrast with the related-magpie report of knee extension beginning before ankle extension, not proof of a biological mismatch or a prescribed crow trajectory. Species, speed, surface, camera and filtering also differ.

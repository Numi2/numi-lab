# Fine ankle-phase screen: gait-direction gain, support failure

The unchanged reference lanes and prefix matched. All six candidate lanes showed the related-corvid hip/knee/ankle median direction sequence in their 5–6 complete stance windows, but all exhibited 20–46 substeps with both feet below the contact threshold in the two-second walking window. Although some foot-speed ratios were lower, every candidate failed the simultaneous-support gate. Toe speed caps and nonfoot contact were not observed.

The six starts are closely related, speeds differ from the magpie study, and the speed integral is a proxy. No candidate is promoted and no autonomous gait, flight or landing is established.

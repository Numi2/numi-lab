# Fine stance ankle phase test

The .15/.20 stance ankle amplitudes improved late extension but caused simultaneous unloading in 33–52 substeps, so test smaller increments .11/.115/.125 around the .10 reference with .125 hip swing and .10 knee stance. Retain original gait controls and the same strict contact speed, progress, support, toe and nonfoot gates. Also require hip-extension, knee-flexion, early-ankle-flexion and late-ankle-extension signs when interpreting stance medians. The estimates are based on related corvids at different speeds and are not numerical crow calibration.

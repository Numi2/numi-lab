# Gentle contact-driven wing transfer

A prior 65% contact-responsive wing-amplitude reduction caused 15/16 collisions when driven by endpoint-only contact readings. The validated diagnostic sensor now measures summed normal impulse across all physics substeps. An unmodified wing continues to lift the feet after brief contact, but causality is not established. Compare much smaller maximum amplitude reductions of 15% and 30%, driven only by an exponentially smoothed measured foot load (5% update per control step), against the existing unchanged-amplitude control on eight matched starts of used development layout J. Recover amplitude when measured load disappears; do not shut off the wing or inject forces. Keep the frozen flight law, toes and existing 25-consecutive-loaded-step landed transition. Prediction for either candidate: at least 6/8 survival to 4500 steps, four starts with ten consecutive bilateral control-step averaged-load readings above 0.1 N per foot and 2.2 N total, and at least one with three consecutive bilateral opposing toe-contact endpoints. Preserve failures. This is a guided exploratory test, not autonomous qualification or biological calibration.

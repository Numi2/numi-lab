# Matched held-command stance-hip pulse

The two constructed-state lanes had identical recorded q/v for 211 intervals before one opposite 0.003 rad right-hip target pulse. All recorded joint targets were equal between lanes throughout the next ten 20 ms intervals, and all 235 intervals had the expected 32 contact-observer records per lane without recorded solver or capacity errors. Opposing stance-foot normals were present at each accepted endpoint in the eleven-interval response window; this does not establish uninterrupted support between endpoints.

The positive-pulse lane initially had its center of mass 0.424 mm farther forward relative to the stance foot and 0.0123 m/s greater relative forward velocity. The position difference remained positive but diminished to 0.083 mm by interval 221; relative velocity changed sign several times. This identifies only a small local response under held target commands at one physically attained stance. The pulse does not establish a stable feedback gain or a reliable toe release, regrip, walking or landing.

Source, compiled executable, commands, complete state and contact substeps, analysis and input hashes are retained. The earlier experiment with continuing feedback remains separately archived for comparison.

# First-gate fixed-height diagnostic

With a fixed 0.85 m high-level command until the first waypoint, all eight first episodes reached that waypoint; the unchanged-actor comparison reached zero. Two of the eight physically failed at the first slalom by steps 1466 and 1585; the other six were incomplete at 1600 steps. Both observed first post-launch obstacle contacts in failed lanes involved left wing and slalom A. This is a privileged fixed stage-specific diagnostic command rather than trained navigation and does not establish route or landing success. Contact is sampled at control endpoints and does not determine first intra-substep contact.

Accepted q/v first differed from the original actor at observer step 103, before the walk and launch, because the override also affected earlier flight-control commands. The different prefix confounds a strict attribution of the improvement to flight height alone. The result supports a flight-only override follow-up with matched gait and launch before inferring the cause. The original host, frozen flight and policies are archived in the walk-launch controls runtime; the corrected replay writer is archived in the replay-writer roundtrip runtime.

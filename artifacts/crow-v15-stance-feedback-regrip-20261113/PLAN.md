# Bounded stance-hip feedback for release and regrip

A matched small one-interval hip-target pulse moved the center of mass forward relative to the supporting foot, with positive initial position response retained across the ten-interval held-command observation window. The preceding release experiment drifted forward until its posture gate blocked returning-foot placement. Test a small feedback correction in lane 1 against an otherwise identical no-correction lane 0 from the same constructed supported stance. Starting in release or return, use relative center-of-mass position and velocity to request clamp(-0.25*(relative_x - 0.010 m) - 0.04*relative_vx, -0.003, +0.003) rad at the stance hip while opposing stance contact is observed. Ramp correction by at most 0.0005 rad per 20 ms interval and ramp it toward zero after loss of that support. All other targets and limits are identical. This is a candidate experiment, not an asserted stable controller.

Run 450 intervals, 32 physics substeps, with complete q/v, joint targets, observer records, geometry and the exact compiled executable. Verify exact lane q/v parity before the first correction, target/rate limits, observer completeness and independent geometric clearance. A pass requires at least ten consecutive qualifying clearance intervals and 100 final consecutive bilateral opposing-grip intervals with the declared minimum support, bounded tilt and speed, no nonfoot contact and no fall. Report partial release, support loss, return blocking and falls even when they fail the gate. A constructed stationary transfer does not establish walking, airborne landing or biological realism.

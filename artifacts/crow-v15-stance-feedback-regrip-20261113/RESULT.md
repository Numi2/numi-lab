# Bounded stance-hip feedback failed to restore grip

The feedback lane and no-feedback control matched exactly for 170 accepted pre-intervention q/v states. The first nonzero correction was at zero-based interval 170; measured correction stayed within 0.00300001 rad and changed by at most 0.00050001 rad per control interval. All 450 intervals completed in both lanes, with complete uniquely indexed 32-substep contact observations and no recorded solver, observer or contact-capacity errors.

Independent clearance analysis found 11 consecutive qualifying release intervals in the control and 12 in the feedback lane. Neither lane produced any interval satisfying the combined bilateral opposing-toe support, tilt and speed grip gate after return. Both fell below 0.1 m root height at interval 306; neither maintained the required final 100 intervals of bilateral grip. The position feedback thus did not solve the stance or regrip problem; a marginal one-interval difference in clearance is not evidence of robust improvement. Geometry independently matched the runtime clearance sensor to within 1.4e-8 m. No nonfoot impulse was recorded in the analyzed contact stream.

Both lanes and their complete state, contact substeps, commands, source, executed binary and inputs are archived. This constructed stationary test does not establish a walking or airborne landing capability.

# Bounded contact-gated hip offset screen

In a validated reconstruction of the strong-balance development runs, the bird center of mass was often 1–3 cm behind the midpoint of its feet at sampled post-contact poses, with larger offsets as some birds tipped. Foot midpoint is not the center of pressure or perch center, so this does not establish stability. Screen small signed normalized hip-target offsets (-0.15, -0.05, +0.05, +0.15; two starts each) only after phase-2 landing and three intervals of measured contact per foot. The low-level frozen flight controller and approach remain unchanged. Run the full 5000-interval first-episode horizon; compare each start to its own retained aligned-descent baseline while recognizing that offset and starting state are confounded. Record all substep contact and toe velocities, partial and nominal-weight impulse windows, failure reason and incomplete timeouts. There is no release or held-out autonomous test; no gain is promoted without independent replication.

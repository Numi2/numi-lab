# Faster supported leg-ramp bracket: failed screen

All push and matched hold first episodes completed 400 ticks on the used development seed and layout. States/actions and contact observations matched through tick 372, with the first target difference at 373. The target-history, gain metadata and modeled effort-bound checks passed. These lanes are correlated settings, not independent replicates.

None of the six push lanes achieved three unloaded ticks through tick 400. The 0.04-, 0.06- and 0.08-second ramps respectively produced 2, 4 and 6 toe-speed threshold substeps in each paired lane; the matched holds had zero. Peak whole-body COM rise was 0.0146–0.0161 m. Peak residual wing flap speed was below 1 rad/s for four lanes but 1.38–1.41 rad/s for the 0.08-second pair, so the latter also fails the wing-holding criterion. All candidate pairs fail the preregistered release screen.

Net push-minus-hold vertical foot impulse over ticks 371–400 was +0.0071–0.0077 N s; this integral can hide transient impulses. The six leg position-drive efforts reached their modeled bounds, and recorded per-joint maximum efforts remained within those bounds. This is no proof of measured bird strength, biological motion or flight.

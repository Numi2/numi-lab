# Phase-adjusted push-off: development candidate

On eight previously used development lanes, the candidate preserves exact pre-intervention endpoint and contact parity, and all have upward impulse on both feet. From interval 348 to the last above-threshold foot contact, ground vertical impulse minus model weight is 0.10174–0.10182 N s versus 0.39577–0.39907 N s for the matched baseline. This is a force-balance diagnostic with aerodynamic and other forces present, not an isolated muscle impulse or a biological target. All eight are airborne and continue to step 500 without observed nonfoot launch impulses or toe-speed caps in the prespecified windows; root height is 0.958–1.018 m. This is a short development screen, not takeoff qualification.

The separately parity-verified substep observer in `crow-v15-downstroke-pushoff-substep-observer-20261112` reports both modeled wing centers moving down approximately 8.68 mm relative to the torso between the endpoint before the gate and the last loaded foot substep. This matches the qualitative ordering reported in a three-American-crow vertical escape study, but wing centers are not feathers, neither wing force nor within-substep causal order is measured, and the study does not supply a quantitative target for this simulated maneuver. The modified oscillator is experimental and the phase transition is not established as biologically realistic. The full continuous development trial is separate.

# Integrated journey walking window

The retained teacher-off localized-route development run uses a two-second gait window (steps 201–300), whereas the separately modified walking endurance trial used eight seconds. In the integrated window, all eight starts had three qualifying clear-swing bouts per foot, alternating left/right, with approximately 3.03–3.14 cm root forward displacement and no recorded nonfoot ground impulse. Lane 3 had 30 substeps with neither foot bearing a recorded normal impulse and two endpoint samples with both foot/toe boxes more than 1 mm above the ground; the other seven had no such unsupported substeps. This is not continuously supported walking across all starts. The longer trial’s 15 bouts per foot must not be presented as the integrated journey result.

The replay frame-to-observer offset was independently checked for lane 5; reconstructed foot/toe positions matched replay within 4.2e-9 m. Clearance is sampled at control endpoints, not swept between them, and the ground height comes from one replay. This is one used development seed and course, not biological or unseen-layout qualification. An initial audit invocation failed an assertion because the copied window bounds had not all been shortened; the corrected analysis is retained.

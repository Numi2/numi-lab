# Provisional contact-point velocity audit

The retained obstacle-free run provides contact points plus full body replay for one development lane. After correcting the separate warmup submission, all 301 walking-window replay configurations and velocities matched the accepted trace within the printed precision. The model exports articulated body positions at the center of mass and their kinematically composed velocities. An exploratory final-substep point-speed calculation gives an impulse-weighted median near 0.0036 m/s and 90th percentile near 0.026 m/s; these are provisional diagnostic values, not a validated slip estimate or a real-crow target.

The check of replayed center-of-mass velocities against 20 ms central position differences disagreed by roughly 0.05–0.06 m/s on average for selected feet/toes, comparable to their reported speed. Fast contact and limited temporal resolution can explain some discrepancy, but the calculation has not established the velocity convention, point timing or substep agreement. Consequently the apparent low slip cannot justify tuning friction or claiming stable nonslip stance. Require a direct synchronized substep body-velocity/contact-point observer, a kinematic finite-difference check at the simulation substep, and independent seeds before using it as a physical gait metric. This retains one used-seed, one-lane result and cannot establish whole-bird biological gait, takeoff, flight or landing.

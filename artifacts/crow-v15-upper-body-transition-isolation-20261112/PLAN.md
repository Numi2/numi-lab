# Isolate six-second upper-body transition

Before viewing this trial, compare an eight-lane, no-toe-command run on used development seed 20261127 against the retained six-second walking sham. Use identical host, seed, actor, body, leg/toe controls and 500-step duration; the only shader change holds the previous filtered action for upper-body action slots 0–6 from 6 to 10 seconds instead of abruptly requesting zero. Flapping amplitude stays disabled by the existing gate. This changes the post-6-second controller, not a crow-biological target.

Require exact state and accepted-contact parity up to and including step 300; complete 500-step first episodes and every endpoint and substep; no recorded error or overflow. Compare both-foot-zero-normal-impulse counts and longest bouts per lane for steps 301–400, non-foot contacts, wing/tail positions, and per-section root travel. Test the prediction that aggregate unsupported substeps in 301–400 fall by at least 80% relative to the retained sham, without added non-foot contacts. Report failures; even a positive result establishes sensitivity to this combined upper-body hold, not its unique causal pathway, species validation or full gait success. Do not promote this hold based on this diagnostic alone.

# Related-crow toe proportions on the current body: exploratory results

The unmodified current 40-DoF body is switch-off identical to the retained development run at all 2,400 recorded lane endpoints and 38,400 contact substeps. The experimental body shortens the two outer forward toe collision segments to 0.72 of the middle digit; this is a related hooded-crow skeletal ratio used only to motivate an exploratory collision-envelope approximation. Skeletal lengths are not collision lengths, American-crow measurements are not established, toe identity is uncertain, and the simplified shapes lack real phalanges and claws.

A changed-world policy fingerprint correctly rejected the first preflight. A diagnostic build excluding world and the eight geometry-dependent contact-envelope radii found the remaining checked observation and action layout inputs match; it does not establish semantic equivalence of observations. Copies of the original policy with unchanged weights and transforms were rebound only for exploratory tests. The production policy contract is unchanged. The first 300-step candidate run was started before the separately planned two-step rebound smoke; see SETUP-DEVIATION.md. A later two-step smoke recorded finite sampled values, 36 maximum active contacts, no reported errors, terminations or resets, and the expected sample counts; it cannot retroactively pass the planned pre-run admission.

On the previously used development seed, all eight candidate lanes survived 300 steps without reported physics failures. During the final two seconds, the candidate moved forward 0.0318 to 0.0327 m, compared with 0.0320 to 0.0339 m for the equal-toe control; both had three left and four right support-qualified swing bouts per lane, no both-feet-unloaded substeps and no recorded nonfoot contact. Sole-clear/toe-low endpoint counts were 190 candidate and 192 control. This is slow exploratory walking, not biological gait qualification.

The related magpie reference reports hallux touchdown and release before the third digit. In complete extracted stance runs containing both groups, both current candidate and equal-toe control had rear-first touchdown 40/40 and front-first toe-off 40/40. This grouping, threshold, gap bridging, simulated digit mapping and species transfer constrain the interpretation; see contact-order.json. On the candidate, sampled accepted toe-ground point speed in low-toe endpoint intervals was median 0.04598 m/s and p95 0.07502 m/s, versus 0.04553 and 0.07160 m/s for the equal-toe control. Contact points and post-step velocities have a small timing mismatch: these are not integrated slip estimates or biological limits. Candidate-specific forward kinematics and velocities were independently checked against recorded lane-5 replay. Full distributions and methods are retained in the JSON outputs.

The toe-proportion change has not corrected release order or low-toe behavior. It is not promoted, and the study does not qualify takeoff, flight, grip, landing, navigation, or realistic American-crow motion. Failed preflight, setup errors, the procedural deviation, controls, source, compressed raw output and audit scripts are retained here. Source and runtime fingerprints are in hashes.json and per-run inputs.json.

# Positive hip-roll pre-release shift did not work

All eight constructed-perch lanes completed 550 control intervals with 32 recorded substeps each, no malformed or missing substeps, and no native solver rejection. Unlike the first prototype, the restored torso/thigh exclusions prevented the immediate anatomical overlap failure. The last-substep foot/toe normal sums matched independently named native endpoint contacts across all 4,400 lane-intervals to within 8.8e-10 N s. The reconstructed foot positions matched the trace within 9.5e-8 m at sampled times.

Neither the positive target arms (+0.10, +0.18, +0.26 rad) nor the zero-target controls achieved even one interval meeting the full unilateral-clearance and opposite-foot-support criterion, and no lane completed the requested release-and-return. All roots dropped below 0.1 m in intervals 328–357. In the sampled pre-release poses, lateral center-of-mass offset from the supporting foot changed by less than 1 mm; a foot center is not a center of pressure. This simple feedforward hip motion did not establish an effective weight transfer. No toe speed-limit measurements were recorded, and no model, controller or biological qualification is implied. An opposite-sign follow-up is retained separately.

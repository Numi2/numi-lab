# Low-speed stop and reverse command adapter diagnostic

The frozen law clamps its input target to at least 0.15 m/s, and the first guided development routes flew past the perch after reaching waypoint five. Test an external adapter that supplies a 0.15 m/s internal command and offsets its reported forward-speed channel by 0.15 minus the true desired speed; this preserves the law's speed-error input but may change other uses of that channel. Test true desired speeds 0.00, -0.10 and +0.10 m/s on preselected development seeds 18722309, 18722341 and 18722369, 80 lanes each, five previously used maneuver modes, 1600 steps, no obstacles, teacher off, fixed head. Require the prior heading, lateral, height, toe and survival gates and the tighter sampled speed error <=0.08 m/s. This is an exploratory flight-only adapter, not an assumption that stopping or landing works. Freeze source remains unchanged; fresh route, perching and all final-layout tests remain open.

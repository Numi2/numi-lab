# Coordinated support-leg action

Changing only the support knee made all developmental birds topple. Native kinematics near stance suggests a coordinated negative knee and positive hip/ankle adjustment may extend the leg while approximately preserving horizontal foot position and orientation. Test two bounded magnitudes at two slow cycle periods with original swing, unchanged contact-based propulsion and no body wrench. Require the identical full 10-second slip, clearance, posture, progress, support and failure screen; a local Jacobian is not proof of dynamic stability.

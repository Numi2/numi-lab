# Event budget and landing result

The 16-event run reproduced all 3,839 recorded q/v states before the original failure exactly, then continued past the previously exhausted four-event budget with no reported failed GPU steps. It reached all five waypoint triggers under the privileged guide on used layout J, but the first episode failed physically at step 4224: the body descended below the perch, and bilateral thigh/perch contact was observed at sample 4225. The simulator subsequently reset the terminated episode; the later samples are not part of the first trial. The maximum bilateral-load streak before failure was three accepted control samples, with 108 endpoint samples showing some toe/foot-perch impulse, and no qualified supported landing. Minimum accepted-step sampled obstacle clearance before the failure was 0.1176 m.

The first contact around step 3839 was followed by a rebound and drift away from the perch. Later contacts showed front toes on the perch while the rear toes remained behind it; the continuous descent then produced the thigh collision. This is an actual approach/contact-control failure, not evidence that the solver's event limit should be increased further. The result does not qualify the larger event budget, full autonomy, the gait, grip, or any unseen layout.

# Autonomous toe curl without leg fold

On one used development layout and seed, seven of eight teacher-off first episodes reached five waypoints; lane 6 reached three, and lane 4 fell after reaching five. Lane 5 maintained a maximum 416 consecutive control intervals of at least half estimated body-weight upward impulse with opposing front and rear toe normals, including the final 25 intervals. Its final 25 endpoint maximum body speed was 0.0058 m/s and maximum angular speed 0.024 rad/s; there was no logged nonfoot perch impulse in its first episode. This is one supported arrival, not robust landing. The lane-7 replay did not show qualifying supported landing.

The reference and toe-curl run matched sampled accepted q/v, policy outputs and contacts through step 369 in all lanes. With substep coverage complete and no recorded capacity overflow, no self-contact normal impulse above 1e-7 N s was observed from step 380 to first termination and no toe speed caps occurred after step 500. Lanes 1, 2 and 3 still had 14, 6 and 3 speed-cap substeps before flight. This trial excludes deliberate release and leg fold. It does not qualify flight recovery, unseen layouts, biological realism or continuous inter-endpoint clearance.

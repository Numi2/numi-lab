# Fixed straight-flight command screen

Teacher off, direct body moments disabled, ground enabled and five course obstacle collisions disabled. Before intervention, all six command lanes matched the preceding reference; the two reference lanes matched throughout 24 s. In total 5,100 state samples and 81,600 contact substeps matched. All eight remained airborne during 8–24 s, without physical termination or recorded post-4-s nonroot speed caps. Four lanes retained early toe/neck cap events during initialization.

For requested speeds .35, .55 and .75 m/s, mean world vx over sampled 12–24 s was respectively .204–.205, .426–.427 and .646–.647 m/s. Each speed had one .95 m and one 1.10 m requested-height lane; sampled heights over 12–24 s were respectively approximately 1.02–1.07 and 1.17–1.22 m. Mean absolute world vy grew from .027–.028 to .091–.092 m/s with speed. World-frame velocity is not exactly the body-frame speed feedback, and 50 Hz samples are not continuous extrema. These are promising bounded model responses but steady errors, drift, one lane per setting, previously used starts and uncalibrated aerodynamic closure prevent flight qualification. Obstacle-disabled waypoint flags do not count as navigation; no turns, perturbation recovery, new layouts or landing were tested.

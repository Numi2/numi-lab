# Development-course teacher robustness

A single matched training seed is insufficient to qualify the route teacher. Before collecting demonstrations, challenge the 64/64 candidate on three development conditions, each with 64 first episodes, collision enabled, no scheduled resets and 3000 steps: training course seed 20261203, former held-out-a seed 20261204, and former held-out-b seed 20261205. The latter two layouts have already been used in development and are explicitly treated as training/development, not final validation. The frozen flight law, timing, and policy remain unchanged. Require 64/64 reach all five without physical failures or failed simulator steps in each condition. Report all lanes, first failure locations and contact candidates. No landing or learned-policy claim is made. Truly unused final layouts and seeds remain reserved.

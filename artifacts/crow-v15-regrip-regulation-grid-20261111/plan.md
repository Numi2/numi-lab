# Regrip regulation after contact-gated foot release

The exact single-start trial cleared and unloaded one foot for 39 consecutive samples while the other bore at least half the bird's weight, then regained support but not sustained bilateral opposed grip. In the last 500 samples each rear toe spent most samples below the grip threshold while occasional impulses above 0.010 N s triggered the original 0.020 rad relief; this suggests an oscillating regrip loop, not proof of cause. Keep the original controller as a reference and compare 15 combinations of rear-toe high-impulse relief increments (0.002, 0.005, 0.010, 0.015, 0.020 rad) and low-impulse reclosure increments (0.002, 0.004, 0.008 rad), applied only after the foot has returned. Preserve thresholds, target caps, foot lift, joint limits, body, solver and all other control. Run 1,500 ticks, require the reference to reproduce all 1,500 saved states and all candidates to match through the first 877 states before regulation differs. Require at least three consecutive truly clear, impulse-free single-foot release samples with the other foot carrying at least half body weight; final 100 consecutive bilateral opposed-grip and support samples; no other-body contact, bounded tilt/speed, no solver failures and all measured grip-pose overlaps at most 0.5 mm. A survivor is a single development start requiring independent replay, both feet, all perturbations, time-resolution and full-route checks. No unused layouts are used.

# Regrip regulation after contact-gated foot release

The baseline exactly reproduced all 1,500 saved states, all candidate traces matched through the first 877 states, and all 16 released at tick 777. Each lane reproduced 39 consecutive samples of separated, impulse-free left toes while the right foot carried at least half body weight. None passed the full release-and-regrip gate. The best terminal-grip count was 47/100 and its worst grip overlap was 0.614 mm; other candidates lost terminal support or had intermittent grip, with worst overlaps up to 1.924 mm. Several supported candidates remained below the overlap limit but did not restore continuous grip. No other-body contact was recorded. Adjusting rear-toe relief and reclosure rates alone did not solve regrip. These are development results on a single start, not full route or biological qualification; no reserved layouts were used.

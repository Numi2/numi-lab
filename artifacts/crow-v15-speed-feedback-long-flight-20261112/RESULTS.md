# 24-second open-flight development test

All eight previously used seed-20261127 starts remained in their first episodes and foot-unloaded through 8–24 s, with no reported physical termination, nonfoot ground impulse, or observed non-root speed-cap hit during 4–24 s. Some starts hit toe or neck limits during initial settling. Lane 5 matched the prior same-command screen through 800 samples and 12,800 contact substeps. Direct body moments remained disabled throughout and the five course obstacle collisions were disabled, so waypoint flags are not valid mission evidence.

In 50 Hz endpoint samples from 12–24 s, world-forward speed averaged 0.232–0.237 m/s across lanes, despite the extra sweep feedback's .55 m/s target; mean absolute world-lateral speed was 0.091–0.110 m/s. Sampled height ranges across lanes were 1.022–1.233 m; maximum absolute sampled roll and pitch were at most .038 and .077 rad. At 24 s world vx was .176–.196 m/s and vy .193–.201 m/s. Route actor commands remained active, so this is not an isolated straight-flight response, and world vx is not exactly body-forward tracking error. The controller is not qualified or frozen; realistic flight, turns, climbs, recovery, route and landing are not established.

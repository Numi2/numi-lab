# Result

With the larger new perturbations, the rear-first release passed the full prespecified grip, release, terminal support and toe-angle gate in 4/8 starts; the other four all showed bilateral opposing grip and subsequently lost it, but two failed terminal support and two maintained support while exceeding the 0.05 rad terminal toe-angle threshold (0.0828 and 0.0700 rad). Simultaneous and front-first openings also passed 4/8. All 8/8 open-toe controls retained terminal support and none had opposed grip. The prediction of at least 6/8 passes was contradicted. This isolated test started already supported on the square perch, and does not demonstrate a landing from flight, qualified toe release, or real-bird behavior. Starts differ from the prior test, so baseline parity is not applicable. The observational contact samples are discrete, not continuous.

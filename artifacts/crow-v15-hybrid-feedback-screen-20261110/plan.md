# Hybrid matched contact-limited feedback screening

The baseline abruptly commands 0.85 rad after initial rear contact. Prior contact-limited experiments used speculative CCD, so compare them anew on the exact hybrid/SAT matched stationary-perch case: (0) original control, (1) rear closure holds its target on measured rear-side contact, (2) rear and front closures hold their respective targets on contact, and (3) both hold on contact and close four times more slowly. Each contact is an aggregate impulse, not individual toe feedback. The support and automatic release law, body, material, drives, geometry and simulation settings stay unchanged. The original arm must exactly reproduce the matched baseline states. Record all runs, opposed grip, support, release, final open posture, and independent rigid-box SAT overlap. Any one-start improvement will require testing on all eight perturbed starts, plus flight, walking and full autonomous qualification; it cannot establish biological performance. No reserved final layout is used.

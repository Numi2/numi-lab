# Pre-contact leg positioning screen

Both control starts reproduced all 200 prior states exactly and all four initial shape sets were separated from the perch. The exploratory pre-contact use of the existing hip correction failed the supported bilateral grip and terminal support checks in both starts, including the previously successful one. It therefore cannot be integrated or promoted to broader testing. One candidate showed some opposing contact with a minimum gap of 0.248 mm but did not satisfy sustained grip or support. This diagnostic does not qualify flight landing or navigation.

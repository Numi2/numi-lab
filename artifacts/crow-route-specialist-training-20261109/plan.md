# Early-route specialist

Continue from the corrective route actor and train only on waypoint stages zero through four, because this actor is never executed after all five goals have been reached. Include qualified teacher episodes, early student recoveries, deterministic student recoveries, and successful full-student recoveries; exclude every failed first episode. Hold out the highest qualified recovery lane per dataset and course for development validation. Weight full-student prefixes twelvefold, deterministic student prefixes eightfold, and earlier student prefixes fourfold, and balance the five route stages. Fixed Adam 0.0001 for 300 epochs, every-fifth-step sampling, head/beak retention. Compare teacher-off execution with the prior route actor on matching starts while the landing specialist remains frozen.

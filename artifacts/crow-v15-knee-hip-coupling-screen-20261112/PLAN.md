# Matched knee-and-hip gait coupling screen

A 0.16 knee-only phase advance improved knee timing but increased right foot tangential contact motion in every lane; in one matched lane that increase was also visible at 800 Hz. Test the constrained hypothesis that reducing only the already-applied hip-swing carrier can retain earlier knee motion while reducing the contact-speed proxy. This is an exploratory coordination intervention, not evidence for a crow-specific target.

Use the prior exact uniform-gait baseline for same lane, same seed and same observer; two existing development seeds (20261126/20261127), eight correlated lanes each, 300 20 ms transitions and 16 physics substeps. Lanes 0–1 use original zero warp, hip carrier 0.125; lanes 2–3 use 0.16 knee warp with hip carrier 0.125; lanes 4–5 use 0.16 knee warp with hip carrier 0.10; lanes 6–7 use 0.16 knee warp with hip carrier 0.075. No other drive or physical parameter is deliberately changed. Compare lanes 4–5 also against the preceding same-lane 0.16-warp/no-hip-change trial to distinguish the compensation from the warp.

Before promotion require: all runs complete; exactly matched baseline controls and pre-intervention states/contact records; zero both-feet-unloaded substeps, no toe-speed cap or nonfoot ground impulse, per-foot 50 Hz p95 contact-speed <=1.5 times same-lane baseline, >=0.05 m forward root displacement, at least one complete recovery per foot with count not reduced, and median knee-minimum phase at least 5 percentage points earlier than same-lane baseline. Preserve all arms irrespective of outcome. This is a development screen, not production, a full mission, or biological validation; endpoint point speed is not continuous slip, starts are correlated, anatomical landmarks and speed differ from the study. No biological or controller qualification without independent follow-up.

# Matched landing-hover calibration

All four 2200-tick development-J arms reproduced q/v through waypoint five, had complete perch-substep records with no overflow, made no perch contact, and had no termination or failed native environment step. Over ticks 1900–2200, the planar RMS root-to-target errors for offsets 0.18, 0.21, 0.24 and 0.27 were respectively 0.1440, 0.0802, 0.0295 and 0.0637 m. The 0.24 arm had mean forward error +0.0109 m, forward-speed RMS 0.0320 m/s and maximum body tilt 0.0421 rad over that window.

Offset 0.24 was selected after this comparison for a separate development descent test. A close match at safe hover height on one exposed start does not establish landing, grip, reliable flight, independent layouts or autonomous navigation.

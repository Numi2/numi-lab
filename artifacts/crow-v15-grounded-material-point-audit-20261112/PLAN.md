# Exploratory fixed-material contact tracking

The prior contact-speed metric samples changing contact points, so it cannot establish cumulative slip. Reanalyze the already observed 800 Hz seed-20261127 lane-4 baseline and knee-warp traces with no new simulation. For each rigid foot/toe body, choose its largest-positive-impulse ground contact at the start of an episode; transform that contact to the body-local frame and follow that same material point through consecutive post-substep states only while some positive contact on that body remains within 0.5 mm in local coordinates of the initial anchor. End the episode immediately on lost contact, body absence, or anchor mismatch; never bridge a missing substep. Require at least four states (3.75 ms) and report rejected short episodes. A new episode may be started at the largest contact. Use static ground and report horizontal fixed-point path and endpoint displacement, duration, duration-weighted coverage and body, plus mismatch from trapezoidal integration of the independently reconstructed material-point velocity.

A matching contact within 0.5 mm is not proof that the exact anchored point remains loaded. Report the bound and only call the quantity a fixed-material point displacement under local-patch proximity, not exact slip. Solver contacts and post-substep q/v are not guaranteed synchronous. Contacts are correlated within one used lane, and this post-hoc tool is not a held-out comparison. Verify transforms and velocity against known synthetic translation/rotation before interpreting records. No biological gait, friction value or mission qualification is implied.

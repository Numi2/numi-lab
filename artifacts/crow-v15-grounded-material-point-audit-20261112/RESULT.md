# Fixed-material point tracking under a nearby-contact condition

The new offline diagnostic follows one fixed body-local material point per simulated foot/toe body while a positive ground contact remains within 0.5 mm of that initial local point. Synthetic translation, rotation, inverse-transform and velocity checks pass. For one previously used seed and lane, the baseline contained 209 qualifying contact episodes and the knee-warp trial 232; their median durations were 13.75 and 15.0 ms. The median horizontal endpoint displacements were 0.729 and 0.868 mm, respectively, and the corresponding 90th percentiles 2.46 and 2.99 mm. These are episode-weighted descriptive values across different contact episodes and must not be treated as independent samples or a causal estimate.

For the two arms, median disagreement between displacement reconstructed from successive positions and trapezoidal velocity integration was about 0.12 mm per qualifying episode; the 90th percentile was 0.23–0.24 mm. The 0.5 mm matching radius and unknown contact/state timing create additional error. A nearby contact does not establish that the exact tracked material point stayed loaded, so these numbers are not exact slip or total stance slip, and they do not prove the friction coefficient is wrong. Continuous ground-contact episodes, independent starts and physical/biological validation remain open.

The input trace files are archived at the commit recorded in `inputs.json`; restore those exact files from Git before rerunning this analysis. The kinematics binary is the existing v15 observer and is identified by SHA-256.

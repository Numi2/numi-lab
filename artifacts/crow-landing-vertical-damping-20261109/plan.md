# Bounded vertical-rate damping during descent

The +0.34 m descent target produced 45 of 64 supported finishes with no physical failures. More aggressive fixed +0.33 m and +0.32 m targets did not improve the result; remaining lanes show repeated vertical bouncing and slow planar drift around the perch. Prediction: a high-level height command that compensates measured vertical rate will reduce bouncing without modifying the frozen flight law or the body. Repeat the 64 matched 20261124 full-start training-layout trials, collision enabled, 3000 steps. After alignment, command clamp(perch.z + 0.34 - 0.30 * vertical velocity, perch.z+0.30, perch.z+0.38); keep all other flight, approach and support-handoff conditions unchanged. Require all 64 finish with at least 25 consecutive sampled upward foot loads and no physical failures. Retain q/v, contacts and failures.

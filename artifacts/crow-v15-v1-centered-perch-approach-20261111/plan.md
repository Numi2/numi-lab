# Center the development perch approach

In the paused-descent trial the rear toe oriented box was 53 mm from the perch at the handoff while only the front toes carried load. As a targeted exploratory intervention change only the teacher's landing target x offset from -0.035 m to +0.025 m, a +60 mm shift, leaving the phase gate, toe controls, flight controller, solver and scene unchanged. This is intended to place the rear toe within reach; it does not establish that the resulting pose is dynamically viable. Run a single full-start development J, 3000 control intervals and 16 substeps, with full contact observation. Compare q/v through waypoint five, actual toe impulses, toe/perch endpoint geometry, weight support and first failure. Require opposed forces for grip and do not call toe closure counters or a supported interval a completed landing. A successful trial still requires release, robustness, autonomous route execution, and untouched final layouts.

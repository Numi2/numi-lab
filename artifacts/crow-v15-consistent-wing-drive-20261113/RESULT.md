# Result: consistent implicit wing gains improve a short development launch

A first attempt stopped safely before simulation because changing model gains changed all policy contract fingerprints. Both actor packs were rebound to the new contract with exact layer arrays, activation, normalization, output transforms and clips checked unchanged. The original weights are not re-trained or validated for the new dynamics; the failed preflight and rebinding are retained.

The model-only research change replaced the flap DOF gain metadata 800/8 with 10/0.1, matching the experimental flap feedback while keeping the physical armature 3e-5, mass, inertia, geometry, contacts, and actuator effort limits unchanged. Both forward dynamics and contact inverse-mass dispatches reported implicit-drive flags, with sampled controller x/y/w scales all 1. The metadata enters both paths through the common model. This addresses a mismatch introduced by this research controller; it is not evidence of a general solver error.

All eight push and hold first episodes completed 400 ticks, with sampled state/action/contact parity through tick 345, correct previous target history and no omitted recorded aero force above 1.4e-10 N. In the 0.65-rad setting, lanes 6 and 7 maintained foot support until a three-interval release beginning at tick 357 and stayed unloaded through tick 400, with zero diagnostic toe-speed threshold substeps or nonfoot normal impulse. Push-minus-hold early normal foot impulse was 0.091 and 0.072 Ns in both recorded early windows; COM peak rise from tick 350 was 0.0939 and 0.0840 m. These two correlated lanes pass the short development screen, not leg-powered takeoff, flight or biological qualification. The wings-on hold also released and stayed unloaded, with COM rise approximately 0.0349 m; this comparison does not isolate leg-only impulse. Smaller settings failed: 0.25 did not release for three intervals, and 0.45 recontacted after release.

At 6.4 Hz, actual flap harmonic amplitudes in lanes 6 and 7 were approximately 0.634 rad vs target 0.631, with -0.41 rad phase, compared with 0.172 vs 0.631 and -2.58 rad before matching gains. For those lanes the late-window mean modeled vertical aero force was approximately 4.20 N vs modeled body weight 4.42 N, and no sampled total-aero power substep was positive in still air. Neither metric validates real crow motion or aerodynamic forces. The current sinusoidal research target stops after 8.02 s, so this 0.86-second unloaded window cannot establish continued flight. Longer no-reset trials, leg-only push isolation, flight maneuvers and disturbed starts, trained navigation and supported landing are outstanding. This result uses one development seed and training layout; the requested final layouts and seeds were not used.

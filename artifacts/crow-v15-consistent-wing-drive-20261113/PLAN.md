# Preregistered v15 wing implicit-drive consistency experiment

A posthoc observer found that the experimental wing controller applies kP=10 and kD=0.1 while the v15 model still advertises gains 800 and 8 to the implicit forward and contact inverse-mass operators. At 1.25 ms this may substantially inflate effective inertia and produce the observed flap lag. Test the research model with exactly the flap DOF stiffness/damping metadata changed to 10 and 0.1, preserving physical armature, mass, inertia, geometry, contact coefficients, limits, teacher-off policy, control law, projected-aero shader and every other DOF. This is a model/controller consistency test, not a blanket solver fix. The metadata is shared by all lanes, so high-drive lanes 0-1 no longer reproduce their previous configuration and are excluded from comparisons.

Use the same three 6.4 Hz flap half-amplitudes 0.25, 0.45 and 0.65 radians, with push and hold arms, used training seed 20261126, eight lanes, 16 substeps and 400 ticks. Verify the compiled model DOF indices, exact old/new gain values and no other model edits; record controller gain scales and implicit drive modes at runtime. The forward and inverse-mass consumers must receive the identical corrected model metadata. Require first-episode and finite observer coverage, exact push/hold state-action-contact parity through tick 345, correct previous-target history through ticks 336-400 and bounded working efforts for lanes 2-7. Compare candidate to the previous amplitude screen as matched starts, not identical states.

Report target/actual harmonic phase and amplitude, tracking RMS, saturation, same-state aerodynamic power in still air, modeled force, support continuity, first three-tick release, first recontact, whole-body COM rise, early push-minus-hold impulse, toe-speed threshold and nonfoot impulse. Preliminary launch requires continuous support until a three-tick release, no recontact through 400, COM rise >=0.05 m, no nonfoot impulse, <=1 toe-speed threshold substep and early push-minus-hold foot impulse >0.05 Ns in both windows in both paired lanes. An apparent pass is only a lead: qualify independent controls, wing-only/leg-only contribution, physically calibrated flight maneuvers, disturbed starts, continuous mission and held-out layouts separately. Preserve any invalid or failed run and do not promote the candidate. No unused layouts or seeds are consumed.

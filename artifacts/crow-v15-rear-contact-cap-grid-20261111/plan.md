# Rear-toe contact authority with front-toe feedback

The 750-tick development near miss had stable late support and 94/100 opposed grip samples, with the six missing samples caused by both rear-toe opposing impulses falling below the fixed gate. The rear joints tracked about 0.84 rad near the 0.85 rad controller target cap. Test whether modest additional bounded rear target range and different slow reacquisition rates improve continuity while keeping the front feedback, body damping, flight model, solver, impulse gate and overlap limit unchanged. Keep the exact prior controller as lane 1; other lanes cross rear target caps 0.86, 0.88, 0.90, 0.94 and 1.00 rad (within the authored 1.20 rad joint limit) with per-tick reacquisition increments 0.002, 0.004 and 0.008 rad. Change only the post-grip feedback; initial gripping remains unchanged. Run 750 ticks, require exact 750-tick reference parity and all-lane early parity, 100 consecutive terminal samples of opposed grip and physical support, no other-body support, bounded tilt and speed, no solver failure, and worst independent grip overlap at most 0.5 mm. Increased penetration invalidates a candidate even if it looks stable. All results are exploratory, and no unused layouts are used.

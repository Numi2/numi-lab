# Ankle phase screen: not admitted

All eight first episodes completed 300 intervals with no logged physical failure or contact overflow. All recorded pre-walk samples matched the previous trial and the zero-offset lane 3 exactly reproduced all prior records. Staggering the ankle drive did not produce a candidate that meets all gates in this used-seed screen: across lanes there were 424 both-feet-unloaded substeps versus zero held, several lanes hit toe velocity caps, and endpoint contact-point speed median/p95 was 0.00806/0.12238 m/s versus 0.00276/0.03484 held. The unchanged zero-offset lane retained 151 mm forward travel with zero unloaded substeps; phase -0.4 rad moved 148 mm but incurred 24 unloaded substeps. The largest tested delay traveled 32 mm and did not show an accepted supported-clearance bout for one foot. Other offsets and per-lane results are in the analysis.

The related-magpie study motivates checking actual anatomical joint timing; merely shifting the ankle motor target does not establish the reported joint coordination. Different phase values used different starts, so the screening differences are not independent paired treatment effects. The candidate is rejected without promotion, and the earlier knee-geometry audit identifies an additional structural mismatch requiring investigation. Low-toe measurements include loaded toe push-off; they are not assumed to be dragging. The full autonomous journey and supported landing remain unqualified.

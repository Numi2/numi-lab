Their relative yaw extremes over intervals 600–1200 are -0.25791 rad and +0.25725 rad, with corresponding lateral offsets -0.3641 m and +0.3439 m; neither comparison has missing observations. This isolates the scripted turning command from the removed symmetric twist for this matched seed. Its pronation saturation over the measured window is zero, with peak tracking error about 0.00197 rad. Both candidate lanes complete 1800 intervals of first-episode flight. The unchanged lane 0 and 1 first episodes fail at 760 and 764 in both runs; do not generalize the candidate-lane screen to all eight lanes.

Both shaders still contain the duplicated initial perturbation, so this result does not qualify the corrected reset. These are scripted, used-seed training-layout tests; they do not establish disturbed-start recovery, leg necessity, biological wing realism, a frozen flight controller, landing, or a held-out mission.

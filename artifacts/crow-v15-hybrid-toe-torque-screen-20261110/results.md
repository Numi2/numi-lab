# Hybrid toe effort-limit screen: insufficient

The original 0.020 N m control exactly reproduced the retained 200-sample hybrid trace. For caps of 0.020, 0.005, 0.001 and 0.00025 N m, the worst accepted-pose toe/perch overlap at opposing grip was 5.034, 4.423, 4.913 and 4.077 mm, respectively. Only the 0.005 arm met this single case's supported-grip/release/terminal-support endpoint test; all four remained far beyond the 0.5 mm overlap screen. No candidate is qualified, and one sampled case cannot establish robustness. The torque settings are estimated research parameters, not crow measurements. Source review shows an effort clamp in the World kernel, but actual effective drive effort under coupled contact has not been independently established; no physical torque-bound claim is made.

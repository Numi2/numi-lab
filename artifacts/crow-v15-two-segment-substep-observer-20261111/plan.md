# Full-substep observer for the 33-body toe prototype

The two-segment model completed a 120-step contact admission but reported only endpoint contacts. In a separate wide-body build, instrument the unmodified MetalWorld submission path to copy contact constraints and statuses after every substep. For this particular admitted model, scene body 33 is the perch, foot bodies are 10 and 13, and toe segments 17-24 and 25-32 are left and right. Report signed vertical force including friction, normal impulse, per-segment normal and signed x-normal impulses, and nonfoot impulse. Use no simulation mutation and compare all accepted q/v and final-substep contact impulse totals against the retained uninstrumented 120-step trial. Require 1,920 observed substeps, no buffer overflow or command error and exact q/v replay. This is observer validation, not a stable-grip, landing or biological test.

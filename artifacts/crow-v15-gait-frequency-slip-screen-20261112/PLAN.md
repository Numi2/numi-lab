# Contact-motion screen at slower gait frequencies

The new substep observer found substantial within-step motion in the .30-stroke, 2 Hz walking baseline. On previously used seed 20261127, retain its physical model, 0.40 crouch, joint phases, load gating and stroke; use two exact 2 Hz controls and paired frequencies 1.0, 1.333 and 1.6 Hz. Use the validated passive substep observer. A promising pair must travel at least .05 m in the two-second window, exhibit no both-feet-unloaded substeps, toe-speed caps or nonfoot impulse, and reduce the integral of normal-impulse-weighted tangential contact-point speed by at least 25% on each foot versus the matched baseline. Compare full-start prefixes and substeps. The metric is not tracked-point slip, these frequencies are exploratory and one used seed is not biological or mission qualification.

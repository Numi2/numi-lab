# Student-visited corrective routing experiment

After completing the prespecified late J and I teacher-recovery tests, use only routing prefixes of those recoveries that pass their independently recorded physical and student-prefix parity qualification. Include the earlier qualifying J recovery as a baseline. Explicitly exclude failed episodes and all samples after reaching waypoint five; neither the supported landing segment nor the earlier failed student rollout is a demonstration. Student-visited observations before each recovery takeover receive weight four and privileged continuation observations weight one; balance waypoint stages 0–4. Start from the exploratory deterministic actor evaluated on J and I. Keep the current v15 body and frozen flight controller unchanged, and preserve the actor's head/beak output by the existing supervised penalty.

Sample every fifth eligible step; use every fifth sampled point from each trajectory only as an in-trajectory optimization diagnostic, not an independent holdout. Train with fixed 0.0001 Adam for 120 epochs. Evaluate the exported deterministic actor teacher-off from full physical resets on the already used J and I development seeds, comparing waypoint count, first physical failure, obstacle clearance, terminal support and toe state to the previous actor. We require improvement on actual autonomous execution, not just fitting error; a failure or hazardous clearance is recorded and drives further correction. Final unused K/L layouts remain sealed. Only a teacher-recovery episode that passes its physical and parity gates may contribute; the exact included sources and dropped attempts must be reported.

# Staged toe extension screen

After gradual opening, compare bounded active toe extension of 0, -0.10, -0.20 and -0.28 rad (two starts each), ramped over 35 control intervals, alongside a +0.65 rad knee lift. The nominal toe lower joint limit is -0.30 rad. Measure all substep contacts, clearance, support, regrip, failures and sampled toe speeds. This constructed stationary bench is not autonomous navigation or biological validation.

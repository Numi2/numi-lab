# Walking ground impulse audit

The passive native observer reran the previous eight-lane sole-support swing screen, seed 20261127, 16 substeps and 300 recorded intervals. Each lane completed its first episode without a reported physical failure. All 2,408 endpoint samples and 38,528 toe/contact substeps, including the additional terminal sample, matched the previous run exactly. The first analysis assumed only 300 records per lane and correctly failed its count assertion; it was corrected to include the retained terminal record before testing parity. This establishes parity for these recorded quantities and starts, not all internal states.

Over steps 201–300 (two seconds), root world-x displacement was 25.9–38.1 mm. Across lanes, the ground-contact world-x impulse from both rear toes combined was +0.500 to +0.665 N s; soles contributed -0.321 to -0.458 N s, and front toes -0.118 to -0.310 N s. Total positive and negative foot-group impulses per substep nearly cancelled, leaving net ground x impulse -0.0072 to +0.0079 N s. The trace identifies where the forces occur, not a proven cause or a prescription to remove friction: stable steady walking can have small net impulse, and changing toe or sole forces may compromise support and release. Group totals combine contacts and do not show mechanical work. World x is only an approximation to forward; root velocity is not center-of-mass velocity; aerodynamic and other external forces are not included. The numerical values are not measurements of an actual crow.

The candidate remains unqualified: excessive low-toe endpoints and some unloaded support persist in the prior swing screen, and no continuous autonomous flight and supported landing is demonstrated. Use this trace to formulate matched interventions on stance/swing timing and foot placement; check speed, clearance, force balance, rear-toe release, other failures and full sequence against held starts. Do not promote the observer or experimental controller to production from this audit.

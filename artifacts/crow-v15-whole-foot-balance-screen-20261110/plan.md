In the same 16-substep development start, compare the two unchanged control arms with (2) the same rear-first release and (3) the same simultaneous slow release, but gate their existing 1.0-gain bounded hip correction on upward contact impulse from either foot sole or toe. The signal is taken from actual final-substep constraint impulses with the correct orientation and same force threshold; no force or state is injected. Both controls must reproduce all 200 previously retained states exactly. Record actual whole-foot signal, support, opposing grip, release, open posture and SAT overlap. This sampled gate can still miss within-step contacts. No success is accepted without sustained supported release, and the previously observed 1.128 mm grip overlap remains independently disqualifying.

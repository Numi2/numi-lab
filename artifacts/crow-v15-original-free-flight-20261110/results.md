# Matched original-body flight control

With the same fixed-head adapter, zero diagnostic policy, obstacle collision masks, commands, seeds and perturbations, the original active-head v15 body passed all 160/160 per-lane flight gates and had no native physical or solver failures. Maximum per-lane speed errors ranged 0.067–0.111 m/s against a 0.20 m/s gate. The corresponding toe-body control failed all 160 speed gates with no physical failures. Correctly bound original-body visual assets and support-group members were used, and the zero policy means image inputs do not drive the test. This paired difference implicates the toe-body changes as a group, but does not isolate a specific mass, joint, collision or numerical mechanism. It does not qualify the toe body, landing, navigation or biological realism.

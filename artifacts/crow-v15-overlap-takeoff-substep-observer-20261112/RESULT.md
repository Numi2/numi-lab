# Substep wing and foot overlap: upward wing motion

The observer reproduced all 2,960 endpoint q/v/actions/contact records and 47,360 contact substeps against the short 7.00 s candidate, with all 3,968 expected q snapshots and no recorded error or overflow. Native FK matched lane-7 wing and torso replay positions within 2.8e-8 m and orientations within 3.1e-8.

In every development lane, the last above-threshold foot normal load was interval 352, substep 4. Relative to the preceding control endpoint, both modeled wing-body centers had moved upward about 18 mm in torso coordinates at that sample; by the end of the interval they had moved upward about 42 mm. This indicates an upstroke, not the pre-toe-off downstroke observed in the American-crow escape study. The observer samples after each substep, so exact within-substep ordering and aerodynamic forces are not known. One used development seed, not independent bird data.

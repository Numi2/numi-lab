# Geometry-aware rear-toe clearance probe

A blanket 0.15-rad knee cap improved clearance by one interval but advanced the fall by four; fixed-state geometry showed the foot and rear toe trade clearance. Test a conditional correction on the combined 0.28-rad hip and 0.14-rad stance-pitch candidate. Candidate lane 1 may raise the knee correction above 0.13 toward 0.15 rad at the existing 0.004 rad/interval rate only when the previous accepted-state rear-toe gap is below 2.5 mm and foot gap above 3 mm. If foot gap drops below 2 mm, correction above 0.13 decreases at the same rate. Otherwise the original controller applies. The gap signals are exact native FK geometry available to this constructed diagnostic, not proven deployable perception; no collision or force is altered.

Require exact 450-state control parity to the combined candidate, paired prefix, independently reconstructed gap-aware target and bounds, all 32 substeps, no native/observer error, nonfoot support or accepted-state toe-speed threshold before failure, and 100 consecutive unchanged clearance, stance-contact, posture, load and speed gates.

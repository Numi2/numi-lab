# Flight actuation audit: direct body moments remain

The current built-in BirdFlow dove pack defines tail.yaw_moment and body.pitch_moment as bodyWrench actuators targeting the body, with scale 0.020 and wrench components 5 and 4 respectively (src/core/RunProgram.cpp). The estimated American-crow pack starts from that dove pack, renames dove identifiers to crow identifiers and adds physical sweep and pronation actuators. The journey task inherits the yaw action and explicitly appends body.pitch_moment. The v15 research flight law issues commands to action 13 for yaw and action 14 for pitch during navigation. The two-toe and hip-roll model construction used for the retained journey does not itself remove those actuators. The active shader and host must still be traced and measured before quantifying actual wrench contributions in a given run.

A commanded body wrench is a model-provided external moment, not a demonstrated aerodynamic force generated by the simulated feathers. Wing, tail and passive aerodynamics are also present, so this static audit does not say that either wrench is solely responsible for a maneuver or that disabling it will cause failure. Nonetheless, waypoint completion or stability with these actuators enabled is insufficient to qualify physically realistic wing-powered steering or pitch control. Qualification requires an explicit matched wrench ablation and, if needed, a physical wing/tail control replacement, with the new flight law requalified before route integration. The 90-degree toe-body turn screen and its slow response are retained separately; no control changes are promoted by this audit.

# Contact epoch and controller signal audit

The observer-only repeat reproduced every one of 54,480 stdout lines from the prior matched pitch-feedback trial, including states, commands and endpoint contacts. Across 900 endpoint events, final-substep normal-only vertical impulses match observer interval `event + 1` exactly; the same-numbered comparison has two missing events and 738 mismatches. All 28,800 normal-only substeps and 900 full intervals were present with no duplicates.

The full-interval impulse includes normal and tangential components; the controller receives only normal at the final substep. In control lane 0, the previously observed full-interval left share changed 0.074 to 0.579 between observer intervals 306 and 307. The corresponding endpoint normal shares changed 0.329 to 0.922. In lane 1, the full-interval share changed 0.017 to 0.441 between intervals 307 and 308 while the endpoint shares changed 0.140 to 0.821. Thus a raw final-substep load ratio is a noisy and different signal, not a direct measurement of the interval total. These comparisons establish timing and signal meaning, not that load transfer caused the subsequent falls. The matched grip trial still fails and no walking or landing qualification follows.

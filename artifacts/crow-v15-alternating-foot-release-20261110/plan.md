# Supported alternating foot release hypothesis

The neutral and -0.12 rad toe-only openings remove opposing grasp but retain front-toe/perch impulses, consistent with the toe roots remaining near the perch. Test a physically actuated alternating foot lift after the existing contact-conditioned release: command the left leg to lift only while the right foot is loaded, return it, then command the right only while the left is loaded. Use the previously exercised bounded walking-joint directions, smoothly ramping each movement; keep the v15 body, frozen flight controller, walking and push, landing, and grip behavior unchanged, and keep toes at the original open target. No external force or position overwrite is used.

Run matched original J and I full starts, teacher off and no intermediate resets, without tuning between them. Before the release phase, verify exact q/v, observations and accepted actions against the unchanged reference. Require all waypoints, no physical/solver failure or nonfoot perch contact, prior opposing grip, at least three consecutive accepted endpoints for each lifted foot with all four toe boxes separated from the perch by more than 1 mm, toe/perch impulse at most 1e-8 N s, opposite-foot load above 0.1 N and upward contact impulse above 0.00005 N s, and renewed sustained bilateral support through the end. A successful endpoint screen must be followed by matched substep impulse observation and replay parity before claiming physical release. Exclude failures and report all. This remains a used-layout diagnostic and has no biological or held-out implication.

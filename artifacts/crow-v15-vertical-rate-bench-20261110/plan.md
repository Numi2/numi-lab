# Matched exploratory vertical-rate feedback bench

Question: can a landing-only adapter reduce the vertical oscillation seen near the perch without changing the frozen flight law? Compare the existing stop adapter against one that doubles the measured vertical-rate input to that same flight law. Neither version contacts obstacles, and neither changes wing-force physics. Sixteen matching ground-start environments with seed 18722533, 1600 steps, five interleaved flight modes, fixed zero actor, and the same estimated body, wing aero, frozen controller and other conditions; seed is a new flight-bench exploratory condition, not a final layout or navigation seed. Every accepted support sample is recorded; sampled states from steps 1000-1500 are the analysis window.

Predeclared prediction: the median across paired lanes of the per-lane RMS vertical speed falls by at least 20%, no lane physically fails, all controller screen tolerances remain met (heading 0.15 rad, lateral 0.20 m/s, forward error 0.08 m/s, height error 0.15 m; toe peak under 12 rad/s), and no solver failure occurs. First episodes only, no retries. A pass only motivates a landing approach trial; it does not prove landing, biological realism, final-route performance, or full flight-controller qualification under the altered feedback.

# Matched vertical-rate feedback

All sixteen lanes in each arm met the fixed-command sampled flight screens, with no recorded physical or solver failure. The per-step observation window was complete for every lane. Doubling the vertical-rate input increased the median lane RMS vertical speed from 0.18088 to 0.19541 m/s, a median paired change of -8.0% for the proposed reduction. The preregistered 20% reduction prediction was contradicted; this adapter is rejected. These are estimated-body exploratory flight observations, not landing or biology qualification.

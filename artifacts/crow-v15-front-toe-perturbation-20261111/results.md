# Perturbed starts for contact-driven toe hold

All 16 lanes began with exactly the previously audited separated shape poses, and the candidate's original start exactly reproduced all 1,000 saved reference traces. The candidate passed the stationary terminal grip and support gate on only 1/8 development starts. The other seven never entered the stable-grip phase and all 1,000 of their candidate traces exactly matched their paired fixed-front controls, showing that the new post-grip feedback never had an opportunity to act. Four stalled while waiting for initial rear-toe contact and three while waiting for combined front/rear contact. Their longest supported intervals were 6–111 samples. Thus the single-start hold improvement is not a robust landing; initial contact acquisition and balance remain the main obstacle. No reserved layout was used.

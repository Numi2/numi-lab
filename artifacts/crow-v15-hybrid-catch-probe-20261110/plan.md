Repeat the full 0.8-second aligned-state catch experiment on all four matched perturbations using native hybrid CCD and CCD-enabled bird shapes. Keep the original geometry, materials, drives, gravity and contact-based neutral/instant/rear-first controls unchanged. This starts from a physically reached guided state with a fresh cache and held non-toe targets, so it is not a continuous route run or autonomous evaluation.

Predeclare no solver failures, no runaway accepted velocities (absolute root linear speed under 2 m/s throughout), no nonfoot impulse before grip, and at least three of four starts with three consecutive 5 ms samples of opposed front/rear contacts on both feet while adequately supported. After opening at 0.5 s require neutral toes and supported near-stationary final posture. Log each endpoint, contact, solver error, and wall time. Success requires independent CCD tunneling/physics checks, robust support, full-sequence walking/takeoff and navigation on both development layouts, and a final teacher-off evaluation on unused layouts and seeds.

## BodyParts3D geometry

The retained source OBJ headers for these BodyParts3D v4.0 members carry the legacy attribution/share-alike notice under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. This candidate conservatively retains that share-alike condition for the adapted optic-nerve geometry and its derived tetrahedral geometry outputs; that designation applies to those data, not to repository C++ or the authored material file.

The current DBCLS archive license page was checked on 2026-10-19 and specifies CC BY 4.0 for the database. It requires this exact credit:

We preserve the source-header CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan condition as the conservative per-source condition for these extracted OBJ files and record the current database-level CC BY 4.0 statement separately. ## Mechanical source

Park J, Shin A, Jafari S, Demer JL. Material properties and effect of preconditioning of human sclera, optic nerve, and optic nerve sheath. *Biomechanics and Modeling in Mechanobiology*. 2021;20:1353-1363. Published online 2021-04-20. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10237-021-01448-2. Open article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8298341/.

The reported optic-nerve longitudinal tangent modulus at 3% strain after preconditioning is 3.4 +/- 2.2 MPa. The material file's isotropic compressible neo-Hookean parameters are our explicitly declared effective proxy; the article's nonlinear uniaxial findings do not establish this proxy as a fit. The article is cited, not copied into this bundle.
